Property Description

Light, Bright, and Ready for Life! Welcome to your turn-key oasis in the desirable Bel Aire Village subdivision. This beautifully remodeled quad-level home brings the "wow" factor the second you step inside, featuring bright, open living spaces, fresh paint, and brand-new flooring throughout. With a massive 2,664 sq ft of finished living space, there is serious room to spread out. The main level offers a spacious living room and dining area anchored by a cozy wood-burning fireplace. This main living area flows effortlessly into the fully updated, modern kitchen, which features white cabinets and sleek countertops perfect for hosting. The unique multi-level design maximizes every inch of space. The upper level layout features three spacious bedrooms, including a generous 14x12 primary bedroom. Moving downstairs, you will find a sprawling 22x16 lower-level family room, a separate laundry room, and a fourth bedroom quietly tucked away for extra privacy. The basement adds even more space with an extra 16x13 recreation room, offering endless setup possibilities for a home gym, game room, or private theater. Outside, your private retreat awaits. Step through the sliding glass doors onto a fantastic, newly built deck overlooking a spacious, fully fenced backyard. It is the ideal setup for weekend BBQs, entertaining friends, letting the pets run, or gathering around the ultimate evening firepit area. Additional features include a 2-car garage and a prime location for shopping, dining, schools, and highway access. Move-in ready, completely updated, and packed with space.
