Property Description

**Charming Riverside Bungalow Full of Character** Tucked in the heart of the Riverside area, this beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom bungalow is full of character at every turn. Hardwood floors run throughout the main level, and the large living room is anchored by a stunning brick decorative fireplace and built-in bookcases on each side. Sunlight pours in through the windows, giving the whole home a warm, bright feel. The large dining room flows into the kitchen with a cozy breakfast nook, perfect for slow mornings. Downstairs, the fully finished basement offers even more living space, epoxy flooring throughout, a full bathroom, a spacious laundry room complete with a built-in cabinet/counter combo and sink. The basement also offers potential for a third bedroom. Outside, enjoy a large 2-car detached garage, a covered patio, and a fenced backyard made for relaxing or entertaining.
