Property Description

Charming, well-maintained, and full of personality, this 2-bedroom, 1-bath home is one you don’t want to miss! From the moment you arrive, you’re greeted by picturesque curb appeal, highlighted by wood-shake siding, a bold red front door, and an inviting front porch. Inside, you’ll find updates throughout along with thoughtful details that give the home its own unique feel. The kitchen features stainless steel countertops, an island, engineered hardwood flooring, and ample cabinet and counter space. All kitchen appliances stay with the home, including the gas range, dishwasher, refrigerator, and microwave. Open to the spacious living room, the kitchen flows easily into a bright gathering space with a beautiful wood-paneled vaulted ceiling and tons of natural light. Down the hall are two bedrooms, each with custom built-in closet systems, along with the full bathroom. Just off the kitchen, the mudroom and laundry area offers convenient backyard access and includes the washer and dryer combo unit, which also stays with the home. Outside, the large private backyard offers plenty of room to relax and entertain. You’ll find a large storage shed with an additional storage room, plus an impressive 3-car garage complete with workbenches that stay with the property. There’s plenty of room for vehicles, hobbies, a workshop, or whatever you need. And then there’s the surprise behind the garage. Tucked away in the backyard, a private swimming pool creates a true backyard oasis and adds an unexpected feature you don’t often find with a home like this. Located near highway access, just a short drive from downtown Wichita, and close to Linwood Park, this home offers both convenience and easy access to everything the city has to offer. Whether you’re a first-time home buyer, investor, downsizing, or simply looking for a well-cared-for home with plenty of extras, this one is worth seeing!
