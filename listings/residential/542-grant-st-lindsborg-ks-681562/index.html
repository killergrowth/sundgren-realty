Property Description

**Completely updated, beautifully finished, and truly move-in ready!** This Lindsborg ranch has been transformed from top to bottom, offering the space, comfort, and updates today's buyers are looking for. The main floor features luxury vinyl plank flooring throughout, new light fixtures, and a generous living room filled with natural light. The updated kitchen offers solid stone countertops, new cabinet fronts, a great pantry, and plenty of room for a dining table. A Brent Sherwood addition provides a beautiful primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and private bath featuring a walk-in shower. Two additional main-floor bedrooms share another full bath. A bonus room currently serves as an office but could easily accommodate main-floor laundry if desired. The fully finished basement adds even more living space with a spacious family room, fourth bedroom with walk-in closet, another full bath, and great storage. The two-car garage offers workshop space and convenient backyard access. Outside, enjoy the new concrete patio, private backyard with beautiful views of Coronado Heights, underground sprinkler system, and storage shed for lawn equipment and tools. Additional improvements include a 2022 roof with Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, new sidewalk, patio, and an added third parking pad. This home truly offers the best of move-in-ready living, with the major improvements already taken care of and plenty of room to enjoy both inside and out!
