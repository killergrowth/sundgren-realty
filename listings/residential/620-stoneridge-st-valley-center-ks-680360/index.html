Property Description

Welcome to 620 S Stoneridge, located in the desirable Prairie Lakes subdivision! This spacious 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers more than 2,500 square feet of living space, a 2-car attached garage, and an oversized driveway. The roof is less than a year old and features Class 4 high-impact-resistant shingles. The inviting living room features a cozy fireplace and flows into the kitchen and dining area, creating a functional space for everyday living and entertaining. Convenient main-level laundry and two main-floor bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite, add to the home's appeal. The primary bedroom features French doors leading to a deck overlooking the private lake, along with a full ensuite bathroom complete with a soaker tub, double-sink vanity, walk-in shower, and walk-in closet. The finished basement offers a huge family room, an additional bedroom, a bonus room, and plenty of extra living space. Outside, enjoy the large fenced backyard and peaceful lake views, making this home the perfect blend of comfort, space, and scenery.
