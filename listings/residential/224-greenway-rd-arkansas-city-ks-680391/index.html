Property Description

Welcome home to 224 Greenway! This charming all-brick ranch offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and cozy living. Featuring 3 bedrooms with a flexible additional space that could serve as a 4th bedroom, home office, or extra hangout area, there’s room to make this home fit your lifestyle. Enjoy gathering around the table in the formal dining room, then unwind on a cold winter evening beside the warmth of the fireplace. A long driveway provides ample parking, complemented by generous carport space and a nice-sized garage. Enjoy multiple covered porch areas, from the welcoming front porch to several inviting spaces in the backyard, offering plenty of options to relax, entertain, or simply enjoy the outdoors. Privacy fencing along both sides of the backyard creates a more secluded setting to unwind and enjoy your outdoor space. The home features a new roof, and the hot water heater was replaced in 2024. All kitchen appliances stay, including the refrigerator, microwave, stove, and dishwasher. With its inviting spaces and room to make it your own, this home is ready for its next owner.
