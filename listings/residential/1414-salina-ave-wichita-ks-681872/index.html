Property Description

Once upon a time in Riverside… Welcome to 1414 N Salina Ave, a storybook home where the original charm has been lovingly preserved. Scalloped wood details appear throughout the home, while tasteful wood paneling, thick trim, and wood flooring keep every room warm and inviting. Thoughtful built-in shelving, crystal doorknobs, and a built-in closet system add the kind of character that’s hard to find today. This 2-bedroom, 1-bath home also offers a finished basement with extra room to spread out, plus a detached two-car garage for parking, storage, or projects. Fully fenced yard. All of this is tucked into the beloved Riverside neighborhood where you can enjoy long nature walks along the river out of your front door! If you’ve been waiting for a home with a story to tell, this is the one. Schedule your showing today!
