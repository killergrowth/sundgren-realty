Property Description

Fresh updates, spacious living areas, and room for everyone! This 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in Wellington offers a versatile layout with plenty of space to spread out. Step inside to the main living room, where beautiful wood floors carry throughout much of the home. Two main-floor bedrooms share an updated full bathroom, while the second living area at the back of the home creates even more room to relax or entertain. Just above the family room, you'll find the private primary bedroom featuring a spacious walk-in closet and its own updated full bathroom with a beautifully tiled tub/shower combination. The kitchen offers plenty of space along with updated cabinetry, countertops, backsplash, and brand-new tile flooring. Fresh paint, updated lighting and fixtures, new plumbing, and brand-new electrical throughout add to the appeal. Complete with a carport and thoughtful updates throughout, this home is ready for its next chapter. Come take a look!
