Property Description

Custom-built in 2023, this home brings together one of a kind design, nearly five acres, and a backyard that makes staying home feel like a getaway. With five bedrooms, three bathrooms, and direct access to Yoder Airport’s 5,100 ft grass runway, there’s as much to appreciate about the setting as the home itself. Inside, wood feature ceilings, a stucco fireplace, and black Andersen 100 Series windows give the home warmth and character, while quartz countertops carry a consistent finish throughout. The 3,690 sq ft home includes a primary suite with a custom 7x7 ft walk in wet room and soaking tub. A 300 sq ft loft offers room for an office or retreat, and the 450 sq ft bonus room above the garage provides flexible space to make your own. The main level entertaining bar connects directly to the back patio and pool, making it easy to move the gathering outdoors. A 20x40 ft saltwater pool with a waterfall and automatic cover anchors the backyard, while the covered patio and outdoor wood-burning fireplace invite slow mornings, summer dinners, and evenings around the fire. More than 25 planted privacy trees will continue to mature into the landscape. For buyers who fly, direct runway access offers the opportunity to make aviation part of everyday life. Yoder Airport’s grass runway features pilot controlled lighting, and engineered plans are available for a 50x70 ft hangar. The owner can also provide a quote for construction. (The hangar is not currently built.) Whether you’re drawn to the custom details, the poolside gatherings, or the freedom to fly home, this property offers a setting worth experiencing in person. No special assessments.
