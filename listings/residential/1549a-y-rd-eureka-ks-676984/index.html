Tucked back a full mile off a private dead-end road in the Flint Hills, this one-of-a-kind 78.7± acre retreat offers a rare combination — a comfortable home, a complete horse setup, and some of the most scenic ground Greenwood County has to offer. Located just 8.5 miles east of Eureka and right off Highway 54, it's an easy drive from anywhere — just over an hour from Wichita, and only 30 minutes to both Fall River Lake and Toronto Lake. Despite the easy access, the property still delivers true seclusion, with the home connected to rural water. The land is beautiful, rolling country with a strong balance of open native grass and wooded areas. The topography rolls down into Bachelor Creek, which winds through the south end and creates outstanding recreational potential — including roughly 15 acres of woods with rolling terrain, scenic peninsulas, and plenty of deer, upland bird, and turkey activity. Previously used as a home and horse property, this place is equally suited to a weekend getaway, a working horse operation, or a homestead to call your own. The A-frame home offers 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a versatile loft. At its center is a large kitchen with island, eating bar, and dining area. The updated main-level bath features a walk-in shower and stackable laundry, while the primary suite enjoys its own full bath. The living room opens through double French doors to a large deck overlooking the property, and off the kitchen is a sunroom/storage area with a wash station. The unfinished walkout basement provides ample storage. Attached to the home with its own exterior entrance is a private guest suite complete with full bathroom, laundry, and kitchen. Built with horses in mind, the improvements are extensive — an oversized 23x23 detached 2-car garage with attached 16x23 insulated workshop, a 30x100 barn with finished tack room and living area, a 3-sided 6-stall horse barn, plus a loafing shed, storage sheds, and dog run. The setup is tied together with plenty of pipe fence, multiple paddocks, a pipe-fenced outdoor arena, and pasture to spare.
