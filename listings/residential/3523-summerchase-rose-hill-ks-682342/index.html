Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful ranch-style home nestled in the desirable Sienna Ranch neighborhood! From the moment you arrive, you'll be drawn to the inviting front porch, the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind with an evening beverage while visiting with friends. Step inside to an open, welcoming floor plan with beautiful views extending through the home to the backyard. The spacious living area flows seamlessly into the kitchen and dining spaces, making it easy to stay connected whether you're entertaining guests or enjoying time with family. A stunning stone-facade gas fireplace adds warmth, charm, and the perfect ambiance for cozy evenings and family gatherings. The kitchen is sure to impress, featuring a large granite-topped island with an eat-in bar, beautiful hardwood cabinetry, and a spacious pantry with plenty of room for all your extras. The generous dining area offers ample space for hosting family dinners and holiday gatherings. Designed with a desirable split-bedroom floor plan, the private primary suite is situated on one end of the home. This spacious retreat features a beautiful bay window overlooking the backyard. The adjoining primary bathroom offers a large soaking tub, separate shower, double-sink vanity, and private water closet. You'll love the convenience of the oversized walk-in closet, which connects directly to the laundry room. On the opposite side of the main level, you'll find two additional generously sized bedrooms and a full bathroom, providing plenty of space for family or guests. The finished basement offers even more room to spread out and entertain! As you head downstairs, you'll be welcomed into a large, versatile living area with windows that bring in natural light and make it feel anything but a basement. The wet bar, complete with beautiful granite countertops matching the kitchen upstairs, creates the perfect setting for game nights, gatherings, or relaxing with friends. Two additional spacious bedrooms and another full bathroom provide excellent accommodations for guests, a growing family, or additional living space. Additional features include a new HVAC system installed in 2024, a heated attached garage with abundant overhead storage, and fresh exterior paint completed in 2024. Step outside, and the backyard truly takes this property to another level! A huge deck offers plenty of room for seating and even a hot tub. Just off the lower patio, you'll find a built-in Big Green Egg cooking station, perfect for outdoor entertaining and grilling enthusiasts. A separate fire pit area provides the ideal spot to gather with family and friends and enjoy those beautiful Kansas evenings. The gorgeous landscaping surrounding the property is truly a must-see! Tucked away in the corner of the backyard is an incredible bonus space: a nearly 400-square-foot man cave or she shed, complete with its own air conditioning. Whether you envision a private retreat, hobby room, home office, or entertaining space, the possibilities are endless. Situated on over half an acre, this property also backs up to a beautiful greenbelt, providing additional privacy and the peace of mind that no homes will be built directly behind you. The expansive lot and open green space create a wonderful sense of privacy and make this backyard feel like your own private oasis. With incredible indoor and outdoor living spaces, five bedrooms, three full bathrooms, a fantastic entertaining area, and an abundance of extras, this home truly has it all. This is one you won't want to miss! Schedule your private showing and come see everything this beautiful Sienna Ranch home has to offer!
