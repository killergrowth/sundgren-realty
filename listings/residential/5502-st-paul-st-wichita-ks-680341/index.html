Property Description

Morning coffee on the covered patio. Conversation around the kitchen island. Holiday dinners that linger long after the plates are cleared. The Ellington Plan by RJ Castle Custom Homes sets the scene for these everyday moments with warm, inviting spaces and the comfort of zero-step living. Situated on a corner lot in Watermarke, this thoughtfully designed home backs to a drainage easement, providing added separation from neighboring homes and a welcome sense of breathing room. Timeless architecture meets modern organic styling through earthy tones, natural stone, and cedar accents. A walkway leads to the covered front porch, offering a welcoming introduction to the warmth within. Inside, natural paint colors, stained wood cabinetry, and carefully chosen accents create an atmosphere that feels elegant and comfortably familiar. High vaulted ceilings unite the living room, kitchen, and dining area, giving the main living space an open, airy feel. Settle into the living room beside an electric fireplace framed by custom nickel-gap paneling and a wood mantel. Across the room, built-in cabinets with a quartz countertop and floating shelves offer space for favorite books, collected treasures, and the personal touches that make a house your home. The kitchen pairs cream-colored perimeter cabinets with a stained wood island, complemented by quartz countertops, a custom-built range hood, and a walk-in pantry. Whether you’re preparing a quiet dinner or setting out appetizers while friends gather nearby, everything feels connected. The spacious dining area welcomes everyday meals just as easily as an extended table for holiday celebrations or a spirited game night. Open the patio door to continue the evening outdoors, where a generous covered patio offers shade, room to relax, and zero-step access to the backyard. Your primary suite provides a restful retreat, with a spacious bedroom beneath vaulted, beamed ceilings. The private bath features a quartz-topped dual vanity, a low-curb tile shower, and a walk-in closet. Two additional spacious bedrooms—including one with its own walk-in closet—offer flexibility for guests, hobbies, or working from home. A conveniently placed full hall bath serves these rooms and remains within easy reach when entertaining. Thoughtful details simplify the daily routine, too. Arrive through the garage and leave bags and jackets at the custom-built drop zone, with a separate laundry room tucked discreetly nearby. Outside, the spacious yard’s finishing touches are included in the price: sod, a sprinkler system, and an irrigation well. Discover how beautifully ease and elegance can live together. Come experience the Ellington in Watermarke and imagine your next chapter with fewer barriers and more room to enjoy being home. Some information may be estimated and is not guaranteed.
