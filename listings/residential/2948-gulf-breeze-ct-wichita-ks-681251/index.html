Property Description

Welcome to 2948 Gulf Breeze, located in the highly desirable Sandcrest neighborhood, one of West Wichita’s premier communities! Situated on over a third of an acre in a beautiful cul-de-sac setting, this immaculate home offers luxury finishes, thoughtful design, and exceptional indoor and outdoor living spaces. Step inside to discover an incredible open floor plan with over 2,000 square feet on the main level, fresh interior paint throughout the main level, and brand-new carpet throughout the home. The main level features three bedrooms, including a beautiful primary suite with a spa-inspired bathroom designed for comfort and relaxation. The fully finished walkout basement is equally impressive, offering a spacious family room filled with natural light, a wet bar perfect for entertaining, a dedicated gym, and additional living space. Outside, enjoy a screened-in patio, low-maintenance composite decking, fresh landscaping, and beautiful stone accents that enhance the home’s curb appeal. With its generous lot, cul-de-sac location, beautiful finishes, and exceptional condition, this home offers an incredible opportunity to enjoy luxury living in Sandcrest.
