Property Description

This beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom home offers modern finishes and move-in-ready convenience! The home has been completely remodeled including brand-new flooring, fresh interior paint, updated light fixtures, and a new kitchen complete with new appliances and granite countertops. All the work has been done—just move in and enjoy!
