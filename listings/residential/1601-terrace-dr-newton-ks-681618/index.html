Property Description

Welcome to 1601 N Terrace Dr, a freshly updated brick home on more than half an acre of mature, tree-shaded corner lot in Newton. Double front doors open to a spacious living and dining room with a bay window seat, recessed lighting, a modern chandelier, and luxury vinyl plank flooring. The updated kitchen features crisp white countertops, a gas cooktop, built-in wall oven and microwave, stainless appliances, freshly painted cabinetry with matte black hardware, and a breakfast bar open to the hearth room. The hearth room is the heart of the home, with rustic wood beams, a brick fireplace, and a slider to the privacy-fenced backyard. The 4 bedrooms offer ceiling fans throughout, with standouts including a room with custom built-in arched bookshelves and an upper-level bedroom with a built-in desk. All 4 full baths add convenience, including a beautifully remodeled bath with large-format tile, a built-in bench, and matte black fixtures. The finished lower level adds a huge carpeted Family/Rec room with recessed lighting, plus two non-conforming rooms that are ideal for an office, gym, or hobby space, along with a laundry room and storage. Also, enjoy the oversized 2-car garage with fresh epoxy flooring, a wide driveway, and room to roam. Move-in ready. Schedule your showing today!
