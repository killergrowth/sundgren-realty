Property Description

Room to live, light to love, and custom details throughout! Welcome to 2709 N Eagle Ct., a custom-built home by Prairie Construction on a large cul-de-sac lot. Featuring five bedrooms, a bonus room, three bathrooms, and 3,146 square feet, plus a three-car garage, this home combines generous space with thoughtful everyday convenience. Spacious rooms, tall ceilings, and oversized windows create a bright, airy atmosphere, while custom built-ins bring character and practical storage. Enjoy wide-open living spaces on the main floor—perfect for entertaining guests or sharing dinner with friends and family. A walk-in pantry keeps kitchen essentials within reach, and the primary suite’s connection to the laundry room adds ease to your daily routine. The lower level offers even more room to spread out, featuring an oversized family room, a bar, two spacious bedrooms, a bonus room ideal for an office or workout space, a bathroom, and ample storage. With room to gather inside and space to enjoy outdoors, this home offers a welcoming setting for everyday living and entertaining. Come experience the thoughtful details that make it special!
