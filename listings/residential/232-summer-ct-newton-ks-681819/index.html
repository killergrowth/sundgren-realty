Property Description

Why build new when so much more is already waiting for you? Welcome to Summer Crossing, where this beautifully maintained, 2025-built home offers all the appeal of new construction with the upgrades, finishing touches, and outdoor improvements already complete! Nestled on a spacious cul-de-sac lot, this exceptional 5-bedroom, 3-bath home features 2,710 square feet of finished living space, including a beautifully finished lower level with convenient mid-level walkout access to the backyard. Step inside to discover soaring vaulted ceilings, an inviting open-concept design, and a thoughtfully planned split-bedroom layout. The main level offers three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with private bath, while the fully finished basement features two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a generous family and recreation room. A convenient mid-level walkout provides direct access to the backyard, creating an easy connection between indoor and outdoor living spaces. The kitchen is truly a standout, featuring a walk-in pantry, upgraded double oven with convection cooking, high-end refrigerator with dual ice makers, upgraded dishwasher, and beautiful cabinet hardware. All kitchen appliances stay! Additional upgrades include custom-fit Blindster window coverings throughout, room-darkening shades in the secondary bedrooms, and a motorized shade with remote control above the staircase. And outside? The work is already done! Enjoy professionally installed sod, beautiful landscaping, a fully fenced backyard, an extended sidewalk, and a welcoming front storm door. These are the improvements that can add significant expense to a new build, and they’re already included here! Location is another reason to fall in love! Summer Crossing offers a wonderful neighborhood atmosphere with convenient access to a nearby park, splash pad, and public fishing pond. You’re also just minutes from the nationally recognized Sand Creek Station Golf Course, Newton’s shopping and healthcare amenities, major highways, and an easy drive to Wichita. Built in 2025, gently lived in, and thoughtfully upgraded, this home is proof that you don’t have to start from scratch to have everything you’ve been dreaming of. From the finished basement to the fenced and landscaped yard, the details have been taken care of so you can simply move in and enjoy. Almost new. Already home.
