Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful 5-bedroom, 3-bath home in desirable Sawmill Creek! With a spacious, open floor plan, there's plenty of room to gather, entertain, or simply enjoy everyday life. The kitchen features granite countertops, a large island with bar seating and extra storage, ample cabinetry, and all appliances included. The generous dining area is perfect for everything from weeknight dinners to holiday gatherings, while the inviting living room features a gas fireplace with a gorgeous mantle. Just off the living room, step onto the covered deck and take in the peaceful pond views and wide-open green space. Whether you're sipping your morning coffee, enjoying dinner outside, or unwinding at the end of the day, this might just become your favorite spot! The split-bedroom floor plan offers a private primary suite, a spacious bath featuring double sinks, granite countertops, a garden tub, separate shower, and walk-in closet. Two additional main-floor bedrooms and a full bath can be closed off for a little extra peace and privacy. Head downstairs to the view-out basement, where you'll find a large family room with beautiful built-in cabinetry and shelving, a second fireplace, two more bedrooms, a full bath, and a dedicated office with built-in desk. The three-car garage features coated floors and soaring ceilings with great storage potential, while the wrought-iron fenced backyard keeps those beautiful pond and common-area views front and center. With room to spread out, spaces to come together, and a setting you'll love coming home to, this one is definitely worth a look!
