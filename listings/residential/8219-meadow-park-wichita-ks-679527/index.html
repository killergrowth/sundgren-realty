Property Description

Welcome home to 8219 W. Meadow Park — a spacious, beautifully built home in the gated Fossil Rim Estates community in west Wichita, set on nearly an acre of private, tree-lined yard with plenty of room to spread out. With 6 bedrooms, 4.5 baths, and about 6,082 square feet, there's space to grow into and make your own. Inside, warm hardwood floors, arched entries, and custom details create a home that feels both special and easy to live in. The main level is built for everyday life — a cozy living and dining space with a gas fireplace, a hearth room perfect for movie nights, and a big, functional kitchen with two islands, a walk-in pantry, and plenty of room to cook and gather. A main-floor office makes working from home easy, and the laundry room is huge and offers a nearby built-in drop-zone bench. The primary suite is a true retreat, with a spa-style bath and a huge walk-in closet, while two more bedrooms on the main level offer flexibility for guests or however you need the space. Downstairs, the finished walk-out basement is made for everyday enjoyment — a big family room around a stone fireplace, a wet bar for entertaining, a billiards area, an exercise room, two additional bedrooms with jack/Jill bath, a separate full bath, and a concrete safe room for extra peace of mind. The backyard is a showstopper! A covered deck with its own fireplace, a brick patio, a fire pit, and a fully fenced yard with a private irrigation well and sprinkler system -- Not to mention great privacy! The side-load three-car garage gives you room for cars, bikes, and gear. All of this sits in the Maize School District, just minutes from Sedgwick County Park, the Northwest YMCA, and NewMarket Square's shops and restaurants — the kind of location that makes daily life easier and weekends more fun.
