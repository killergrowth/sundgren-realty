Property Description

Nestled in the highly sought-after Overbrook Addition of Crestview Country Club and feeding into premier Andover schools, this exceptional 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom ranch-style home offers a rare combination of space, style, and freedom with no HOA and no special assessments. From the moment you step inside, you will notice beautiful wood flooring flowing throughout the main-floor living areas and a great floor plan with generous room sizes you don't usually find. The heart of the home features a kitchen equipped with clean enamel cabinets, striking granite countertops, and recently replaced appliances. Designed for seamless entertaining, the kitchen opens directly to the spacious living area. Retreat to the ample primary bedroom, featuring an impressively updated primary bath complete with a relaxing jetted tub and a brand-new shower. The remaining main-floor bedrooms are also unusually large. Downstairs, the extensively finished basement drastically expands your living space while still offering great storage solutions. Outside, the property is crowned by a durable concrete tile roof. Step out onto the deck to overlook a beautifully fenced yard shaded by mature trees—your own private oasis. Car enthusiasts and hobbyists will also love the oversized 3-car garage, providing plenty of room to work.
