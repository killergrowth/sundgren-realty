Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully designed five-bedroom, three-bathroom residence offering just under 2,800 square feet of thoughtfully crafted living space in the highly sought-after Maize South School District! Perfectly nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this home combines comfort, style, and functionality in one exceptional package. From the moment you step inside, you’ll be greeted by an inviting open floor plan filled with natural light, soaring ceilings, and an effortless flow that’s perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The spacious kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring abundant cabinetry, generous counter space, and an oversized island that opens seamlessly into the dining and living areas. The expansive primary suite offers a relaxing retreat with a spa-like bathroom and a large walk-in closet, while the additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, home offices, or growing families. The fully finished lower level creates even more room to spread out with a large family room ideal for movie nights, game days, or hosting gatherings. Step outside and enjoy peaceful evenings in the private backyard setting with the added charm and privacy that comes from being tucked away at the end of the cul-de-sac. With its unbeatable location, spacious layout, and warm inviting atmosphere, this home truly offers the perfect blend of elegance and everyday comfort. Opportunities like this in the Maize South School District do not come around often!
