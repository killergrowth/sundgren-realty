Property Description

Welcome home to this inviting 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo offering spacious living areas and a comfortable, functional layout. Both bedrooms are conveniently located on the main level, along with a generous living room, dining area, and kitchen. The partially finished basement provides even more room to spread out, with a family room additional entertaining. Gather around the cozy electric fireplace and make yourself at home! Recent updates include new Pella sliding doors and picture window, a new water heater and sump pump, and added insulation in the garage walls and attic space. The all-electric home has no gas service and includes kitchen appliances and a water softener. Enjoy even more with access to the community pool and cabana, a great place to cool off, and make the most of the warmer months!
