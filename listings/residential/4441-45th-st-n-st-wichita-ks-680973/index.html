Property Description

Introducing Eagles Landing, an exceptional investment portfolio offering 32 residential units across 16 duplexes, NO SPECIALS, and in one of Wichita’s most strategically positioned locations. Situated at the southwest corner of 45th Street and Oliver, this remarkable property offers outstanding visibility, convenient access to K-96, and close proximity to Koch Industries, major employers, shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences. Built in 2022, each of the 32 units features four bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, modern finishes throughout, and an attached two-car garage. The thoughtfully designed floor plans provide the space, functionality, and contemporary features that today’s renters are looking for. Each residence also includes a private back patio with partitioned outdoor spaces, while select units enjoy beautiful water views. In addition to the 64 attached garage spaces throughout the development, the property offers an abundance of additional parking. One of the most significant financial advantages of this portfolio is that all special assessments have been paid in full, eliminating a substantial expense often associated with newer developments. With modern construction, desirable amenities, spacious four-bedroom layouts, and an exceptional location, Eagles Landing presents a compelling opportunity for investors seeking a substantial residential portfolio with long-term potential. Offered for sale for the first time since construction, this is a rare opportunity to acquire an entire 32-unit development in a highly accessible Wichita location. Whether your investment strategy centers on long-term rental income, portfolio expansion, or future appreciation potential, Eagles Landing deserves serious consideration.
