Property Description

Welcome to this extraordinary custom-built estate in the highly sought-after gated community of Fossil Rim Estates, offering an incredible combination of luxury, privacy, recreation, and space on 1.27 beautifully treed acres next to a pond in the heart of northwest Wichita and the desirable Maize South School District. With just under 6,000 square feet, a full walkout basement, 5 bedrooms, 4 full bathrooms, 2 half bathrooms, 2 offices, a theater room, wine room, exercise room, game room, in-ground saltwater pool, sport court, 4-car garage? and tile roof, this remarkable property truly offers every amenity imaginable. From the moment you enter, you'll appreciate the impressive vaulted cathedral ceilings, gorgeous tile flooring, expansive windows, and stunning views of the private yard and heavily wooded surroundings. The spacious great room features a beautiful fireplace, abundant natural light, and oversized sliding doors leading to the covered composite deck, creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. The chef's kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring stainless steel appliances, a brand new gas cooktop, large center granite island with eating bar, huge walk-in pantry, and a spacious dining area surrounded by windows overlooking the spectacular backyard. A formal dining room provides additional space for hosting family and friends. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, complete with a spacious sitting room currently utilized as a private office but equally suited for a nursery, reading room, or exercise space. The suite also features a beautifully appointed bath and an oversized walk-in dressing closet. On the opposite side of the home are two generously sized bedrooms connected by a Jack-and-Jill bath, along with a powder bath and large laundry room. The finished walkout basement was designed for entertainment and relaxation. Highlights include a large family room with fireplace, fully equipped wet bar with appliances, wine room, theater room, exercise room, game room, two additional bedrooms, and multiple bathrooms. Recent improvements include a beautifully remodeled basement bedroom, bathroom with heated floors and walk-in closet. Step outside and discover your own private resort. The backyard features a spectacular saltwater swimming pool complete with lighting, waterfall, fountains, water features, basketball goal, new liner, new pumps, and new heater. Additional outdoor amenities include a covered patio, tiered composite deck with clear-view railing, stone fire pit, fire bowls, sport court, irrigation well, sprinkler system, professional landscaping, and an oversized dog run. The backyard landscaping has been thoughtfully prepared for the next owner, with landscape rock already on site and ready for installation after future plantings are selected. The oversized 4-car garage is equipped with additional cabinetry, counters, utility sink, and even a stove, making it ideal for hobbyists, entertaining, or workshop space. Additional features include smart-home technology controlling the oven, pool, HVAC systems, and sprinkler system?. Home includes a water softener for added convenience. Perfectly positioned on a private, heavily wooded lot backing to a neighborhood pond, this property offers the rare combination of privacy, acreage, and convenience. Enjoy breathtaking views in every season while being just minutes from shopping, dining, schools, and city amenities.
