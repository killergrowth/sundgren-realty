Property Description

Enjoy beautiful views of the Little Arkansas River from this inviting 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath home! The living room, dining area, and kitchen feature attractive flooring and vaulted ceilings, creating an open and welcoming atmosphere. Step through the sliding glass door onto the spacious covered patio, the perfect place to relax, entertain, and take in the scenic river views. The main level also features a jetted tub for added comfort and relaxation. The basement offers a cozy family room with a wood-burning fireplace and gas starter, along with two spacious finished rooms, additional storage, and a separate room equipped with a shower and sink, providing plenty of flexible space for a variety of needs. Roof, water heater and HVAC have been replaced in the last few years. Outdoor enjoyment is a highlight of this property! Take advantage of the ample parking area with room for an RV, plus a basketball goal for shooting hoops. Head down to the river and unwind by the gazebo and firepit, complete with party lights that create a wonderful atmosphere for gatherings with family and friends. Whether you're enjoying a quiet morning on the deck, an evening by the fire, or time spent along the river, this property offers a unique combination of comfortable living, versatile space, and outdoor enjoyment. Come see all this river-view property has to offer!
