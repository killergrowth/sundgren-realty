Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful, spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offering 3,123 square feet of living space, tucked away on a quiet dead-end street and surrounded by mature trees and gorgeous landscaping! Step inside to a welcoming entryway featuring tall ceilings and abundant natural light, creating a bright and inviting first impression. The home offers a formal dining room perfect for hosting gatherings, a shaded covered front porch, and a spacious 16x16 three-season sunroom that provides the perfect spot to relax and take in the peaceful surroundings. The kitchen features granite countertops, a walk-in pantry, and plenty of space to prepare meals and entertain. Additional conveniences include a central vacuum system, a sprinkler system, a half bath located just off the garage, and a spacious three-car garage. The primary suite offers a large walk-in closet and the added convenience of its own private laundry room, providing both comfort and functionality. Downstairs, the walkout basement expands the home's living space with two additional bedrooms, a bathroom, and a large family room complete with a cozy gas fireplace, making it an ideal space for relaxing, entertaining, or accommodating guests. A wet bar area adds even more potential for hosting, while the utility room provides abundant storage, a second laundry area, a humidifier, and direct access to the garage. The basement also features a recessed walkout area with stairs leading up to the backyard, providing convenient outdoor access. Recent updates include a new roof in 2025, fresh exterior paint in 2025, and a new deck in 2025, offering peace of mind and inviting outdoor spaces to enjoy. The backyard is especially appealing, with beautiful landscaping, mature trees, and views of the adjoining field where wildlife can often be spotted. Whether you're enjoying your morning coffee in the sunroom, entertaining on the deck, or unwinding by the fireplace, this home offers plenty of space to spread out and enjoy a peaceful setting while still providing the features and conveniences today's buyers appreciate.
