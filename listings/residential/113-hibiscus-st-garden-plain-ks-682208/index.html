Property Description

Welcome home to this stunning two-story residence in Pretty Flowers Estates, one of Garden Plain’s most desirable neighborhoods. Built in 2004 and set on a 0.34-acre lot overlooking the pond, this home offers more than 3,700 finished square feet, five bedrooms, and three and a half baths. A striking brick and stone front, a welcoming circle drive, and a spacious side-load three-car garage make a lasting first impression. A grand foyer with soaring ceilings and beautiful built-in cabinetry sets the tone the moment you step inside. Just off the entry, a private office with custom built-ins and elegant glass French doors offers the perfect work-from-home space. The formal dining room features hardwood floors and a large picture window that fills the room with natural light. The breathtaking two-story living room has floor-to-ceiling windows and a beautiful fireplace, and it has an open, inviting feel. The gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining. It has hardwood floors, granite countertops, a center island with an eating bar, double ovens, a generous pantry, abundant cabinetry, and a spacious breakfast nook. An impressive staircase leads to the luxurious primary suite, with room for a sitting area or vanity and a private covered deck overlooking the pond. The spa-like ensuite offers separate vanities, a walk-in shower, a private water closet, a large jetted tub, and an incredible walk-in closet with extensive shelving and hanging space. Two oversized bedrooms on the upper level are connected by a Jack-and-Jill bath, and each one has its own walk-in closet. A versatile bonus room and a dedicated laundry room with additional storage complete the upper level. The walk-out lower level is designed for entertaining. It has a spacious family room with a gas fireplace, a wet bar, two large bedrooms, a full bathroom, a concrete storm room, and ample storage. Outdoor living is just as impressive, with covered and uncovered deck areas overlooking the beautiful pond. Multiple raised garden beds and a basketball goal add to the backyard’s appeal. Additional features include a sprinkler system, an irrigation well, a humidifier, and an owned water softener. The home is in the Renwick School District with Garden Plain elementary, middle, and high schools. Neighborhood amenities include a community pool, a playground, a greenbelt, and the pond, so this property is as functional as it is beautiful.
