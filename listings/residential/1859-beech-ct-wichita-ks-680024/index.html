Property Description

Pride of ownership shines throughout this lovingly cared-for twin home, owned by the same owner for 33 years! Featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a 1-car garage, this home offers a comfortable layout with thoughtful updates and timeless details. The main floor includes two bedrooms and a full bathroom, while the finished basement provides a spacious second living area, a third bedroom, and an additional full bathroom. Enjoy the updated kitchen and bath, Andersen windows, and classic California shutters. A cedar closet adds a special touch, while the fireplace with a gas starter creates a cozy focal point for relaxing evenings. Located on a cul-de-sac, the home also offers a fully fenced backyard with professional landscaping, a sprinkler system, and a brick patio tucked beneath a beautiful mature shade tree - the perfect setting for outdoor enjoyment or entertaining.
