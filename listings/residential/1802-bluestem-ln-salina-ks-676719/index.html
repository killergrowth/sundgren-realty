Property Description

Welcome to this exceptional 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home with a 3-car garage, situated on over half an acre in one of East Salina's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering the perfect blend of space, style, and functionality, this home is designed for comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. Step inside to find an inviting open-concept floor plan featuring a spacious living area, formal dining room, and beautifully remodeled bathrooms. Fresh exterior paint enhances the home's curb appeal, while the charming front porch provides the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee. The covered back patio overlooks the expansive backyard, offering plenty of room for outdoor gatherings, play, or future additions. The fully finished basement expands your living space with two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and two versatile bonus rooms ideal for a home office, gym, playroom, media room, or guest accommodations.
