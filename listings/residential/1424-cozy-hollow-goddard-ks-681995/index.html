Property Description

Imagine coming home to a place where every detail feels intentional, every space has a purpose, and there’s always room for one more memory. Welcome to The Abram by A7 Homes. From holidays filled with laughter to quiet evenings gathered around the fireplace, this home was designed for the moments that make life meaningful. Vaulted ceilings, and a beautiful gas fireplace with a custom media center create a living space that feels both elegant and inviting. The thoughtfully designed kitchen features quartz countertops, a spacious island, and beautiful finishes that make everyday living and entertaining a pleasure. Just beyond the living area, an expansive 26-foot covered deck offers the perfect setting for outdoor gatherings or simply enjoying a peaceful evening. The primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with a beautifully tiled shower, double vanities, and a custom walk-in closet featuring built-in dressers. Two additional main-floor bedrooms offer comfort and versatility. Downstairs, the finished view-out basement is an entertainer’s dream, complete with a spacious family room, Rec room, full wet bar, two additional bedrooms, and a full bathroom. And for a little unexpected charm, a hidden bookcase opens to a finished craft room, adding a unique touch of personality to this already exceptional home. A welcoming covered front porch, beautiful stone accents, and a three-car garage complete this thoughtfully designed home. The beauty of The Abram isn’t just in the details you see today, but in the memories you’ll create tomorrow. PLEASE NOTE: This property is a model home and is NOT FOR SALE.
