Property Description

Immaculate Gatewood Beauty with Private Spa & Outdoor Oasis! Welcome to this stunning one-of-a-kind Lakepoint home, full of charm, character, and luxurious updates! From the moment you walk through the wood and glass front door, you'll notice the attention to detail—from the spacious entryway with dual coat closets to the elegant wood and glass staircase. The inviting living room features an abundance of floor length windows, architectural beamed ceiling, wood burning fireplace with gas logs and a large number of built-ins. The formal dining room is perfect for entertaining with wood flooring, boxed-out windows and architectural beamed ceiling, creating an elegant space. A chef’s dream kitchen awaits, complete with abundant cabinetry, double boxed-out windows above the sink, lazy Susan, large island, marble countertops, pendant & can lighting, wood flooring, upgraded range plus a second wall oven & microwave and a pantry. The adjoining breakfast nook includes a bay window and wood flooring. The Guest bath boasts a glass door entry, floating vanity, chandelier, crown molding and wainscot tile. Upstairs, enjoy a bright loft space complete with built-ins, boxed-out windows, chandelier, crown molding and LVT floor. The expansive primary suite features an elegant coffered ceiling, crown molding, a ceiling fan, and triple windows. The luxurious en-suite bath features a double furniture-style vanity, floating tub, a 5-ft tile shower with bench & rain head, wainscot tile, skylight, heated towel rack, large makeup station, crown molding and tile floors. Adjacent to the bath is a huge primary closet with windows, an abundance of shelving and a washer & dryer. On the opposite side of the home is a generous sized bedroom with large windows, crown molding and a walk-in closet. The updated hall bath boasts triple windows with a round-top transom, vaulted ceiling, glass door entry with transom, oversized marble top vanity, tub with tile shower, and tile flooring. The finished basement offers a theater-style family room with a projector, can lighting, crown molding and a wet bar with marble and wood tops. A glass door leads into a spacious rec room/office with crown molding and a walk-in closet. Adjoining the rec room is your own private Spa complete with a built-in sauna, separate steam room behind a glass wall with a Jacuzzi tub & daylight window, floating vanity, upgraded toilet and linen shelving. Step outside to the show-stopping backyard! A 35x45 tiered deck with massive stone fireplace and 12x12 wood beams is perfect for gatherings, plus a paver patio with hot tub hookup and pergola with 12x12 posts create an outdoor space you’ll never want to leave. The 2-car garage is 22x22 with a door to the backyard, opener and fresh paint. The 50-year roof is brand new this year. Don’t miss this incredible opportunity to enjoy modern style living every single day in this wonderful Lakepoint home!
