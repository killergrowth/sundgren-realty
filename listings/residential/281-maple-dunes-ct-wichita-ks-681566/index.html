Property Description

Welcome to easy, low-maintenance living in Maple Dunes! This spacious ranch-style patio home offers an open, inviting floor plan with generous living and dining areas, perfect for everyday comfort and entertaining. The kitchen features a center island, abundant cabinetry and counter space, tile flooring, a built-in storage area and a casual dining space. Just off the kitchen, enjoy the climate-controlled sunroom with expansive windows, offering a comfortable space to relax year-round. The main level also features a convenient laundry room, second bedroom and a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings, a large walk-in closet, dual vanities, a separate shower and a jetted tub. The fully finished walkout basement provides even more room to spread out, featuring a large family/recreation room with a fireplace and built-in bookcases, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom and two generous storage areas. Additional highlights include a finished two-car garage, HOA-maintained lawn mowing and no special taxes! Conveniently located in West Wichita near Kellogg/US-54, with easy access to grocery stores, restaurants, shopping and everyday amenities. Vacant, move-in ready and designed for easy living!
