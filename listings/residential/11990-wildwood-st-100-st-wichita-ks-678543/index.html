Property Description

Welcome to Pawnee Crossing, one of West Wichita’s newest developments, approximately one-half mile south of Pawnee on 119th Street in the Goddard School District. Each side of these zero-entry twin homes offers three bedrooms, two bathrooms, and an attached two-car garage. The open-concept living area features an electric fireplace, luxury vinyl tile flooring, enameled white trim, and granite or quartz countertops. The kitchen includes an eating island, large walk-in pantry, and stainless steel range, dishwasher, built-in microwave. The primary suite includes a private bathroom with walk-in shower and generously sized walk-in closet. A separate laundry room is near the garage. Exterior amenities include a fully landscaped, fenced yard, sprinkler system, and shared irrigation well. The HOA fee is approximately $85 per unit monthly and covers mowing, irrigation, and common-area maintenance. General and special taxes have not been fully assessed. Special taxes are estimated at approximately $177.00 monthly based on a 15-year payout.
