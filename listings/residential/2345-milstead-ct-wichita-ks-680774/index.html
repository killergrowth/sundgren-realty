Property Description

Located on a cul-de-sac in the Goddard School District, this 5-bedroom, 2-bath home has no special taxes, and offers a finished basement, a fully fenced backyard, and the potential to add a third bathroom. The main floor features a spacious living room that flows into the dining area and kitchen, complete with a peninsula eating bar and access to the backyard. The spacious primary bedroom offers vaulted ceilings, plenty of natural light, a full bathroom, and a walk-in closet. Laminate flooring runs throughout the main level, including all three bedrooms. The finished basement adds even more living space with a recreation room featuring new carpet, two additional bedrooms, and plenty of storage. Plumbing is already roughed in for a third bathroom in the current laundry area. Additional laundry hookups are available in the adjacent storage area. Out back, enjoy the patio and fully fenced yard with a privacy screen, two large storage sheds, a garden area, and a sprinkler system. With five bedrooms, two separate living areas, and a spacious backyard, this home offers plenty of room for comfortable living, hobbies, and storage.
