Property Description

This property checks all the boxes!!! Enjoy the perfect blend of space, comfort, and convenience on this beautifully updated property located just minutes south of Salina, with only the last 1/8 mile on gravel. Situated on 6.24 acres, this home offers privacy, functionality, and room to enjoy country life. Built in 2009, this home is in better-than-new condition! The exterior has been refreshed with new shingles and gutters (2025), new interior and exterior paint (2025), and a newly planted Arborvitae tree line along the north edge of the driveway (2025) creating future privacy and curb appeal. The well motor was also replaced in 2025. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and RV owners will appreciate the insulated attached 3-car garage plus a detached 2-car garage (24 X 24) with RV hookup. There is a chain-link fenced and beautifully landscaped backyard plus a gazebo and patio perfect for watching sunrises and in the evenings, relax on the front porch and watch the sun set! Three gates to access the backyard with one being a double gate! Inside, the home features an inviting open-concept, split floor plan centered around a spacious vaulted great room designed for comfortable living and entertaining. The kitchen offers a breakfast bar, abundant storage, and includes stainless steel appliances (microwave, dishwasher and electric stove/oven) that remain with the home. This home includes 3 bedrooms, including the large primary suite with a walk-in closet and a relaxing jetted Jacuzzi tub. A unique bonus is the heated and cooled mudroom complete with a dog door leading into the fenced backyard for everyday convenience. Laundry is conveniently located on the main floor also! The finished basement expands your living and entertaining space with an extra-large granite-top wet bar, complete with beverage and wine refrigerators, plus an office, ample storage rooms, and a cozy electric fireplace. A special feature is the 120-inch hidden projection screen with projector for watching your sporting events while entertaining your friends and family. There is a bathroom for added convenience. This home is all electric, on well with peroxide system, water softener and septic. Located in the Southeast of Saline School District, this property offers the opportunity to enjoy country acreage without sacrificing proximity to town.
