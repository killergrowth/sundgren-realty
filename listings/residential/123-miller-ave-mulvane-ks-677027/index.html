Property Description

Need something affordable and comfortable? Try this 3 bedroom, 2 bath rambling ranch home in a lovely and established neighborhood, just a minute or two away from shopping and schools! So much to love here! Large privacy fenced yard with access gates, an expanded patio with beautiful pergola, stately trees, and garden beds ready for your ideas! The vaulted and oversized family/rec area with its built-in gas fireplace and 2nd full bath also gives you access to an indoor storm shelter! Main floor laundry and separate utility room are so handy in the tiled kitchen and you can use the double-doored pantry and extensive countertops to create a wonderful reason to use your formal wood-floored dining area....kitchen appliances all remain! As a bonus...most of the electrical and plumbing was updated approximately 10 years ago....we think you should like this one!
