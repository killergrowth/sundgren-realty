Property Description

Rare combination of country lifestyle and everyday convenience! Enjoy the peace and quiet of an approximately 1-acre property just minutes north of Salina offering a ranch-style home which features over 2,000 sq. ft. of finished living space with 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and a full basement with two egress windows, ready to be finished to suit your needs. This property offers space for up to 6 garage stalls and the location is an easy drive to paved highways. The main level welcomes you with fresh interior paint, all-new flooring and a spacious living area featuring vaulted ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace insert—perfect for relaxing on cool evenings. The kitchen offers an eat-at bar and pantry, while the main-floor laundry adds everyday ease. The expansive primary suite is a true retreat with a separate jetted tub and shower, private water closet and two closets. Outside, enjoy a deck, chain-link fenced yard and plenty of room to enjoy the country setting. For those needing garage space, this property delivers! A breezeway features stunning brick flooring connecting the home to a 3-car garage. In addition, the 30' x 36' detached garage offers 220 power, providing excellent space for vehicles, hobbies, workshop needs or additional storage—enough garage space for an additional 3 vehicles. Recent improvements include a new class-4 shingled roof and gutters installed in 2024, new mechanical systems in 2021, freshly painted exterior, and new porch balusters. With acreage, generous living space, multiple garages and convenient access to Salina, this property offers a rare combination of country lifestyle and everyday convenience.
