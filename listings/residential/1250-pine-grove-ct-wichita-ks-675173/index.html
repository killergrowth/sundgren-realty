Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully cared for home tucked away on a quiet cul de sac, where timeless design and everyday comfort come together effortlessly. From the moment you step into the soaring foyer, you’re welcomed by warm engineered hardwood floors, vaulted ceilings, and natural light that fills the main level. Designed for both gathering and everyday living, the formal dining room offers the perfect space for hosting, while the living room, centered around one of two fireplaces, creates a comfortable place to relax and unwind. The kitchen features a center island, generous prep space, and a sunny dining area overlooking the backyard patio, ideal for morning coffee or quiet evenings at home. Don't miss the spacious laundry room offering exceptional storage and added pantry space that makes daily living easier. The main floor primary suite offers a peaceful escape with dual vanities, separate tub and shower, and an updated soaking tub designed for relaxing at the end of the day. Upstairs, you’ll find a versatile loft, three additional bedrooms, and a full bath, creating flexible space for family, guests, or a home office. The basement expands your living space with a large family room anchored by a second fireplace, a recreation area with wet bar, one additional bedroom and full bath. There is a separate partially finished bonus space with wood floors, currently used as a workshop that could easily become an office, hobby room, workout space, or more! Outside, enjoy the fenced and irrigated backyard complete with a sprinkler system and space to relax or entertain. Neighborhood amenities include a community pool, playground, tennis and pickleball courts, basketball court, baseball diamond, scenic walking paths, and a tranquil neighborhood lake. This is home that offers space, character, and the kind of comfort that makes everyday living feel special.
