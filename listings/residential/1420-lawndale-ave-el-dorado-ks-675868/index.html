Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom (1 partial) ranch-style home offering 1,606 square feet of comfortable main-level living in a convenient El Dorado location! Priced at just $175,000, this well-maintained home features a functional floor plan with a generous living area, formal dining room, spacious galley kitchen, and a large separate laundry room. Recent updates include new carpet throughout the home within the last three years and fresh interior paint in many areas, making it move-in ready. The oversized covered back patio (14' x 38') provides the perfect space for relaxing, entertaining, or enjoying your backyard, which also includes a storage shed and an attached, lockable temperature-controlled storage room, which is ideal for additional storage, hobbies, or seasonal items. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate the impressive carport, providing excellent covered parking for your boat, motorhome, or other recreational vehicles. Located just minutes from El Dorado Lake, you'll have quick access to some of the area's best boating, fishing, camping, hiking, and outdoor recreation. Additional features include an attached one-car garage, an extra-wide driveway with room for additional parking, and an 8,678-square-foot lot. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, and recreational amenities, this home offers both everyday convenience and weekend adventure. Don't miss your opportunity to make this charming El Dorado home your own! Offered by Erika Head of Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate Wostal Realty.
