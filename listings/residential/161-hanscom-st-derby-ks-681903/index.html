Property Description

Tour the Morgan A by Don Klausmeyer Construction in Foxfire Phase 2! Google maps hasn't caught up with us yet, so you'll find Foxfire off 55th St. South between Woodlawn and Oliver, turning onto Forest Park to enter the neighborhood. This mid-level walkout plan offers 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, and an open main floor that connects the kitchen, dining, and living areas. The kitchen features quartz countertops and a large island with seating, while oversized windows brighten the dining area. The split-bedroom layout gives the primary suite its own space, complete with a vaulted ceiling, a shower, a double vanity with quartz countertops, and a generous walk-in closet. A bench with hooks in the laundry room provides a handy spot for coats, bags, and shoes. The covered patio is a great place to unwind outdoors, and the unfinished view-out basement gives you room to grow. It’s already framed for a spacious rec room, two additional bedrooms, and a third full bath, giving you a head start on future finishing. Located in the Derby school district, Foxfire is close to local parks, shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences, with easy access to McConnell Air Force Base and east Wichita. More new homes are available in Foxfire Phase 2. Stop by the model Saturday through Thursday, 1–5 p.m., to tour this home and explore other available floor plans.
