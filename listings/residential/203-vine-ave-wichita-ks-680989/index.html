Property Description

Excellent investment opportunity in the heart of Wichita’s vibrant Delano area! This brand-new duplex offers incredible flexibility for investors or owner-occupants looking to live in one side while renting out the other. Both units are vacant and move-in ready, allowing the new owner to select their own tenants, establish rental rates, and maximize income potential from day one. Located in a high-demand rental area near Delano, downtown Wichita, and major highway access, this property offers convenience and long-term investment appeal. Each unit features 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a spacious 2-car garage, an open-concept layout, granite countertops throughout, and durable LVP flooring. Unlike many new construction properties, this duplex has NO HOA and NO SPECIAL TAXES, helping keep monthly expenses lower and improving overall cash flow potential. With modern construction, minimal anticipated maintenance, and two vacant units ready for occupancy, this is a rare opportunity to add a versatile, income-producing property to your portfolio or offset your mortgage by living in one side and renting the other!
