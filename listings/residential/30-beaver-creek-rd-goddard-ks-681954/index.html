Property Description

It’s 2026, and your dream home from the 1980s has finally arrived! In a world of cookie-cutter homes, 30 W. Beaver Cir. in Goddard stands proudly apart. Originally envisioned in its architectural blueprints as the “Self-Sustainable Home of Tomorrow,” this extraordinary residence is a celebration of visionary design, timeless craftsmanship, and architectural individuality. Nestled on a full acre within the Goddard School District, this remarkable home showcases soaring ceilings, expansive walls of glass, striking natural stonework, warm wood accents, and an abundance of natural light that beautifully connects the indoors with the outdoors. Every angle offers something unexpected, capturing the character and creative spirit of an era when architecture dared to be different. With Christmas just around the corner, imagine a beautifully decorated tree beneath the towering ceilings, stockings hung along the magnificent stone fireplace, and loved ones gathered together celebrating the holidays. The expansive living area creates an unforgettable setting for entertaining, making memories, and enjoying life’s most meaningful moments. Additional features of this incredible house also included two master bedrooms and exterior access from every bedroom! More than just a home, this is a piece of architectural artistry that was truly ahead of its time. An opportunity to own something distinctive, something memorable, and something that could never be mistaken for the house next door. The home of tomorrow has arrived. Welcome home!
