Property Description

Welcome to 616 W Gatewood Ln, a home designed to fit the way you live, gather, and unwind. With five bedrooms, three baths, and approximately 2,336 sq. ft. of thoughtfully arranged space, this Andover residence offers room to spread out while still feeling warm and connected. Step inside and you’re immediately greeted by a light-filled main level where vaulted ceilings and a cozy fireplace set the tone for everyday comfort and memorable evenings. The living room flows naturally into the dining area and a well-appointed kitchen with abundant cabinetry and easy access to the covered deck—perfect for morning coffee, casual dinners, or hosting friends as the seasons change. The main-level primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with its own private bath and walk-in closet, while an additional bedroom on this floor adds flexibility for guests, a nursery, or a home office. Upstairs, a private bedroom creates even more possibilities—ideal for hobbies, work, or a quiet escape. Downstairs, the home truly expands your living options with a spacious recreation room made for movie nights, game days, or gatherings that spill comfortably into the rest of the home. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a separate utility room complete this level, giving everyone their own space to relax and recharge. Out back, your own private oasis awaits. A fenced yard surrounds an inviting in-ground pool, complemented by an expansive patio and covered deck that make outdoor living effortless. Mature trees add shade and privacy, creating the perfect backdrop for summer afternoons and evening get-togethers. With its brick-accented curb appeal, three-car attached garage, and versatile layout, 616 W Gatewood Ln isn’t just a house—it’s a place where everyday living feels a little more special.
