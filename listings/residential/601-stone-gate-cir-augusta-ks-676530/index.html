Property Description

Priced to sell! No specials! Welcome home to this beautifully updated and maintained property at 601 Stone Gate Cir. in Stone Lake Estates. This 4 bedroom, 3 full bath house with the full finished basement has been pristinely taken care of with pride of ownership showing from the manicured and curated landscaping to the welcoming and refined interior. The spacious primary bedroom and ensuite bath featuring dual sinks are move-in ready and provide ample space. The easy to care for solid surface countertops in the kitchen and main floor laundry make living easy. Updates include new flooring and paint throughout the main floor that create the perfect space for either relaxing or entertaining others. Additional upgrades that the new owner will enjoy - the screened in porch and expanded patio space in the backyard. Whether you are enjoying those crisp mornings and evenings or Fall and Spring or gathering for cookouts in the summer, these additional upgraded spaces provide the perfect spot to do them all. The fully finished basement features two full bedrooms, a full bathroom, a convenient we bar for entertaining, and a spacious entertainment room complete with fireplace and pool table, which the new owner will be able to enjoy as it stays with the property. The large, 3 car garage also has been updated to include a mini-split featuring both heating and cooling making this space another great option for entertaining, workshop, gym, and more. Conveniently located to parks, shopping, the Augusta City Lake, and 21st St. N and with neighborhood amenities such as pool, playground, and pond - this property has it all! And...with the roof being only 6 years old, the kitchen appliances staying with the property, no specials, and the number of updates done - this property is special and will make it easy for its new owner.
