Property Description

LAKE LIVING WITH POTENTIAL! Looking for a little peace and quiet at Marion County Lake? This 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath home offers a wonderful opportunity to enjoy lake living without all the yard work! Tucked away on the back row and backing up to a peaceful pasture, this home offers a quiet setting just moments from all the fun the lake has to offer! Step inside to a spacious living room featuring vaulted ceilings and a cozy gas fireplace, the perfect place to relax after a day on the water. Just off the living room, there is a bedroom and attached bath. Next you will find a dining area with a convenient eat in counter and a charming kitchen complete with a built in desk. A generously sized laundry room adds to the home's functionality. Upstairs, you will find two nicely sized bedrooms with a shared bathroom between them. With two garage spaces, there is plenty of room for vehicles, your lawnmower, lake toys and even your watercraft! The small, low maintenance yard means less time working and more time enjoying family time on the lake! This home could use a little TLC, including some fresh paint and flooring, but with a little vision and some personal touches, it could be your perfect lake getaway or year round retreat. And the location? Just minutes from the swim beach, public boat docks and loading ramp! Bring your ideas, roll up your sleeves and make this lake home your own!
