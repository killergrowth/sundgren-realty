About This Property

Country living with room for everyone! Situated on approximately 3± acres, this nicely updated property offers a unique opportunity with a spacious main home and a charming attached barndominium connected by a breezeway. Relax on the covered front porch and enjoy the peaceful setting surrounded by mature trees. The main home features a desirable split-bedroom floor plan with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The beautifully updated kitchen boasts butcher block countertops, a large island, and plenty of workspace, while the spacious living and dining room combination creates an inviting area for gathering. The primary suite includes a walk-in closet and a private en suite bathroom. The attached barndominium offers its own private living space with 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, a full kitchen, and separate washer and dryer hookups, making it ideal for guests, extended family, or rental potential. Outside, you'll find a 24x30 oversized detached 2-car garage, plenty of open space to enjoy, and a backyard filled with mature trees that provide privacy and a beautiful natural setting. This one-of-a-kind property combines modern updates, flexible living arrangements, and the tranquility of country life.
