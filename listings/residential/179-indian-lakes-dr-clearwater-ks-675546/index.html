Property Description

INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY! Start or expand your real estate portfolio with this quality-built duplex unit in the highly desirable Clearwater School District. This individual duplex unit is one of six available for purchase, giving investors the flexibility to acquire one property or multiple units to build a strong income-producing portfolio. Conveniently located within the city limits near schools, shopping, and everyday amenities, this property offers excellent rental appeal in a community with consistently strong tenant demand. Currently leased for $1,295 per month with a fixed lease through 11/30/2026, providing immediate rental income for the new owner. Offering approximately 1,050 square feet, this well-designed unit features an open-concept floor plan with 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. The spacious kitchen includes a large island, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and ample cabinet space. All kitchen appliances—including the refrigerator, range/oven, dishwasher, and microwave—remain with the property. The private primary suite offers a full bath and generous closet space. Additional features include two more bedrooms, a second full bathroom, washer/dryer hookups and low-maintenance slab construction with no basement. Exterior amenities include an attached one-car garage, additional driveway and street parking, a covered front porch with attractive cedar posts, and sprinkler system. The duplex units are connected only at the garage walls, providing added privacy and reduced noise transfer. Each duplex unit has its own legal description, allowing buyers the flexibility to own one or multiple investment properties. Whether you're purchasing your first rental or adding to an established portfolio, this turnkey property offers a quality investment in a growing community with excellent long-term rental potential. Property is offered as an individual unit or in a 5-property package of 179 & 185 N Indian Lakes Dr & 182, 184 & 186 N Tomahawk St, priced at $625,000.
