Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully maintained patio home in the desirable English Country Gardens neighborhood, offering comfortable living, thoughtful updates, and an abundance of storage. This inviting home features 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms on the main level, with a third bedroom, full bathroom, family room, and exceptionally spacious storage room in the daylight basement. The main level features beautiful transition-less hardwood flooring and an open, inviting layout. Vaulted ceilings extend through the living room, kitchen, dining area, and primary bedroom, while the remaining areas feature 8-foot ceilings. Ceiling fans are featured throughout the bedrooms and living spaces for added comfort. The well-appointed kitchen offers a walk-in pantry, appliances that remain with the home including a gas range, and convenient pull-out shelving in the cabinetry for easy access and organization. The pantry and laundry room feature automatic lighting. The main-floor laundry room also provides convenient access directly from the garage. Laundry washer and dryer will remain with the home as well. The spacious primary suite includes a 4.9' x 7' walk-in closet and an updated primary bathroom featuring a double-sink vanity, linen closet, and remodeled tiled walk-in shower. Throughout the home, Hunter Douglas Silhouette custom blinds provide both style and functionality, while the easy-to-clean windows pull out for convenient maintenance. The daylight basement provides additional flexibility with a family room, third bedroom, full bathroom, and an impressive 42' x 9' storage area—offering an exceptional amount of space for seasonal items, hobbies, household storage, and more. Outdoor living includes a covered front porch and a covered 11' x 11' backyard deck, creating comfortable spaces to enjoy the outdoors. The fenced backyard adds privacy, while the oversized 21' x 19' two-car garage features coated concrete flooring and plenty of room for vehicles and additional storage. Additional features include a Manabloc plumbing system, an ADT alarm system that is currently not in use, and a brand-new roof installed in October 2026. The home has been very well maintained and thoughtfully cared for. As part of the English Country Gardens HOA, residents enjoy low-maintenance exterior services including mowing, weeding of front flower beds, sprinkler/irrigation services, and snow removal. With its spacious layout, extensive storage, thoughtful updates, low-maintenance lifestyle, and convenient features throughout, this home offers a wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable living in a well-established patio home community.
