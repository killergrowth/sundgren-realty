Property Description

Welcome home to Carriage Crossing! From the moment you walk through the door, this charming ranch-style home in the sought-after Maize School District feels like it was made for you. Warm wood laminate flooring runs throughout, so the open living, dining and kitchen spaces feel easy and connected for everyday life and for hosting. The kitchen has a handy pantry and flows right into the dining area, giving you room to gather. You’ll love the primary suite on the main level. Head downstairs to the finished daylight basement and find a huge rec room with a wet bar, the perfect spot for game nights, movie marathons or entertaining friends. Out back, relax on the patio and enjoy the privacy of the wood-fenced yard, which stays green thanks to the sprinkler system on an irrigation well. An attached 2-car garage adds even more convenience. Homes like this in Maize don’t last long. Don’t miss your chance, schedule your showing today!
