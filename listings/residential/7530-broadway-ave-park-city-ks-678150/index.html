HomeSteps, A Unit of Freddie Mac offers this contemporary ranch style masonry and stone home. The entire main level is an open floor split bedrooms layout. The home's main floor has over 3000 SF with 4 bedrooms, 2 1/2 baths up and an attached three (3) car garage. The primary bedroom on the west end of the home includes an ensuite bath soaker tub, w/double sinks and recycled glass countertop, a tiled shower, a separator wall for toilet and a walk-in closet. The living room next door features wood floors and picture window overlooking the pool area and landscape. Moving to the middle of the home is the kitchen and island with a large breakfast bar have recycled glass countertops. Going to the east side of the hom is the informal dining area just off the kitchen with new ProVia double sliding vinyl doors to the backyard and the inground pool (not servicable). There is a main floor laundry room that also acts as a mud room from the garage just off the half bath and the 3 split bedrooms at the east wing. The fence around the backyard of the home is chain link and the pipe fence from the house going west about two hundred yards to US 81 is for running the horses. The other improvements having deferred maintenance are on a parcel that's +/- 20 AC featuring a +/- 5 AC tank or pond with aquatic and bird wildlife. The tank or pond has 2 cabins in stages of rewiring and plumbing that have decks over the tank for fishing, hooking bullfrogs or just thinking over. Deer and other wildlife are seen most every night. There is a 42' X 32' metal out building together with a 9 stall horse barn. going beyond and to the east there are fruit and pecan trees plus a smaller additional pond on the east end of the parcel. The deferred maintenance home has OG 90s fixtures and is offered "as is" with a new heritage style composition roof and gutter installed by HD Roofing of Derby as approved by the Freddie Mac.
