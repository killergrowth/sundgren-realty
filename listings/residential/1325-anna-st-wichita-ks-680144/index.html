Property Description

Welcome home to this 3-bedroom ranch in Wichita’s Orchard Park neighborhood! The main level offers an updated kitchen with eating space, a spacious living room, and an attached garage. The finished basement adds plenty of additional living space with an impressive 34x12 family room featuring a fireplace, along with a large 11x11 bonus room perfect for an office, hobby room, workout space, or whatever fits your needs. You’ll also find a dedicated laundry area and excellent storage space with built-in shelving and cabinetry. Step outside to a large fenced backyard with mature shade trees, patio and deck areas, raised garden beds, and a storage shed. A new roof in 2026 adds another great update. Located in the Wichita Public Schools district with convenient access to shopping, dining, parks, and major roadways. Come take a look and see all the space this Orchard Park ranch has to offer!
