Property Description

This spacious South Salina home offers 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and plenty of room to spread out! The finished basement features a comfortable family room, dedicated office space, and abundant storage. Recent improvements include a new roof and gutters, with new Pella Windows scheduled for installation and some new doors coming soon. Step outside to enjoy a beautifully landscaped backyard featuring a privacy hedge, paver patio, and charming rock garden with an additional patio area. The property also includes a water well with a newly replaced pump, an underground sprinkler system, and a storage shed with additional attic storage. A 2-car attached garage with built-in cabinetry provides even more storage and convenience. This home offers a great combination of indoor living space, outdoor entertaining areas, and valuable updates! Located near lots of shopping. Coronado School District.
