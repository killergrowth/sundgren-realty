Property Description

Instant equity opportunity in the Goddard school district! Welcome home to this beautiful ranch style property built in 2021! This home offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and an open living area with a thoughtfully designed living space. Situated on a spacious almost .25 acre lot, the property features a desirable three car garage and a daylight basement offering additional potential for building out future living space, recreation, or storage. The full bathroom in the basement is already complete, so you have the opportunity to finish the remainder of the living area to your liking! The flooring has been updated in the bedrooms and fresh paint greets you on every wall. The large backyard has a fence and would be easy to enclose on the front sides if you prefer. There are also sprinklers and an irrigation well to make lawn upkeep easy! Enjoy the convenience of newer construction, a functional and beautiful layout, and a great location in the Goddard community. Whether you're looking for your first home, downsizing, or simply wanting a home with room to grow, this property is one you won't want to miss! This one is clean and move in ready!
