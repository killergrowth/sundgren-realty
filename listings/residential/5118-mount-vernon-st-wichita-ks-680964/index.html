Property Description

Welcome to 5118 E Mount Vernon St! This 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom ranch home offers an exceptional opportunity for a first-time homebuyer looking to build sweat equity or an investor seeking a solid property with upside potential. The practical single-level floor plan features a functional layout with hardwood flooring, a gas stove, and a dedicated main-level laundry room. Ready for a buyer's personal touch with minor repairs and cosmetic updates to make it truly shine, this property offers great foundational value. Outside, enjoy a 1-car attached garage, additional driveway parking, and a fully fenced yard on a generous corner lot. Conveniently located on Wichita's east side with quick access to local amenities and schools. Bring your ideas and make this home your own!
