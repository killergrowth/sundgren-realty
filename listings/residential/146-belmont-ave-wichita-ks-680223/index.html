Property Description

Historic charm, thoughtful renovations, and the magic of College Hill come together beautifully at 146 S Belmont. Just blocks from College Hill Park and close to wonderful schools, beloved local shops, and some of Wichita’s best dining, this stunning 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers more than a beautiful place to live. It offers a lifestyle. Enjoy mornings on the porch, afternoons at the park, and evenings exploring nearby restaurants, theaters, and museums. With tree-lined streets, distinctive architecture, and neighbors who still wave hello, it’s easy to see why College Hill is so cherished. An oversized wraparound front porch sets the tone for this exceptional home. Inside, hardwood floors, charming built-ins, decorative fireplaces, and timeless architectural details blend beautifully with renovations designed for modern living. The inviting living room features built-ins and a decorative fireplace, while a separate family room with a second decorative fireplace offers the perfect place to curl up with a book. A gracious formal dining room captures the elegance of a classic College Hill home. The renovated kitchen features quartz countertops and ample storage, complemented by a conveniently tucked-away half bath and a practical mudroom. Upstairs, all four spacious bedrooms feature hardwood floors and share the same level, along with a laundry area that makes everyday chores easier. The extraordinary primary suite is a standout, offering an enormous bedroom, walk-in closet, and renovated private bathroom with a spa-like shower. An adjoining sunroom creates a sunshine-filled retreat ideal for an office, sitting room, nursery, or simply a peaceful place to unwind. The outdoor spaces are equally inviting, with an easy-to-maintain backyard, brick-paved patio, additional covered patio, grapevines, and garden spaces for those who love to grow. An attached, side-load garage adds everyday convenience. With a new roof, beautifully renovated kitchen and bathrooms, and the historic character that College Hill dwellers treasure, this home truly checks the boxes. Settle into a neighborhood where a stroll becomes a favorite ritual, local outings are part of everyday life, and coming home feels a little magical. 146 S Belmont is ready for its next owners to cherish.
