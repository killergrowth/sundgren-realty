Property Description

Welcome to this ONE owner, well cared for home in a quiet and friendly cul de sac neighborhood. First step inside and you will know it has been well loved for many years. Main level is 1,176 SF which boasts 3 spacious bedrooms, kitchen/dining combo, and 1.5 bath. Guest bath upstairs has a walk in jacuzzi tub-no matter the age who doesn't want to enjoy that! Large family room and laundry room, a second full bath, and a bonus room which could easily become a fourth bedroom with an addition of an egress window in the basement. Home has over 2,300 SF to accommodate growing families or anyone wanting to plant roots. Foundation repair system was installed in 2019 that has a lifetime transferable warranty to ease your mind and add worry free value. Deep two car garage, complimented with work benches and great storage space. Step outside into your little slice of paradise. A fenced in backyard with a nice sized deck surrounded by mature trees to give some much needed shade. Sprinkler system that is hooked up to well water is a great perk to keep the lawn and landscaping looking its' best. A nice shed on cement slab. This home has many updates in recent years which include: new flooring (2018), freshly painted living room, hallway, and master bedroom (2026), new electric panel (2020), new water heater (2021), new furnace (2019), new refrigerator (2017), and new Sturdi-Bilt garage door opener (2025). With a home that has been meticulously cared for in such a great neighborhood-this one will surely go quick!
