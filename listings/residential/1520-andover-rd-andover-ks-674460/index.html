Property Description

Check out this charming 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home located on a large lot in Andover. This home has been updated throughout and the sellers have kept it impeccably clean during their ownership. The main level showcases a trendy, functional kitchen, large living room and access to the pond through the back yard. The basement features a large family room, electric fireplace, and a modern full bathroom. The large back patio with a newly fenced back yard (not to mention a wonderful view of the water), would be great for entertaining family and friends. Simultaneously enjoy a coveted school district (Andover USD 385), and the peace and tranquility of small town living along with the convenience of being located minutes from Kellogg and all the amenities of city life. Property in this area does not come available very often. Do not miss the opportunity to make this home yours and love all the aspects it has to offer. Seller chooses to make no repairs and has priced home accordingly.
