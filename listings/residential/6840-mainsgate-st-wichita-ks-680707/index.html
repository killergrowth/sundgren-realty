Property Description

Spacious Living, Beautiful HOA Greenbelt & Fantastic Location! Enjoy the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and beautiful surroundings in this inviting 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath home offers a contemporary feel with vaulted ceilings and cozy breakfast space to enjoy your morning coffee. Approximately 2,000 square feet of total living space, including a basement family room! Guest bathroom offers a modern bidet-equipped toilet, adding a touch of everyday luxury. Vaulted ceilings create an open, airy atmosphere, while two separate eating areas offer flexibility for casual dining and entertaining. The kitchen features white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, tile backsplash, generous counter space, easy access slide out drawers and attractive luxury plank flooring. Convenient main-floor laundry adds everyday practicality, and both full bathrooms offer tub/shower combinations. The basement family room provides additional space for entertaining, relaxing, hobbies, or movie nights. Step outside to enjoy a private fenced yard with mature trees, a covered entry deck, and storage shed. Just beyond the backyard lies an expansive HOA greenbelt featuring open grassy areas, mature trees, and winding walking paths—a wonderful extension of the outdoor setting without the responsibility of maintaining all that extra green space! With fifteen-plus acres of common area, Comotara has room for flying kites, flying RC planes or just simply running around. Although motorized vehicles are not allowed in the Common Areas (other than maintenance vehicles), there is more than enough room for other activities. Association amenities include two playgrounds, picnic shelter with grill, soccer field, sand volleyball and softball/baseball backstop.
