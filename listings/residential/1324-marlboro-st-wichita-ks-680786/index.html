Property Description

Welcome home! This freshly updated 3-bedroom property is packed with personality, thoughtful features, and cute, modern color palettes throughout—making it the ultimate starter home or low-maintenance sanctuary for a small family. Inside, you’ll fall in love with the versatile split-bedroom floor plan designed for real life. Enjoy peace and privacy in the expansive primary retreat—a smart garage conversion tucked away from the rest of the home, complete with a custom closet featuring built-in shelving and sliding door access. Two additional bedrooms sit on the opposite side of the home, keeping everyone comfortable. Hosting is a breeze with both a dedicated formal dining room and a cozy eat-in kitchen space. Storage is abundant, featuring a massive pantry that offers the perfect opportunity to convert into a main-level laundry room and free up your kitchen footprint! Step outside to an inviting front porch that’s ready for your favorite potted plants, seasonal decor, or morning coffee. The massive fully fenced backyard is functional, complete with a storage shed and an oversized gate with trailer access. Combined with an extra-wide driveway with comfortable parking for up to 4 vehicles, this cute home delivers maximum value at an accessible price point!
