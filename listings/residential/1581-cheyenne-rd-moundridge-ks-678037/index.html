Property Description

Welcome to 1581 Cheyenne Rd. Moundridge, a beautifully updated 5-bedroom, 4-bath home situated on 5.6 acres and centrally located between Moundridge, McPherson, and Inman. This one-of-a-kind farmstead offers the perfect combination of country living, modern updates, and incredible outdoor amenities. The home has been extensively updated, including a new basement completed in 2015, updated electrical and plumbing, and a brand-new roof in 2026. Inside, you'll find beautiful original hardwood floors, brand-new carpet, recessed lighting, granite countertops, custom Becker cabinetry, and a cozy wood-burning stove. Outside is where this property truly sets itself apart. A 40x60 round-top building features concrete floors and electricity, providing excellent space for storage, hobbies, equipment, or a workshop. The property also includes an immaculately maintained and incredibly unique granary 45x43, two silos with exciting potential for entertaining spaces, a garden shed, and a concrete pad ready for a future garage. Enjoy your own private pond with a dedicated well, making it easy to maintain and fill, plus a second well serving the property. Fenced property lines add to the functionality of this acreage. With 5.6 acres, abundant improvements, unique outbuildings, and a beautifully renovated home, this is far more than just a house, it's a property you need to experience in person.
