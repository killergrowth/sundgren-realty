Property Description

WOW! This one truly has it all! Tucked away on a cul-de-sac and situated on a large lot, this impressive 5-bedroom, 3-bath home offers exceptional space, amenities, and outdoor living. The main level features 3 bedrooms, a spacious living room with a gas fireplace, and a gorgeous updated kitchen designed for entertaining. Granite countertops, a large island with eating space, walk-in pantry, and TWO dishwashers make this kitchen both beautiful and incredibly functional. A formal dining room/flex space and main-level laundry add even more convenience. The fully finished walk-out basement expands the living space with a large family/rec room featuring another fireplace, 2 additional bedrooms, a full bath, and an enormous storage area. Step outside and the fun really begins! Enjoy the saltwater, heated in-ground pool, covered patio, and a fantastic pool house complete with its own kitchen, full bathroom, and golf cart garage—perfect for entertaining, pool days, or hosting guests. Additional features include a full-yard sprinkler system with automatic watering for the raised garden beds, a whole-home gas-powered generator, and a new water heater. The oversized 3-car garage is fully insulated and equipped with a mini-split for year-round comfort. With generous indoor and outdoor living spaces, incredible entertaining potential, and thoughtful upgrades throughout, this is far more than just a home—it’s a lifestyle! Come see everything this exceptional property has to offer!
