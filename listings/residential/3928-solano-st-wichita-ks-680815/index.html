Property Description

Some homes are built to impress. This one was built to stay! Welcome to an extraordinary opportunity in The Courtyards at Estancia, where luxury, thoughtful design and effortless living come together beautifully! Built by Perfection Builders in 2018, this exceptional, single-owner patio home was designed with uncompromising attention to detail. Every upgrade, every finish and every extra was thoughtfully considered, creating a truly special home that stands apart from the ordinary. Offering 2,053 square feet of beautifully appointed living space, this meticulously maintained residence features 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and versatile additional spaces designed for both comfort and entertaining. The stunning kitchen serves as the heart of the home, opening into generous living and dining areas perfect for gathering with family and friends. ALL APPLIANCES ARE INCLUDED, making this exceptional home even more move-in ready! A dedicated office and flexible bonus room offer endless possibilities for working from home, hosting guests, enjoying hobbies or creating a special space for visiting grandkids. Situated on one of the larger lots within the community, the outdoor spaces are equally impressive. Enjoy peaceful mornings on the front porch, afternoons surrounded by beautiful landscaping or evenings entertaining on the inviting covered patio. Every space was designed to be enjoyed! But the lifestyle is where The Courtyards at Estancia truly shines! HOA dues cover landscaping and lawn care, sprinkler maintenance with central irrigation wells, trash service, snow removal and exterior building maintenance (excluding roofs, windows and doors). Imagine spending less time maintaining your property and more time enjoying everything this remarkable community has to offer! Courtyards homeowners and their guests also enjoy exclusive access to an extraordinary clubhouse and recreation area featuring an outdoor heated saltwater swimming pool, pickleball courts, a fitness center with resistance and cardio equipment, a spacious great room with kitchen, a card and game room, and convenient restroom facilities. A neighborhood playground adds even more to the community experience. It’s a private, resort-style lifestyle just beyond your front door! From its pristine condition and extensive upgrades to its thoughtful design and exceptional amenities, this is so much more than a patio home. It’s an opportunity to enjoy an extraordinary lifestyle without compromising on quality, comfort or style. Beautifully designed. Exceptionally maintained. Effortlessly enjoyed. Because there really is no place like home!
