Property Description

Enjoy the space, privacy, and possibilities of country living on nearly 5 acres, conveniently located just a few miles off Hwy 400. Situated back from the road, this property offers a peaceful setting with room for livestock, outdoor activities, and making your homestead dreams a reality. Built in 1978, the home features 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, an attached 1-car garage, and a spacious 2-car shop. Step inside to a bright, welcoming family room with plenty of natural light. The kitchen and dining area are located at the back of the home, offering an open, comfortable space for everyday living, family gatherings, and entertaining. A back porch just off the kitchen provides a great spot to enjoy the outdoors. The dishwasher, refrigerator, and oven all stay with the home for added convenience. The main-floor primary bedroom offers generous space and a private bathroom. You'll also appreciate the dedicated utility room. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms and another full bathroom provide space for family, guests, or a home office. The unfinished basement offers additional storage and plenty of potential for future improvements to suit your needs. Outside, the nearly 5-acre tract is set up for livestock making it an appealing option for those seeking a small homestead, hobby farm, or peaceful country retreat. The attached garage and detached 2-car shop provide convenient parking, workspace, and storage. If you've been looking for a property with acreage, a functional home, outbuildings, and room to make it your own, this Severy property is full of possibilities. Come explore the opportunity to enjoy country living at 1684 8th St.!
