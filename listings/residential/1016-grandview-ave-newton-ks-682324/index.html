Property Description

Mid-Century Style on Grandview! Located on a huge corner lot along one of Newton’s most desirable streets, this mid-century modern ranch blends classic character, great natural light, and plenty of room to live and entertain. The living room includes a striking wood-burning fireplace featuring the same stone carried from the home's exterior, and floor-to-ceiling windows framing spanning views of the backyard and oversized deck. New carpet on the main floor adds a fresh touch, while the beautiful windows throughout bring in an abundance of natural light. The kitchen features Becker cabinetry, hardwood floors, a charming corner window above the sink, and includes the dishwasher and stove. There’s room for both formal dining and a casual breakfast nook. The spacious laundry and utility area offers pantry space and additional cabinetry, with the washer and dryer included. The main floor offers three bedrooms, including a primary suite with double closets and a private full bath. A second full bath with tub/shower combination serves the remaining bedrooms. Downstairs, the possibilities continue with a large family room featuring built-in bookshelves, an additional bedroom with egress window, and a full bath. Outside, enjoy the nicely landscaped corner lot, oversized deck, and fully fenced private backyard with wood privacy fencing. A side-load garage adds convenience and curb appeal. Classic mid-century character, a prime Grandview location, and a backyard made for enjoying.
