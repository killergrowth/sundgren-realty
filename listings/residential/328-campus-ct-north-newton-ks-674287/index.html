About This Property

Prepare to be impressed by this pristine, one-owner, custom-built masterpiece located at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in this desirable neighborhood. Combining high-grade craftsmanship with an expansive, thoughtful layout, this home is designed for both elegant everyday living and effortless entertaining. Step inside to a bright, open-concept main floor featuring gorgeous hardwood floors that flow seamlessly through the living and dining areas. The heart of the home is the inviting living room, anchored by a stately gas fireplace and flanked by large windows that frame views of the private backyard. The chef-inspired kitchen boasts durable tile flooring, a dedicated breakfast nook, and easy access to a mid-level walk-out. Step outside to your private oasis—a spacious back deck that connects to a stunning, matching attached gazebo, perfect for morning coffee or evening gatherings. The main level hosts a luxurious primary suite alongside a second generously sized bedroom and the second full bath. The primary bath is a spa-like retreat, complete with dual vanities and a walk-in shower and a large walk-in closet. Downstairs feels like a second home. Surrounded by large view-out windows, the massive family room features a second cozy fireplace and a kitchenette with an eating area—ideal for hosting game nights or long-term guests. The lower level also includes two additional oversized bedrooms (one with large view-out windows and the other with an extra-large egress window to allow in a lot of natural light), a third full bathroom, and a massive storage room. Complete with an oversized three-car garage and located just steps from scenic walking paths, this rare, meticulously maintained property is ready for its next chapter.
