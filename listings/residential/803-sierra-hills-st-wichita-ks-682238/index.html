Property Description

Welcome to this beautiful home on a large, wooded lot and lake view from outside deck, located in Country Hollow! This three-car garage home offers great curb appeal! Step inside to the living room which a gas fireplace and vaulted ceilings. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances with a Quartz counters top and very model back splash, and a pantry along with tons of cabinet space and storage. The dining area is right off the kitchen and provides the perfect space for entertaining and game night! The roomy master bedroom features a coffered ceiling and an en suite bathroom which provides a soaker tub, separate shower, two sinks, and a large walk-in closet. Two bedrooms, a guest bathroom, and a laundry room complete the main level. The basement offers a large family room and one additional bedroom and one bathroom, with a wet bar. Outside, you’ll enjoy beautiful views of the neighborhood lake from the deck or patio! The neighborhood features a greenbelt, lake, playground, and swimming pool. Don’t hesitate to call this place your home - it won’t last long!
