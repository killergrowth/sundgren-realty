Property Description

Hello Walton! Built in 1900, this traditional five-bedroom home situated on a corner lot on the edge of town offers a flexible layout and plenty of room to make it your own. A 2005 addition expanded the main floor with a spacious kitchen with pantry cabinets, a primary bedroom full bathroom, and a laundry area. A living room, formal dining room, second bedroom, and additional full bath round out the main level. Upstairs, three more bedrooms offer space for your living needs, guests, hobbies, or a home office. The partially finished basement includes an extra living space and a bathroom that needs drywall and a vanity. The attached two-car garage features a workbench and room for projects or tinkering. Outside, enjoy a quiet moment on the brick patio beneath mature cedar trees or roam the spacious backyard. If you’ve been looking for more space on the edge of town, countryside views, and a place you can put your own touch on, this may be the one! There may even be enough room out back for that future shop you’ve been wanting!
