WATER VIEWS WITH A POOL ... MASSIVE 6 CAR GARAGE SPACE ... So much for your money than building! This remarkable executive residence offers an exceptional lifestyle with premium features throughout. A formal dining room with striking vaulted, beamed ceilings complements a bright breakfast nook with views of the pond and private putting green.
