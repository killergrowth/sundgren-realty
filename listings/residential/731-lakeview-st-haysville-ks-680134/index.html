Property Description

Welcome to 731 S Lakeview in Haysville! This exceptionally well-maintained home has been immaculately cared for inside and out, with thoughtful updates and features around every corner. Located on a desirable lake lot with beautiful views and direct access to the community pond, this property offers a peaceful setting while still providing plenty of space for everyday living and entertaining. The community pond is stocked with bass, catfish, and more, creating a beautiful backdrop to enjoy right from your own backyard. The exterior is just as impressive as the interior, featuring lush green grass, an irrigation system supplied by a well, an upgraded wrought-iron fence, an oversized 3-car garage, and no neighboring home on one side of the property. The backyard is truly an entertainer’s dream, complete with an above-ground pool that is included with the sale of the home, plus plenty of space to enjoy the outdoors. The hot tub is negotiable with an acceptable offer. Inside, you’ll find 5 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, with updates throughout that provide a modern, stylish feel while maintaining a warm and inviting atmosphere. Luxury vinyl flooring runs throughout the main-level living areas, while all three main-level bedrooms feature new carpet. The finished lower level provides additional living space, two bedrooms, a full bathroom, and additional mechanical and storage space. The lower-level family room features built-in speakers and a wet bar, with the mini fridge remaining with the home. The kitchen features brand-new appliances, and the home also includes three new upgraded toilets. The oversized 3-car garage is another standout feature, offering abundant room for vehicles, storage, hobbies, and entertaining. Built-in speakers in the garage will remain with the home as well. With its combination of modern updates, exceptional maintenance, incredible outdoor amenities, peaceful pond setting, and spacious 3-car garage, this is a property that truly needs to be seen to appreciate everything it has to offer. With an acceptable offer, the sellers are willing to include the hot tub, upstairs couch, kitchen table, patio umbrella, and trampoline with the sale of the home. Please note: The garage projector does not remain with the home. TVs and TV mounts do not remain.
