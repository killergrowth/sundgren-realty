Property Description

1116 N Saint Paul proves that a great home doesn't have to be a big one! Located in Wichita's established Indian Hills neighborhood, this fully updated 3 bedroom, 1 bathroom home offers 714 square feet of comfortable living space on a surprisingly spacious 0.22 acre lot with a detached 2 car garage. Built in 1928, this home brings together the character of an older neighborhood with the fresh finishes of a modern renovation. Inside, durable luxury vinyl flooring runs throughout the living spaces, while the beautifully remodeled kitchen is the real standout with new cabinetry, quartz countertops, and a tile backsplash that makes this compact home feel anything but ordinary. The living room offers a comfortable place to unwind, while the kitchen provides more room to cook and gather than you might expect. All three bedrooms are tucked along one side of the home, and while each is modest in size, they offer flexibility for a first-time homeowner, someone looking to simplify, or anyone needing a dedicated home office, nursery, or hobby room. The full bathroom is conveniently located toward the back of the home. Outside is where this property really surprises you. The large private lot provides plenty of room for pets, gardening, outdoor gatherings, or simply enjoying some breathing room. Even better, the detached 2 car garage features brand new doors and openers, offering valuable space for vehicles, projects, or additional storage, with a separate backyard shed for even more flexibility. Located in Indian Hills with convenient access to west Wichita shopping, restaurants, parks, and everyday essentials, this home offers an approachable way to enjoy an established neighborhood without taking on more house than you need. If you've been looking for a fresh start, a manageable home, and a garage and yard that give you room to do more, 1116 N Saint Paul might be exactly the right fit.
