Property Description

4+ Bedroom, 2.5 bath, ranch home located in the heart of Anthony, KS. This well-maintained home with over 3,500 square feet of comfortable living space is sure to meet your needs. The main level showcases stunning custom mahogany trim throughout, creating warmth and elegance. A spacious living room with a cozy gas fireplace flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, perfect for gatherings and entertaining. There are original hardwood floors under the green carpet! The kitchen is truly a cook’s dream, featuring stainless steel appliances, a double oven/range, granite countertops, abundant cabinetry, and a charming breakfast nook for casual dining. A secondary entrance on the north side of the home leads into a small foyer with access to the kitchen or bedroom hallway. The master bedroom boasts double closets and sliding doors to the enclosed deck. Two other spacious bedrooms are on the main floor, one with a half bath and another sliding door leading out to the deck. A full bath with double sinks and tub/shower combo, splits two of the main-floor bedrooms. Upstairs are two more spacious rooms, one bedroom and one bonus room that would make a great play or reading room. The fully finished basement expands your living space even further, featuring an impressive 55x15 family room, a full bath, a storage room with an egress window providing natural daylight (perfect for a hobby or craft room), and a second kitchen ideal for entertaining, holidays, or multi-generational living. The home is connected to the garage and backyard by an enclosed, composite deck that spans the entire west side of the home and offers an exterior entrance and garage access. This inviting space is perfect for entertaining guests or enjoying your morning coffee year-round. The attached 2-car garage offers more storage space with a small workshop area. This spacious, well-cared-for home is a rare find—don’t miss your opportunity to make it yours!
