Property Description

Fresh, updated, and move-in ready! This charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath brick home in Wichita offers a refreshed look with thoughtful updates throughout. Enjoy the warmth of newly exposed wood floors in the living room and bedrooms, complemented by new LVP flooring in the kitchen and dining area and updated flooring in the laundry room. The home features fresh interior and exterior paint, updated lighting, and popcorn ceiling removal for a clean, modern feel. Major improvements include a new roof and gutters, along with an upgraded electrical panel. Whether you're searching for an affordable place to call home or a great investment opportunity, this refreshed property is full of value and ready for its next chapter!
