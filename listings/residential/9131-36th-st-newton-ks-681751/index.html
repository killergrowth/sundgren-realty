Property Description

Discover country living on 10 beautiful acres northeast of Newton! This remodeled farmhouse offers 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and an oversized attached 3-car garage. Surrounded by mature trees, the property features a picturesque pond with a dock, providing a peaceful setting to relax, fish, or enjoy the outdoors. Equestrian amenities include three 13' x 33' horse runs, pipe fencing, gates, and a loafing shed. Two additional outbuildings and an insulated, climate-controlled shop offer plenty of space for equipment, hobbies, and storage. The privacy-fenced backyard with irrigation provides a comfortable space for outdoor entertaining. Whether you're looking for a hobby farm, room for horses, or simply a little more space and privacy, this property has so much to offer. Schedule your private showing today!
