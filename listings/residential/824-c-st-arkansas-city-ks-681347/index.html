Property Description

3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, 1,617 sq. ft., and TWO LOTS included in the sale! Welcome to 824 N C Street, a charming home situated along one of Arkansas City's beautiful brick-paved streets, offering spacious living, unique architectural details, and one of the largest combined yard spaces on the entire block! One of the property's biggest highlights is the adjoining corner lot at 826 N C Street, included with the sale! Previously occupied by a separate residence that was removed several years ago, this additional lot now provides an impressive amount of open yard space. Whether you're looking to extend your privacy fencing, create a garden, expand your outdoor entertaining area, or simply enjoy the extra room, this property offers plenty of possibilities! Inside, you'll find an inviting floor plan featuring attractive tile flooring throughout the main-level foyer, spacious living and dining areas, and primary bedroom. Charming arched openings accented by decorative columns add character and elegance, while the flowing layout creates a welcoming atmosphere for everyday living and entertaining. The spacious kitchen features an abundance of natural wood cabinetry, generous countertop space, an attractive mosaic tile backsplash, and stainless steel appliances. **Best of all, all kitchen appliances stay with the home!** A separate laundry room adds convenience and keeps household chores tucked away from the main living areas. The main level also features a primary bedroom and full bathroom, providing the convenience of having essential living spaces downstairs. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms with freshly cleaned carpeting and a second full bathroom, offering a comfortable and practical layout. Step outside and enjoy a beautiful private patio enclosed by a charming combination of brick and wood privacy fencing! This inviting outdoor space is perfect for relaxing evenings, entertaining guests, or enjoying a weekend cookout. Just steps away, the covered carport provides convenient backyard parking with easy access through the sliding glass door. Bringing in groceries, unloading supplies, or making multiple trips between the kitchen and patio is a breeze, making those Sunday cookouts an easy choice! Beyond the patio, the adjoining corner lot offers even more outdoor space to enjoy, with plenty of potential to customize the yard to fit your needs. A rare opportunity to enjoy the benefits of two lots within an established neighborhood! Conveniently located near local parks, schools, churches, and other Arkansas City amenities, this property combines neighborhood charm, everyday convenience, and exceptional outdoor space. With three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a private patio, covered parking, and TWO LOTS included in the sale, 824 N C Street is a property worth seeing in person!
