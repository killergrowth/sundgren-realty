Property Description

This inviting 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers comfortable living in a desirable Hesston neighborhood. Built in 2001, the home sits on a nicely sized lot and provides a functional layout with plenty of space for everyday living, working, relaxing and entertaining. The finished basement adds valuable flexibility with an office and an additional room currently used as a workout room. Whether you need dedicated workspace, a home gym, hobby space or room for guests, these versatile areas can easily adapt to your needs. Step outside to the back patio and enjoy one of the home's special features - a beautiful view of the Kansas sunset. It's the perfect spot to unwind at the end of the day, entertain friends or simply enjoy the peaceful setting. Come take a look and imagine yourself enjoying all this home has to offer!
