Property Description

Welcome home to 10903 W Dora Ct! This inviting 4-bedroom, 3-bath home in the Goddard School District offers a wonderful combination of comfort, thoughtful updates and functional living space. The kitchen features concrete countertops throughout, stainless steel appliances that remain with the home and updated lighting that adds character and style. The primary bathroom features a beautifully tiled walk-in shower, creating a comfortable retreat. Downstairs, the finished basement offers a spacious family room, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom and a separate laundry/storage room. Even better, the washer and dryer remain with the home! Step outside to enjoy the wood-fenced backyard with established trees, a sprinkler system and a deck that has recently received a protective coating, making it ready for outdoor enjoyment. A generously sized, two-sided storage shed with electricity provides excellent space for tools, hobbies and additional storage. With attractive exterior paint, previously updated HVAC and water heater, and a freshly professionally cleaned interior, this home is ready to welcome its next owners. Come see all that this charming property has to offer!
