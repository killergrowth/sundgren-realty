Property Description

What if your next home came with a place to host birthday parties, holiday gatherings, and family get-togethers without having to fit everyone into your living room? This El Dorado condo offers just that possibility, with reservable community rooms featuring a kitchen, bathroom, tables, and seating, plus access to an in-ground saltwater pool and community grill! Inside, this 2-bedroom, 2 full bathroom, and 2 ½-bath condo has seen extensive updates, including replacement windows, a new energy-efficient front entry door and storm door, a sliding glass patio door, fresh professional paint in several areas, new carpet and padding in both upstairs bedrooms and the hallway, and luxury vinyl plank flooring in the primary bathroom vanity area. Additional improvements include an updated primary bedroom vanity, sink, faucet, lighting, ceiling fan, and trim, along with new interior doors and hardware, updated plumbing components, new GFCI outlets, and a new hallway bathroom exhaust fan. Much of the lighting has also been upgraded to LED. Even better, all appliances stay, including the washer and dryer! The HOA covers water and trash, and residents can reserve the community rooms, saltwater pool for special occasions, and breezeway between buildings A and B. With so many updates and community amenities, this condo offers more than just a place to live. Come see if it feels like home!
