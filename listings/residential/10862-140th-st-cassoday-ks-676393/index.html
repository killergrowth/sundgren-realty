Property Description

Welcome to an extraordinary Flint Hills ranch where luxury country living, modern farmhouse design, and business opportunity come together on approximately 40+/- picturesque acres. This beautifully crafted 5-bedroom, stick-built barndominium offers the character buyers love, while delivering the quality construction and upscale finishes expected in a custom home. Inside, the thoughtfully designed floor plan features soaring ceilings, abundant natural light, sliding barn doors, and expansive open-concept living spaces that create a warm, inviting atmosphere. The chef's kitchen is the heart of the home, showcasing custom cabinetry, generous workspace, and seamless flow into the dining and living areas, perfect for entertaining or everyday life. A spacious drop zone, oversized laundry room with dog wash, and ample storage provide exceptional functionality for country living. A separate bedroom wing creates privacy and comfort for family and guests while preserving a peaceful primary suite retreat. Large windows throughout the home capture breathtaking panoramic views of the rolling Flint Hills, bringing the beauty of the surrounding prairie indoors. Step outside to enjoy a covered patio overlooking the Kansas countryside, where spectacular sunrises, sunsets, and abundant wildlife become part of daily life. For livestock producers and entrepreneurs, this property is uniquely equipped with substantial cattle feeding infrastructure, including well-maintained pens and facilities designed for efficient operation (including a new 60'x100' horse barn).The existing turnkey cattle business is available for purchase separately, offering an exceptional opportunity for buyers seeking an income-producing operation. Alternatively, with an acceptable offer, the seller is willing to remove the bunks and pens, broadcast native grass seed, and restore the acreage to beautiful Flint Hills pasture, making the property equally attractive as a horse property, homesteading paradise, recreational ranch, or private country estate. Additional highlights include private setting with no through traffic thanks to the closure of 140th Road at the railroad tracks. The property offers quiet surroundings now that the railroad has eliminated train whistles. Convenient access to Highway 177 and the Kansas Turnpike (I-35) makes for easy hauling of livestock, horses, equipment, or trailers. Whether you're searching for a Kansas ranch, horse property, homestead, cattle operation, country estate, or simply a peaceful home on acreage in the heart of the Flint Hills, this remarkable property offers endless possibilities. Experience the perfect balance of luxury, functionality, privacy, and productivity. Schedule your private showing today and discover everything this exceptional Flint Hills property has to offer.
