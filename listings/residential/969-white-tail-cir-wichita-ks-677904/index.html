Property Description

Public Remarks A rare opportunity to own an exceptional home on a corner lot in the highly sought-after neighborhood and Andover school district! With expansive living areas, thoughtful details, and exceptional indoor-outdoor spaces, this home offers tremendous value and room to live, entertain, and grow. A charming covered front porch welcomes you into the home, where a tiled entryway and 10-foot ceilings throughout the main level create an open, spacious feel. The elegant formal dining room features beautiful wood flooring, providing the perfect setting for family gatherings and entertaining. At the heart of the home is the inviting main-floor hearth room, complete with a gas fireplace, built-in entertainment center, and doors leading directly to the covered deck. The spacious, fully applianced kitchen offers granite countertops, tile flooring, breakfast bar, dedicated eating area, and a convenient pantry. The main-floor laundry room is thoughtfully designed with cabinet storage and a hanging bar and a single sink for added convenience. The private split-bedroom floor plan offers an impressive primary suite featuring coffered ceilings, an oversized walk-in closet, and a luxurious primary bathroom with a Whirlpool tub, walkin shower, and quartz double vanities. The fully finished view-out/walk-out (below grade) provides an incredible amount of additional living space. The oversized family room features another gas-log fireplace and wet bar, complete with a dishwasher and space for a full-size refrigerator-ideal for entertaining, game nights, or extended living arrangements. The lower level also includes two view-out bedrooms, two full bathrooms, an office or hobby room, and generous storage space. Step outside to enjoy the beautifully appointed backyard featuring a wrought-iron fence, covered deck, covered patio, sprinkler system. The property also includes a desirable oversized 3-car garage. Residents can enjoy nearby community amenities including an area swimming pool, jogging path, lake/pond, and playground. With 5 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, smart ring doorbell, smart thermostat and smart sprinkler system controller and multiple living space, multiple fireplaces, extensive finished lower-level living, exceptional outdoor spaces, and an outstanding Andover school district location, and minutes away from shopping strips and golf course and so many trails around, this is far more than a house it is a place designed for living, entertaining, and creating lasting memories.
