Property Description

Move-in ready and thoughtfully updated, this well-maintained 4 BR, 3 BA ranch offers the ease of turnkey living with inviting spaces for everyday enjoyment and entertaining. From the well-manicured lawn and attractive curb appeal to the fresh interior finishes, you'll appreciate the care and improvements throughout. Newer carpet, luxury vinyl flooring, and fresh interior paint give the home a crisp, refreshed feel. The open-concept living area features vaulted ceilings, a gas fireplace, and decorative shelving, flowing seamlessly into the dining area and kitchen with a pantry and reverse osmosis drinking water system. A convenient main-floor laundry room, three main-floor bedrooms, and a full hall bath complete the main living level. The spacious primary suite offers large windows, a decorative trayed ceiling, and a remodeled ensuite featuring dual sinks, new vanity, new flooring, fresh paint, an updated shower, soaker tub, and a walk-in closet. Entertaining is easy in the finished view-out basement with additional living space including a sprawling family room, wet bar, room for a sectional and game table, plus a fourth bedroom and full bath. The finished three-car garage is an exceptional bonus, featuring insulated walls and ceiling, a mini-split heating and cooling, epoxy floors, and Bluetooth-enabled garage door openers. Whether for hobbies, projects, or simply enjoying a comfortable workspace year-round, this garage offers versatility rarely found. Additional peace of mind comes with a newer air conditioning system and hot water heater (2024), along with a whole-house water softener. Enjoy a fenced yard with a mature tree-lined backdrop, freshly stained covered deck, brick patio, and storage shed. It's an inviting setting for summer gatherings, barbecues, or evenings on the patio. The neighborhood adds even more appeal with a community pool, playground, ponds, and sidewalks, while quick access to Kellogg, shopping, and dining makes everyday living convenient. An appealing combination of move-in-ready condition, meaningful updates, and outstanding functionality!
