Located in a prime east-side neighborhood off Central between Rock and Woodlawn, this well-maintained brick ranch offers an unbeatable location surrounded by everyday conveniences, including Dillons, Planet Fitness, restaurants, shopping, and more. The home features an attached two-car garage, a covered front entry, and a backyard with a covered patio, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Inside, you're welcomed by a foyer that leads to the formal living room, highlighted by a large picture window that fills the space with natural light. The living room flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, making it ideal for gatherings. The galley-style kitchen offers ample cabinet space, a cozy breakfast nook with a bay window, and includes all major appliances: refrigerator, oven, and dishwasher. Just off the kitchen, you'll find a convenient laundry area with shelving and a half bath. One of the home's standout features is the inviting main-floor family room, complete with wood-beamed ceilings, a wood-burning fireplace, and direct access to the backyard. The spacious primary suite includes an en-suite bath with double sinks and a tub/shower combination. Two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath complete the main level, providing plenty of space for family or guests. A partial basement provides abundant storage space and room for hobbies, a workshop, or future possibilities.
