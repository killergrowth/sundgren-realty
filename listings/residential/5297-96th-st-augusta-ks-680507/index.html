**ONE-OF-A-KIND HOME • OVER 7 ACRES • HEATED SHOP!** Looking for something with character, charm, and room to roam? This 3-bedroom home (with a loft offering potential for a 4th bedroom) is anything but ordinary! Soaring two-story ceilings, exposed beams, and beautiful custom trim work make this home truly unique. The spacious kitchen is a cook's dream with double ovens, a six-burner gas cooktop with griddle option, two sinks, a movable island, and storage galore! The primary suite features floor-to-ceiling built-ins, generous closet space, and a private ensuite with double vanities and a whirlpool tub. The split-bedroom floor plan offers two additional bedrooms with a Jack-and-Jill bath and separate entrance. Previously used as a mother-in-law suite, this space offers wonderful flexibility for guests, multigenerational living, or potential income opportunities, subject to applicable requirements. And don't forget the oversized, detached three-car garage with a heated, insulated workshop and its own full bathroom! A newer roof, two heating and cooling systems, convenient highway access, and an asphalt-milled drive to help keep the dust down are added bonuses. **Country living, incredible character, and endless possibilities—all on over 7 acres!** Schedule your showing today!
