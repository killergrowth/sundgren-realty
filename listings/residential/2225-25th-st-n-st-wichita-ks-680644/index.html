Property Description

Charm, character, and cozy living come together in this inviting Wichita home! Featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a 1-car garage, this home offers a new roof (2026), warm details and flexible spaces to make your own. The living room welcomes you with a charming fireplace and large windows that fill the space with natural light. Wood flooring throughout most of the main floor adds warmth and character, while the kitchen brings its own charm with French doors leading to a bonus living space perfect for a second sitting area, plant sanctuary, hobby room, or whatever fits your lifestyle. The primary suite includes a private en-suite bathroom, adding comfort and convenience. Step outside to enjoy the back deck and fully fenced yard, with a storage shed ready for lawn equipment, gardening supplies, or holiday decorations. With inviting indoor spaces and room to enjoy the outdoors, this home is full of possibilities and ready for its next chapter!
