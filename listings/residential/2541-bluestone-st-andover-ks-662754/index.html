About This Property

This Stonegate floorplan built by Buckert Contracting is beautiful! Don't miss the opportunity to own one of the last new homes in the desired Cornerstone neighborhood. 5 beds, 3 baths, 2975 sq ft with a 3-car garage and a fenced backyard. 10ft ceilings on main floor with wood beams in living room. Stone and wood accent walls around fireplace and oven in kitchen. Dining room has tongue & groove wood ceiling. Stunning front entry with oversized metal pivot door and wood accent wall. Quartz countertops throughout home. Large kitchen with side coffee bar and walk-in pantry. Sliding glass door from dining room opens onto a covered deck. Nice drop zone off garage entry with plenty of storage. Laundry room is accessible from both mudroom and master closet for ease of putting laundry away. Also has a folding table with additional cabinets above and below. The master bath has double sinks in vanity with window above for natural light. Full tiled shower with bench at one end and rain shower. Master bedroom wood accent wall with trey ceiling above. Full finished basement with a walk-out pit to the backyard. Comes with 1 year builder warranty. Andover school district! 3 community swimming pools with a park complete with basketball and pickleball courts.
