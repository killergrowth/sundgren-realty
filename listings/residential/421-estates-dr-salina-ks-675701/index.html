Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful East Salina ranch featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and a spacious 3-car garage. With new flooring throughout, this home offers a fresh, modern feel from the moment you step inside. The open-concept kitchen and dining area flow seamlessly to the covered composite patio, complete with built-in surround sound—perfect for entertaining or relaxing outdoors. The desirable split-bedroom floor plan provides privacy, with a spacious primary suite featuring a built-in window seat, walk-in closet, jetted tub, and separate shower. A second main-floor bedroom and full hallway bathroom offer flexibility for guests or a home office. The finished basement includes a large family room, two additional bedrooms, a bonus room ideal for an office, gym, or hobby space, plus abundant storage. Outside, you'll appreciate the fenced backyard with a sprinkler system. For added peace of mind, the home will also come with a brand-new roof, garage doors, and gutters.
