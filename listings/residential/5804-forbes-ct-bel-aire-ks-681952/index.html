Property Description

Beautifully appointed and designed for easy living, this H&H Homebuilders patio home sits on a generous cul-de-sac homesite in Bel Aire’s upscale Irongate neighborhood. Offering 1,800 square feet, three bedrooms and two full baths, it blends thoughtful design with inviting spaces for everyday living and entertaining. A welcoming entry with 10-foot ceilings opens to a sun-filled great room, where floor-to-ceiling windows bring the outdoors in. The open layout flows into a gourmet kitchen with granite countertops and a baker’s pantry that doubles as a storm shelter. Luxury tile plank flooring adds warmth and style, while the primary retreat features a beautifully finished shower with floor-to-ceiling designer tile. A double outdoor patio offers room to relax and gather, while a three-car garage and tall crawl space beneath the home provide generous storage. Enjoy maintenance-provided living with dues covering lawn care, snow removal, trash service, and access to the community pool and clubhouse. Built by H&H Homebuilders, with craftsmanship you can see, integrity behind every wall, and the personal communication that makes coming home feel right from the start.
