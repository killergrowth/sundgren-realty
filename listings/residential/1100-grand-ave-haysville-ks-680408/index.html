This unique 5.77-acre property featuring three substantial buildings consisting of approximately 23,400 square feet, 73 parking spaces, and multiple access points from Grand Ave. Originally developed as a church campus, the property offers an exceptional combination of existing facilities, open land, and light commercial zoning that creates a wide range of possibilities for the next owner. The campus includes three separate buildings, each offering different features and potential uses: Building One – Approximately 6,442 SF Built in 1965 and most recently utilized as a daycare facility, this building features several large rooms, a kitchen area with commercial hood, and flexible interior space that could accommodate a variety of educational, nonprofit, office, community, or commercial uses. Building Two – Approximately 8,800 SF Built in 2007, this building currently serves as a gymnasium and auditorium and includes a stage, multiple large rooms, offices, storage areas, restrooms, and an approximately 1,400-SF loft overlooking the gym and stage. The existing layout featuring 20ft sidewall height and 24ft peak height could be particularly attractive to churches, schools, community organizations, event operators, recreational users, and other users requiring large open-span or assembly space. Building Three – Approximately 8,000 SF Previously utilized as a church, this building includes four offices, two entrances, a large kitchen, and an established worship/assembly configuration capable of accommodating approximately 300 people. Existing pews will transfer with the property. The building provides an immediate opportunity for a growing congregation or organization seeking an established facility while also offering potential for adaptive reuse. North end of the property features approximately 2.2 undeveloped acres that is zoned light commercial with a protective overlay. The undeveloped northern portion of the property adds another layer of opportunity, offering room for future expansion, additional development, parking, outdoor uses, or simply additional land for the next owner's vision. Whether the buyer is looking for an established church campus, school or nonprofit facility, event and recreation space, commercial/light industrial operation, or a value-add investment opportunity, this property offers the scale, infrastructure, acreage, and flexibility to support a wide range of possibilities.
