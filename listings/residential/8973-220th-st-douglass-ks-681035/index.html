Property Description

A TRUE HIDDEN GEM! Discover the perfect blend of peaceful country living and modern convenience with this beautiful limestone and brick ranch nestled on 2.2 acres surrounded by mature trees. A circular driveway welcomes you to this incredible property featuring 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, an attached 2-car garage, and TWO impressive outbuildings! Step inside to an inviting open floor plan with vaulted ceilings, a spacious kitchen with a huge walk-in pantry, and a cozy hearth room featuring a wood-burning fireplace. The primary suite offers a large walk-in closet, private bath with dual sinks, separate tub and shower, and its own private deck with a hot tub that can remain! Two additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. Just off the dining area, a large deck provides the perfect setting for outdoor entertaining, complete with a fun slide to the backyard for the kids! The finished view-out basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring a spacious family room, game room, full wet bar, two additional bedrooms, and a generous full bathroom. Perfect for entertaining family and friends! The outbuildings are truly a dream! The 30' x 50' shop features concrete floors, two oversized doors, with its own bathroom, and a covered side deck. The second 24' x 50' building includes a 14' x 14' electric overhead door and 50-amp RV hookup, making it ideal for storing your camper, boat, or other recreational toys! Additional highlights include geothermal heating and cooling, septic system, TWO water wells, and a 9-zone sprinkler system for easy lawn maintenance. Major improvements within the last 2–3 years include a new roof, gutters, water heater, pressure tank, and replacement of plumbing lines from outside into the home. Properties like this are hard to find! Enjoy the privacy, space, and endless possibilities of country living while remaining conveniently close to city amenities.
