Property Description

Rare opportunity to own a fully renovated mixed-use property in the heart of Downtown Newton. Offering approximately 5,250 square feet, this unique property combines beautifully updated commercial space with a stunning upper-level residence, creating endless possibilities for business owners, entrepreneurs, investors, and owner-occupants alike. The main level has been extensively renovated and features versatile showroom space, private offices, conference and training areas, storage rooms, and workshop space suitable for a wide variety of business operations. Positioned on a highly visible corner with Main Street frontage, the property offers exceptional exposure in one of Newton's most recognizable downtown locations. Upstairs, you'll find a fully renovated loft-style residence featuring an expansive open-concept living area, modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances, oversized eating bar, abundant cabinetry, dedicated laundry area, two spacious bedrooms, two updated bathrooms, and contemporary finishes throughout. A rooftop access door provides additional flexibility and a unique feature rarely found in downtown properties. Adding to the property's appeal, it is strategically located within the proposed Depot District redevelopment area, a transformative project currently gaining momentum in Newton. The City has secured an option to purchase the historic Newton Depot and is actively seeking development proposals that would combine the Depot with the former Dillons site to create a revitalized mixed-use district. As redevelopment plans continue to take shape, this property stands to benefit from increased investment, visibility, and activity in the surrounding area. Whether you're looking to operate your business downstairs while living upstairs, generate income from one or both spaces, expand your investment portfolio, or establish a flagship location for your business, this property offers flexibility that is difficult to find. Properties offering this combination of residential living, commercial functionality, income-producing potential, and proximity to a potentially transformative redevelopment district rarely become available in Downtown Newton. Don't miss your opportunity to position yourself in the center of Newton's next chapter. Schedule your private tour today and discover the possibilities at 302 N Main Street!
