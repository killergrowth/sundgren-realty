Property Description

Riverside Living with No HOA – 4 Bed, 3 Bath, Built in 2022 Welcome to 4429 S Mount Carmel, a nearly new 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home with 2,160 square feet and a backyard most buyers only dream about. A river runs right behind the property, so you can cast a line for an evening of fishing without ever leaving home. Inside, durable LVP flooring flows through the main living areas, and the kitchen features granite countertops. Every bedroom includes a huge walk-in closet with built-in wood shelving. Inside the primary suite, the Jack-and-Jill bathroom has dual sinks, which makes busy mornings easier. The previous owners added several upgrades outside. There’s a brand-new front patio for your morning coffee and freshly laid sod. A sprinkler system keeps the lawn green with little effort. The fence is fully owned on all three sides, so there’s no shared-fence hassle with neighbors. With no HOA, you have the freedom to make this home your own. It’s located in the Haysville school district (USD 261). Homes this new with river frontage don’t come along often, so schedule your showing today!
