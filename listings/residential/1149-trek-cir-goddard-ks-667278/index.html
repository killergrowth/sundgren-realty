Property Description

Where Small-Town Charm Meets Countryside Living! Discover the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and modern living in this brand-new 3-bedroom, 2-bath home located on a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable west Goddard. Designed with active lifestyles in mind, this thoughtfully designed split-bedroom floor plan offers an inviting open-concept layout, modern finishes, and an oversized 3-car garage. The private primary suite features an en-suite bathroom, walk-in closet, and walk-in shower. The spacious living and dining areas create the perfect setting for entertaining or relaxing. The modern kitchen is sure to impress with a large island and eating bar, pantry, and beautiful quartz countertops. Quartz countertops continue throughout both bathrooms for a cohesive, upscale finish. Additional highlights include a spacious laundry/mudroom, 9-foot ceilings, and an impressive 10-foot coffered ceiling in the main living area. All kitchen appliances are included, even the refrigerator! Just bring your washer and dryer. Enjoy outdoor living with covered front and back patios and a large, fully fenced, sodded backyard. Community amenities make this location even more desirable! Residents enjoy access to a swimming pool, storm shelter, playground, and ponds. The community is conveniently located adjacent to the Prairie Sunset Trail, offering opportunities to hike and bike east toward west Wichita and Pawnee Prairie Park or west toward Garden Plain. Enjoy the convenience of being within walking distance of all four Goddard schools, with shopping, dining, and other everyday conveniences nearby. QUALITY CONSTRUCTION UPGRADES INCLUDE: 2x6 exterior wall construction R-19 insulation 40-inch footing/foundation Category 3 roof shingles Brand-new construction, thoughtful design, exceptional community amenities, and a fantastic Goddard location make this home one you won't want to miss!
