Located in the desirable Goddard School District, this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers the perfect combination of comfortable main-floor living, quality craftsmanship, and room to spread out! Beautiful alder woodwork, vaulted ceilings, and hardwood floors bring warmth and character to the open living spaces.
