Property Description

Affordable Mulvane living with a spacious lot! Welcome to 603 S Central Ave, a charming 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom ranch built in 1953. This home offers a practical layout, laminate flooring throughout, an attached 1-car garage, and a spacious 0.25-acre lot with plenty of room to enjoy the outdoors. The kitchen is functional, and the appliances transfer with the property, helping make the move-in process that much easier. Whether you're a first-time homebuyer, downsizing, or looking for an investment property, this home offers an opportunity to own in Mulvane without sacrificing outdoor space. Enjoy the convenience of being close to Mulvane's local schools, shopping, dining, parks, and everyday amenities, with Wichita just a short drive away. If you've been looking for an affordable home with a bigger lot, garage, and practical layout, put 603 S Central Ave on your list! Schedule your showing today.
