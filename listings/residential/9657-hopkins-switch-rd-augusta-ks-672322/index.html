Property Description

Welcome home to one of most unique properties in south central Kansas! 9657 SW Hopkins Switch is located just minutes east of Augusta and offers an experience unlike any other. The home has been completely renovated and is spotless. BRAND NEW appliances will stay with the home. Project space is ample with 2 detached garages(one has air compressor, bathroom, and split unit AC), RV carport with full hookups, additional covered parking and numerous storage buildings. Once you’ve seen the home and buildings, the real fun begins: the seller has spent countless hours curating what he refers to as ‘Preservation Acres’, centered around the display of Americana from years ago. Take a walk around the pond to enjoy beautifully crafted native limestone patios, arch bridges, the lookout tower, 1929 train car clubhouse, and lighthouse which offers full amenities while overlooking the water. This property is truly one-of-a-kind, bring your offer and don't miss out on this property!
