Located in the desirable Westlink addition of West Wichita, this mostly brick ranch offers 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and inviting indoor and outdoor living spaces. The home features a covered front entry and an attached two-car garage. Inside, the living and dining areas flow together beautifully, featuring hardwood floors, a gas fireplace, and access to the enclosed back porch. The kitchen offers a charming breakfast nook with a bay window, gas hookups, and stainless steel appliances. The primary suite features a tray ceiling and a private ensuite bathroom with granite countertops, double sinks, and a walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom with a tub/shower combination, and a dedicated main-floor laundry area complete the main level. The finished basement provides even more living space, including a large recreation or family room with a fireplace, two spacious finished rooms, a full bathroom, and a storage room with a workshop area. Enjoy relaxing evenings and entertaining guests in the screened-in back porch, complete with recessed lighting, a ceiling fan, and plenty of room for family and friends. The privacy-fenced backyard is beautifully landscaped with flower beds, patio space, and a storage shed. A sprinkler system and drip irrigation lines in the flower beds help keep the landscaping looking its best. Additional updates, according to the seller, include a roof that is approximately seven years old, an HVAC air conditioning unit that is approximately five years old, and a 50-gallon water heater that is approximately one year old. With a desirable West Wichita location, a functional layout, a finished basement, and inviting outdoor spaces, this home offers plenty of room to relax, entertain, and enjoy!
