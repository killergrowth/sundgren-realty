The home is situated on a beautifully wooded over half-acre lot, and offers lovely curb appeal with mature landscaping, a charming brick walkway, and a welcoming front porch-the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee or relax in the evening. Out back, you'll find your own private retreat featuring a resort-style salt water pool, built-in outdoor grill area, covered sitting area, and fire-pit, all surround by mature trees creating a peaceful setting for entertaining or unwinding.
