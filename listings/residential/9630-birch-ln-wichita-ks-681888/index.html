Property Description

Welcome home to this charming 1950's brick rancher, offering over 1,500 square feet of comfortable, one-level living! Full of character and timeless appeal, this 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home features a spacious living room PLUS a separate family room, providing plenty of room to relax, entertain, and enjoy everyday living. You’ll love the abundance of original character, including charming built-ins, original hardwood floors and details that give this home its own unique personality. If you’re looking for classic brick construction, functional one-level living, and plenty of charm in a fantastic established neighborhood, Make this house your HOME TODAY.
