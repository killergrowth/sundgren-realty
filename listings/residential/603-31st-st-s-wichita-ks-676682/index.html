Property Description

If you’ve been waiting for the perfect opportunity to build equity, this is it! Offering over 2,000 square feet of main-floor living space for just $200,000, this home is packed with potential and ready for your personal touch. Inside, you’ll find 4 generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite complete with a large en suite bathroom and walk-in closet. The wide-open layout creates an inviting flow between the living room, kitchen, and dining area, making it ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A large laundry room adds even more convenience. Outside, enjoy a covered front porch, covered back patio, outdoor storage shed, plenty of parking, and a desirable corner lot. Whether you’re a first-time buyer looking for room to grow or an investor searching for incredible value, opportunities like this don’t come around often. Priced to sell, this is your chance to own a spacious home with endless potential at an unbeatable price.
