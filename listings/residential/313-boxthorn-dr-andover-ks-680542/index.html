Property Description

Luxury, comfort, and effortless living come together beautifully in this stunning Ashford floor plan by Perfection Builders! Offering just under 1,600 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this exceptional two-bedroom, two-bathroom patio home delivers the perfect combination of sophisticated finishes, functional design, and low-maintenance luxury. From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the abundance of natural light pouring through beautiful windows and elegant transoms, creating a bright, airy, and inviting living space. The gorgeous kitchen is truly the heart of the home, featuring upgraded cabinetry, an expansive island, and an open layout that's equally suited for entertaining guests or enjoying a quiet evening at home. The spacious primary suite is a retreat all its own, complete with a beautifully appointed bathroom and generous walk-in closet that doubles as an insulated concrete form (ICF) safe room and storm shelter, providing an added measure of security and peace of mind. The generously sized guest bedroom also features its own walk-in closet, offering exceptional comfort and storage. Outdoor living is just as inviting, with both a charming covered front porch and a wonderfully private covered side patio, perfect for morning coffee, evening cocktails, or simply enjoying a little peace and quiet. But the luxury doesn't stop at your front door! Life in The Courtyards at Cornerstone means enjoying the freedom of a truly low-maintenance lifestyle. Lawn care, flower bed maintenance, and snow removal are all taken care of, giving you more time to enjoy the things you love. Residents also enjoy access to an extraordinary private clubhouse featuring a heated saltwater swimming pool, 24/7 fitness center, pickleball courts, game room, beautifully appointed gathering spaces, and a fully equipped kitchen. Manicured walking paths and beautifully maintained community grounds make every day feel a little more like a vacation. Ideally located with convenient access to the best shopping, dining, entertainment, and everyday amenities that Andover and East Wichita have to offer, this home delivers an exceptional lifestyle in an equally exceptional location. Beautifully designed. Thoughtfully appointed. Effortlessly maintained. Welcome home to 313 W Boxthorn Drive, where luxury living is simply a way of life!
