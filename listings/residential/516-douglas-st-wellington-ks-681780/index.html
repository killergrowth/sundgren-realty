Property Description

A large lot sets this well maintained quad level apart, pairing 1,640 square feet of living space with an extra lot for a total of 14,140 sq.ft. Mature shade trees frame the expansive yard, while fresh exterior paint gives the home a crisp welcome. Inside, three bedrooms and two and one half bathrooms are arranged across multiple levels, creating natural separation between gathering spaces and private retreats. Wide windows brighten the living room, which flows easily into the formal dining room. The eat in kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, long counters, and a sliding door leading outside to the refinished deck. On the lower level, the family room brings warmth with a brick fireplace, built in shelving, and direct access to the outside. Whether hosting a barbecue or simply claiming a quiet spot beneath the trees, this outdoor setting has room to breathe. A dedicated laundry area, generous closet storage, and an attached two car garage help keep daily life organized. The roof and heating and cooling system were replaced in 2017. The partially finished basement adds a bonus room, and lots of extra storage space. Recreation and schools are close at hand: Wellington Middle School, Hibbs Hooten Baseball Field, Coach John Gifford Football Stadium, Wellington High School, and Elementary School are all within just a short distance.
