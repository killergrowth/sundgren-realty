About This Property

Escape to peaceful country living with this beautifully remodeled rural property in Butler County offering the perfect mix of charm, function, and acreage! Situated on approximately 4.2 acres, this updated home features the hard-to-find combination of land, a large shop, modern updates, and the quiet lifestyle buyers are searching for.
