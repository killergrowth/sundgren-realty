Property Description

ONLINE AUCTION: Online bidding starts 11/10/26 at 2PM CT and online bidding ends on 11/19/26 at 2PM CT. Investors, start your engines! This sprawling 3 bed 1 bath Bungalow is primed and ready for a refresh. Clocking in at nearly 1300 square feet, this home packs a powerful punch. Those with a keen eye will see hints of Craftsman details throughout: original hardwood doors (with glass inserts), glass door knobs, heavy baseboards, custom built-ins, and boxed beam columns. The home feels open and expansive, while still maintaining designated compartments, as was the tradition of the era. The back two bedrooms and hallway feature original hardwood floors, which leads one to wonder what lies under the carpet at the front of the home. Below in the unfinished basement, you'll find a recently installed HVAC system (~4yrs old), the hot water heater, and a large area for storage. The home is situated nicely on a quarter of an acre, with fencing, large grass covered areas, and mature trees overhead. Make plans now to tour this home during the marketing period, and then be sure to get registered for the Absolute Auction so that you're ready to place your final bid by Thursday November 19th @ 2pm.
