About This Property

Completely rebuilt from the inside out, this beautifully updated two-story home offers the perfect blend of classic charm and modern peace of mind. Featuring 3 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, nearly every major component has been replaced, making this home truly move-in ready. Extensive improvements include all-new electrical with a 200-amp service, new gas, water, and sewer lines, a new water heater, and the addition of central heating and air. The roof, windows, and siding are approximately 10 years old, while the entire interior has been taken down to the studs and thoughtfully rebuilt with today's style and functionality in mind. The bright, inviting interior features durable LVP flooring throughout the main level and cozy carpet in all upstairs bedrooms. The spacious kitchen comes complete with a full appliance package, making it ready for everyday living from day one. Outside, relax on the covered front porch and enjoy the charm of small-town living in Potwin.
