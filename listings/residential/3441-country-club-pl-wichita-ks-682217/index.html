Property Description

Location, location, location...Close to Wesley Hospital and all the desired east side restaurants. Beautiful College Hill charm shows through on this 1.5 story home just across the street from MacDonald Golf Course. Beautiful wood floors and lots of natural light. Owner has removed all of the knob and tube, and with the new roof in October of 26', makes insurance a breeze. Seller has also had new sewer lines ran, refinished all wood floors, new sheetrock in several areas, new windows in the living room and has also added new AC units upstairs. You'll appreciate the size of the (2) master bedrooms, one on main level, the other upstairs. Home features a walk out deck in 2nd floor Master. Don't miss this opportunity to own your own Tudor classic.
