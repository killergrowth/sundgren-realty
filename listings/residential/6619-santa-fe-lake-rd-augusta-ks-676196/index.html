Property Description

MULTIPLE OFFERS RECEIVED, DEADLINE TO SUBMIT 5:00 P.M., TUESDAY, JULY 21, 2026. Nestled on 4.6 secluded acres, this private country estate offers an exceptional blend of luxury, privacy, and convenience with easy blacktop access just minutes from Wichita. Completely remodeled with exceptional designer finishes, this stunning home welcomes you with soaring ceilings, abundant natural light, and an open-concept floor plan. The spacious living and dining areas are anchored by a cozy wood-burning stove, creating a warm and inviting gathering place. The thoughtfully designed floorplan flows seamlessly into the impressive gourmet kitchen featuring custom cabinetry, a dual-fuel six-burner WOLF stainless steel range, Sub-Zero refrigeration, and a built-in ice maker. The private primary suite occupies its own level, with vaulted ceilings, a spacious walk-in closet, and a luxurious spa-inspired bath. Every bathroom has been remodeled with tile, seamless glass shower enclosures, and premium Moen and Kohler fixtures. The finished basement offers an inviting second living area with polished concrete floors, a cozy fireplace, an additional bedroom with full bathroom and a beautifully designed office space featuring custom black cabinetry. Thoughtful updates throughout include a newer HVAC in 2023, a tankless water heater, whole-home water filtration system, new interior paint, and new carpet. Step outside to the covered patio, where an outdoor fireplace creates the perfect setting for entertaining or quiet evenings overlooking the peaceful surroundings. Complementing the home are a heated and insulated 30' x 40' workshop, a separate heated and insulated 2 1/2 - car garage, a wrought-iron fenced area for pets, and a fire pit perfect for gatherings under the stars. Truly one of a kind, this country estate blends high end design, incredible character, and modern amenities that are seldom found.
