Property Description

Full of character, thoughtfully updated, and designed for gathering, this one-of-a-kind mid-century home offers a rare combination of architectural charm, generous living spaces, and an incredible backyard retreat. Tucked into an established east Wichita neighborhood known for its mid-century homes, just off 13th and Woodlawn, you’ll love the tree-lined streets and convenient access to Rockwell Library, Edgemoor Park, and local favorites like Il Primo Espresso Cafe, Red Bird Sandwiches, and Great Harvest Bread Co. A welcoming front porch sets the tone, offering the perfect spot for morning coffee and neighborhood views. Inside, the open, flowing floor plan is made for entertaining, with a spacious main living area featuring a charming brick gas fireplace, updated LVP flooring, a dedicated dining area, and large windows that fill the space with natural light. The beautifully updated kitchen continues the easy flow, featuring ceiling-height shaker cabinetry, granite countertops, and a walk-in pantry. With direct access to the back deck and pool, hosting friends and family feels effortless. A generously sized laundry room and convenient half bath sit just off the kitchen. And, enjoy a spacious separate family room with a picture window, which offers the perfect spot for movie nights, a second gathering space, or a flexible-use room. Two comfortable secondary bedrooms share an updated hall bath with a tub/shower combination and fresh finishes. The primary suite is a standout, with glass French doors opening directly to the back patio and pool area. An adjoining former bedroom has been transformed into an expansive walk-in closet, complete with abundant storage and room for a home office, hobby space, or dressing area. The beautifully updated ensuite bath feels like a private retreat, with marble countertops, modern finishes, and a spacious shower. But the real showstopper is the backyard. Designed for long summer evenings and unforgettable gatherings, this private outdoor oasis features a generous patio, pergola, string lights, and a beautiful saltwater pool surrounded by low-maintenance green turf. Pool upgrades include a new liner in 2022, a new salt converter, upgraded equipment, and a custom cover. It’s the kind of backyard that makes staying home feel like a vacation. Storage is thoughtfully tucked throughout the home, and an unfinished partial basement provides even more flexibility for a workout area, hobbies, or additional storage. More than $100,000 in improvements in recent years add peace of mind, including a new roof in 2024, new Woodbridge windows, a new sump pump, a French drain around the pool area, new pool liner, turf, attic fan, and water heater. Distinctive mid-century architecture, an entertaining-friendly layout, meaningful updates, and a backyard you’ll never want to leave make this a rare opportunity to find so much character, space, and lifestyle in one special east Wichita home.
