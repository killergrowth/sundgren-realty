Property Description

Welcome to 1148 N Parkwood Ln, Wichita, KS! This charming ranch-style home offers easy, one-level living in an amazing Wichita location. Inside, you’ll find a sleek, remodeled kitchen that feels both modern and cozy. Under-cabinet lighting sets the mood for everything from morning coffee to evening meal prep. Every appliance remains with the home, including the washer and dryer, so you can move right in without a shopping trip. The layout flows effortlessly, making everyday living simple and comfortable. Two spacious living areas give everyone room to spread out. A cedar coat closet near the entry keeps coats and seasonal items organized and smelling fresh. With three bedrooms and two updated bathrooms, there’s space for everyone! Comfort and efficiency are built in. Newer double-pane windows help keep the home quiet and energy efficient, while a zoned HVAC system lets you fine-tune the temperature in different areas of the house. The updated electrical panel offers peace of mind, and the attic fan helps move hot air out during the warmer months to keep cooling costs down. Step out back and discover your own private oasis, perfect for relaxing, entertaining, and making summer memories. The partial 8-foot privacy fence provides seclusion and a sense of retreat. The oversized two-car garage has room for vehicles, tools, and hobbies, and the double doors in the back make it easy to bring in equipment or move straight out to the backyard.
