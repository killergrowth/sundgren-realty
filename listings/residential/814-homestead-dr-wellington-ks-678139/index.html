Property Description

Welcome to this beautiful and spacious home in the desirable Settlers Creek Addition! A new roof will be installed before closing, providing added peace of mind for the next homeowner. With its fresh, bright feel, modern décor, and inviting open floor plan, this home offers plenty of space for everyday living, entertaining, and enjoying time with family and friends—all situated on an impressive 0.70-acre lot with NO HOA! The modern brick exterior provides great curb appeal, while the three-car garage and expansive driveway offer abundant parking for family and guests. The oversized lot is one of the standout features of this property, providing significantly more room than a typical in-town property. With generous space on both sides of the home, you have the flexibility to use the property for additional parking, storage, landscaping, outdoor activities, or future improvements. The NO HOA allows for added flexibility and freedom in how you use your property, subject of course to applicable city and county regulations. There is ample room for RV parking, boat parking, camper parking, utility trailers, recreational vehicles, and other equipment, making this property an excellent option for those who enjoy camping, boating, traveling, or outdoor recreation. The expansive lot also provides room to build a detached shop, additional garage, workshop, or other permitted structure, offering the opportunity to create the space you've been looking for. With plenty of room around the home, you can have the convenience of additional storage and recreational parking without giving up valuable yard space. Inside, you'll find vaulted ceilings, a charming bay window, modern finishes, and an abundance of natural light that creates a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The main floor features two bedrooms and convenient main-floor laundry, along with a beautiful kitchen offering attractive cabinetry, a spacious island, and stainless-steel stove and dishwasher. The open living and kitchen areas provide plenty of room for gatherings and everyday living. The finished basement is an entertainer's dream, featuring a large family room with a wet bar and tile flooring. This versatile space could easily serve as a theater room, game room, recreation area, or comfortable gathering space. Additional basement bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, an office, hobbies, or additional living arrangements. Step outside to the fenced backyard and back patio, perfect for summer cookouts, relaxing evenings, entertaining, or enjoying the extra outdoor space this property provides. The expansive lot gives you room to spread out and enjoy the outdoors without feeling crowded by neighboring homes. Another major advantage is the extra space and privacy between neighboring properties. Enjoy the breathing room and flexibility of a larger lot while still having the convenience of living in town. With three additional lots included with the property, there is an exceptional amount of land and potential for future use. Whether you're looking for a home with room for your family, plenty of parking, space for an RV or boat, room to build a shop, or simply want the freedom and privacy that comes with a larger property, this Settlers Creek home offers an uncommon combination of space, flexibility, convenience, and future potential—with NO HOA! With its desirable location, modern décor, vaulted ceilings, finished basement, main-floor laundry, abundant parking, fenced yard, 0.70-acre setting, three additional lots, NO HOA, and substantial room for RVs, boats, campers, trailers, a future shop, or other permitted improvements, this property offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the space and flexibility of a larger property while remaining conveniently located in town.
