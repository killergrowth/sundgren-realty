Property Description

Fresh updates and plenty of room to spread out! This 3-bedroom, 2-bath condo offers a versatile 1.5-story layout with a main-floor primary bedroom, finished basement and oversized two-car garage. Recent improvements include new neutral paint throughout, new bathroom flooring, and refinished kitchen and bathroom cabinets. The living room features a vaulted ceiling and fireplace, creating an inviting gathering space. The open layout connects the living and dining areas, while the kitchen offers ample cabinetry, a black range and a sliding glass door leading to the front entrance area. The main-floor primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet, direct access to the private backyard, and convenient access to a bathroom with a shower and connecting second vanity area. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom provide space for family, guests or a home office. The finished basement features a family room and a spacious nonconforming bedroom with extensive closet space, offering flexibility for hobbies or additional living needs. An unfinished area provides even more storage. Enjoy a private, fenced outdoor area with a patio and artificial grass for easy upkeep, accessible through the main-floor primary bedroom. The oversized two-car garage provides additional room for parking and storage. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants and Wichita State University. Schedule a showing to explore the open living spaces, vaulted ceiling, finished basement, storage, garage and private maintenance-free outdoor space in person.
