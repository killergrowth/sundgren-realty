Property Description

Beautifully designed and fully completed, this exceptional Cedar Ranch residence offers an opportunity to enjoy sophisticated new construction without the wait. Set on more than 1.03 acres with views toward the lake and woods, this 1.5-story home features 6 bedrooms, 5.5 bathrooms, a 3-car garage, and thoughtfully designed living spaces throughout. A dramatic entry welcomes you into the home and leads to an impressive great room with soaring cathedral ceilings, designer beams, and floor-to-ceiling windows that fill the space with natural light while capturing the surrounding views. The great room opens to a covered deck with an outdoor fireplace, creating a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. The kitchen serves as a natural centerpiece with a generous island, breakfast nook, distinctive pendant lighting, and an adjacent formal dining area. The main level includes three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a large walk-in closet featuring a central storage island and an ensuite bath with dual sinks and a large tub. Two additional bedrooms share a full bathroom. A versatile upstairs bonus room with its own half bath provides flexible space for a home office, hobby room, playroom, or additional gathering area. The fully finished view-out basement with exterior access expands the home's living and entertaining space with a spacious family room, dining area, wet bar, two additional bedrooms, and two full bathrooms. A rear deck provides convenient access to the backyard and continues the home's connection to the outdoors. Originally built as a model home by Bob Cook Homes LLC and featured in the Fall Parade of Homes, this residence showcases a refined blend of architectural scale, high-quality finishes, abundant natural light, and functional design. With construction complete and the home ready for its next owner, buyers can experience the finished residence firsthand and make Cedar Ranch their home without the extended timeline of building from the ground up. Conveniently located near schools, shopping, and everyday amenities, this distinctive Cedar Ranch property offers generous space, beautiful surroundings, and a finished home designed to be enjoyed from day one.
