About This Property

Don't let the address fool you. It is 13 minutes from Goddard, 21 minutes from New Market Square, under 30 minutes to McConnel Air Force Base or Wesley hospital. The property is situated on a 40+/-acre lot, offering a private and secluded setting. The barndominium is an impressive structure that demands to be seen with an impressive spanning of 8200 square feet in barn space alone. With its large size and unique design, it stands out among other properties. The living quarters feature three bedrooms, equipped with walk-in showers and walk-in closets for added convenience. The kitchen showcases solid-surface countertops, and the living areas boast 10 to 14-footceilings, giving it an open and spacious feel. Other notable features of the property include reverse osmosis filtration system, power assisted flush toilets, a tankless Rinnai system for efficient water heating, radiant floor heating, pellet stove heating, an upgraded metal roof for increased durability, and underground electric with 200-amp panels and an additional two panels are ready for additional structures, hardwired security system, additional loft, and multiple mini splits through out for more controlled temperatures. It offers ample space for storage, livestock, or any other purpose you may have in mind. The property itself is divided into two tracts, totaling approximately 40 acres. This provides plenty of room to roam and explore. The land is a mix of open fields and wooded areas, providing diverse opportunities for recreation and enjoyment. Whether you're into hunting, hiking, caring for animals or simply appreciating the beauty of nature, this property has it all.
