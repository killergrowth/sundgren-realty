Property Description

Welcome to the Monarch, a thoughtfully designed 1,270 sq ft single-level home from Comfort Homes, now under construction in Foxfire's brand-new Phase 2. This is one of over 12 spec homes currently being built in this section, with the very first homes in the phase already underway. Built on a slab with a zero entry design and split bedroom layout, the Monarch makes everyday living simple and accessible while giving each bedroom its own sense of privacy. Inside, an open concept floor plan ties the kitchen and living areas together seamlessly, finished with durable luxury vinyl plank flooring and quartz countertops. Wide trim throughout the main living space adds a refined, custom feel, and the patio door slider comes complete with blinds already in place. The primary suite is a standout, featuring a box ceiling overhead and a bathroom designed for daily ease — a step-in shower, double vanity, and a roomy walk-in closet for plenty of storage. Outside, enjoy a 12' x 10' covered patio perfect for relaxing regardless of the weather. Set in Derby, this home puts you in one of the area's most sought-after school districts, with easy access to shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences. McConnell AFB is just about a mile away, and east Wichita is a short 10-minute drive, making this an ideal location for military families and commuters alike. A metal safe room in the garage is available for an additional fee, and landscaping packages can be added depending on completion timing (seasonal availability applies). Beyond this home, Foxfire Phase 2 also offers homesites for custom builds, including around a half dozen lake lots and another half dozen tree-lined lots along the back of the development.
