Property Description

Charming Character & Flexible Living Space in a Convenient Wichita Location! This inviting 2-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom home offers plenty of room to spread out with two main-floor living areas, a formal dining room, large oversized shop and an attached 1-car garage. The front living room welcomes you with abundant natural light, while the second living area at the back of the home adds plenty of character with a beautiful brick fireplace and vaulted ceilings with wood beams that continue into the kitchen for a cohesive, warm feel. Large windows throughout the main level fill the home with natural light and create a bright, comfortable atmosphere and new carpet in both bedrooms. The unfinished concrete basement offers even more potential, whether you need a storm shelter or envision finishing the space into a hobby room, game room, entertaining area, or additional living space. With its combination of charm, functional living areas, and opportunity to make the space your own, this home is ready for its next chapter!
