Property Description

Back on the market and it's your opportunity! Check out what $299,000 can buy! Welcome to this spacious all-brick 3-bedroom, 3-bath ranch situated on a generous .42-acre double lot! Custom built with efficiency in mind, this home offers plenty of space, thoughtful updates, and some seriously impressive extras. Beautiful custom wood floors flow through the living room and hallway, while tile adds style and durability to the kitchen and dining area. The oversized laundry room offers abundant storage, and newer carpet and a newer hot water tank add to the list of updates, including a new insulated garage door! Downstairs, you’ll find a huge 35' x 12.75' family room, a bonus room currently used as a bedroom, and an incredible 32' x 11' indoor workshop—perfect for hobbies, projects, or additional storage. The outdoor space is where this property really stands out! A heated and cooled 23' x 21' detached workshop is currently used as a quilting studio and includes its own cellar—ideal for temperature-sensitive storage or as a storm shelter. Two additional storage sheds provide even more room for lawn and garden equipment. Even better, the home, workshop, and sheds all received new roofs this year! With a large lot, multiple workshops, abundant storage, and versatile living spaces, this property offers possibilities you simply don’t find every day!
