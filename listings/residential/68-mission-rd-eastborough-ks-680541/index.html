Property Description

Discover one of Eastborough’s most distinctive mid-century modern residences, offering 5,049 square feet of architectural character, thoughtfully designed living spaces, and exceptional versatility. Built in 1959, this six-bedroom, five-bath home features dramatic stonework, vaulted wood-beamed ceilings, expansive windows, and a private courtyard connecting the home’s indoor and outdoor living areas. The inviting family room centers around a striking stone fireplace, while the dining room’s domed ceiling and walls of glass create an unforgettable setting overlooking the courtyard. The kitchen retains distinctive mid-century design elements, including terrazzo flooring, custom cabinetry, an indoor grill, and generous workspace. A spacious primary suite opens directly to the courtyard, while the updated basement provides additional living and entertaining space with a wet bar, office, and hobby area. Adding remarkable flexibility is a separate accessory dwelling unit, completed in 2020, with its own entrance, kitchen, living area, bedroom, bathroom, and laundry. A premium DaVinci roof, circular driveway, and desirable Eastborough location near neighborhood parks and walking paths complete this extraordinary property. Freshly presented and ready for its next chapter, 68 S Mission Rd offers a rare opportunity to enjoy timeless architecture with room to make it your own. This home will be available for viewing exclusively this Saturday and Sunday. All interested buyers are encouraged to complete their due diligence and submit their highest and best offer by Monday, 10/19, at noon.
