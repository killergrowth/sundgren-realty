Property Description

This 3 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom home with fully finished basement is better than new with custom touches and huge backyard backing to the treeline for added privacy. Welcome into the large foyer and enter the great room, complete with stone fireplace, built-in bookshelves, and huge sunny windows. The open floor plan allows for easy conversation between the living room, dining room, and kitchen. A large island, stainless steel appliances, quartz counters, and a walk-in pantry make this kitchen an easy and beautiful workspace. the powder bath on the main level keeps guest out of your private quarters. Upstairs there is a large loft, open to the foyer. The primary ensuite has a walk-in closet and full bath. The two additional bedrooms share a jack-n-jill bathroom. The laundry room is conveniently located on this upper floor for added convenience. The finished basement includes an accent wall with modern shiplap. a full bathroom, and several closets for storage. The 3 car attached garage provides additional space for cars and toys. The backyard is fully fenced with a large deck, and both the swingset AND playhouse remain with the home! Things stay green with the sprinkler system! Conveniently located near shopping, schools, the base, and Wichita businesses, this home is a 10/10 and ready for YOU!
