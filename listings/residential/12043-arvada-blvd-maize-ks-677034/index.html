Property Description

The Cottonwood floor plan offers a thoughtfully designed two-story layout with 3 bedrooms, all conveniently located upstairs, along with 2.5 bathrooms. The upstairs laundry room is ideally situated near the bedrooms, making everyday chores more convenient. The spacious primary suite features a private en-suite bathroom with double sinks, providing both comfort and functionality. Throughout the home, you'll find beautiful Nutmeg cabinetry in the kitchen and bathrooms, complemented by elegant quartz countertops. The modern kitchen is designed for both cooking and entertaining, featuring stainless steel appliances, a center island, and plenty of workspace and storage. Located within the highly desirable Maize School District, this home also offers the added value of low special taxes. Outside, you'll enjoy a fully finished yard with sod, sprinkler system, and professional landscaping included, making it move-in ready from day one. The HOA is $75/month which covers mowing, lawn and sprinkler maintenance.
