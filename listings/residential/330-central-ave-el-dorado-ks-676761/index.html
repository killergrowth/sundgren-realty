Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully cared-for 2 bedroom, 1 bath Carriage House condo - the only two-bedroom in the entire building, offering a rare blend of space, comfort, and convenience! Step inside to a welcoming formal entryway that leads into a cozy layout with carpet throughout the living areas, creating a soft, inviting feel from the moment you arrive. The kitchen has been tastefully remodeled with solid-surface countertops, offering both style and durability, and even has a pantry. A formal dining room provides the perfect spot for gatherings, and the covered, carpeted patio extends your living space even further - ideal for morning coffee or relaxing evenings. The bathroom has been fully updated with a beautiful walk-in shower and solid-surface counters, giving the space a clean, modern finish. You’ll also appreciate the convenience of in-unit laundry and the ease of low-maintenance living. This condo includes one designated under-carport parking spot, and even better - the elevator assessment has already been paid in full for this unit, adding tremendous long-term value. This community is designed for residents 55 and better, with a maximum of two occupants and two small pets per unit. Monthly HOA dues include cable TV, water, trash service, snow removal, exterior maintenance of the property and common areas, and exterior insurance. Each unit is responsible for the taxes and insurance for their unit. Come tour this welcoming 55+ community!
