Property Description

Wonderful ranch home with hardwood floors on the main floor in excellent condition and fresh new interior paint. The main floor bathroom has been totally remodeled with newer tub/shower combo, toilet, vanity and tile. The kitchen offers a dining area and also raised counter for additional dining or great for a buffet. Eating area in the kitchen area that has also been updated and offers a dishwasher, range/oven and refrigerator. The basement has all new carpet and paint, the family room has an egress window, and the additional 4th bedroom also with an egress window, also a bonus room with a closet and new carpet. Beautiful custom cabinets throughout the home. The basement also offers a remodeled 2nd full bathroom. The home shows pride of ownership, fenced spacious yard on corner lot. Carport. Newer patio in backyard, great for entertaining family and friends. Move in ready!
