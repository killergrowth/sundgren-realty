Looking for a home that offers plenty of space to work, store, park, and play? This beautiful home maintains a comfortable temperature with Andersen windows throughout. Bright Natural light and a convenient Egress window located in the basement with New ducting for comfortable temperatures! The basement has a durable concrete top that makes this home Perfect for Stormy weather! kitchen and bathroom include granite counter tops with beautiful cabinetry. Lets not forget the Pool and park that is in walking distance and so conveniently located to all shopping in El dorado! You must really come check out the new charm this home has to offer!
