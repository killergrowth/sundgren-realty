Property Description

LAKE VIEWS. DESIGNER TOUCHES. EXCEPTIONAL ENTERTAINING SPACE. Welcome to 1428 N Aster, a beautifully refreshed 5-bedroom, 3-bath Andover residence offering an exceptional combination of style, space, and lifestyle. Thoughtfully enhanced by the current sellers, this home has been given a fresh, elevated feel designed to stand out from the competition. Step inside to a bright and inviting interior featuring freshly painted walls in a lighter, contemporary palette that beautifully complements the home's natural light. Carefully selected wallpaper accents add sophisticated personality and designer-inspired character, creating distinctive focal points throughout the home. The open-concept main level provides an effortless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas, while expansive windows showcase the peaceful lake setting beyond. Whether enjoying a quiet morning or entertaining a crowd, the views create an impressive backdrop for everyday living. The fully finished basement takes entertaining to another level with a stylish wet bar, projection screen for movie nights, two additional bedrooms, and a dedicated office. This versatile space provides room for guests, work, recreation, or simply relaxing in your own private retreat. Outside, the lifestyle continues. The sellers have recently done extensive yard work including re-grading the yard, re-doing the landscaping to create a fresh, polished setting around the home. An oversized pergola, expansive concrete patio, cozy firepit, and tranquil lake views create an exceptional outdoor entertaining area perfect for summer gatherings, relaxing evenings, and memorable nights with family and friends. With the private water access, you can sit in your own back yard and fish or take out your kayak for a relaxing stroll on the water. A 3-car attached garage provides generous space for vehicles, storage, hobbies, and everyday convenience. Built in 2018 and located in the desirable Prairie Creek community, this home offers the rare combination of 5 bedrooms, 3 full baths, extensive finished living space, a 3-car garage, lake views, beautiful outdoor entertaining areas, and stylish recent updates. This isn't simply a home with great features—it's a home designed around the way people want to live, entertain, relax, and enjoy their surroundings. Come experience the lake views, thoughtful updates, and exceptional indoor-outdoor living at 1428 N Aster.
