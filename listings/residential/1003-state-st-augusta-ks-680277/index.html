NO MINIMUM, NO RESERVE!!! Step into the charm and character of this beautiful two-story, 4-bedroom, 2-bath historic home in Augusta, conveniently located just minutes from Kellogg. Situated next to Augusta Middle School and within a short walk of Garfield Elementary School, this home offers both historic charm and an ideal location. Situated on a picturesque brick road, the property offers fantastic curb appeal with its classic brick exterior, impressive covered front porch with two staircases, and convenient carport. An enclosed sunroom is located just off the front porch, providing additional space to relax and enjoy the character of this historic home. Inside, the formal living room features beautiful hardwood flooring and opens to a versatile office space complete with a fireplace. French doors lead into the formal dining room, which features distinctive wood-beamed ceilings and a built-in china cabinet, adding warmth and character to the space. The kitchen features electric hookups and access to the basement. Two bedrooms are located on the main floor, along with a full bathroom featuring tile flooring and a bathtub. The upper level offers two additional bedrooms, a second full bathroom with a tub/shower combination, and a family room providing additional living space. An unfinished basement offers plenty of additional storage. With its historic charm, spacious layout, convenient location, and unique architectural details, this Augusta home is a great opportunity for buyers looking for character and potential.
