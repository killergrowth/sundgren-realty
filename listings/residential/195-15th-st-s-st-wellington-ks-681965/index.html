Property Description

What you need to know about this country property. Just a few miles west of Wellington on HWY 160, which offers great convenience to get you into town. Lots of trees and beautiful sunsets. This is a 2022 Solitaire Presidio manufactured home in good condition and offers modern comfort, quality upgrades, and outstanding outbuildings. This spacious 4-bedroom, 2-bath home features a desirable split-bedroom floor plan and an open-concept living area. The kitchen offers a large island with bar seating, pantry, and all appliances. The private primary suite includes double vanities, a relaxing soaker tub, walk-in shower, oversized walk-in closet, and a private exterior door leading directly to the newly constructed covered deck with built in plant boxes and wide stairs and to the swim spa. Three additional bedrooms are generously sized with ample closet space. This home is exceptionally clean and offers upgrades not commonly found in manufactured homes. The home features a large front porch and a back deck. The property is loaded with extras, including a 30' x 50' insulated shop with electricity and a finished studio apartment complete with a mini-split heating and cooling system and full bathroom—ideal for guests, hobbies, or a home office. An additional 12' x 24' garage provides even more storage or workspace. Outdoor swim spa 7'10½" x 13'8" (50" deep) is included in this sale and features a 20-year shell warranty and a 10-year equipment warranty, and an above-ground storm shelter for peace of mind. Whether you are looking for room to entertain, space for hobbies, or simply a country lifestyle with modern conveniences, this property offers it all.
