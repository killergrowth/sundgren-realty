Property Description

This stunning 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath home is a rare opportunity in NE El Dorado! From the moment you step inside, you'll be captivated by soaring 18-foot ceilings, an abundance of natural light, and a spacious floor plan designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The formal living and dining rooms flow seamlessly into the heart of the home, creating an inviting space you'll love coming home to. The beautifully designed kitchen offers granite countertops, solid wood cabinetry, a walk-in pantry, and a large movable prep island that can be arranged to fit your needs. Just off the kitchen, you'll find an updated half bath and one of this home's standout features: a spacious screened-in deck. Whether you're enjoying your morning coffee, hosting family and friends, grilling out, or simply relaxing bug-free on a warm Kansas evening, this space is sure to become your favorite place to unwind. It truly extends your living space and creates the perfect indoor-outdoor lifestyle. Upstairs, you'll find four spacious bedrooms, including a beautiful primary suite complete with two walk-in closets. The finished basement offers even more living space with a large family room, an additional bedroom, and a full bathroom, making it ideal for guests, hobbies, or a home office. This home has been thoughtfully updated with the improvements that matter most. The high-efficiency two-stage HVAC system is less than two years old, and the garage is equipped with a mini split for year-round comfort. The roof, gutters, and downspouts were all replaced in the fall of 2025, and fresh paint throughout gives the home a bright, updated feel. As an added bonus, all kitchen appliances and the combination washer and dryer stay with the home, making this property truly move-in ready. With its spacious layout, quality updates, and incredible features, this breathtaking home is one you won't want to miss!
