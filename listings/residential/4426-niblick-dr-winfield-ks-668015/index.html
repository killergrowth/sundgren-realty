Property Description

NEW NEW NEW! You won’t want to miss this beautiful new construction home in a desirable neighborhood amid the Quail Ridge Golf Course and there is not an HOA fee! (Covenants do exist). This cul-de-sac corner lot, custom built, two bed, two bath, home was completed in November of 2024 by McKenna and Company. Walking through the front door you’ll immediately see the well placed and lit display creating a warm and inviting feeling. Continue into the open living room (Above the fireplace is wired for a TV ) and kitchen area where the vaulted ceiling with accent beam, fireplace and the tastefully selected cabinets and countertops bring everything together. Don’t miss the walk-in pantry with a lot of space to store and organize your kitchen necessities. Moving down the hall you will pass the laundry (with a pocket door for privacy) and interior door to the garage, making carrying in your groceries a breeze. The master bedroom boasts a walk-in closet with built-ins that doubles as a safe room. This charming and well done home has everything you need from custom blinds, master bath walk-in shower, zero entry, walk-in closet, pantry, and much more! It has it all without the burden of too much to maintain and the peace of mind that everything is new.
