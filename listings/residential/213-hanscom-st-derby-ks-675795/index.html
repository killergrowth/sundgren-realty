Property Description

Comfort Homes' brand-new Nick Jr. model (NOT FOR SALE) in Foxfire Phase 2 is complete and open Saturday-Thursday 1-5 pm (closed Fridays). Come see this 1,245 sq ft, 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in person and explore everything Foxfire Phase 2 has to offer while you're here. The open concept main floor features vaulted ceilings, luxury vinyl plank flooring in the main living areas, quartz countertops in the kitchen and baths, stainless appliances, and luxury trim throughout. The split bedroom layout gives the primary suite its own wing, complete with a box ceiling, walk-in shower, and a generous walk-in closet. A practical laundry room entry from the 3-car garage includes a bench and hooks — the kind of everyday detail that makes a difference. Step outside to a covered deck and patio, ideal for enjoying the outdoors in any weather. Downstairs, a full viewout basement has the potential to nearly double your potential living space. Already framed for two additional bedrooms, a rec room, and a rough-in bath, it's ready for future finishing on your timeline. Located in Derby, one of the Wichita metro's most sought-after communities for schools, shopping, dining, and overall livability, with McConnell AFB just about a mile away and east Wichita a short 10-minute drive. There are over a dozen spec homes currently under construction in Phase 2, with additional homesites available for custom builds — including roughly a half dozen lake lots and a half dozen tree-lined lots along the back of the development. Come out and choose your new home!
