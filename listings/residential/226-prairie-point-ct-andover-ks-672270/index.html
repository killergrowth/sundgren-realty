About This Property

This beautiful one owner home features 5,400 SF of living area and is located on a secluded cul-de-sac in spectacular Flint Hills National. Wonderful craftsmanship and well planned storage space can be found throughout this gently lived in home. The open design provides great flow for entertaining guests or just relaxing with family. The main floor features a spacious living room with fireplace, vaulted ceilings and great views of the beautifully landscaped backyard, two large dining areas, a large kitchen with center island and walk-in pantry, a generously sized master suite with easy access to the covered deck, a second bedroom suite and beautiful office with a full wall of built-in bookshelves. The walkout basement includes a fantastic family room and game room area with fireplace and wet bar, two additional bedroom suites and an additional office. Outside you can enjoy the beauty and privacy of the incredibly well landscaped backyard and common area from either the covered deck or lower-level patio. The privacy of the property is enhanced even more by having common area directly across from the front of the home. Flint Hills National is a spectacular 640-acre private, gated retreat that wraps around the nationally recognized and Tom Fazio designed Flint Hills National Golf Club. Few places rival the beauty and artistic integrity of Flint Hills National Residences. Located in the award winning Andover school district.
