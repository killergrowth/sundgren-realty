Property Description

Exceptional finishes, extensive quality upgrades and a charming water view setting defines this beautifully appointed and immaculately cared for patio home. Dramatic living area showcases tall, cathedral ceilings, gabled windows, statement wood beams and floor-to-ceiling handpicked-chiseled stone fireplace, while 10' tray ceilings, decorative board-and-batten accents and upgraded lighting adds character throughout. Showcasing timeless elegance, the kitchen features top of the line custom made hickory RD Henry soft-close Shaker cabinetry, an expansive 12.5' island, upgraded 100% natural Quartzite countertops, well-designed coffee bar, large walk-in pantry with automatic lighting as well as an extensive GE appliance package; which includes a 36" five-burner gas cooktop, smart convection wall oven, microwave, three-rack dishwasher and French-door refrigerator. The primary suite is a welcoming retreat and includes a versatile adjoining sitting/bonus room plus a spa-inspired bath with a fully tiled wet area, freestanding soaking tub, rain shower, hand shower, dual sinks with wall-mounted brushed-gold faucets, makeup vanity and Toto Washlet bidet seat. Needing an extra room for versatility ---- this home offers two total bedrooms and a dedicated office space with French-doors & plenty of natural lighting. Situated on a premium water lot with a view of trees that can be enjoyed from several locations including the main living area, primary suite, screened-in porch, & large private patio area in the backyard. This east-facing backyard provides many gorgeous sunrises as well as frequent wildlife spottings. The lack of back neighbors and views of nature all contribute to a feeling of privacy within the home, porch & patio areas. There are numerous thoughtful & discerning upgrades throughout the home. A more thorough list is available but a few include wide-plank LVP, brick-look tile and matte-black hexagon tile, upgraded plumbing fixtures, designer lighting, Shaker-style interior doors, Lennox HVAC, whole-home hospital grade REME HALO in-duct air purification system, 50-gallon water heater, Ring doorbell wiring, customizable TRIM Lights and an above-ground storm shelter off the laundry room. The zero-entry home is situated deep within the neighborhood and as the road meanders through one can enjoy the water views & trees that the area amenities are situated along. HOA amenities include the typical lawn care, snow removal, irrigation, as well a vibrant community clubhouse, fitness center, heated saltwater pool and pickleball courts. Come view this home ---- it will not disappoint.
