Property Description

Beautiful water views, timeless character, and an abundance of space make this 4-bedroom home truly special! From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the beautiful hardwood floors, spacious open floor plan, and expansive windows that capture the picturesque setting. The heart of the home is the impressive kitchen and hearth room, featuring abundant cabinetry, generous counter space, a dining nook, and a cozy fireplace surrounded by windows overlooking the water. Step outside onto the oversized covered deck, the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee, entertain guests, or simply relax while taking in the beautiful water views and mature trees. The spacious primary suite offers a double-sided fireplace shared with the private bathroom, creating the perfect ambiance while relaxing in the soaking tub, along with two separate walk-in closets. Downstairs, you'll find an expansive family and recreation area complete with a wet bar, an additional fireplace, and abundant storage space with endless possibilities. A 3-car garage completes this exceptional home. With its thoughtful layout, generous living spaces, and incredible setting, this home offers the perfect opportunity to bring your own vision to a property with so much to love!
