Property Description

Experience refined design, premium finishes, and modern elegance—now offered at an improved price. A rare opportunity to own new at exceptional value. Indulge in maintenance-free living at 1622 S Dove Place—an elegant residence nestled within Dove Landing, an exclusive gated 55+ community in Goddard, KS. Just moments from the premier shopping and dining of New Market Square and west Wichita, this exceptional home offers both serenity and convenience. With no special tax assessments and a host of high-end features, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy timeless luxury in a gated neighborhood. Designed for effortless living, Dove Landing offers a greenbelt and HOA services that include lawn care, snow removal, and trash—so your days are free to enjoy the best of the area. Explore the scenic Prairie Sunset Trail from nearby Linear Park or spend your time at local attractions like Blast Off Bay Water Park, Genesis Health Club, coffee shops, City of Goddard Library and more. From the moment you step through the ZERO-ENTRY solid wood front door with sidelight to let in natural light and enhance the appearance of the entrance, you are greeted by soaring 10-foot ceilings and light-filled interiors that display elegance and warmth. The expansive living room features a stone thermostatic gas fireplace adorned with a wood mantel creating a perfect setting for both relaxing and entertaining. The gourmet kitchen is a true showpiece, offering custom cabinetry, granite countertops, an oversized island with seating, a large pantry, and premium stainless-steel appliances—all designed with the chef in mind. Adjacent, the dining alcove opens to a covered patio. The split floor plan ensures privacy, with the luxurious primary suite featuring a custom-tiled zero-entry walk-in shower, dual vanities with granite counters and illuminated mirrors, and a spacious walk-in closet that doubles as a concrete safe room. A secondary en-suite bedroom, an additional guest room/office, a stylish half bath, and a designer laundry room with custom cabinets and a folding counter complete the home’s refined layout. The oversized, insulated three-car garage includes sheetrock finish, automatic openers, and true zero-entry access for seamless convenience. HOA dues of $1,920 annually include trash service, lawn maintenance, and snow removal. A one-time $1,000 initiation fee applies. Looking to personalize your own luxury retreat? Custom builds are available through Steve Seiler Construction, LLC. Opportunities like this are rare—schedule your private tour today and experience the art of refined living at Dove Landing.
