Property Description

Set on approximately 4.8 acres and off a paved road, this charming two-story home offers room to spread out both inside and out. The home features 5 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, providing plenty of space for a variety of living arrangements. The main level offers comfortable living areas and a functional layout, while the additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, office space, hobbies, or other needs. Outside, the property offers an impressive collection of outbuildings, including a historic barn with an attached lean-to shed/stable and a silo, adding character and functionality while providing excellent space for livestock, storage, equipment, or hobby use. A detached 2-car garage provides additional parking and storage. With its combination of acreage, historic outbuildings, garage space, and a generously sized home, this property offers a unique opportunity to enjoy the space, character, and versatility of country living while remaining conveniently located near Lyons.
