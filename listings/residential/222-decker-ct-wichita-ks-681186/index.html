Welcome to this spacious 4+ bedroom, 3-bath home with an attached 3-car garage, located in the highly desirable Glen at Auburn Hills neighborhood and Goddard School District. Featuring comfortable living spaces, a finished basement, and a variety of outdoor amenities, this home offers plenty of room for everyday living, entertaining, and relaxation. Inside, you are greeted by the heart of the home, the living room, featuring a fireplace that adds warmth and character to the space. Just beyond, the kitchen and dining area come together in a functional layout with dedicated eating space and a dining alcove. The kitchen features laminate countertops, an island with an eating bar, and stainless steel appliances, including the dishwasher, range/oven, and refrigerator. A dedicated laundry room is conveniently located just off the kitchen. The main level includes 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. The primary suite features a walk-in closet and private en-suite bathroom with a double vanity, jetted tub, and walk-in shower. The second bathroom offers a tub/shower combination. Downstairs, the finished basement provides an inviting space for entertaining and relaxing, complete with a wet bar and spacious recreation area. This level also includes a fourth bedroom, a third bathroom, and an office that can accommodate a variety of uses, whether you need a workspace, hobby room, or additional flex space. Step outside to enjoy the deck and covered patio, offering options for outdoor gatherings and relaxation. The property is fully fenced, and a basketball hoop in the driveway provides an added opportunity for outdoor recreation. Residents can enjoy neighborhood amenities including a lake/pond, walking and jogging paths, a community pool, and playground facilities. The home is also conveniently located near Ziggy's Pizza, Walgreens, and Dillons.
