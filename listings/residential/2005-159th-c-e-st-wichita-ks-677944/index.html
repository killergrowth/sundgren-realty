Property Description

Situated in the coveted Sweetgrass at The Province community, this stunning Nies Homes 1½-story residence offers an exceptional blend of sophisticated living, thoughtful design, and everyday convenience. Enjoy Wichita taxes along with the highly regarded Andover School District, all while being minutes from the very best East Wichita has to offer. Positioned on a rare 0.57-acre East-facing lot, this beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, 6-bathroom home combines timeless architecture with elevated modern finishes and an effortless sense of style spread over 4,700 square feet. From the painted brick exterior and clean architectural lines to the crisp white interior, refined finishes, and soaring ceilings, every detail contributes to the home's warm and sophisticated appeal. The great room serves as an impressive focal point, featuring 12-foot ceilings, a dramatic fireplace framed by custom built-ins and picture lighting, and expansive windows overlooking the backyard. At the heart of the home, the gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, offering a substantial quartz island with seating, designer pendant lighting, double ovens, a built-in side-by-side refrigerator and freezer, gas range, striking quartz backsplash, glass-front display cabinetry, and a generous walk-in pantry. A dedicated drinks and coffee station featuring waterfall quartz houses an ice maker and beverage refrigerator, adding an exceptional level of convenience and sophistication to make entertaining effortless. The oversized dining area, surrounded by windows, opens directly to the covered patio, creating a seamless transition between indoor and outdoor living. The luxurious primary suite provides a private retreat with a raised ceiling and spa-inspired bath featuring an oversized tiled shower, expansive dual vanity, private water closet, and large walk-in closet with built-ins and vanity. Direct access from the closet to the laundry room adds a level of convenience that is both practical and luxurious. The main level also includes a private pocket office with built-in desk, a second en-suite bedroom, a dedicated drop zone conveniently located off the garage, and a powder bath. Upstairs, a private bedroom suite with its own full bath and walk-in closet provides flexible space for teenagers, guests, extended family, etc. The fully finished lower level extends the home's living and entertaining possibilities with high ceilings, a spacious family room, generous recreation area, and stylish wet bar complete with dishwasher and beverage cooler. Two additional bedrooms are thoughtfully positioned at opposite ends of the home for privacy. One features an en-suite bath, while the other is conveniently located near another full bath. An additional bonus room could easily function as a home gym, hobby space, office, or additional living area. The oversized four-car garage is another standout feature, providing ample room for vehicles, storage, and hobbies, along with a rarely found package delivery door for added convenience and security. Outdoors, the covered patio overlooks the expansive, fully fenced, manicured backyard and provides an inviting setting for morning coffee, outdoor dining, and entertaining. The East-facing orientation allows the backyard to bask in gentle morning sunlight, while the generous lot provides plenty of room for recreation, fun on the in-ground trampoline, and future enhancements. The sellers' future pool and landscaping renderings can be provided upon request. This remarkable property offers the feel of new construction without the wait. With its generous living spaces, elevated finishes, abundant natural light, exceptional entertaining areas, oversized four-car garage, and prime location, this home presents a rare opportunity to enjoy turnkey luxury living in one of East Wichita's most desirable communities.
