Property Description

Classic bungalow charm meets an impressive list of modern updates in this beautifully refreshed Lindsborg home! This property offers the character and warmth of an older home with the peace of mind that comes from knowing so much of the work has already been done. The entire interior has been freshly painted, along with a brand-new roof featuring impact-resistant shingles, a new heating and air system, and all-new windows. Exterior improvements include a new driveway, carport, sidewalks, and a large concrete back patio overlooking a spacious yard with mature shade trees and alley access. Inside, the main floor welcomes you with a grand entry, spacious living room, formal dining room featuring a built-in hutch, and kitchen with adjoining mudroom and convenient half bath. Gorgeous woodwork and hardwood floors throughout. The second floor offers three bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a wonderful sunroom filled with natural light and updated for year-round enjoyment. The basement has also received numerous updates and provides great storage and laundry space, with the opportunity to create additional finished living space if desired. Outside, the completely rebuilt front porch is ready for a porch swing or comfortable seating. From the major improvements to the thoughtful details, the investment in making this home truly move-in ready is evident throughout. Enjoy classic bungalow living just two blocks from downtown!
