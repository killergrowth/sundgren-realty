Property Description

Discover modern, accessibility-focused living at 9200 E Ofelia St in Valley Center, KS! This beautifully designed new construction home offers 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, an attached 3-car garage, and a spacious 0.46-acre lot. Thoughtfully planned with ADA accessibility in mind, this home emphasizes ease of movement, comfort, and functionality without compromising on style. The spacious open-concept floor plan provides generous room for mobility and everyday living, seamlessly connecting the living room, kitchen, and dining areas. The kitchen offers ample workspace and convenient access to the formal dining room. The oversized primary suite features a private en suite bathroom and generous space for added comfort and convenience. Three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms provide flexibility for a variety of living arrangements, including multigenerational living. Designed with long-term accessibility and independence in mind, this home is an excellent opportunity for buyers seeking a more accommodating living environment. Enjoy modern finishes, quality craftsmanship, and the convenience of brand-new construction on nearly half an acre in Valley Center. Experience a home where accessibility, comfort, and thoughtful design come together!
