About This Property

Who do you know that is looking for a spacious, character-filled home in one of Newton's most desirable neighborhoods? Welcome to 608 W 8th St! This impressive 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom bi-level home sits on a beautiful, wooded corner lot shaded by mature Sycamore trees, offering fantastic curb appeal and instant access to the nearby Sand Creek Bike Path. Inside, you will find over 2,600 square feet of expansive living space designed for comfortable family living and entertaining. The main level features a large, light-filled living room with a formal dining area, alongside a cozy family room complete with a wet bar. A functional kitchen area includes pull-out shelving, a breakfast nook, and a dedicated coffee bar space, offering a practical layout for daily meals. This floor plan provides great flexibility, with three large bedrooms on the upper level—including a private master suite—and a fully finished walk-out basement that features a large recreation room, a fourth bedroom, and an additional bathroom. On colder evenings, you can gather around either of the home's two warm, wood-burning fireplaces. Step outside to enjoy your private, fenced backyard from the covered patio, perfect for weekend BBQs. Complete with a two-car attached garage and an underground sprinkler system, this home truly has it all. Schedule your private tour today!
