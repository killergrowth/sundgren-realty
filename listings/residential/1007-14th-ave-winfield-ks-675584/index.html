About This Property

Thoughtfully restored with warmth, character, and comfort in mind, this charming three bedroom bungalow is the kind of home that simply feels good the moment you arrive. A welcoming front porch sets the tone, leading into light-filled living spaces where original hardwood floors, timeless details, and carefully chosen updates honor the home's character while making everyday living effortless. The beautifully updated kitchen features original painted cabinetry, butcher block countertops, a farmhouse sink, new appliances, and a custom breakfast nook designed for slow mornings. A newly renovated bathroom, convenient main-level laundry, updated plumbing, a brand new furnace, fresh interior and exterior paint, and refinished hardwood floors provide peace of mind without sacrificing the home's soul. Outside, you'll find an oversized lot with a spacious fenced backyard, deck and room to gather, garden or simply enjoy a quiet evening. The detached garage is large enough to house a workshop or extra storage. Nestled in an established neighborhood near Cherry Street and Alright Parks, this is more than a beautifully restored home - it's a place that truly feels like home
