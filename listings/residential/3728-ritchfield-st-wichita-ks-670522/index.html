Property Description

PRICE IMPROVEMENT! Welcome to easy, low-maintenance living in the Courtyards at Brookfield, located in the desirable Circle School District. From the moment you arrive, the home creates a charming and welcoming first impression, custom-built with a three-car garage with 8-foot-tall garage doors that include a storm shelter. Up front, you'll take in the beautiful exterior featuring a partial stone façade, well-maintained landscaping, and lush lawn kept nice and green by the sprinkler system. Inside, a welcoming foyer opens to the living room, and the main living spaces unfold with an impressive, open design that feels both elevated and inviting. The living room includes a stunning electric fireplace with custom built-in shelving that pairs perfectly with the flooring, all highlighted by expansive windows, and tray ceilings for an upscale touch. The kitchen is equally impressive, featuring stylish under-counter lighting, designer tile backsplash, quartz countertops, abundant cabinetry, large eating bar island, and built-in wall oven with cooktop for a clean, modern finish. The main floor continues with two bedrooms, two baths, and a bonus room/office area to suit your needs, plus a well-appointed laundry room with a wash sink and built-in storage. Step outside to enjoy a partially covered patio overlooking a spacious wooded private backyard, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Residents enjoy exceptional community amenities, including a clubhouse, exercise facility, jogging paths, a lake, a swimming pool, and pickleball courts. HOA dues cover exterior maintenance, lawn care, snow removal, trash service, and common area upkeep, delivering an effortless lifestyle with elevated style throughout. Schedule your private showing and hurry to see this gem today before it's gone! Seller is including the refrigerator, washer, and dryer with the purchase of home. The Courtyards at Brookfield is located South of 37th Street East between Greenwich and 127th Street East, inside the Circle School District.
