Property Description

Have you been dreaming about more space for your hobbies or business? Do you need more storage? This west side gem of a home is just what you've been looking for! Let's start at the front door. You're welcomed into a cozy living dining space with plenty of room for a drop zone for backpacks, shoes and coats. A very large dinning space is ready to host massive meals for your family and friends. The living room has access to the back yard and the kitchen has recently been remodeled with new countertops, fresh cabinet paint and appliances. Down the hall you'll find two bed rooms and the main floor bathroom. Head down stairs to find a complete master suite complete with another living space and massive master closet. Into the back yard you'll find a beautiful clean concrete patio leading to your new 30 X 50 shop. Your dreams are coming true. This is ideal for a woodworking shop, car shop, storage, play space or what every you can imagine! The lot is nearly half an acre so there is still so much yard space for outdoor enjoyment. It is hard to believe this home has so much and is tucked into a quite west Wichita neighborhood.
