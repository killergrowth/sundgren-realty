Property Description

Start the morning with coffee at the granite island and sunlight coming through the living room windows. Walk out the zero-entry door onto the covered patio, and the 6' privacy fence gives you a flat backyard that’s all yours. This 2022 patio home in Casa Bella has 1,450 sq ft with three bedrooms and two baths. The primary suite sits on its own side behind a barn door, with a dual-sink vanity and a walk-in closet with built-in shelving. The kitchen has a walk-in pantry, white cabinets and stainless appliances. A drop zone by the garage door and a main-floor laundry room keep the day organized, and the refrigerator, washer and dryer stay. The 3-car garage includes a tornado shelter. The neighborhood has a community pond, and this home sits on a cul-de-sac. Call today for your private tour.
