Property Description

Luxury, thoughtful design, and exceptional updates come together in this stunning home situated on 1.2 acres. At the heart of the home is a brand-new kitchen by SAW Creations, beautifully appointed with soapstone countertops, a La Cornue range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, and Sub-Zero beverage refrigerator. Abundant natural light, new flooring, and upgraded light fixtures throughout create a bright and inviting atmosphere. Multiple living spaces on the main floor plus formal dining and a breakfast nook. Offering five bedrooms, the home features two bedrooms on the main level and three upstairs. The impressive upstairs primary suite is a true retreat with a luxurious steam shower, separate soaking tub, and massive hidden walk-in closet. The main floor also includes a full guest bathroom plus a cleverly hidden half bath you won't want to miss! Outdoor living is just as impressive, with multiple seating and entertaining areas throughout the property. Two upper-level outdoor spaces feature low-maintenance turf, providing beautiful spots to relax and enjoy the surroundings. Additional upgrades include a premium DaVinci Providence Slate roof, a sprinkler system connected to a private well for watering, a whole-house generator serving both the home and garage, built in year round holiday lighting and an RV power hookup. With high-end finishes, extensive updates, incredible indoor and outdoor living spaces, and 1.2 acres to enjoy, this property offers a combination of luxury, functionality, and space that's difficult to find.
