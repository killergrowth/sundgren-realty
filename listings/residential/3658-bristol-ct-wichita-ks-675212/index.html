Property Description

This brand new Nies home is located in the highly sought-after Brookfield neighborhood. A stunning home that is the Monarch floor plan by Nies Homes and offers an impressive 3,335 square feet of beautifully designed living space, including 1,893 square feet on the main level and 1,442 square feet of finished basement space. The modern design is filled with natural light, highlighted by expansive floor-to-ceiling windows throughout many of the rooms. The decor and lighting is exceptional! Landscaping is already completed, this home is truly move-in ready and waiting for its next owner.
