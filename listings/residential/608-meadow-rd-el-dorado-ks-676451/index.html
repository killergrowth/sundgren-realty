Property Description

Welcome home to 608 E. Meadow Rd! If you have been looking for a great home in a great location look no further. This 3 bedroom, 2 bath home offers numerous updates both inside and out. Greeting you as you enter the front door is the formal dining room, kitchen and living room. Recent flooring and a fabulous kitchen, with new backsplash, gives this open floor plan the perfect setting for family gatherings. Also located on the main level is the master bedroom with master bath, with upstairs laundry hook ups, and two additional bedrooms and a recently remodeled bathroom. The basement remodel offers a large family room with new fireplace and a fabulous epoxy floor. The extra bonus room has many options and could make a great office. The extra-large mechanical/storage room provides plenty of space with another laundry hook up. A lovely back yard and attached garage with RV parking make this the complete home. Recent improvements that add security to your new home include new heating and cooling, new water line from meter to house and new sewer line repairs. Take the time to see this wonderful home in person! Book your showing today!
