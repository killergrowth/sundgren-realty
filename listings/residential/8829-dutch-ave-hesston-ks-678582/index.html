Property Description

A Country Setting You'll Never Want to Leave. Properties like this rarely come along. 13 beautiful wooded acres with a creek meandering alongside the property, mature trees providing incredible shade, and a blacktop road leading you home. Located in the Hesston school district, this one-owner custom home offers the privacy and beauty of country living with the conveniences you want. The property is designed to be enjoyed outdoors. Winding trails, bridges, pergolas, covered patios, a screened porch, and a thoughtfully designed fire pit create spaces to gather, relax, garden, or simply enjoy the sounds of nature. Beautiful perennials and extensive xeriscaping feature native, drought-tolerant Kansas plants that blend naturally into the landscape. Inside, the home has been lovingly maintained and thoughtfully updated over the years. Hardwood floors, replacement Andersen windows, and an updated kitchen with quartz countertops, new cabinetry, large island, pantry, newer stainless appliances, pull-out storage, and a charming bay window with cushioned seat, create a comfortable and inviting space. The kitchen opens to the family room with a stone gas fireplace, while French doors lead to a stone patio and pergola. The formal living room offers another gas fireplace and opens to a spacious screened porch, one of those spaces that makes you want to linger on a summer evening. A formal dining room with built-in cabinetry, beautiful entry with open staircase, main-floor laundry, and guest half bath complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a private full bath, along with three additional bedrooms and a shared hall bath. The unfinished basement offers tremendous potential with four egress windows and multiple possibilities for future living space. And then there's the 30 x 40 heated and cooled outbuilding. A true bonus for anyone needing workspace, storage, hobbies, or entertaining space. It features three garage bays and a full bath. CLASS IV ROOF. Rural water, exceptional outdoor living, mature woods, a creek, custom craftsmanship, and room to breathe—this is the kind of property that feels like home the moment you arrive.
