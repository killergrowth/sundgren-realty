About This Property

Welcome home to this beautifully updated brick ranch nestled in one of Hillsboro's most desirable neighborhoods! From the moment you arrive, you will appreciate the fresh exterior updates, including a brand new roof, new guttering and fresh paint, giving this home exceptional curb appeal and peace of mind. Step inside to discover a spacious living room and dining room featuring newer wood flooring and a lovely wood burning fireplace that creates a warm, welcoming atmosphere for everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen is both stylish and functional with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances and a convenient eat in bar, making it the perfect gathering place for family and friends. Just off the kitchen, you will fall in love with the inviting den, where a charming fireplace, abundant natural light and picturesque views create the perfect place to relax. This home offers 4 bedrooms plus a dedicated office that could easily serve as a fifth bedroom. The office features built in bookcases and connects to one of the home's most unique spaces, a delightful greenhouse room surrounded by windows, complete with its own wash sink and built in watering faucet, making it a dream for plant lovers or hobby gardeners. The spacious primary suite has been tastefully updated with luxury vinyl flooring and a beautifully remodeled ensuite bath featuring a walk in shower, soaking tub and elegant Onyx vanity. A main floor laundry adds everyday convenience. Downstairs, you will find even more living space with a large family room, a versatile hobby room and plenty of storage for all your needs. Step outside to enjoy the beautifully maintained yard, kept lush with an irrigation well and underground sprinkler system. The location is just as impressive. You will enjoy peaceful surroundings while being within walking distance of the golf course, city parks and the community swimming pool. If you have been searching for a move in ready home that combines quality updates, generous living space and a fantastic location, this Hillsboro gem is one you do not want to miss.
