Property Description

Welcome to 801 S. Lightner, a beautifully maintained brick ranch situated on a desirable corner lot offering timeless curb appeal and thoughtful updates throughout. Whether you're purchasing your very first home, downsizing, or simply looking for a move-in ready property in a convenient east Wichita location, this home checks all the boxes. Offering 3 spacious bedrooms, 1 beautifully updated bathroom, and an oversized detached 2-car garage, this home is ready for its next chapter. Step inside to discover warm bamboo wood flooring flowing through the living room and hallway, complemented by fresh neutral paint that creates a bright and inviting atmosphere. The cozy living room features custom built-ins that provide both character and practical storage, while the seller has painted both the built-ins and kitchen cabinetry to give the home a fresh, updated feel. The updated kitchen offers attractive tile flooring, a decorative tile backsplash, abundant cabinet space, and all kitchen appliances remain with the home, making moving day that much easier. A separate laundry room provides additional convenience and functionality. All three bedrooms offer comfortable neutral carpeting, while the updated bathroom showcases beautiful tile surrounding the tub and shower. The HVAC system and water heater were both replaced in 2018, providing added peace of mind for the next owner. Step outside to enjoy the fully fenced backyard, where the seller added a privacy fence to create the perfect place for entertaining, gardening, letting pets play, or simply relaxing after a long day. Don't miss the charming herb planter out front that adds both curb appeal and a welcoming touch. The oversized detached garage provides excellent space for parking, storage, hobbies, or a workshop. Conveniently located just minutes from Kellogg, this home provides quick access throughout Wichita while placing you close to Towne East Square, Costco, and all the shopping, dining, and entertainment along Rock Road. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate nearby Schweiter Park with its playgrounds, tennis courts, basketball courts, picnic shelters, and ball fields, while Boston Park offers a recreation center, splash pad, playground, and additional green space for family fun. Combining classic brick charm, tasteful updates, newer mechanicals, a fantastic backyard, and an exceptionally convenient location, this move-in ready home offers an outstanding opportunity to enjoy affordable homeownership.
