Property Description

331 N 127th St E is an exciting condominium development entering a new chapter as plans move forward into Phase 2. This growing community offers an excellent opportunity for buyers seeking spacious, low-maintenance living in a desirable east Wichita location. Phase 1 is currently available, with no special assessments. Annual HOA dues are $3540 and cover the entire exterior of the units, providing homeowners with added convenience and peace of mind. All units feature LVP, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, stylish finishes, and 2-car garages. With Phase 2 moving forward, this development is coming to life again and offering an exciting opportunity to join a growing Townhome community with a new outdoor pavilion/gazebo.
