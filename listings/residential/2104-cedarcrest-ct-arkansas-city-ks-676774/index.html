Property Description

If you've been dreaming of a place where evenings feel a little quieter, neighbors still wave, and home is more than just a house, this inviting brick ranch is one you won't want to miss. Nestled on a quiet dead-end street with minimal through traffic, this home offers the peaceful setting that's becoming harder to find, all on nearly a 1-acre lot. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home features 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, two spacious living areas, a large sunroom, and a finished basement complete with an oversized family/game room, full bathroom, and abundant storage. The spacious primary suite is conveniently located on the main level, while three additional bedrooms provide room for family, guests, or a home office. The heart of the home is the open kitchen, complete with an island, breakfast bar, built-in desk, dining area, formal dining room, and all kitchen appliances included. Whether you're hosting holiday dinners, cheering on your favorite team, or enjoying a quiet evening at home, this home offers space for every season of life. Step outside and enjoy morning coffee on the covered front porch, relaxing evenings on the covered back porch, or time spent in the privacy-fenced backyard. A 2-car attached garage and detached garage/shop provide ample space for vehicles, hobbies, and extra storage. Warm, welcoming, and filled with the kind of charm that makes a house feel like home, this is a property you'll simply have to experience in person. Schedule your private showing today!
