Property Description

This standout home has so much to offer! The 20x40 saltwater pool and a climate-controlled 24x30 detached shop are two standout features of this east Wichita property. With 5 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and more than 4,300 square feet, there's plenty of room inside too. Vaulted ceilings and a sweeping staircase greet you at the entry. The updated kitchen features custom cabinetry and quartz countertops that overlooks a light-filled hearth room and cozy fireplace. Check out the garden room with multiple patio doors, perfect for enjoying morning coffee or a quiet space to read a book. Upstairs, the spacious bedrooms include a very unique, versatile one above the garage. The primary bath has a jetted tub and quartz counters. The basement has its own bedroom, bathroom, and kitchenette, making it a comfortable setup for overnight guests or extended stays. Outside, two covered patios and a climate-controlled pool house with room for games gives everyone a place to gather. The pool features an automatic cover, underwater lighting, diving board, and built-in bench seating. The detached shop is fully insulated and has its own electrical panel. There's even a chicken coop ready to go in the backyard! An oversized driveway and side parking area offers room for an RV or boat. Newer windows on backside of home. There is also a class 4 impact-resistant roof that is only one year old, along with all new gutters. Did I mention no HOA?! You'll see plenty of deer and wild turkeys in the open field out front and plenty of sunsets out back. This home has something for everyone and is ready for its next owner!
