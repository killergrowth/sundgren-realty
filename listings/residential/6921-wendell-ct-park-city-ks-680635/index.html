Property Description

Pond Views on a Quiet Park City Cul-de-Sac Welcome home to this spacious 5 bedroom, 3 bathroom home tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac in Park City. The open floor plan centers on a living room with a cozy fireplace, and from the deck you can enjoy a relaxing pond view with your morning coffee or evening sunset. Fresh new carpet throughout means the home is truly move-in ready. The main level offers three bedrooms and two bathrooms, including a large primary suite with an updated primary bath, along with convenient main floor laundry. Downstairs, the view out basement features a large finished family room, two more bedrooms, and a full bathroom, perfect for guests or extra living space. Outside, a sprinkler system keeps the lawn green with ease, and the backyard is fully enclosed by a wood privacy fence, ideal for pets, play, and entertaining. Peace of mind comes standard with a roof that’s only 2½ years old. All appliances stay, including the washer and dryer. With just over $5,000 remaining in specials, this one offers great value.
