Property Description

Looking for space! This 5 bedroom 3 bath ranch home is located in Casa Bella! This home has granite surfaces throughout, vaulted ceilings, wood lam & tile flooring on the main floor except in the bedrooms, painted trim, covered deck, Master suite has a garden tub and shower and dual vanities, with the laundry connected to the master bedroom! Entertaining space in the basement with a wet bar and room for a full size fridge. Plenty of space, with a 3 car garage. There is also a large bonus room downstairs, great for a workout room or office!! Close proximity to schools and just around the corner from the pool/playground area just add to the list of nice amenities!!
