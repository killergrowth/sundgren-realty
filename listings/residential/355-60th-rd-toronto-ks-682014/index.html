Property Description

Experience the best of country living on 19.5 fully fenced acres with this beautifully updated farmhouse offering the perfect blend of modern convenience and rural lifestyle. With 5 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and extensive updates completed in 2018, this property is move-in ready and built for years of enjoyment. The spacious, updated kitchen features solid surface countertops, ample cabinetry, and a large walk-in pantry, making it ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The main floor also includes a full bath for guests and an oversized laundry/mud room designed to handle the demands of country life. Major improvements include all new electrical wiring, PEX plumbing, metal roof, windows, doors, and HVAC system in 2018, plus a new water heater in 2024. Step outside and discover a property that's ready for livestock, gardening, recreation, and outdoor adventure. Amenities include a food plot, areas of timber, a small greenhouse, fenced garden area, chicken coop, 2-car detached garage, and carport. Whether you're looking to start a hobby farm, homestead, or simply enjoy wide-open spaces, this acreage offers endless possibilities. Outdoor enthusiasts will especially appreciate the location. Just minutes from Toronto Lake and Woodson State Fishing Lake, this property offers convenient access to fishing, boating, wildlife viewing, and other outdoor recreation. With its mix of timber, open ground, food plot, and nearby public hunting opportunities, it's a property that will appeal to sportsmen, hunters, and fishermen alike. If you've been searching for an updated country home with acreage, practical improvements, and exceptional recreational opportunities nearby, this one deserves a look.
