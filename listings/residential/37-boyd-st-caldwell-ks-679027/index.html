Property Description

Space, privacy, and small-town convenience come together in a way that is hard to find in Caldwell! Situated on a generous 0.68-acre lot within the city limits, this 2,565-square-foot, 1 1/2-story home offers the feel of country living with the convenience of being in town. With a fully fenced yard, mature trees, multiple outbuildings, and an impressive 30x40 heated and cooled workshop, there is room here to spread out, pursue your hobbies, store your extras, and enjoy the kind of outdoor space that is increasingly hard to find. Inside, the home offers a comfortable open-concept layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen opens to the dining area and features a center island that creates a natural gathering spot. Enjoy having loads of cabinets and countertop space for meal prep as well as water filtration which provides reverse osmosis drinking water. Wood laminate flooring flows throughout the space, while fresh interior paint gives the home a clean, refreshed feel. The main floor includes a spacious bedroom and a large, updated bathroom with double sinks and a tub/shower combination. Upstairs, two additional good-sized bedrooms, plus a bonus room, and a second full bathroom provide plenty of flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. The real surprise continues outside. The oversized 30x40 detached shop features heating, cooling, garage door openers, and plenty of room for a serious workshop, projects, equipment, or storage, with alley access for added convenience. A 1920 barn offers even more storage along with additional one-car garage parking and electricity. You’ll also find a double detached carport, a storage container with electricity, and a back patio, all surrounded by a privacy-fenced yard where mature trees create a peaceful setting. An insurance claim has been filed, and the roof is scheduled to be replaced, giving the next owner the benefit of a brand-new roof and added peace of mind. Conveniently located near the city pool and Sumner County Fairgrounds and within the Caldwell school district, this property offers an exceptional combination of space, functionality, privacy, and value. If you have been looking for a home where there is truly room for everything, this one deserves a look.
