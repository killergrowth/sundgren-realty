Property Description

Space inside and out on more than half an acre! This 3 bedroom, 2 bath ranch sits on a 0.55-acre corner lot in Belle Plaine with 1,612 square feet and an oversized attached garage with room for four or more vehicles, open rafters, and built-in shelving, giving you plenty of space for a workshop, hobbies, or extra storage. Inside, the bright living room flows into a kitchen with an abundance of wood cabinetry, a pantry, a new dishwasher, and room for a table, along with a separate dining room. A large paneled family room adds more gathering space, and one of the three bedrooms features built-in shelving. One full bathroom has a tub/shower combo and the other a walk-in shower. Every room but one has a ceiling fan. A laundry room and a separate storage room keep things organized, and a cellar adds even more storage. Outside, enjoy the patio and a wide-open yard with mature trees. Schedule your showing today!
