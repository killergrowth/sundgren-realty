Property Description

Welcome to 7720 W Shadow Lakes, a beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offering 2,366 square feet of finished living space in the desirable Forest Lakes neighborhood! Situated on a beautiful lake lot with water access, this home features peaceful water views, mature trees, and an inviting setting from the moment you arrive. Step inside to find a welcoming formal living and dining area, perfect for entertaining. Continue down the hall to the spacious hearth room that flows seamlessly into the kitchen, featuring a large island and plenty of room to gather. Just off the kitchen and hearth room, a private patio provides the perfect retreat for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or relaxing with friends and family. The main level offers two bedrooms and two bathrooms, including a spacious primary suite with beautiful lake views, a large ensuite bathroom with double sinks, a tub, separate shower, and walk-in closet. The finished basement provides even more room to spread out with a generous recreation room, newly added wet bar, third bedroom, and full bathroom. An additional unfinished room offers excellent potential for a future nonconforming bedroom, home office, fitness room, or bonus space. Thoughtfully updated with today's design trends in mind, this home blends modern finishes with timeless appeal. Extensive improvements within the past few years include 16 new windows, all-new main-level flooring, a new roof, gutters and downspouts, fresh exterior paint, beautifully updated landscaping, a new patio and landscape wall, all-new main-level doors, trim, and hardware, a new front storm door, and fresh interior paint throughout. These carefully selected updates create a fresh, cohesive feel while maintaining the warmth and character of the home. The property also features a sprinkler system and irrigation well, making it easy to maintain the beautiful landscaping and lawn. Located in the desirable Maize school district, this home is conveniently situated near the neighborhood pool, NewMarket Square shopping and dining, and easy access to K-96. Golf enthusiasts will love being just a golf cart ride away from Reflection Ridge Golf Course! With its beautiful lake setting, thoughtfully selected updates, inviting living spaces, and convenient location, this Forest Lakes home offers a wonderful blend of modern style, comfort, and functionality.
