Property Description

Nestled in Wichita's established Willowbend neighborhood, this move-in-ready home welcomes you with an abundance of natural light, comfortable living spaces, and a feeling that makes you want to settle in and stay awhile. Every space reflects the care that's gone into maintaining this property, allowing you to simply move in and start enjoying all it has to offer. And with a back patio overlooking a portion of the Willowbend golf course, the appeal extends well beyond the walls of the home. It's a beautiful combination of a home that's ready to enjoy and a neighborhood you'll love being part of. Stepping through the front door, you'll find a welcoming kitchen with space for a breakfast table, making everyday meals and morning coffee feel right at home. From there, the generously sized living room takes center stage, with a beautiful fireplace serving as the focal point and an abundance of natural light pouring in through the windows. An additional dining area offers plenty of room for a larger table, perfect for hosting family and friends or gathering around for special occasions. Just beyond, the screened-in patio serves as a wonderful extension of these gathering spaces, bringing the outdoors a little closer while offering a comfortable place to relax, entertain, or simply enjoy the surroundings. The main level also features a second bedroom and full bathroom, along with an incredibly spacious primary bedroom that offers a comfortable retreat of its own. One especially convenient feature is the direct access from the primary bedroom to the backyard, making it easy to step outside and enjoy the patio whenever you'd like. Completing the main floor is a dedicated laundry room conveniently located just off the garage, adding another thoughtful touch to a home designed for comfortable, everyday living. Heading downstairs, you'll find even more to love, starting with a generously sized family room that manages to feel both spacious and cozy. A second fireplace adds warmth and character, creating the perfect setting for a weekend of binge-watching your favorite shows, hosting friends for game day, or simply settling in for a relaxing evening. Two exceptionally large bedrooms and an additional bathroom provide plenty of comfortable living space, but there's one more surprise waiting! Tucked away on this level is a versatile bonus room that could easily become a home office, playroom, workout space, or whatever best fits your lifestyle. It's another example of how this home offers the space and flexibility to truly make it your own. And then there's the backyard, a space that might just become your favorite part of calling this place home. Between the patio, deck, and screened-in porch, there are plenty of ways to enjoy the outdoors while taking in views of a portion of the Willowbend golf course. Picture slow mornings with a cup of coffee, evenings spent unwinding after a long day, or gathering with friends to enjoy the beautiful surroundings. It's an outdoor setting that invites you to spend more time enjoying life and less time worrying about what needs to be done. Because here, it's not about endless upkeep or a never-ending to-do list. It's about less maintenance and a whole lot more living! A home this well cared for, in a neighborhood as inviting as Willowbend, is something worth getting excited about. From the natural light that fills the living spaces to the generous rooms, thoughtful layout, and outdoor areas overlooking the golf course, 3940 N Sweet Bay offers so many reasons to love where you live. And perhaps the best part? It's ready for you to move right in and start enjoying it all. Take a walk through the neighborhood, spend an evening on the patio, invite your favorite people over, or simply enjoy the comfort of being home. This is more than a beautiful, move-in-ready property. It's an opportunity to settle into a wonderful community, enjoy your surroundings, and spend more time doing the things you love.
