Welcome to this unique 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath home featuring an attached 1-car garage and distinctive mid-century details throughout with substantial family room and basement addition. Per the seller, this home has had just one owner since 1957! The formal living room opens into the kitchen, with additional dining space conveniently located nearby. The kitchen features tile countertops, and the dishwasher, microwave, range/oven, and refrigerator all transfer with the property. The home also features several notable improvements, including a Class 4 impact-resistant roof installed in 2016, a garbage disposal added in January 2017, a hot water tank installed in 2010, and a garage door opener installed in 2008. The KitchenAid dishwasher was installed in 2008 as well. Just beyond the formal living room and kitchen, you'll find a spacious family room addition featuring vaulted ceilings, grass cloth wallpaper, and a freestanding mid-century modern wood-burning fireplace. This inviting space opens into a beautiful sunroom with floor-to-ceiling windows and a skylight, bringing in plenty of natural light. The sunroom provides access to the brick paver patio, creating a great connection between indoor and outdoor living. The living space includes two bedrooms, with the primary bedroom offering two closets and a decorative glass panel. The full bathroom features a walk-in shower and stained-glass windows, adding to the home's character. At the front of the home, a mudroom leads to the laundry room and half bathroom. The washer and dryer transfer with the property. Downstairs, the basement offers a rec room and an additional finished room that could serve as a workspace, hobby room, or extra storage that was previously used as a photography dark room. Outside, the fully fenced backyard features a small workshop complete with stained-glass windows and skylights. With its one-owner history, mid-century charm, and distinctive architectural details, this home is truly one of a kind and offers an opportunity you won't want to miss!
