Property Description

ONE-OF-A-KIND SOUTHWESTERN RETREAT — AN ENTERTAINER'S DREAM & HOBBYIST'S PARADISE! Located in the Valley Center School District, this unique 3-bedroom, 2-bath, 1,645 sq. ft. manufactured home offers an incredible combination of Southwestern charm, privacy, and exceptional amenities! Situated on .73 acres the property offers a spacious setting surrounded by mature trees and NO HOA! Step inside and discover the warmth and character the sellers have thoughtfully created! Beautiful pine wood floors flow through the living and dining areas, while a stunning stucco fireplace with matching built-ins and an authentic Saltillo tile hearth creates an inviting focal point. The spacious dining area features an impressive oversized built-in china cabinet, while the kitchen offers a convenient pantry and eating bar. The primary suite includes a private bath with double sinks and TWO walk-in closets! Bedroom two features a built-in desk and counter, while bedroom three has been used as a music studio. The sellers are offering an allowance toward converting this space back into a traditional bedroom. Major improvements include a roof that's approximately 2 years old, new vinyl siding, a high-efficiency HVAC system with new ductwork, and additional tie-downs. All refrigerators, freezers, washer, and dryer remain! FOR THE HOBBYIST, WOODWORKER, OR CAR ENTHUSIAST — THIS PROPERTY DELIVERS! A four-car carport sits in front of a two-car garage with an attached 16' x 22' workshop featuring a wood-burning stove, window A/C unit, extensive built-in cabinetry, workbenches, and shelving. Whether you enjoy woodworking, restoring vehicles, or simply need space for projects, you'll appreciate the versatility! Additional amenities include a 7' x 7' paint storage room, covered storage for lawn equipment, a 10' x 20' storage shed with refrigerator, and another approximately 20' x 5'8" storage area. Electrical features include 200-amp service at the garage and 100-amp service to the workshop and home. AND THE OUTDOOR ENTERTAINING SPACE IS TRULY SOMETHING SPECIAL! Imagine hosting gatherings, cookouts, and celebrations in your very own approximately 20' x 30' covered BBQ pavilion! Complete with a roof-mounted exhaust fan, bar seating on two sides, and an adjoining prep and storage area featuring cabinets, a sink, refrigerators, and freezers, this space was designed for entertaining! TWO separate outdoor bathrooms provide added convenience for guests. A large covered buffet-style counter connects the entertaining area to another storage building, creating an ideal setup for serving food and hosting gatherings. Nearby, an expansive shaded paver patio offers another wonderful outdoor dining area, while the huge concrete patio, just one year old, provides even more room to relax and enjoy. Mature trees and a wonderfully private setting make this property feel like your own personal retreat. An in-ground storm shelter adds peace of mind, and with NO HOA, you'll appreciate the freedom to enjoy your property. THIS ISN'T JUST A HOME — IT'S A PLACE TO ENTERTAIN, CREATE, PURSUE YOUR HOBBIES, AND MAKE LASTING MEMORIES! With so many unique features and possibilities, this is truly a property you need to experience in person! Manufactured home is not on a permanent foundation.
