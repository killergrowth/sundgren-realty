Property Description

Welcome home to this well-designed ranch offering comfortable living, an open floor plan, and a setting at the end of a quiet, non-through street. The main floor features two bedrooms and two full baths, including a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet and an ensuite bath complete with dual sinks, separate jetted tub, shower, and private water closet. The vaulted living room creates an inviting gathering space, while the open kitchen offers granite countertops, an island, eating bar, and abundant storage. A separate breakfast nook and full-size dining area provide plenty of options for everyday meals and entertaining. Thoughtfully designed, the layout offers an open feel while maintaining just enough separation to keep the kitchen from being fully visible upon entry. Main-floor laundry is conveniently located in a separate room with a utility sink. The view-out basement expands the living space with a generously sized family room, two additional bedrooms, a full bath, ample storage, and a 9x5 finished bonus space ideal for a small office, craft area, or additional organized storage. Outside, enjoy the fenced backyard with its lush lawn and covered deck, perfect for relaxing or outdoor gatherings. Additional highlights include a brand new electric panel with whole house surge protector, Class IV roof installed in 2022, a new A/C unit in 2026 with an upgraded air handler, an insulated garage, and freshly repainted exterior. Located in the Mulvane school district with convenient access to Derby and McConnell Air Force Base, this home offers a wonderful combination of space, thoughtful features, and a lovely setting.
