Property Description

Move-in ready and in a great northeast Wichita location, this 3 bedroom 2 bath charming brick ranch is quite the SHOCKER and we mean that in the best way. Just a quick walk from Wichita State University, and within steps of Mueller Aerospace and Engineering Discovery Magnet Elementary and the Woodard Recreation Center, this home combines location and convenience with exceptional pride of ownership. Inside, you'll find beautiful hardwood floors in impeccable condition throughout the living room, dining room, and both main-floor bedrooms. The well-appointed hall bath features tile flooring, while the kitchen offers updated appliances that remain with the home. Fresh interior and exterior paint mean there's nothing left to do but move in and enjoy. The finished lower level expands your living space with tile flooring throughout, here you will enjoy a spacious family and recreation room, a large third bedroom with an egress window, second full bathroom, and laundry room. Out back, you'll love the inviting three-season sunroom overlooking the fully fenced backyard perfect for morning coffee, relaxing evenings, or entertaining friends. A covered carport adds convenience and protection from the elements. With its outstanding condition, thoughtful updates, and fantastic location near WSU, shopping, parks, and quick access to the rest of Wichita, this home truly checks every box. Don't miss your chance to make this northeast Wichita gem your own.
