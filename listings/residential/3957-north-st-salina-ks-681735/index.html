Property Description

Spend summer afternoons by the pool, then watch the sun go down from the hot tub on the covered patio. Just minutes east of Salina, this 44-acre property pairs approximately 5,500 square feet of living space with a 20-by-40 foot in-ground pool, a 30-by-50 foot seven-stall block barn and a separate 27-by-50 foot pole shed. There is room for horses, equipment and whatever projects you have been waiting to tackle. The kitchen is built for hosting with two prep islands, white cabinetry, a walk-in pantry and a built-in glass-front hutch. The adjoining dining room has a vaulted wood-beamed ceiling and a fireplace, and sliding doors open right onto the covered patio. Two main-floor living rooms and two basement recreation areas give everyone room to gather or spread out. The main floor primary suite has its own fireplace and a vaulted, beamed ceiling. The en suite bath features a soaking tub, two sinks, a walk-in tiled shower and private laundry. There is an additional bedroom with attached bath on this level. Upstairs, two nice-sized bedrooms offer generous closet space, and a third loft-style bedroom has an adjoining area for games, play or a home office. The home was remodeled in 2017, the primary suite was added in 2022 and the metal roof is newer. If you have been looking for room for horses, a backyard made for summer, and an easy drive into Salina, message me for details to schedule a private showing.
