Property Description

New listing in North Newton, KS! This home has just about everything a family could want in a home. It features over 3300 SF in main floor and basement living areas. it has 5 total bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, a beautiful kitchen with granite counters, undercabinet lighting, beautiful gas range, and pantry. The basement has a massive family room with 'heated flooring', 2 bedrooms, one bath, an additional finished room for storage or an office area. Amazing to see! The oversized garage features 2 insulated doors, 2 garage door openers, and attached is an additional 900 SF storage / workshop area. This is amazing as well! This area features bright lighting, lots of storage, a full bathroom, A /C, and also a 'heated floor.' Nothing like it in Newton. On the exterior, there is a 224 SF maintenance free composite deck, a sprinkler system, and water well as a time and money saving feature. This home is a great value, be sure to take the time to view!
