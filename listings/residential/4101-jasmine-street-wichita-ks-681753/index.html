Property Description

SELLERS ARE OFFERING A 2/1 INTEREST RATE BUY DOWN WITH AN ACCEPTABLE OFFER- an exceptional opportunity to enjoy lower initial monthly mortgage payments while making this extraordinary home yours (subject to buyer qualification and lender approval). Perfectly positioned along the 11th hole of Willowbend Golf Course, this pristine sprawling ranch offers breathtaking panoramic golf course and water views. Thoughtfully designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home features zoned HVAC, a security system, and sun-soaked living spaces filled with expansive windows, recessed lighting, decorative art niches, and quality finishes throughout. Refinished hardwood floors and plush carpeting in excellent condition add to the home's impeccable appeal. The inviting living room flows into a formal dining room highlighted by a chandelier and coffered ceiling. The beautifully updated kitchen showcases granite countertops, tile backsplash, a center island, breakfast bar, under-cabinet lighting, walk-in pantry, and informal dining area. The dishwasher and oven are both less than five years old. Just beyond, the hearth room offers a cozy fireplace and built-in desk, creating the perfect gathering space. The spacious main-floor primary suite serves as a private retreat, with large windows framing spectacular views, direct access to the deck, a trayed ceiling with ceiling fan, custom walk-in closet, and luxurious ensuite featuring dual granite vanities, whirlpool tub, and separate shower. An adjoining sitting room with French doors provides endless opportunities as an office, reading room, or a nursery. A second main-floor bedroom features an ensuite bath, while a convenient powder room accommodates guests. Entertaining is effortless in the finished walk-out basement with a generous family room with a wet bar, large windows, a game room (pool table stays with the home), two spacious bedrooms, a full bathroom, bonus room, studio with built-in desk and closet, and a hidden secret room. Outdoor living is equally impressive, with a deck featuring newer deck boards, a retractable electric awning, and a convenient gas line for grilling. The covered lower patio offers motorized screens that can be lowered at the touch of a button for added privacy and protection from the elements. Both spaces provide wonderful vantage points for enjoying the sweeping fairway and water views. This exceptional home combines timeless quality, meticulous care, thoughtful updates, and a resort-style setting just minutes from major highways, shopping, dining, and Koch Industries.
