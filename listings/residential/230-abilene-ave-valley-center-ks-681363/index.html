Property Description

Beautiful brick and cedar ranch with lovely landscaping and inviting curb appeal welcomes you! In the heart of Valley Center, just two blocks from the elementary school and located in the highly sought-after Valley Center School District. Inside, you'll find beautiful wood floors, a formal living room and dining room, and a spacious kitchen with plenty of storage and all appliances included. The darling breakfast nook is a favorite spot, featuring built-in display shelves and a triple window that welcomes in the morning sunshine. The wonderful main-floor family room is full of character, with vaulted cedar ceilings and walls, plus a cozy wood-burning fireplace that makes this space feel warm and inviting. Three bedrooms are conveniently located on the main floor, including a primary bedroom with double closets and a bath. The finished basement adds even more room to spread out, with a large recreation room and a separate game room including a pool table. Outside, enjoy the beautifully landscaped and fully fenced backyard, complete with a screened-in porch—perfect for relaxing or entertaining. An irrigation well and sprinkler system help keep everything looking its best. The large backyard shed features an overhead door and convenient alley access, ideal for hobbies, storage, or workshop space. Additional features include a NEW ROOF, a deep single-car garage, and a two-car driveway pad. Character, comfort, and a wonderful location—this Valley Center home is ready to welcome its next owner!
