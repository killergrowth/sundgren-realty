Property Description

Charming All-Brick City Homestead. Welcome to your dream "in the city homestead!" This enduring, all-brick home is built on an expansive .84-acre lot, offering an elite level of privacy, efficiency, and self-sufficient living right in town. Property Highlights: Built to Last, masterfully constructed with timeless quality, passive design principles that naturally manage temperature swings against all Kansas weather. Recent Updates: Brand-new Class 3 impact-resistant roof installed across the main house, detached garage, and shop (November 2025). Massive Workshop. Features a heavy-duty 26x60 wood-frame shop with an office area. Side concrete driveway pad makes turning around easy, and abundant parking for your trucks, RV, or projects. Interior Comforts: Step inside a solid, quiet sanctuary completely insulated from the outside world. The layout is optimized for large family gatherings. Picture cozy winters next to a wood-burning fireplace or taking in the seasons through the large front picture window and closed in sun porch. Backyard Paradise The expansive backyard is a gardener's paradise, designed for a sustainable lifestyle. It comes complete with an established asparagus patch and blueberry bushes. Mature peach and cherry trees and a beautiful custom herb spiral. Plenty of open space for kids to run free all day and still raise backyard chickens. This unique property seamlessly combines robust craftsmanship with the serenity of country-style living. Schedule your private tour today!
