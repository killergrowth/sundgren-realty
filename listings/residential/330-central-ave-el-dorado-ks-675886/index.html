Property Description

Enjoy low-maintenance living in this well-maintained 1-bedroom, 1-bath condominium designed exclusively for residents age 55 and older at The Carriage House. This inviting home features a bright and comfortable layout with a private balcony/sun porch—perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Thoughtful owner upgrades include additional insulation in the bedroom for improved energy efficiency and comfort, along with added overhead lighting to create a brighter, more welcoming living space. There is a laundry room in the basement for residents and secure storage as well. Need in unit laundry? The HOA allows them to be installed! Each apartment has a designated parking spot in a carport close to the building with additional parking for guests. This property must be owner occupied, cannot be rented. Occupants must be 55 years or older, maximum of 2 people per unit. HOA dues for 2025 have been $433.67/month, and include $159.50 per month over 20yrs for the new building elevator. HOA dues include cable tv, trash, water, snow removal, exterior maintenance of the property and common areas. Does not include property taxes and insurance. Pets are allowed, 2 small animals per unit. Additional info can be provided on restrictions. Whether you're looking to downsize or simplify your lifestyle, this condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and easy living in a community designed for those 55 and better!
