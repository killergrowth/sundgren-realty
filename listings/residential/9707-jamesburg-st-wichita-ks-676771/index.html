Offering 4 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a fully finished basement, this home combines timeless design with extensive modern upgrades, making it truly move in ready. From the moment you arrive, you'll appreciate the exceptional curb appeal and the significant exterior improvements completed within the last year. Step inside and you'll immediately notice the attention to detail that continues throughout the entire home.
