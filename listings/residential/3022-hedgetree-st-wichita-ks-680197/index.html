Property Description

Welcome home to 3022 N Hedgetree St! This move-in ready home offers an open-concept floor plan with inviting spaces throughout, featuring a large updated kitchen with stunning quartz countertops, ample cabinet space, and all kitchen appliances included, making your move even easier. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining area and living room, where a vaulted ceiling adds to the spacious and airy feel, creating the perfect space for everyday living and entertaining. The main level includes three spacious bedrooms and two full bathrooms, including a primary suite with its own en suite bathroom. Updated bathrooms and kitchen, newer paint, and brand new carpet and paint in the family room provide a fresh, modern feel. Downstairs, you'll find a huge family room perfect for entertaining, a bonus room with endless possibilities, a full bathroom and a very large storage room. Step outside to enjoy the fully fenced backyard complete with a newer patio and pergola, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or hosting gatherings. Additional updates include a brand-new roof and gutters installed in June 2026. Located in a prime area close to shopping, restaurants, and convenient access to K-96, this home combines comfort, style, and an unbeatable location. After being lovingly cared for and cherished by the same owners for more than 20 years, this special home is ready for its next chapter and new memories to be made. Don't miss this gem!
