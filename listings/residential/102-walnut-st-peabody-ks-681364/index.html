Property Description

Prime Commercial Property with Excellent Access and Visibility! Discover the possibilities with this approximately 4,200 sq. ft. commercial building, formerly known as the Cameo Rose Antique Store. Ideally situated in a small-town setting, this versatile property offers outstanding accessibility, visibility, and potential for a variety of business opportunities. Stretching from Walnut Street to the alley, the property offers convenient access and excellent flexibility for business operations. Whether you're looking to establish a new business, expand an existing operation, or invest in commercial real estate, this property offers space, accessibility, and opportunity in a great small-town location.
