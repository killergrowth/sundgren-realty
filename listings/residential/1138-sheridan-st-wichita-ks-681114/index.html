Property Description

Welcome to 1138 N Sheridan! Offering space, versatility, and endless possibilities, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home is full of potential. Storage is plentiful both inside the home and throughout the exterior, while the impressive 4+ car garage and additional covered parking provide plenty of room for everyday vehicles, recreational vehicles, hobbies, and more. If you're looking for space, parking, and the opportunity to make a property your own, this one is full of possibilities!
