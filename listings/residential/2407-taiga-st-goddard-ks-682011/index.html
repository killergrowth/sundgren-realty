Property Description

Explore these stunning new duplexes, just west of 167th off Maple in Goddard. Special offer with accepted contract-- Seller will pay $10,000 towards buyer's closing costs, HOA's and/or add on's. "The Abbington" features a beautiful 3-bedroom, 2-bath layout with a 2-car garage and spans 1,470 sq. ft. Enjoy the fully fenced and landscaped yard. Step inside to find a spacious living room adorned with vaulted ceilings and a custom beam. The kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances—including a dishwasher, microwave, stove and fridge—and a convenient walk-in pantry. The master bedroom includes a generous walk-in closet, while the master bath features a double vanity for added comfort. Relax outdoors on your side patio or back patio, perfect for enjoying the fresh air. The exterior showcases partial brick front and concrete Hardie siding all around. As part of the community, you'll have access to a future clubhouse with a basement that features a pool, a stocked lake and walking paths! The HOA takes care of mowing, trash, irrigation, and amenities with fees of $125 monthly. Don’t miss out on this incredible opportunity! Pictures are representative of the model. General and special taxes have not been fully assessed yet. Lot size is based on both units. Taxes will be split between both units. Specials for half duplex estimated at $153/month.
