Property Description

Stunning sunsets and peaceful country living! That’s what you’ll find on this 4.6 acre property. Featuring a solid home with open floor plan, roomy kitchen and beautiful views in every direction! Enjoy two main floor bedrooms, easy care laminate tile throughout and a custom-built, zero entry shower that is a dream! All doors are 36” and have lever handles. The basement has a roomy feel with 9’ ceilings, plenty of lighting, a finished bathroom, a partially finished bedroom with egress (no closet), a bonus room, laundry and 2nd egress window. The corn/pellet stove keeps the whole house warm and cozy in winter. The oversized garage has separate heating & air and again, lots of lighting. This property also boasts a HUGE shop with concrete containment that is perfect for equipment storage, repairs, or your next business venture! The separate office, with bathroom and fridge, is perfect for your small business or possible ADU use. Older sheds provide storage and flexibility.
