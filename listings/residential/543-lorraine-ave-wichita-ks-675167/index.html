Property Description

This immaculate home was built with 12-inch-thick concrete walls and an additional 4 inches of insulation, providing exceptional durability, energy efficiency, and year-round comfort. Enjoy the peace and quiet with minimal road noise, added wind and tornado resistance, and low utility costs. The roof has also been updated with Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, offering potential insurance savings and long-lasting durability. Enjoy spacious, low-maintenance living in this beautifully designed, zero-entry, ADA-compliant home offering 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 2,880 square feet, and a 2-car attached garage. The open, airy floor plan creates a welcoming atmosphere, with a generous living area that flows seamlessly into the large kitchen featuring abundant cabinet space, two pantries, and plenty of room for cooking and entertaining. The primary suite includes a private en suite bathroom, while an additional bedroom and a convenient main-floor laundry room with built-in storage add to the home's functionality. Downstairs, you'll find a large family room perfect for relaxing or entertaining, along with two additional spacious bedrooms and a full bathroom. A chair lift provides easy access between levels, adding to the home's thoughtful accessibility features. Lawn maintenance is taken care of, giving you more time to enjoy your home and the friendly neighborhood. Conveniently located with quick access to Kellogg, this home offers the perfect combination of comfort, accessibility, space, and convenience.
