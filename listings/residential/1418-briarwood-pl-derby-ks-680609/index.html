Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious, well-maintained 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home in the desirable Broadmoor Hills neighborhood! Built in 2001 by respected builder Richard Standrich, this 3,080-square-foot home offers a thoughtfully designed split-bedroom floor plan, 9-foot ceilings throughout, and plenty of room to live, relax, and entertain. Situated on a cul-de-sac lot, the home combines comfortable living spaces with an ideal location close to Rock Road, Dillons, Derby Golf & Country Club, Boeing, and McConnell Air Force Base. The kitchen features beautiful quartz countertops, real wood flooring, and under-cabinet lighting, creating a welcoming and functional space for everyday living. The main-level laundry adds convenience, while the spacious primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, audio pre-wiring, a walk-in closet, and a private bath complete with a corner Jacuzzi tub, tile flooring, and a separate shower and toilet area. Enjoy two inviting living areas, each pre-wired for audio. The upstairs living room features a gas fireplace insert, while the downstairs living room offers a gas space heater, providing flexible options for relaxing, entertaining, or creating a separate recreation space. Additional highlights include Anderson windows throughout, a Class 4 roof approximately five years old, and an oversized three-car garage featuring 8-foot doors and 12-foot ceilings. Outside, the privacy-fenced backyard and sprinkler system make outdoor enjoyment and lawn care a breeze. With five bedrooms, three full baths, versatile living spaces, quality features, and a convenient location, this Broadmoor Hills home is ready for its next owner.
