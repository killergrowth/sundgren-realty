Property Description

Professionally managed and well cared for, duplex property offers 2 units, an appealing opportunity for investors looking for a solid addition to their portfolio. Generous living spaces and spacious corner lot with country feel while still being part of an established neighborhood. Duplex with over 4200 sqft, 8 bedrooms, 6 bathrooms, 2 storage rooms and 4 cars of garage space. Welcoming covered front porch. Each side has 2 bedrooms on the main floor with primary bedroom with ensuite bathroom with step in shower. Open concept living room, dining room and kitchen with granite countertops. Hall bath and main floor laundry. The finished basement with day light windows features family room, 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, and ample storage room. Two car attached garage. Fully occupied or potential owner occupy for this one. There is a community pool for relaxing days to cool off in the summer months. Quick access to highway 254 and easy to go anywhere.
