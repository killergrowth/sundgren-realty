Property Description

Welcome to 849 N Brook Forest Rd in Derby, Kansas, where updated finishes, multiple living spaces and a finished basement come together in a home with plenty of room to spread out. This 3-bedroom Derby ranch offers approximately 2,400 square feet of total floor-plan area, an attached two-car garage and a versatile layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Step inside to a spacious living room filled with natural light from a wall of windows. Updated flooring and neutral finishes carry throughout much of the home, creating a warm, move-in-ready feel. The separate dining room features a large bay window and modern statement lighting, providing a beautiful spot for everything from weeknight dinners to holiday gatherings. The updated kitchen is at the heart of the home, featuring granite countertops, crisp cabinetry, subway-tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances and a generous peninsula with additional prep and gathering space. Even better, the kitchen flows directly into a second main-floor living area anchored by a painted-brick fireplace with a wood mantel. It’s a great setup for entertaining, allowing the kitchen and family room to stay connected while still offering distinct spaces throughout the home. All three bedrooms are conveniently located on the main floor, along with updated bathrooms featuring contemporary vanities, lighting and finishes. The spacious double-sink vanity provides extra functionality, while the home’s thoughtful floor plan keeps the bedroom areas separate from the primary gathering spaces. Head downstairs and you’ll find even more room to make your own. The finished basement features an expansive recreation room, bonus room/home office, bathroom and laundry area, along with substantial utility and storage space. Create a movie room, game room, home gym, dedicated workspace, hobby room—or a combination of several. The bonus room does not have an egress window and is not represented as a bedroom, but it adds valuable finished space and flexibility to the lower level. The location makes everyday life convenient, too. Situated in an established Derby neighborhood, 849 N Brook Forest Rd provides quick access to Derby shopping, restaurants, parks, recreation and other community amenities, while keeping Wichita within an easy drive. With an updated kitchen and bathrooms, three main-floor bedrooms, two separate main-floor living areas, fireplace, finished basement, bonus room/home office, abundant storage and attached two-car garage, this home offers a combination of space and versatility that’s hard to appreciate until you see it in person. If you’ve been searching for an updated ranch home for sale in Derby, KS with a finished basement and two-car garage, put 849 N Brook Forest Rd on your must-see list.
