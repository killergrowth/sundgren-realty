Property Description

$75,800 BELOW COUNTY TAX VALUATION! Some homes simply offer more, and this is one you'll want to see in person! Custom designed with thoughtful upgrades throughout, this move-in-ready 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath home delivers exceptional quality, space, and functionality at a price that deserves attention. The impressive living room features expansive windows, and a stacked-stone fireplace with custom mantel. The kitchen is equally impressive with two-tone cabinetry, gas cooktop, stainless steel appliances that convey, an oversized pantry, and space to add additional built-in appliances. The main-floor primary suite offers a beautifully finished bathroom with extensive subway tile, a full-tile walk-in shower with custom glass door, dual vanities, soft-close cabinetry, private water closet, and a walk-in closet with built-ins. Two additional main-floor bedrooms feature built-in desk areas and share a thoughtfully designed Hollywood bathroom. A convenient drop zone, separate laundry room, and powder bath complete the main level. Downstairs, 9-foot ceilings and abundant natural light make the finished walkout basement feel like an extension of the main floor, with an enormous family and recreation room, walk-up wet bar, two spacious bedrooms, a third full bath, and exceptional storage, including a separate room with potential for a future exercise room, playroom, or additional finished space. Outside, the professionally landscaped, east-facing backyard offers privacy, mature trees, pond views, an expansive deck, and an oversized curved patio, all perfectly positioned for shaded afternoon and evening enjoyment. Even the fully insulated three-car garage goes above and beyond with an electric vehicle charging hookup, hot and cold water connections, and a wall drain providing flexibility for a secondary cooking or utility area. Hot tub connections are also in place near the walkout patio. Located in Andover schools and offered at $539,000, well below the county's $614,800 tax valuation, this is an exceptional opportunity to own a truly customized home. Showings begin next week!
