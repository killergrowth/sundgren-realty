Charming All-Brick Home in Mulvane. Welcome to this timeless all-brick ranch nestled on a quiet, highly desirable street in Mulvane. Perfect for young families or empty nesters, this 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom home effortlessly blends classic character with enjoyment! Property HighlightsCharacter-Rich Kitchen: The galley-style kitchen stands out with new countertops, sink and an abundance of original, double-stack cabinets that maximize storage while retaining gorgeous vintage charm. Remodeled Bathroom: Rest easy knowing the home features a freshly updated, move-in-ready bathroom. *Don't miss the original hardwoods preserved by carpet, ready for your big reveal! **Converted garage could easily be turned back into a garage or 4th bedroom. Backyard Oasis: Beat the summer heat with your own above-ground pool—a proven neighborhood favorite for entertaining. The yard also features an oversized utility shed for tools, hobbies, or extra storage. Ultimate Location Convenience Everything you need is within a convenient 5-minute walk: Shopping & Dining: Grab essentials easily at Dillons, Casey’s, and Dollar General.
