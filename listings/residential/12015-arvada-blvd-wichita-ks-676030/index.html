Property Description

Welcome to this stunning 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home with a 2 car garage in the highly desirable Arvada subdivision in Maize, Kansas. The popular Redbud floor plan offers a thoughtfully designed two-story layout featuring modern finishes, an open-concept design, and abundant natural light throughout. The spacious living room showcases a stylish linear fireplace, creating a warm and inviting focal point. Luxury vinyl plank flooring extends through the main living areas, providing both durability and easy maintenance. The well appointed kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining and living spaces, making it perfect for entertaining. The private primary suite is conveniently located on the main floor and offers a relaxing retreat with a spacious ensuite bath and walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms and ample living space provide flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. Located in the sought-after Maize school district, this beautiful home combines contemporary style, functional living, and a prime location in one of the area's most desirable neighborhoods. Don't miss the opportunity to make this exceptional home yours! The HOA is $75/month which covers mowing, lawn and sprinkler maintenance.
