Property Description

Sitting at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in a great Andover neighborhood, this ranch home is on nearly half an acre with room to breathe. Inside you'll find vaulted ceilings, rich hardwood floors, and an open floor plan that flows from the living room into the kitchen and dining. A two-way gas fireplace is shared between the living room and the primary suite, so both spaces feel warm and cozy. The kitchen has an eating bar, pantry, range hood, gas hookup, and space for a table, plus a formal dining area for holidays and hosting. Thanks to the split floor plan, the primary suite gets its own privacy, with a whirlpool tub, separate shower, double sinks, and a walk-in closet. Main floor laundry makes everyday life easy. The finished walkout basement is made for entertaining, with a huge rec room and wet bar, more bedrooms, a full bath, a cedar closet, lots of storage, and a concrete storm room. Out back, the covered deck and covered patio look over the fenced yard, which stays green with an irrigation well and sprinkler system. A side-load 3-car garage rounds it all out. The neighborhood has a clubhouse, pool, playground, walking paths, greenbelt, and pond. This one truly has it all!
