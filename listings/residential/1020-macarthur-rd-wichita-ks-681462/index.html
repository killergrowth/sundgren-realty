Property Description

One-of-a-kind ranch with room to grow! A white Austin stone facade with charming wagon-wheel accents welcomes you home. The circle drive offers ample parking and easy in-and-out access. Inside, a bright front living room sets a cheerful tone. The updated kitchen is the heart of the home, with new cabinetry, quartz countertops, and new flooring. It opens to a spacious family room, with a cozy eating nook for quick meals and a peninsula for casual dining. The primary bedroom features its own bathroom with a shower and wonderful natural light. Three additional bedrooms and a newly renovated main bathroom complete the main level. From the 2-car garage, you’ll step into a convenient laundry drop zone that could double as extra pantry space, with access to the backyard. Outside, a concrete pad is ready for your fire pit, and sidewalks wrap around the house for easy access. The showstopper is the separate building just south of the home, already plumbed for a bathroom and ready for your personal touch. Use it as a home office, studio, workshop, or guest space. A rare find! Come see this one in person!
