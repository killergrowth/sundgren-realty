Property Description

There’s something special about a home that simply can’t be replicated. This beautiful all-brick Craftsman bungalow in Newton’s Historic District is filled with timeless character, original craftsmanship, and the kind of details that make you fall in love the moment you walk through the door. With over 2,000 square feet, beautiful hardwood floors, original woodwork, built-ins, paneled doors, crystal doorknobs, and functioning transom windows, the charm is everywhere you look. The formal dining room features a lovely built-in Mission-style china hutch, while the oversized kitchen is full of possibilities, offering floor-to-ceiling cabinetry, granite countertops, gas cooking, an abundance of storage, a pantry, and all kitchen appliances included. A charming breakfast nook adds the perfect spot for morning coffee. The main-floor bath is a true step back in time, featuring original penny tile flooring and a classic clawfoot tub. Upstairs, two exceptionally large bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bath, with additional built-ins and character throughout. The basement offers a finished bonus space with a decorative fireplace—perfect for a family room, hobby space, or home office—with plenty of additional storage and room to expand or finish to fit your needs. Outside, enjoy the nicely landscaped setting and the appeal of this established historic neighborhood. The property may qualify for historic district tax credits for eligible improvements and maintenance. A home rich in history, filled with character, and ready for its next chapter. If you appreciate old-house charm and craftsmanship, this one is worth a closer look!
