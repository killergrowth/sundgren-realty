Property Description

Welcome to 2114 S Chautauqua! This 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch offers 1,448 square feet of one-level living with an attached 2-car garage and plenty of potential to make it your own. Beautiful original hardwood floors throughout the home are a highlight of this home! And you will have peace of mind with a new water and new sewer line. Inside, you'll find a functional layout with both formal dining and an eat-in kitchen, main-floor laundry, and a full bath conveniently located just off the kitchen with a 2nd full bath by the bedrooms. Outside, enjoy a fenced backyard with a large patio and space to relax, play or entertain. Conveniently located with easy access to Kellogg and nearby amenities. Property is being sold AS-IS.
