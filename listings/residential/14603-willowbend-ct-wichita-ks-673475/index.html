Property Description

NEWLY CONSTRUCTED - NO SPECIALS Hidden gem in the highly sought-after Timber Lake Estates! Situated on a beautifully treed half-acre lot, this extensively reimagined luxury home offers an exceptional combination of scale, design, craftsmanship, and modern infrastructure rarely found in an established neighborhood. Inside, you’ll find oversized entertaining spaces, engineered hardwood flooring, gorgeous custom selections, and a layout designed for both everyday living and large-scale gatherings. The main floor features a dramatic foyer, formal dining room, dedicated office, expansive family room, and a stunning chef’s kitchen with sitting area and an enormous walk-in pantry. Upstairs offers three oversized bedroom suites, each with walk-in closets and beautifully customized baths, including a luxurious primary suite filled with natural light and spacious enough for a sitting area or private retreat. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility with a large family room anchored by a second fireplace, a 4th bedroom and bath, dedicated exercise room, and abundant storage space. Beyond the design and finishes, the level of reconstruction and modernization completed on this home is extraordinary. Major improvements include extensive structural framing updates, a new presidential roof, new windows and exterior doors, all-new electrical, plumbing, HVAC systems and ductwork, insulation, cabinetry, premium countertops, flooring, trim, appliances, fixtures, security pre-wiring, fiber-ready infrastructure, landscaping, irrigation, and more. Timber Lake Estates residents enjoy scenic lakes, common areas, mature trees, and a premier east-side location close to shopping, dining, and schools. A rare opportunity to own a truly turnkey luxury property with the presence and character of an established home paired with the feel and functionality of modern construction.
