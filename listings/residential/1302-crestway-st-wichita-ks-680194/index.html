Property Description

Cute, affordable, and full of potential, this 2-bedroom, 1-bath home offers an excellent opportunity for an investor or buyer looking for a manageable property in a convenient location. The 825-square-foot layout includes a comfortable living room with adjoining dining area, kitchen with dishwasher, two bedrooms, and a full bath, with hardwood floors and crisp white paint adding a clean, classic feel. A corner lot, one-car attached garage, and fenced backyard provide desirable outdoor space, while the unfinished basement offers additional storage and a practical place to take shelter during a storm. Located directly across from McDonald Golf Course and just minutes from Wichita State University, this property offers a lot of potential at an affordable price point.
