Excellent opportunity to acquire a recently constructed multifamily investment portfolio located along Hickory Creek Street, Hickory Creek Court and Wheatland Street in west Wichita, Kansas. The portfolio consists of 13 duplexes totaling 26 rental units, with the flexibility to purchase the entire portfolio or smaller, seller-approved packages. Built in 2025, each duplex offers approximately 2,488 SF of combined finished living area, with each unit featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, an attached 2-car garage and single-level living on a slab foundation. Interior finishes showcased include open-concept floor plans, vaulted ceilings, modern cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, wood-look flooring, recessed lighting and primary suites with private bathrooms. Exterior features shown include covered patios and fenced backyards. Based on the seller-provided rent roll, each unit is scheduled at $1,595 per month, representing a potential gross rental income of $3,190 per duplex monthly and $41,470 monthly ($497,640 annually) for the entire portfolio, assuming full occupancy. Conveniently located near W. Kellogg and S. 135th Street West, the properties offer easy access to shopping, dining, major roadways and Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National Airport. This portfolio presents an attractive opportunity for investors seeking recently constructed residential rental properties within a concentrated geographic area.
