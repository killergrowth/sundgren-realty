About This Property

Welcome home to this thoughtfully designed single-family brick home, perfectly situated on a premier 0.39-acre lot in Augusta’s highly coveted Knebler Addition. Offering 4 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and over 4,000 square feet of elegant living space, this traditional ranch-style home beautifully blends executive comfort with small-town charm. Step inside to discover a bright layout featuring a warm main floor hearth room anchored by a gas fireplace flanked by custom built-ins. This space flows seamlessly into a gorgeous kitchen complete with premium Corian countertops. Just off the kitchen, the informal dining room offers peaceful, scenic pond views right from the window, along with a walk-out deck perfect for effortless outdoor dining. For daily convenience, the spacious 2-car garage provides direct, weather-protected entry straight into the house, leading right past a dedicated main floor laundry room equipped with a utility sink and an adjacent half bathroom. The main-floor master suite serves as a private retreat, boasting an en-suite bathroom complete with dual vanities, a separate walk-in shower, and a relaxing jacuzzi tub. Downstairs, the massive basement recreation room is built for ultimate entertainment—perfect for game days and movie nights. This huge lower level features a second gas fireplace with built-ins, a dedicated wet bar equipped with a sink and refrigerator space, and a walk-out to a covered patio. Outdoor living is a true retreat on this property, highlighted by a private backyard gazebo that provides a beautiful backdrop for relaxing, drinking your morning coffee, or hosting summer gatherings. Located in an established neighborhood just moments from Augusta City Lake, Castle Park, and the community splash pad, you'll enjoy quick access to incredible outdoor recreation and local amenities. Please note: The listing agent is related to the seller.
