Property Description

Looking for a little more space without giving up convenience? Located on the north side of Rose Hill and just minutes from Andover, this 3-bedroom home sits on approximately half an acre with open fields surrounding the property, giving it a peaceful country feel with easy access to town. Inside, you'll find a comfortable and functional layout with original hardwood flooring adding character throughout portions of the home. The third bedroom adds valuable flexibility for a bedroom, home office, playroom, or whatever fits your needs. Outside is where this property really stands out. Mature trees and the surrounding fields create a setting that feels much larger than the half-acre lot. The detached heated and cooled shop provides year-round space for projects, hobbies, storage, or a workshop, in addition to the attached garage. With a little room to spread out, a versatile shop, and a convenient location between Rose Hill and Andover, this property offers a combination that's getting harder to find at this price point.
