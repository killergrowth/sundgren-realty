Property Description

Welcome home to 130 S 8th St, where timeless vintage charm blends seamlessly with tasteful, modern updates! This move-in-ready home offers the perfect balance of historic craftsmanship and fresh, stylish living. From the moment you step inside, you'll fall in love with the beautifully preserved original woodwork, adding rich character and warmth throughout the main living areas. The entire home features brand-new flooring and new interior paint. At the heart of the home, the fully remodeled kitchen is ready for cooking and entertaining, boasting sleek new finishes, updated cabinetry, and contemporary updates designed with functionality in mind. The remodeled bathroom, thoughtfully upgraded with clean lines and modern fixtures, is another bonus. The primary bedroom is conveniently located on the main floor, and there are three additional bedrooms upstairs. Situated on a generous lot in a peaceful Conway Springs neighborhood, you're just minutes from local schools, the park and more. Set up your private showing today!
