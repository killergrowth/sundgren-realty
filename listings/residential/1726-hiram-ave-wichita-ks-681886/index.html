Property Description

This 2-bedroom, 1-bath ranch sits on a 0.15-acre lot and offers plenty of potential for someone ready to make it their own. Wood flooring runs through the living room and both bedrooms, with the living room opening into the dining area. The kitchen features white cabinetry and space for a table, while the separate laundry room just off the kitchen offers built-in shelving for additional storage. A full bath is conveniently located between the two bedrooms. Outside, enjoy a spacious backyard and a detached 1-car garage with room for parking, tools, and extra storage.
