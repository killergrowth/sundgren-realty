Welcome to 915 N Pinecrest! Located directly across from Edgemoor Park, this 3-bedroom, 1-bath home offers a functional floor plan with two spacious living areas on the main level, providing flexibility for entertaining, relaxing, or creating a home office. Beautiful original oak hardwood floors, refinished just a few years ago, add warmth and character throughout.
