Property Description

Beautifully situated in The Village Addition, this unique home sets on a half acre lot surrounded by mature trees. The curb appeal is stunning! The backyard is just as gorgeous as the front along with a patio to enjoy as you sit and enjoy the scenery and animals that happen too pass by. By the way, all yard maintenance is included. As you step inside, the grand scale of space will catch your attention right away! A fireplace centers the living area for a cozy and comfortable engagement with others or to snuggle on the couch with a warm blanket watching a movie. The dining area is able to host a large group or dinner and guests will remember the time they had sitting around the table. The kitchen is right off of the dining area perfectly located to set meals at the dining table or read a great book in the sunroom enjoying all of the natural light coming through the big beautiful windows. On the opposite side of the home there are three bedrooms, a primary bedroom with an ensuite and the remaining two bedrooms are connected with a jack and jill bathroom. There's so much to view as you walk through so schedule a tour and see for yourself.
