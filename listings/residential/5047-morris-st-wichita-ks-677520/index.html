Property Description

Welcome to this charming all-brick ranch home offering spacious living, abundant natural light, and nearly half an acre of beautifully established grounds. With 3 bedrooms and 2 baths, this home features two separate living areas, each filled with natural light from huge windows. One living room offers a cozy fireplace, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The bedrooms feature original wood flooring that adds warmth and character. The oversized kitchen is sure to impress with a center island, tons of cabinet and counter space, and a dedicated dining area. Just off the dining room are two separate sunrooms, providing additional versatile space and peaceful views of the mature trees surrounding the property. A large, separate laundry area includes a convenient sink. Outside, enjoy the privacy and space of the huge, almost half-acre lot, complete with mature trees, a detached 2-car garage, and additional patio space in the driveway. With its classic brick exterior, spacious layout, original character, and generous lot, this home offers plenty of room to make it your own! **Seller is offering a $2000 flooring allowance with an acceptable offer**
