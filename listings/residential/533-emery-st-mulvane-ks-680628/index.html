Property Description

Built in 1920 and completely renovated, this 4-bedroom, 2-bath home at 533 Emery in Mulvane offers 2,093 square feet on a 0.32-acre lot, with the character of an older home and the updates of a newer one. The renovation covers the parts of a home that matter most. The kitchen was redone from the ground up, with a new layout, cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, and a full set of appliances. Both bathrooms were rebuilt with new layouts, vanities, and showers. Newer flooring runs throughout, & the interior and exterior have fresh paint. The main floor has two bedrooms and a full bath, which works well for guests, a home office, or anyone who prefers to skip the stairs. Off the kitchen, a bonus/flex room with walls of windows fills with natural light. Use it as a sunroom, a second living area, or a quiet spot for morning coffee. Upstairs, two more bedrooms share a full bath and an open common area between them. Keep them as two bedrooms with a built-in hangout, gaming, or study space, or combine the level into one large primary suite. Outside, the detached one-car garage doubles as a workshop, and the storage shed in the back has a playhouse above it. With the major updates already done, the next owner can move in without a project list. Schedule a showing to see how the layout fits your household.
