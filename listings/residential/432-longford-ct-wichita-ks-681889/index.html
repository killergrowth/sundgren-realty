Property Description

Distinctive architecture, modern updates, and exceptional living spaces come together in this beautifully transformed Brookhollow home! From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the striking vaulted ceilings, rich exposed wood beams, and stunning architectural details that create warmth and character throughout. The spacious living area features a dramatic fireplace and expansive windows, bringing in natural light while creating an inviting focal point. The open concept design flows effortlessly into the dining area and beautifully updated kitchen, complete with contemporary finishes, stainless steel appliances, generous workspace, and an inviting breakfast bar. Additional built in cabinetry and serving space add both style and functionality. Architectural charm, contemporary style, impressive living spaces, and a sought-after Brookhollow location, this property is a truly special opportunity.
