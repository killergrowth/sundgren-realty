Property Description

Space, style, and a layout designed for everyday living- this nearly new Maize home checks all the boxes! Sitting on a fully fenced lot of more than 1/3 acre, the home makes a statement from the start with its distinctive tower-style front porch entry and oversized windows that bring in plenty of natural light. Inside, the open-concept main level features soaring beamed ceilings and a spacious kitchen designed to be both beautiful and functional, with floating shelves, a convenient coffee/wine bar, and a large pantry. A focal-point sliding door opens to the raised, covered concrete patio, creating an easy connection between indoor and outdoor living. The split-bedroom main floor keeps the primary suite tucked away for privacy. The primary retreat feels like a spa, featuring a stunning wet room with a large walk-in shower and soaking tub. The spacious walk-in closet connects directly to the laundry room for added convenience. Two additional bedrooms and a full bath are located on the opposite side of the home. Downstairs, the finished basement gives you room to spread out with an exceptionally large family/rec room complete with a wet bar. Bedrooms four and five are positioned together on one side of the basement, along with another full bathroom featuring a separate vanity area from the tub/shower- deal when multiple people are getting ready at the same time. And storage? There’s plenty of it throughout. With generous living spaces, thoughtful separation of bedrooms, a fantastic primary suite, finished basement, and a large fenced lot, this home offers the kind of space that works just as well for everyday life as it does for entertaining. Come take a look- you may be surprised just how much this home has to offer!
