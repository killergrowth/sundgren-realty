Property Description

Unique opportunity in Andover, Kansas! Situated on 1.38 acres with mature trees and a peaceful, private setting, this property features TWO separate homes offering rustic charm and renovation potential. The primary residence is a 720 sq. ft., 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom cabin-style home featuring an inviting open-concept living area, beautiful stone fireplace, natural wood cabinetry, open kitchen shelving, tile flooring, and a spacious laundry/storage area. The second home offers approximately 900 sq. ft. and is in need of a complete renovation, providing an opportunity for buyers looking to restore or reimagine the additional living space. A tree-lined gravel driveway welcomes you onto the property, where mature landscaping and open grassy areas create a relaxing country atmosphere. Whether you're looking for acreage, a unique residential property, or a renovation opportunity, this property offers plenty of possibilities. Any intended use of the second home is subject to applicable zoning and local requirements. The livable cabin-style home is currently tenant-occupied on a month-to-month lease.
