Property Description

This beautifully remodeled 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom home at 160 S features modern updates throughout, including fresh paint, new flooring, an upgraded kitchen, and elegant cedar accents. The spacious yard offers ample room for a shop, garage, or other additions. Conveniently located near the grade school, this home is perfect for families or first-time buyers seeking a move-in-ready property with future potential. Have peace of mind knowing that your kitchen appliances, water heater and HVAC system are all only two years old and the roof is only two years old. This property was originally three separate lots that have been joined to form one larger lot. There is an additional water meter with water spigot located on the north side of the property. This would make an excellent area to build an additional shop or garage.
