Property Description

Beautifully maintained, one-owner, custom-built all-brick home in the Haysville school district! The inviting open main-floor layout features front and back open-bay windows, while the finished basement offers additional living space and a flexible bonus room with a closet that could serve as a fourth bedroom. The updated kitchen boasts granite countertops, a newer backsplash, and a new sink and fixture. A new roof will be installed, with the buyer having the opportunity to choose the shingle color! The HVAC system is approximately eight years old. Step outside to a beautifully landscaped backyard retreat featuring mature hedges, shrubs, flowering plants, roses, and charming garden benches. Enjoy the enclosed covered deck, established sod, irrigation system, and a storage shed equipped with 120V electricity and lighting. Inside, discover a unique cedar-lined closet with oak shelving that adds character and functionality. Located in a quiet neighborhood, this lovingly cared-for home offers timeless brick construction, versatile living space, and a backyard perfect for relaxing and enjoying the outdoors.
