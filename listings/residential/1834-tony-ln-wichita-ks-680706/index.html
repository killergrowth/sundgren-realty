Property Description

This lovely 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom family home is perfectly situated on a quiet corner lot! Step inside to an inviting, open-concept main level designed for family gatherings and everyday living. The living room boasts beautiful natural light from skylights above! It flows seamlessly into a dedicated dining area and around to the kitchen, creating one lovely light-filled space perfect for family game nights or casual get-togethers with friends. Upstairs you’ll find all three bedrooms, including the primary bedroom with a beautifully remodeled bath. Laundry room is placed upstairs where you need it most. Downstairs you’ll find a very spacious family room with an adjacent sizable rec room! The possibilities are endless in this amazing space fit for holiday gatherings, movie nights, or teenager gaming hangouts! You'll also find one more bedroom full bath, giving plenty of space for guests! The backyard offers a very generous space for outdoor fun, gardening, or simply relaxing. It is the perfect place to watch the sunrise in the mornings with coffee or gather around the fire pit for late night family time. This home combines modern comfort, practical features, and a wonderful family-friendly layout ready for new owners to make lasting memories!
