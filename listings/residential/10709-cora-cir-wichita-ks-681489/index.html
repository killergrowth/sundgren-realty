Property Description

RARE FIND — a beautifully maintained 5-acre property in Maize offering space, privacy, and a home full of character. This 2,500 sq ft two-story features 3 bedrooms plus an office and 3 baths, along with a 2-car attached garage and an oversized 3-car detached garage with dedicated workspace. Upstairs, you’ll find three bedrooms and a convenient laundry room, including a spacious primary suite, a makeup desk, walk-in closet, and a distinctive curved tudor-style bathroom with a walk-in shower and dual sinks. The main floor centers around a warm living room with a wood-burning stove and large windows framing views of the acreage, all leading out to a wraparound covered porch perfect for watching wildlife. The kitchen offers granite counters, abundant cabinetry, a built-in desk, and access to an optional main-floor laundry area. An office with double doors opening to the large front patio and a full bath complete the main level. The lower level provides a high-ceiling basement with excellent storage and the private well system provides drinking water (no water bill). Outside, the detached 3-car garage with extra workspace and an additional shed offer even more room for tools, equipment, and hobbies. The fully cement floored outbuilding/shop includes a car pit, ideal for automotive work. A property with this much potential in the Maize School District is truly a rare opportunity. Recent upgrades include new roofs, water heater and well pump. And 2 AC units for optimal comfort. Horses allowed. A dream home in a dream location.
