Property Description

Welcome to 739 N Mount Carmel! This cute, well-maintained home is conveniently located just outside the Indian Hills area and right off West Central, putting you close to restaurants, shopping, and everyday conveniences. This home has been refreshed inside and out and is ready for its next owner! Recent updates include a brand-new roof on both the home and detached garage, all-new windows throughout, fresh interior and exterior paint, and new carpet. Step inside to a bright and inviting living room filled with natural light, followed by a spacious formal dining area with plenty of room to gather. The kitchen features granite countertops and a coordinating backsplash, while the well-kept bathroom offers a newer vanity. The laundry has its own dedicated area with convenient storage shelving for added functionality. Outside, a long driveway leads to the detached one-car garage, providing plenty of off-street parking. Out back, you'll find a very spacious backyard and patio, offering lots of possibilities for entertaining, relaxing, gardening, or simply enjoying the extra outdoor space. And for additional peace of mind, the seller is offering a one-year home warranty! With numerous recent updates, a great location, and plenty of indoor and outdoor space, this home is one you won't want to miss. Schedule your showing today!
