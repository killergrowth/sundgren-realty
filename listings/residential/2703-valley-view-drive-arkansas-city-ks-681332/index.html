Property Description

Location, space, and comfortable living come together in this inviting ranch-style home in northeast Arkansas City! Situated directly across from the golf course, this property offers an appealing setting and a floor plan designed to accommodate a variety of lifestyles. The exterior was updated in 2026 with an Emerson metal roof, and Continental siding protected by a 50-year transferrable warranty. Inside, you'll appreciate a spacious main living room and a cozy hearth / dining room centered around a wood-burning fireplace. The kitchen features abundant cabinetry, and a functional layout that makes meal preparation and entertaining enjoyable. Just off the kitchen, the breakfast nook features a large window overlooking a backyard with stunning sunset views. The primary bedroom includes its own full bathroom, while the additional bedrooms and second full bath provide flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. Outside, enjoy the covered patio and fenced backyard, complete with a separate chain-link fenced area that is ideal for pets and luxurious hot tub. An oversized attached two-car garage provides additional room for parking. And a backyard shed offers additional storage or a place for hobbies. Conveniently located in a sought-after neighborhood, this home offers a great opportunity to enjoy comfortable living near the golf course, shopping, dining and more.
