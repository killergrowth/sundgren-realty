Property Description

Want brand new without the wait? Welcome to 4427 Jill! This beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom ranch offers the fresh feel of new construction with upgrades you won't find in a typical new build! Situated on a spacious corner lot, this home features meticulous landscaping, a private well and sprinkler system, custom concrete landscape The open-concept main floor is designed for comfortable everyday living and entertaining, with a desirable split-bedroom floor plan offering three bedrooms upstairs. The spacious primary suite features a large walk-in shower, double-sink vanity, and walk-in closet. You'll also love the convenient main-floor laundry and mudroom complete with custom cabinetry for organization. Downstairs, the view-out basement is filled with natural light and offers an expansive family room, wet bar, and plenty of space to entertain. Two additional bedrooms, a third full bathroom, and an abundance of storage. Location is everything! Tucked into a beautiful neighborhood within the Maize School District, this home offers convenient access to K-96, shopping, dining, and everything the Maize Road corridor has to offer. All the benefits of a newer home, the upgrades already completed, and absolutely no waiting to move in!
