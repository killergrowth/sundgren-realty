Property Description

Welcome home to this fabulous 4bed/3bath/2car mid-century modern ranch in East Wichita's desirable Village neighborhood! Architectural character and pride of ownership shine throughout this beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated home. As you arrive, you'll appreciate the huge corner lot with mature trees and colorful landscaping complementing the mostly-brick exterior, stamped concrete driveway, and fully-fenced backyard. Step inside to discover gorgeous hardwood floors, built-in display shelves, abundant natural light, and timeless architectural details that give this home its distinctive charm. The spacious formal living and dining rooms offer wonderful spaces for entertaining, while the beautifully updated kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, an eating bar, and a generous walk-in pantry. Just beyond, the inviting family room boasts a wall of windows overlooking the backyard, a wood-burning fireplace, and plenty of space for a casual dining area. The thoughtfully designed primary suite is a true retreat, complete with its own wood-burning fireplace, an enormous walk-in closet, and a beautifully appointed private bath featuring marble floors and countertops, dual vanities, and a spacious tiled walk-in shower. Three additional generously sized bedrooms offer ample closet space and an abundance of natural light, while the home's two additional full bathrooms include another beautifully renovated bath with stylish finishes. An unfinished partial basement offers opportunities for storage or storm shelter. Outside, the fully-fenced backyard provides a private setting for relaxing or entertaining, complete with an expansive patio, covered seating area, storage building, and recently stained privacy fence. The oversized corner lot and gorgeous landscaping create a peaceful outdoor retreat, while the two-car side-load garage adds convenience and curb appeal. Beyond the upgraded finishes, this home offers an impressive list of major improvements, including a Class IV impact-resistant roof installed in 2025, a two-year-old two-stage AC unit, furnace and whole-home humidifier with WiFi-controlled thermostat, a 6-month-old water heater, radon mitigation system installed in 2022, updated electrical panel, and newer storm windows. With so many major updates already completed, you can spend more time enjoying everything this exceptional home has to offer. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, schools, and Kellogg for easy access throughout Wichita, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy the character of mid-century architecture with the comfort and convenience of modern improvements.
