Property Description

A huge covered front porch welcomes you to this charming 1½-story home offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and an additional office on nearly a quarter-acre in Salina’s South Santa Fe Avenue Historic District, conveniently located near downtown! Gorgeous original oak floors, a beautiful staircase with detailed wood railing, and a formal dining room with built-ins showcase the home’s timeless character. The huge living room provides plenty of space to gather, while a main-floor bedroom, full bathroom, and laundry add convenience. All kitchen appliances stay. Upstairs, you’ll find two more large bedrooms, a new 3/4 bathroom, and a separate office. Generous closets, tons of storage, and a partial unfinished basement provide room for the extras. Outside, two-car storage includes a lean-to structure. A new roof and fresh exterior paint complete the package. Call today to schedule your private showing.
