Property Description

This is truly a one of a kind property that when I say this rarely comes up, this truly RARELY comes available. This home is a one owner home that was custom built on 3 lots so it sits within a NON-HOA subdivision on 5.43 acres of land. The sellers loved privacy so this home backs to their own private lake and sellers own the treed area behind the lake for even more privacy. You only see neighbors across the street from your front door but secluded privacy in the back. The outdoor aspect is amazing with stocked pond that sellers loved to use and fish from their "cabin" with the kids. There is a beach area exclusive for only this home to launch paddleboards, kayaks, and more. The yard has sprinkler system that runs throughout all the grassy areas and connected to a private irrigation well. Inside the home, you will be able to instantly see the custom aspects from 2 main level primary suites, 1/2 baths for guests on each floor, fireplaces on each floor, windows that have been all replaced in home (except around front door) and all were done with extended height to capture the outdoors inside with as much natural light at possible. Every bedroom except one and all living spaces have uninterrupted views of the private lake. The home was designed with 2 bedrooms and 2.5 full baths on main floor, 2 bedrooms and 1.5 full baths in basement (the 4th bedroom is not finished as of now). The basement offers a true walk out at grade and is plumbed for a future wet bar and wired for surround sound. There is an oversized large storage room with concrete safe room. The home has a brand new roof, new exterior paint 2026, new garage doors 2026, new interior paint 2026, and so much more. Come see this one today as truly photos do not do this home justice as you must see to believe it in person.
