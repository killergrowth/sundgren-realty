Property Description

Welcome Home to 1340 S. Greenwich! Breathtaking water views, an oversized composite deck, and an exceptional floor plan make this 4-bedroom, 4-bath home a rare opportunity. Built in 1981, this home offers a side-load 2-car garage and inviting living spaces designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The beautiful living room features a vaulted ceiling, fireplace with tile surround, built-in speakers, and two sliding glass doors that frame stunning water views. Enjoy both formal and informal dining areas adjacent to the well-appointed kitchen with a breakfast bar, two sinks, tile backsplash, pull-out spice rack, pull-out cabinet shelving, built-in wine rack, and all appliances remaining. The expansive main-floor primary suite overlooks the water and offers a walk-in closet plus a private bath with tile flooring, dual sinks, tile shower, and linen closet. Hardwood flooring extends throughout much of the main level, which also includes a convenient laundry room, central vacuum system, and intercom system. Upstairs features an additional bedroom with its own private bath and a loft overlooking the living room below. The massive finished view-out basement provides exceptional additional living space with a spacious family room featuring a second fireplace with tile surround, recessed lighting, built-in speakers, and view-out windows. A large game room offers direct access to the backyard, while two bonus rooms provide flexible space for a home office, hobby room, fitness area, or additional storage. Step outside to the tiered oversized composite deck overlooking the water—an ideal setting for relaxing, entertaining, or enjoying peaceful sunsets. If you've been searching for spacious living, multiple entertaining areas, and spectacular waterfront views, this exceptional home is ready to welcome you home.
