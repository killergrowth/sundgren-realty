Property Description

For Sale! Discover a remarkable country retreat situated on 61.49± acres at 8300 N 44th St E in Valley Center, Kansas. This scenic property offers a beautiful combination of open pasture, heavy timber, a large pond, and Chisholm Creek frontage, providing an ideal setting for peaceful country living, recreational activities, or an equestrian lifestyle. Built in 2006, the 2,400± SF barndominium features approximately 1,152 SF of finished living space with 1 bedroom, 1 full bathroom, an open living area, kitchen, and additional space for storage or workshop use. The property also includes a loafing shed for animals, a firepit, and picturesque views throughout the acreage. With expansive pastureland, mature trees, and natural water features, this property offers a unique opportunity to enjoy rural living while remaining conveniently located near Valley Center, Park City, and the Wichita area.
