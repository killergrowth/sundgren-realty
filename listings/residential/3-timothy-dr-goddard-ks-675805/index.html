Property Description

You want to walk into your backyard and go fishing. You don't want a cookie cutter home design or aesthetic. Welcome home to 3 Timothy in Goddard, Kansas! This amazing home is one you have to come see. It sits on just over half an acre with a treed lot and beautiful view of the lake. The first thing you will notice is the grotto style covered front porch with arched windows and a cozy sitting area. Go through the double doors and you are greeted with a 2 story fireplace and windows! This is what a great room is supposed to look like! Soaring ceilings with lots of detail, light, and texture. The great room also offers amazing views of the water from the deck that extends almost the full length of the main floor. The open dining area leads to the kitchen, offering plenty of storage and counter space and ready for you to cook up a storm with double ovens. The large built in restaurant style booth will fit the whole family and is perfect for a quick breakfast or cozy dinner. Upstairs you will find two large bedrooms, and a huge master suite. The master offers a private balcony with views of the lake, walk in closet, and a baby blue tub long enough for the tallest person to enjoy a soak. You can also access a walkway overlooking the great room! Follow it around to access the balcony on the front of the house, and take the stairs back down to the great room. The walkout/viewout basement has an enormous rec room and wood burning fireplace, and the largest bar! Hand built by the seller, it is made for entertaining and you are sure to make some great memories here! The basement offers two more bedrooms, another full bath, large laundry area, and a 24X24 unfinished safe room/storage area. Let's talk about the detached oversized 2 car shop for a second! Its ready for all of your hobby or side hustle needs with 220 and 110 and exhaust fans! The backyard is your own personal paradise. The walkout basement leads to a covered patio, firepit, water feature, and of course the lake! There is even a shed to store all the fishing rods and outdoor toys. Plan extra time to tour this one- there is so much to see! Call Denise for more info or to schedule your private tour today!
