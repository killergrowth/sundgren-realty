6 total bedrooms! (2 in basement are non-conforming). Huge kitchen with 7-foot island, 6-foot buffet, and cabinets on all 4 walls! Brand new slow-close cabinets with trash can slider and two lazy Susans. New air-fryer convection stove, microwave, and dishwasher. Main floor, including bedrooms, has new luxury vinyl flooring. Master bath has classic black and white tile floor with new dual sink vanities and fully remodeled shower with dual shower heads.
