Property Description

Located in central Salina just one block west of 9th Street, 314 S. 10th St. offers a convenient location close to downtown and Cottonwood Elementary School. This 4-bedroom, 2-full-bath home features one bathroom on the main floor and a second bathroom upstairs, providing flexibility. Laundry is set on the main floor, meaning no steps to trek over. The property also includes newer vinyl siding, a 1 1/2-year-old roof and a small rear deck, adding to its appeal and functionality. The sewer connection to the city main has been completely redone. With a little tender loving care, this home has great potential and would make an excellent rental property or investment opportunity. This property is being sold as is.
