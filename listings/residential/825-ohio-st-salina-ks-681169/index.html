Property Description

Solid two bedroom home at 825 S Ohio. Home has a circle drive for easy entry and exit from the home, and two car attached garage. Inside there is a modern kitchen and all appliances remain. Home has two bedrooms, two baths on the main level. Living room has wood burning fireplace. There is a main floor laundry with room for a daybed if needed. The back yard is spacious and fenced, so plenty of space for kids and pets to play. There is also a storage shed. Downstairs you'll find a fun rec room that is huge! There is so much space for multiple furniture layouts, gaming room, even space to add another bedroom or craft room if desired. The lower level also has a half bath.
