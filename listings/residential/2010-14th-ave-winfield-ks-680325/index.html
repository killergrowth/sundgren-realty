Property Description

Looking for space, storage, and a little room to enjoy the outdoors? This 3-bedroom home sits on approximately half an acre and offers plenty of potential! Inside, you'll find new flooring and subflooring, a separate dining room, and tons of storage throughout. The home features 1 full bathroom and 2 half bathrooms. Outside, enjoy established garden beds featuring basil, cilantro, and mint, a storage shed, and a spacious 24' x 30' carport. The roof is approximately 5 years old, and the property backs up to a spring-fed creek, adding a unique touch to the outdoor setting. Whether you're looking for your next project or a property with room to make it your own, this home is full of possibilities. Property is being sold in its present condition, AS IS.
