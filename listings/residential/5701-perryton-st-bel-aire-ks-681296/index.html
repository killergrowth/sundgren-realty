Property Description

Welcome to 5701 E. Perryton, a truly distinctive mid-century modern home in Bel Aire offering architectural character, versatile living spaces, and a setting that's hard to find. From the moment you arrive, the home's oversized breezeway makes a statement. Featuring a modern frosted glass overhead garage door, impressive open space, and 240V hookups, this unique area offers endless possibilities for entertaining, additional parking, hobbies, recreation, or whatever best suits your lifestyle. The breezeway also connects to an additional attached garage with an interior storage shed, providing even more flexibility and storage. Situated on an impressive 0.41-acre lot, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers a thoughtfully designed main level along with a finished basement featuring a fourth non-conforming bedroom and additional living space. Step inside through the double entry doors to find beautiful brick flooring, vaulted ceilings, distinctive architectural details, and eye-catching light fixtures that give the home its unmistakable mid-century modern appeal. The spacious living area centers around a striking double-sided fireplace and flows naturally into the surrounding spaces, creating an inviting atmosphere throughout. The kitchen combines style and functionality with a large island offering seating for four, granite countertops, two pantries, and stainless steel appliances that all remain with the home. The main-level primary suite offers generous space, vaulted ceilings, and a private bathroom featuring a walk-in tiled shower and distinctive concrete sink. Just outside the bedroom, a private patio provides the perfect spot for morning coffee or relaxing at the end of the day. A second full bathroom and convenient main-level laundry complete the first floor. Downstairs, the finished basement provides even more room to spread out, featuring a large additional living area, a fourth non-conforming bedroom, and an exceptionally spacious storage room. The home's double-sided fireplace adds another unique touch, bringing warmth and character to the lower-level living space as well. Outside, the expansive backyard offers plenty of room to enjoy the outdoors, with mature trees, a fully fenced yard, a built-in horseshoe pit, and a freshly poured oversized concrete patio with concrete steps. Whether you're hosting friends, enjoying a quiet evening outside, or simply taking advantage of the extra space, this property offers plenty of opportunities to make it your own. With its unmistakable mid-century modern character, impressive lot, flexible living spaces, and one-of-a-kind breezeway, 5701 E. Perryton is far from an ordinary home. It's a property that blends distinctive design with everyday functionality and offers features you simply won't find in many others.
