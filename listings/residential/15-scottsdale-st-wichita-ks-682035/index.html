Property Description

Welcome to 15 N Scottsdale, a beautifully maintained residence in the desirable Scottsdale at Shadybrook Farm. Offering five bedrooms, three full baths, one half bath, and a spacious three-car garage, this impressive home has timeless appeal, generous living areas, and private backyard outdoor setting with mature trees that backs up to a Creek. Tastefully updated kitchen and hearth room serves as the heart and center of the home, with a Beautiful Double sided Gas Fireplace. Granite countertops, a gas range, double ovens in the kitchen, with open windows makes it ideal for gatherings. An Inviting Backyard retreat is a standout feature, Saltwater inground Swimming Pool, Patio, Mature trees, and established landscaping.
