Property Description

Welcome home to 1481 S Shiloh Ct. This well-maintained 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom, 3 car garage home is sure to impress. From the moment you arrive, the curb appeal and charm are evident with a welcoming covered front porch, mature landscaping, and a fully fenced backyard. Inside, you'll find an open concept living space with solid surface flooring, newer carpets throughout main floor bedrooms, updated lighting, granite kitchen countertops, a spacious walk-in pantry, a separate laundry room, and a desirable split-bedroom floor plan that offers both privacy and functionality. The outdoor living space is equally inviting, featuring a recently stained deck that leads to a partially covered back patio area perfect for entertaining or relaxing. The finished basement adds valuable living space with view out windows featuring a large rec room, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a wet bar. All kitchen and laundry appliances will remain with the home, making your move even easier. Listed below appraised value and meticulously maintained, this move-in-ready home offers exceptional value and pride of ownership throughout. Bally Clare offers a neighborhood pool, playground and fishing pond with sidewalk paths. If you've been searching for a home that combines space, updates, and affordability, this one deserves a spot at the top of your list.
