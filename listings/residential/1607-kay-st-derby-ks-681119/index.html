Property Description

Welcome to 1607 E Kay! Nestled in a beautiful, established Derby neighborhood with walking paths, two ponds, and NO SPECIALS, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath home with a 3-car garage has so much to offer! From the inviting front porch to the spacious interior, there's plenty of room to feel right at home. Inside, you'll find 9-foot ceilings, beautiful tray ceiling details, a kitchen with solid surface countertops and a double oven, and plenty of space for gathering with family and friends. But the backyard might just steal the show! Whether you're hosting summer get-togethers or enjoying a quiet afternoon, the 18x36 in-ground pool and stamped concrete patio create a wonderful space to relax, entertain, and make memories. With updates including Class 4 impact-resistant shingles and a newer HVAC system, plus a 3-car garage featuring impressive 13-foot ceilings and abundant storage space, this home offers a wonderful combination of space, comfort, and charm. Come see what makes this one so special!
