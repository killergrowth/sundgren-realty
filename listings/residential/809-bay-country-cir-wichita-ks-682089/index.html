Property Description

Welcome to 809 N Bay Country Cir, a spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath ranch offering more than 2,500 square feet of finished living space and tremendous potential to make it your own. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Country Meadows West, this home offers a comfortable and flexible floor plan with three bedrooms and two baths on the main level, including the primary suite and convenient main-floor laundry. The finished basement provides even more room to spread out with an additional bedroom, full bath, expansive family and recreation room, and plenty of space for storage, hobbies, a home office or future updates. Outside, the property continues to stand apart with a fenced backyard, covered patio, additional garage/workshop, storage building and dedicated concrete RV parking, providing options that can be difficult to find in this price range. Open land behind the property adds to the sense of space and privacy while still enjoying the conveniences of an established neighborhood. Located in the highly regarded Goddard School District, the home offers easy access to west Wichita shopping, dining, entertainment, NewMarket Square and major thoroughfares, making trips throughout the Wichita area convenient. With generous indoor and outdoor living space, a desirable west-side location and a collection of unique features already in place, 809 N Bay Country Cir represents an excellent opportunity for a buyer with vision to add personal touches, make thoughtful updates and create a home that truly fits their lifestyle for years to come.
