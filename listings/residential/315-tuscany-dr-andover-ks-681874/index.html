Property Description

Investors - Spacious ranch home offering great potential in an established neighborhood! Features include a large fenced backyard, covered front porch, oversized wood deck and huge custom stamped concrete patio. Inside, you'll find a vaulted living room with a gas fireplace, a kitchen with eating bar and tile flooring, and a primary suite featuring vaulted ceilings, a large walk-in closet, double raised vanities, and a separate tub and shower. The finished basement includes a large family room, spacious 4th bedroom with double doors and direct bath access, plus abundant storage space. Additional features include tile flooring in the kitchen, bathrooms, and laundry room, privacy fencing, and an electric garage door opener. Property is being sold as-is and is in need of cosmetic updating throughout, including flooring, paint, kitchen, and bathroom improvements. Excellent opportunity for investors, flippers, or buyers looking to build equity through renovations.
