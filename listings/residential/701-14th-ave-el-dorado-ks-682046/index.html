Property Description

Welcome home to this move-in-ready bi-level in El Dorado! Featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and beautiful curb appeal, this home is sure to impress. The inviting front porch, accented by classic white columns, offers the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the neighborhood. Inside, you'll find all-new carpet throughout. The upper level features a galley-style kitchen that opens to a formal dining room, along with three bedrooms and a full bathroom. The lower level offers a spacious family room, an additional bathroom, and a separate laundry room with plenty of extra storage space, plus convenient access to the garage. Step outside from the kitchen onto the large covered deck overlooking the spacious, fully fenced backyard. Enjoy even more outdoor living with a patio area, large storage shed, and RV parking. With plenty of space inside and out, this home is ready for its next owner to move in and make it their own!
