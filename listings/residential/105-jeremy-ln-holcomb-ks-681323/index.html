Property Description

Looking for a home with good bones and room to build equity? 105 Jeremy Lane delivers. The ranch sits on a lot with mature shade trees, a covered front porch, and a wide driveway with plenty of parking. Big-ticket items are already handled: the roof was replaced in 2025, and the HVAC system was recently serviced. The kitchen offers warm oak cabinetry and open display shelving. It comes with stainless appliances, including a gas range and a built-in microwave. A breakfast bar and wood-look flooring finish the space, and an attached wall of built-ins adds storage and a ready-made coffee bar. The main level has three bedrooms, all with ceiling fans, and a full bath with a large vanity and a wall-to-wall mirror. The full basement is where this home really stands out. It includes: A spacious family or rec room, a long bonus area with built-in cabinets and a workbench, a dedicated laundry room, and a separate storage room with built-in shelving and an egress-style window. There's plenty of room for a gym, a workshop, or hobby space, with the potential to finish more square footage and add value down the road. Whether you're buying your first home, right-sizing, or adding to a portfolio, this low-maintenance home is worth a look. Schedule your private showing today!
