Property Description

Recently REDUCED IN PRICE, this custom-built, full-brick home in Derby’s sought-after Oakwood Valley Estates offers over 4,000 square feet, 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, a 55,000-gallon swimming pool, a wooded 0.70-acre lot with a creek, and a 4-car tandem garage. The spacious entry and soaring ceilings create an impressive welcome. The main floor includes a formal living room, formal dining room, kitchen with informal dining area, and two fireplaces. The primary bedroom features a balcony overlooking the backyard. Its newly remodeled custom bathroom offers a large walk-in shower and a modern, spa-like design. The kitchen also features a brand-new, never-used matching stainless-steel oven, microwave, and dishwasher. The basement adds space to relax and entertain, with a third fireplace, wet bar, and room for a card or game table. Outside, the covered deck overlooks the fenced backyard, wooded setting, and creek. The impressive pool includes an extended shallow area for added enjoyment. Enjoy a peaceful setting with the convenience of city amenities, plus abundant parking, storage, and workspace in the tandem garage. Title work has been started at Kansas Secured Title.
