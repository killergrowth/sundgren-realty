Property Description

This is an excellent opportunity to own a custom-built, full-brick home in the highly sought-after Oakwood Valley Estates neighborhood of Derby, Kansas. Boasting over 4,000 square feet, this remarkably well-maintained, 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home features a 55,000-gallon swimming pool, a large wooded lot with a creek, and a 4-car tandem garage. The spacious entryway and soaring ceilings create an impressive welcome. The main floor offers a formal living room, formal dining room, informal dining area in the kitchen, and two fireplaces that bring warmth and character to the living spaces. The primary bedroom includes a balcony overlooking the beautiful backyard, while the newly remodeled custom primary bathroom features a large walk-in shower and a modern, spa-like design. The basement provides additional space for relaxing and entertaining with a third fireplace, wet bar, and room for a card or game table. Outside, the covered deck overlooks the expansive fenced backyard, wooded setting, and creek. The impressive backyard swimming pool includes an extended shallow area for added enjoyment. Enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of country living with the convenience of city amenities. The 4-car tandem garage provides abundant parking, storage, and workspace, completing this distinctive Oakwood Valley Estates home. Title work has been started at Kansas Secured Title
