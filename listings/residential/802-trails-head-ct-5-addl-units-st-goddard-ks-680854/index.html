Property Description

GREAT OPPORTUNITY to own a package of 6 NEW CONSTRUCTION duplexes with a 1-year builder warranty in the Goddard school district!!! Located in the Trails End Addition, this package is ideal for investors seeking high-end, low-maintenance rentals. The combination of low maintenance, and Goddard schools, makes this a highly attractive investment that commands competitive rent rates. These properties have quality finishes throughout. Each unit is identical, offering nearly 1,200 SF of open-concept living space, featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and a 2-car garage. Luxury vinyl flooring runs throughout the entire units, and quartz countertops on every surface -- kitchen and both bathrooms. The kitchen features a spacious pantry, and appliances include range/oven, dishwasher and microwave. Both the primary and second bedrooms feature walk-in closets. Ten-foot living room ceilings create a spacious feel, while the light, bright and clean color palette gives the properties a fresh, modern look. The large kitchen island offers plenty of room for meal prep or casual dining. HOA dues include access to the community pool, clubhouse, mowing, and sprinkler system service for added convenience. Both general and special taxes have not been fully spread yet and have been estimated per current county tax records and mil levy calculations. Special taxes are estimated at $354 per month, per building. Properties are offered as a package of six or individual duplex buildings at $365,000 per building. Buyers and agents to verify all information pertinent to buyer.
