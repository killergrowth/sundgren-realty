Property Description

Charming 4-Bedroom Home on a Spacious Corner Lot with Modern Updates and Solar Panels! Welcome home to this inviting 4-bedroom, 1-bath property situated on a desirable corner lot and featuring numerous updates and improvements! Inside, you'll find beautiful hardwood floors throughout the main living areas, updated paint, updated light fixtures, and a new HVAC system installed in 2025. Step outside and enjoy the large backyard, complete with a recently added privacy fence and an oversized covered patio, perfect for entertaining guests, grilling out, or simply relaxing outdoors. With four bedrooms, numerous updates, a spacious corner lot, and the added benefit of solar energy, this home has plenty to offer. Schedule your private showing today!
