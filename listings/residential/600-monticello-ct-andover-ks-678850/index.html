Property Description

The Georgetown floor plan by Liberty Communities offers a comfortable 4-bedroom, 2-bath layout with 1,608 square feet of single-level living in the desirable Andover school district. This plan makes excellent use of space with an open living and dining area that connects naturally to the kitchen, creating an easy everyday flow for gathering, relaxing, and entertaining. The primary suite is positioned toward the back of the home for added privacy and features its own bath and walk-in closet, while three additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, office space, hobbies, or growing household needs. A dedicated laundry area near the garage entry adds convenience, and the covered front porch and rear patio give the home inviting indoor-outdoor appeal. Located near Andover amenities with convenient access to east Wichita, the Georgetown blends practical design, modern new construction, and the versatility buyers are looking for.
