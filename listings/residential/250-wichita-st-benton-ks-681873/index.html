Property Description

Welcome to 250 S Wichita St in the heart of Benton—where small-town charm meets fantastic modern updates! This delightful 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom home packs an incredible amount of character and comfort into every inch of its well-designed layout. Step up to the inviting covered front porch, featuring classic white columns and plenty of room to relax in the shade. Inside, you'll immediately notice the airy, open floor plan flooded with natural light. The home feels incredibly fresh thanks to brand-new, plush grey carpet in the living spaces and crisp white interior paint throughout. And the updates don't stop there—you'll find beautiful new flooring flowing seamlessly through the kitchen, laundry room, and bathroom, giving practically the whole house a wonderful, move-in-ready feel! You'll also love the gorgeous built-in shelving that adds a custom, charming touch to the space. Entertain with ease in the formal dining room, which connects to a well-equipped kitchen. The kitchen boasts lovely wood cabinetry, ample counter space, and the fantastic bonus that all the appliances stay with the home. Doing the dishes is hardly a chore when you have a window perfectly positioned right above the kitchen sink, overlooking the backyard. The full bathroom is neatly updated, showcasing a stylish grey vanity alongside the new floors. Plus, you'll have plenty of storage with a fantastic, large master closet. The attractive green exterior was just painted in 2022, giving the home outstanding curb appeal. Out back, features a concrete patio offers a great space for weekend grilling, container gardening, or simply enjoying the fresh air. Location is everything, and this spot absolutely delivers. You are conveniently situated close to the local school, daycare, city building, and a cozy neighborhood coffee shop. Plus, being right off Highway 254 means commuting and running errands is a total breeze.
