Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 3-bath home situated on a corner lot in a great location near McConnell Air Force Base! Pride of ownership shines throughout this inviting property. The lovely main-floor living room features a cozy fireplace, creating the perfect place to relax and unwind. The spacious kitchen offers plenty of room to cook and gather, featuring concrete countertops and a beautiful bay window that fills the space with natural light. It's the perfect spot for your favorite plants or even a charming indoor herb garden! Downstairs, you'll find a large family room complete with a projector and screen, ideal for movie nights, entertaining, or watching the big game. An additional finished room in the basement features new sheetrock and carpet, providing flexible space for a home office, hobby room, or whatever suits your needs. Abundant storage throughout the home provides plenty of space to keep everything organized and tucked away. Recent improvements include a brand-new roof and basement wall repairs. Step outside to enjoy the large deck overlooking the fully fenced backyard, offering a wonderful setting for outdoor gatherings, grilling, or simply relaxing. A 2-car garage adds convenience and even more storage possibilities. With its spacious layout, thoughtful updates, generous storage, desirable location, and obvious pride of ownership, this home has so much to offer. Schedule your showing today!
