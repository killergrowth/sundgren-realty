About This Property

Luxury living with every detail in place! This stunning custom-built home offers a dramatic entrance with glass and iron doors leading into a spacious Great Room with quarter-sawn oak floors and beautiful lake views. The gourmet kitchen features a gas range, double ovens, warming drawer, pot filler, walk-in pantry, granite countertops, and abundant storage with custom drawers and lighting. A hearth room, formal living and dining areas, and a private office complete the main living spaces. The main level includes a guest suite with full bath and walk-in closet, plus a luxurious Master Suite with a barrel ceiling, gas fireplace, deck access, soaking tub, walk-through shower, and custom walk-in closet. Downstairs, enjoy 10-foot ceilings, a full second kitchen, home theater, two more bedrooms with en suite baths, a game room, safe room, and a cozy sitting area with a fireplace. A second laundry and full bath provide easy pool access. Outside is an entertainer’s dream: a saltwater pool, hot tub, outdoor kitchen with TV, plenty of seating, new gas fire pit, new fire wall for ambiance, sandy beach, and a private boat dock. Additional features include double furnaces and water heaters, UV-protected windows, a 4-car garage, and a separate 3-bedroom, 3-bath guest house on a 1-acre double lot.
