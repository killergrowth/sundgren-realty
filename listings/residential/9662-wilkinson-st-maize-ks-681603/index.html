Property Description

MAIZE SCHOOL DISTRICT. LAKE LOT. SALTWATER POOL. YOU NEED THIS ADDRESS! Backyard dreams meet everyday comfort in this five-year-young M&M Construction home, perfectly positioned in the desirable Henwick community. From the lush lawn and thoughtfully placed landscaping to the charming curb appeal and incredible backyard retreat, this address makes a statement from the moment you arrive. Offering five bedrooms, three bathrooms, and a three-car garage, the thoughtfully designed split-bedroom, open-concept floor plan delivers functionality and features you’ve been looking for. The kitchen showcases a spacious island with eating bar, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, pantry, and plenty of room to gather. The private primary suite is a retreat of its own, complete with a stylish accent wall and beautiful neighborhood lake views. The ensuite bath offers a double vanity, relaxing soaker tub, separate shower, water closet, and spacious wardrobe closet. Everyday convenience is built right in, with a functional drop zone and separate laundry room just off the garage entry. Downstairs, the spacious view-out basement expands your living and entertaining possibilities with a wet bar, recreation space, and plenty of room to unwind, host, or make your own. And then there’s the backyard. Enjoy morning coffee on the covered deck, afternoons on the spacious patio, and endless summer days in the show-stopping 20’ x 40’ heated, saltwater pool with tanning ledge. The fully fenced backyard, storage space, and sprinkler system on well water round out this outdoor setup. It’s your own private escape, with the added bonus of a lake lot in one of Maize’s sought-after communities. The pool is closed for the season but professionally maintained year-round by Diamond Pools. Pool photos are available upon request. Henwick offers the convenient accessibility to major amenities, K96, and Maize schools while enjoying the luxuries of a neighborhood clubhouse, pool, playground, and more. Five bedrooms. Maize schools. Lake views. A backyard made for summer. This is the address you’ve been waiting for!
