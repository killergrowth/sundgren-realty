Property Description

Welcome home to this charming four-bedroom, two-bath home offering comfort, functionality, and room to grow! Fresh paint on the interior/exterior. The master bedroom is spacious with an updated fully tilled walk in shower and two shower heads. You have 2 additional bedrooms, with a hall bath on the main level along with updated counter tops, new range and a large stainless farm sink. You will also find large living room and spacious dining room for those family dinners. In the basement you will find plenty of storage space, your laundry area and a large 4th bedroom featuring an egress window and a closet, making it a great opportunity for a guest space, home office, theater or recreation area. The home also features a detached oversized garage providing electricity for a workshop, additional room for storage, or your everyday needs. Enjoy the outdoors in the backyard on your patio and complete with raised planters perfect for growing your own vegetables, herbs, and seasonal favorites. With versatile living spaces and thoughtful features throughout, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and opportunity to make it your own!
