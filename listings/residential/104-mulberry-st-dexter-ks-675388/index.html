About This Property

This modular home sits on a permanent foundation in the quiet town of Dexter, KS. The lot spans the block from Central St to Valley Street giving plenty of room from neighbors. The lot includes the home, a detached, oversized 3 car garage with work space, built in storage shelves and separate 200 amp electric service. There is a secluded pavilion surrounded by a grove of mature Kansas pecan trees. There is a retractable projection screen for summer night outdoors movies. The lot is filled with a wide variety of trees and shrubs. Included is a garden shed to keep tools safe and handy. The home is adorned with natural limestone columns, archways, and aesthetic masonry including the grand entry to the home. Before entering, you'll notice the wrap around porch, perfect for morning coffee or listening to the ball games across the street. You'll enter into a formal foyer with a vaulted ceiling. Immediately to your left you will find a large office behind double French doors. To your right is the formal living room wrapped by huge windows. The formal dining room adjoins under another vaulted ceiling. Moving into the gourmet kitchen, the expansive island with veggie sink makes meal prep a pleasure. A built in desk allows for checking recipes. There is space for a dinette here. At the opposite end is an eating bar. All of this is under another vaulted ceiling feature. This opens into the huge family room under cathedral ceiling and a wood burning fireplace with stone front and glass doors. Moving into the hall, you'll find double doors entering into the Master Suite. Another vaulted ceiling feature is topped off with an arch transom window. The walk in closet will easily hold your clothes and has extra overhead shelving, making the 10' ceiling an appreciated storage space. The Master Ensuite brings relaxing luxury. A large vanity area boasts double sinks and separate vanity/make-up area with mirror and sitting chair. A massive whirlpool-jet tub begs to take your stress away and down the drain. A large separate shower room has a tiled 5' glass enclosed shower with dual showers, rainfall and standard hand shower. The commode is here as well with a linen cabinet handy for towels and extra paper products. Finding your way back down the hall are 2 similar sized bedrooms, both with large walk in closets. The hall bathroom includes an oversized tub/shower combo. Moving on, you'll find yourself now in the laundry room at the back door. This room too, holds special features. One of the 2 HVAC units, a water softener, whole house water filter system and the tankless, gas water heater. Never run out of hot water again. This large property is perfect for entertaining outside under the Wisteria covered pergola, the cozy pavilion in the pecan grove, the large lot or the more intimate porch area. Or if Kansas weather drives you inside, the huge kitchen, eating bar, dinette area, and formal dining room will still allow many guests to enjoy themselves. There are numerous smaller bells & whistles throughout this grand home that must be seen. THIS HOME IS OWNED AND IS BEING OFFEED BY A LICENSED KANSAS REAL ESTATE SALESPERSON
