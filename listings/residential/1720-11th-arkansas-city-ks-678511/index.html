Property Description

OPEN, INVITING & READY TO WELCOME YOU HOME! From the moment you step inside this 3-bedroom, 1-bath ranch, you’ll appreciate the open floor plan and comfortable, easy-living layout. The spacious living room is the heart of the home, featuring a beautiful fireplace that creates the perfect place to gather. And it’s not just for looks the fireplace insert is ducted throughout the home, providing an additional heat source during those cold Kansas months and helping keep heating costs down. Durable vinyl plank flooring extends through most of the home, with carpet in two of the bedrooms. The eat-in kitchen provides plenty of space for meals and gatherings and includes a microwave. A door from the kitchen leads to the patio and fenced backyard. All three bedrooms offer comfortable space, and the bathroom has been nicely remodeled. Need a little extra space? You’ll love the 22x22 detached workshop/one-car garage! Whether you need room for a vehicle, hobbies, projects, tools, or extra storage, this is a great bonus. The property also offers additional parking and convenient alley access. Located close to schools and offering an open layout, cozy fireplace, fenced backyard, and that hard-to-find workshop space, this home checks a lot of boxes. Come take a look and see if this could be the one you’ve been waiting for!
