About This Property

An iconic Campus Woods property, recognized and admired in the area, is now available for its next chapter. Situated on a beautifully wooded corner lot with exceptional curb appeal, this remarkable home offers over 5,000 square feet of carefully designed living space. Mature trees, outstanding landscaping, an irrigation well, a sprinkler system, and expansive outdoor living areas create a private retreat. Inside, oak hardwood floors, custom millwork, vaulted ceilings, and walls of NEW PELLA WINDOWS (2023) showcase the quality and craftsmanship found throughout. The grand living room features built-in cabinetry, a gas fireplace highlighted with an original Phil Epp painting above, and access to an oversized deck with a gazebo overlooking the park-like backyard. The heart of the home is a stunning kitchen featuring Becker Custom Cabinetry, granite countertops, accent lighting, coffee bar, eating bar, double ovens, cooktop, built-in microwave, pantry space, and both casual and formal dining areas. The formal dining room is highlighted by oak floors and a built-in china hutch. The private primary suite is a retreat of its own, complete with coffered ceiling details, window seat, oversized walk-in closet, attached laundry room, and a beautifully remodeled bath featuring double sinks, custom cabinetry, and a tiled walk-in shower with dual shower heads and built-in seating. A spectacular Colorado-inspired addition creates one of the home's most memorable spaces. Featuring soaring vaulted ceilings, cedar walls and ceilings, exposed beams, and a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, it is the perfect gathering room for entertaining, games, or simply relaxing. The finished lower level offers incredible flexibility with additional bedrooms, a large family room with a third fireplace, wet bar, dedicated game room, full bath, storm shelter, abundant storage, and cedar-lined closet space. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and entertainers alike will appreciate the oversized three-car garage with built-in cabinetry and the exceptional attached workshop/man cave; an ideal space for projects, collections, and recreation. The space was designed to replace the window/door section with garage doors if you should choose. Additional amenities include an alarm system, whole-house vacuum system, water softener, enclosed breezeway, multiple pantry spaces, and a NEW IR ROOF installed in 2022. Just moments from Bethel College, walking paths, and convenient access to Highway 135, this is more than a home; it is one of Campus Woods' landmark properties.
