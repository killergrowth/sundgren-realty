Property Description

Located on the southeast side of Toronto Lake, this 3 bedroom, 2 bath home offers a peaceful rural retreat. Situated on 6.6 acres surrounded by timber and natural Kansas beauty, this location provides easy access to hunting, fishing, boating, and everything outdoor. The 1,188sf house is a manufactured home on a permanent foundation built in 1990. It has a large open kitchen/dining area, enclosed sun porch with wood burning stove, central heat and air, and new (2022) metal roof. There is a 2+ car detached garage with room to work on your projects.
