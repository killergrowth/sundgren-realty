Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully maintained, move-in ready home in The Seasons Addition, located in the desirable Goddard Eisenhower School District with NO SPECIAL TAXES! Fresh, neutral finishes throughout make this home easy to love! Enjoy peace of mind with a new roof and fresh interior and exterior paint, all completed within the past two years. The inviting open floor plan features LVP flooring throughout the main-floor living areas and a spacious kitchen with an island, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. All appliances remain, including the refrigerator, washer, and dryer! The primary bedroom offers two closets for added convenience and storage. Downstairs, the finished daylight basement provides even more living space with a comfortable family room, a separate bonus room with french doors that's ideal for a home office, exercise room, theatre room, or hobby space, plus a third bedroom and second full bathroom. A dedicated storage room also houses the washer and dryer. Step outside to enjoy the large backyard, complete with a privacy fence and storage shed, offering plenty of room for outdoor activities and entertaining. The garage has a slat wall system and metal rack for storage that will remain with the home. With its neutral décor, thoughtful layout, numerous recent updates, and NO SPECIALS, this home is truly move-in ready. Don't miss this fantastic opportunity in The Seasons!
