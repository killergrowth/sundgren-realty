Property Description

Space, updates and nearly three-quarters of an acre—this west Wichita ranch has so much to offer! Situated on a .69-acre lot, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home offers over 3,200 sq. ft. of finished living space and has been freshly painted inside and out, giving the home a bright, airy and updated feel. Even better, the big-ticket items are already done with a brand-new roof and gutters! Step inside to beautiful wood flooring and a spacious living room with a gas fireplace. The large kitchen offers granite countertops, abundant cabinet and counter space, a breakfast bar and pantry, with a convenient main-floor laundry and mudroom nearby. The primary suite features a private bath with double sinks and separate tub and shower, while new carpet adds a fresh touch to an additional main-floor bedroom. The finished daylight basement provides plenty of room to spread out with a large family/rec room, wet bar, additional bedrooms and bath, plus great storage space. Outside, the .69-acre lot provides room to relax, garden, play or entertain, with a covered patio, fenced yard and storage shed. The sprinkler system is supplied by an irrigation well—a great bonus for a property this size. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists and anyone needing workspace will appreciate the oversized tandem garage. Use the additional space as a third-car garage or create a dedicated workshop while still maintaining two-car parking. With fresh updates, a new roof, over 3,200 finished sq. ft., a flexible garage/workshop and a hard-to-find .69-acre lot, 2226 N 135th St W offers space both inside and out!
