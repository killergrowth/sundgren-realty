Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully maintained brick ranch offering the perfect combination of space, comfort, and functionality. Situated on nearly half an acre, this exceptional property features 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, a dedicated office, and an additional non-conforming bedroom in the finished basement—providing flexible space for guests, hobbies, a home gym, or whatever best fits your lifestyle. From the moment you step inside, you’ll appreciate the home’s soaring vaulted ceilings accented with beautiful exposed beams, creating an open, inviting atmosphere full of warmth and character. Engineered hardwood flooring, fresh paint throughout, and thoughtful updates completed over the past few years make this home truly move-in ready. The spacious main level offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, inviting living areas, and a layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Downstairs, the fully finished basement significantly expands the home’s functionality with an additional bedroom, dedicated office, non-conforming bedroom, full bathroom, a large family room, game room, and generous storage. Whether you need extra space for guests, multigenerational living, working from home, hobbies, or simply room to spread out, this home offers endless possibilities. Outside, you’ll discover your own private retreat designed for both relaxation and entertaining. The expansive, fully fenced backyard offers exceptional outdoor living space with a spacious patio and an enclosed porch perfect for year-round enjoyment. Whether you’re hosting summer barbecues, enjoying quiet evenings under the stars, or simply unwinding in your own backyard oasis, this property delivers the ideal blend of privacy and comfort. A large storage shed adds even more convenience, providing ample space for lawn equipment, gardening tools, and outdoor essentials. In addition to the attached two-car garage, you’ll find an impressive finished 20’ x 30’ detached garage featuring carpet flooring, built-in workbenches, abundant storage, air conditioning, and a setup ready for a wood-burning stove. Whether you’re dreaming of the ultimate workshop, game-day hangout, hobby space, home business, or additional entertaining area, this detached garage is truly one of the property’s standout features. With generously sized bedrooms, multiple living and entertaining areas, abundant storage throughout, and over four garage spaces, this home truly checks every box. Properties offering this much finished living space, thoughtful updates, timeless character, and a detached garage of this caliber are rarely available. If you’ve been searching for a home that offers room to grow while delivering quality, versatility, and exceptional indoor and outdoor living, this Halstead gem is one you won’t want to miss.
