Property Description

Life in Reflection Ridge is truly exceptional. Situated on a coveted street bordered by golf course views on both sides, this beautifully maintained home offers generous living space, thoughtful updates, and access to one of the area's most desirable communities. Inside, you'll find an updated kitchen, a formal dining room, a spacious laundry room with utility sink, four bedrooms, and two dedicated office spaces, with one conveniently located on the main floor and another upstairs. The expansive primary suite serves as a private retreat, featuring a walk-in closet and a well-appointed en suite bath. The finished basement provides outstanding flexibility with a second full kitchen, a large family room, and abundant storage space, making it ideal for entertaining, multi-generational living, or extended guest stays. Step outside to enjoy partial views of Hole 6 from the stamped concrete patio overlooking the beautifully landscaped backyard. The elevated front porch with classic brick accents enhances the home's welcoming curb appeal. Additional highlights include a three-car garage and a new roof for added peace of mind. Residents of Reflection Ridge enjoy an outstanding lifestyle with access to pickleball and tennis courts, multiple playgrounds, walking paths, a championship golf course, and a community swimming pool. Located within the Maize South school district and just minutes from K-96 and the new Market Square development, this home offers the perfect balance of convenience, recreation, and refined golf-course living.
