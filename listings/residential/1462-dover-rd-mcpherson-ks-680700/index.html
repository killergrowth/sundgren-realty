Property Description

Move-in ready and full of charm, this beautiful McPherson home is situated on a quiet street and offers great curb appeal, a large fenced backyard, and plenty of space for everyday living and entertaining. Inside, you’ll find abundant natural light, beautiful hardwood floors, and spacious living areas. The kitchen features quartz countertops and a generous dining area, while both the living room and family room provide plenty of room to relax and gather. The finished basement adds even more versatility with a family room, entertainment room, wet bar, and separate laundry room. With 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, there’s room for everyone! Outside, enjoy the added appeal of an inground swimming pool. The sellers are offering a buyer allowance at closing to repair the pool or fill it in, giving the next owner flexibility to make it their own.
