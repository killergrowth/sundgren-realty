Property Description

Charming, well-maintained, and thoughtfully updated, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home is full of character and offers so much more than you might expect! Tastefully remodeled while preserving its original charm, you'll love the beautiful hardwood floors, timeless granite countertops, and tile flooring in the kitchen. The master bedroom offers cozy carpeting, while the spacious laundry and mudroom area adds everyday convenience. And let's talk about a few features you don't often find in this price range, TWO full bathrooms and a primary bedroom with its own walk-in closet! Outside, the darling covered front porch is the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, while the newer, generously sized back deck provides a great space for relaxing or entertaining. The detached garage, complete with an overhead door and convenient alley access, offers additional parking or storage, and a second detached structure provides even more possibilities! With its inviting charm, thoughtful updates, and hard-to-find features, this is one you'll want to see in person.
