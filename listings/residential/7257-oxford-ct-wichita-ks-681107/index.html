Property Description

Tucked on a cul-de-sac near 21st & Rock Road, this 1.5-story home offers 2,439 total livable square feet and a layout full of possibilities. The kitchen stands out with upgraded cabinetry, new quartz countertops, engineered hardwood floors, and a spacious eating area. Two main-floor bedrooms, a full bath, laundry room, open office, living room, and formal dining room provide plenty of room to spread out. Upstairs, the oversized 15' x 25' primary suite features vaulted ceilings, a double-sink vanity, soaking tub, and separate shower. The basement adds an open rec room with walk-in storage plus a separate bonus room ready for your vision. Outside, enjoy a large backyard backing to HOA common area and a playground. Just minutes from Bradley Fair and Wichita’s new Cheesecake Factory, this home offers space, character, and the opportunity to make it your own.
