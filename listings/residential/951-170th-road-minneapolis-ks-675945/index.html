Property Description

Welcome to 951 N. 170th Rd., Minneapolis, KS—a peaceful country property offering comfort, functionality, and room to enjoy rural living on just shy of 5 acres ±. The 960-square-foot modular home sits on a permanent concrete foundation and features two bedrooms and two bathrooms, providing a comfortable and efficient layout. For added peace of mind, the property also includes a storm cellar that is only a couple of years old. A standout feature is the approximately 30' x 40' fully insulated shop, thoughtfully equipped for a variety of uses. Inside, you'll find a full bathroom, generator outlet, RV hookup with waste disposal site, and ample space for hobbies, storage, or a workshop. Whether you're looking for a country residence, a place to store recreational vehicles, or a property with versatile outbuildings, this acreage offers the perfect combination of convenience, practicality, and open space.
