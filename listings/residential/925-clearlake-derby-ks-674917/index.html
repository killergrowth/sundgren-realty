Property Description

NOW OFFERED AT $339,000 — an even better opportunity for low-maintenance patio home living in the highly sought-after Courtyard at The Oaks! This beautifully maintained, zero-entry home combines stylish finishes, a spacious open layout, and resort-style community amenities for an easy, lock-it-and-leave-it lifestyle. This 2-bedroom, 2-bath patio home features an open, airy floor plan with vaulted ceilings, luxury vinyl flooring, and abundant natural light. The stunning kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring quartz countertops, abundant cabinetry, under-cabinet lighting, a spacious pantry, dedicated coffee bar, and a full suite of appliances. The inviting living room is highlighted by an electric fireplace and flows seamlessly to the private covered patio—an ideal place to enjoy your morning coffee, unwind after a long day, or entertain family and friends. The spacious primary suite offers a relaxing retreat with dual vanities, a beautifully tiled walk-in shower, and an oversized walk-in closet. A second bedroom and full bath provide flexible space for overnight guests, a home office, or your favorite hobbies. Additional highlights include a separate laundry room, oversized two-car garage, thoughtful storage throughout, and quality finishes that make this home truly move-in ready. Life at Courtyard at The Oaks extends beyond your front door. Residents enjoy outstanding community amenities including a clubhouse, swimming pool, pickleball courts, and beautifully maintained common areas. With the HOA handling lawn care, exterior painting, snow removal, trash, you'll enjoy the freedom of a true low-maintenance lifestyle. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, healthcare, parks, and everything Derby has to offer, this home combines everyday convenience with exceptional comfort. Whether you're downsizing, simplifying, or simply looking for a home that lets you enjoy life instead of maintaining it, this exceptional patio home offers everything you've been waiting for. Come discover the comfort, convenience, and welcoming community that make Courtyard at The Oaks one of Derby's most desirable places to call home.
