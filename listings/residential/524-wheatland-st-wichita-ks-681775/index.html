Property Description

A daylight basement with its own wet bar sets this 4 bed, 3 bath ranch apart. The home offers 2,304 sq ft of finished space. Upstairs, the living room has vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, and luxury vinyl flooring that carries through the main living areas. The kitchen has dark cabinetry, an island, an eating bar, and tile flooring, and it opens to the dining area. Just off the living space, a sunroom with walls of windows and a wood accent wall looks out over the backyard. The primary suite includes a walk-in closet and a private bath with a double vanity, and two more bedrooms share a full hall bath. A separate laundry room with cabinets adds convenience. Downstairs, the large family room features a bar with a sink and a stove/oven, along with a fourth bedroom, a third full bath with a double vanity, and storage space. Outside, the wood-fenced backyard has mature trees, a patio, and an outbuilding for extra storage, and there’s a 2-car attached garage. Schedule your showing today!
