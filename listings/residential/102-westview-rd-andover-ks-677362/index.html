Property Description

Well-maintained mid-century ranch in desirable Willowbrook, situated on a spacious .46-acre corner lot with mature landscaping, fruit trees, and a fully fenced yard. This four-bedroom home offers a finished basement and numerous recent updates, including a newer HVAC, roof, water heater, electrical panel, main water line, dishwasher, luxury vinyl flooring, kitchen countertops and backsplash, fresh interior paint, and bathroom. A storage shed provides additional outdoor storage. With many of the major updates already completed, this home is move-in ready and offers plenty of space both inside and out. Enjoy the convenience of being close to Andover schools, YMCA, shopping, grocery stores, restaurants, medical services, coffee shops, convenience stores, and major commuter routes. Willowbrook is an established neighborhood known for its mature trees, larger lots, and convenient location.
