About This Property

Are you looking for a move-in ready ranch style home with space to spread out inside and out? You may have just found it! This inviting ranch-style home sits on a large lot in a great location and offers comfortable living with several updates already done. The bright living room features a large bay window that fills the space with natural light. The dining area includes updated flooring and French doors leading to a covered deck and fully privacy-fenced backyard. There’s plenty of room for a future swimming pool, a garden, or both. The yard also includes a lawn irrigation system that has not been used in recent years but remains in place for a future owner to evaluate. The kitchen features granite countertops, updated lighting, plumbing fixtures, cabinet hardware, and appliances that remain with the home. Just off the kitchen is a half bath, separate laundry area, and access to the attached two-car garage. The main floor includes two bedrooms, a full bathroom, along with the master bedroom with its own beautifully updated bathroom. Downstairs you’ll find a cozy family room with updated carpet and paint, TONS of additional storage, 3 good sized bonus rooms and potential for a fourth bedroom with the addition of an egress window. Plumbing is already in place for a future bathroom. Additional features include a tankless on-demand water heater and smart thermostat. Move in, get comfortable, and enjoy!
