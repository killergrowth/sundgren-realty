Property Description

Come see this lovely bungalow home right in the heart of Riverside, it is totally traditional with all the things you might expect. Hardwood floors, built in shelving and cabinets, a great front porch perfect for a porch swing and most of all, it doesn't look like all the homes around it. There is plenty of room for everyone, 5 bedrooms, 2 main floor and 3 upstairs and two baths, one on each floor. There are beautiful stained-glass lights, lots of windows and built ins around the fireplace. The fireplace is decorative as it was closed off because the chimney and firebox were sized for a 1920's gas heater, it won't vent woodsmoke properly. The sunroom has an area rug that stays, all appliances in the kitchen stay and shelving in the dining room stays. The basement is unfinished but there is lots of shelving down there that stays as well. This home has a brand-new roof and has been newly painted; the new roof will help insurance rates. The garage has alley access and a door into the backyard to enter the home from there. The backyard is fenced and has a gate to the back as well. The area boasts so many things to do. There is the golf course, botanica, the art museum, walks by the river, tennis courts and Riverside Park. There is such a charm to these Riverside homes, come and see for yourself.
