About This Property

Welcome home to this custom-built Palazzo patio home, where comfort, style, and low-maintenance living come together beautifully. Featuring 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, this zero-entry home is designed for effortless, lock-and-leave living and is tucked away on one of the neighborhood's larger lots, offering added privacy and handsome curb appeal. Inside, you'll find an abundance of natural light highlighting the thoughtfully designed open floor plan. The inviting living room features a cozy fireplace, while the chef's kitchen impresses with a large quartz island, pendant lighting, beautiful backsplash, above-cabinet lighting, and a spacious walk-in pantry. The primary suite is a true retreat with a generous walk-in closet and a spa-inspired, zero-entry tiled shower complete with a built-in bench. The second bedroom offers flexibility as a guest room, home office, or den. A separate, oversized laundry room adds convenience to everyday living. Step outside to the covered east-facing patio, the perfect place to enjoy cool, comfortable evenings. Additional highlights include a tornado shelter in the attached two-car garage and custom blinds throughout. Move-in ready and in pristine, like-new condition, this home offers exceptional value with the refrigerator, washer, dryer, and garage door openers all included. Enjoy outstanding neighborhood amenities including pickle ball courts, a swimming pool, and a clubhouse that fosters a welcoming sense of community. Offering comfort, versatility, and custom-quality finishes throughout, this exceptional home is truly better than new.
