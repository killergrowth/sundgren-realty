Property Description

Coming soon! Luxury, privacy, and character come together in one incredible home. If you've been waiting for a newly updated, one-of-a-kind property in the heart of Wichita, this is it! Welcome to one of Wichita's hidden gems. Nestled on a beautifully landscaped, private half-acre lot just moments from the Arkansas River and its scenic walking and biking paths, this custom-built retreat offers the perfect blend of rustic elegance and modern luxury. Soaring windows flood the home with natural light, while dramatic architectural details and a warm Colorado ski-lodge feel create an inviting atmosphere unlike anything else on the market. The backyard is a true private oasis featuring a stunning in-ground salt water pool, mature landscaping, and multiple spaces for relaxing or entertaining. Recent pool updates include a new filter (2024) and new pool pump (2026), making it ready for years of summer enjoyment. Inside, extensive renovations make this home completely move-in ready. Beautiful new wood flooring flows throughout, popcorn ceilings have been removed and retextured in the bedrooms and kitchen, and the primary suite features new recessed lighting. The office, formal dining room, and half bath have been refreshed with wallpaper removal, while the half bath also features a new vanity. The beautifully remodeled kitchen is the centerpiece of the home, showcasing gorgeous quartz countertops and a complete suite of new appliances, including a double oven, range, microwave, and refrigerator—perfect for everyday living and effortless entertaining. Significant mechanical and infrastructure improvements provide exceptional peace of mind, including an updated electrical panel, updated outlets and switches throughout, all-new smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, a sump pump, French drains, an electric vehicle charging hookup in the carport, and a hot tub electrical hookup. The exterior has been freshly painted, and for even greater value, the roof will be replaced prior to closing with Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, offering enhanced durability and the potential for insurance savings. Homes offering this level of privacy, architectural character, luxury amenities, and extensive updates rarely become available—especially in a location that combines peaceful seclusion with convenient access to shopping, dining, schools, and the Arkansas River corridor.
