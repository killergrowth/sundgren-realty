Property Description

This adorable mid-century ranch makes a perfect starter home or investment opportunity located in a desirable Derby neighborhood. This home features 3 bedrooms with an additional non-conforming 4th bedroom. The back porch has been finished to provide an additional 250 square feet that is not included in the county numbers. The galley kitchen offers a newer range and microwave and dining area. Adjacent to the kitchen is a laundry room that includes a washer/dryer and large walk-in pantry along with a rough in half bathroom. The large fenced back yard contains an oversized two car detached garage with a workshop area perfect for those car junkies or hobbyist. The seller also has a home at 2472 S Capri Lane, Wichita KS 67037 also located in the Derby school district. Call for your showing today while this property is still available.
