Property Description

Full of character, charm, and wonderful additions, this Lindsborg ranch home is much larger than it appears from the street! Enjoy an unbeatable location just two blocks from Main Street and close to North Park and Bethany College. The welcoming front foyer and living room feature high ceilings, leading into a home with an abundance of living space and versatility. The front of the home offers a formal dining room with a beautiful built-in hutch and two bedrooms. A spacious master suite addition features vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet, and a private full bath. The charming kitchen includes a convenient butler’s pantry and opens into an impressive family room addition with vaulted ceilings, a gas fireplace, and a wall of windows showcasing beautiful views of the backyard and mature trees. An additional full bath is located just off the kitchen, along with laundry hookups, offering multiple laundry location options. A fourth bedroom with its own en-suite bath provides the perfect private space for guests. Outside, enjoy a fenced backyard with mature shade trees, beautiful landscaping, and a sprinkler system. The outdoor living options are truly exceptional, featuring a patio, covered patio, and screened-in porch, along with alley access. With four bedrooms, three baths, generous living spaces, and so much character, this home offers far more than meets the eye in a fantastic Lindsborg location!
