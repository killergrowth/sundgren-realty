Property Description

Own a piece of Herington history! Built around 1881, this historic home is confirmed to have been the former residence of Monroe D. Herington, the man the city was named after. While the exterior reflects its historic roots, the interior offers modern updates and everyday comfort. Improvements include updated electrical with a newer panel, rewiring throughout much of the home, all-new plumbing, and a tankless water heater. The kitchen and bathrooms have also been updated, giving the home a refreshed, modern feel. Tall ceilings, oversized windows, abundant natural light, and generously sized rooms add to the appeal. Located in Herington, the property offers small-town living with convenient access to Fort Riley, Manhattan and K-State, and Wichita. A unique opportunity to own a home with a special connection to the area’s history while enjoying the conveniences of modern living.
