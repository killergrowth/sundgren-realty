Property Description

Welcome to this impressive 5-bedroom, 4-bath custom-built ranch home, perfectly positioned at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac on a secluded lake lot in the Fox Ridge subdivision . Blending luxury with an inviting, welcoming atmosphere, this home offers an oversized 3-car garage, flexible living spaces, and a thoughtfully designed floor plan with the space and features that are hide to find. The spacious kitchen features a large island, hidden walk-in pantry, and granite countertops, with granite counter tops carried throughout the home. The ranch-style main level offers a desirable split-bedroom layout, with the primary suite located at one end of the home and another bedroom positioned at the opposite end. A convenient office nook is located upstairs, along with the bonus suite. The finished walk-out and view-out basement is made for entertaining, featuring a bar, additional bedrooms with a convenient Jack-and-Jill bath, family room and game room plus a small bonus room tucked behind the bar that is ideal for storage or use as an additional pantry. One of the home’s standout features is the private bonus suite above the garage, complete with a walk-in closet, full bathroom, and coffee nook—perfect for guests, older children, a private office, or additional living space.Just outside the bonus room on the landing is a small space perfect for a home office or place to read or study. Step outside and enjoy the privacy of the lake setting and an east-facing backyard that provides welcome afternoon shade. An irrigation well and sprinkler system make maintaining the property easy. Recent updates add even more value, including a brand-new roof and freshly painted exterior. With its custom design, versatile floor plan, oversized garage, beautiful lake setting, and cul-de-sac location, 8846 W Westlakes Ct offers a rare combination of luxury, comfort, privacy, and inviting functionality. Don't let this one pass you by.
