Property Description

Located in the Chalet Ridge Addition in southeast Wichita, this newer ranch-style single-family home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, approximately 1,340 square feet of finished living space, and an attached 2-car garage. The home features an open floor plan with a spacious living area, kitchen with center island, dining area, and modern finishes throughout. Additional features include a primary suite with private bathroom and walk-in closet, separate laundry room, and fenced backyard. The property is currently tenant-occupied and is part of a seven-home portfolio with monthly rental rates ranging from $1,750 to $1,850 per home. This may appeal to buyers seeking an occupied rental property or a future personal residence, subject to existing lease terms. Each home is listed individually, and the seller will also consider offers for multiple homes or the entire seven-property portfolio.
