Property Description

CHDO (Community Housing Development Corporation) is proud to present this beautifully remodeled ranch home, updated from top to bottom and ready for its next owner! Lovingly renovated with a first-time homebuyer in mind, this move-in-ready property offers modern updates and comfortable living throughout. The main floor features three bedrooms, a completely remodeled four-piece bathroom, fresh paint, and new flooring. The inviting living room showcases beautiful wood floors and opens into the brand-new kitchen, complete with a desk area perfect for working from home or homework time. You'll also love the oversized pantry and separate laundry room with a convenient folding table. The finished basement provides even more living space with a large family room featuring an egress window, a fourth bedroom with its own full bath and egress window, plus a bonus room ideal for a home office, hobbies, or additional flex space. There's also plenty of storage! Outside, enjoy the covered back porch with access from the kitchen and dining area, a fenced backyard, and storage shed. Major improvements include a brand-new roof, freshly poured concrete driveway, and an additional parking pad. All the hard work has been done. Simply move in and enjoy!
