Property Description

Move-in ready and beautifully maintained! This charming 2-bedroom, 2-bath duplex is tucked into an established neighborhood just minutes from Wichita's Towne East Square, shopping, dining and everyday conveniences. Exceptionally clean and tidy, the home has been professionally cleaned and is ready for its next owner! Both bedrooms and bathrooms are conveniently located on the main level, and the kitchen features stainless steel appliances that remain with the home. Downstairs, a finished, carpeted bonus room provides flexible space for a home office, hobby room or whatever suits your needs. The unfinished basement is exceptionally clean and offers wonderful potential for additional living space, including a future family room with an existing egress window. Laundry is also located in the basement. Offered at $175,000, below the current Sedgwick County tax-appraised value, this well-cared-for property is a great opportunity for buyers looking for a move-in-ready home with room to make it their own!
