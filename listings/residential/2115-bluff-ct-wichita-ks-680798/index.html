Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious 4-bedroom, 2-bath traditional 1.5-story home tucked away on a secluded cul-de-sac with a beautiful wooded creek lot! Full of character and possibilities, this home offers generous living spaces, abundant storage, and beautiful hardwood floors throughout. The main level features a large family room filled with natural light from expansive windows, along with a generously sized kitchen boasting TONS of cabinet space. Just off the kitchen, a versatile bonus living area with a cozy fireplace offers the perfect spot for a dining room, second family room, or additional gathering space. A main-floor bedroom and full bathroom add convenience and flexibility. Upstairs, you'll find three additional bedrooms and another full bathroom. The basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring a huge family room plus a large unfinished area with endless potential for hobbies, a workshop, storage, or future customization. Additional features include a 2-car attached garage and two separate driveway/parking areas, providing plenty of space for vehicles and guests. Outside, enjoy the spacious lot with a peaceful wooded setting and creek, creating a private retreat to call home. With room to grow, plenty of storage, versatile living spaces, and so much potential, this home is ready for its next chapter
