Property Description

Welcome to this well-maintained 1½-story home offering approximately 2,330 sq. ft. of finished living space on a spacious and fully wood fenced almost 1/2-acre lot (0.41-acre). With 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths, this home offers a flexible floor plan featuring 3 bedrooms on the main level and a private upstairs primary suite with an en-suite bath. The updated kitchen has sit-in kitchen bar seating and brand new butcher-block countertops. Wood laminate flooring extends throughout the main living areas, while the bedrooms feature carpet for added warmth and comfort. The main level also offers two separate living/family areas and a dedicated laundry room. Numerous improvements include a new roof and newer electrical, HVAC, and water heater. The kitchen features freshly painted cabinets, new countertops, and all stainless-steel appliances that stay with the home. Primary suite updated. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced backyard, two storage sheds one is a 12X16 shop with electricity and the other is a shed for storage, there is an attached 1-car garage, a covered carport, and additional space for RV or trailer parking. With its generous lot, ample living space, multiple parking options, a covered patio, mature trees, and thoughtful improvements, this property offers a wonderful combination of space, comfort, and functionality!
