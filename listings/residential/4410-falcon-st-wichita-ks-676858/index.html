Property Description

Welcome home to 4410 E. Falcon St. in Wichita! This cozy, warm, and light-filled home has been lovingly cared for and is truly move-in ready. Ideally located with quick access to Hwy 254, K-96, and I-35, you'll enjoy an easy commute while coming home to a peaceful, well-maintained retreat. The inviting main level features beautiful real hardwood floors, an airy layout, convenient main floor laundry, and a kitchen complete with granite countertops, with all appliances staying for the new owners. The main floor offers 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a private en-suite bathroom. Downstairs, you'll love the massive family room—perfect for movie nights, entertaining, a game room, or a home office—along with a large half bathroom and a dedicated storage room to keep everything organized. Step outside to a beautifully landscaped, fully fenced backyard featuring an irrigation sprinkler system, updated deck boards, and a pergola, creating the perfect space to relax or host family and friends. This home has been extensively updated within the last five years, including a new Lennox HVAC system, real hardwood flooring on the main level, new basement carpet, granite countertops, new sump pump, fresh landscaping, new porch railings and stairs, new doors, updated deck boards with a pergola addition, and fresh interior and exterior paint. Combining timeless charm, thoughtful updates, and an unbeatable location, this beautifully maintained home is ready for you to move in and start making memories!
