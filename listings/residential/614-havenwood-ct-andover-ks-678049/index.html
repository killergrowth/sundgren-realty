Property Description

Tucked into a quiet cul-de-sac in Crescent Lakes, this one checks the boxes buyers actually care about. Four bedrooms, three baths, and 2,468 square feet spread across a main level built for everyday living, plus a finished basement that adds a fifth living space, bedroom with huge walk in closet, bath, and recreation room. The kitchen got the full treatment in 2023: new cabinets, a big island, quartz counters, under cabinet lighting and a walk-in pantry. The ceiling is also wired to add pendant lights if desired. Hardwood floors run through the main level, refreshed in 2023 alongside new basement flooring. A wood burning fireplace anchors the living room. The big-ticket items are already handled: New high impact roof in 2025, new HVAC in 2024, new water heater in 2021. Out back, a fenced yard and east facing deck sit on a private cul-de-sac lot with room to spread out. Crescent Lakes brings the extras: two swimming pools, a playground, sidewalks throughout, and 55 acres of common ground. 614 Havenwood Ct is located in Andover Central schools (USD 385), close to shopping, easy walkability to Andover amenities with an easy commute into Wichita.
