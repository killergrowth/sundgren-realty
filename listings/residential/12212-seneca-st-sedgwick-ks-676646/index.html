About This Property

COUNTRY LIVING WITH CITY CONVENIENCE! Located on 7.79 acres, this one of a kind property offers the space, privacy, and natural beauty buyers are looking for while still providing quick access to nearby amenities. Enjoy a stocked private pond, a scenic creek running through the land, and wildlife throughout the seasons, including white tailed deer and wild turkeys. The property is approximately 10 minutes from Park City and south Newton and only about 2.5 miles from I 135, making travel to Wichita and surrounding areas convenient. Inside, the home stands out with custom quarter sawn woodwork, detailed carpentry, fresh interior and exterior paint, new carpet, a new roof, and new gutters. The additional 1,200 square foot flex space offers endless possibilities for a workshop, game room, man cave, studio, hobby area, or potential additional living space. The property also includes a designated private shooting area with a substantial earthen berm, adding another unique recreational feature for outdoor enthusiasts. A home warranty through America’s Preferred Home Warranty is included for added peace of mind.
