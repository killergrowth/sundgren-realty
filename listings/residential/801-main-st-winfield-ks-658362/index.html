Property Description

A great opportunity awaits the next entrepreneur. Chef established, chef owned and operated, Biederman’s Bistro & Catering is a turn key restaurant located in Kansas in the bustling town of Winfield. It is ready for you to come take over and add your own twist or keep the menu of favorites that their established customers keep coming back for. Going strong for over 20 years; They’ve raised their family in the housing above the restaurant where everyone has taken their turn hosting, cooking, waiting and doing dishes. Literally built from the bottom up, “Biederman’s” (named after the boys’ pet turtle) became a dream come true after the chef completed Culinary Arts school. The restaurant is currently operating, but the owners are ready to move on to the next chapter of their lives. Biederman’s is fully equipped with state-of-the-art kitchen that was completely remodeled in 2020. The dining area (designed by their niece) has a wonderful historic ambience. Literally, it has everything you need and comes ready to operate!
