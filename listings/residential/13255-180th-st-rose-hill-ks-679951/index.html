Property Description

This is a wonderful homestead close to Wichita, KS. This home sits on a very wooded, creek-front lot just under 10 acres. Preppers, this is an easy place to secure with one way in, and great site lines from the house. The home is partially obscured from the road entrance, which is gated and roughly 200 yards from the road. The home could go off grid with a whole house propane or diesel powered generator. There are guinea birds that stay with the home, and are great at alerting anything out of the norm and eating insects in the summer months. The home is an updated 1966 farm home, with the owners suite on the main level and two bedrooms upstairs. There is a full bathroom upstairs and a full bath on the main floor. The kitchen is an updated spacious area with beautiful rustic style cabinetry and granite countertops. The basement has a finished family room a concrete storm room. There are many recent upgrades, such as a new all electric modern heat pump HVAC system, new septic system, new roof all within the last one or two years. There is a Culligan water softener that is seller owned, and coveys with the home to nicely treat the well water. The owner states the school district is actually Douglas, but their children went to Rose Hill schools, and the districts cooperate due to the district line adjoining so they allow choice. (Buyer to verify school district if applicable.) This is a huge opportunity to own a unique homestead close to McConnell Air Force Base, Andover, Derby, and northeast Wichita, KS. This property has potential to be a beautiful 2nd vacation home or potential unique short term rental home. Bring on the fall fire-pit under the changing color of the trees, with the sound of the creek flowing. Come check out this house & land, and make it your home on the range.
