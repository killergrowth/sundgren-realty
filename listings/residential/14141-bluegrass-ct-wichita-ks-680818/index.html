Property Description

14141 W Bluegrass Ct offers one of those rare settings in Highland Springs, with a private wooded backdrop that makes you feel like you've escaped into the woods. Inside, this 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom home showcases soaring ceilings, beautiful wood craftsmanship, and a split bedroom floor plan that brings both character and comfort to everyday living. From the moment you enter, the attention to detail is unmistakable. Elegant wood trim, a beautifully crafted staircase, and a stunning double-sided fireplace give the main living areas a warmth that's difficult to recreate. A flexible room near the entry offers space for formal dining or a home office, while the spacious living room opens visually toward the breakfast nook, where wood floors and oversized windows frame the trees outside. The kitchen offers generous workspace, all appliances included, and a surprisingly large walk-in pantry that makes staying organized easy. The primary suite enjoys its own peaceful view of the woods, along with a spacious private bathroom and walk-in closet. A second bedroom and full bathroom complete the main level, while convenient main-floor laundry sits just off the attached 3 car garage. Downstairs, the unfinished basement offers an incredible opportunity to create additional living space and build equity while making the home your own. But the real magic happens out back. The updated deck features both covered and uncovered areas, giving you a place to enjoy morning coffee, host dinner with friends, or simply listen to the trees on a quiet evening. With stairs leading down to the yard and no typical backyard view of neighboring homes, this outdoor setting feels like a retreat. Beautiful craftsmanship, flexible living space, and one of Highland Springs' most memorable wooded settings make 14141 W Bluegrass Ct a home worth experiencing in person.
