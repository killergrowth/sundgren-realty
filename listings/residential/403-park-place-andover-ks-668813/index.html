About This Property

The Beechwood is a two-bedroom, two 1/2 bath, TWO-STORY home in Heritage Commons featuring an open-concept layout designed for low-maintenance living. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining and living room, creating a comfortable space for everyday living and entertaining. The primary suite includes a private bath and ample closet space, while a secondary bedroom offers flexibility for guests. A bonus room adds additional living space or used for an In home office. A private patio extends the living space outdoors. HOA services include lawn care, irrigation, snow removal, and maintenance of common areas. Community amenities include a clubhouse, fitness center, game room, and heated saltwater pool. Conveniently located near walking paths, parks, shopping, and dining, the Beechwood delivers easy living in a well-maintained neighborhood.
