Property Description

Welcome to Fox Ridge, one of Wichita’s most desirable neighborhoods! This stunning custom-built home offers the perfect combination of space, privacy, and lifestyle. Situated on an oversized, nearly half-acre lot with no backyard neighbors, you'll love the peaceful, private setting and beautiful views. Inside, you'll find 5 spacious bedrooms PLUS dedicated office space, a loft bedroom with its own HVAC zone, two pantries, and a cozy fireplace. The oversized lot is just as impressive, with a sprinkler system and irrigation well to keep the yard looking its best. A new mini-split system for the garage is also included and ready for installation! Life in Fox Ridge is hard to beat, with two neighborhood pools (one featuring lifeguards), beautiful walking paths, and multiple scenic ponds. Enjoy the convenience of being just minutes from some of Wichita’s best shopping, restaurants, and entertainment.
