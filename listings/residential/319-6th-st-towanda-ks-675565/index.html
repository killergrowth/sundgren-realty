Property Description

Don't miss this beautifully remodeled 3-bedroom, 2-bath manufactured home in Towanda on a large lot that is just shy of an acre! Extensive updates have transformed this home, including new studs, drywall, insulation, subflooring, PEX plumbing, and hot water service to one of the exterior hydrants. The open-concept floor plan offers a spacious, modern feel with luxury vinyl flooring throughout the main living areas. The kitchen is a true showstopper, featuring a massive island with a built-in range, attractive vent hood, soft-close cabinets and drawers, a convenient slide-out spice rack, and all new appliances. The split-bedroom layout provides privacy, with the primary suite situated separately from the secondary bedrooms, and all featuring walk-in closets. The primary bathroom is currently unfinished but already framed and ready for the next owner to complete. Work remaining includes installation of the shower, tub surround, flooring, and some plumbing components. Additional trim work throughout the home is also awaiting completion. Out back is a large covered deck, a fenced-in yard, and multiple storage sheds. With so many major improvements already completed, this property offers the perfect opportunity to enjoy modern updates while adding your own finishing touches.
