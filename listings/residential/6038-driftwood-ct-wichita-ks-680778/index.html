Property Description

Welcome to this beautiful waterfront home in Edgewater! Offering 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, a 3-car garage, and nearly 3,000 square feet of finished living space, there's plenty of room to spread out and enjoy. The home features fresh interior and exterior paint, along with a new roof installed in November 2025 with TAMKO Heritage 30 shingles. Inside, you'll find a spacious living room with vaulted ceilings and a cozy fireplace. The kitchen features granite countertops, a center island, eating bar, and pantry, with an adjoining dining area. A convenient drop zone off the garage and main-floor laundry add everyday functionality. The split-bedroom floor plan includes a spacious primary suite with dual sinks, a separate tub and shower, and a walk-in closet. The finished view-out basement offers a large family room, two additional bedrooms, and a full bathroom. You'll also find a bonus room that provides additional flexibility for a home office, hobby room, workout space, or whatever fits your needs! Step outside to enjoy peaceful pond views from the covered patio and a backyard that backs up to the water. Located in the Maize School District, Edgewater also offers neighborhood amenities including a swimming pool and clubhouse.
