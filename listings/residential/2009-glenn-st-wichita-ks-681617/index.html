Property Description

Space and fresh big-ticket updates set this 3-bedroom, 1-bath ranch apart at 2009 S Glenn St. At 1,434 square feet, it offers more room than comparable homes nearby. The roof was replaced within the past year and the exterior has all-new siding, so the two largest maintenance items are already done. Inside, a large main-floor family room and a bonus room (currently used as an office) add flexibility beyond the three bedrooms. Hardwood floors run through the bedrooms, and a gas fireplace anchors the family room. The backyard is fully fenced, with a covered patio and storage shed included. The home does need some cosmetic work — reflected in the price.
