About This Property

If you are looking for a property with serious shop space, room to spread out, and the convenience of being in town, this one deserves a closer look. Built in 1964, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in Walton offers approximately 4 acres, an attached 2-car garage, detached 1-car garage, three wells and an impressive 40' x 50' insulated steel building. The home offers a comfortable and functional floor plan with plenty of storage throughout. The kitchen has an entire wall of cabinetry, room for a small breakfast table and a pass-through to the formal dining room. Just off the family room is an additional small kitchen area, making snacks, drinks, or entertaining convenient. The hallway leading to the bedrooms provides even more storage with two large closets as well as a built-in desk. The main bathroom was completely remodeled in 2021. Newer flooring and paint can be found in the family room, bedrooms and hallway, and the main living areas all have updated ceiling fans with light fixtures. A new electric water heater and heat pump system were installed in 2023. The basement houses the mechanical systems and includes a storage room/storm shelter. A wheel chair ramp is currently installed in the house and can be easily removed if not needed. Now for the feature that truly sets this property apart-the 40' x 50' insulated all steel building constructed in 2024. With 14-foot sidewalls, this building was designed to be used. It features two insulated overhead doors with windows-a 12' x 12' door and a 16' x 10' door-both equipped with commercial-grade openers, plus a separate walk-in door. The shop offers a ventilation fan, 100-amp electrical service, a dedicated 50-amp welding circuit and an air compressor circuit. The 6-inch concrete floor was constructed to accommodate a car lift. Whether you need space for automotive work, restoration projects, equipment, woodworking, hobbies, or simply having an exceptional amount of usable shop space, this building offers the space and infrastructure that can be difficult and expensive to duplicate. An additional one car garage provides even more space to spread out while three wells offer convenient water for the property. The residence is located on a city lot within the city limits and offers city amenities. The additional garage, steel building and acreage extend north of the homesite in Harvey County, creating an unusual combination of in-town convenience and extra space. This property is much more than a 3-bedroom house. The shop, garage, acreage and improvements make this a property possibilities well beyond the house itself.
