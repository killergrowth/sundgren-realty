Property Description

Acreage, privacy, a fishing pond, multiple living spaces, and incredible garage space—this property has room for it all! Nestled on approximately 2.5 acres at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac, this unique property offers the space and privacy you’ve been looking for while still providing all the comforts of home. Inside, you’ll find an updated kitchen, brand-new carpet, two fireplaces, two family rooms on the main floor, and two additional recreation rooms in the basement—giving you plenty of room to gather, relax, entertain, or create spaces tailored to your needs. A brand-new roof will also be included. Step outside and discover your own private retreat! The approximately 12-foot-deep fishing pond is stocked with channel catfish, bluegill, and green sunfish, with the possibility of black crappie, and features a well-fed pump. A 12-zone sprinkler system supplied by a well helps keep the grounds looking their best. The garage and outbuilding setup is another standout feature, with an attached two-car garage plus an additional “cabin”/outbuilding featuring electricity, a solid foundation, and an overhead door—ideal for a workshop, hobbies, storage, or extra equipment. Recent improvements include a new HVAC system, new hot water heater, water softener, backup generator, and new carpet. The basement has also had epoxy injections and, per the seller, has had zero water intrusion since the work was completed. Septic information is currently being verified. If you’ve been searching for a property that offers space, privacy, recreation, and room to make it your own, this one deserves a closer look! BRAND NEW ROOF WILL BE INSTALLED BEFORE CLOSING!
