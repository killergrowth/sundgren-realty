About This Property

Welcome to 1908 Cypress Lane, a beautifully maintained ranch-style home tucked into a mature Newton neighborhood. This inviting property offers classic curb appeal with freshly painted white brick, distinctive green shutters and front door, mature shade trees, and a welcoming covered front porch. Inside, the home offers comfortable living spaces, 4 bedrooms, 2 baths, and approximately 1,926 square feet of finished living area, including a full finished basement. The basement provides valuable additional living space and features a wood-burning fireplace. The wide driveway and established landscaping add to the home's functionality and appeal. Located near Northridge Elementary and Newton High School, this is a wonderful opportunity to own a solid, well-cared-for home in a desirable Newton neighborhood.
