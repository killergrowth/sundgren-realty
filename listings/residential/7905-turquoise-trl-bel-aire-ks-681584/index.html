Property Description

Beautiful home located in the prestigious Elk Creek Addition neighborhood surrounded by ponds and stunning scenery. This five bedroom, three and a half bathroom, three car garage has unique custom details throughout and the home making it a perfect place to call home. Outside, the home boasts great curb appeal with a long covered front porch, perfect for morning coffee. There is a large backyard with direct access to the neighborhood pond. Inside is a spacious open floorplan with sightlines throughout the home and out to the beautiful scenery in the backyard. This space is perfect for entertaining. Kitchen has two-tone cabinetry and custom island with granite and quartz countertops. Storage shouldn't be a concern with plenty of cabinet space including a pantry. Living room has gas fireplace with built in cabinets and shelving. Above the garage there is another custom loft area that could be used as an office or extra bedroom. The three bedrooms on the main level are a good size and there is an additional half bath off of the mudroom and garage. There is a master suite with attached bathroom and easy access to the laundry. The master bathroom has his and her sinks with separate tub, shower, and water closet. There is a walk-in master closet with custom organization system. Laundry has two access points. Downstairs is a huge living room with wet bar. There is plenty of space for pool table and theatre room setup. There are view out windows to still give a view to the pond out back and allows natural light. The basement has two additional large bedrooms with full guest bathroom. There is a huge covered back deck with easy access from the dining area with plenty of room for guests and barbecuing. Low specials also adds a lot of value. The home has never been lived in.
