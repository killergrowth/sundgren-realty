Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful, fully updated gem featuring a unique and spacious open-concept layout! The light-filled main level seamlessly connects the living, dining, and kitchen areas, anchored by broad windows that fill the space with abundant natural light and highlight fresh paint and wood laminate flooring. The redesigned kitchen is a chef's dream, boasting brand-new soft-close cabinetry, sleek quartz countertops, a modern range hood, and a functional layout designed for easy meal prep. You'll also find on the main floor, two generous bedrooms, both equipped with spacious walk-in closets and a fully updated bathroom complete with a tub/shower combo. Expand your living space in the partially finished full basement, complete with fresh paint, cozy new carpet, and convenient half bath, ideal for a rec room, home office, or media space. Outside, enjoy a freshly painted exterior, a brand-new composition roof for peace of mind, and a great patio space perfect for outdoor entertaining. Move-in ready and packed with upgrades!
