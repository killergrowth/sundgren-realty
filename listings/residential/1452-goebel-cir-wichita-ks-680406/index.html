Property Description

Comfort, space, and outdoor enjoyment come together in this inviting twin home in Wichita! Featuring 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and a 2-car garage, this home offers a functional layout with room to spread out. Step into the living room, which flows seamlessly into the dining area and kitchen, creating a connected space for everyday living and gathering with friends. Beautiful LVP flooring runs throughout the home, adding a fresh, cohesive look. Downstairs, the basement expands your options with a rec/family room and an additional bonus room ready for your hobbies, home office, workout space, or extra storage. Outside, enjoy the concrete patio and paver area, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The large, fully fenced backyard is surrounded by mature trees that provide welcome shade and a sense of privacy. With comfortable living spaces inside and plenty of room to enjoy the outdoors, this Wichita twin home is ready for its next chapter!
