Property Description

Welcome to the Francis floor plan by Liberty Communities! This 1 story home features 3 carpeted bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a kitchen with Black Pearl granite counter tops, soft close cabinets and one large pantry, LVP flooring, window blinds, ceiling fan in primary bedroom and living room, a concrete patio, and a 2-car tandem garage with a FEMA approved storm shelter. The following appliances are included: Stove/oven, microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher, washer and dryer. Irrigation, sod, bushes and 1 tree are also included. Color of home will be Pewter Green with White trim and Cedar accents. It will not have extra storage on side of garage, but in back. *Interior photos are from a previously built version of this plan; all finishes will be the same as what you see in the photos.
