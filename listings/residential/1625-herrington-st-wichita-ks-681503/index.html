Property Description

Enjoy NO SPECIAL TAXES and comfortable living in this refreshed 2-bedroom, 2-bath Wichita home featuring a full view-out basement with room to expand! Built by Don Klausmeyer Construction in 2008, this home features fresh paint and new carpet throughout the main level, along with a new roof to be installed prior to closing. The main level includes an open-concept kitchen with an island and main-floor laundry. The primary suite features vaulted ceilings, a private ensuite bathroom with dual sinks, and a walk-in closet. The full unfinished view-out basement is partially framed, with a bathroom rough-in already in place, offering the opportunity to add a spacious rec room, third bedroom, and third bathroom. A 2-car garage provides convenient parking, while the fully fenced backyard and deck offer a great space to relax or entertain. Best of all, the special taxes have already been paid off, saving you the added expense of outstanding specials found in some nearby newer homes. Move in, enjoy the space you have, and finish the basement when you're ready!
