Property Description

What if living on nearly 7 acres could feel like living on 150? That's the sense you get at this 6.7-acre slice of heaven in Conway Springs, sitting in the Clearwater School District and completely surrounded by farm acreage and pasture land — the kind of setting that has the potential to make you feel like a ruler of your own little kingdom. Custom built in 1998 and lovingly updated over the years by the original owners, this 4 bed / 3 bath home offers plenty of space, up and down, for the whole family to spread out — and a new roof will be installed prior to closing, so buyers can move in worry-free. Upstairs, you're welcomed into a large great room with updated wood laminate flooring running throughout the living room, dining room, and kitchen. The recently renovated kitchen features a large island with bar-height seating, so the chef stays part of the party while the meal comes together. Just off the kitchen, step out onto a giant covered deck facing the southwest — perfectly positioned to take in those gorgeous Kansas sunsets while you survey your own little slice of heaven. The main level also includes a nicely sized bedroom with a walk-in closet and full bath, along with a generously proportioned primary suite, which includes a large walk-in closet & an ensuite bathroom with jetted tub and separate shower. Head downstairs, and you'll find where the family will likely spend most of their time: an updated family room with a full-size kitchenette, two more nicely sized bedrooms, and a full bathroom. Custom sliding barn doors open straight out to the backyard and a covered patio, blurring the line between indoor and outdoor living. And don't skip the 60x40 outbuilding, added in 2015 — it might just be the icing on the cake. One side is fully insulated, ideal for parking another vehicle, storing lawn equipment, or tackling that project you've been wanting to tinker with. The remaining two-thirds is set up for large farm equipment and livestock, complete with indoor corrals that open directly to fenced pasture for easy grazing. Properties like this rarely come available — so don't wait to schedule your private showing.
