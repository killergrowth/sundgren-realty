Property Description

This bright, welcoming bungalow offers 3 bedrooms, 1 bath and modern finishes throughout. You’ll find 1,060 square feet on one level, with luxury vinyl flooring through the living areas and all three bedrooms. The living and dining area flow together in one bright, open space, with ceiling fans throughout. The kitchen features gray cabinetry, butcher-block countertops, tile flooring and laundry hookups, with the refrigerator, dishwasher and range all included. The bathroom features marble-look tile and a modern vanity. Additional features include an upgraded electrical panel, French drains, storm windows and central air. Out back, a wood privacy fence encloses the yard, offering space to relax or garden. The property is currently tenant occupied. Schedule your showing today!
