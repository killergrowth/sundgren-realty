Property Description

**Brand-New Duplex with Modern Finishes and Investment Potential!** Welcome to 317 N Doris St, an impressive brand-new duplex featuring two thoughtfully designed units, each offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a 2-car garage, and 1,293 square feet of living space, for a combined total of 6 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, 2,586 square feet, and four garage spaces. Each side features its own distinctive kitchen design, giving the property a unique blend of modern style and upscale finishes. Both units offer spacious open-concept kitchen, dining, and living areas with contemporary gray LVP flooring throughout, large kitchen islands, white quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and stylish modern hardware. One kitchen showcases muted gray cabinetry paired with a striking green tile backsplash and sleek black accents, while the other features rich green cabinetry, a light tile backsplash, and warm brass-toned hardware. Modern lighting, recessed lighting, and ceiling fans complement both units, while decorative interior doors add a designer-inspired touch beyond typical new construction. Each unit also features two beautifully finished bathrooms with quartz countertops and tiled surrounds, including a guest bathroom with distinctive black-and-white tile flooring and a full tile shower and tub, along with a primary bathroom featuring a full-tile walk-in shower. Additional highlights include spacious walk-in closets in both primary bedrooms, separate laundry rooms, and modern black closet rods. Outside, each unit enjoys a generously sized backyard with room to relax and enjoy the outdoor space. An 8' x 8' concrete patio off each primary bedroom will be installed prior to closing. Please note that final grading has not yet been completed, particularly in the backyards. The property will be fully graded soon, and the front yard and portions of the backyards will be seeded with grass. Whether you're looking to expand your investment portfolio, add a new construction rental property, or explore the possibilities of owning a duplex, this property offers modern design, functional layouts, and the appeal of two independent living spaces in one opportunity. Don't miss your chance to own this brand-new duplex!
