Property Description

Welcome to 801 E Goeldner Ln in Derby—an all-brick ranch that combines classic craftsmanship with stylish, modern updates throughout. This 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home offers the perfect blend of character, comfort, and functional living space. Step inside and immediately notice the warmth of the original woodwork, adding timeless charm you simply don’t find in newer homes. The main floor has been thoughtfully updated with new luxury vinyl plank flooring, creating a clean, cohesive look while maintaining durability for everyday living. The master bathroom features tile flooring, offering a touch of distinction and practicality. The spacious kitchen and adjoining dining area provide plenty of room for gathering, whether you're hosting holidays or enjoying a quiet night in. With ample space to cook, dine, and entertain, this area truly serves as the heart of the home. Downstairs, the finished basement expands your living space with a huge rec room—perfect for a media room, game space, workout area, or whatever fits your lifestyle. A non-conforming bedroom adds flexibility for guests, a home office, or additional storage, along with a convenient half bath. The all-brick exterior not only delivers timeless curb appeal but also offers low maintenance and long-term durability. This home has been well cared for and thoughtfully updated, making it truly move-in ready. Located in a desirable Derby neighborhood, you’ll enjoy a quiet setting with easy access to schools, parks, and everyday amenities. If you’re looking for a home with solid construction, modern updates, and plenty of space to spread out, this one checks all the boxes.
