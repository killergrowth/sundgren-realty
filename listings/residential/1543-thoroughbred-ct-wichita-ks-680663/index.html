Property Description

Looking for room to spread out without giving up the conveniences of city living? This inviting Northwest Wichita ranch offers over 2,450 square feet of finished living space, 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and a fantastic location in the Maize School District, with a little taste of country living! Great curb appeal and a welcoming front porch set the stage for everything this home has to offer. Step inside to discover beautiful engineered hardwood flooring throughout the main living areas, creating a seamless flow between the spacious living room, kitchen, and dining area. A charming bay window brings in plenty of natural light, while the gas fireplace with tile surround adds warmth and character. The kitchen is designed with both function and style in mind, offering granite countertops, attractive tile backsplash, generous cabinetry, and a pantry. All kitchen appliances remain, including the recently replaced dishwasher and disposal. The adjoining dining area features French doors leading to a durable composite deck, making it easy to take the gathering outdoors. The generously sized primary suite offers a private bath complete with Onyx countertops, dual sinks, a jetted garden tub, and a beautifully tiled separate shower. Every bedroom features a walk-in closet, providing excellent storage throughout the home. A second main-floor bedroom, full bathroom, and spacious laundry room add to the home's practical layout. Need even more living space? The finished view-out basement delivers over 1,000 square feet of possibilities! An expansive family room provides plenty of room for movie nights, recreation, or entertaining, while the impressive wet bar offers seating for four or more. A third bedroom, full bathroom, and additional storage complete this versatile lower level. Outside, you'll appreciate the possibilities of a backyard measuring over one-third of an acre! Enjoy relaxing or entertaining on the low-maintenance composite deck, complete with composite steps. An irrigation well and underground sprinkler system help simplify lawn care. The oversized three-car garage, measuring approximately 26 feet 10 inches deep, offers exceptional space for vehicles, equipment, and projects. Recent improvements include new carpet in the second bedroom, a sump pump, irrigation well pump, and sprinkler heads. A radon mitigation system is also installed. Located just minutes from the shopping, restaurants, and everyday conveniences along Maize Road, this property offers the space you've been looking for in a location that's hard to beat. **Spacious living, thoughtful features, and a terrific Northwest Wichita location—this is one you'll want to see in person!
