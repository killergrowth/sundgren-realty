Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully designed, 4-bedroom, 2-bath home featuring a spacious 2-car garage and convenient single-level living with no steps at the entry. The modern kitchen boasts crisp white cabinetry, elegant white quartz countertops, and bold black hardware for a sleek, contemporary look. The open-concept living and dining area provides plenty of room for everyday living and entertaining, with direct access to the fenced backyard—perfect for relaxing or enjoying outdoor gatherings. The primary suite offers a private retreat with a walk-in shower, while the second bathroom features stylish, quality finishes. Conveniently located near McConnell Air Force Base, Christa McAuliffe K-8, Southeast High School, major highways, and everyday amenities, this home combines modern style, functionality, and accessibility. Whether you're a first-time buyer, looking for room to grow, or ready for your next chapter, this move-in-ready home is ready to welcome you!
