Property Description

Welcome to 506 W Songbird St! Built in 1997, this 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home has been lovingly cared for by one owner. The seller has enjoyed 29 wonderful years here and is ready to pass it along to its next owners. During their ownership, the basement was fully finished, adding a spacious family room, an additional bedroom and bathroom, along with extra storage space. The seller has gone the extra mile to ensure this home is truly move-in ready! In October 2026 the home received a new roof (both house & shed), new gutters, fresh exterior paint including the shed, siding repairs, fence repairs and reinforcement, completed yard clean up, professional cleaning of the interior, windows and carpets. All appliances convey with the home, including washer & dryer. Heat and air were replaced in 2019. Located right down the street from Woodland Valley Park and in a tucked away area, you will enjoy low traffic and a quiet neighborhood. With so much already taken care of, the next owners can settle in and enjoy all this home has to offer!
