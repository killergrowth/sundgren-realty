Property Description

WONDERFUL OPPORTUNITY to own a NEW CONSTRUCTION duplex with a 1-year builder warranty and NO MOWING! Located in the Trails End Addition within the Goddard school district, this property is ideal for investors seeking a high-end, low-maintenance rental or for those looking to live in one side and rent out the other. The combination of low maintenance and Goddard schools, makes this a highly attractive investment that commands competitive rent rates. This property has quality finishes throughout. Each unit offers nearly 1,200 SF of open-concept living space, featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and a 2-car garage. Luxury vinyl flooring runs throughout the entire home, and quartz countertops on every surface -- kitchen and both bathrooms. The kitchen features a spacious pantry and appliances include range/oven, dishwasher and microwave. Both the primary and second bedrooms feature walk-in closets. Ten-foot living room ceilings create a spacious feel, while the light, bright and clean color palette gives the property a fresh, modern look. The large kitchen island offers plenty of room for meal prep or casual dining. HOA dues include access to the community pool, clubhouse, mowing, and sprinkler system service for added convenience. Both general and special taxes have not been fully spread yet and have been estimated per current county tax records and mil levy calculations. Special taxes are estimated at $354 per month, per building. Six duplex buildings are currently available for purchase, offered as individual units (with separate legal descriptions), full buildings, or as a package of all six. Buyers and agents to verify all information pertinent to buyer.
