The circle drive with side load oversized 3 car garage as well as mature and crafted landscaping/nightscaping magically greet you. Enter the custom built front doors to the warmth of the 14' ceiling entry with see through fireplace and half bath. The east wing houses the master quarters coffee bar entry, bedroom, bath with his/her sinks, large tub, water closet w heated toilet & large walk through tiled shower that enters/exits from bath or closet. The large master closet houses beautiful high/low hanging bars and wonderful built ins. An additional bedroom currently used as office completes the east wing. The incredible living room with 14' beamed ceiling & floor to ceiling windows overlook the incredible back yard. Currently used as the formal living & dining room you will marvel at the beauty of the scraped real wood floors that are throughout the main floor. The fireplace makes for warm ambiance. The hearth room kitchen has a 2nd fireplace, large granite island, breakfast nook with serving bar & built ins, Wolf 6 burner gas stove w grill, pot filler, sub zero built in fridge, Wolf double ovens, built in microwave, ASKO dishwasher all presented beautifully with a stone back wall. The west side of this incredible home leads to the 3rd bedroom with ensuite bath, & door to screened in porch, large walk in pantry, drop zone, coffee bar, large laundry room with built in desk, sink, folding table, washer/dryer that stay, and separate wrapping room with cubbies for storage. Door to oversized 3 car garage with lift to storage is simply wonderful! Exit basement through the 2nd screened in porch with hot tub, to the incredible back yard with 50 x 20 gunite pool, flagstone shallow seating area, steps to deep end and electric pool cover. There is a built in sand box & pad for play equipment, fire pit, separated fenced area for dogs, bricked garage bin area with extra parking, irrigation well & sprinkler system, beautiful established landscaping and is fully fenced.
