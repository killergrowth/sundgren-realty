Property Description

BRAND NEW TWIN HOME | SOUTH POINTE | POND VIEWS | CONVENIENT LOCATION Awesome area amenities, convenient commutes, and brand-new construction! This exceptional twin home/duplex in the growing South Pointe neighborhood offers a true multifamily opportunity, featuring a highly functional 4-bedroom split floor plan on each side. Estimated completion is early September 2026. Each side offers 4 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a 2-car garage, providing plenty of space for comfortable living. The split-bedroom layout offers privacy, with a spacious primary suite featuring a walk-in closet and walk-in shower, plus a secondary full bathroom with a tub/shower combination. Designed for modern living, the interiors feature vaulted living areas, durable LVP flooring, quartz countertops in the kitchen and both bathrooms, and a convenient walk-in pantry. Covered patios extend the living space outdoors and overlook fully fenced, east-facing backyards with beautiful views of the reserve pond. The low-maintenance outdoor spaces are ideal for relaxing, entertaining, or enjoying the surrounding scenery. Located strategically on Maize Road, South Pointe provides convenient access to US-54/KS-400 (Kellogg Avenue) to the south and K-42 to the north, making commutes throughout the Wichita area easy and convenient. The property is also close to major Wichita-area employers and is located within the Goddard Public School District. Enjoy an active lifestyle with nearby access to Pawnee Prairie Park, Prairie Sunset Trail, Tex Consolver Municipal Golf Course, and Eisenhower National Airport. The HOA covers lawn care, helping provide a low-maintenance ownership experience, while residents are responsible for utilities. With two brand-new 4-bedroom units, desirable modern finishes, pond views, a convenient west Wichita location, and strong multifamily potential, this South Pointe twin home is an outstanding opportunity for owner-occupants, investors, or multigenerational living.
