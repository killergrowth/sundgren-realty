Property Description

Welcome home to this fabulous 5bed/3bath/3car mid-level walkout ranch located in the coveted Maize School District, just minutes from shopping, restaurants, grocery stores, and everyday conveniences. As you arrive, you'll note the gorgeous stone and brick construction with cedar accents and front shutters. Inside, this home offers expansive open living spaces, abundant natural light, custom crown molding on the cabinetry and windows, beautiful light fixtures, engineered hardwood flooring, and fresh paint throughout much of the home. The spacious kitchen has been beautifully updated with new quartz countertops and tile backsplash, custom range hood, gas cook top, and huge center island overlooking the adjacent dining space. Just inside the garage entrance, you'll appreciate the convenient built-in drop zone with bench seating for added organization and storage. The primary bedroom offers a high trayed ceiling, walk-in closet, and en suite bath with quartz topped dual vanities and modern finishes. Both additional main floor bedrooms are open and bright with contemporary peel and stick wall paper accents, and the main floor hall bath is as clean and updated as the rest of the home. The finished walkout basement is designed for entertaining with a large family room that is wired for a projector and surround sound system, a custom wet bar, and beautiful floor-to-ceiling enclosed display cabinets. Both view out basement bedrooms are sizable with ample closet space. A whole house surround sound system extends throughout the home, including the garage and back patio, creating the perfect atmosphere whether you're entertaining guests or simply relaxing at home. Step outside to enjoy the oversized concrete patio spanning the length of the home, overlooking the large backyard complete with a wood privacy fence, sprinkler, irrigation well, and fresh landscaping rock for added curb appeal. With quality upgrades throughout, generous living space, and an outstanding location in one of the area's most sought-after school districts, this home is one you won't want to miss.
