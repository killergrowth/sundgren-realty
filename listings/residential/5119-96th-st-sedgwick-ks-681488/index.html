Property Description

Room to roam and a farmhouse full of character! Set on 3.72 acres in Sedgwick, this updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath home combines inviting living spaces with mature trees, open skies, and room to enjoy the outdoors. Hardwood floors, beautiful woodwork, and black Pella windows give the interior a bright, welcoming feel. The living room features a cozy fireplace and wood mantel, while the updated kitchen offers quartz countertops and new cabinetry. Upstairs, the spacious bedroom has room to create a sitting area, playroom, office nook, or hobby space alongside your bedroom furniture. The partially finished basement adds usable space complete with a third bedroom and second full bathroom, with unfinished areas available for storage or future finishing. Outside, the 35' wide x 26'-deep detached three-car garage provides generous space for parking, tools, and projects. An older barn currently used for horse shelter and fenced areas complement the country setting, while the new concrete patio, installed in October 2026, offers a spot to relax and take it all in. Extensive improvements during the sellers’ ownership include a newly constructed basement and foundation, new electrical wiring and plumbing, updated HVAC and ductwork, insulation, siding, and windows. More recent improvements include a new metal roof and gutters in spring 2026 and a lagoon installation in May 2026. With farmhouse charm, substantial updates, and acreage to make your own, this property is ready for you!
