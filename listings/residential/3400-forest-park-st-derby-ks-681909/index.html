Property Description

Backing up to trees in Foxfire Phase 2, this 1,336-square-foot Phantom plan by Comfort Homes offers 3 bedrooms and 2 baths in a split-bedroom layout. A large quartz-topped island anchors the kitchen, which overlooks the living area, with a dedicated dining area just off the kitchen that has large windows and lots of light. Upgraded trim adds detail throughout the main living areas, and a built-in bench with hooks in the laundry room keeps coats, bags, and shoes organized. The primary suite features a box ceiling, a spacious walk-in closet, and a private bath with a tiled shower and tile flooring. Downstairs, the unfinished view-out basement is already framed for two additional bedrooms, a bath, and a rec room, giving you a head start on future finishing. Take in the view of the trees from the 12' x 10' covered deck, or gather with friends on the large ground-level patio. Foxfire is in the Derby school district, close to local parks, shopping, and dining, with convenient access to McConnell Air Force Base and east Wichita. Stop by the model Saturday through Thursday, 1–5 p.m., to tour this home and explore other new homes available in Phase 2. Google maps hasn't caught up with us yet, so you'll find Foxfire turning onto Forest Park St from 55th St. South between Woodlawn and Oliver.
