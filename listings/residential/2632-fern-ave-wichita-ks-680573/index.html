Welcome home to this beautifully updated ranch offering over 1,500 square feet of living space, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a bonus room, and plenty of room to make yourself at home! From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the fresh, inviting feel and thoughtful updates throughout. Fresh interior paint and beautiful flooring throughout complement the updates, giving the home a cohesive, welcoming feel! Need a little extra room?
