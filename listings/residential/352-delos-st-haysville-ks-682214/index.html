Property Description

Welcome home to this stunning, fully remodeled Haysville gem, where every detail has been thoughtfully updated so you can simply move in and enjoy. Step inside to fresh designer finishes, new flooring throughout, and a bright, open feel that makes every room welcoming. The kitchen is the heart of the home, with gleaming quartz countertops, a large new island made for gathering, all-new cabinetry with a pantry, and new appliances. The finished lower level is a true retreat, with a cozy family room, a 4th bedroom with a walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired full bath with a double vanity. Peace of mind comes standard: the roof, gutters, AC, and interior and exterior paint are all new. Outside, sip your morning coffee on the covered back porch, then head to the impressive oversized 4-car detached garage with carport, a dream space for a workshop, hobbies, or toys. All of this sits on a spacious quarter-acre lot just walking distance from Nelson Elementary. Homes this beautifully finished don’t come along often. Schedule your private showing today!
