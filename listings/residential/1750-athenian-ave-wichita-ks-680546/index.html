Property Description

This amazing Womer-built home sits on a corner lot in Indian Hills Riverbend! Recently remodeled with a fresh, contemporary touch, it is move-in ready. The kitchen stands out, featuring quartz counter tops, Bluetooth enabled appliances, and an induction cooktop. It opens seamlessly to a dining area which shares a two-way fireplace with the spacious living room, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Most of the home features luxury vinyl or newer carpeting, although we believe most rooms have the original hardwood underneath. On the upper level are two updated baths and four bedrooms, one showcases original oak flooring and the master with en-suite bathroom and walk in closet. The lower level provides exceptional flexibility with a family/media room and an office or den with view out windows—perfect for work, hobbies, or casual entertaining. A large workshop/storage area, laundry, and a separate furnace room complete this level. Outside, the fenced backyard includes a well and sprinkler system for easy maintenance. Ideal for hobbyists or gardeners, the property offers multiple outbuildings: a heated garage accessed from 17th Street with RV parking, a large shed for yard equipment, and an attached sunroom/greenhouse. Enjoy peaceful moments by the water feature or relax on one of the two patios. And just a short stroll away, you’ll find yourself for a nature break on the scenic bike path along the Arkansas River. This is truly a special home—full of character, comfort, and thoughtful amenities.
