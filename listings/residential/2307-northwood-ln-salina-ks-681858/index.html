Property Description

Looking for plenty of space in a great spot? Just one block east of Meadowlark Elementary School, this roomy, ONE-OWNER, ranch home sits on a mature corner lot and gives you about 3,000 square feet of finished living space to spread out in. On the main floor, you’ll love having two separate living areas—one with a great fireplace to cozy up next to, and another that offers a dining location for those more formal situations. The spacious kitchen comes with all the appliances and tons of cabinet space, plus you get three bedrooms and two full baths all on the main level. Downstairs, the finished basement keeps the good stuff going with a huge rec room featuring built-in storage, two extra bonus rooms for whatever you need, and a third full bath. It's a super comfortable, versatile home in an unbeatable neighborhood—come take a look before it's gone!
