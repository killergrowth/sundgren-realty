They say they don't build them like they used to, and when you look at a home like this, they're absolutely right. Welcome to 116 N. F St. in the heart of Wellington, Kansas. Built in 1907, this home is a masterpiece of early 20th-century craftsmanship, blending timeless character with the modern comforts you actually want! With over 3,400 sq. ft. across 2½ stories and a guess house there's room for everyone. We're talking 4 spacious bedrooms, 3½ bathrooms, and gorgeous natural light pouring into every corner. It's the kind of home where holidays are hosted, memories are made, and every room feels like it has a story to tell. But the story doesn't stop with the finished spaces. Up above, you have a massive unfinished attic, and down below, a basement. Whether you're dreaming of a home theater, a hobby space, or just infinite storage, you have a total blank canvas to work with. Now, stepping outside onto your generous quarter-acre lot, you'll find a 30' x 24' detached two-car garage, but that's not the best part. Keep it as a lucrative income stream, use it as the ultimate private home office, or offer it as a mother-in-law suite. The flexibility here is incredible. Location-wise, you're just minutes' walking distance from downtown Wellington, giving you the perfect blend of quiet neighborhood charm and everyday convenience. Homes with this level of historic charm are rare. Homes with this much space are hard to find. But a historic home with this much space and a proven income-producing guest house?
