Property Description

Truly exceptional lakefront estate situated on two expansive lots totaling 1.23 acres in The Moorings. Featuring a primary residence with over 5,600 sq. ft., a separate three-bedroom guest house, and an incredible resort-style outdoor setting, this one-of-a-kind property offers beautiful lake views and modern finishes throughout. The primary residence offers more than 2,900 sq. ft. on the main level with expansive living areas, abundant natural light, and an incredible primary suite highlighted by a spa-like bathroom. The finished basement features heated floors, a beautiful theater room, and additional living and entertaining space. Exceptional storage can be found throughout the home, along with the convenience of laundry hookups on both the main level and in the basement. The outdoor space is truly remarkable and designed for both entertaining and relaxation, featuring a stunning swimming pool, hot tub, cabana, outdoor kitchen, multiple fire pits, expansive deck and patio areas, beautiful landscaping, and direct access to the lake. The separate guest house is impressive in its own right, offering three additional bedrooms, a luxury kitchen, full basement, abundant storage, and laundry hookups, providing an incredible space for family, guests, or extended stays. Complete with a five-car garage, two residences, 1.23 acres, exceptional indoor and outdoor living spaces, and direct lake access, this is a rare opportunity to own one of the area’s most distinctive luxury estates.
