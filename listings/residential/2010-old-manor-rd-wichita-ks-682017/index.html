Property Description

Move-in ready and beautifully updated! This charming ranch home offers 2 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, plus a finished basement with a non-conforming third bedroom, providing extra space for guests, a home office, or hobby room. Recent improvements include newer windows, roof, HVAC system, electrical panel, water heater, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, fresh interior and exterior paint, updated bathrooms, newer carpet, and modern light fixtures.
