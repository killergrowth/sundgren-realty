Property Description

Two decks, a finished walkout basement, and quartz countertops in this 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath ranch in Leewood Village. You get 2,096 finished square feet, a two car garage, and a 0.17 acre lot in northeast Wichita. The main level is open, with a light filled kitchen, dining, and living room flowing together around a fireplace. The kitchen has quartz countertops, plenty of cabinets, and real prep space. The oversized primary bedroom is also on the main floor, with room for a king bed and sitting area plus its own private bath, and there’s a half bath for guests. The view out, walkout lower level brings in natural light and adds two more bedrooms, a full bath, laundry, and flexible space for guests, a home office, gym, or hobbies. Outside, two decks overlook the backyard, giving you room to grill, relax, and entertain. The home has two fireplaces, and several Blink security cameras plus a Blink video doorbell stay with the home. K-96 is minutes away, Rock Road restaurants and shopping are close, and Bradley Fair, Chisholm Creek Park, and the Great Plains Nature Center are an easy drive. Well maintained, move in ready, and it includes a home warranty for added peace of mind. With an acceptable offer, the seller is offering a 2/1 temporary rate buydown plus a 1% lender credit, lowering the buyer’s rate by 2% in year one and 1% in year two.
