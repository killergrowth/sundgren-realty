Property Description

Welcome to your own private retreat on Wichita’s desirable Westside! Ideally located near Maple and 119th Street, just minutes from Kellogg (HWY 54), this custom-built 1994 home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, multiple living spaces, and an incredible backyard oasis in the sought-after Goddard School District. Enjoy a virtually maintenance-free exterior featuring Everlast siding installed by Mid America Exteriors, with a transferable lifetime guarantee. The siding, trim, soffits, and eaves never need painting. Energy-efficient double-pane windows were installed at the same time, and stainless steel mesh gutter guards help keep gutters clear of leaves. New roof in 8/2019 per seller. Inside, the welcoming foyer features beautiful granite and marble flooring. The kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, an eating bar, and custom granite and marble countertops. The spacious living/dining combination features vaulted ceilings, a cozy fireplace, natural light, and direct backyard access. The main-level primary suite includes a walk-in closet and private ensuite bath plus a convenient mudroom off the oversized garage. In the basement you'll find a generous family/recreation room with a second fireplace, a third bedroom, laundry area, and ample storage. The fully fenced backyard is truly one of a kind, featuring a 7-foot-deep koi pond and custom water feature with three stone structures and multiple waterfalls, thoughtfully designed and built over several years. The six-zone sprinkler system maintains the lawn but also waters backyard planters and helps maintain the pond’s water level. The SimTek EcoStone privacy fence offers the look of natural stone with low-maintenance durability. Designed to withstand continuous winds of 110 MPH and gusts up to 130 MPH, it features galvanized steel reinforcement inside the posts and panels. The oversized attached 3-car garage includes built-in shelving with ample space for hobbies and storage. The irrigation/personal-use well has been inspected by the City of Wichita and passed inspection. Please see the Property Information Packet for the Inspection Report, seller-provided list of property features, and items the seller is willing to sell to the buyer, if desired.
