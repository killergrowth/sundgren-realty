Property Description

If you've been looking for space, privacy, and the freedom to enjoy country living without sacrificing comfort and convenience, this property is a must-see! Situated on 12.3 acres, this spacious 7-bedroom, 3-bathroom ranch offers an incredible combination of comfortable living, versatile space, and outdoor amenities that are hard to find all in one property. Inside, you'll find a welcoming home with plenty of room for family, guests, entertaining, or working from home. The spacious kitchen features abundant cabinetry, generous counter space, stainless steel appliances, and a charming center island that makes it the perfect gathering place. Warm wood flooring, comfortable living spaces, and inviting bedrooms create a home that is both functional and welcoming. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and anyone needing extra storage will appreciate the two-car attached garage PLUS a separate two-car detached garage, providing plenty of room for vehicles, equipment, recreational toys, and workshop space. Adding even more value and efficiency, the home is equipped with solar panels, offering the potential for reduced energy costs and greater energy independence. The outdoor amenities truly set this property apart! Enjoy the freedom of a fenced pasture, a chicken coop, and a livestock lean-to, making this property ideal for anyone dreaming of a hobby farm or country lifestyle. Additional features include a dedicated shooting range and an invisible dog fence, providing plenty of opportunities for recreation and flexibility for pet owners. Whether you're dreaming of a small hobby farm, a place to raise a family, room for animals, space for your hobbies, or simply a private retreat away from the hustle and bustle, this property offers endless possibilities.
