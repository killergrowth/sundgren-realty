Property Description

Beautiful, huge home with 2 kitchens & 2 laundry areas: one each on the main floor and in the basement. There are a total of 8 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms in the home. The main floor features 6 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and 3 separate living areas. The beautifully remodeled kitchen has quartz countertops, large pantry and an eating peninsula. It opens to a spacious great room with built-in shelving/cabinets and has an attached formal dining area. The primary suite is large and features a decorative fireplace, 2 closets, deck access, and a large bathroom with separate tub and shower. The basement has a full kitchen, large living room with decorative fireplace, 2 bedrooms with egress, one and one-half bathrooms, a laundry area and 2 bonus/storage rooms. The backyard is fenced with a small storage shed and large, partially covered deck. There is a long driveway along the north side of the house which could accommodate tandem parking. Please note that fireplaces are decorative only. Listing agent is related to the seller.
