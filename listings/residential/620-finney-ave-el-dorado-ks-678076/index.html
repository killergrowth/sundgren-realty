Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully built 2024 home offering 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a thoughtfully designed open floor plan perfect for everyday living and entertaining. This home is confirmed to be in the Neighborhood Revitalization Program, offering a 95% tax rebate for a total of 10 years! (Benefit transfers to new owner!) The spacious living room flows seamlessly into the kitchen featuring high ceilings, granite countertops, an eating bar, pantry, and all appliances included — even the washer and dryer! Blinds throughout the home provide added privacy and convenience. The primary suite offers a private retreat complete with a walk-in closet and an en suite bathroom featuring granite countertops and double sinks. Step outside to enjoy the privacy-fenced backyard complete with a patio area perfect for relaxing or entertaining, along with a sprinkler system for easy lawn maintenance. Additional features include a 2-car garage with a built-in storm shelter for added peace of mind. Modern comfort, functional spaces, and move-in ready convenience make this home one you won’t want to miss!
