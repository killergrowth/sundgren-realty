Property Description

Unique Commercial Property with Victorian Charm and Residential Potential! This distinctive 1½-story property offers a unique opportunity for a business owner or someone looking for a home with character and charm. Currently converted into a beauty salon, this two-bedroom, two-bath property retains many of the features that make older homes so special. The interior showcases beautiful original hardwood trim, Victorian-style décor, and a warm, inviting atmosphere. The layout includes a kitchen, living room, dining room, two upstairs bedrooms, laundry room, and a fireplace. The upstairs bathroom features a charming clawfoot tub, adding to the home’s timeless appeal. Additional spaces include a spacious 15’ x 14’ massage room and a 12’ x 16’ sun porch featuring original wood flooring. Currently listed as commercial property with the county, this property offers excellent potential for a variety of business uses. It may also offer the opportunity to be converted back into a residential home, subject to verification and approval by the county. Whether you’re looking for a unique business location or a property with the possibility of becoming a charming home again, this one is full of character and possibilities!
