Property Description

Built just in 2024, this beautifully appointed patio home blends modern style with easy, low-maintenance living, plus LOW SPECIALS of just $7,500 total. Vaulted ceilings and abundant natural light create an open, airy main living space centered around a sleek linear fireplace. The kitchen is made for gathering, with a large contrasting island with seating, light quartz-style countertops, stainless appliances, pendant lighting, and plenty of cabinet space. The private primary suite features a striking accent wall, a spa-like bath with a walk-in shower, and a walk-in closet with built-in shelving. Two additional bedrooms, a second full bath, and a main-floor laundry room with cabinetry complete the thoughtful single-level layout. Outside, the corner lot offers a spacious yard and an attached 3-car garage with room for vehicles, storage, and hobbies. Quality craftsmanship and attention to detail throughout make this home a must-see!
