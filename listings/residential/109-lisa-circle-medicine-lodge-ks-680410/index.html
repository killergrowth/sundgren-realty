Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious 5-bedroom, 3-bath ranch-style home offering comfort, functionality, and plenty of room to spread out! Located on a quiet cul-de-sac in Medicine Lodge, this home combines inviting living spaces with versatile features both inside and out. The main level features a chef-inspired kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances, an eating bar, and an adjoining dining room, making it the perfect space for everyday meals and entertaining. The cozy living room boasts attractive laminate flooring, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The spacious master bedroom offers a walk-in closet and private en-suite bathroom, while two additional upstairs bedrooms provide plenty of room for family, guests, or a home office. The fully finished basement adds incredible living space, featuring a huge family room, a second kitchen area equipped with a sink, refrigerator, and stove, two generously sized bedrooms, and a full bathroom. Whether you need extra space for entertaining, guests, or multi-generational living, the possibilities are endless! Additional highlights include a large laundry room, a convenient two-car attached garage, and a fully fenced backyard designed for relaxing and entertaining. Enjoy outdoor gatherings on the patio, while the insulated storage shed with electricity offers the perfect setup for a workshop, hobby space, or additional storage. Situated in the welcoming community of Medicine Lodge, home of the historic Peace Treaty Festival and a thriving school district, this property offers space, convenience, and small-town charm. With room for everyone and flexible living spaces throughout, this home is ready for its next chapter. Don't miss your opportunity to make it yours!
