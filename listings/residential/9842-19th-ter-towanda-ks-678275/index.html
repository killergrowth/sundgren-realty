About This Property

Situated on 3.6 acres, this picture-perfect dream home offers space, privacy, and versatility, where country living meets everyday comfort. Offering over 2700 square feet of living space, this home features 3 bedrooms plus a bonus room and 3 full bathrooms. The impressive 30’ x 50’ fully powered detached shop is a standout feature with epoxy coated floors, full insulation, tons of outlets, and LED lighting. Inside the home, the main living area features vaulted ceilings, open living and dining areas, and generous cabinet and counter space in the kitchen. The primary suite features a beautifully updated bathroom, with an additional bedroom and bathroom on this main level. Downstairs expands the possibilities even further with a bonus room, another bedroom, a full bathroom, and additional living space with plenty of room to entertain, relax, or simply enjoy everyday life. The covered deck is perfect for morning coffee and you’ll appreciate the fenced portion of the backyard. Major updates include a new roof that is less than one year old, and the septic system was inspected and replaced in 2023. Adding to the home’s appeal is a closed-loop geothermal system, providing efficient heating and cooling for year-round comfort. If you've been searching for acreage without giving up gorgeous living space, this property is for you!
