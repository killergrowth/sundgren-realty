Property Description

The Cassia, part of the Urban Series at Heritage Commons, is a three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath home designed for modern, low-maintenance living. The main level features a kitchen with large island, dining area, living space with electric fireplace and shiplap detail, and a powder room with access from the zero-entry garage. Upstairs includes an owner’s suite with private bath and walk-in closet, two additional bedrooms, a full bath, a bonus family room, and a centrally located laundry room. A covered patio provides outdoor living space. Community amenities include a clubhouse, heated saltwater pool, fitness center, game room, lawn care, irrigation, and snow removal. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, and walking paths.
