About This Property

Once-in-a-lifetime opportunity on 1.3 Acres in Augusta! Experience the best of both worlds — acreage living right in town! This charming home sits on 1.3 acres with paved access, offering plenty of space to park extra vehicles, an RV, or even start that garden you’ve always wanted. Move-in ready and full of character, this spacious 3,090 sq ft home features three large bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and an abundance of storage space throughout. The living room is bright and welcoming with a vaulted ceiling, original birch woodwork, and an electric fireplace that creates a cozy, timeless atmosphere. The family room provides even more space to gather and includes its own gas fireplace plus access to the fenced backyard — perfect for entertaining or relaxing. You’ll love the two dining areas, ideal for both casual meals and special occasions and the built in birch cabinetry to showcase special keepsakes or family photos. The kitchen offers solid surface counter space, timeless and original Geneva brand metal cabinetry in perfect condition, and room to make it your own. The half basement, accessed from the garage adds even more flexibility and plenty of space for a workshop, hobby area, or additional storage. An oversized attached two-car garage provides easy access to the home, while the detached outbuilding offers exceptional versatility — ideal for a workshop, office, studio, or hobby space. The upstairs loft area provides even more storage options. If you own your own business and have vehicles or equipment to store, this property will provide the accommodations! Outside, the remaining acreage includes a carport and open space ready for your personal touch — express your passions for gardening, recreation, or future projects with extra acreage to do your thing. This is a rare find — a property that offers privacy, room to grow, and small-town charm all in one convenient Augusta location! Hot Tub on back patio and Refrigerator do not convey with the home.
