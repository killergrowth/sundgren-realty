Property Description

Located at 121 S. Argonia St. in Argonia, Kansas, this impressive modern barndominium offers the perfect combination of comfortable living, contemporary finishes, and an oversized shop area. Built in 2022, this 3,800 +/- square foot wood-frame structure features a 50' x 76' footprint, with approximately 1,800 square feet of beautifully designed two-story living space. Step inside and enjoy an inviting living area highlighted by soaring vaulted ceilings, an oversized ceiling fan, and an open, spacious feel. The home features 3 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, with plush carpeting throughout the bedrooms and upper-level living areas. The kitchen is thoughtfully finished with top-of-the-line countertops, stainless steel appliances, and deep kitchen sinks, creating a functional space for everyday living and entertaining. The oversized master suite offers a relaxing retreat, complete with a custom tile and glass walk-in shower, large soaking tub, and spacious walk-in closet. Additional amenities include zoned heating and cooling, a tankless water heater, and a large laundry room directly off the shop. One of the standout features of this property is the incredible 40' x 50' shop space. Fully insulated and equipped with two large electric overhead doors, natural gas heat, and water, this shop provides outstanding flexibility for hobbies, equipment storage, business operations, or simply having room to work and play. Outside, enjoy the covered wraparound porch extending along the east and south sides of the home, providing a great place to relax and take in the neighborhood. Whether you're looking for a modern home with room for your hobbies, a property with exceptional workspace, or the convenience of combining your living and working spaces under one roof, this impressive barndominium delivers a unique opportunity. Don't miss your chance to own this beautifully designed property that brings together modern living, functionality, and an exceptional shop space.
