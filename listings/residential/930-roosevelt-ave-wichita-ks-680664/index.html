Property Description

Exquisite elegance meets timeless charm in this sprawling, full-brick ranch nestled along the prestigious McDonald Golf Course in Wichita! Situated in the highly sought-after Sleepy Hollow neighborhood, known for its distinctive character, mature trees, and beautiful surroundings, this exceptional 4-bedroom, 2-bath home has been thoughtfully updated with remarkable attention to detail. From the beautiful hardwood floors to the extensive modern improvements, every element reflects quality and care. The remodeled kitchen features new appliances and a large built-in pantry, offering both style and functionality. Enjoy peace of mind with a new roof, windows, plumbing, electrical, heating and air, and more. Picture windows invite the outdoors in, filling the home with natural light while showcasing beautiful golf course views. A second main-floor den provides valuable additional living space, perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or creating a cozy retreat. The spacious ranch-style layout offers timeless appeal, while the large covered porch and new patio provide the perfect settings to enjoy peaceful mornings and relaxing evenings surrounded by mature trees and picturesque golf course scenery. Ideally located near Wesley Medical Center, KU School of Medicine, and Wichita State University, this remarkable property combines an enviable golf course setting with exceptional convenience. Rarely does a home offer this level of updating, enduring character, and desirable location. An extraordinary opportunity to experience the best of Wichita living!
