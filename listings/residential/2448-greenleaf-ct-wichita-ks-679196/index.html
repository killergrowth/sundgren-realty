Property Description

This Tallgrass Greenleaf home tucked on a peaceful cut-de-sac lot, with incredible golf course views of the 14th hole from this resort like back yard is one that you will not want to miss. Since ownership, this property has undergone major additions and renovations that make it extraordinary! Upon entering this beautiful property, you will notice immediately, the vaulted ceiling with beam detail, focal fireplace with built-in shelving - the pleasing color tones that enhance the palette of decor throughout, a spectacular bar area which was added to make entertaining in this space feel distinctive. The kitchen was entirely transformed in 2025 and offers a unique charm with materials, Italian range hood, brick, quartz, updated window design, new appliances. It connects to a prep/service kitchen with added oven, sink, powder bathroom. The primary bedroom offers a true sanctuary --- spacious, remodeled bathroom with steam shower additional shower heads, laundry added between closet and bathroom, added huge walk-in closet space and the sitting room, could be office was added to the home along with the attached sunroom with additional fireplace, screened porch with electric shades that connects it all seamlessly and can be utilized for most months of the year. This home has an additional 3 bedrooms on the story and a half, full bath and is split apart for added privacy. Downstairs offers a family room/game area, bar, exercise room, full bathroom and massive storage area. The backyard of this property is truly an oasis. Cocktail pool, pergola, a temperature controlled pool house that was added and offers an additional 900 square feet of the most ultimate space for entertaining, with wine refrigerator, sink, ice maker opens to the backyard, could also be used as a guest quarters, golf cart storage and extra garage space make an added bonus. New Certainteed Presidential Class 4 roof installed September 2026. Conveniently located close to medical, shopping and highway systems. .
