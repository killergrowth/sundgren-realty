Property Description

Set on nearly an acre in Emerald Bay, this exceptional luxury residence at 4855 W Brisco St offers the perfect combination of modern design, space, and resort style living. Built in 2019 and meticulously maintained by its original owner, every detail of this home showcases quality craftsmanship and thoughtful design. Step inside to find expansive living areas filled with natural light from oversized windows, complemented by stunning modern wood accents throughout. The main floor features three spacious bedrooms, including a show stopping primary suite with wood flooring and a spa inspired bathroom complete with dual vanities, abundant storage, a freestanding soaking tub, and a custom tile and glass shower with multiple shower heads. The walk in closet is equally impressive, offering both space and functionality. An additional living area on the main floor, finished with tile flooring, provides flexibility for entertaining or relaxing. The heart of the home flows seamlessly into the kitchen and living spaces, making it ideal for both everyday living and hosting. Downstairs, the walk out basement expands your living options with another large family room, a wet bar, and two additional bedrooms, each with their own private bathrooms. You will also find two versatile finished rooms currently used as a piano room and gym, perfect for a home office, hobby space, or playroom. Additional highlights include wood flooring throughout the main living areas and primary bedroom, a spacious four car garage with sleek epoxy coated floors, and ample storage throughout the home. Step outside to your private backyard oasis designed for relaxation and entertaining. Enjoy the resort style in ground pool, hot tub, shaded lounge areas, and outdoor bar space, all just steps from convenient lake access. This is truly a rare opportunity to own a one of a kind property that combines luxury, comfort, and lifestyle.
