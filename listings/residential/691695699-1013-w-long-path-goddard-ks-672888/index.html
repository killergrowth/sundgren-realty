About This Property

Investment opportunity in the desirable Trails End Addition in Goddard. This package offering includes four newer ranch-style homes being sold together for $1,350,000. All homes feature zero-entry access, no basements, and storm shelters located in the garages. These well-maintained properties provide an excellent opportunity for investors seeking immediate rental income, as all homes are currently tenant occupied.
