Property Description

Welcome home to this charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath home in Park City, offering nearly 1,600 square feet of space between the main floor and partially finished basement! Inside, you’ll find hardwood flooring, fresh paint in the bedroom and kitchen, and a versatile basement perfect for a second living area, home office, or bonus space. The current owners have made numerous improvements, including updated plumbing throughout, a new water line to the refrigerator, new kitchen and bathtub faucets, fresh carpet on the basement stairs, and an A/C unit and water heater replaced approximately five years ago. A home security system adds an extra layer of convenience. Outside, enjoy a spacious, fenced backyard with a covered patio featuring newly installed awning supports! The covered front porch, attached single-car garage, and additional carport provide even more to love. With plenty of indoor and outdoor space, this home is ready for its next chapter.
