Welcome to this inviting 3-bedroom, 3-bath home with an attached 2-car garage, located in the highly desirable Crown Heights South neighborhood. Enjoy the convenience of being just minutes from College Hill with quick access to Kellogg, making commuting and enjoying nearby shopping, dining, and entertainment a breeze. With numerous quality updates throughout, this home is truly move-in ready. Step inside to a welcoming living room featuring gorgeous oak flooring and a cozy gas-starter fireplace. The adjoining dining area creates a functional and open layout, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Just off the dining room, you'll find a spacious den, offering an additional gathering space to relax with family and friends. The completely remodeled kitchen is the heart of the home, showcasing custom two-tone wood cabinetry, granite countertops, a tile backsplash, an oversized undermount sink with updated fixtures, and ceramic tile flooring. High-end stainless steel appliances include a GE Monogram 6-burner gas range, Monogram vent hood, built-in microwave, warming drawer, Bosch dishwasher, disposal, and refrigerator, all of which transfer with the property. A dedicated laundry room with built-in cabinetry is conveniently located just off the kitchen. The main level offers three bedrooms, all featuring beautiful oak flooring. The spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet and a private en-suite bathroom with granite countertops, a fully tiled walk-in shower, custom tile flooring, and updated fixtures. A second full bathroom on the main level also features updated tile flooring and a tub/shower combination. Downstairs, the finished basement expands the living space with a large family/recreation room highlighted by a second fireplace, creating the perfect space for entertaining or relaxing. You'll also find a third full bathroom with a walk-in shower and a large utility/storage room offering abundant storage. Outside, the home showcases a full brick exterior with permanent metal siding on the trim, soffits, and fascia for low-maintenance living. Additional exterior improvements include seamless gutters with leaf guards and an updated insulated raised-panel garage doors. The fenced backyard is surrounded by mature trees, beautiful landscaping, and a patio that's perfect for grilling, entertaining, or simply unwinding outdoors. Additional updates include updated central heating and air, providing comfort and efficiency for years to come. With extensive updates, quality finishes, two fireplaces, and a sought-after location near College Hill and Kellogg, this home offers exceptional comfort, character, and functionality.
