Property Description

This charming 2-bedroom, 2-bath is ready and waiting for you! When you walk in the front door, you're greeted with bright, natural light and a vaulted ceiling, giving it a spacious feel. The kitchen and dining space are semi-open with views to the living area, and come with all kitchen appliances. 2 bedrooms and a full bath complete the main floor. In the basement you will find a large family/rec room, a nice sized bonus room that can be used for office space, guests or hobbies, and a full bath with laundry, to include the washer and dryer. Outside, you can enjoy patio space off the living area on the east side of the home, and a fenced backyard giving you plenty of space for entertaining, gardening or for your pets to run. You can also enjoy quiet evenings or morning coffee on your front porch. The exterior was painted in 2024, along with a newer water heater in 2023, and newer interior paint for peace of mind. Located on a corner lot in the Goddard school district, this home has easy access to highways, amenities and shopping. Come and see for yourself!
