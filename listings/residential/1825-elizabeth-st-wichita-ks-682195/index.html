Property Description

TWO HOUSES ON ONE LOT--AN OUTSTANDING OPPORTUNITY! Located directly across from Aley Park, this unique property features two separate homes on one parcel, offering exceptional versatility and income-producing potential. Live in the main house while renting the second home, add both residences to your rental portfolio, or take advantage of the space with multigenerational living. The main house offers 1,276 sq. ft. with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a functional floor plan, main-floor laundry, and appliances that remain with the property. Recent improvements include new carpet, fresh interior and exterior paint, new siding, new windows, updated bathroom vanities, and a new roof with Class 3 impact-resistant shingles installed in 2026. The property also includes a basement, a two-car detached garage, a private driveway, and additional street parking. The second house provides an additional 616 sq. ft. with 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom. It has also been refreshed with new interior and exterior paint, new siding, new windows, a remodeled bathroom, and a new roof with Class 3 impact-resistant shingles. The second home is currently rented on a month-to-month lease, providing immediate rental-income potential. Please do not disturb the tenant. This property offers multiple possibilities in a convenient location near shopping, restaurants, parks, and everyday amenities. Opportunities to own two separate houses on one lot do not come along often—schedule your showing and explore the possibilities! The Listing Agent is related to the Seller.
