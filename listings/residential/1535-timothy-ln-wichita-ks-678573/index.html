Property Description

Welcome home to this adorable property offering great curb appeal, thoughtful updates, and a layout that makes everyday living easy. Step inside to a welcoming foyer that flows into the open-concept dining area, kitchen, and spacious living room. An updated sliding door fills the space with natural light and leads to a composite deck overlooking the large, fenced backyard.The home features 3 bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms, including the half bathroom off the primary. Outside, the backyard is ready for whatever you envision. Host summer gatherings, let the kids or pets play, or create the perfect space for your favorite hobbies.
