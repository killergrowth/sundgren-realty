Property Description

Welcome to 315 S. Oak St in Whitewater, Kansas! Situated on nearly half an acre, this 3 bedroom, 3 bath ranch home has so much to offer inside and out! The warm and inviting living room features a cozy fireplace, and the kitchen comes fully applianced. Even the washer and dryer stay! The primary bedroom has its own private bath, and you'll also find main floor laundry, a full basement, and an attached 2 car garage. Step outside to a lush, private backyard oasis with mature trees, a recently added privacy fence, and an above ground swimming pool! There's plenty of space to relax, entertain, or just enjoy the outdoors. And let's talk about the HUGE shop! Whether you need room for hobbies, working on vehicles, storing your big toys, or bringing your big ideas to life, this space has so many possibilities! The property is located on a cul de sac with a creek and beautiful wooded surroundings. Enjoy small town living in Whitewater, Kansas, just a short drive from Wichita and all the conveniences of the city. With nearly half an acre, a swimming pool, a huge shop, and plenty of living space, this property has a lot to offer! Come take a look!
