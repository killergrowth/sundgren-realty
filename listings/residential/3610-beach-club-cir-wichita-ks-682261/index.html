Property Description

Your weekend state of mind just found a permanent address. Welcome to custom lakefront luxury on Pier 37 Lake, where a sandy backyard beach, a saltwater pool and dinner by boat are all part of the appeal. Beyond the community's coded entry gates, 6 bedrooms, 3.5 baths and 5,097 finished square feet make room for long weekends, overnight guests and the luxury of spreading out. Just be prepared for guests to extend their visit. Start outside, where the saltwater pool heats up, cools down and makes a very persuasive case for staying home. Warm the water on cooler days or cool it during a Kansas summer, then settle into a sunny spot on the pool terrace or a shady seat beneath the covered outdoor space. This is the kind of backyard that turns a small get-together into an all-afternoon affair. The pool area is prewired for speakers, a ramp simplifies moving equipment to the beach, and the community permits docks for those envisioning a future addition. The arrival is all substance and style: a brick exterior with stone accents, a concrete tile roof and a striking iron front door. Inside, the 2,889 sq. ft. main level is dressed to impress. A coffered ceiling, stone fireplace and built-in cabinetry give the great room warmth and presence, while the dining room's barrel ceiling brings a little architectural drama to dinner. The connected living spaces keep the conversation flowing from the kitchen to the table. The kitchen is ready for quiet breakfasts and those "we invited a few more people" evenings. A generous island anchors the kitchen, complemented by rich cabinetry, contrasting countertops, pendant lighting and an apron-front sink. All kitchen appliances remain, including the induction cooktop. A gas connection for a future range and an oven area planned to accommodate double ovens leave room for your culinary ambitions. The all-seasons room, updated with new windows, offers a front-row seat to the lake and a very convincing reason to linger over coffee. The primary suite is your invitation to slow down: wake to water views, sink into a soaking tub, or enjoy a shower with two showerheads and tile all the way to the ceiling. Illuminated mirrors and contrasting tile accents add a polished, spa-like finish. The home also leaves room for life's next chapter, with a main-level office pre-framed for potential bedroom conversion and ADA-height toilets throughout. Downstairs, 2,208 finished square feet keep the possibilities coming. A spacious family room and wet bar are ready for game days and evenings with friends, with additional space for an office or gym. An extra storage room offers home-theater potential. Your popcorn deserves a proper venue. Walkout access makes it easy to take the gathering outdoors. The luxury is in the details, and the comfort is built right in. Separate HVAC systems help maintain comfortable temperatures throughout the home, complemented by Low-E windows and LED lighting. Built-in speakers carry your soundtrack throughout the home, and the camera system stays. Even the garage received the full treatment with fully insulated, soundproof cast iron doors paired with industrial openers and equipment selected for durability and built to stand the test of time. When it's time for a bite or a drink, walk, boat or even swim over to Deano's on Pier 37. Dinner plans just became a perfectly reasonable excuse to buy a boat! Bring your lake-day plans, your favorite people and a calendar with some breathing room. This is a home that gives an ordinary Tuesday something to celebrate.
