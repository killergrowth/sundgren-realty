Property Description

Welcome home to this charming and tastefully updated twin home tucked away at the back of a quiet cul de sac in desirable northeast Wichita! Situated in a peaceful residential neighborhood with a beautiful backdrop of mature trees and natural vegetation, this home offers a wonderful combination of convenience, comfort, and tranquility. Step inside to discover updated flooring and an open, agreeable floor plan where the kitchen, dining area, and living room flow naturally together, creating an inviting space for everyday living or entertaining. The living room is especially cozy with a beautiful wood burning fireplace surrounded by tasteful tile and capped with a painted mantel, perfect for those crisp Kansas fall evenings! The dining area features an attractive five light fixture that helps define the space, while the convenient kitchen eating bar and open pass through allow everyone to stay connected, whether preparing dinner or enjoying time together. The kitchen is equally impressive with updated granite countertops, a classic subway tile backsplash, refreshed cabinetry and hardware, and a convenient built in microwave above the stove. Offering two bedrooms on the main floor, a centrally located full bathroom with double sinks, medicine cabinets, and generous vanity storage, this home makes excellent use of its space. Downstairs, the appeal continues with a spacious second family room featuring large viewout windows that bring in natural light and provide a pleasant connection to the backyard. An additional bedroom and versatile bonus room without an egress window offer flexibility for an office, hobby space, or additional storage. The basement laundry area also includes convenient shelving for supplies and organization. But one of the most enjoyable features of this property may be waiting just outside! Step onto the elevated back deck and take in the peaceful views of mature trees, lush vegetation, and the occasional visiting wildlife. It is a wonderful place to enjoy your morning coffee, unwind after a long day, or simply appreciate a little quiet in the middle of the city. As an added bonus, the entire interior is scheduled to receive fresh paint before closing, providing an even more refreshed space for the next owner to enjoy. If you have been searching for a comfortable, thoughtfully updated home with inviting living spaces and a beautiful natural setting, this is one you will want to experience for yourself. Come see it soon!
