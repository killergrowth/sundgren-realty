Property Description

There’s something special about coming home to a place that feels peaceful the moment you arrive. Tucked away in Criss 1st Addition and just minutes from El Dorado Lake, 1450 Chelle Ct offers the kind of setting where mornings are quiet, evenings are relaxing, and there’s room for everyone to truly spread out and feel at home. Step inside and you’re welcomed by soaring vaulted ceilings, beautiful natural light, and a remodeled kitchen that feels like the heart of the home. Whether you’re hosting holidays, gathering for dinner, or enjoying a quiet night in, the layout offers plenty of room to live comfortably without ever feeling crowded. With 6 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, there’s space for family, guests, a home office, hobby room, or whatever life brings next. The basement adds even more flexibility, along with two separate designated storage areas that give you the kind of storage space that is hard to find. Out back is where this home really shines. Step onto the covered back porch and take in the peaceful pond views, watch the geese gather along the water, and enjoy a backyard setting that feels like your own private retreat. The covered front porch adds even more charm and gives you another perfect place to sit, unwind, and enjoy the neighborhood. From the remodeled kitchen and vaulted ceilings to the incredible amount of space, storage, and beautiful pond views, this home was made to be lived in and enjoyed. Located in Criss 1st Addition near El Dorado Lake, 1450 Chelle Ct offers comfort, space, and a setting that is truly hard to beat.
