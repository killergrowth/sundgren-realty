Property Description

Looking for a home with acreage in Wichita, KS, a massive heated workshop, and room for all your hobbies? Welcome to 1801 W 53rd St N, a unique North Wichita property offering 1.19 acres across two lots, an impressive 35x50 heated and insulated shop, and a spacious 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom tri-level home with 1,850 square feet of finished living space. Whether you're a car enthusiast, hobbyist, or simply searching for a property with extra garage space, RV parking, and room to spread out, this home delivers a combination of features that's increasingly difficult to find within Wichita city limits. Step inside and discover a functional, versatile floor plan featuring a comfortable main-level living room, an inviting kitchen with adjoining dining area, and a spacious finished lower-level family room with a wood-burning fireplace, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining. The split-bedroom layout provides additional privacy, with the primary suite situated on its own upper level and featuring a private bathroom with double sinks. Two additional bedrooms and another full bathroom provide flexibility for guests, a home office, or everyday living. Outside, the property truly shines! The approximately 35x50 heated and insulated detached workshop offers exceptional space for automotive projects, woodworking, equipment storage, recreational vehicles, or a variety of other hobbies. With 4+ garage spaces, oversized parking areas, RV parking, and additional storage buildings, there's plenty of room for vehicles, trailers, tools, and equipment. The expansive 1.19-acre property includes two lots at 1801 and 1819 W 53rd St N, offering generous outdoor space for gardening, entertaining, recreation, or simply enjoying the privacy and flexibility of a larger property. Relax on the covered patio or elevated deck overlooking the yard, while a recently replaced central air conditioning system provides an additional valuable improvement. Conveniently located in North Wichita with access to shopping, dining, and major roadways, this property offers the rare opportunity to enjoy acreage living, extensive garage and workshop space, and the convenience of an established Wichita location.
