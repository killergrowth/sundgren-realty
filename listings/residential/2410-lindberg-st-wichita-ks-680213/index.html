Property Description

WELCOME HOME TO TALLGRASS EAST! Discover the perfect blend of style, comfort, space, and exceptional outdoor living in this beautifully updated ranch nestled in one of East Wichita’s most sought-after communities. Offering 5 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a versatile floor plan, this home is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Step inside to a spacious living room, with a separate office that could also serve as a formal dining room. The open kitchen features a solid-surface island with abundant seating and storage with a window overlooking the backyard. Adjacent to the kitchen, the inviting family room offers a cozy fireplace and multiple built-ins, creating a comfortable space to gather and relax. The breakfast nook provides easy access to an incredible outdoor retreat. Enjoy outdoor living at its finest with a large deck, a screened-in porch, and an additional upper-level deck. The walkout lower level leads to a custom patio and firepit area, creating the perfect setting for relaxing evenings, weekend gatherings, and entertaining family and friends. The private primary suite offers a peaceful escape, complete with a spacious walk-in closet and an ensuite bathroom featuring a soaking tub, separate shower, and large double-sink vanity. Two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, linen storage, and a coat closet complete the main level. Conveniently located just off the kitchen and family room, you'll also find main-floor laundry, a half bath, and easy access to the garage. The finished walkout basement expands your living and entertaining options with a large family room, a designated game area, two additional bedrooms, and a full bathroom. Two generously sized storage rooms provide plenty of space for seasonal items, hobbies, and all your extras. Thoughtful updates and improvements add to the home's comfort, efficiency, and peace of mind, including a high-efficiency HVAC system with humidifier, a Class IV roof, energy-efficient windows throughout, a wrought-iron fence, and neutral interior and exterior paint. The result is a welcoming, move-in-ready home with space and flexibility to fit your lifestyle. Living in Tallgrass East means enjoying an exceptional selection of neighborhood amenities, including a community swimming pool, clubhouse, tennis and pickleball courts, ball fields, and sidewalks throughout the neighborhood. SPACE, STYLE, UPDATES, AND OUTDOOR LIVING—THIS TALLGRASS EAST HOME HAS IT ALL! Come experience everything this exceptional property has to offer and discover why it deserves the top spot on your list!
