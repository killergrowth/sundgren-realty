Property Description

This solid little home sits on a quiet street and still carries the charm of the 1950s, right down to the original hardwood floors. The family room is warm and inviting with a real fireplace, and out back you’ve got a covered patio plus a privacy-fenced yard—plenty of room to spread out without the neighbors watching. The kitchen is practical and ready to go, with the fridge, dishwasher, stove, disposal, and even the microwave already in place. Laundry is right off the kitchen in the attached single-car garage, which keeps things simple. Nice extras from the era include the built-in cabinets in the hallway and a real closet in the bathroom. Come take a look and see for yourself.
