Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, 2-bath home in highly sought-after north Derby. Enjoy the best of both worlds: a friendly small-town community with big-city amenities just minutes away, and convenient access to McConnell Air Force Base. The heart of the home is the large, open-concept kitchen, a bright, modern white design with plenty of counter space, giant island and walk-in pantry, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The adjoining living area is anchored by a cozy electric fireplace with a stone surround, creating a warm focal point for gatherings. The split-bedroom floor plan offers privacy and flexibility, with the primary suite set apart from the secondary bedrooms. Neutral décor throughout makes the home move-in ready and easy to personalize. Step outside to the newly screened-in back porch and enjoy bug-free evenings year-round. A spacious 3-car garage provides abundant room for vehicles, tools, and toys. Enjoy game and movie nights in the spacious family basement with two additional bedrooms, and there is already a rough-in for a third bathroom, giving you the opportunity to add value and finish the space to suit your needs. Don’t miss your chance to own this move-in-ready home with room to grow. Schedule your private showing today!
