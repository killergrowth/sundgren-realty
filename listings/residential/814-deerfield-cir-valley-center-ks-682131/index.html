Property Description

Welcome to 814 Deerfield Circle, where comfort, character, and an incredible backyard setting come together in sought-after Valley Center! Originally built as a model home, this beautiful 4-bedroom, 3-bath ranch offers thoughtful details, an inviting floor plan, and a setting that's becoming harder to find! Nestled in an established neighborhood with mature landscaping, beautiful shade trees, and no neighbors directly behind, this home is sure to impress! Step inside and you'll immediately appreciate the open-concept feel, vaulted living room ceiling, and impressive window that fills the space with natural light. The living room is warm and welcoming, featuring a wood-burning fireplace converted with a gas log insert, perfect for cozy Kansas evenings! The living room flows seamlessly into the dining area, which connects to the kitchen, creating a wonderful layout for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, a brand-new dishwasher, electric range, and a sink overlooking the beautiful backyard. Sliding glass doors off the dining area provide easy access to the extended patio, making indoor and outdoor entertaining a breeze! The primary suite offers double closets and a private ensuite bathroom, providing a comfortable retreat at the end of the day. With four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and additional living space in the finished basement, there's plenty of room to spread out! Step outside and you'll quickly discover one of this property's greatest features! The fully fenced backyard feels like a private retreat, surrounded by mature landscaping and a gorgeous canopy of established shade trees. Whether you're enjoying your morning coffee, hosting a summer barbecue, or simply unwinding after a long day, the extended patio provides the perfect outdoor living space. With no neighbors directly behind, you'll appreciate the added sense of privacy and openness. Keeping this beautiful yard looking its best is easy thanks to the sprinkler system supplied by its own irrigation well, complete with a powerful 1 HP pump and excellent water pressure! A garden shed provides convenient storage for all your outdoor essentials. The upgrades and extras don't stop there! A NEW Class 4 impact-resistant roof and NEW gutters provide peace of mind and potential insurance savings. The home also features a water softener and a garage that's anything but ordinary! Fully finished, insulated, and equipped with HVAC, this versatile space is ideal for hobbies, projects, a workshop, or simply enjoying a more comfortable garage year-round. As a former model home, this property was designed with those special touches that make a house feel like HOME! LOCATION, LOCATION, LOCATION! Enjoy the charm of small-town living with the convenience of Wichita just a short drive away. Valley Center offers a wonderful sense of community, hometown traditions, and plenty of recreational opportunities! Enjoy community parks, walking trails, fishing ponds, splash pads, the city swimming pool, recreation center, and the beloved annual Fall Festival. Even better, elementary and intermediate schools are within walking distance! And here's another bonus buyers will LOVE: NO HOA and NO SPECIAL ASSESSMENTS! Enjoy all the benefits of this established neighborhood without the added expense! With 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, an inviting ranch-style floor plan, numerous improvements, an exceptionally equipped garage, and a backyard that truly feels like an escape, 814 Deerfield Circle offers the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and charm. Come experience everything this wonderful property has to offer! Don’t just dream it… OWN it.
