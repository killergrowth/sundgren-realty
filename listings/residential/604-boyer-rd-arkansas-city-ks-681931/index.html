Property Description

There’s no place like HOME for the holidays, and this beautiful property is ready to welcome its next chapter! Imagine the Christmas tree glowing through the large picture windows, holiday dinners in the formal dining room, and memories being made in the THREE spacious sitting areas! Offering 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a half bath, this inviting home has room for everyone to gather, celebrate, and enjoy!
