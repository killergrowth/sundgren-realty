Property Description

Move-In-Ready! Welcome to this charming, renovated, tri-level home. Enter into the main living space with beautiful hardwood floors. On this level there are three bedrooms. Each of the bedrooms have the same beautiful, refinished hardwood floors. On the main level you will find a full bathroom. The kitchen is conveniently located on this level, furnished with new granite countertops, new faucet and sink. A new stove and new refrigerator have been installed. The microwave and dishwasher stay with the house. The pantry is a must-have in any home. There are just three steps up from the kitchen to the dining room. There are beautiful hardwood floors here too. The dining room leads out to the newly painted deck that overlooks a fenced back yard. The upper level consists of the master bedroom and another full bathroom. The bathrooms have new granite vanity tops, new sinks and faucets. To add to your convenience, the laundry room is situated on this level as well. When you go down the same three stairs to the kitchen, you will be able to get down a flight of newly carpeted steps to the basement. The basement has two renovated "bonus" rooms complete with LVP floors. The furnace room is located in the basement and has a new electric water-heater. Seller is related to agent.
