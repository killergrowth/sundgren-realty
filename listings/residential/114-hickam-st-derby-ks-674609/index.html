Property Description

Now under construction in Foxfire's brand-new Phase 2, this 1,336 sq ft Phantom plan from Comfort Homes is the first home underway in this section — with over 12 additional spec homes also in progress and available for sale. Single-level living on a slab with a zero entry design and split bedroom layout offers easy accessibility and added privacy, while the open concept design creates an easy flow between the main living spaces. Finishes include luxury vinyl plank flooring, quartz countertops, and wide trim throughout the main living area, plus blinds already installed on the patio door slider. The primary suite features a box ceiling for architectural interest, along with a bathroom offering a step-in shower, double vanity, and a spacious walk-in closet. Out back, a 12' x 10' covered patio offers shade and weather protection. Located in Derby — known for its highly regarded school district, convenient shopping and dining, and strong sense of community — this home is also about a mile from McConnell AFB and roughly 10 minutes from east Wichita, offering convenience and a great fit for commuters. A metal safe room in the garage is available for an additional fee, and landscaping packages can be added depending on completion timing (seasonal availability applies). Beyond this home, Phase 2 also offers homesites for custom builds, including roughly a half dozen lake lots and another half dozen tree-lined lots along the back of the development. Phase 1 sold out quickly, and with strong early interest, Phase 2 is expected to move fast as well! Contact us for current construction photos and to learn more!
