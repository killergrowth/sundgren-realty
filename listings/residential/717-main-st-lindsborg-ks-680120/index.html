Property Description

Charming Lindsborg home located just off the bricks and close to Bethany College and downtown! Full of character, this home features original woodwork, refinished wood floors, and a welcoming grand entry. The main floor offers an open living/dining area, remodeled kitchen with pantry/side room, bedroom, and newly remodeld bathroom. Upstairs are three additional bedrooms, all with refinished wood floors, another updated bathroom, and a second kitchenette/multipurpose space ideal for an office, craft room, or hobby area. Enjoy the outdoors from the remodeled enclosed side porch, covered front porch, back patio, and large lot with mature shade trees and new landscaping. The oversized detached two-car garage features a large loft for additional storage or workspace, plus a new concrete driveway and additional storage shed. Basement provides excellent storage with potential for additional living space. Recent updates include new gutters and downspouts, exterior paint completed in June, updated plumbing, remodeled bathrooms and porch, new landscaping, and refinished wood floors. A wonderful combination of historic charm, thoughtful updates, generous space, and a great Lindsborg location!
