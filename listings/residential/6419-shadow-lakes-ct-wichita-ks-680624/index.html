Property Description

Outdoor living at its best! Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Ridge Port, this well-maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom ranch offers multiple spaces to relax and entertain. From the spacious covered deck to the walkout patio and charming garden pond, the backyard is something special! Add in a new roof, fresh exterior paint, an updated guest bathroom, and an oversized 3-car garage, and there’s even more to love! Inside, you’ll find soaring ceilings, large windows, and an inviting living room filled with natural light. The open kitchen and dining area offer plenty of space for gathering, while the primary suite features vaulted ceilings, private deck access, and an en suite bathroom. An additional bedroom and updated full bathroom complete the main floor. The finished walkout basement offers even more living space with a cozy fireplace, built-in shelving, a wet bar, two additional bedrooms, and a full bathroom. Beyond the home, Ridge Port offers incredible neighborhood amenities including a swimming pool, pickleball and basketball courts, playground, and a lake for boating, waterskiing, fishing, and swimming. With no special assessments and a convenient West Wichita location near 29th and Ridge, this home has so much to offer. Schedule your showing today!
