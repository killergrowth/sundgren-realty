Property Description

CHARMING DELANO BUNGALOW WITH CHARACTER, SPACE & SO MUCH POTENTIAL! Welcome to 128 N Sedgwick St, a charming home offering classic character in Wichita’s popular Delano area. An inviting covered front porch sets the tone and welcomes you into a spacious living and dining area featuring beautiful stained woodwork, including hardwood doors, window trim and a distinctive room partition that adds warmth and personality. The kitchen is conveniently located toward the back of the home with easy access to the utility area and rear porch leading to the concrete patio and fenced backyard. A former sunroom is currently being used for storage but offers great possibilities for additional living space, a home office, hobby room or potential third bedroom. Outside, you’ll find a long driveway leading to a spacious 24' x 16' detached garage with workbench, providing plenty of room for parking, storage, projects or a workshop area. An additional storage shed, fenced yard and concrete patio add even more functionality to the outdoor space. Whether you appreciate the home’s vintage character as-is or have ideas for future updates, there is plenty of opportunity here to create something special. And the location is hard to beat! Enjoy convenient access to Douglas Avenue, Delano-area shopping, dining and entertainment, with Friends University just a few blocks away. Whether you're looking for a place to call home or an addition to your investment portfolio, 128 N Sedgwick offers character, location and possibilities all in one.
