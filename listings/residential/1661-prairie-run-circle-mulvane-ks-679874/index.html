Property Description

Welcome to 1661 N Prairie Run Circle; where generous living spaces, thoughtful updates and inviting outdoor areas come together in the Country Walk neighborhood of Mulvane. This 3-bedroom, 4-bath home offers over 2,000 square feet of finished living space with a layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Step inside to a light-filled living room featuring vaulted ceilings and a fireplace that creates a natural focal point for the main living area. The kitchen and dining spaces flow easily together, while the main-level laundry adds everyday convenience. The spacious primary suite offers a walk-in closet and private bath complete with dual sinks, a soaking tub, and separate shower. Downstairs, the finished basement significantly expands the home’s possibilities with additional recreation and living space as well a bedroom, 2 bathrooms, and storage; providing plenty of flexibility for a home gym, media room, hobbies, or guests. Step outside to the spacious deck and enjoy a private, fenced backyard with a gate that opens to a beautiful, peaceful view of mature trees and a pond, creating a country-like feel right in your own backyard. The community also features three ponds and a playground, offering additional outdoor enjoyment just steps from home. An irrigation well and sprinkler system help simplify lawn care, while the attached two-car garage provides convenient parking and extra storage. Whether you're enjoying your morning coffee, hosting gatherings, or simply taking in the scenery, this outdoor space offers a wonderful blend of privacy, nature, and community living. 1661 N Prairie Run Circle offers room to make the space your own.
