Property Description

Don’t miss this 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom ranch in the heart of Valley Center! Set in the Valley Center School District, the home is just a short walk from Lions Park, the city pool, the pickleball courts, and the community building. You’re also only a few blocks from local favorites on Meridian like Big Larry’s, and the annual Fall Festival on Main Street is within walking distance. Pulling up to the home, you’ll notice the brick accents, wood siding, and mature trees shading the front yard, along with a basketball goal by the driveway. The home also has a brand-new Class 4 impact-resistant roof, which can help lower your homeowner’s insurance and your overall monthly costs. Step inside and you’ll see right away how well this home has been maintained. The kitchen features a subway tile backsplash, generous counter space, and a raised eating bar overlooking the dining area. Newer luxury vinyl plank flooring runs through both spaces, and a sliding glass door leads out to the covered back deck. From there, the dining area opens to the living room, anchored by a brick fireplace with gas logs. Down the hall you’ll find three bedrooms and a full bathroom with a tub and shower. The primary bedroom includes its own private bathroom with a walk-in shower. Head downstairs to the full basement, where the finished half offers a large family room and game area with space for movie nights, entertaining, or a home gym. The unfinished half provides tons of storage along with the laundry area, plus plenty of room for a workshop or future expansion. In the backyard you’ll love the large deck, with a covered section for relaxing and an open section for grilling. Mature trees provide plenty of shade throughout the summer. The lawn is in great shape, and the fully fenced yard includes a very large shed for extra storage or hobby space. Move-in ready homes in this location don’t come up often in Valley Center.
