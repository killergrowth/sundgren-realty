Property Description

Welcome to 1909 N High St! This charming 3-bedroom, 1-bath ranch offers 1,064 square feet of comfortable, single-level living with several great updates already completed! Major improvements include a NEW ROOF installed in November 2025, complete with Class 3 impact-resistant shingles and a transferable 10-year workmanship warranty, plus a NEW SEWER LINE from the home to the city main installed in 2025. Inside, you'll find updated luxury vinyl plank flooring in the bedrooms, along with a new dishwasher and range. All kitchen appliances stay, making your move that much easier! One of the standout features of this property is the unique backyard entertaining area! Enjoy your own little outdoor oasis featuring built-in concrete tables, countertop space, and an outdoor kitchen-style setup, perfect for cookouts, gatherings, or simply relaxing outside. Mature trees provide plenty of shade and add to the inviting outdoor atmosphere. Additional features include an attached one-car garage, main-floor laundry, and a convenient single-story layout. With major updates already taken care of and a backyard space you don't see every day, this home has plenty to offer. Schedule your showing today!
