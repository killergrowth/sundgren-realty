Property Description

Some homes just check the boxes. Others stand out because of the details—and this is one of them! Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated, this home offers an impressive combination of style, comfort, and functionality. Gorgeous hardwood floors flow through the main-floor living areas, including the inviting living room with coffered ceiling, gas log fireplace, transom windows, and built-in bookcase featuring a hidden electronic TV lift. Nicely remodeled kitchen with quartz countertops, tile backsplash, induction range with exterior vented hood, soft-close drawers, upper and lower cabinet lighting, pull-out trash cabinet, and pantry with pull-out shelving and all kitchen appliances stay. An eating bar, informal dining area, and formal dining room with wainscoting provide plenty of dining options. Sliding glass doors lead to the deck overlooking the spacious backyard. Split bedroom floor plan has a nice sized master primary suite with private bath featuring travertine stone countertops, separate shower, jetted tub, & walk-in closet. Main floor laundry has tile floors, sink & built-in collapsible drying rack. Completing the main floor are two more bedrooms (one with an attractive accent wall) and an updated guest bath with quartz countertop. Wood stairs lead to the finished walkout lower level. Open rec room with LVP flooring and space for a pool table and game area, a fourth bedroom with accent wall & walk through closet, and third bathroom with solid-surface countertop. Bonus room could be a home office, exercise room, hobby space, or play area. Large storage room contains a built in workbench Situated on over 1/3 acre, the property features an expansive yard, deck, and walkout patio. Additional highlights include most of the siding and windows replaced/updated, sprinkler system, water softener, reverse osmosis filtration under the sink and to the kitchen refrigerator. Add in the neighborhood amenities and convenient location, and there's even more to appreciate.
