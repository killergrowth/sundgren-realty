Property Description

Timeless craftsmanship, thoughtfully designed living spaces, and peaceful pond views! This meticulously maintained, single owner, 1½-story home nicely located in the middle of the established Tiara Pines neighborhood offers approximately 3,700 square feet of finished living space, 5 bedrooms + 2 bonus rooms, 3½ bathrooms, and a wonderful combination of traditional character and everyday functionality. Built in 2000, the home showcases beautiful hardwood flooring, detailed medium-oak woodwork, granite countertops, and the warmth of classic design. Unlike today's predominantly open-concept layouts, this home offers intentionally defined spaces, including a formal dining room in addition to a spacious eat-in kitchen. Whether hosting holiday gatherings, enjoying a quiet evening, or simply appreciating the room to spread out, this floor plan offers flexibility for a variety of lifestyles. The main-floor primary suite and generously sized laundry room with sink add convenience to everyday living, while 3 bedrooms are located on the second level, an additional bedroom is downstairs along with 2 bonus basement rooms, providing plenty of options for guests, hobbies, home offices, or other needs. Step outside onto the composite deck and enjoy the peaceful setting overlooking the neighborhood pond. It's an inviting spot for morning coffee, evening relaxation, or simply taking in the view. A spacious three-car garage provides additional functionality, while the Derby school district adds to the home's appeal. Entertain on the main level's gorgeous symmetrical formal living room, with vaulted ceilings and statement fireplace (cue Christmas Tree) or retreat to downstairs to a spacious family room complete with wet bar, space for a pool table and safe room! Beautifully cared for and rich in traditional detail, this home offers something increasingly distinctive: craftsmanship, defined living spaces, generous square footage, and a picturesque pond side setting. Bonus feature: roof was replaced Feb 2026! Come experience all this traditional home has to offer!
