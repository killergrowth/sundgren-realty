Property Description

COMING SOON! Showings will begin 11/28. Welcome to 6402 E Oneida! Conveniently located in the heart of East Wichita, just minutes from the shopping and dining options at Normandie Shopping Center, this move-in-ready ranch-style home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and comfortable, easy living. Extensively updated in 2022, the home beautifully blends modern updates with original charm, including gorgeous original hardwood floors throughout. The spacious living room offers plenty of room to relax, along with additional built-in storage and cabinetry. Just off the family room, the dining area provides a great gathering space, while the cozy wood-burning fireplace adds warmth and character. All three bedrooms are nicely sized, with the primary bedroom featuring a walk-in closet. You'll also appreciate two full bathrooms and a convenient drop zone with additional pantry storage. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced backyard designed for entertaining or unwinding. The patio features a pergola, while the separate firepit area offers additional seating and string lights, creating the perfect setting for relaxing evenings outdoors. An attached two-car garage with built in shelving completes the package! Schedule your showing today.
