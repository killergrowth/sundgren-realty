Property Description

Rarely does a property capture the history, architecture and unmistakable charm of College Hill quite like 3823 E Lewis St. Built in 1922 and situated on an extraordinary .85-acre wooded lot in the heart of one of Wichita’s most sought-after historic neighborhoods, this distinctive Spanish Revival home offers approximately 2,700 square feet of finished living space, 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths and a backyard setting that feels like a private retreat. From the moment you arrive, the clay tile roof, stucco exterior and mature tree canopy hint at the character waiting inside, where arched openings, ornate ironwork, exposed wood beams, rich hardwoods, hand-carved details and dramatic fireplaces showcase craftsmanship rarely found in newer homes. Generous living and gathering spaces provide room to entertain, relax and create a home that fits your lifestyle, while French doors and abundant windows connect the interior to the beautiful grounds beyond. Step outside and discover one of the property’s defining features: a secluded backyard oasis centered around an in-ground swimming pool and surrounded by mature trees, brick patios, covered outdoor living areas and a pool house. With nearly an acre of space, the property offers a remarkable combination of privacy and convenience that is especially difficult to find in College Hill. A detached garage, additional carport and expansive grounds provide even more flexibility. And the location is every bit as compelling as the home itself, placing you near College Hill Park, the Douglas Avenue corridor, local restaurants like Larcher's, The Belmont and Ziggy's, coffee shops and neighborhood favorites, with convenient access to downtown Wichita and destinations throughout the city. For buyers searching for a historic College Hill home with a pool, substantial living space, architectural character and an exceptional lot, this home offers a combination that is truly difficult to duplicate. This is more than a home with history; it is an opportunity to become part of the next chapter of one of College Hill’s most distinctive properties.
