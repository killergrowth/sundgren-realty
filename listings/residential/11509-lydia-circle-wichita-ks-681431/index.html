Property Description

Cute, updated, and full of charm! This 2-bedroom, 3-bath home offers two comfortable living areas and plenty of space to fit your lifestyle. The kitchen features quartz countertops, while main-floor laundry adds everyday convenience. The primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet, with quartz countertops adding a fresh touch to the primary bathroom. Downstairs, a second living area with a wet bar makes a great spot for entertaining or relaxing. A bonus room off the downstairs bathroom suite offers flexible space for a home office or workout room. Step outside to enjoy the oversized deck—perfect for cookouts or a quiet evening overlooking the fenced backyard. An outdoor storage shed provides extra room for tools and seasonal items, while the two-car garage rounds out this charming home.
