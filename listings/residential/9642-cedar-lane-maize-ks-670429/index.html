About This Property

The Olive is an award winning 6 bedroom 3 bath home that also has an office! Arch door entry opens to a great room with a tray ceiling and a 16’ slider that opens up to a 19’ deck. Custom kitchen cabinets are beautiful and a trademark of this builder. You’ll love the coffee bar/beverage bar niche in the dining area, complete with a sink. The kitchen even has room for a beverage fridge. The primary suite is beautiful with a lovely high vaulted ceiling, and the bathroom has a gorgeous rich toned full tile shower with dual shower heads and wand. Laundry connects to primary closet. The dedicated office on the main level has a pocket door, and lots of built in cabinets and book cases for your needs. In addition to the extra large deck, you can access the back yard from a mid level walk out door on the way to the basement. Stunning wet bar with lit cabinets is found in the huge family room. The bedrooms are all larger and give room for everyone to have their own space. There is also a large unfinished area for your storage needs. The garage is oversized and has extra space behind the 3rd car bay for storage, bikes, lawn mowers, or a work shop area. The details in this home are amazing and quality craftmanship show everywhere.
