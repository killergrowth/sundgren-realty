Property Description

Looking for a home that feels brand new without the brand-new price tag? Welcome to 617 N Plum in Moline, Kansas! This beautifully updated home offers extensive improvements inside and out, providing a fresh, modern feel in a peaceful small-town setting. Situated on a quiet dead-end street, the property offers a sense of privacy and a large backyard with plenty of room to enjoy outdoor activities, gardening, entertaining, or simply relaxing. The exterior features fresh paint and a brand-new roof on both the house and the shed, giving the property a refreshed appearance. Step inside to discover an updated interior with new walls, fresh paint, new lighting, updated plumbing and electrical, and new mini-split systems. The open-concept layout creates a spacious living area that flows into the kitchen and dining space, offering a comfortable setting for everyday living and gathering with family and friends. Two bedrooms are conveniently located just north of the main living area, providing comfortable spaces to unwind. The brand-new bathroom adds to the home's fresh, updated appeal. You'll also appreciate the dedicated utility room, which offers convenient access to the backyard and adds functionality to the home's layout. Outside, the expansive backyard provides endless possibilities for making the space your own. Whether you envision a garden, outdoor seating area, play space, or room for pets to roam, there is plenty of space to enjoy. The detached shed offers additional storage potential for lawn equipment, tools, and outdoor essentials. With extensive updates throughout, a spacious open living area, a large backyard, and a quiet location in Moline, this home is ready for its next chapter. If you're looking for a refreshed home with modern updates and room to enjoy the outdoors, 617 N Plum is one to put on your list!
