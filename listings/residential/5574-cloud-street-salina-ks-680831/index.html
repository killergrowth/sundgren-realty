Property Description

Discover the freedom of country living just a short distance from Salina. Set on 34 acres, this inviting barndominium offers 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, and a 3-car garage, with plenty of space to relax, work, and enjoy the outdoors. Inside, the open-concept layout features custom soft close cabinetry and a wood-burning fireplace with blower. Everyday conveniences include a refrigerator, stove, dishwasher, water softener, and on-demand hot water heater. You’ll also find a pantry/safe room and an attached bonus shop or mancave just behind the garage. Above the garage is an unfinished storage area, prepped and ready to finish. The property is HCI Fiber ready and energy efficient. Outside, there’s a 30-amp electrical and water hookup for a camper or RV, along with a shed and a chicken coop with loft. With 34 acres to explore, there are also opportunities to enjoy the local wildlife. There’s much more to discover at this distinctive property—come see it for yourself!
