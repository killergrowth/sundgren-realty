Property Description

Welcome to this wonderful newer ranch home in Marquette, Kansas, offering space, comfort, and versatility on a double lot! The inviting open feel begins with vaulted ceilings in the living room and dining areas, complemented by both a formal dining space and a cozy breakfast nook. The main floor features a private master suite with a walk-in closet and full bath, two additional bedrooms, another full bath, and convenient main-floor laundry with easy access to the attached two-car garage. The fully finished basement provides even more room to spread out, featuring a family room, separate den, fourth bedroom, full bath, and an additional bonus room with the potential to add an egress window for even more living space. And there's more! The detached 28' x 24' garage is fully insulated and equipped with a workbench, making it ideal for hobbies, projects, a workshop, or extra storage. With a double lot, generous living space, and two garages, this property offers so much flexibility for everyday living and entertaining. Conveniently located close to Marquette's local amenities, this is a fantastic opportunity to enjoy small-town living with room to grow!
