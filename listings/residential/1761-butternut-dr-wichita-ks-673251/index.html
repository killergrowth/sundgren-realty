Property Description

For Sale! Nestled at the end of S. Butternut Drive in the sought-after Arbor Lakes Estates community, this custom two-story home built by Clifford Nies in 1992 delivers a scale and level of craftsmanship that sets it apart from the moment you arrive. Substantially remodeled in 2020, the home offers over 4,000 square feet above grade, two master suites, five bedrooms, four and a half baths, and a three-car garage. The craftsmanship throughout reflects a level of customization that simply isn't replicated in production builds — from the wood-burning fireplace with gas starter and formal dining room with pass-through window, to the main-floor laundry with chute, built-in two-person desk, and mudroom bench off the garage. The upper master suite features an onyx shower and is served by three full baths across the upper level. The mostly unfinished basement offers over 2,000 square feet already framed for two bedrooms and a bath — a blank canvas with real upside. Major systems have been addressed: Trane HVAC (2024), water heaters (2019), CertainTeed Landmark TL IR Class 4 roof by Heiland Roofing with documentation on file, lifetime windows, blown-in insulation, and a Rainbird sprinkler system on a dedicated irrigation well. All appliances transfer — KitchenAid suite (refrigerator excluded), LG microwave, Speed Queen washer and dryer, and Frigidaire upright freezer. Low average utilities bills is a testament to how well this home was built and maintained. Tucked into Hunter's Pointe with a fishable community pond and neighborhood events throughout the year, and just minutes from Christa McAuliffe Academy K-8, Southeast High School, and K-96 via Kellogg.
