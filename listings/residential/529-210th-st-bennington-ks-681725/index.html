Property Description

Situated on 3.46 tree-lined acres, this property offers privacy, beautiful views, and a spacious, updated main-floor living area. The open floor plan features beautiful wood floors, exposed wood beams, and tons of natural light. The kitchen has custom wood cabinetry, an island, and all appliances, including a gas range. Main-floor laundry, and a primary bedroom with a large walk-in closet and unfinished bathroom provide an opportunity to add your finishing touches. Enjoy peaceful country surroundings with room to spread out!
