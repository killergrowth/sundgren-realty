Property Description

Welcome home to this updated three-bedroom, one-bath ranch in Country Acres! Major improvements include new siding and an updated kitchen featuring new cabinets, countertops, and sink, with all kitcehn appliances staying. New laminate flooring throughout, fresh paint across the entire interior, and new baseboards give the home a clean, modern feel. Additional updates include newer vinyl double-hung windows, a newer garage door, an updated electrical panel, and a 2022 water heater. Gutter guards round out the exterior features. The large backyard shed with 220-volt electrical service offers space for hobbies and projects, while an attached one-car garage and large carport provide ample covered parking. All three bedrooms are on the main floor, with a finished basement family room and separate utility area below.
