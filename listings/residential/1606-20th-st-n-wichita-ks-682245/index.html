Property Description

Unique opportunity. Everything is brand new, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, drywall, paint, flooring, etc. It's a brand new home, on the inside of a former existing brick home! Seller took the house to the studs, redesigned the floor plan. A modern home with luxury vinyl flooring, granite counter tops, black metal accents and ceiling fans. Main floor has master bedroom, other bedroom and a bathroom between them. Living room, dining room and 1/2 bath. Kitchen features brand new appliances: Refrigerator with water in the door, microwave, stove/oven, dishwasher and disposal. Lower level has a laundry room with a washer and dryer, rec room, bedroom with a large walk in closet, small office/hobby area, and a large bathroom with 2 sinks. This is 1/2 of a duplex or twin home. The other side, 1608, is not finished.
