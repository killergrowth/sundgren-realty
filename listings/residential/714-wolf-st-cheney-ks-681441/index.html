Property Description

Welcome Home to 714 N. Wolf, Cheney, KS! Space, style, and thoughtful updates come together in this beautifully remodeled home featuring 5 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, an oversized 2-car garage, and approximately 3,488 square feet of living space! From the moment you arrive, you’ll appreciate the inviting curb appeal, mature trees, brand-new front steps and sidewalks. The exterior offers even more to love with a 5-zone sprinkler system servicing the front and side yards, plus a spacious yard with plenty of room for RV parking or a future shed. Step inside and discover a beautifully updated interior with an open, welcoming floor plan. The living room features a cozy gas fireplace, complemented by a staircase with custom newel posts. The remodeled kitchen is sure to impress with soft-close cabinetry, a farmhouse sink, and Whirlpool appliances. You’ll appreciate the thoughtful details throughout, including water-resistant luxury vinyl flooring on the main level, upgraded carpet padding, easy-open door handles. Looking for extra space to spread out? You’ll love the HUGE sunroom filled with natural light, along with an additional main-floor family room offering flexibility for entertaining, relaxing, or everyday living. The finished basement provides even more room with an impressive 34’ x 13’ family room featuring a wood-burning fireplace, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a separate laundry room. Outside, enjoy the large partially covered patio and spacious fenced backyard, perfect for entertaining, family gatherings, pets, or simply enjoying the outdoors. Location is another bonus! Just 20 minutes west of Wichita with convenient highway access, Cheney offers small-town charm and plenty of amenities, including local dining, the highly regarded Cheney school system, and the beautiful Cherry Oaks 18-hole golf course. With incredible space, extensive updates, and room for everyone, this home truly has so much to offer!
