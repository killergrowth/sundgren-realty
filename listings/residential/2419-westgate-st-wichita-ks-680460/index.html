Property Description

Located in the highly desirable Southern Ridge neighborhood, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath + bonus room home offers over 2,800 sq. ft. of living space, and a 3-car garage! Step inside to a welcoming foyer with a coat closet, and your eyes will immediately be drawn to the large living room windows with newer plantation shutters and vaulted ceilings. The living room flows right into the kitchen and dining area, creating a great space for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen features granite countertops, a pantry closet, garbage disposal, and stainless steel appliances that all stay, including a new microwave, range, dishwasher and refrigerator. Just off the kitchen, the main-floor laundry room provides convenient access to the garage. The main floor also includes the primary suite and two additional bedrooms with a full bathroom. The primary bedroom features a tray ceiling, a charming bay window with a built-in seating area, and newer flooring. The ensuite bathroom offers quartz countertops, double sinks, a large soaking tub, separate shower and walk-in closet. Downstairs, you'll find a spacious view-out basement with plenty of natural light and room to create separate living and recreation areas. A large fourth bedroom with a walk-in closet, a full bathroom, and an additional bonus room provide plenty of flexibility for your needs. Outside, enjoy the newly painted deck overlooking a wood-fenced backyard, along with a generously sized concrete patio perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The roof was replaced in 2022. One of the best features? Open the backyard gate and step right onto the neighborhood walking trails! Southern Ridge offers fantastic community amenities, including a swimming pool, basketball and tennis courts, a playground, and two ponds. It's more than just a place to call home—it's a neighborhood where neighbors connect, friendships grow at the pool, and morning coffee walks become part of your routine. Goddard Schools!
