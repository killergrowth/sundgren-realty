Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully renovated, move-in-ready home in West Wichita! Tucked away in a quiet neighborhood on a peaceful cul-de-sac, this stunning 4 bed, 3 bath single-family home offers a beautiful setting with a lake view and plenty of updates throughout. Step inside to find fresh interior paint, new modern light fixtures, wood laminate flooring throughout most of the home, and brand-new carpet in all four bedrooms.
