Property Description

This is one you have to walk through to fully appreciate. Originally built as a model home, this meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers 2,700+ finished square feet, PLUS an additional unfinished basement room ready to be completed as a potential FIFTH BEDROOM. A BRAND-NEW Class 3 impact-resistant roof and NEW gutters were also installed in 2026, adding valuable peace of mind from day one. From the moment you step through the striking 4' iron pivot door (hellooo grand entrance!!), the home feels bright, polished and anything but ordinary. Vaulted ceilings, warm wood details and oversized windows give the main living space the elevated feel of a luxury model home, while the open kitchen, dining and living areas flow together effortlessly. The kitchen anchors the space with black cabinetry, a black granite waterfall island and a large walk-in pantry with plug-ins for your small appliances and plenty of additional counter space. The dining area is surrounded by natural light and defined by a distinctive black ceiling detail, with views overlooking the second living space below. One of the home's most unique features is this cozy, carpeted second living area. Surrounded by oversized windows and anchored by a fireplace, it feels warm and inviting while filled with natural light. Sliding glass doors open directly to the patio, creating an easy indoor-outdoor flow for entertaining, relaxing or simply enjoying a second gathering space away from the main living room. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath and laundry room complete this level, along with direct access to the oversized three-car garage with no steps into the home. Privately tucked away from the main living areas, the primary suite feels like its own wing of the home. Double doors close off the suite, with the bedroom to one side and a spa-inspired bath and large walk-in closet to the other. The bath features a freestanding slipper tub, tiled walk-in shower, dual vanity, private water closet and distinctive tile and under-cabinet lighting. The finished basement adds another spacious family room, fourth bedroom and full bath, along with the unfinished room that could become a fifth bedroom, home office, gym or additional finished space. Outside, enjoy a fenced backyard with mature trees and multiple patio areas, plus an irrigation well. Edgewater neighborhood amenities include a community pool and walking paths. Beautiful in photos, even better in person. This is a home where the design, condition and unique layout truly set it apart.
