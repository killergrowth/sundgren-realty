Property Description

Discover the charm of this well cared for full brick home, nestled in an established neighborhood at 1631 Glasgow N St. This property provides a wonderful layout that combines classic architectural details with modern updates. The main living space features a combined formal living and dining room, offering a versatile area for hosting. The heart of the home is the main floor family room, which is highlighted by a beamed ceiling, beautiful pegged hardwood floors, and a cozy wood-burning fireplace. A door from the family room leads directly to the backyard, seamlessly extending your living space outdoors. The kitchen has been thoughtfully updated with newer appliances, including a microwave, cooktop, double oven, and dishwasher, and features an attached eating area for casual meals. Accommodations include three bedrooms, with two sharing a convenient updated Jack-and-Jill bathroom equipped with a shower. A second fully remodeled bathroom, located in the hall near the primary bedroom, offers the luxury of a separate tub and shower. Completing the main level is a dedicated laundry room. Outdoor living is equally inviting, with a fenced yard adorned by mature trees. Additional practical features include an oversized attached two-car garage and a partial basement that provides extra space for a recreation room. A new roof will be installed prior to closing!! This home will not last long!
