Property Description

Investor special in west Wichita. 1633 N Joann is a 3 bedroom, 1 bath ranch on a 0.18 acre lot, and the expensive work is already done. The roof is about two years old, and the furnace and AC were replaced in 2018. What’s left is cosmetic. A covered front porch opens to a spacious living room with a large picture window. The kitchen has a long run of white cabinets, a double sink, a working dishwasher that stays, and an eat-in dining area. Original built-ins line the hallway, all three bedrooms have ceiling fans, and the full bath has a tub and shower combination with additional built in. The carpet, kitchen finishes and bath are ready for updates. A sunroom addition with its own AC adds bonus space and connects to a separate laundry room with hookups. Outside, the fully fenced yard has mature shade trees and a storage shed, and the long concrete driveway leads to a rebuilt 1 car garage with an opener and a workbench. Whether you plan to renovate and resell or hold it as a rental, the bones are here. This property is being sold as-is.
