Property Description

Experience low-maintenance living with stunning views at 1401 W. Riverview Blvd. Unit 2D! Opportunities to own in the highly sought-after River View community are few and far between, making this a truly special offering. This beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 2-bath condominium features an open, light-filled floor plan with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the Arkansas River. The spacious living and dining areas flow seamlessly to a private balcony, creating the perfect space to relax or entertain. The updated kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, solid surface countertops, stainless steel appliances, and generous prep space. The primary suite includes a large walk-in closet and an en-suite bath, while the second bedroom is ideal for guests, a home office, or hobby space. A full-size in-unit laundry room and additional indoor storage add everyday convenience. Residents enjoy secure building access, elevator service, covered parking, a fitness room, and the private River Room for entertaining. The HOA fee covers heat, air conditioning, water, sewer, trash, shared plumbing, building insurance, exterior maintenance, grounds maintenance, reserve funding, and more, making ownership simple and predictable. This owner-occupied, non-smoking, pet-free, adult community is just minutes from Botanica, the Wichita Art Museum, and downtown Wichita, offering the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and scenic riverfront living. With very few units becoming available in this exclusive community, this is a rare chance to own one of Wichita's hidden gems.
