Property Description

Here is a rare opportunity to own a mid-century gem which backs up to the natural prairie, towering trees and the Little Arkansas. This beautiful home has been lovingly updated while preserving its natural charm inside and out. Situated on a half acre lot, this mid-century rambler offers a covered front porch, front patio with seating area and a beautiful brick exterior. As you make your way inside, you will notice the clean lines that make up the layout of this home. The kitchen is a galley style with great functionality. It has a breakfast bar which is open to the hearth room, perfect for entertaining. A large sliding glass door makes indoor/outdoor entertaining seamless with a large screened in porch. Your plants will love this room and it's a great place to have an outdoor dining space. There is a second sliding glass door which leads into your living area with grand vaulted ceilings, built-ins and more of that mid-century charm that we love about this home. Four bedrooms including the primary suite as well as a full hall bathroom with tub and shower combination are situated together. The primary suite has a walk-through closet as well as an ensuite bathroom with a walk-in shower. To the basement, you will find a part of the home that has not been touched by time. This room would be incredible as a teen hangout space or rec room for gatherings. There is an original bar, built in seating with colorful cushions in perfect condition and an atrium or pond space for a turtle or decorative items. Even the laundry room is not mediocre in this home. You'll find storage galore in the form of plenty of cabinet space. As if the home were not enough to entice you, step outside and notice the wildlife that calls your backyard home. Prairie grasses make this a popular spot for many kinds of birds such as egrets, hawks, and even the occasional eagle. Deer find solace in the expansive and untouched nature and you will find them grazing around the property. There are trails that surround the Little Arkansas and you can hike in your own backyard. Plan to see this incredible property and make it your "home sweet home" today.
