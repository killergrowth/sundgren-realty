Property Description

Charming 3-bedroom home, perfectly situated on a bright corner lot just down the street from the WSU campus! Step inside to find freshly refinished hardwood floors running throughout and a crisp, clean coat of paint that makes the entire space feel airy and move-in ready. The spacious living room features a beautiful oversized window that floods the space with an abundance of natural light. You'll love cooking and entertaining in the expansive kitchen dining combo, complete with quartz counters and plenty of space for both meal prep and family dinners. Highlights include 3 bright bedrooms, a fully updated bathroom, and a separate mudroom/laundry to keep entry clutter out of sight. A 1-car garage provides extra storage and secure parking. Whether you are looking for a fantastic starter home, a turnkey space close to campus, or a solid investment, this bright and beautifully updated property is ready for its new owners!
