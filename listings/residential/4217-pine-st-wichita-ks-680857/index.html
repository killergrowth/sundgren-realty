Property Description

Brick duplex on a corner lot with a brand-new roof (March 2026). Each unit has 2 bedrooms, 1 bath, and a basement, and the two layouts are nearly identical, which keeps management simple. The building is brick on three sides with low-maintenance vinyl siding on the back. A covered front porch runs across the front, and each unit has its own front and rear entrance. Each side has its own HVAC unit and separate electric and gas meters, which helps keep utility costs with the tenants. A large concrete parking pad off the side street gives plenty of off-street parking. Mature shade trees and a generous yard make the property attractive to long-term tenants. With the roof already replaced, the next owner can put money toward returns instead of big repairs. This property is a good fit for an investor adding to a portfolio, or for a buyer who wants to live in one side and let the other side’s rent help pay the mortgage. Both units are currently occupied. Please give 24 hours’ notice for showings.
