Property Description

Spacious and bright, this 6-bedroom home offers room to spread out and enjoy! With 6 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and multiple living areas, this home offers an impressive amount of space and a versatile layout. Located in the established Glenn Village neighborhood of southwest Wichita, the home welcomes you with impressive double front doors opening to a spacious foyer. The open-concept living room, kitchen, and dining area create a natural gathering space, complete with a wood-burning fireplace in the living room. The kitchen features a large island, plenty of storage, and included appliances. The bright, modern interior has updated finishes and luxury vinyl flooring throughout much of the home. Convenient main-floor laundry is located just off the kitchen, with the washer and dryer included as well. The primary suite features a private bathroom and access to the balcony. Two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the upper level, while three more bedrooms and another full bathroom are located on the main level. The finished basement adds even more flexibility with a large family room and a bonus room ideal for a home office, hobby space, or workout space. Outside, the backyard offers plenty of room to enjoy, with a deck, fire pit area, and storage shed. The attached 2-car garage includes built-in shelving, along with convenient access to the front porch and side yard. The home has been well cared for, and a new roof has been approved by the seller's insurance and will be replaced, offering added peace of mind for the next owner. Conveniently located near the neighborhood park, schools, shopping, and everyday amenities, this home combines generous living space, a bright and inviting interior, and a great outdoor setup. If you've been looking for a home with room for everyone and then some, this one is worth seeing!
