About This Property

Step into timeless charm with this classic old style concrete and stucco home nestled on a beautifully shaded 0.87 acre corner lot in Lincolnville, Kansas. Surrounded by mature trees, established flower beds and abundant garden space, this property is a true gardener's paradise. The welcoming wrap around porch sets the tone for this character filled home offering over 3,200 square feet of living space, plus a huge open attic with endless possibilities for storage, hobbies or future expansion. Inside, you will find 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, along with spacious living areas designed for both everyday comfort and entertaining. The heart of the home is the stunning great room featuring vaulted ceilings, expansive windows that flood the space with natural light and a gas fireplace that provides warmth on chilly evenings or ambiance year round. Combined with the adjoining bonus room, you will enjoy nearly 800 square feet of family gathering and entertainment space. Rich in historic character, both the formal dining room and living room feature beautiful pocket doors, adding elegance and functionality. The homes boiler system provides comfortable radiant heat throughout, while window air conditioning units keep the home cool during the summer months. Outside, you will appreciate the detached 3-car garage, plus a large carport ideal for a camper, boat or oversized vehicle. Whether you are relaxing on the porch, tending the flower gardens or enjoying the shade of the mature trees, this unique property offers the perfect blend of small town living, historic charm and outdoor beauty. Do not miss the opportunity to own one of Lincolnville's distinctive homes with room to grow, entertain and create lasting memories.
