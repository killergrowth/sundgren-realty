About This Property

1511 S Phyllis Ln is the kind of Andover property where summer evenings become the reason you rush home. This 3 bedroom, 3 bathroom home sits on 0.8 acres with mature trees, an in-ground pool, lake views, and a west-facing backyard that puts Kansas sunsets on full display from the covered patio and pool deck. Inside, the home has just as much personality, starting with a sunken living room the current owners have loved so much they say it is a must-have in their next home. The stone fireplace, wood floors, and views toward the water make this a gathering space that feels genuinely special. The main living areas continue with a formal dining room and a more casual eating space connected to the L-shaped kitchen featuring white cabinetry, granite countertops, tile backsplash, an island with cooktop and additional seating, and plenty of room for everyday life. Just off the attached 2 car garage, a pantry, large laundry room, and convenient half bathroom keep the practical side of the home organized. All three bedrooms are located together on the main level, including a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet, private bathroom with tiled walk-in shower, and its own sliding door to the covered back porch. Downstairs, another massive stone fireplace, concrete floors, architectural arches, and an abundance of finished space give this basement a character all its own. There is room for movie nights, games, a home gym, office, play space, or several of those all at once, plus nonconforming rooms that offer even more possibilities and future equity potential. But when the weather warms up, memories move outside. Swim in the in-ground pool, spread out across the large concrete patio, relax beneath the covered porch, or watch the sun disappear over the lake through the mature trees. A sprinkler system supplied by well water takes care of the large yard. With 0.8 of an acre, a pool, water views, multiple indoor gathering spaces, and a setting that feels like an escape at the end of every day, 1511 S Phyllis Ln offers a lifestyle that is difficult to recreate in the Andover School District.
