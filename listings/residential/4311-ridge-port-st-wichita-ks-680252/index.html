Property Description

Welcome to effortless living in the Edgewater! This exceptional 5 bed, 3 bath, 2,700 sq ft home is designed for how you actually live. The main level features a smart split-bedroom plan with 3 bedrooms, including a private primary suite with dual quartz vanities, & a 4-seasons walk-in closet with direct laundry access. At the heart of the home is a fully applianced kitchen built for entertaining - gas range, granite island with eating bar, and a large pantry.
