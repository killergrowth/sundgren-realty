Property Description

Welcome home to 125 S Elizabeth Avenue, where curb appeal is just the beginning! This charming 3-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom home welcomes you with a covered patio, perfect for morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Inside, original hardwood floors showcase the character of this 1954 home, while the spacious living room steals the show with a gas fireplace and built-in shelving. The formal dining area flows into an updated kitchen featuring metal-finished Youngstown cabinetry, a vented range hood, and open shelving. All kitchen appliances convey, and a nearby pantry provides additional storage. A versatile flex room offers endless possibilities for a workshop, home gym, or extra storage. The backyard has been thoughtfully updated for low-maintenance living and entertaining, complete with a patio, pebble rock beds, and stepping stones. Additional peace of mind comes with a newer roof (2023) and a seller-provided home warranty of the buyer’s choice, valued up to $650. Full of charm, functionality, and cozy living, this home is ready for its next owner. Schedule your showing today!
