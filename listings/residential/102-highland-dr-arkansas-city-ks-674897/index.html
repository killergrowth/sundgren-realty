Property Description

Situated on a corner lot that's nearly a third of an acre, this spacious ranch-style home offers timeless mid-century character, generous living spaces, and thoughtful updates throughout. The stone exterior, mature shade trees, and inviting curb appeal create a welcoming first impression, while a brand new Class 4 fiberglass shingle roof provides added peace of mind. Inside, vaulted ceilings and a stunning stone fireplace anchor the expansive living room, complemented by built-in shelves and cabinets that provide both charm and practical storage. The kitchen is open to the dining area and features retro-style cabinetry refreshed with crisp white paint and updated hardware, stainless appliances, subway tile backsplash, a large farmhouse sink, and windows overlooking the yard. Three bedrooms, all with ceiling fans, and one and a half updated baths are located on the main floor, along with the convenience of a main floor laundry room. Downstairs, the finished basement offers exceptional flexibility with a large family and recreation room featuring a brick fireplace, kitchenette, extensive built-in storage, and plenty of space for hobbies, entertaining, game nights, or everyday living. Outside, the covered patio with brick and stone pavers overlooks the fully fenced back yard, while the expansive front and side yards provide even more room to enjoy the outdoors beneath the shade of mature trees. Conveniently located near local schools, shopping, dining, and everyday amenities, this well-maintained home offers the space, character, and versatility you've been searching for.
