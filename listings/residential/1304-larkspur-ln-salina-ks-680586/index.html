Property Description

Enjoy golf course views and exceptional outdoor living in this four-year-old home offering approximately 3,200 square feet of living space. Nestled along the Municipal Golf Course with a great view of the 15th tee box, this four-bedroom home features a heated, saltwater in-ground pool surrounded by an expansive pool deck and patio. A three-car garage with epoxy floors and a roof approximately two months old add to its appeal. Inside, a vaulted ceiling and exposed beam highlight the open-concept living and dining areas. Living room windows overlook the backyard, while a cozy gas fireplace provides warmth for chilly fall and winter evenings. The white kitchen features a prep and eating island that comfortably seats three to four adults. The gas stove/oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, and microwave in the coffee station/buffet all remain. Step from the coffee area onto the covered, screened side deck—an inviting spot for morning coffee and catching up on the news. The pantry, laundry, and garage access are conveniently located nearby, with additional access to the screened porch directly from the garage. The dining area opens onto a spacious, covered, elevated deck overlooking the pool. A spiral staircase leads to the pool deck and patio below, creating inviting spaces for relaxing and entertaining. The expansive primary suite features a coffered ceiling and a spa-like ensuite with a soaking tub, double vanities, a large walk-in shower with a rain showerhead, a walk-in closet, and a private toilet room. A second bedroom and a four-piece bathroom complete this level. The walk-out basement offers two additional bedrooms, another bathroom, and a large recreation room plumbed for a future wet bar. A second gas fireplace adds warmth and ambiance to this comfortable gathering space. With inviting gathering spaces, peaceful golf course views, and your own backyard pool retreat, this home offers a beautiful setting for everyday living and entertaining. From morning coffee on the screened deck to evenings by the fireplace, there’s a special place to enjoy every season. Schedule your private showing and experience it for yourself.
