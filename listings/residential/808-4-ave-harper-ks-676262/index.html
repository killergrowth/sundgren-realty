Property Description

Located just outside Harper, Kansas, only 45 minutes southwest of Wichita, this beautifully updated traditional two-story home offers privacy, space, and exceptional curb appeal on approximately 3.3 total acres. The residence is situated on a 2.1-acre lot, with an additional 1.1-acre lot across the street included with the property. Featuring approximately 3,200 square feet above ground, the home showcases a mostly brick front, newer vinyl siding, vinyl windows, a new metal roof, a circle driveway, and a side-load two-car garage. The private backyard is surrounded by an abundance of mature trees and features an impressive 24' x 28' composite deck—perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A picturesque creek meanders through the tree rows behind the home, enhancing the natural beauty, privacy, and peaceful country atmosphere. Inside, you’ll find modern lighting, updated bathrooms, mostly luxury vinyl plank flooring, and abundant cabinetry and storage throughout. The main floor offers a formal living room, study, or library with privacy doors; a spacious family room with a wood-burning fireplace and walk-up wet bar; a formal dining room; and a separate dining area adjoining the kitchen. The updated kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, an island, and a built-in desk. Two staircases provide convenient access to the second floor, where five generously sized bedrooms and the laundry area are located. The oversized fifth bedroom above the garage would also make an excellent home office, recreation room, or flex space. Additional features include all-electric utilities, private water, a water softener, reverse-osmosis system, electric pet fence, and a partial basement that serves as the utility room. With its peaceful setting, extensive updates, versatile floor plan, remarkable amount of storage, and the scenic creek bordering the rear tree line, this one-of-a-kind property offers the perfect blend of country privacy and small-town convenience.
