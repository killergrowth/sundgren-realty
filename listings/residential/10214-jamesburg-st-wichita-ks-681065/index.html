Property Description

Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated, this home offers comfortable living spaces, desirable features, and a prime west-side location just minutes from New Market Square! The seller has replaced all but two windows with highly rated, insulated Pella windows featuring convenient between-the-glass blinds for easy cleaning. A newer roof and gutters add peace of mind, while the siding was updated in 2014. The 5-year-old composite deck offers low-maintenance outdoor enjoyment for years to come. Inside, vaulted ceilings create an open, airy feel, while a beautiful staircase and wide hallway provide easy access to the bedrooms. The updated primary bathroom includes a convenient laundry chute, a feature the seller says will be hard to leave behind! A formal living room with large front-facing windows offers a welcoming space filled with natural light. Outdoors, enjoy the spacious composite deck surrounded by mature trees that provide plenty of shade, making it the perfect spot for morning coffee, weekend gatherings, or relaxing evenings. The location is hard to beat! New Market Square is just minutes away, with locally owned restaurants, boutiques, clothing stores, major retailers, grocery shopping, banks, and more. With thoughtful updates, inviting living spaces, and a convenient west-side location, this home is ready to welcome its next owner!
