Property Description

Welcome to 2305 N Squirrel Run, where space, quality and flexibility come together on a beautiful corner lot of more than half an acre. With 5 bedrooms and more than 4,600 square feet, this distinctive home offers room to live, work, gather and entertain without sacrificing the convenience of a well-planned layout. A circle drive provides easy front-door access and leads into a main level filled with generous living spaces. The spacious living room features a cozy fireplace, while the expansive family room offers even more room to relax or host a crowd. A formal dining room, huge kitchen and adjoining breakfast room provide multiple places for everyday meals, holidays and entertaining. With easy access to a back deck or the screened in porch! The impressive main-level primary suite features an oversized bedroom, walk-in shower and abundant closet and storage space. Main-level laundry and a convenient safe room add functionality, while upstairs you'll find four additional generously sized bedrooms, a versatile loft or dedicated office space. And the fourth bedroom has an ensuite bath and three trundle beds that make for a wonderful grandkid bedroom, craft room/bonus bedroom or sleepover room! Quality details can be found throughout the home, including Pella windows and doors, solid wood doors, beautiful hardwood floors in many rooms and abundant natural light. An oversized 2-car garage provides additional space for vehicles and storage. Step outside to enjoy the screened-in patio and expansive yard with plenty of room to enjoy the property. The location offers another bonus, with Arkansas City High School, tennis courts, the football field and baseball diamonds nearby. From its historic downtown and Wilson Park to Cowley College and the community's distinctive local character, Arkansas City offers plenty to explore beyond the neighborhood. If you've been searching for a home with truly exceptional space and a floor plan that can adapt to the way you live, 2305 N Squirrel Run deserves a closer look.
