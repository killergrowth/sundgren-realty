Property Description

Best acreage in the neighborhood is the reason why this one owner-home was built here! If you are looking for space, this well maintianed parcel is it. Just under 5 acres and nestled at the end of the road is this 1600 square foot gem. From the exterior, there is an "office" with an exterior door, further in is the mudroom (main floor laundry) and half bath. Travel on to the huge kitchen with a ton of storage and natural light - appliances included. Watch the massive backyard from the sliding door to the covered patio. Perfect gathering and entertaing space with the adjacent family room. Guests coming over, have them enter through the front formal living with built in. Formal diining room would not take much to make it into a 3rd main floor bedroom (what it used to be). Bedrooms have plenty of space, wonderful views, and complete the main floor. Basement is the ultimate fun space while not outside! Huge family game room, full bath, two bonus rooms and plentiful storage. Let the property speak for itself! No HOA and the house sits with a great location for spacing all the way around. Both the pole barn (approx 34x34) and shed (approx. 24x14) have some concrete floor. Check with local authorities for keeping horses and buiding shops.
