Property Description

A RARE FIND IN GODDARD! 5 bedrooms, an open and inviting floor plan, beautifully updated interior and exterior, and an exceptional location, with NO HOA and NO SPECIALS! This move-in-ready ranch offers the fresh feel of a newer home in an established neighborhood within the Goddard School District. Enjoy walking-distance access to all grade levels of Goddard schools, Linear Park, Prairie Sunset Trail, the city pool, and downtown’s Main Street & community events. With no neighbors directly across the street, the setting adds to this home’s standout appeal. Inside, original wood cabinetry pairs beautifully with luxury vinyl flooring, a fresh color palette, updated lighting and natural light that brightens the open- concept kitchen, dining and living room space, creating a comfortable inviting blend of character and style for everyday living and entertaining. The wood burning brick fireplace adds even more warmth and charm, while main-floor laundry offers everyday convenience. With TWO bedrooms upstairs and THREE downstairs, plus two full bathrooms and a spacious basement family room, bonus flex area and additional storage, there’s room for all your needs. The backyard is ready to enjoy, with an expansive concrete patio spanning the side and rear of the home and a fully fenced yard—perfect for gathering, grilling, playing, or relaxing. A shed with electricity provides space for storage, hobbies, or projects. The newly expanded driveway adds convenient parking, including room alongside the home for an RV or boat. Experience Goddard living at its best! This turnkey home combines modern functional living space, outstanding outdoor space and walkable access to so much of what makes Goddard special—this is a combination that’s hard to find!
