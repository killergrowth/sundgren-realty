Property Description

Charming, character-filled, and anything but cookie-cutter! This super cute 4- bedroom, 3-bath home in the heart of Goddard has the perfect mix of charm, updates, and functionality. With a great 2-story layout, all four bedrooms are upstairs—along with the upstairs laundry (hello, convenience!). The kitchen was beautifully updated in 2025, the main water line was replaced in 2026, and a reverse osmosis system was added in 2024. Outside, the curb appeal is hard to miss, with a welcoming front porch that is perfect for morning coffee or relaxing evenings. And the location? So convenient! You’re just minutes from Goddard’s parks, pools, schools, shopping, and everyday essentials. Enjoy the small-town feel of Goddard while being part of a growing community with plenty happening around you. No HOA, tons of charm, and a floor plan that just makes sense—this is one you’ll want to see!
