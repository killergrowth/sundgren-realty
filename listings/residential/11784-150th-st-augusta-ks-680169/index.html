Property Description

Country Living with Upgrades on 3.5 Acres! Enjoy space, freedom, and practical updates in this 4 bed/3 bath home set on 3.5 beautiful acres right off a paved blacktop road. Inside enjoy a modern interior palette accented by recently updated luxury vinyl plank flooring through the main living room, primary suite, and an additional main level bedroom. The bright kitchen comes equipped with all appliances, an eating bar, and direct access to the back lawn through glass doors in the dining area. Downstairs, the fully finished basement with new carpet expands your living space with additional room for a home theater, rec space, or home office, plus plenty of dedicated storage. Outdoors, the acreage is fully equipped for homesteading, hobby farming, or animal care. Amenities include a fully fenced pen enclosure featuring a brand-new run-in livestock shed, a 1-year-old custom chicken coop, raised garden beds, and a 2-car detached garage for parking, shop projects, or equipment storage. Located within quick driving distance to both Augusta and Andover conveniences while retaining the privacy and quiet of rural Butler County living.
