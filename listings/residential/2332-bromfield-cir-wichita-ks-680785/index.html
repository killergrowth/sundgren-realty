Full of character, this home features a tall, covered entry and an attached, side-load two-car garage. The fenced backyard offers a spacious patio, perfect for relaxing or entertaining outdoors. Step inside to discover a spacious three-bedroom, three-bathroom home with a functional and inviting layout. The formal dining room is conveniently located just off the entry, while straight ahead, the large living room impresses with vaulted ceilings, a wood-burning fireplace, and French doors leading to the backyard. The kitchen and dining combination opens to both the formal dining room and the living room, creating a comfortable flow for everyday living and entertaining. A large window overlooks the front yard, and the kitchen includes an oven and dishwasher. The primary bedroom features a private en-suite bathroom with a walk-in shower, along with a walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level. The hall bathroom offers double sinks and a tub/shower combination. The full, partially finished basement provides even more space to spread out, featuring a large family or recreation room, a full bathroom, and additional rooms ideal for storage, hobbies, or potential future finished living space. With its distinctive mid-century character, spacious living areas, and flexible basement space, this home offers a wonderful opportunity to make it your own in a sought-after northeast location!
