Property Description

Willowbend living at its finest-where luxury, lifestyle, and location come together. Tucked into one of Wichita's most sought-after neighborhoods, this impressive 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath residence delivers the space, style, and setting you've ben waiting for. From the moment you arrive, the home feels thoughtfully designed for the way you actually live. A dedicated office just off the foyer makes working from home effortless, while the formal dining room sets the stage for everything from holiday dinners to relaxed entertaining. The main living area and kitchen flow beautifully together, creating an inviting backdrop for everyday life and memorable gatherings alike. The updated kitchen features a huge granite island with eating bar, stainless steel appliances, desk, walk-in pantry, range hood and plenty of workspace. Topping of the main floor is a guest powder bath and a spacious laundry room. The primary suite is the private retreat, offering a comfortable escape at the end of the day. It offers a spacious bedroom with an updated private bath with stand-alone tub, walk-in tile shower and a walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms provide incredible flexibility for whatever your lifestyle demands. The basement provides an additional living space with rec room, 5th bedroom, 3rd full bath & storage. And then there's the backyard-your own private getaway!! A sparkling in-ground pool with fountains (new liner 2022) takes center stage, surrounded by an expansive patio, firepit and deck ready for summer entertaining, quiet mornings, and long evenings under the stars. Add in thoughtful updates, new upper level AC, generous storage, an exceptional location, and easy access to golf, shopping, dining, major roadways, this home checks all the boxes.
