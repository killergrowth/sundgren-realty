Property Description

This wonderfully designed home by All Tek Builders is under construction and still a work in progress as the finishing touches are being completed. This beauty offers 5 bedrooms, 3 full baths, a spacious open living room with modern fireplace, floating shelves and lots of brilliant light from the large windows that spills into the dining area. The kitchen is adorned with a large island, beautiful iridescent decorative tile, stainless steel appliances and a large pantry. The main level finish is complete with a separate laundry space off the garage, a main floor bath with tile shower, a guest room with ceiling fan and hinged outward swinging closet doors. The primary bedroom has coffered ceilings with recessed lighting, ceiling fan, large sliding window, a beautiful bath with double vanity, gorgeous tiled shower with added rain-head, glass shower door and walk-in closet. Downstairs you'll appreciate the spacious family room featuring a wet bar, three bedrooms, a full bath, game closet, under stair storage and utility room. You'll want to relax or enjoy entertaining on the large covered deck that overlooks your backyard space. As a bonus, this home is located on a quiet cul-de-sac street.
