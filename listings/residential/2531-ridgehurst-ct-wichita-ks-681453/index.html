Property Description

Welcome to this beautiful 5 bedroom, 3 bathroom home in the desirable Andover School District with stunning views of rolling hills and open farmland! You enter in to a foyer and spacious main living area that features large windows that showcase the picturesque views and an open concept layout connecting the living room, kitchen, and dining area. The kitchen is beautifully appointed with granite countertops, brass hardware, real wood cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a walk-in pantry. Gorgeous hardwood floors flow throughout the kitchen and dining area. The desirable split bedroom floor plan offers privacy, while the spacious primary suite features stylish finishes, updated lighting, and an en-suite bathroom with granite countertops, separate vanities, tile flooring, a garden tub, and a walk-in closet. Downstairs, you'll find an expansive family room complete with a cozy fireplace and wet bar, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and abundant storage space. Step outside to enjoy the private, fully fenced backyard with no rear neighbors and unobstructed views of the surrounding countryside. The oversized patio is the perfect place to entertain or unwind while watching breathtaking Kansas sunsets over the farmland. Gas hookups are already in place for a future outdoor kitchen! To top it all off, the neighborhood offers fantastic amenities including a swimming pool, pond, walking paths, and playground. With this home's location within Wichita city limits, you will enjoy lower property taxes while getting the benefit of Andover School District! This home offers the perfect combination of space, comfort, beautiful views, and neighborhood amenities - schedule your showing today!
