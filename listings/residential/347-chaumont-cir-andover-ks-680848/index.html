Property Description

A RARE FIND! Welcome to 347 N. Chaumont, an extraordinary custom-built home by Bill Blair that combines exceptional craftsmanship, timeless French Country charm, and the warm, inviting finishes of Magnolia-inspired design. Situated on an incredible .98-acre lot with NO SPECIAL TAXES, this property offers a rare combination of newer construction, luxury finishes, generous outdoor space, and neighborhood amenities! Only six years old, this beautiful home showcases incredible attention to detail and quality construction throughout. The thoughtfully designed split-bedroom floor plan features three spacious bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms on the main level. The open-concept living, dining, and kitchen areas create the perfect setting for both everyday living and entertaining. A large walk-in pantry, spacious mudroom, and separate main-floor laundry room add exceptional functionality to this already impressive home. The beautifully finished basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring separate recreation and game areas, a gorgeous wet bar, two additional generously sized bedrooms, a full bathroom, and abundant storage space. The outdoor living spaces are truly something special! Enjoy the beautiful covered deck and expansive patio, complete with a custom pergola featuring adjustable overhead louvers and shades for added comfort and versatility. The nearly one-acre lot provides incredible space to entertain, play, and relax, including a dedicated playground area and beautiful native grasses along the back of the property. An irrigation well and sprinkler system are additional bonuses! Located in a wonderful neighborhood with access to a community pool and playground, this property truly offers the best of both worlds. Custom construction, exceptional finishes, nearly an acre of land, and NO SPECIAL TAXES—all in a home that’s only six years old! Opportunities like this are few and far between. Don’t miss your chance to own this one-of-a-kind property!
