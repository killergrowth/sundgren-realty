Property Description

*1% Lender credit incentive!* This move-in-ready 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home in the heart of Derby offers over 2,300 square feet of living space with updated bathrooms, beautiful hardwood floors and a fully fenced backyard, all on a large corner lot in a quiet cul-de-sac. Unlike many split-level homes, the layout is welcoming without the typical staircase right inside the front door. Upstairs you’ll find three spacious bedrooms and an updated bathroom, with the option to convert one bedroom into a large walk-in closet or dressing room for the perfect primary suite. The lower level features a spacious family room with a charming brick gas-start fireplace, a remodeled full bathroom with a beautiful tiled shower and plenty of room to spread out. Outside, enjoy the huge backyard with a covered patio, mature trees that provide plenty of shade, a separate storage shed and room to relax, entertain or play. You’ll also love being just minutes from three beautiful parks, making it easy to enjoy everything Derby has to offer.
