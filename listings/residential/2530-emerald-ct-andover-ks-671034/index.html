Property Description

Don’t miss out on the opportunity to own one of the last NEW homes in Cornerstone. This Autumn floorplan by Prairie Construction is located on a quiet cul-de-sac on a nice tree line lot. 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 3174 sqft. Open main living area. Master suite has an oversized tiled shower, dual sinks, and a grand walk-in closet with chest of drawers. Office space/library off master bedroom. 2 additional bedrooms and full bath on other end of living area. Lots of upgrades and built-ins in this home that really make the finished product spectacular. Upgraded 93% efficient furnace. Lower patio area is just steps down from the raised concrete deck. Perfect backyard entertaining space with a fully fenced yard. Daylight basement with 9ft basement walls really open up the basement area. Downstairs there are 2 bedrooms, a bathroom, a rec room with wet bar and a storage room. 3-car garage with 3rd bay extra deep to accommodate a truck. There is a 4th garage door on rear of house that opens onto a second patio area. Well, irrigation & landscaping included. Comes with 1-year builder warranty. Great location near the award-winning Robert Martin Elementary School in Andover School District. 3 community swimming pools, nice park with basketball court and pickleball court. HOA dues are only $35/month and specials are only $190/month.
