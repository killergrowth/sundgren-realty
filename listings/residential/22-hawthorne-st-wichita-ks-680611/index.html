From the front door, go through the sunroom to the living room with warm hardwood floors and a beautiful brick fireplace with an electric insert. You'll continue into the open dining room and hall, past 1 full bath and 2 bedrooms, featuring new carpet; one with hardwood floors that match the hall, and the second with parquet wood-style flooring. The primary bedroom with new carpet and ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet completes this wing of the home.
