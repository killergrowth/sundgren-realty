Property Description

Updated and move-in ready in the Southeast of Saline school district! This home features over 3,000 sqft of living space, an open-concept floor plan with luxury vinyl plank flooring throughout the main floor and fresh paint throughout. The kitchen offers a tile backsplash and some newer appliances, while the main-floor laundry provides plenty of storage. Three bedrooms and two bathrooms are located on the main floor, including a primary suite with a walk-in shower. The basement offers a spacious rec room, a fourth bonus room, and ample storage. Outside, enjoy a covered patio, fenced backyard, sprinkler system, and an extra parking pad. There’s also backyard space for a future building!
