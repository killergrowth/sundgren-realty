NEW CONSTRUCTION. Welcome home to this beautifully designed new construction ranch by Superior Homes, located in the sought-after Skyview at Block 49 addition in Bel Aire! Offering 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and over 2,300 square feet of finished living space, this home combines quality craftsmanship with modern style. The open-concept main level features a spacious living room with a cozy electric fireplace and vaulted ceiling, flowing seamlessly into the kitchen complete with granite countertops, a large island, walk-in pantry, range hood, and stainless steel appliances.
