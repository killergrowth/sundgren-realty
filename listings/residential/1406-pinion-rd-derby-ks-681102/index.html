Property Description

Welcome to 1406 E Pinion Rd, where everyday living meets resort-inspired relaxation. This inviting 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home offers an exceptional combination of comfort, entertainment, and thoughtful updates. Step outside to your own backyard getaway, complete with an in-ground vinyl-lined saltwater swimming pool, hot tub, and fenced yard - perfect for summer gatherings, weekend relaxation, or simply unwinding after a busy day. And wait until you see the closet space in the upstairs bedroom! There’s plenty of room for your entire wardrobe… and maybe even a little more shopping! Inside, you'll discover beautifully updated bathrooms, a dedicated sauna room, and a versatile bonus room that doubles as a home theater, creating the perfect setting for movie nights, entertaining, or your favorite hobbies. Additional highlights include a new HVAC unit, Class 4 roof, a new salt cell for the pool, and an EV charging receptacle in the garage. Situated in the sought-after Tanglewood neighborhood, this home offers convenient access to Derby schools, parks, shopping, and dining.
