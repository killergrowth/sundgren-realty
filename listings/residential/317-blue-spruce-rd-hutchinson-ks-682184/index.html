Property Description

Enjoy the best of country living in a peaceful neighborhood just north of Hutchinson! This inviting home offers a wonderful blend of comfort, convenience, and outdoor enjoyment, with numerous updates already completed for added peace of mind. The home features several significant improvements, including a new high efficiency HVAC system, kitchen appliances, and updated basement electrical outlets and panel, all completed in 2022. The kidney-shaped swimming pool was also upgraded to a saltwater system in 2022, and offers the perfect setting for relaxing and entertaining. Additional improvements include a new garden shed in 2023, along with a dedicated exterior electrical box and outlet for food truck, RV, or other special use, and an upgraded whole house well pump in 2024. New LED lighting in the garage adds another practical touch. With its desirable country-neighborhood setting, refreshing pool, and extensive list of updates, this property offers a great opportunity to enjoy a little extra space and a relaxed lifestyle just minutes from Hutchinson!
