About This Property

Welcome home to this charming and well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offering 1,441 square feet of comfortable living space on a spacious corner lot! With mature shade trees, alley access, a 2-car carport, and recent updates already completed, this home is ready for its next owners. The roof was replaced in the spring of 2024, and the low-maintenance vinyl siding, new front porch, and rear deck add to the home's curb appeal and outdoor enjoyment. Inside, you'll find a bright, inviting floor plan featuring a formal dining room and a versatile main-floor office with built-in bookshelves—perfect for working from home, a nursery, hobby room, or reading space. The kitchen offers easy access to a spacious hidden laundry room, while the main-floor primary bedroom provides convenient everyday living. Original hardwood floors add warmth and character throughout portions of the home. Upstairs are two generously sized bedrooms and a full bath. One bedroom features a skylight, allowing you to enjoy beautiful natural light during the day and a view of the stars at night. A dedicated mini-split heating and cooling system keeps the upstairs comfortable year-round. Outside, you'll appreciate the large corner lot with mature shade trees, alley access, plenty of yard space to enjoy, a rear deck for relaxing or entertaining, and a small cellar that provides additional storage. If you're looking for a move-in ready home with character, flexible living space, and valuable updates already completed, this one deserves a look.
