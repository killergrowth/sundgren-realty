Property Description

Quad-level home in Derby on a mature, tree-filled lot with 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and a 2-car garage. The open main level combines living and dining space with a kitchen featuring painted cabinetry, a butcher block peninsula, tile backsplash, gas cooktop, built-in wall ovens and stainless appliances. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has hardwood floors, a walk-in closet and a full bath with double vanity and tub/shower, plus a second bedroom with its own walk-in closet. The lower-level family room offers a wood-burning fireplace, wood-look tile floors and exterior access, with a third bedroom and 3/4 bath nearby. Outside, enjoy a concrete patio, a fenced backyard and a cabin-style storage shed with a porch. The roof is about 12 years old. Schedule your showing today!
