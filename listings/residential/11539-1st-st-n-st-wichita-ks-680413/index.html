Online bidding ends Friday, November 13th at 11:00 AM. 11539 W 1st St N brings something special to Wichita's established Westlink neighborhood: architectural character, soaring vaulted ceilings, and a three-level layout that gives everyone a little room to call their own. This 3 bedroom, 3 bathroom home sits on a 0.24 acre lot with mature trees, an attached 2 car garage, and solar panels with the existing loan to be paid in full by the seller at closing! Inside, a spacious entryway provides a convenient drop zone for coats, shoes, and everyday essentials before opening into a living room where the vaulted ceilings immediately steal the show. Sliding doors off the dining room lead to a charming front courtyard, while the living room opens to a covered sunroom overlooking the backyard. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite overlooks the vaulted living area and includes a walk-in closet, generous vanity, and private bathroom. Just across the hall, a light-filled bonus room offers a wonderful spot for reading, crafting, hobbies, or a home office. Downstairs in the basement, a second large living room with a fireplace and wet bar creates a completely different place to gather, whether it's movie nights, watching the game, or hosting friends. A third bedroom and full bathroom complete the lower level, offering additional privacy for guests or family. Outside, the mature trees and established yard add to the charm of this unique property. Located in west Wichita near shopping, dining, parks, and everyday conveniences, Westlink offers the appeal of an established neighborhood without sacrificing accessibility.
