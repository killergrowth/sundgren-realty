Property Description

INCREDIBLE OPPORTUNITY—A DREAM PROPERTY FOR COUNTRY LIVING & HOBBIES! Discover this beautifully remodeled ranch boasting over 2,000 sqft on the main level, plus a fully finished basement, perfectly situated on expansive land. The impressive 60' x 50' shop features a cement floor, electricity, and a separate driveway—ideal for any enthusiast or entrepreneur. Enjoy a serene stocked pond, fenced yard, dedicated shooting berm, and the peace of an all-electric home. Two wells provide flexibility: one for drinking water, the other for lawn irrigation, plus a lagoon for added convenience. Inside, the gourmet kitchen shines with granite countertops, High End Black Stainless Appliances and new luxury vinyl plank flooring flows throughout most of the home. Choose your own new roof color—installation is scheduled soon! Versatile layout was converted from 5 bedrooms plus an office to 3 bedrooms plus an office, creating spacious living areas. Host in the elegant 16' formal dining room or relax in the expansive 21' main floor family/hearth room. Seller is a licensed real estate agent in Kansas—schedule your showing today and experience this extraordinary property!
