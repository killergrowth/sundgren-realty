Property Description

Enjoy the peace and privacy of country living while remaining conveniently close to Wichita and surrounding communities. Situated on nearly 10 acres, this property offers plenty of space for livestock, equipment, hobbies, rural living. The home features a spacious family room with gas fireplace and patio doors opening to a deck and concrete patio-perfect for relaxing or entertaining while enjoying the outdoors. Kitchen has been updated and offers an eating bar and separate eat-in dining area. A large formal living room and formal dining room provide additional space for gatherings and special occasions.
