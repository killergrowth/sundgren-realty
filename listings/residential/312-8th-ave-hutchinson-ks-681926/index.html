Property Description

This one-story home welcomes you with a covered front porch, the perfect spot for your morning coffee, and a brick walkway leading up to the door. Inside are 2 bedrooms, 1 bath, and 860 square feet, with vinyl flooring in the main living areas and carpet in both bedrooms for a cozy feel. The living room has ceiling fans and plenty of natural light, making it an inviting place to relax at the end of the day. The kitchen and dining area share one open space, so it’s easy to cook and visit at the same time. A separate office gives you room to work from home or pursue a hobby, the laundry room has its own space with 220 electric, and there’s an unfinished basement. Outside, there’s a storage building and wood fencing, plus plenty of yard to enjoy. This one has a lot to offer, so schedule your showing today!
