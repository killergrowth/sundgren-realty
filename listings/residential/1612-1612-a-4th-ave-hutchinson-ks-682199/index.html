Property Description

Looking to grow your rental portfolio quickly, with every property in one place? Here is your rare chance to buy a complete rental portfolio at one address! 1612 W 4th Street in Hutchinson is a compound of ten rental houses, plus a large all-concrete round-top building that’s currently leased. With current gross rents over $7,000 per month, investors can add ten doors and an extra income stream in one deal instead of buying properties one at a time. The units range from studios to two-story family homes, so they fit many kinds of tenants. The main house at 1612 and Unit G are both two-story, 3-bedroom, 2-bath homes. Unit H is also a 3-bedroom house with 1 bath. Units A, B and F are 2-bedroom, 1-bath houses. Units C and K are 1-bedroom, 1-bath, and Units D and E are studio units with 1-bath. The owner reports strong rental demand, with vacancies filling quickly when they come open. The current owner has kept up with the property and made many recent updates throughout. An established property manager who knows the units and tenants can stay on if the buyer would like, which makes the transition easy for an investor who wants to stay hands-off. The round-top building is currently leased for storage, adding income beyond the ten residential units. The 9 houses in back are all electric units and tenants only pay electricity. Owner / landlord takes care of trash and water for those 9 houses. Tenant pays all utilities on the main house up front at 1612. This is an established, income-producing property with a solid tenant base and proven demand. It fits an investor looking to grow a portfolio in one transaction or a buyer who wants a turnkey multi-unit property with management already in place. Don’t miss this opportunity to add a ready-made rental portfolio in one move! Buyers and agents to verify all information pertinent to buyer.
