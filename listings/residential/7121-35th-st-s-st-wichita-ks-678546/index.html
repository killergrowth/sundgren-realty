Property Description

Welcome to this meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home nestled on a beautiful half-acre lot in a mature neighborhood on the outskirts of town, offering the perfect blend of neighborhood living and a peaceful rural feel. The beautifully landscaped lawn is kept lush with the convenience of an irrigation well and sprinkler system. Inside, you’ll find a spacious and inviting layout with a large kitchen featuring granite countertops and plenty of room for cooking, gathering, and entertaining. The living room and dining room showcase beautiful hardwood floors, creating a warm and timeless feel throughout the main living spaces. A convenient central vacuum system adds to the comfort and ease of maintaining this spacious home. The finished basement provides an abundance of additional living space, including a huge family room with a fireplace, a spacious bedroom, full bathroom, and two bonus rooms—perfect for guests, a home office, hobby space, or whatever your needs may be. Main floor AND basement laundry hook-uos, you choose. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and anyone needing extra storage will appreciate the impressive garage setup, featuring a 2-car attached garage plus a 2-car detached garage with a separate workshop in the back with A/C. Outside, the expansive half-acre lot provides plenty of room to enjoy the beautifully maintained grounds and peaceful surroundings. There is also room to park your RV and the electrical to plug it in. Paved frontage from one side of the driveway and dirt road on the other entrance of the driveway. This is a rare opportunity to enjoy space, character, and a little bit of country living while still being conveniently located near town. Energy Efficient Daikin HVAC was New in 2021 along with many other things in the last few years. Listing agent and seller are related.
