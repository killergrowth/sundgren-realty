Property Description

Nestled in the heart of Stonegate Estates on a wooded corner lot is this 3 bedroom, 3 bath spacious family home. The property features a living room with wood burning fireplace, a large rec room with wet bar, a family room and a sun room with access to the large deck. The kitchen features granite counters, all major appliances remain as well as a trash compactor. The wood laminate flooring has been updated and there is even a pot filler faucet over the range. The Master bedroom has twin walk in closets and a master bath. The master bath has a full quartz jet tub, twin sinks and a separate shower. The property has been well cared for and is move in ready, however some would choose to do some basic interior decor updating. The roof is less than a year old and is a class IV hail resistant roof. There is a well water fed sprinkler system to keep everything green and a spacious garage with a new opener for all of your toys.
