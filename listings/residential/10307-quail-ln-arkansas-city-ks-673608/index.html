Property Description

Just outside city limits—where you get all the freedom of country living without giving up convenience—this custom-built beauty sits on 1.6 acres and delivers the perfect blend of peace, space, and practicality. Whether you want room for animals, room to breathe, or room to spread out and enjoy life without neighbors hovering, this property hits the mark the second you pull in. And let’s talk about the showstopper: the 80×40×16 insulated shop. This isn’t just a shop—it’s a lifestyle upgrade. With a 6-inch concrete floor, four overhead doors that include two 14-ft drive-through bays (yes, bring the RV, the boat, the hauler… even the semi), plus 10-ft and 8-ft doors, this space is built for big toys, big projects, and big possibility. A covered patio right off the shop sets you up for easy evenings with friends after a day of tinkering or adventuring. Then there's the home—custom built in 2008 and designed for comfortable, everyday living with nearly 2,800 sq ft of well-planned space. The open kitchen, dining, and living area makes the heart of the home feel warm and inviting, perfect for hosting or keeping everyone connected. On the main level you’ll find a split master floor plan that gives privacy where it counts. The spacious primary suite features a true walk-in closet, double sinks, a separate tub and shower, and plenty of room to unwind. Two additional main-level bedrooms and a full bath sit on the opposite side, keeping things quiet and functional for the whole household. Step out either of the sliding doors to enjoy the covered patio or the wide-open backyard—your own private 1.6-acre retreat ideal for pets, play, and peaceful nights under the Kansas sky. Head downstairs and the finished basement opens even more possibilities with a large family room, bonus flex area, a fourth bedroom with private bath access, laundry/utility room, and an unfinished wet bar/kitchenette area ready for your custom finishing touches. If you want to lock in even more privacy, there is an optional additional 2.4-acre parcel across the street (listed separately) round out this one-of-a-kind package. If you’ve been craving space, freedom, and a property that finally checks every box—this is the one. Come see why life feels better out here.
