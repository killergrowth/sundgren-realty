Property Description

Come See this stunning new-construction home by Cresta Heritage Homes that perfectly balances modern elegance with everyday comfort. Built on a spacious 0.40-acre lot in the peaceful Bellechase subdivision, this Modern Prairie-style ranch welcomes you with soaring 12-foot ceilings and a bright, open-concept layout designed for gatherings and entertaining. The gourmet kitchen features an oversized island, granite countertops, and a walk-in pantry, while the living area is anchored by a cozy gas fireplace. Retreat to the primary suite to enjoy a fully tiled spa-like shower, or step outside to your choice of a covered front porch or a composite back deck to take in the quiet surroundings.
