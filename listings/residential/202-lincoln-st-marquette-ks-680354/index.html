Property Description

Full of charm, character, and thoughtful updates, this adorable Marquette bungalow is a true gem! Located on a corner lot just one block from downtown, this home beautifully blends original woodwork and timeless character with modern improvements. The main floor offers a living room, dining room, cozy den with gas fireplace, and a main-floor primary bedroom with a remodeled bath featuring a walk-in shower. The totally remodeled kitchen and updated laundry room add convenience and style, while the enclosed screened-in back porch provides the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the outdoors. Upstairs, you’ll find three additional bedrooms and a beautifully remodeled full bath. Step outside to enjoy the beautifully landscaped yard with sprinkler system, concrete patio, and inviting front porch complete with a porch swing—perfect for quiet mornings, evening breezes, or curling up with a good book. Additional updates include a new roof and new electrical panels. The impressive four-car garage with alley access and workshop space offers room for vehicles, hobbies, projects, and extra storage. With its blend of historic charm, modern updates, inviting outdoor spaces, and an unbeatable location close to all that downtown Marquette has to offer, this special home is ready to welcome its next owners!
