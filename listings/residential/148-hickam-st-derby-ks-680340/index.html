Property Description

Brand new Clover plan in Foxfire, and it’s all on one level. Google maps hasn't caught up with us yet, but we're on 55th St south between Woodlawn and Oliver - just turn in on Forest Park and you'll find us! At 1,270 sq ft, this home has 3 bedrooms and 2 baths with no stairs to climb, so everyday living is easy. The finishes make a great first impression, with luxury vinyl flooring and wide trim in the main living areas for a custom look, and quartz countertops that bring a clean, high-end feel to the kitchen and baths. Out back, the covered patio is the perfect spot for morning coffee, evening grilling, or just kicking back when the weather’s nice. The 3 car garage has plenty of room for the vehicles, the lawn equipment, and all the stuff that tends to pile up. A metal safe room can be added in the garage, and landscape items can be added depending on time of purchase (seasonal availablity applies). Foxfire has additional spec homes in various stages of construction, along with custom homesites still available. Come out and visit the model Saturday through Thursday from 1 - 5 pm (closed Fridays) to explore all that this incredible community has to offer!
