Property Description

Enjoy low-maintenance living in the heart of College Hill! This upstairs- 1 bedroom, 1-bathroom condo offers 814 sq ft of comfortable, light-filled space in a quiet, 55+ community just 5 minutes from downtown. The comlex has a beautifully landscaped courtyard and heated pool—perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Inside, the spacious layout includes a full bathroom, a large living area, comfortable bedroom, and dining area next to the ample kitchen. Leave the car behind and walk to nearby Larchers, Belmont, Vora's, Frosty's, Andy's and Clifton Square, where you’ll find shops, restaurants, and neighborhood charm. With an unbeatable location, peaceful setting, and vibrant community, this condo has everything you need to feel right at home. Monthly HOA fees ($352) include insurance for the building structure and exterior, maintenance of common areas, lawn, furnaces and hot water tanks, trash, water, AC electric bill for common areas (shared/communitycondenser), pool maintenance, snow removal, free laundry, clubhouse use, locker storage space in basement, and 1 assigned space in covered carport. Home is all electric. Purchase contract and application must be submitted to HOA for sale approval. Per HOA this is a non smoking condo, limit of 1 pet- 25 lb limit, and 55+ community.
