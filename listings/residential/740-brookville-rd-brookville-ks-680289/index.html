Property Description

Discover the appeal of country living with this spacious ranch-style home situated on 8.74 acres of beautiful countryside, approximately 15 miles from Salina, Kansas. Enjoy the peace and privacy of a rural setting while benefiting from convenient access via major highways and all-blacktop roads. Built in 2009, this impressive home features a walkout basement, a 4-plus-car garage, and a shop, offering plenty of room for vehicles, hobbies, equipment, and storage. The main level offers 2,100 square feet of living space, with a thoughtfully designed layout featuring three generously sized bedrooms, three bathrooms, a formal dining room, a welcoming living room with fireplace, a well-appointed kitchen, and the convenience of main-floor laundry. The partially finished walkout basement provides abundant additional storage and exciting possibilities for future expansion, allowing you to customize the space to fit your needs. Energy efficiency is another attractive feature, with a geothermal heating and cooling system complemented by propane backup. Water is supplied by the Post Rock Rural Water District. With acreage, spacious living, versatile garage and shop facilities, and a desirable country location, this property offers an opportunity to enjoy a lifestyle that combines comfort, functionality, and the beauty of the outdoors. There is so much more to see and experience that only a private tour can fully reveal!
