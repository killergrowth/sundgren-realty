Property Description

Welcome to 1708 N Cleary Ln in Goddard! This 4-bedroom, 3-bath ranch has several major updates already completed or underway, including a new roof being installed with new gutters and downspouts, fresh interior paint, and new carpet in the carpeted areas. The exterior was also painted in 2022, giving buyers a strong list of recent improvements right from the start. Offering approximately 1,808 finished square feet, the home features a full finished basement and an oversized attached 2-car garage. The main level includes a comfortable living room with vaulted ceiling, kitchen and dining area, convenient main-floor laundry, a primary bedroom with private bath, and two additional bedrooms. The finished basement adds even more usable space with a large family room, fourth bedroom, full bath, bonus room, and additional storage. Outside, enjoy the covered deck and fenced backyard, while the oversized garage provides extra space beyond a typical 2-car setup. The home is also move-in ready with all kitchen appliances staying, along with the washer and dryer. The owned water softener and water purification system will remain with the property as well. Located in the Goddard School District with convenient access to nearby shopping, dining, and west Wichita amenities, this home combines plenty of space with several recent major improvements.
