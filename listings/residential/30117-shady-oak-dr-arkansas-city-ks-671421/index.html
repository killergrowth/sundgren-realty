Property Description

Imagine ending each day surrounded by trees, wildlife, and complete peace—welcome to your own slice of country serenity. Tucked away on 1.5 wooded acres in the highly sought-after Whispering Oaks subdivision just east of Arkansas City, this beautifully updated home blends modern comfort with peaceful, scenic surroundings. From the moment you arrive, the home makes a statement with its striking modern exterior, new siding, and a Class 4 impact-resistant roof with a transferable warranty. Inside, you’ll find a spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bath layout filled with thoughtful updates, including luxury vinyl flooring, fresh interior paint, new vinyl windows, and a completely remodeled kitchen. The kitchen is designed to impress with new cabinetry, granite countertops, a copper farmhouse sink, a large pantry, and an eating bar that flows seamlessly into the dining and family rooms—perfect for gathering and entertaining. At the heart of the home, the inviting family room features vaulted ceilings, an impressive all-stone fireplace, and expansive views of the surrounding landscape. The primary suite offers a private retreat with vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet, and tranquil views of the property. Step outside and experience what truly sets this property apart. The grounds have been transformed into a private oasis with two newly constructed ponds, beautiful landscaping, and peaceful spaces to unwind. Enjoy the sounds of the nearby creek, watch deer roam through the trees, and relax on the spacious back deck as the sun sets. Need extra space? This property delivers with RV parking/storage and a brand-new 30x30 detached shop—ideal for vehicles, hobbies, or projects. Opportunities like this don’t come along often. With its rare combination of natural beauty, modern updates, and secluded setting, this property is truly one of a kind. Schedule your showing today.
