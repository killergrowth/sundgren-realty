Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully updated ranch located in Wheatland Addition in Southwest Wichita, within the Haysville School District! With a new roof, new siding, and newer windows, many of the big-ticket improvements have already been taken care of! Step inside to find fresh new carpet throughout the main-floor living and bedroom areas and an inviting living room featuring a cozy woodburning fireplace. The spacious kitchen offers granite countertops and beautiful hardwood flooring that extends into the dining area. The main floor features two bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a convenient separate laundry room. The primary suite offers a walk-in closet and private bathroom complete with a relaxing jetted tub, separate shower, and dual vanities. Downstairs, the daylight basement is already framed and ready for sheetrock, providing an excellent opportunity to add additional finished living space and customize it to fit your needs! Outside, enjoy the fully fenced backyard featuring a patio and firepit, perfect for relaxing evenings or entertaining friends and family. An attached two-car garage completes this wonderful home. With numerous exterior updates, fresh interior improvements, and the potential to expand your living space, this home offers an incredible opportunity to make it your own.
