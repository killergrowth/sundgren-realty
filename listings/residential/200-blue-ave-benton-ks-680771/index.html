Property Description

Welcome home to this clean, move-in-ready 2bed/2bath/2car half twin home with two additional basement bonus rooms in quiet Benton, KS! Offering an open floor plan and numerous recent improvements, this property is a great opportunity for homeowners and investors alike. Major updates include a brand new roof, new vinyl siding, new garage door, new sliding patio door, and new thermostat. Step inside to find attractive, low-maintenance wood laminate flooring, fresh neutral paint, and updated fixtures, faucets, and lighting throughout the main level. The spacious living room features a striking stone-faced fireplace and beamed ceiling, and flows seamlessly into the open kitchen, where all appliances remain for the buyer's convenience. The primary bedroom offers a private ensuite bath with dual vanities, shower, and convenient access to the main floor laundry. A second full bathroom features a brand new tub and shower surround. Downstairs, you'll find a generously sized family/rec room with new carpeting, along with two spacious bonus rooms with closets, and a large storage/mechanical room. The oversized two-car garage provides even more storage with a large built-in closet. Situated on a corner lot in an established neighborhood, this home offers convenient access to shopping, groceries, and major highways. With so many improvements already completed, this affordable, move-in-ready property is one you won't want to miss. Schedule your private showing today!
