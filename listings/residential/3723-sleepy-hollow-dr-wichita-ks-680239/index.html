Property Description

Charming Sleepy Hollow home with timeless character, thoughtful updates, and exceptional curb appeal! Welcome to 3723 Sleepy Hollow Dr., a lovingly cared-for home in the heart of the highly desirable Sleepy Hollow neighborhood. Homes on this street rarely come available, making this a special opportunity to own a property in such a wonderful location. From the moment you arrive, the inviting front porch and beautiful landscaping create an unmistakable sense of home, with lovely views overlooking Sleepy Hollow Park. Inside, the character and architectural details continue throughout. The formal living room features beautiful hardwood floors, an inviting fireplace, original sconces, and an abundance of natural light from the many windows. Just off the living room, the formal dining room flows seamlessly into the updated kitchen and charming breakfast nook. The kitchen is both beautiful and functional, featuring quartz countertops, quality appliances, a walk-in pantry, and additional built-in cabinetry with glass-front doors. The main level offers an especially desirable floor plan, including a spacious family room/mudroom at the back of the home with direct access to the backyard, a true main-floor bedroom with closet, and a full hall bathroom. Whether used as a guest suite, home office, or bedroom, the main-level bedroom adds wonderful flexibility to the home. Upstairs, the charm continues with spacious bedrooms, built-ins, and excellent storage. The primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms provide plenty of space for family and guests. A second full hall bathroom with shower serves the upper level. Storage is exceptional throughout, with multiple walk-in closets and even a cedar closet conveniently located in the hallway. The basement adds even more versatility, with a spacious laundry area, washer and dryer that remain, and abundant storage space. Newer HVAC and water heater! Outside, the property is just as impressive. Enjoy the fenced backyard, mature landscaping, sprinkler system, and wonderful curb appeal. A double-car garage provides ample parking and storage, with an additional greenhouse/storage area located behind the garage. The home also features a high-end Decrabond roof and hardwood flooring, adding to its long-term appeal and enduring character. All of this is located in a convenient and established neighborhood close to shopping, restaurants, Wesley Hospital, and schools. Timeless charm, thoughtful updates, exceptional storage, and a location that rarely offers an opportunity like this. 3723 Sleepy Hollow Dr is truly a special place to call home.
