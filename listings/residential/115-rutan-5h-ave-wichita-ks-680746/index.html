Property Description

Welcome to the historic Hillcrest, one of Wichita’s most iconic landmarks, offering the charm of College Hill with the ease of maintenance-free living. This newly renovated, move-in ready fifth-floor residence features 1 bedroom, 1 bath, and 744 square feet of beautifully updated living space with abundant windows and sun-soaked living spaces. The efficient floor plan features a spacious entry closet, comfortable living room, eat-in kitchen, and one bedroom with an attached full bath. Refinished original hardwood floors, crisp white paint, and refinished custom maple cabinetry create a clean, timeless look, while the kitchen shines with new granite countertops, backsplash, sink, appliances, and garbage disposal. New luxury vinyl flooring adds a fresh touch to the eat-in kitchen, and the bathroom features refinished original tile flooring and a refinished bathtub. Additional improvements include select new light fixtures, two ceiling fans, and double-pane vinyl windows installed in 2026 with a 20-year warranty. Building updates include a new tankless hot water system and a boiler replaced in 2022, while the air-conditioning condenser for this unit was replaced in 2021. Life at The Hillcrest extends far beyond the walls of your home. Residents enjoy exceptional amenities including a gracious first-floor gathering room with fireplace, a 10th-floor Sky Room with sweeping views, exercise room, laundry facilities, 24-hour security, pest control, and doorman service. The building also offers a guest apartment available for residents to reserve for overnight visitors. Monthly dues of $845 cover exterior maintenance, exterior insurance, utilities, trash service, basic cable, internet, grounds maintenance, and property taxes. Enjoy walkability to College Hill Park, churches, schools, and a vibrant restaurant scene featuring favorites such as Vora Restaurant European, The Belmont, Larcher's Market, The Wine Dive, Dempsey's Burger Pub, College Hill Deli, and Harry's Uptown Bar & Grill. Convenient access to downtown, Wichita State University, and major highways makes it easy to enjoy everythingWichita has to offer. No pets. No smoking. Subject to Hillcrest Board approval.
