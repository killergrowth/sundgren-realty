Property Description

Lake Cabin, full of character! Looking for a lake getaway that is anything but ordinary? This beautiful 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath cabin at Marion County Lake is packed with personality, custom details and that relaxed lake life feeling you have been dreaming about. Step inside and you will immediately notice the soaring vaulted ceilings, beautiful flooring and an abundance of natural light that make this home feel bright, open and welcoming. The primary suite is a showstopper! The spacious primary bath features his and hers dressing vanities with a large ceramic tile walk in shower between them, complete with three shower heads for a truly spa like experience. And wait until you see the kitchen! The custom countertops were handcrafted using a variety of beautiful woods, giving the kitchen a one of a kind look. The island is topped with a gorgeous live edge cedar bar, adding even more warmth and rustic character. You will also love the practical extras, including a separate laundry room and walk in pantry, all with vintage style. The home is on rural water and it has its own well. But here is one of my favorite surprises…just off the kitchen is a cozy little room the owners affectionately call “The Speakeasy.” How fun is that?! And now for the BEST part…IT COMES FULLY FURNISHED! That means less time moving furniture and more time enjoying the lake. Bring your clothes, grab the keys and start making memories! Enjoy a great view of the lake from your easy chair! Whether you are searching for a full time lake home, weekend retreat or your own special escape, this one is truly something you need to see in person. Lake life is calling…and this time, it comes furnished!
