Property Description

You are going to fall head over heals with this adorable farmhouse. You will be delighted to find thoughtful modern designs through that don't take away an ounce of character. From the baseboards to the moulding this home is exactly what you're looking for. As soon as you arrive on the property you'll begin to feel the relaxation the serene setting provides. The front porch is just begging for some comfy chairs and you drinking a glass of tea. Head inside to an inviting living room with a bedroom, office space or play room immediately to your left. Straight back you'll find another living space that is currently being used for living and dining. But the choice is yours on how you lay out these very practical and useable rooms. Take a little step down to the cozy kitchen with granite counter tops to prepare your favorite dishes. Down the hall you'll find the half bath and laundry room combo as well as the pantry, drop zone and screened in back porch. There is a lovely little fenced in back yard space perfect to keep pets or small children contained for saftey. You will also find the full bathroom on the main level right at the base of the stairs. Upstairs you will find three more bedrooms that have a surprising amount of closet space. Back outside you'll find a large outbuilding with 2 garage doors. This would be great for storage or a shop. Part of the acreage is a long strip, think pan handle. It's currently grown up with trees but could be used for hunting, or cleared for riding ATVs or something fun! This is definitely a property you need to see. Book your showing today!
