Property Description

Water Views, a Theater Room Like No Other & a Home That Truly Has It All The kind of home you slow down for. Custom landscaping islands frame the front and sides with color and texture that makes a neighborhood feel like a destination. The exterior was professionally painted with a high-acrylic semi-gloss built to last — and the sprinkler system runs on a private well, tucked neatly into the landscaping so it disappears into the curb appeal. Every edge stays immaculate without costing you a dime in city water. Step inside and something shifts. Natural light draws your eye straight through to a wall of windows showcasing a peaceful, park-like water view that feels more like a painting than a backyard. Bamboo hardwood flooring runs warm underfoot, and the quality of everything — the finishes, the flow, the light — tells you immediately this home has been loved. The kitchen is where you'll want to stay. Re-faced cabinetry frames gorgeous granite countertops, and a full bay of windows keeps the water view front and center. A whole-house water softener and reverse osmosis system mean the tap water is as pure as the setting outside. Every window screen was replaced in 2025 — nothing blurs the view. Step onto the newly rebuilt extra-large covered deck and the world slows down. The primary ensuite is generous, serene, and quietly spectacular. That same water view greets you every morning — soft light, still water, a peaceful start most people only dream about. A brand-new shower panel brings a sleek, spa-like finish. Downstairs, the walkout basement is fully finished and completely thought through. A wet bar anchors the entertaining space, the hot tub under the deck runs on a new pump, and the bathrooms have been upgraded with new shower panels — clean, modern, move-in ready. Two of the three shower panels (primary bathroom and downstairs) are brand new; the third is fully functional and in great working order — just not new. And then there's the theater room — also a fully reinforced safe room, purpose-built for Kansas tornadoes and severe weather. When storms roll in, this is exactly where you want to be. When skies are clear, you'll never want to leave anyway. One wall is painted floor to ceiling with top-of-the-line screen paint that turns every movie night or Chiefs game into an event. All seating and equipment stays. Buyers don't walk into a bonus room — they walk into an experience and the peace of mind that comes with it. The yard is immaculate front to back. Ornamental aluminum fencing frames the water view and gives the property a sense of privacy buyers feel instantly. Swans glide across the pond in the morning, egrets and a great blue heron appear like clockwork, and the ponds hold bass, bluegill, and perch — catch and release, right outside your door. This isn't a water feature. It's an ecosystem. And it comes with the house. Beyond 3,308 sq ft of finished living space, additional unfinished area brings the total footprint to 3,847 — storage that makes you cancel your storage unit.
