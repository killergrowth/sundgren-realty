Property Description

Welcome home to 2441 N. Plumthicket Ct.! 4 bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, open floor plan, main-floor primary suite, finished basement, and backyard retreat with an inground pool! Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, and K-96, offering easy access to dining, retail, and major routes throughout Wichita. This spacious home offers an exceptional combination of comfortable living, entertaining spaces, and thoughtful amenities throughout. Step inside to discover soaring vaulted ceilings, expansive windows that fill the home with natural light, and an inviting open layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. The beautiful kitchen features granite countertops, under-cabinet lighting, a center island, breakfast bar, pantry with convenient pull-out shelving, and a wet bar complete with an ice maker. Both formal and informal dining areas provide plenty of options for hosting gatherings or enjoying everyday meals. The main-floor laundry room offers a folding counter, built-in shelving, utility sink, and convenient access to a half bathroom. The spacious main-floor primary suite is a true retreat, featuring hardwood flooring, built-in speakers, custom cabinetry, and a sliding glass door leading directly to the sunroom. The private primary bathroom offers tile flooring, dual sinks, a bidet, tiled shower with dual showerheads, relaxing soaking tub, linen closet, and a generous walk-in closet with a built-in dresser. Upstairs, you'll find a versatile loft area and an office with extensive built-in cabinetry, offering flexible spaces for working, studying, or hobbies. The fully finished basement expands the living and entertaining possibilities with a large family room featuring built-ins and a wood stove, a dedicated game area, and a wet bar. Two additional bonus rooms provide flexible space for hobbies, exercise, crafts, or other uses. A walk-in cedar closet, abundant storage areas, and a utility sink add even more functionality. Step outside and enjoy your own backyard retreat! The outdoor living space features a patio with a hot tub, an inviting inground swimming pool with an automatic cover, and a fenced dog run. Whether hosting poolside gatherings, relaxing in the hot tub, or enjoying the outdoor space, this backyard offers wonderful opportunities for recreation and entertaining. With multiple living areas, generous storage, a functional floor plan, and impressive indoor and outdoor amenities, this Wichita property has so much to offer.
