Property Description

Welcome to this charming 4-bedroom, 2-bath home offering beautifully finished living space, a spacious backyard, and an attached 2-car garage. Nestled in the established Greenbriar Manor neighborhood, this well-maintained home offers a functional floor plan with multiple living areas and plenty of room to spread out. Step inside to discover a bright and inviting living room featuring beautiful hardwood floors, neutral walls, and abundant natural light. The adjoining kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, generous cabinet storage, a pantry, and a convenient dining area, creating a comfortable space for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, you’ll find three bedrooms with hardwood flooring and ceiling fans, along with a full bathroom featuring an extended vanity and tub/shower combination. The lower level provides an additional spacious family room with hardwood flooring, offering the perfect setting for movie nights, entertaining, or relaxing. A fourth bedroom and second full bathroom provide flexibility for guests, a home office, or additional living arrangements. The basement offers a dedicated laundry area and additional storage space. Outside, enjoy a generously sized, fully fenced backyard with mature trees, a patio for outdoor entertaining, and plenty of green space for gardening, pets, or play. The attached 2-car garage provides convenient parking and additional storage. With its versatile floor plan, hardwood flooring, multiple living spaces, and established neighborhood setting, this home offers a wonderful combination of comfort, functionality, and value. Conveniently located near schools, shopping, dining, and everyday amenities, this home is ready to welcome its next owner!
