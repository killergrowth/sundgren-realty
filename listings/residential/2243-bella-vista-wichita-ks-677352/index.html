Welcome to this inviting 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home located in NW Wichita near the Arkansas River. Featuring an attached 2-car garage, spacious living areas, and a backyard surrounded by mature trees, this home offers comfort and functionality in a convenient location. Step inside through the foyer, where you'll be welcomed into the family room featuring beautiful wood details and built-in cabinetry. Just off the family room is the formal dining room, providing a dedicated space for meals and entertaining. Continue into the kitchen, where you'll find laminate countertops, an eating bar, and plenty of workspace. The refrigerator, microwave, oven/range, and second oven all transfer with the property. Just beyond the kitchen is a dedicated laundry room, with convenient access to the half bathroom. The living room provides another inviting gathering space, highlighted by a wood-burning fireplace. The home offers three bedrooms and two full bathrooms. The spacious primary bedroom includes a sitting room with direct access to the backyard deck, creating a private space to relax or enjoy the outdoors. The primary en-suite bathroom features a vanity desk and tub/shower combination. A second full bathroom also includes a tub/shower combination. Downstairs, the basement expands the living space with a recreation room and dry bar, offering a great area for entertaining, relaxing, or hobbies. The oversized attached 2-car garage provides plenty of room for private parking, storage, or additional workspace. Outside, enjoy the backyard with a patio area, privacy fencing, and mature trees providing shade and greenery. The roof was replaced in 2025, adding a valuable recent improvement to the home. With its spacious layout, unique wood details, multiple living areas, and convenient location near the Arkansas River, this home offers a great opportunity for its next owner.
