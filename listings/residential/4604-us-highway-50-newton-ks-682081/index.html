Property Description

DRONE PHOTOS & PROPERTY OUTLINE COMING SOON. Welcome to Cattail Springs, a beautiful rural retreat situated on over 17 acres of scenic landscape, privacy, and natural beauty! Built in 2001 and tucked away down a peaceful, tree-lined driveway, this unique property features a spacious shop with guest suite, spring-fed stocked ponds, mature trees, sweeping prairie views, and abundant wildlife, including deer, monarch butterflies, and more. Explore the grounds along scenic trails and golf-cart pathways, including a charming cart bridge connecting two ponds. Take a leisurely stroll through the "Enchanted Forest," a landscaped trail winding among the trees, and discover the fruit-bearing arbor and decorative 24-foot windmill that add to the property's picturesque setting. Inside, the expansive layout welcomes you with beautiful oak hardwood floors throughout the main living area and generous windows showcasing stunning views of the property. The open kitchen features oak cabinetry, electric cooking appliances, a pantry, and an inviting eat-in dining area. Nearby is the convenient main-floor laundry with an attached half bath. Spacious living areas offer flexibility for additional dining, everyday comfort, and entertaining. The generous primary bedroom features an en-suite bathroom, walk-in closet, sweeping views, and direct access to the deck, where you can unwind while taking in breathtaking prairie sunsets. Downstairs, the walkout basement offers additional living space, including two versatile bonus rooms, a generously sized bathroom, convenient storage, and patio access overlooking the scenic acreage. Adding exceptional versatility is the impressive 115' x 40' metal building, known as "The Lodge." This expansive structure features an office or guest suite complete with kitchen and bathroom, workshop and storage area, covered parking, and a 16' x 24' covered patio. Whether enjoying the peaceful surroundings, exploring the trails, entertaining guests, or pursuing hobbies, Cattail Springs offers a wonderful combination of comfort, recreation, and functionality. More than a home, it's a place to explore, unwind, gather, and enjoy the beauty of nature right outside your door. Discover the possibilities and experience the beauty of country living! So much to see here, come take a look!
