Property Description

Some properties impress, but this one truly TURNS HEADS! Immaculately maintained, updated, and one of a kind in the Auburn Hills subdivision, you’ll notice the difference the moment you pull up! Inside, you’ll find a fully updated kitchen featuring new smart appliances, quartz countertops, new cabinetry, and a spacious dining area. The main living room is filled with natural light from large windows, creating a warm and inviting space. The primary bedroom offers coffered ceilings, a large walk-in closet with built-in shelving, and private access to the deck where you can start your mornings with coffee! Laundry is on the main floor, located just off the garage and includes cabinet storage, hanging space, and a mudroom area for hanging coats and storing shoes. Downstairs, the home continues to impress with a large family room centered around a fireplace, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and plenty of room to relax or entertain. The walkout basement leads directly to the hot tub, which STAYS with the property. Walk outside, and you’ll love the fully finished three car garage with granite garage flooring. It is clean and functional to use for a man cave, tools, or parking vehicles. A partially covered deck overlooks the Auburn Hills golf course, fairway, and pond, giving you a beautiful view out the backyard. The deck leads down to a stamped concrete patio, amazing landscaping, and a newly installed outdoor kitchen, perfect for entertaining or enjoying your evenings outside. It keeps getting better! A new roof was installed in 2025 with impact resistant shingles. Fresh paint throughout the interior of the home and new luxury flooring on the main level. Auburn Hills community gives you access to four neighborhood pools and two clubhouses. The sellers have loved this home and the prime location near shopping and dining with quick access to 151st and Maple. This is a home that has been loved, maintained, and is pristine at every turn! Welcome home to Auburn Hills living done right! This one won’t last long! You're going to love it!
