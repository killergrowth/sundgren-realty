Property Description

Some homes offer more than square footage - they offer a neighborhood and community you will be excited to call home. Welcome to 134 E Creekstone St, a spacious five-bedroom, three bath home in Mulvane's Country Walk neighborhood. Inside, the remodeled kitchen and living area create an open, inviting heart of the home. The wall between the two spaces was removed for better flow, and the kitchen features an updated island with a matching granite countertop. Three generously sized bedrooms are located upstairs, and two of the home's three bathrooms have been beautifully remodeled. Updated LVP flooring completes much of the upper and lower levels. Downstairs, the large recreation room is ready for movie nights, game days or gatherings around the wood burning fireplace. A wet bar makes entertaining easy, while two additional bedrooms and a comfortable shared bathroom provide flexible space for guests, hobbies, offices or a variety of household needs. The fenced backyard offers room to play, relax or entertain outdoors. Beyond the property, Country Walk is the kind of neighborhood where residents are regularly out walking, biking and enjoying the nearby park and fishing pond - both approximately a three minute walk from the home. A neighborhood route provides easy access to Mulvane High School, and USD 263's shuttle system connects students with the district's other schools. Mulvane is known for its strong school spirit, active community and charming downtown. Main Street hosts parades, seasonal celebrations and the city's long running Old Settlers festivities, along with local restaurants, shops and gathering places. From this home, south Derby is just minutes away, while K-15 and the Kansas Turnpike provide convenient access to Wichita and the surrounding area. The sellers have also addressed numerous major updates and maintenance items, including replacement of most upstairs windows, exterior siding and paint work, chimney chase repairs, improvements to the deck connection and flashing, an insulated garage door, newer downstairs carpet and additional fence work. Spacious, updated and connected to everything that makes Mulvane special, this home offers a wonderful opportunity to experience life in Country Walk. Schedule your private showing today.
