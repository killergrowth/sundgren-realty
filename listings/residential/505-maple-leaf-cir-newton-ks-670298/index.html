About This Property

This Zero Threshold home with a fully finished basement from Heritage Home Works in Autumn Glen's new phase has many features you are going to love and it's right across the street from the new playground. The open living room features a vaulted ceiling, floor to ceiling stone surround fireplace and big picture windows letting in lots of sunshine. Kitchen highlights include Cambria countertops and splash, walk in pantry, range hood vented to the outside, and an island. Off the kitchen/dining combo you can walk out onto the back covered patio that will be great for entertaining family and friends. A separate laundry room is just around the corner and connects to the master bedroom and back around. There are many great features of the master bathroom including an extra-large zero entry tiled shower, separate room for the toilet, plus a his and hers sink with lots of counter space. The basement features the third and fourth bedrooms, and large family room with a wet bar. Additional features include a tankless water heater with re-circulation loop for faster hot water at each faucet, R-38 attic, cellulose wall insulation, and 92% gas furnace, and 14 SEER AC. Rare combo of zero entry and with a basement. Home will be completed in early April. Make plans to see today!
