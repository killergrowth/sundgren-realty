Property Description

Pretty as a picture inside and out, this gorgeous fully remodeled home is nestled in sought-after Tallgrass Wilderness, where stately homes, mature trees and generous lots create an exceptional setting. Extensively remodeled with an eye for both elegance and function, the 4,522-square-foot 1½-story floor plan offers 5 bedrooms, 4 full baths, 1 half bath, a 3-car garage and a spacious 1/3-acre lot with a beautifully renovated saltwater swimming pool. From the moment you enter, the quality and attention to detail are evident, with refinished hardwoods, new baseboards and casings, fresh cosmetics, LED lighting, beautiful fixtures and an inviting flow designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The heart of the home is the spectacular chef’s kitchen, featuring updated cabinetry, luxury stone and tile finishes, generous pantry storage, upscale appliances, gas range, double ovens and a center island. An open pass-through bar connects the kitchen, living and dining spaces, creating an effortless entertaining environment, while the formal dining room adds timeless appeal with wainscoting and beautiful lighting. The front office is equally impressive with custom built-in bookcases and a fireplace. The main-floor primary suite is a true retreat, offering a spacious sitting area with a double-sided fireplace and a stunning ensuite bath with dual vanities, a large shower, separate soaking tub, water closet and walk-in closet. Upstairs, three generously sized bedrooms feature excellent closet storage, including one ensuite bedroom and two bedrooms connected by a Jack-and-Jill bath, all with thoughtful updates throughout. The finished basement provides exceptional additional living and entertaining space, along with room for a workout area and hobbies. Outdoors, the beautifully landscaped yard is enclosed by a wrought-iron fence and features a covered patio, sprinkler system and recently renovated saltwater pool, creating a wonderful private setting for relaxing or entertaining. Additional improvements include siding replaced in 2017 and an EV charger in the garage. Located in the Circle School District with easy access to K-96, shopping, dining and schools, this exceptional property delivers a rare combination of an established Tallgrass Wilderness setting, impressive scale and a truly beautiful, comprehensive remodel.
