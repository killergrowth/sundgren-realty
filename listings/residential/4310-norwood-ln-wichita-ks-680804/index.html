Property Description

Coming Soon! Showings start Saturday October 24th. Discover the space, privacy, and natural surroundings that make Lost Acres Addition one of Wichita's hidden gems! This spacious home offers a comfortable blend of indoor and outdoor living, with a neutral color palette and beautiful real wood flooring throughout both levels. The main floor features three bedrooms, including a generous primary suite with its own full bathroom and two closets. Downstairs, the finished basement provides even more room to spread out, featuring a large family room with a wood-burning fireplace that creates an inviting setting for gatherings or relaxing evenings at home. An additional recreation room offers plenty of flexibility for hobbies, entertaining, or the potential to create an additional bedroom suite. Outdoor living is equally impressive! Step outside to a privacy-fenced backyard featuring a spacious two-tiered deck, perfect for grilling, entertaining, or simply enjoying the peaceful setting. Mature trees and the wooded area beyond the backyard create a beautiful natural backdrop with a sense of privacy that's hard to find in the city. Lost Acres is a tucked-away neighborhood offering convenient access to nearby nature trails, biking paths, and the dog park at the Kansas Humane Society. With quick access to K-96, you'll enjoy the tranquility of this established neighborhood without sacrificing convenience to shopping, dining, and destinations throughout Wichita and the surrounding communities. If you've been searching for a home with room to spread out, exceptional outdoor living, and a setting that feels removed from the hustle of the city, this one deserves a closer look!
