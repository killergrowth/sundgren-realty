Property Description

Super Move-In Ready Home in a Great Northwest Wichita Neighborhood! This fantastic home is clean as a whistle and ready for its new owner! With all-new interior paint, new flooring, and updates, you can move right in and enjoy! A steel entry door with a glass round-top design welcomes you into the tiled foyer with a convenient coat closet. The generous living room is filled with natural light from a large multi-pane picture window with flanking windows on each side. The sharp kitchen boasts oak cabinetry with granite countertops, decorative glass tile backsplash, new range, dishwasher, refrigerator, new vinyl flooring, and pantry. Just off the kitchen is a spacious dining area featuring double windows, ceiling fan, and new vinyl flooring. On the opposite side of the kitchen, you'll find convenient access to the covered deck along with the laundry area, complete with washer and dryer that remain with the home. The primary bedroom offers double windows overlooking the backyard, an additional window, and ceiling fan. The adjoining half bath features a pedestal sink, medicine cabinet, and window. Two additional bedrooms include attractive boxed-out double windows and lighted closets. Outdoor living is another highlight! Enjoy the fantastic 8' x 21' covered deck with an adjoining patio, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The front yard features a sprinkler system that has already been winterized. The attached garage boasts a new overhead door, opener, a 3' x 11' storage area at the back, and a convenient walk-through door to the deck. You'll also appreciate the two-car-wide driveway with a double-width approach to the street and an additional 9' x 24' concrete pad beside the garage. Additional updates include a NEW ROOF IN JUNE of 2026, along with low-maintenance vinyl siding and soffits! With fresh paint, new flooring, updated kitchen features, and great outdoor living space, this home is truly move-in ready. Don't miss this great house in a great northwest Wichita neighborhood!
