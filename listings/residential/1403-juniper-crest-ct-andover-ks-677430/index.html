About This Property

A truly exceptional offering in the prestigious gated community of Flint Hills, this stunning Nies Homes residence embodies timeless luxury. Since purchasing the home, the current owners have completed an extensive remodel and addition, thoughtfully enhancing the residence while preserving the integrity of its original design. All work was completed by Nies Homes, the home’s original builder, resulting in a seamless transformation that feels intentional from beginning to end. From the moment you step inside, this home is nothing short of a showstopper. Rarely does a home of this caliber come to market in such pristine condition, offering the look and feel of new construction--- without the wait. Soaring ceilings, oversized windows, and an abundance of natural light set the tone throughout the main level. A welcoming foyer leads to a private office or sitting room, an impressive great room, and an elegant dining space framed by floor-to-ceiling windows. The beautifully finished kitchen features an adjoining butler’s pantry, while the ultra-luxurious primary suite completes the main level. Designed as a private retreat, the primary suite offers picturesque views of the pool and tree-lined backyard, a stunning fireplace, and a spa-inspired bathroom featuring a freestanding soaking tub, split vanities, and exquisite finishes throughout. The expansive walk-in closet includes custom built-in shelving and direct access to the laundry room for everyday convenience. Outside, your backyard oasis awaits. A stunning pool with a shallow tanning ledge invites you to relax and soak up the sun. The newly added stone privacy wall along the concrete patio creates a peaceful setting for morning coffee, evening sunsets, or simply taking in the beauty of the surrounding landscape. The expansive backyard provides ample room to further enhance the property with a pool house or detached garage. The second story offers a spacious loft, an additional living area overlooking the dramatic two-story great room, two bedrooms, and a full bathroom with double vanity. The finished lower level provides exceptional space and flexibility with a large family room, private bedroom, two full bathrooms, a bonus room perfectly suited for a fifth bedroom, a dedicated fitness room, and an oversized storage room. Luxury extends beyond the home itself with NO SPECIAL ASSESSMENT TAXES and an HOA that provides lawn mowing, fertilization, sprinkler system opening and winterization, and driveway snow removal when accumulations exceed two inches—offering effortless, low-maintenance living year-round. Surrounded by mature trees and natural beauty, Flint Hills offers the privacy and tranquility of country living while remaining just minutes from shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences. Luxury. Privacy. Timeless design. Welcome to one of Flint Hills’ finest residences.
