Property Description

This charming 4-bedroom, 1.5-bath property in Anthony, Kansas, is full of character, warmth, and inviting spaces! Enjoy the charm of the covered front porch, complete with a porch swing—the perfect place to relax and unwind or have morning coffee. The spacious living room features beautifully refinished hardwood floors, a new ceiling, and updated lighting, creating a fresh and welcoming atmosphere. The formal dining room continues the hardwood flooring and offers a cozy bench seat nestled in front of plentiful windows, providing the perfect spot to enjoy natural light and gather with family and friends. The kitchen offers a delightful breakfast nook, an island with a stove, a dishwasher, and an abundance of cabinet space for all your storage needs. The main level also includes one bedroom, a convenient half bath, and a laundry area. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms, each featuring walk-in closets, including a generously sized primary bedroom with two walk-in closets! A full bathroom and an additional bonus room provide even more flexibility, making the space ideal for a home office, hobby room, or extra storage. Outside, the property features an attached one-car garage and a spacious, fenced backyard with plenty of room for kids to play, pets to roam, or a garden to grow. With its beautiful hardwood floors, spacious rooms, abundant storage, and charming outdoor spaces, this home offers a wonderful blend of character and functionality.
