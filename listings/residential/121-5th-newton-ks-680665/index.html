Property Description

Looking for classic charm without the long project list? 121 SE 5th St is fully renovated and ready for its new owner! This is a 2-bed, 1-bath home in Newton featuring vintage charm and major modern updates throughout. An inviting living and dining area designed for flexible furniture arrangements, great natural lighting, and effortless everyday living. Newly Renovated Kitchen - A true centerpiece featuring sleek updated cabinetry, clean finishes, and an abundance of counter space that makes meal prep and entertaining a joy. Two comfortable bedrooms with generous lighting and versatile layouts. Fresh interior and exterior paint provide crisp curb appeal. Major upgrades include a brand-new HVAC system and ductwork (2024), a new hot water heater. Step out back to a massive, fully wood-fenced back yard—a private sanctuary ideal for pets, outdoor play, lush gardens, or weekend barbecues. Includes a dedicated outdoor storage shed to keep tools, lawn care equipment, and seasonal gear neatly stowed away. There is a partial basement that has 448 unfinished s.f. that is accessed with a pull up door in the hall floor. Check out this turn key- ready to move in home and make it your own!
