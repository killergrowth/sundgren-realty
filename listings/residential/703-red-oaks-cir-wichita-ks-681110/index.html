Property Description

It's back on the market with a new and improved PRICE and ready to be your dream HOME....A little bit of country tucked right into East Wichita! This beautifully remodeled, move-in-ready home sits on approximately half an acre and offers 4 bedrooms PLUS a bonus room, 3 bathrooms, gorgeous updates, incredible outdoor living and possibilities you simply don't find every day in this location. The outdoor space is truly something special. A wraparound porch overlooks a beautifully landscaped dream yard complete with paved walking paths winding through the backyard, built-in benches, a dedicated play area and charming playhouse. Whether it's kids playing, summer evenings with friends, morning coffee on the porch or simply having room to breathe, this yard was made to be enjoyed. A new, long second driveway adds abundant parking and access to the property's permitted 40' x 30' detached building. Built on its own foundation, insulated and equipped with electrical, the building also features its own porch and a 12' x 28' loft. Use it as an incredible workshop, second garage, studio or hobby space, or explore the potential to finish it as a future second dwelling, multigenerational space or house-hacking opportunity. Inside, the main home has already been beautifully renovated, giving you the best of both worlds: move-in-ready living today with incredible possibilities for tomorrow. It's hard to find land, a dream backyard, additional parking, a permitted workshop/garage with loft AND a beautifully remodeled home without leaving the convenience of East Wichita. This property brings it all together in one truly unique package. Come see it in person and imagine the possibilities!
