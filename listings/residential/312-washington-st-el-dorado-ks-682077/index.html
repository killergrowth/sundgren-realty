Property Description

A Timeless Beauty Filled with Charm and Character! Built in 1918, this beautiful 1,567 sq. ft. home is full of character and timeless charm. Featuring gorgeous original woodwork, original wood floors, and some of the home's original light fixtures, this home beautifully showcases its historic craftsmanship. The formal dining room offers charming built-ins, while the living room features beautiful French doors that open into a bedroom, adding a touch of elegance and versatility. With 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, there's plenty of room to enjoy. Two bedrooms are conveniently located on the main floor, along with a separate laundry room. Upstairs, you'll find a private primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and half bath. Full of warmth, character, and unique details you just don't find in newer homes, this 1918 beauty is ready to steal your heart!
