Property Description

New Listing!! Old world charm meets modern efficiency in this truly one of a kind stone beauty, perfectly situated on a corner lot. From the moment you arrive, the all stone exterior and inviting front porch set the tone for the character found throughout the home. Inside, the living room features a stunning stone wood-burning fireplace, creating a warm and welcoming centerpiece. The opposite end of the living room is perfect for a cozy sitting area. The fully remodeled kitchen offers updated cabinetry, abundant storage and includes all the kitchen appliances, including the washer and dryer. Prepare to be amazed by the show stopping master suite. Cathedral ceilings elevate the bedroom, while the spa inspired master bath features a dramatic rock accent wall, freestanding soaking tub, and a walk-in shower and closet - easily one of the most unique and impressive suites You'll find. The master suite includes its own private screened in porch with access to the backyard. It truly does feel like a retreat. The finished basement provides additional living space with a common area, ideal for a game room or family room, a full bathroom with walk-in shower and two additional bedrooms with egress windows. Utility room is also located downstairs. Outside, you can enjoy a spacious backyard with privacy fence, a dedicated outdoor sitting area perfect for morning coffee or weekend barbecues. The tool shed would be great for storage or workshop . This home is rich in character, thoughtfully, updated, and unlike anything else on the market. Call the listing agent for more information and to see this gem!
