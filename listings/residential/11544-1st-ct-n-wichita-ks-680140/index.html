Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious 5-bedroom, 3-full-bath home nestled on a quiet, tree-lined cul-de-sac! With plenty of room both upstairs and down, this home offers flexible living space. The full finished basement is perfect for entertaining or relaxing, featuring a large family room and wet bar. Upstairs, the open-concept kitchen and living area provides a warm, inviting gathering space, highlighted by beautiful engineered wood floors and an electric fireplace. The primary suite offers a private retreat with its own full bath featuring a tub/shower combination and a walk-in closet. Outside, relax at the end of the day on the spacious covered deck, enjoy great curb appeal and the peaceful setting of a quiet, established neighborhood. Solar panels in the back are paid off and remain with the home. The 2-car garage includes additional storage space, adding even more functionality to this well-designed home.Conveniently located close to shopping, dining, and everyday amenities, this home offers the space, comfort, and location you've been looking for!
