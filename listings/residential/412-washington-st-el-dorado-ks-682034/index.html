Property Description

Charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath home offering 1,152 square feet and plenty of natural light. The natural stone veneer facade adds distinctive curb appeal, while the open kitchen makes a stylish statement with modern dark cabinetry, a spacious island with warm wood slat detailing and a built-in cooktop, a striking mosaic backsplash, and pendant lighting. Multiple windows brighten the space, creating a welcoming spot to cook and gather. The updated bathroom features LVP flooring, and brand-new carpet runs throughout the hallway and both bedrooms and laminate flooring throughout the Livingroom and kitchen. Laundry is conveniently placed in the kitchen with counter tops and storage for easy accessibility and convenience! A detached two-car garage offers 12-foot ceilings, east- and west-facing doors and a third garage door for convenient drive-through access, and alley access, with potential room for RV storage
