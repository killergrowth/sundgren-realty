Property Description

Welcome Home to 1912 State St.! Charming, comfortable, and conveniently located near beautiful Augusta City Lake, this inviting 3-bedroom, 1-bath home is ready to welcome its next owners! One of the things you'll love most about this home is its wonderful location, just minutes from Augusta City Lake and all the outdoor enjoyment that comes with it! Imagine starting your morning with a peaceful stroll along the walking paths, spending a sunny afternoon enjoying the lake, or simply taking in the beautiful scenery and fresh air. With parks, playgrounds, and recreational activities so close to home, there's always something to enjoy nearby! From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the warm, welcoming feel and the natural light that fills the home through its large windows. Newer, low-maintenance wood-look laminate flooring flows throughout, offering a fresh, updated look while making everyday upkeep a breeze. And here's a little hidden bonus—hardwood floors are waiting underneath the laminate in the living room and bedrooms! The comfortable living room is a wonderful place to settle in after a busy day, catch up with friends, or simply enjoy a quiet evening at home. Just beyond, the dedicated dining room is filled with charm, featuring a lovely chandelier and French doors that open to the covered deck. It's easy to picture morning coffee on the deck, relaxed evenings outdoors, or gathering around the table for your favorite meals. The light and bright kitchen is both cheerful and functional, offering a convenient pantry for extra storage and most kitchen appliances remaining with the home. Whether you're preparing a quick breakfast or cooking a favorite family recipe, this kitchen is ready for everyday moments and special occasions alike. All three bedrooms offer comfortable spaces and wonderful natural light, giving you flexibility for bedrooms, a home office, hobby room, or whatever best suits your needs. A full bathroom completes the interior, while the 1-car garage provides added convenience and storage. Outside, you'll appreciate the additional parking space, offering extra room for vehicles or guests. And for added peace of mind during Kansas storm season, the property also features a storm shelter with a sump pump, providing a valuable feature when severe weather rolls through. These thoughtful extras add to the everyday convenience and practicality of this charming home! And the location truly adds to the appeal! In addition to being so close to Augusta City Lake and its scenic walking paths, you'll enjoy convenient access to Bill Reed Park, Garvin Park, the splash pad, Castle Park playground, and the dog park. Whether you enjoy spending time outdoors, exploring local parks, or taking your four-legged companion out for some fresh air, there are plenty of opportunities to get out and enjoy the community. Elementary, middle, and high schools are also conveniently located nearby, adding to the everyday practicality of this Augusta address. If you've been dreaming of a place that feels like home the moment you walk through the door, with manageable living spaces, thoughtful features, and outdoor recreation just minutes away, this may be just what you've been waiting for! From the newer flooring and hidden hardwoods to the cheerful kitchen, charming dining room, covered deck, storm shelter, extra parking, and fantastic location near Augusta City Lake, there's so much to appreciate about 1912 State St. Come take a look, imagine the possibilities, and see how easily you could feel right at home!
