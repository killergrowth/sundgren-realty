Property Description

Discover the perfect balance of comfort, privacy, and functionality in this spacious ranch-style home tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac. Designed with everyday living in mind, this home offers a thoughtfully arranged floor plan featuring a private primary suite situated separately from the additional bedrooms. The primary suite provides a relaxing retreat, complete with an ensuite bathroom featuring a jetted Jacuzzi-style tub and a generous walk-in closet. Step outside onto the deck, an inviting space for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or simply enjoying the fresh air. The finished basement expands the possibilities with additional living space and abundant storage to keep everything organized. Outside, the fenced backyard offers room for outdoor activities along with a convenient storage shed for tools and equipment. An attached three-car garage provides additional space for vehicles, hobbies, and storage. With its combination of comfortable living areas, practical amenities, and versatile spaces, this property delivers the flexibility to complement a variety of lifestyles.
