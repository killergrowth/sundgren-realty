Property Description

Located on a large corner lot in Winfield, this 3 bedroom, 2 bath home features beautiful historic details and charm throughout. Built in 1901, the home showcases original woodwork, hardwood floors, tall windows, and the excellent craftsmanship of its era. The covered front porch offers the perfect spot to relax with your morning coffee or unwind at the end of the day. With 1,582 square feet of living space within, there’s plenty of room to spread out, including a large living room filled with natural light and featuring a wood-burning stove. The adjoining dining area provides plenty of space for gathering and entertaining, while the roomy kitchen offers lots of cabinet and counter space along with a separate pantry area for additional storage. You’ll also find three generously sized bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a dedicated laundry room with ample storage. Outside, mature trees provide plenty of shade, with a covered patio area and fenced side and back yards offering even more room to enjoy the outdoors. A detached garage with electricity provides additional parking and storage space or room for a shop. With its original details, spacious layout, and expansive corner lot, this home has so much to offer! Schedule your private showing today and come see it before it's gone!
