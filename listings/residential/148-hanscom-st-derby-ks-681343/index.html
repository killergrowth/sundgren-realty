Property Description

Make your move to Foxfire’s newest phase now, in time for the holidays! Google maps hasn't caught up with us yet, but we're on 55th St south between Woodlawn and Oliver - just turn in on Forest Park and you'll find us! Built by Comfort Homes, the Nick Jr. plan offers 1,245 square feet with 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, and a 3-car garage, plus room to grow in the full view-out basement. Vaulted ceilings, luxury trim, and vinyl plank flooring create an inviting main living area, with an open layout that keeps the kitchen, dining, and living spaces connected. Appliances will include the range, dishwasher, and microwave, and quartz countertops carry through the kitchen and both baths. The split-bedroom design gives the primary suite added privacy, with a box ceiling, walk-in shower, and generous walk-in closet. Just inside the garage, the laundry room includes a convenient drop zone with a bench and hooks. A covered deck and patio extend your living space outdoors, while the unfinished basement offers the opportunity to nearly double your finished square footage. Framing for two additional bedrooms and a rec room, along with a rough-in for a future bath, gives you a head start whenever you’re ready to finish. Enjoy Derby’s shopping, dining, parks, and schools, with convenient access to McConnell AFB and east Wichita. While Google Maps catches up with our newest streets, you can find Foxfire on 55th St. South between Woodlawn and Oliver—turn onto Forest Park and come explore Phase 2!
