Property Description

Welcome Home to 10213 E. Summerfield, a beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home in east Wichita’s desirable Waterfront area, offering an inviting blend of refined finishes, spacious living areas, abundant natural light and exceptional attention to detail. From the moment you enter, the open floor plan, large windows and plantation shutters create a light-filled setting that feels both elegant and welcoming. The lovely living room is anchored by a fireplace and handsome built-in cabinetry, with formal and informal dining rooms providing wonderful spaces for everything from everyday meals to special gatherings. At the heart of the home is a gorgeous granite kitchen designed with both beauty and function in mind. Beautiful tile backsplash, under-cabinet lighting and conveniently placed under-cabinet electrical outlets complement the generous workspace, while the island with breakfast bar creates a natural gathering place. Bosch and KitchenAid appliances add to the well-appointed kitchen, and the walk-in pantry offers a dedicated coffee bar and microwave for additional convenience. The spacious primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with carpeting, large windows, plantation shutters, a trayed ceiling with ceiling fan and built-in speakers. The beautifully finished primary bath features tile flooring, granite countertops, dual sinks, two walk-in closets with three-level hanging rods, a tiled shower with dual shower heads and a soaker tub—creating a private space designed for comfort and relaxation. Thoughtful details continue throughout the home. The main-floor laundry room is anything but ordinary, featuring granite countertops, a hanging rod and built-in desk. A built-in mud bench adds convenient everyday storage, while a safe room, zoned heating and air and wiring for a security system add even more functionality. A versatile bonus room offers another exceptional living space with a fireplace, large windows and sliding-door access to the patio. Whether used as a home office, sitting room or additional entertaining area, this flexible room extends the home’s comfortable living space. A granite wet bar with beautiful tile accents adds another polished feature and creates a convenient setting for entertaining. Step outside to the covered patio, complete with a ceiling fan for comfortable outdoor living and wrought-iron fencing surrounding the yard. The oversized finished 2-car attached garage is equally impressive, with an epoxied floor and additional room for storage. With its open layout, multiple fireplaces, granite and quartz surfaces, custom built-ins, plantation shutters, abundant windows, wet bar, home-office potential, covered outdoor living and oversized garage, this home combines upscale finishes with spaces designed for everyday enjoyment. Conveniently located near 13th and Webb in east Wichita, with easy access to The Waterfront area and nearby restaurants, shopping, grocery and entertainment. A beautifully appointed Wichita home with the space, finishes and thoughtful details to enjoy from the moment you arrive.
