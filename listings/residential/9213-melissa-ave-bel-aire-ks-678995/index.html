This thoughtfully designed 4-bedroom and 3-bathroom home offers 1,592 sq. ft. of living space with an open-concept layout, plenty of natural light, and a functional floor plan built for everyday living. The spacious kitchen opens directly to the dining and living areas and features granite countertops, a large center island, plenty of cabinet space, and a walk-in pantry.
