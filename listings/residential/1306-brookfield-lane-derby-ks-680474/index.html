Property Description

Check out this lovely, updated home located in the heart of Derby on a large cul-de-sac lot in the Tanglewood Elementary district. The home offers over 2776 square feet with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms on the main floor with an additional non-conforming bedroom with an ensuite in the basement. The spacious kitchen has beautiful Corian countertops and newer stainless-steel appliances that will remain with the home. Enjoy your morning coffee in the kitchen’s bay window eating nook overlooking the expansive backyard. The backyard is the ultimate entertaining area for family and friends with the large covered deck with built-in seating, patio for those summer BBQ’s and yard games. The main for is rounded out with a large formal dining is perfect for family gatherings and dinner parties, living room with a brick fireplace and main floor laundry. The bathrooms in the home have all been beautifully updated with new fixtures, tiled floors and surrounds. The basement provides an abundance of additional living space and would make great movie room and has an additional room that could be used as a nonconforming bedroom. The home is topped off with a durable Metal roof that offers peace of mind with energy efficiency, low maintenance, and superior protection against the Kansas Storms.
