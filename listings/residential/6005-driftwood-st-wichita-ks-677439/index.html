Property Description

Welcome to 6005 W. Driftwood, Wichita, KS 67205, a lakefront home in the Maize School District offering beautiful water views, flexible living spaces, and an incredible setup for entertaining. The main living area immediately takes advantage of the setting with soaring ceilings, exposed wood beams, and oversized windows overlooking the lake. The bright, updated kitchen features white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, a stylish backsplash, walk in pantry and a large center island with plenty of room for seating and gathering. One of the home’s standout spaces is the window-filled flex room with a fireplace. With abundant natural light and views of the water, it would make an amazing plant room, home office, reading retreat, creative space, or teen hangout. Downstairs, the finished basement is ready for fun. With a spacious recreation area, wet bar, and plenty of room for a big-screen TV, this may become everyone’s favorite place to watch Chiefs football with friends, shoot a game of pool, or simply relax on the weekends. Outside, the backyard delivers the lifestyle buyers are looking for. Enjoy the fenced yard, mature trees, lake views, and outdoor entertaining area with a large wood pergola—an ideal setting for morning coffee, evening drinks, or watching the sunset over the water. If you’ve been searching for a lakefront home in northwest Wichita with Maize schools, a finished basement, flexible living space, and beautiful views, 6005 W. Driftwood deserves a spot at the top of your list.
