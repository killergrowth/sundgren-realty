Property Description

A truly exceptional transformation in the heart of The Village! Originally built in 1958, this remarkable mid-century ranch has undergone an extensive, top-to-bottom reconstruction, thoughtfully preserving its architectural character while introducing the quality, comfort and peace of mind of an essentially new home. Nearly every element has been replaced, including electrical, plumbing, HVAC, windows, roof, insulation and drywall, creating an immaculate home that beautifully marries timeless 1950s architecture with today's expectations. Set on an impressive half-acre corner lot beneath a canopy of mature trees, the property makes an unforgettable first impression with its handsome painted brick exterior, circular drive and four-car side-load garage. Inside, crisp white walls, gorgeous acacia wood flooring and carefully preserved original materials create a warm, sophisticated setting. Original marble flooring welcomes you in the foyer, while the original slate floors in the sunken family room add character and authenticity. Formal living and dining rooms provide gracious gathering spaces, while the family room is a showstopper, featuring a stone-surround fireplace with decorative metal hood, exposed wood beams and a beautifully appointed wet bar. A new Pella glass door opens to the stone patio, where an outdoor fireplace creates an inviting extension of the living space. The kitchen is exceptionally appointed with professional Wolf appliances, including double ovens and a gas range, along with a center island, eating bar and built-in desk. Just beyond, an oversized laundry and mudroom offers abundant storage, a second full-size refrigerator and wonderful functionality for everyday living or entertaining on a larger scale. Privately situated at this end of the home, an ensuite bedroom offers an ideal home office or guest retreat. The opposite wing provides three additional bedrooms, two full baths, a convenient guest powder room and a generous storage closet. Two bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bath with a freestanding tub and tile shower, while the primary suite is a destination of its own. The gorgeous bath features dual vanities, a private water closet and walk-in shower, complemented by an exceptional collection of closets. One particularly spacious closet steps down into a delightful dressing room, creating a special place to begin and end each day. The fenced backyard, mature landscaping and stone patio with fireplace provide a wonderful setting for outdoor enjoyment. Immaculately executed and thoughtfully curated to honor the architecture of its era, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy the charm and presence of a classic 1950s ranch with the extensive improvements of a virtually new home. Conveniently located near downtown Wichita, Kellogg and the shopping and dining destinations of northeast Wichita, this is an extraordinary home that truly must be experienced in person!
