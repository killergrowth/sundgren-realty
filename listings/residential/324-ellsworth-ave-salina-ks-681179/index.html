Property Description

Charming Sunset Park Cottage with Character & Updates! Welcome to 324 W Ellsworth Ave., a delightful 2-bedroom, 1-bath cottage located in the popular Sunset Park neighborhood. This 907 sq. ft. home blends original character with thoughtful updates and comfortable living spaces. Step inside to discover beautiful hardwood floors in the bedrooms, a decorative fireplace in the living room, and a charming corner cabinet in the dining room. The updated kitchen features a tiled floor with refrigerator and range included, while the convenient main-floor laundry is located in the mud room of the main floor complete with pet door to the back yard. Enjoy the screened-in side porch, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing evenings. The full basement offers additional space, with new laminate flooring in some of the basement and a radon mitigation system is already in place. Outside, you'll find a fenced backyard with a deck and raised garden beds, providing a wonderful space for gardening, entertaining, or simply enjoying your own private outdoor retreat. Both the interior and the exterior have been freshly painted and new roof shingles and gutters were just installed in 2026. A detached 1-car garage adds convenient storage and parking. Ideally located in central Salina with easy access to neighborhood amenities, this charming cottage is ready to welcome its next owner.
