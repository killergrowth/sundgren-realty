Property Description

Spacious, affordable, and priced to sell! Situated on nearly half an acre in the charming community of Rose Hill, this home offers plenty of room inside and out. The property welcomes you with a unique circle drive and features 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a private en-suite bathroom and his-and-hers closets. The finished basement offers a huge family room, perfect for entertaining or relaxing, along with an abundance of storage space. Outside, you'll love the massive fenced backyard with endless possibilities for outdoor entertaining, pets, or play. Additional highlights include newer windows and updated mechanical systems for added peace of mind. Enjoy the best of small-town living in Rose Hill, known for its welcoming community and excellent schools, all while being just a short drive from Wichita. With so much space and value, this home is an opportunity you won't want to miss!
