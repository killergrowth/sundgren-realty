Property Description

Tucked neatly into the established Country Walk neighborhood —just behind Mulvane High School with quick access to K-15 and Derby's Rock Road strip—this exceptional, one-owner residence delivers just under 2700 finished square feet on a generous & private quarter acre-plus parcel. 5 total bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and a triple-car garage effortlessly make this house easy to call home. From the moment you step onto the welcoming front porch, the home’s open-concept layout reveals itself with natural light beaming into the living room, kitchen, & dining area. A clever pantry/ laundry room combo helps keep daily routines organized. The main level offers a desirable split-bedroom configuration, placing two bedrooms and one bathroom on one side and a private primary suite on the other. Inside the primary suite, unwind in the jetted tub, step into the walk-in shower, or enjoy the sheer convenience of Jack-and-Jill vanities. The tankless water heater also allows for leisurely hot soaks. Downstairs, the finished basement is built for whatever entertainment looks like for you! Featuring two additional bedrooms and a bathroom, this level boasts a sprawling recreation room paired with an entirely separate family lounge—ideal for dual hosting, a home theater, game night, or simply spreading out. Step outside to discover a sprawling back patio ready for sunset gatherings, paired with an expansive yard complete with a handy storage shed. This home comes equipped with solar panels at no additional cost to the buyer and will keep their electric bills reduced to a minimum! Rounding out the lifestyle, the neighborhood HOA takes care of 3 ponds and a community playground, leaving you with plenty of opportunities to tend to hobbies and meet neighbors.
