Property Description

Just outside of Salina, right off blacktop, and sitting on just under an acre! If you've been looking for a little more room to spread out while staying close to town, this property has so much to offer. With 5 bedrooms, multiple living spaces, and a spacious 3-car garage with plenty of room for tools and a workshop area, there's space for everyone and everything! The main floor features four bedrooms and two full bathrooms, including a large primary suite addition with sliding glass doors leading to the back deck. You'll also appreciate the newer granite countertops and open living areas throughout the home. Downstairs, the remodeled basement provides additional living space and a fifth bedroom complete with an added egress window. Big-ticket improvements include a brand-new roof, siding, and gutters, along with a newer garage door and septic tank. Outside, the nearly one-acre lot offers endless possibilities! Whether you've been wanting to build your own shop, have a few chickens, start a garden, or simply enjoy a little extra space between neighbors, there's room to bring your ideas to life. Enjoy the feel of country living with the convenience of being just minutes from Salina!
