Property Description

Charming 1920 Home on 1.67 acres. Step into timeless charm and modern comfort with this beautiful 1920 home situated on a peaceful 1.67 acre lot in Marion, Kansas. From the moment you enter the foyer, you will be greeted by an inviting open staircase and gorgeous wood parquet flooring, just two of the many beautiful details found throughout the home. The main floor features a spacious living room, a dining room with lovely built in cabinetry, and a versatile bedroom currently used as an office. The kitchen combines style and function with ceramic tile flooring, quartz countertops and a charming baker’s nook. Main floor laundry adds everyday convenience. Two years ago, the owners added a fantastic sunroom and covered deck off the back of the home. Large Pella windows provide beautiful views of the surrounding property, where abundant wildlife creates a peaceful outdoor setting. Bird lovers will especially appreciate this wonderful location. Upstairs, you will find three nice sized bedrooms and a full bathroom. The home has seen numerous updates, including separate HVAC systems serving the main and upper floors, just three years old. Outdoor amenities include an attached three-car carport, a 40' x 30' shop and two Derksen buildings offering abundant storage space. A Generac generator provides added peace of mind. With its blend of historic character, thoughtful updates, generous outbuildings and serene acreage, this special Marion property offers a rare opportunity to enjoy country like living with plenty of space and comfort. It's the best of both worlds.
