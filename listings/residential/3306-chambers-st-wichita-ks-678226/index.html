Property Description

Welcome to this thoughtfully designed home in the Fontana subdivision and highly desirable Maize School District! SPECIAL TAXES ALMOST PAID!!! Featuring a sought-after bonus room floor plan, spacious living areas, impressive architectural details and water views, this home offers flexibility for everyday living, entertaining, multi-generational living or remote office work. The light-filled living room features soaring vaulted ceilings, wood beams, oversized windows, true wood flooring and a gas fireplace with tile and custom wood accents. The open layout flows into the kitchen and dining area, where a barrel ceiling with indirect lighting creates a warm gathering space. The large, functional kitchen includes a gas range, rustic beech cabinetry, under-cabinet lighting and a hidden walk-in pantry. The primary suite offers a private retreat with French doors leading to the en suite bath, complete with dual vanities, a large walk-in shower, soaker tub and separate linen closet. The spacious walk-in closet includes organized floor-to-ceiling storage for seasonal clothing and shoes and connects directly to the main-floor laundry room. Additional main-floor conveniences include a functional drop zone near the garage and laundry area with hanging space and upper cubby storage. The second main-floor bedroom offers generous space and is adjacent to a full bathroom with a tub/shower and granite-topped vanity. Privately located by itself upstairs, the additional bedroom offers exceptional flexibility with zoned HVAC, an angled ceiling, large walk-in closet and private full en suite bathroom with a granite-topped vanity. Ideal as a second primary suite, private guest room, multi-generational living space or quiet remote office away from the home's high-traffic areas. The finished walkout basement provides incredible additional living and entertaining space. The expansive family room features a stone and brick-faced gas fireplace, mounted electric screen and projector for movie nights and game-day gatherings, plus a separate recreation and game table area. The wet bar includes tile flooring, seating for bar stools, abundant cabinetry, granite upper and lower counters, a full-size refrigerator, dishwasher and wine rack. Two additional bedrooms, each with walk-in closets, a full bathroom with tub/shower and a storage room complete the basement, providing even more flexibility for guests, extended family or a remote office. Outside, enjoy two distinct outdoor living areas: a large, covered deck off the dining room with outdoor speakers, and a large custom stone patio off the walkout basement. The patio is electrically wired to accommodate a hot tub or firepit, and features a built-in stone privacy screen. Both spaces provide plenty of room to relax or entertain while enjoying the water views. The garage also offers ample storage with ceiling pallet storage, built-in cabinets with countertop workspace and wood shelving that remain with the property.
