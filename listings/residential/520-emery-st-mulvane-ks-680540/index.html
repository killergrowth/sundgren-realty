Property Description

FLEXIBLE LIVING, STUNNING STYLE & A BACKYARD OASIS IN MULVANE’S COLLEGE HILL ADDITION! Discover the possibilities in this beautiful 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom property on nearly half an acre! A standout feature is the separate guest living suite, connected by a sunroom/atrium and complete with its own bedroom, living room, kitchenette, full bathroom, and separate AC unit. Perfect for multigenerational living, guests, or added flexibility! The main ranch-style home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a desirable split-bedroom floor plan. The inviting open layout features a light-filled living room with a recently serviced gas fireplace and a beautiful kitchen boasting quartz countertops, stylish backsplash, large island, farmhouse sink, stainless steel appliances, abundant storage, and luxury vinyl flooring. The private primary suite includes a walk-in closet and ensuite with double sinks and a step-in shower. Two additional bedrooms, a full guest bath with separate soaking tub and shower, main-floor laundry, and an oversized attached 2-car garage with gas heater complete the interior. Outside, you will find your own private retreat! Enjoy the pergola-covered patio overlooking a fully stocked pond with a soothing waterfall. Beautiful landscaping, garden beds with drip irrigation, a sprinkler system, fully fenced backyard, two storage sheds, extra parking, and RV hookup with sliding alley gate add even more appeal. Versatile living, inviting spaces, and exceptional outdoor enjoyment ~ this is a one-of-a-kind property!
