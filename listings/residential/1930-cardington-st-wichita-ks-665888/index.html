Property Description

One-owner home located just a few blocks from area lakes, with nearby sidewalks for outdoor enjoyment. This property offers a blend of thoughtful upgrades and unique features not commonly found. The open-concept main level is filled with natural light from a skylight and features hardwood flooring and granite surfaces throughout. The living room boasts a vaulted ceiling and opens seamlessly to the kitchen and dining area. A former wood-burning fireplace has been converted to an electric fireplace insert and is finished with a marble tile surround, creating a striking focal point. The kitchen is designed for serious cooking and entertaining, featuring a commercial-grade gas range with six large burners, oversized oven, and commercial-level vent hood. A chef’s delight, this range easily accommodates large pots and pans. Additional kitchen highlights include a granite island and countertops, tile backsplash and range surround, pull-out shelving, and a newer dishwasher. The upper level includes all three bedrooms, including a private primary suite with en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet. All bathrooms throughout the home feature tile floors and tile surrounds. The lower level provides flexible living space with view-out windows, a recreation room, and a ¾ bathroom. Outdoor amenities make this home stand out, featuring a gardener’s retreat with peach, pear, and nectarine trees, irrigation well, sprinkler system, and greenhouse. The privacy-fenced yard includes a large deck with raised and covered gazebo area, plus a spacious storage area beneath the deck offering excellent bonus storage. Water features include a pond with waterfall and a fountain, complemented by a generous stamped concrete patio—altogether creating a distinctive outdoor setting. Additional features include an insulated garage, brand-new water heater, and a high-efficiency commercial-grade HVAC system (approximately 4 years old). Located in the Maize School District with no specials—this home is move-in ready.
