Property Description

Back by popular demand, the JoJo Plan by M&M Custom Construction is now available in Watermarke! Set on a quiet cul-de-sac lot just a short walk from future neighborhood amenities—including the clubhouse, second pool, playground, and pickleball court planned to begin construction later this year—this stunning new home blends timeless curb appeal with thoughtful, functional design. A welcoming front elevation leads into a defined entryway featuring a tray ceiling with wood accent, chandelier, and convenient coat closet. The spacious living room is filled with natural light from large picture windows and showcases a tray ceiling with wood beams, an electric fireplace, and a wood mantel. The rear-facing kitchen is designed to impress with quartz countertops, abundant cabinetry, a custom range hood vented to the exterior, and a walk-in pantry. A bright dining area with a large picture window and hanging light fixture completes the main living space. The luxurious primary suite offers a spacious bedroom, private bath with tile shower, soaker tub, dual vanity with sconce accent lighting, and a large walk-in closet with seasonal hanging. An additional main-floor bedroom and full hall bath provide comfort and flexibility. The flex level features a zero-step garage entrance, drop zone, separate laundry room, and a second spacious living area surrounded by large picture windows, plus sliding glass doors leading to a 12' x 22' covered patio. The fully finished basement adds even more living space with a massive family room, walk-up wet bar, two additional spacious bedrooms, a full bath, and generous storage.
