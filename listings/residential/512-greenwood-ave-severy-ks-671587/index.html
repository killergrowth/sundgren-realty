Property Description

This family home is ready for it's next family! Very open kitchen, living and dining room area, this home has 2 bedrooms on the main level and an additional 2 bedrooms in the upstairs, which is accessed by a pull down ladder. This home sits on a lot that is a tad bit larger than 1 acre and sits at the north end of Severy. This home has a metal roof as well as an area that is accessed through a drop down ladder that leads to 2 additional rooms! The garage/workshop (that has a concrete floor) and a pole barn that is open on the east side as well as a partial cellar are also bonuses this property offers. There is covered parking on the east side of the home by way of an attached carport. Two wood burning stoves provide additional heat for the house. To the east and north of the home is a large lot with several large shade trees.
