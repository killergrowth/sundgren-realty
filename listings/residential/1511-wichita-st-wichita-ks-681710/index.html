Property Description

This bungalow has original character and plenty of potential, with a covered front porch, hardwood floors, and built-in shelving already in place. The home offers 3 bedrooms and 1 bath. Hardwood runs from the living room into the formal dining room, where the built-ins and ceiling fans give the space a classic feel. The kitchen has gray cabinetry and a walk-in pantry lined with shelving. A separate laundry room with windows lets in natural light. Downstairs, the partially finished basement has a rec/family room alongside a long stretch of storage space. Outside, the fenced yard and storage building round out the lot. It needs some work, but it’s a solid opportunity to make updates and add value. Being sold as-is. Schedule your showing today!
