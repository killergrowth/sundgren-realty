PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: Enjoy golf course living at Unit 23 in Rolling Hills Estates Condominiums, a unique three-level condo built in 1973 with scenic views of Rolling Hills Golf Course. The main floor features a living room with brick fireplace, kitchen with all appliances included, and a convenient half bath near the front entryway. Step outside to the private patio and enjoy views of the golf course, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs, you’ll find two bedrooms and a Jack-and-Jill bathroom featuring two sinks and three vanities for added functionality and convenience. A second-floor balcony overlooks the beautiful rolling landscape of the golf course, providing a peaceful place to enjoy the view. The basement offers a spacious, open living area with a dry bar, ideal for a recreation room, hobby space, or additional entertaining area. A separate utility room includes a washer and dryer. Parking and storage are exceptional features, with an attached garage plus an additional detached garage located across the drive. With golf course views from both the main-floor patio and second-floor balcony, generous multi-level living spaces, and two garage options, Unit 23 offers a distinctive opportunity to enjoy comfortable condo living in Wichita.
