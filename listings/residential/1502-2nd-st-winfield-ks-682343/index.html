Property Description

Lease this renovated Winfield home with or without an exclusive option to purchase. Perfect for buyers who expect to be mortgage-ready within the next couple of years but aren't quite there today — live in the home now while you prepare to purchase! Built in 1910, this refreshed Winfield home blends classic charm with modern updates. A covered front porch with a wood floor welcomes you in, and the living room features floor-to-ceiling bookshelves and a original-style fireplace for decorative charm. Fresh paint inside and out, luxury vinyl tile flooring throughout, and new knockdown ceilings give the home a fresh look. The kitchen features new cabinets and quartz countertops, and the baths have new quartz countertops as well. The primary bath has a tub/shower with a new tile surround, and the hall bath features a large tiled shower. Updates also include code-compliant electrical work and all-new plumbing throughout. A small room with its own side entrance could serve as a home office. Also listed for sale, MLS #682336. Tenant-buyer responsible for minor day-to-day upkeep. All applicants screened as future buyers (income, employment, credit). Pets OK with an additional annual fee of $350. Inquire for option terms. Available December 1, 2026.
