Property Description

Situated on 14.2 acres between Andover and Augusta, this barndominium is a standout property that has to be seen in person to be fully appreciated. The home delivers 2,904 square feet of quality living space, anchored by a great room with elevated ceilings and room to spare. The kitchen features a pantry that rivals some bedrooms in size. Mini-split units throughout the home offer precise, room-by-room climate control. The 1,000 sq ft attached garage handles vehicles and projects with ease, while a separate additional garage provides dedicated storage for lawnmowers, boats, and UTVs. Out back, a large patio overlooks a beautiful pond, making the outdoor living just as impressive as the inside. The crown jewel for the hobbyist or entrepreneur is the 60'x40' shop — built with two 14' overhead doors, 15' sidewalls, and its own bathroom. A truly unique offering with something for everyone.
