Property Description

WELCOME TO YOUR OWN BACKYARD RETREAT! Tucked away on a spacious .39-acre cul-de-sac lot in West Wichita, this distinctive quad-level home offers an exceptional combination of comfortable living, flexible spaces, and outdoor entertaining. Featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and approximately 2,667 finished square feet, this property has plenty to offer both inside and out! Step inside to discover an inviting living room featuring beautiful luxury vinyl flooring and a striking stone fireplace. Just a few steps away, the spacious kitchen is designed for gathering, complete with granite countertops, an island with eating bar, stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, and an adjoining dining area. Whether hosting a dinner party or enjoying a casual evening at home, this layout offers wonderful versatility. The upper level features a generous primary suite with a private bathroom, double sinks, granite countertops, and a separate shower. Additional bedrooms provide comfortable accommodations, while the lower-level family room offers another inviting gathering space with a second fireplace. Need room to work from home, pursue hobbies, or simply spread out? The partially finished basement includes a dedicated office, additional storage, and a concrete storm room. NOW, LET'S TALK ABOUT THE BACKYARD! Step outside to discover an outdoor retreat designed for making memories. The expansive deck provides plenty of space for entertaining, outdoor dining, or relaxing while overlooking the in-ground swimming pool. Mature trees create a beautiful backdrop, while the sports court offers even more opportunities for outdoor recreation. From summer pool parties to quiet evenings under the trees, this backyard is truly something special! Recent improvements include a NEW ROOF, NEW GUTTERING, NEW POOL LINER, AND FRESH EXTERIOR PAINT! Additional amenities include an attached oversized two-car garage, sprinkler system, and irrigation well. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, and everyday amenities, this home offers the space to spread out, the features to entertain, and a backyard you'll look forward to enjoying.
