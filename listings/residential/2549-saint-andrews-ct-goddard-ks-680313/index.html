Property Description

Tucked away on a cul-de-sac in Goddard, this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers inviting gathering spaces, a functional layout, and room to spread out both inside and out! The main living room features a two-way fireplace shared with the kitchen hearth room, creating a cozy connection between the spaces. A formal dining room adds the perfect setting for hosting holidays and special occasions, while the open kitchen offers plenty of storage and an adjoining dining area for everyday meals. Enjoy a little sunshine in every season in the enclosed sunroom, or step outside to the large fenced backyard with a concrete patio, ready for outdoor dining, entertaining, and relaxing. The main floor features a spacious primary bedroom with an en suite bathroom complete with dual sinks, a garden tub, and a separate shower, plus two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom. Downstairs, the finished basement expands your living and entertaining options with a large family/rec room and wet bar, making it an ideal spot for movie nights, game tables, or watching the big game. An additional bedroom, full bathroom, and bonus room provide even more flexibility for guests, hobbies, a home office, or extra living space. Completing the package is a 3-car attached garage with plenty of room for vehicles, storage, and more. With generous living space, multiple gathering areas, and a cul-de-sac location, this Goddard home is ready for its next chapter!
