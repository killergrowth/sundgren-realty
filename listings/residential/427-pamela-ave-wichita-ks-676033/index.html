Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully remodeled home in the desirable Westlink neighborhood, offering over 3,000 square feet of thoughtfully updated living space on a spacious 0.36-acre lot. Blending timeless mid-century modern character with today's modern updates, this one-of-a-kind property is truly move-in ready. Inside, you'll find 4 bedrooms plus a non-conforming bedroom in the finished basement, 3 full bathrooms, and a highly functional layout complete with a main-floor laundry room. Nearly every surface has been refreshed with new flooring throughout, fresh interior and exterior paint, and beautifully updated upstairs bathrooms, creating a bright, welcoming feel from the moment you step inside. The kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring stainless steel appliances, a flat-top stove built into the island, and ample prep and storage space. The spacious living room is anchored by a stunning limestone fireplace with a gas starter and wood-burning capability, adding warmth, charm, and a true focal point to the home. Mid-century details continue throughout, including the original stained-glass front storm door, preserving the home's unique architectural character. Additional updates include several newer windows and a new impact-resistant roof installed in 2023. Step outside to your own private backyard oasis! The fully fenced backyard features an inground swimming pool complete with a safety gate and a new pool pump installed in 2024, making it ready for summer enjoyment. The oversized lot also offers an exterior storage shed, providing plenty of room for lawn equipment, pool supplies, and extra storage. Located in the established Westlink neighborhood, you'll enjoy convenient access to shopping, dining, parks, and major highways while coming home to a property that offers the perfect balance of character, space, and modern updates. With over 3,000 square feet, a finished basement, thoughtful renovations, timeless mid-century style, and an incredible backyard retreat, this is a home you won't want to miss.
