Property Description

Rare live/work opportunity on N Broadway! Three addresses sell together: a fully gated brick residence, a fenced commercial lot with a large shop and brick outbuilding, and a paved parking lot. The property is zoned General Commercial. The 4 bed, 3 bath home sits behind mature trees and a fenced yard. A covered front porch opens to a great room with a fireplace, exposed wood beams and matching wood trim. The main floor has a bedroom with French doors that works well as a home office, plus a kitchen with hardwood cabinets, granite counters and tile. A second bedroom, a full bath and a breakfast room complete the level. The finished basement adds a second full kitchen, a large dining area, two more bedrooms, a full bath and a mechanical/laundry room. Upstairs, a flexible bonus level with its own bath can be an office, a game room or a primary suite. Outside you'll find a private, landscaped tropical-style backyard, a driveway that fits four cars and a detached garage. The commercial side offers paved parking, a brick office building and a shop with 12'+ overhead doors, ready for your business, fleet or startup. Live and work in one place, with easy Broadway access for your customers. Schedule your private showing today!
