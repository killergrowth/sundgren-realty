Property Description

Get excited to see this spacious ranch style home! Outside you will notice the 2-car garage, sodded front yard, and front steps that can easily be adapted to a ramp. The big back yard is fenced with a nice storage shed, big open deck, and a large, covered deck with a hot tub. The lot is almost 1/2 acre with a sprinkler system on separate meter. Come inside to 4 Large bedrooms, 2 full baths, big living, dining and family rooms, a BIG kitchen, laundry room and more - all on the main level!!! The living room, kitchen, family room, and hallways have beautiful hardwood floors, lots of windows, & stunning light fixtures. This BIG kitchen features tons of cabinets, drawers, and butcher block counter tops. There's a pantry, eating bar, stainless steel range, dishwasher, microwave, and hood, too. The family room has a gas fireplace with a customized TV area above mantel. The recently renovated bathrooms may be your favorite thing - they are beautiful! There's Zero Entry tiled showers, rain shower heads with handheld hoses, combo vent/lights and tiled floors in both. The front bath has custom built cabinets, double sinks, lighted mirrors, lovely lighted ceiling fan, pocket door and is completely handicapped accessible! There is another bedroom in the basement, more storage and even a second laundry room with a sink. Comfort is maintained by a high efficiency HVAC unit (October 2019), a whole house attic fan, 18" of insulation in the attic and a separate HVAC unit (Furnace December 2020) for the basement and rear of the home. Home warranty from Choice Home Warranty will be provided with a full price contract. Come see this great home and make it your own! You don't want to miss this one!!
