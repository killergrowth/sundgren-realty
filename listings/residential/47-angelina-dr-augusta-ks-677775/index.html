About This Property

Location, location, location!!! Incredible one-owner lakefront home with outstanding curb appeal in peaceful Lakewood Estates! This 4 bedroom / 2.5 bath home features a number of recent updates and improvements and sits just steps away from Augusta Lake! What does this mean for you? It means you can walk out your back door to fish… or grab a kayak or SUP from your garage and be on the lake the next minute… or simply enjoy the view of the water as you settle in for a serene meal or drink on your back deck! The deck and pergola roof are constructed from vintage redwood boards, and there’s a dedicated space and electrical hookups for your hot tub. Other exterior amenities include the sprinkler system and landscape irrigation, irrigation well, gardening and riding mower storage room behind the garage, beautiful mature trees, new driveway, and brand new roof! Inside the home you’ll notice newer whirlpool kitchen appliances, new carpet in the basement family room and bedrooms, and beautiful Pella windows. Stay cozy and comfortable on cold nights with the gas living room fireplace and the woodburning fireplace in the basement family room. The formal dining room, breakfast nook, and eating bar in the kitchen give you lots of options for meals; but your favorite place will inevitably be the back deck as the weather begins to cool! Properties on this street next to the lake don’t come up very often. Schedule your showing today for the opportunity to make this home yours!
