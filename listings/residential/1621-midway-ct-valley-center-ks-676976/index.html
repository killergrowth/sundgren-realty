Property Description

** Final Stages 9-22-26** Welcome to this beautiful new construction home by Superior Homes in the desirable Prairie Lakes addition! This thoughtfully designed zero-entry ranch offers 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a spacious 3-car garage with 1,254 square feet of stylish living space. The open-concept floor plan features luxury vinyl flooring, vaulted ceilings, and a bright living room with an electric fireplace, creating the perfect space for relaxing or entertaining. The kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, a large island, walk-in pantry, stainless steel appliances, and a functional kitchen/dining combination. The private primary suite includes granite countertops in the en-suite bathroom and a spacious walk-in shower. Additional features include a separate main-floor laundry room, covered patio, sprinkler system, humidifier, and builder warranty. Located in the growing Prairie Lakes community with neighborhood jogging paths and easy access to schools, shopping, and major highways.
