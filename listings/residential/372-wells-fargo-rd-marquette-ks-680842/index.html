Property Description

*Beautiful Country Retreat* This breathtaking 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom, 2 Car Garage, 3 Acre country gem is a vision of “Pride of Ownership”. Throughout the entire home, the updates, care, and love for the home is apparent in every room. The main floor is complete with spacious living space with gas fireplace, dining area with sparkling bay window, and an updated kitchen area that you will love! Also on the main floor is a spacious Master Bedroom Suite with walk in shower, soaker tub, double vanities, and large walk-in closet. Lastly for the main floor, there’s an additional guest bedroom, guest bathroom, and spacious Mud Room with main floor laundry and pantry space as well! When you move down to the basement, you’ll find a vast Rec Room area where the possibilities are endless, as well as 2 more additional bedrooms, bathroom, and vast storage spaces! You will fall in love with the breathtaking Country Views from the amazing wrap around porch area! One can also escape to the 30’ x 40’ insulated shop area, complete with 220 outlet, gas furnace during the winter and attic fan to bring in the cool fall/spring air! Last but not least, the property also houses and stunning 6 Stall Horse Stable with double doors in each stall and corrals. This property truly is a one-of-a-kind opportunity! Don’t miss you chance to see this country retreat!
