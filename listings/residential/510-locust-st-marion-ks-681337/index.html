Property Description

Welcome to 510 N Locust in Marion, KS! Priced at $179,900, this charming 1.5-story home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and over 2,400 sq. ft. of space! The spacious living room features beautiful wood beams and a wood-burning fireplace, with a formal dining room just off the main living area. You'll also find plenty of original built-ins and storage space throughout the home. The primary suite is conveniently located on the main level, while upstairs you'll find two generously sized bedrooms. The unfinished basement offers even more storage and the potential for additional living space in the future. Outside, enjoy the huge backyard, deck, and backyard bar area, perfect for entertaining or relaxing! Located on a quiet, low-traffic street near Marion schools, historic downtown, and local parks, with Marion County Lake and Marion Reservoir just a short drive away. **Big-ticket updates include a roof that's only one year old and an HVAC unit replaced in 2019!** The dishwasher, microwave, and garage refrigerator will remain with the home. A pre-listing inspection has already been completed, with the report attached in the MLS for buyer review. With plenty of character, room to spread out, and a backyard that's hard to beat, this home has so much to offer!
