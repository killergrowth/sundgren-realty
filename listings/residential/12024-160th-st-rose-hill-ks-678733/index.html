Property Description

Country living meets convenience in this beautifully remodeled 3-bedroom, 2-bath farmhouse situated on 4 acres! Just one mile off blacktop this home offers 1,692 square feet of thoughtfully updated living space, this property combines the charm and character of a farmhouse with the functionality for everyday living. Inside, you’ll find large bedrooms, an inviting open living and dining area complete with a wood burning fireplace, and a spacious kitchen with plenty of room to make it your own. Conveniently placed electrical outlets in the center of the kitchen make adding an island an easy option. Just beyond the kitchen is a large bonus room that previously served as an oversized pantry, with space for a large freezer, but could easily be transformed into an office, hobby room, or additional storage. Adjacent to this room is an exceptionally large laundry and mud room, offering plenty of space for everyday country living. The spacious primary suite is ready for a king-sized bed, features a large walk-in closet and private bathroom with a walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms are ready for children or guests. A small basement area beneath the front of the home provides valuable storage, houses utilities, and offers a designated storm-shelter space. The attached carport is long enough to accommodate an extended SUV and provides the perfect covered area for unloading groceries or passengers without worrying about the weather. For those who need room to work, create, or store equipment, the oversized insulated 4-car shop is a standout feature. The shop offers a fully concrete floor, 220V power, welder outlets, an air compressor with compressor hose lines run throughout the shop, and hopscotch wiring designed to help prevent circuit overload. It is also set up for the installation of a wood-burning stove, making this an incredibly functional workspace year-round. The property is currently served by a private well, while rural water and the meter are already located on the property, providing the opportunity to run rural water to the home if desired. Outside, the 4 acres offer plenty of room for hobbies, gardening, livestock, recreation, or simply enjoying the peace and space of country living. The east side of the property features established asparagus plants and apple trees that produce an abundance of apples in the fall. The west side, accessible just west of the driveway, lies the majority of the acreage and includes a shooting range. A large, open front porch is the perfect place to relax in the evenings, visit with neighbors and friends, and enjoy the slower pace of rural living. If you’re looking for a move-in-ready farmhouse with acreage, a phenomenal shop, room for livestock and hobbies, and the opportunity to make the property your own, this one deserves a look!
