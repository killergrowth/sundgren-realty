Property Description

Timeless charm meets beautifully restored, modern-day living! Thoughtfully renovated from top to bottom, this stunning home blends the character of its original design with the comfort and peace of mind of extensive modern upgrades. The beautifully redesigned kitchen features gorgeous quartz countertops, KitchenAid appliances, and newly installed hardwood flooring that seamlessly complements the meticulously restored original hardwoods throughout the home. No detail has been overlooked, with all-new plumbing throughout, updated lighting, and new grounded electrical outlets and switches. Major improvements include a brand-new HVAC system, water heater, and Class III roof, providing lasting value and peace of mind for years to come. Foundation warranty conveys! With four spacious bedrooms, two generously sized bathrooms, and three expansive living areas, this home offers an abundance of room to entertain, relax, and grow. An oversized storage room provides exceptional functionality, along with additional space offering exciting possibilities for future expansion. Step outside to enjoy the beautifully designed, expansive covered back patio, perfect for entertaining or simply unwinding while overlooking the fully fenced backyard. Every corner of this home reflects a thoughtful balance of timeless character, quality craftsmanship, and modern convenience.
