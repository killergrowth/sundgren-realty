Property Description

Welcome to your own peaceful retreat on 4.1 acres in Towanda! Nestled among beautiful mature trees and surrounded by nature, this charming 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers 2,311 square feet of living space, combining country charm, comfortable living, and plenty of room to spread out. Step inside and you'll immediately appreciate the warm and inviting atmosphere, featuring vaulted ceilings with exposed beams, a stunning stone gas fireplace upstairs, and a spacious kitchen complete with a large island that's perfect for gathering with family and friends. One of the kitchen's most charming features is the sink overlooking the property, offering beautiful views of the surrounding landscape and mature trees. Even everyday moments come with a view! The beautifully remodeled bathrooms add a fresh, updated touch while maintaining the home's character and charm. Downstairs, a pellet stove provides an additional source of warmth and creates another cozy space to relax and unwind. Another major highlight is the brand-new roof on the home, offering a valuable recent improvement for the next owners. Step outside onto the spacious, partially covered deck and take in the incredible views! Surrounded by mature trees and abundant wildlife, this outdoor space is the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, entertain guests, or simply relax and appreciate the peaceful setting. With 4.1 acres to enjoy, there's plenty of room to explore, unwind, and experience the beauty of country living. Need space for hobbies, projects, equipment, or storage? This property has you covered! The 24x30 north shop features 220-volt electrical service and a new roof, offering a functional workspace with plenty of possibilities. A second outbuilding provides additional versatility with a lean-to area and a 12x30 pull-through space, ideal for equipment, trailers, or extra storage. Additional features include a whole-home humidifier and a singular outdoor spigots with well-water, making outdoor watering convenient. As an added bonus, the property is located within an HOA neighborhood offering residents fishing privileges at two ponds approximately half a mile away! From the beautiful views out the kitchen window to the peaceful evenings spent on the deck, this property offers a setting that's hard to beat. With 2,311 square feet of living space, a brand-new roof on the home, mature trees surrounding the property, two versatile outbuildings, abundant wildlife, and plenty of space to enjoy, this is more than just a home—it's an opportunity to embrace the country lifestyle you've been dreaming of. Come experience it for yourself!
