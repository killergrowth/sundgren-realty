Property Description

A little country and a lot of possibilities! If you've been looking for a property that offers a little more room, a little more flexibility, and a whole lot of possibilities, this one deserves a closer look! Situated on nearly 3.5 acres of beautiful, tree-lined property, this unique two-story home offers approximately 2,600 square feet, 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and a setup that's hard to find. The main level features two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen, comfortable living space, and a generously sized dining room. But what really sets this property apart is the completely self-contained living space upstairs! Featuring its own full kitchen, living area, dining space, laundry, two additional bedrooms, and a full bathroom, the upper level offers incredible flexibility. With access from both inside the main home and its own exterior entrance, the possibilities are endless. Whether you need space for extended family, multigenerational living, guests, or are looking for potential rental income, this setup offers options you won't find just anywhere. A private balcony overlooking the front yard is the perfect finishing touch. Both living spaces share one utility account. The outside is just as appealing, with room to work, play, garden, and enjoy the country setting. The property includes a detached two-car garage, an impressive three-car shop, and even an air-conditioned she shed! You'll also find a chicken coop and a wonderful assortment of established apple, pear, and pecan trees throughout the property. And with additional open acreage, there's plenty of room to dream up your next project, hobby, or outdoor adventure. Whether you're looking for room to spread out, space for family, a place to pursue your hobbies, or simply a property that offers more than the ordinary, this one checks a lot of boxes. Unique properties like this don't come along every day. Come see all the possibilities for yourself!
