Property Description

Imagine coming home to a place that feels comfortable, welcoming, and easy to love! This inviting 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch offers the charm of simple living with thoughtful features that make everyday life a little sweeter. Step inside and enjoy the natural light spilling throughout the spacious living and dining room combination, creating a warm, airy atmosphere perfect for gathering with family and friends or settling in for a quiet evening at home. You'll appreciate the ease of one-level living, updated neutral bathrooms, and a separate laundry room with wonderful pantry space to keep life organized. Just imagine starting your mornings with a cup of coffee on the 11x27 screened-in porch, where the composite decking creates a low-maintenance space to slow down, breathe in the fresh air, and enjoy a little outdoor time. When it's time to tackle the weekend projects, the attached two-car garage offers plenty of convenience. And the extras make this property even more special! A private water well and sprinkler system help keep the lawn looking lush and green, while camper hookups at the rear of the property with alley access offer added flexibility. A bonus storage shed provides even more room for the things you love. Conveniently located near schools and just a short drive to Wichita, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, practicality, and everyday enjoyment. Whether you're gathering around the table, relaxing on the porch, or simply enjoying the ease of a home that feels just right, you'll find plenty of reasons to fall in love! Seller is offering in current condition and will not make repairs. Cash or conventional loans only. Per county, roof was permitted/replaced in 2019.
