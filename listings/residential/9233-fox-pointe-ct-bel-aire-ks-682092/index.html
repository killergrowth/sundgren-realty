Property Description

Stunning brand-new 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhome tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in the heart of Bel Aire! Offering a refreshing change from traditional townhome layouts, this thoughtfully designed floor plan provides an exceptional amount of space, functionality, and flexibility throughout.
