Property Description

Welcome to 7814 W Barrington, a beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath quad-level home offering more than 2,500 square feet of comfortable and versatile living space. Tucked away at the end of a cul-de-sac in Barrington Place, this home has been thoughtfully cared for and is ready for its next owners to make it their own. Inside, the quad-level layout provides plenty of room to spread out while still feeling connected. The main level offers a welcoming living space and a dining area with room for a large table, conveniently located just off the kitchen. All kitchen appliances remain with the home, making move-in that much easier. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious primary suite complete with a private bathroom and walk-in closet, along with additional bedrooms and conveniently located upper-level laundry with its own dedicated closet. The lower level provides a second family room, creating flexible space for movie nights, a playroom, home office, or whatever fits your lifestyle. Built-in storage throughout the home adds even more functionality. The home also features two fireplaces, offering future owners the opportunity to enjoy even more warmth and character. Outside, the spacious yard and cul-de-sac location provide plenty of room to enjoy the outdoors. Major improvements have already been taken care of, including a new HVAC system in 2024 and a new roof in March 2026. With a neutral palette, generous living space, two-car garage, and a layout that can easily adapt as your needs change, this well-maintained home is truly move-in ready.
