Property Description

Welcome to this charming 2 bedroom, 1 bath home located in the highly desired Circle School District. Offering over 1,500 square feet of comfortable living space, this property features both a living room and a family room, giving you the flexibility to create the perfect layout for everyday living and entertaining. The home is finished with durable metal siding and includes a 2-car carport for convenient covered parking. Inside, you’ll appreciate the large laundry/mud room, ideal for storage, organization, and keeping daily messes contained. Step outside to enjoy a screened-in porch, perfect for relaxing in any season. The fenced yard provides space for pets or play, and a backyard shed adds extra storage for tools, hobbies, or outdoor equipment. This property blends space, function, and location - an excellent opportunity in Circle Schools.
