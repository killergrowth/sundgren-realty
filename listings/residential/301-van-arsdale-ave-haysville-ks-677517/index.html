Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully remodeled and inviting Haysville home, perfectly situated on a spacious corner lot! Conveniently located near shopping, dining, businesses, and everyday amenities, this home offers comfort, style, functionality, and plenty of room to enjoy. Start your day or unwind in the evening on the covered front porch, the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the neighborhood. Forget the dated 1950s kitchen and bathroom! This home features a remodeled kitchen and bathroom, offering a fresh, updated feel throughout. The remodeled kitchen is designed with both style and functionality in mind, featuring a convenient coffee bar, pantry, lazy Susan, and utility cubbies to keep everything organized and within easy reach. The spacious family room is filled with natural light and features a wood-burning fireplace and ceiling fan, creating the perfect place to gather, relax, and make memories. Ceiling fans throughout the home add comfort and air circulation. The separate dining room opens through sliding doors to a fantastic patio area, making indoor/outdoor entertaining easy and enjoyable. The primary bedroom offers a private dressing room, providing extra space for clothing, getting ready, and storage. Need parking? This home delivers! The oversized side-entry two-car garage, extended driveway, and pull-through access to the backyard provide plenty of room for vehicles and recreational equipment. There is additional parking for an RV, camper, boat, or other recreational vehicles. A convenient laundry/mudroom is located directly off the garage and includes a second full bathroom, adding even more functionality to this great home. Window coverings stay with the home, adding convenience for the new owners. With its remodeled kitchen and bathroom, covered front porch, ceiling fans, primary bedroom dressing room, corner-lot location, generous parking, and great outdoor space, this is a home you won't want to miss! Bring your buyers and come see this one! It’s ready to impress!
