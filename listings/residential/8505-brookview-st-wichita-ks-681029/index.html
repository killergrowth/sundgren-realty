Property Description

Beautiful 4-Bedroom Home in Tyler’s Landing – Maize South Schools! Welcome to this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home featuring a 3-car garage and an inviting open floor plan perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The main floor boasts beautiful wood flooring, convenient main-floor laundry, and a spacious primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and a relaxing corner jetted tub. The finished basement offers even more living space, including a large recreation room with a wet bar and a bonus room perfect for a home office, workout space, or hobby room. Step outside to enjoy the privacy of a wood-fenced backyard, ideal for relaxing or entertaining, complete with a hot tub. The yard also features a sprinkler system supplied by a private well. Located in the desirable Tyler’s Landing neighborhood and within the Maize South school district, this home offers a wonderful combination of space, comfort, and functionality.
