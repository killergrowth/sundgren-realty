Property Description

Discover the potential in this 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo in Wichita’s unique Treehouse Condominium Addition, located in the desirable Goddard School District. Featuring new flooring throughout and priced with additional updates in mind, this home offers a great opportunity to bring your own style and ideas to life while creating a space that’s truly your own. The open-concept living area features soaring vaulted ceilings and a striking stone wood-burning fireplace, along with an adjoining kitchen and plenty of room to gather and entertain. An upstairs loft adds valuable flexibility and would make a great home office, hobby space, or additional sitting area. Step outside onto the private balcony surrounded by mature trees and enjoy the peaceful, tucked-away setting—with the community pool just steps from your door, making it easy to enjoy a relaxing swim or spend a summer afternoon poolside. Enjoy the convenience of maintenance-free living with an HOA that covers exterior maintenance, lawn care, snow removal, and trash, along with access to the community pool and beautiful green spaces throughout the neighborhood. Whether you’re looking for a home to personalize or an opportunity to add value with some updating, this condo is priced to let you make it your own.
