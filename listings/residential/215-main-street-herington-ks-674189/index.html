Property Description

Built circa 1881, this historic Herington home blends a remarkable piece of local history with extensive modernization. Confirmed to be the former home of Monroe D. Herington, for whom the city is named, the property offers a rare opportunity to own one of the community's earliest residences while benefiting from many major updates. Over the years, the home has undergone substantial updates designed to preserve its character while improving day-to-day functionality. The main level has been extensively renovated and features updated electrical service with a newer panel, updated wiring throughout much of the home, and updated plumbing systems throughout. A tankless water heater adds modern efficiency, while the kitchen and bathrooms have been thoughtfully refreshed with a clean, neutral aesthetic that complements the home's historic charm rather than competing with it. Original architectural details remain a defining feature of the property. Tall ceilings, oversized windows, abundant natural light, and spacious rooms reflect the craftsmanship and proportions often found in homes of this era. The result is a home that honors its history while offering the infrastructure and conveniences expected by today's homeowners. Located approximately 33 miles from Fort Riley, 42 miles from Manhattan, and about 75 miles from Wichita, this property provides a unique combination of accessibility and small-town living. Enjoy the peace and slower pace of Herington while remaining within comfortable driving distance of major employers, Kansas State University, shopping, dining, entertainment, and regional amenities. Whether you're drawn to the home's historical significance, its preserved character, or the extensive updates already completed, 215 W Main Street represents a rare opportunity to own a meaningful piece of Kansas history without sacrificing modern convenience.
