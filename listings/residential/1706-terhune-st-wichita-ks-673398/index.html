Property Description

Welcome to a pristine 4,370 sq ft ranch built by Leewood Homes! Situated on a serene, tree-lined lot with an East-facing backyard, in the coveted Stonebridge neighborhood, this exquisite residence exudes quality throughout. The home's curb appeal is undeniable; the impeccable attention to detail continues upon entry, with white trim and beautiful finishes complementing the interior. The main level features a 3-bedroom split plan, including the popular Jack and Jill bathroom concept with walk-in closets for both bedrooms. Large windows with transoms bathe the interiors in natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. An impressive great room features custom-built cabinets flanking a gorgeous stone fireplace that extends from floor to ceiling. While 3/4" white oak flooring runs throughout the main living areas, the 14-foot coffered ceiling with painted beams enhances the grandeur. Combining functionality and style, the gourmet kitchen offers a large center island/eating bar, a gas range, decorative vent hood, walk-in pantry, and a timeless herringbone-patterned subway tile backsplash. Under-cabinet lighting illuminates the workspaces, while the ample cabinetry offers plenty of storage. Enjoy the formal dining room, bright and airy spaces, two fireplaces, and powder rooms on both levels, a rare amenity. Retreat to the primary suite, showcasing a 16' x 18' bedroom with a soaring vaulted ceiling, deck access, and abundant natural light. The spa-like bath offers unparalleled luxury with a custom-built walk-in shower, expansive double vanity, private toilet closet, and a free-standing tub perfect for relaxation. A spacious walk-in closet conveniently connects to the laundry room, a highly sought-after feature for modern buyers. Entertaining is effortless in the gorgeous basement complete with 9' ceilings, finishes that rival the main level, a stylish bar area with space for a full-sized refrigerator, a glass-door wine closet, family room, fireplace, built-in shelving, two additional bedrooms with walk-in closets, a Jack and Jill bathroom, and storage. Extend your living space outdoors in a private fenced backyard showcasing a covered deck, concrete patio, sprinkler system, and an irrigation well. Leewood Homes stands out from the rest with their smart design, refined finishes, and unparalleled quality construction. Located in Sedgwick County within the Andover School District, this showstopper offers the best of both worlds; don't miss!
