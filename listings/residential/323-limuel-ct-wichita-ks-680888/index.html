Property Description

If you are looking for a move-in ready home in Auburn Hills, this is it!! As you pull up, you will appreciate the meticulously maintained yard, landscaping and the inviting front porch. Once inside, the open floor plan and abundance of natural light set the stage! The kitchen offers many cabinets, plenty of granite countertop space and a pantry. Don't miss the main floor laundry just off the kitchen. The primary bedroom is conveniently situated on the opposite side of the house from the other bedrooms. It will easily accommodate all of your furnishings and boasts a private en suite bathroom featuring double sinks, a whirlpool tub, separate shower and his and her closets! Downstairs you will find the best spot for entertaining! The family room is huge and includes a wet bar. Two more bedrooms, a third full bathroom and a large storage room complete this level. The exterior is just as impressive as the inside. Enjoy your morning coffee or evenings on the oversized deck surrounded by mature trees! The backyard is fully fenced, and there is an irrigation well and sprinkler system to boot! You can't beat this location! Schedule your private showing today!
