About This Property

Welcome to the Haven Bay Plan by Paul Gray Homes, beautifully situated on a corner cul-de-sac lot along Castaway’s 145-acre private boating lake. From the timeless front elevation to the thoughtfully designed lakefront living spaces, this home offers the perfect blend of everyday comfort and resort-style luxury. Step inside to a grand entry with a tray ceiling and dramatic two-story accent wall that highlights the staircase. The spacious living room features a beamed tray ceiling, gas fireplace, arched niches with built-in cabinets and floating shelves, and expansive picture windows that frame sparkling lake views and fill the home with natural light. The kitchen is both elegant and functional, showcasing a 10-foot island, custom-built range hood, abundant cabinetry, and a walk-in pantry tucked behind an 8-foot fluted glass door. The adjoining dining area is surrounded by large picture windows, creating a bright and inviting space for meals and gatherings. A walk-out door leads to the covered lanai with aluminum railing, designed for low-maintenance outdoor living and year-round enjoyment. The luxurious main-level master suite features a spacious bedroom, private bath with full tile shower, dual vanity, and generous walk-in closet with direct access to the laundry room, complete with built-in cabinetry and folding table. Two additional spacious bedrooms, each with their own walk-in closet, and a full hall bath complete the main floor. The dramatic staircase leads to the fully finished walk-out lower level, ideal for entertaining. Enjoy a large family room with a custom arched entertainment niche, plus a stunning wet bar with island, granite countertops, space for a full-size refrigerator, and walk-out access to the covered patio. Two additional bedrooms, including one with a walk-in closet, a full bath, and ample storage complete the lower level. Outdoors, the backyard is a true lakefront oasis with a large yard, private beach, option to add a boat dock, and a concrete storage room beneath the precast concrete lanai—perfect for beach toys, lake gear, and boating essentials. A dedicated half bath accessible from the back patio is ideal for days spent on the beach or out on the boat, adding convenience to every lakefront gathering. Come experience the Castaway lifestyle, where every day feels like a dream vacation at the lake. Some information may be estimated and is not guaranteed. Please verify schools with USD 266.
