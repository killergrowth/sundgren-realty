Property Description

No tricks, just a home full of treats! This thoughtfully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home in the Goddard School District offers a bonus room, multiple living areas, and plenty of character. Vaulted ceilings, vinyl flooring, and stylish accent walls create an inviting feel, while the kitchen features warm wood cabinetry and stainless steel appliances. Downstairs, a spacious family room with a wet bar is ready for movie nights and entertaining. The bonus room offers flexibility for an office, hobbies, or workouts. Enjoy a covered front porch, fenced backyard with mature trees, and attached two-car garage. There’s plenty to fall for at 422 S Limuel!
