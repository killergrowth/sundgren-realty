Auction Date: BIDDING ENDS THURSDAY DECEMBER 10, 2026 4:00 PM * Auction Location: ONLINE ONLY AUCTION! * PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: 3 bedroom 2 bath home with 2268 square feet of finished living area on 4.5 acres PLUS a 60' x 45' shop building near Rose Hill in Butler County, Kansas. New carpet was installed the end of 2022, and the interior of the house was painted this year. The roof, siding and exterior paint is all new as of 2022, and the front porch was installed in 2023. The interior of the house is very clean, functional, and ready to go! There is a storm shelter in the house that can also be used as a vault. The 60' x 45' shop building has a partial concrete floor, heat and air, air compressor included, and 3 phase 240/480 power. The entire east boundary has a tree row, as does part of the west boundary offering a nice element of privacy. In addition the rural water, there is also a functioning water well with a new pump. Blacktop frontage all along the north boundary provides easy and quality access. The balance of the land could be used as hay meadow or potential place to keep animals. Rarely do you find an affordable, quality, rural property with these amenities so take advantage of this great opportunity!
