The Jim James Memorial 101 Club is a nearly 80-acre recreational property with an exceptional variety of buildings, amenities, and potential uses. Located just north of Wichita off 101st Street N. in Valley Center, this one-of-a-kind property provides an incredible opportunity to acquire a large recreational campus with extensive indoor and outdoor facilities. With nearly 80 acres, multiple gathering spaces, and a wide range of recreational amenities already in place, the possibilities for the next owner are endless. Whether preserved as a recreational retreat or transformed into something entirely new, the Jim James Memorial 101 Club offers exceptional space and versatility. Notwithstanding the clearly marked cemetery area northwest of the main fellowship hall on the property, the Seller is not aware of whether the markers are for actual graves or just memorials near trees that have been planted. The Buyer should exercise its own due diligence and become familiar with the legal requirements for maintenance of gravesites on the property. Buyer is receiving the property subject to the right of the surviving spouse to be buried next to her husband at death.
