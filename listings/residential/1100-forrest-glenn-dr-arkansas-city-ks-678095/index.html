About This Property

Looking for a quiet residential neighborhood with plenty of room for your growing family? This move-in-ready ranch is located on a quiet, low-traffic street and offers the space and flexibility of 4 to 5 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. Step inside to a welcoming living room that leads into the kitchen and dining combination featuring beautiful quartz countertops, a crisp white tile backsplash, and sliding glass doors opening to the fenced backyard. The private primary suite is located on one side of the home and features a completely remodeled bathroom with beautiful marble and granite finishes. Two additional bedrooms, a shared bathroom, and convenient main-level laundry to complete the main floor. Downstairs, you’ll find even more finished living space with a spacious family room, fourth bedroom, and third bathroom. An additional finished room is currently being used as a workout room but offers the flexibility to serve as a fifth bedroom, home office, or hobby room. Outside, the home’s approximately 95% brick exterior offers attractive, low-maintenance living, while the fenced backyard provides plenty of space to enjoy the outdoors. With its updated finishes, generous living space, and quiet, low-traffic location, this home is move-in ready and waiting for its new owners! Call listing agent to schedule a personal showing.
