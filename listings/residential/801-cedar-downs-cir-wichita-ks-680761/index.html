Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully designed new-construction patio home, where quality craftsmanship, modern conveniences, and thoughtful upgrades come together! Situated on a desirable corner lot, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers 1,744 square feet of living space, an oversized 821-square-foot, fully finished 3-car garage, and an impressive list of features you won't find in your typical new construction. Step inside to discover soaring 10-foot ceilings, dramatic vaulted ceilings, and an 11-foot ceiling in the primary bedroom, creating an open, spacious atmosphere. The inviting living area features a sleek electric fireplace, beautiful finishes, and an open-concept design that flows seamlessly into the kitchen and dining area. The kitchen showcases beautiful cabinetry, generous counter space, and a spacious walk-in pantry. The primary suite offers a private retreat with a beautifully appointed bathroom and walk-in closet. Additional highlights include custom built-ins, a dedicated laundry room, a sunroom, and abundant storage throughout. The garage is another standout feature! This fully finished, oversized 3-car garage features 8-foot-tall garage doors, a dedicated 220V connection for electric vehicle charging, and a separate 220V connection for an air compressor—perfect for automotive enthusiasts, hobbyists, or anyone needing additional workspace. Built with convenience, efficiency, and peace of mind in mind, this home also includes a dedicated safe room, tankless water heater, high-efficiency HVAC system, and a unique, dedicated Amazon/package delivery room accessible from the front porch. Enjoy the covered front porch, covered rear patio, and the added space of a corner lot. New sod and yard installation are included, making this home even more appealing. From elevated ceilings and custom finishes to an oversized garage and forward-thinking features, this exceptional new-construction home offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and modern living.
