Property Description

The holidays are just around the corner, and this inviting 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath, 2-half-bath home offers the perfect setting for family gatherings, entertaining friends, and creating lasting memories. With 2,090 square feet of living space, a desirable cul-de-sac location, a corner lot, and no HOA, this home has so much to offer! As you arrive, you’ll appreciate the well-maintained yard, sprinkler system, and welcoming curb appeal. Step inside to a spacious 17' x 16'10" living room featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace, luxury vinyl wood flooring, and a vaulted ceiling that opens to the second level, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere. The kitchen measures 13'7" x 12'5" and offers abundant cabinetry and counter space, making meal preparation and holiday cooking a breeze. The oven, range, microwave, dishwasher, and disposal will remain with the home. The kitchen opens to the generous 15'3" x 10' dining room, providing plenty of space for a large dining table. An eating bar offers additional seating, making this layout ideal for entertaining. The main level also includes a convenient laundry room and half bath. Upstairs, you’ll find a spacious 19'5" x 13' primary suite featuring a walk-in closet and private bathroom. A second bedroom measuring 12'6" x 10'4" and an additional full bathroom complete the upper level. The finished basement provides even more room to relax and entertain. Gather in the 16'3" x 15'8" family room, where a second wood-burning fireplace creates the perfect spot to cozy up on chilly winter evenings. This level also includes another bedroom measuring 12' x 11', a half bath, and a large storage room. A large freezer in the basement storage room will also remain with the property. Step through the patio door and discover your own private backyard retreat! The spacious patio is perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply enjoying a quiet evening outdoors. The generously sized yard features mature trees and a 10' x 8' garden shed for additional storage. With two wood-burning fireplaces, multiple gathering spaces, a functional layout, and a private backyard, this home is ready to welcome you for the holidays and beyond! Don’t miss your opportunity to make this wonderful property your new home!
