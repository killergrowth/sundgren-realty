Property Description

Enjoy the best of country living with this custom-built KKI Design-Build home in Grass Valley Estates, located just off a paved road near Garden Plain. Situated on 2 acres, this approximately 1800-square-foot, 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers plenty of space, privacy, and flexibility while remaining close to city conveniences. The open-concept floor plan features a spacious living area and a well-appointed kitchen with a large island and walk-in pantry. The primary suite offers a large bathroom with a tiled walk-in shower, dual sinks, and spacious walk-in closet. Enjoy outdoor living from the covered deck, with 2 acres providing plenty of room to relax, entertain, or add an outbuilding. The property also includes a 3-car garage, and outbuildings are permitted. Additional highlights include no HOA and no specials. Located approximately 15 minutes from Wichita, 5 minutes from Goddard, and just minutes from Garden Plain, with Garden Plain schools. A rare opportunity to enjoy a custom-built home, acreage, and country setting without giving up convenient access to the Wichita area.
