About This Property

Welcome to your own slice of Kansas paradise—where the road winds a little slower, the skies stretch a little wider, and every sunset feels like it was put on just for you. Tucked at the end of a private, tree-lined 1/8-mile driveway, this custom log home is perfectly positioned on 29.44 rolling, fully fenced acres, offering a rare blend of seclusion, serenity, and sweeping prairie views in every direction. Reference MLS #672264 with a different acreage total. Crafted from hand-peeled Western Red Cedar logs sourced from British Columbia and milled in Colorado, the home is a true work of art—rich in character, warmth, and enduring quality. Step inside and you’re immediately drawn to the breathtaking great room, where soaring 22-foot vaulted ceilings of exposed whole logs create a sense of grandeur, while a striking stone fireplace anchors the space with cozy charm. Sunlight pours through expansive windows, filling the home with a golden glow and framing the natural beauty that surrounds you. Outside, the land unfolds with endless opportunity. Whether you're watching horses graze in the pasture, tending to livestock, or simply enjoying the quiet rhythm of country life, this property is designed to support it all. Thoughtfully outfitted with metal fencing, cross-fenced sections, and multiple outbuildings, it includes a 30x45 metal barn with loft space and a large pole barn complete with horse stalls, tack room, and feeding setup—everything you need for a working ranch or hobby farm. A durable metal roof adds peace of mind, while the combination of tillable ground and open grassland offers flexibility for a variety of agricultural pursuits. Here, you’ll find the perfect balance of rustic elegance and practical function—a place where mornings begin with fresh air and open horizons, and evenings end under a canopy of stars. Whether you’re seeking a peaceful retreat, a legacy property, or a place to live out your rural lifestyle dreams, this exceptional offering delivers it all—just minutes from the conveniences of town.
