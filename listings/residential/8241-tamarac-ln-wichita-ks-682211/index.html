Property Description

Space, updates, and endless possibilities come together in this 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home with a 2-car attached garage! Step inside to discover a fresh, updated feel with brand-new LVP flooring throughout the main living areas and new carpet in the bedrooms. The spacious kitchen offers plenty of room to cook, gather, and entertain, with convenient access to both the living room and formal dining room. A true highlight of the home is the oversized sunroom, offering a beautiful space to enjoy natural light year-round. Whether you envision a cozy sitting area, indoor garden, entertaining space, or your favorite spot for morning coffee, the possibilities are endless!
