Property Description

BEAUTIFUL home with many updates and NO SPECIALS! This 4 bedroom, 3 bath Bi-level home is located in a quiet, established west central neighborhood, offers a great 2 car attached garage and is ready to move in. The living room is not only spacious but provides lots of natural lighting from the huge front windows, a vaulted ceiling and it opens up onto the wonder full back deck, which is perfect for cookouts. enjoyable entertaining, The kitchen provides a wonderful layout, lots of cabinet space, a great eating bar and a full complement of appliances. Two of the bedrooms are located on this level, including the very spacious master, which features wood laminate flooring, 2 closets and an attached bath for added convenience. Another full bathroom completes this upper level. The lower level is just as fantastic and offers a huge family room with a cozy fireplace for chilly evenings at home, wood flooring Two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, a large laundry area (with folding table & sink), as well as tons of storage, can all be found on this lower level. Outside is so fantastic that you may not want to go back inside! Looking down from the covered deck you will find a fabulous patio (under a pergola) that is perfect for relaxing after a long day, a privacy fenced back yard and beautiful mature trees for shade and privacy. This is truly an oasis for just you, or a wonderful entertaining space to share with others. This home is move in ready and just waiting for you! Set up your appointment before it is gone!
