Property Description

Welcome to 7421 S Pattie! Situated on nearly an acre, this spacious 4-bedroom, 2-bath home offers 1,960 square feet of living space with TWO separate living areas, providing plenty of room to spread out! This 2000 Spirit manufactured home sits on a county-approved permanent concrete foundation. The property has undergone numerous major improvements, professionally completed by local contractors, including: Electrical service and updated panel: Elite Electric – February 2025 Septic system: Girrens Excavating – October 2025 HVAC system: Honest HVAC – November 2025 Private well: Bearden Pump & Well – September 2025 Foundation footings: Mobile Home Services – April 2024 Skirting, gravel driveway and interior improvements: Weaver Construction – September 2025 The septic system also includes a one-year warranty from the date of installation! Inside, you'll find new flooring in the kitchen, utility room and primary bathroom, along with new carpet in the living room and primary bedroom. While the home could use a little TLC and some finishing touches, many of the major improvements have already been completed, giving the next owner a great opportunity to add their own personal touch. With nearly an acre to enjoy, spacious living areas and significant infrastructure improvements already completed, this property offers great potential for those looking for extra space and a place to make their own!
