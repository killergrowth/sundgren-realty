Property Description

LOCATION, LIFESTYLE & PRIDE OF OWNERSHIP! Welcome to this exceptional home in highly sought-after Sterling Farms, located in the Maize School District! With over 3,100 sq. ft. of living space, 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and an incredible amount of storage, this home offers the space and functionality you’ve been looking for. The location is hard to beat — situated directly across from the neighborhood lake, playground and pool, you can relax on your back deck while keeping an eye on the kids enjoying all the amenities right outside your door. Mature landscaping and a wrought-iron fence add to the charm and privacy of the beautifully maintained yard. Inside, the pride of ownership truly shines. The updated primary suite is a standout, featuring a beautifully refreshed en suite, heated floors, and a spacious walk-in closet. The main floor offers a formal dining room, convenient laundry, and a welcoming hearth room with a charming loft area that could easily serve as a home office, reading nook, or playroom. The kitchen is enhanced by additional accent lighting, with more lighting featured above the hallway for a warm and inviting atmosphere. And storage? You’ll love the enormous storage room that provides plenty of space for everything from seasonal décor to household necessities. The 3-car garage offers even more functionality, including a rear garage door on the third stall — perfect for lawn equipment, bikes, toys, or simply providing convenient backyard access. Add in a brand-new roof, fantastic neighborhood amenities, an ideal location, and meticulous care throughout, and you’ll quickly see why this one is special. A home that truly has it all — space, style, convenience and pride of ownership!
