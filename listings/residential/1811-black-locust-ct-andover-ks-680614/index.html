Property Description

Beautifully updated lakeview home in the Andover School District! Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac lot, this stunning 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers gorgeous lake views, modern updates, and an inviting open-concept floor plan designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Freshly updated with new flooring, gorgeous new lighting, quartz countertops, and stainless steel appliances, this home is truly move-in ready! The spacious living and dining areas flow seamlessly into the beautifully updated kitchen, while the back deck provides the perfect setting to relax, entertain, and take in the peaceful lake views. The primary suite offers a private retreat with an ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet. The finished view-out basement provides even more living space with a generous recreation room, two additional guest bedrooms, and plenty of room for entertaining. With its beautiful updates, desirable cul-de-sac location, and picturesque waterfront setting, this exceptional home offers the perfect combination of comfort, style, and lakeside living!
