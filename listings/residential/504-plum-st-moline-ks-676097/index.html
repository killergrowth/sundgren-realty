Property Description

Welcome to 504 N. Plum Street in Moline, where over a century of exceptional craftsmanship blends beautifully with the updates and conveniences today's buyers appreciate. This lovingly maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bath home showcases the character of a bygone era while offering the peace of mind that comes with many important improvements already completed. Situated on a spacious, shaded corner lot, you'll immediately be drawn to the home's inviting covered front and back porches. Whether you're sipping your morning coffee on the back porch, relaxing with a good book on the charming front porch, or entertaining friends and family outdoors, these welcoming spaces are sure to become favorite gathering spots. Step inside and discover the timeless beauty of original hardwood floors, stunning natural woodwork, solid wood doors, and classic architectural details that reflect the quality craftsmanship rarely found today. The spacious living room flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, creating an ideal layout for everyday living and entertaining. The main-floor primary bedroom offers added convenience and flexibility, while the remaining three generously sized bedrooms upstairs provide plenty of room for family, guests, or a home office. A rare bonus is that every bedroom features a walk-in closet, offering exceptional storage throughout the home. Upstairs also features a full bathroom complete with a charming vintage soaking tub that perfectly complements the home's historic appeal. The cozy kitchen is perfectly sized for preparing family meals and holiday baking and includes a comfortable breakfast nook for casual dining. All kitchen appliances stay, including the refrigerator, range/oven, microwave, and dishwasher, along with the washer and dryer, making this home truly move-in ready. Beyond its charm, this home has been thoughtfully updated with many major improvements. The HVAC was replaced in July 2023 along with new foam insulation in the attic. The roof is approximately 8 years old, the windows were replaced about 15 years ago, and the home has been completely rewired with updated electrical and features new plumbing, offering added confidence for its next owner. A 2-year-old on-demand (tankless) water heater provides energy-efficient hot water, and the oversized detached garage includes a garage door opener along with plenty of space for vehicles, storage, or hobbies. If you've been searching for a home that combines historic character with modern updates, quality construction, generous storage, and move-in-ready convenience, 504 N. Plum is the one you've been waiting for.
