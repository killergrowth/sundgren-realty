Property Description

Welcome home to this beautiful property in the desirable Rainbow Lakes neighborhood! Offering an impressive insulated three-car garage PLUS an additional workshop, this home combines comfortable living, thoughtful upgrades, and plenty of space for entertaining. Step inside to discover an inviting open floor plan featuring a two-way fireplace shared between the living room and hearth-style kitchen. Natural sunlight fills the living room through a large picture window overlooking the backyard. The spacious kitchen boasts a large granite island, 10 ft. ceilings, and sliding glass doors leading to an expansive covered composite deck complete with optional roll-down shades. Whether entertaining friends or enjoying a quiet morning coffee, this outdoor space is sure to become a favorite! Just off the deck, you'll find a generous backyard with an additional patio, perfect for grilling and gathering around a fire pit. The view-out basement offers even more living and entertaining space, featuring a large recreation room with a second two-way fireplace shared with the wet bar area. You'll also appreciate the additional fourth room, playroom or office, along with a full bathroom. The list of upgrades and extras is impressive! Features include an irrigation well with pressure tank, expandable to accommodate additional outdoor hose bibs; Cat 7 cabling; 18 SEER inverter-driven air conditioning; 97% modulating gas furnace with communicating system and Wi-Fi thermostat; 50-gallon water heater; and a new 60-amp auxiliary garage panel with a 220-volt outlet for a welder or additional A/C. The insulated three-car garage is further enhanced by an additional 14 x 11 workshop, expanding the third stall and providing excellent space for hobbies, projects, or extra storage. Located in the beautiful, well-established Rainbow Lakes neighborhood, this home offers the perfect combination of space, functionality, and desirable amenities.
