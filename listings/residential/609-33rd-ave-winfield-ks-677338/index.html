Property Description

Beautiful Custom-Built Home on a Large Lot – 5 Bedrooms, 2.5 Baths, Updated & Move-In Ready This stunning custom-built 5 bedroom, 2.5 bath home offers modern updates, an open floor plan, and exceptional space inside and out. Situated on a huge lot in a quiet, friendly neighborhood, this property is truly move-in ready. You’ll love the inviting curb appeal, well-maintained landscaping, and relaxing outdoor spaces—from the charming front porch to the peaceful back deck. Inside, abundant natural daylight showcases the updated kitchen featuring granite countertops, wood floors, and a convenient eating bar that opens to the living room with a cozy fireplace—perfect for entertaining or everyday living. A dedicated office provides an ideal workspace. The spacious primary bedroom includes a private ensuite with an impressive walk-in shower and dual sinks. Four bedrooms are located on one level, with an additional bedroom in the finished basement—an ideal layout for many lifestyles. The newly updated basement offers a large family room with beautifully finished floors, creating even more space to relax or entertain. Outside, the fully fenced backyard features mature trees that provide shade, privacy, and a serene setting to enjoy morning coffee or unwind at the end of the day.
