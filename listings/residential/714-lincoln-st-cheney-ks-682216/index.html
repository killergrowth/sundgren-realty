Property Description

Welcome home to 714 N Lincoln Street, located in the heart of small-town Cheney, Kansas! This spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers the perfect combination of thoughtful updates, functional living spaces, and room to spread out, all within walking distance of local schools! Step inside to discover beautiful new luxury vinyl flooring throughout the main level, ample storage, and a beautifully updated kitchen featuring a Bosch dishwasher and appliances that remain with the home. The generously sized primary bedroom offers a private attached bathroom, creating a comfortable retreat. A huge mudroom with convenient main-floor laundry adds everyday functionality and plenty of space to stay organized. Downstairs, the finished basement provides additional living space along with TWO bonus rooms, offering endless possibilities for a home office, hobby room, workout space, or whatever fits your lifestyle! Outside, you'll love the large yard, expansive deck perfect for entertaining, two storage sheds, and a sprinkler system serviced by a well. A one-car garage provides additional parking and storage. To top it all off, this home comes with a home warranty for added peace of mind! With its inviting spaces, fantastic updates, and convenient location in the charming community of Cheney, this home has so much to offer. Come see it for yourself!
