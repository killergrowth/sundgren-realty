Property Description

2515 Fairway Circle has quite possibly the most impressive lot in Longview at The Oaks and sets deep at the top of a cul-de-sac. A spacious ranch layout that sits on nearly 1/2 ACRE and backs to the 10th Hole on the golf course. Better than new, this property has NO specials, a 3-year-NEW Class 4 Roof, as well as a 3-year-NEW Lennox HVAC system. The main living area boasts newly-refinished, GORGEOUS hardwood floors. Enjoy the large front porch, and a beautiful view from nearly every window. The most unique and newer space in the home is the AMAZING sunroom right off the living room - it is not every day that we see a home like this one; so many spaces to enjoy, no matter the season. Split bedroom floor plan with large rooms and excellent closet spaces. Formal dining room off the foyer can also provide another flexible second living space. The spaciousness and easy flow of the kitchen leads to another more casual dining space with views of the course and green spaces. Granite counter tops, upgraded appliances include a gas range, large walk-in pantry and extensive cabinets, including a beautiful built-in cabinet display - another excellent spot for hosting gatherings. The master suite holds bathroom with plenty of natural light and an impressive walk-in closet with seasonal clothing rack organization. The mid-level walk out leads to a sprawling back patio, complete with a private corner for hot tub placement, and a very nice storage shed. This basement is complete with a spacious wet bar, two additional 15' x 15' bedrooms, large view out windows throughout, and storage spaces complete with shelving. This gorgeous and unique home always provides AMAZING sunset views on the sprawling back patio and the very best of outdoor living! Don't let this one pass you by...
