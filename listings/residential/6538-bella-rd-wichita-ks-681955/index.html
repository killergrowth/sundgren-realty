Property Description

Welcome home! Lovingly cared for by its original owner, this custom one-story home blends traditional and timeless architecture on 1.17 acres framed by mature trees. You’ll notice the curb appeal right away: a welcoming courtyard, classic brick exterior, an expansive deck for outdoor living, and a detached garage. Step inside to soaring windows, including bay groupings, graceful arched doorways, stone fireplaces, custom built-ins, and rich craftsman woodwork. The open kitchen is made for gathering, with a freestanding island, a six-seat eating bar, and a desk nook that’s ready to become your coffee bar. Host holidays in the generous dining room, which seats up to fourteen. Main-floor bedrooms include walk-in closets and built-in desks. The primary suite is a true retreat, with a soaking tub, separate shower, dual vanities, a private dressing room, and a large walk-in closet with built-ins. The oversized laundry room makes daily chores easier, with abundant counter space, storage, and a full-size sink. Downstairs is built for entertaining. It has a game room, wet bar, play nook, and a spacious family room with a built-in entertainment center. Two more bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bath, and there’s also a powder room, plenty of storage, and a storm room. Zoned HVAC, dual water heaters, and well-maintained mechanicals keep the home comfortable and efficient. Set in the established Rio Vista Estates neighborhood, just steps from the Arkansas River, this home offers charm, space, and a place to make lasting memories.
