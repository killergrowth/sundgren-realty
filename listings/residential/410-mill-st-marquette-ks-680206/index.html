Property Description

One-of-a-kind Marquette treasure! Nestled along the edge of town with fields and nature to the south, this beautiful bungalow sits on nearly 3 acres of shaded, peaceful property. Rich in history, character and charm, this home has been lovingly maintained and thoughtfully remodeled by local carpenter John Johnson, with beautiful craftsmanship throughout. The main floor features a living room, mudroom, main-floor laundry, fireplace room, kitchen with breakfast nook, bedroom and full bath. Covered porches wrap three sides of the home, offering peaceful places to enjoy morning coffee, afternoon shade or quiet evenings while taking in the beautiful setting and views from every window. The porches may become your favorite part of this special home! Recent improvements include a new roof, new paint and stain on the siding, and new Anderson windows throughout. Upstairs are 3 additional bedrooms, fabulous storage and another full bath. The finished basement is an incredible bonus with a full kitchenette, family room, 2 office spaces, additional storage, bathroom and walkout access to the outdoors. Mature trees, open views and nearly 3 acres provide a private country feel while remaining close to town. A truly special home with timeless character, thoughtful updates and a setting that simply cannot be duplicated. This is more than a house—it is a home with a story, a history and a warmth you can feel the moment you arrive!
