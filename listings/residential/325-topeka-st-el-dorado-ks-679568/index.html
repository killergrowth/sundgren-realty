Property Description

Welcome to 325 N Topeka, a home that makes a beautiful first impression and keeps getting better. This two bedroom, one bath bungalow has the bright, easy charm of a sunny California home at a Kansas price. Beautiful landscaping and brand new concrete in the front lead you to an inviting porch, perfect for slow mornings with coffee. Inside, sunlight pours through new windows, and every room feels fresh, polished, and absolutely pristine. It looks beautifully staged, but it isn’t. The owner has simply cared for it that well. Granite countertops and main floor laundry add everyday ease, while a new roof, new mini-split system, and brand-new sewer line show the attention given to the home beyond what you can see. Out back, the new concrete continues with a gorgeous patio overlooking the privacy fenced yard. There’s also a two car garage and an unfinished basement that offers additional space and a place to go during severe weather. Whether you’re looking for your first home, ready to downsize, or exploring its potential as an Airbnb or VRBO, there’s so much to love here. The owner is proud of the care she’s put into 325 N Topeka, and I’m proud to show it. Schedule your private showing with me today!
