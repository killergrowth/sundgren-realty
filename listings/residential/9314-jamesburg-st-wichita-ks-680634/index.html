Property Description

Welcome to this well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch in Jamesburg Park! With 1,314 square feet on the main level plus additional finished space in the basement, this home offers room to spread out and make it your own. Enjoy its established character today while adding your personal touches and updates over time. Sunset Park is visible from the home, putting outdoor recreation just a short distance away! The basement features a generously sized finished family room, another large unfinished room with possibilities for hobbies, recreation, or additional living space, and a framed area with an egress window offering potential for a fourth bedroom. Rough-in plumbing is also in place for a future third bathroom, providing a head start on expanding the home’s finished space. All appliances stay including washer and dryer. Nice storage shed in backyard stays as well. Newer HVAC (4 years old) and water heater (2 years old). Conveniently located near west Wichita shopping, dining, and everyday amenities in the Maize school district, this home is ready for its next chapter. Schedule your showing today!
