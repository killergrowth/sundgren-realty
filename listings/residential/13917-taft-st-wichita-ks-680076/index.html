Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully updates 5 bedroom, 3 bath home in Goddard Schools! It has been freshly painted inside and out and complemented by carpet installed approximately one year ago. Rich wood floors and finishes set an elegant tone throughout. The open concept kitchen with eating bar flows into the dining area and out to the Timber Tech deck, where you’ll enjoy a peaceful view of the pond and fountain just steps away. Built in speakers throughout the home — and out on the deck — make everyday living and entertaining effortless. The spacious primary suite features surround sound, calming water views, dual sinks, a jetted tub, and a beautifully tiled shower. The finished basement is designed for gathering, offering custom built ins, included TV and speakers, a wet bar and walk out access to a possible hot tub area (already wired and ready). An irrigation well adds convenience and efficiency to outdoor maintenance. This home is stunning and completely move-in ready!!!!
