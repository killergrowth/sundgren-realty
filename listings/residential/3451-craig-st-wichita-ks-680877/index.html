Property Description

So much square footage and endless possibilities! This spacious home features an open-concept main living area filled with natural light and a cozy fireplace. The HUGE kitchen offers abundant counter space, plenty of cabinetry, and a large pantry, making it perfect for cooking and entertaining. A generously sized laundry room is conveniently located on the main level. The home includes three bedrooms, highlighted by an impressive 39' x 15' primary suite with its own private bathroom. A versatile bonus room can serve as a non-conforming fourth bedroom, home office, playroom, or additional living area. Just off the bonus room is an incredible 20' x 24' sunroom, offering endless possibilities for a game room, hobby space, home gym, entertaining area, or even a hot tub retreat. Additional features include a two-car attached garage and a HUGE unfinished basement that provides excellent storage or the opportunity to create even more finished living space. The home offers a great opportunity for cosmetic improvements, allowing you to customize it to your style while building equity. If you're looking for space, flexibility, and potential, this home is a must-see!
