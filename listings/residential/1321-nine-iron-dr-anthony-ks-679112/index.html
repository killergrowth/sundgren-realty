Property Description

1321 Nine Iron Road offers a unique opportunity to reposition an established ±4,200 SF commercial property in Anthony, Kansas, for a variety of hospitality, entertainment, recreation and destination-oriented uses. Situated on a proposed ±2.5-acre site, subject to final replat, the property benefits from its proximity to Anthony Lake, Anthony Golf Club, Anthony Gun Club, regional hunting activity and community events that attract visitors from beyond the immediate local population. Originally developed for restaurant and hospitality use, the building features an existing commercial kitchen, bar area, multiple dining and gathering spaces, entertainment/dance area, drive-up/service capability, offices, back-of-house areas and multiple restrooms. Recent improvements include an HVAC replacement in 2025 and water heater improvements in 2023. Potential concepts include a destination restaurant or supper club, hunting lodge or sportsman's retreat, boutique hospitality concept, event venue, private club, corporate retreat or other recreation and destination-oriented uses. Additional acreage may be available. Offered at $625,000, the property presents an opportunity to create a regional destination serving outdoor enthusiasts, hunters, golfers, groups, events and visitors throughout south-central Kansas. Potential uses are subject to applicable zoning, codes, licensing and governmental approvals.
