Property Description

Welcome to 1007 S Dodge Ave, a comfortable 3-bedroom, 1-bath home offering practical features, generous outdoor space, and a convenient central Wichita location. The covered front porch provides a welcoming place to relax, while the vinyl siding and vinyl windows help simplify exterior maintenance. Inside, you'll find a spacious living room with attractive luxury vinyl flooring, three comfortably sized bedrooms, and a kitchen offering ample cabinet and counter space, plus all appliances included with the home. An unfinished basement provides additional storage space, helping keep the main living areas organized. Outside, the wood privacy-fenced backyard offers plenty of room for outdoor activities, gardening, or entertaining. A detached one-car garage and extended driveway provide additional parking options. Conveniently located within walking distance of Wichita West High School, with easy access to Kellogg for commuting throughout the city. You're also just a short drive from the Historic Delano District, downtown Wichita, Riverfront Stadium, and a variety of local restaurants, shops, and entertainment. Whether you're looking for your first home, downsizing, or adding to your investment portfolio, this property offers a practical combination of space, convenience, and everyday functionality.
