Property Description

Welcome to 9423 E Cross Creek, a beautifully maintained home nestled in one of East Wichita's desirable Lakepoint neighborhood. Close to Wichita Country club, Collegiate School and Saint Thomas Aquinas school. Designer owned home. Rare 1 acre lot right in the middle of city.This spacious residence offers an inviting open floor plan designed for both comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. The well appointed kitchen features high-end appliances ample custom cabinetry, generous counter space, and a seamless flow into the dining and living areas. The primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with a private bath and generous closet space, while additional bedrooms offer flexibility for family and guests. The main floor office provides a nice space for working from home. Large windows throughout fill the home with endless natural light. The upstairs features a wonderful loft area and additional spacious bedrooms. In the basement you will find an additional living area, game room and what an incredible wet bar, making entertaining so easy from any where in this stately home. Step outside to enjoy the backyard, perfect for outdoor gatherings, gardening, or simply unwinding after a long day. Enjoy the sounds of the beautiful waterfall. You are surrounded with privacy with all the mature trees, a creek running nearby with a beautiful bridge and gazebo. The gorgeous vaulted wood ceiling screened in porch is a wonderful extension of the home brining additional living and entertaining space. You will also find outdoors a stone patio with a fireplace to gather around. The entire yard has lightscaping making for a beautiful retreat in the evenings. New DaVinci roof coming. This home is one of kind with the 1 acre wooded lot!
