Property Description

Welcome to 709 S Lexington Rd! This 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers 1,693 square feet of living space, a 1-car attached garage, and a great combination of charm and practical updates. As you step inside, you’ll find a bright living area with hardwood floors, creating a warm and inviting first impression. The main level also features a spacious kitchen and dining combination, along with a large family room centered around a fireplace, offering plenty of space to gather and relax. Two bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level, while the upper level offers an additional bedroom and half bath. Downstairs, the finished basement provides a spacious recreation room with plenty of room to spread out, along with a large storage room featuring laundry hookups. Outside, enjoy the covered front porch, oversized driveway, privacy-fenced backyard, and new back deck, offering additional space to relax or entertain. Recent improvements include fresh paint on the main and upper levels, new carpet on the main level, new exterior doors at the back and garage entry, a new garage door opener, new closet doors in the primary bedroom, and a new interior door for the primary bedroom. The main bathroom was remodeled within the past 10 years, and the HVAC system is approximately 4 years old. The home also features a full security system with four cameras, including a doorbell camera. The security system may be transferred to the buyer; otherwise, the cameras will be retained by the seller. With generous living spaces, useful updates, and room to enjoy both inside and out, this home is ready for its next owner!
