Property Description

Welcome to your private country retreat just outside Wichita in Sedgwick, KS! Situated on 4.83 secluded acres, this beautifully remodeled 4-bedroom, 2-bath home offers the perfect combination of comfortable living, updated amenities, and space for your hobbies, equipment, or business needs. The home has been fully remodeled within the last five years and features a brand-new roof, providing peace of mind for years to come. Inside, you’ll find a functional layout with plenty of room to spread out, including a versatile flex space that can be used as an office, playroom, hobby area, or additional living space. The kitchen offers a convenient walk-in pantry, providing excellent storage and organization. The spacious primary suite features a walk-in closet and private bathroom complete with an oversized shower. Outside is where this property truly stands out. A private driveway leads back to the secluded property, offering plenty of privacy while still being conveniently located just outside Wichita. The impressive 70' x 40' shop provides abundant space for vehicles, equipment, storage, hobbies, or a workshop. The exterior of the shop also features an additional 70' x 30' covered concrete area, creating even more usable space for outdoor projects, parking, or equipment. With 4.83 acres, a fully remodeled home, new roof, spacious shop, additional covered concrete area, and a private setting, this property offers a rare opportunity to enjoy acreage and country living with convenient access to Wichita.
