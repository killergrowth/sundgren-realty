Property Description

Welcome to The Andrew, where thoughtfully designed spaces, abundant natural light, and low-maintenance living come together in the highly sought-after Goddard School District. Offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a 2-car garage, this inviting floor plan is designed to make everyday living comfortable and effortless. The spacious primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with plenty of room to unwind, while the open-concept kitchen, dining, and living areas are filled with natural light, creating a bright and welcoming atmosphere. Step outside to the covered patio, the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, unwind after a long day, or entertain family and friends. Built to meet current energy code requirements and backed by a builder warranty, each home offers modern construction with quality finishes, including Luxury Vinyl Plank flooring, luxury carpet, Class 3 impact-resistant Tamko shingles, a dishwasher, microwave, and range/oven. The impact-resistant shingles may qualify homeowners for insurance discounts—buyers are encouraged to verify eligibility with their insurance provider. Outside, every home includes sod, irrigation, and privacy fencing, while the HOA provides lawn care and sprinkler maintenance, making homeownership easier and giving you more time to enjoy your home. Whether you're looking for your first home, downsizing, or simply wanting a move-in-ready home with less upkeep, The Andrew offers an ideal blend of comfort and convenience. Choose from standard or lake-view lots and enjoy the advantages of a completed new home without the wait of building from the ground up. Conveniently located on the Wichita/Goddard border, you'll enjoy quick access to shopping, dining, and everyday amenities while benefiting from the award-winning Goddard School District. Builder Incentive: Qualified buyers submitting an acceptable offer may choose $5,000 toward allowable closing costs or a builder-selected appliance package.
