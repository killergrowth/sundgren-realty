Property Description

Don’t miss this 2-bedroom, 3-bathroom home plus a non-conforming third bedroom in the basement, tucked on a quiet cul-de-sac in Valley Center! Priced below the county appraised value, this home is a great opportunity to move in and make it your own. The roof was also replaced at the end of 2025, so one of the biggest projects is already taken care of. You’ll love the location, just across Meridian from the new Valley Rec Center. Valley Center Middle School is right across the street, with the intermediate school to the south and Valley Center High School to the north, all within walking distance. Pulling up to the home, you’ll notice the brick veneer front and a large covered front porch. Step inside to new luxury vinyl plank flooring in the entry, which opens into the main living room. Here you’ll find new carpet and a sliding glass door out to the back deck. Much of the home has been refreshed with new carpet, paint, and trim, while the kitchen and baths are ready for your personal touches. On the main floor you’ll find two large bedrooms and a full hall bathroom. The primary bedroom is a true highlight, measuring 14x16 with a massive walk-in closet and a private full bathroom. On the other side of the home, the kitchen features new luxury vinyl plank flooring and original finishes. It’s an ideal space to invest and create a kitchen that fits your style. Head downstairs to find a spacious family room with new carpet and paint. The very large non-conforming third bedroom has its own bathroom with a walk-in shower, making it a great fit for guests, a home office, or a workout space. An additional small finished room and a large unfinished area for storage or future expansion complete the lower level. In the backyard you’ll love the large wood deck, a fully fenced yard, and mature trees that provide plenty of shade. With a newer roof, a great location, and a price below county value, this home leaves plenty of room to add value. Don’t miss out on this great opportunity. Schedule your private tour today!
