Property Description

Welcome to Coyote Ridge in Wellington, where comfort, space, and thoughtful design come together! This inviting ranch-style home on the west side of town offers the perfect setting for everyday living and effortless entertaining. As you enter you will take in the natural light, open-concept design, and welcoming flow from the living room to the dining area and kitchen. A stone-accented electric fireplace creates a cozy focal point, while granite countertops, convenient bar seating, and a separate dining area make gathering with family and friends a pleasure. Just off the kitchen, a thoughtfully transformed former laundry room adds an unexpected bonus: additional cabinetry, a beautiful accent wall, and a versatile buffet and serving area perfect for a coffee bar, entertaining station, or extra meal-prep space. The split-bedroom layout offers a private primary retreat featuring coffered ceilings, a walk-in closet, and an ensuite bath with dual sinks, a separate shower, and a relaxing soaking tub. Two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level. The finished basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring an oversized family room with a gas fireplace and wet bar that functions much like a second kitchen. Two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom provide added flexibility, along with a mechanical and storage room. Laundry is currently located downstairs, but main-floor hookups remain in place, offering the option to have laundry facilities on both levels or easily convert the additional kitchen space back to its original use. Outside, the backyard is ready to become your favorite place to unwind. Enjoy morning coffee or evening conversations on the covered deck or head down to the lower-level patio for another outdoor gathering space. The fully fenced backyard provides room to play, relax, garden, or simply enjoy the outdoors. A spacious three-car garage rounds out this wonderful property with plenty of room for vehicles, hobbies, and storage. With five bedrooms, three bathrooms, multiple living spaces, and inviting indoor and outdoor gathering areas, this Coyote Ridge home offers the space and versatility to make yourself right at home!
