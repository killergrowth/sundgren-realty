Property Description

Don't miss this opportunity to own a home in the highly sought-after Greens at Country Place! This freestanding patio home offers a desirable floor plan with two main-floor primary suites, each featuring its own ensuite bathroom and dedicated dressing area. An additional main-floor bedroom provides flexibility for use as an office or guest bedroom, with convenient access to a full bathroom. Enter through the charming courtyard, complete with brick pavers and copper guttering, and discover the beautiful hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, and exceptional craftsmanship throughout. Richly paneled walls, detailed millwork, and thoughtfully designed ceiling details create an elegant atmosphere with European-inspired architectural character. The spacious living room and formal dining room overlook the oversized backyard and patio areas, creating a wonderful setting for entertaining or everyday enjoyment. Both rooms feature soaring ceilings and an abundance of natural light. The bright and airy kitchen offers an island with eating space, an additional dining area, and convenient access to the laundry room with wash sink, two-car garage, and basement. The finished basement offers additional living and entertaining space, including a bedroom, full bathroom, large family recreation room with fireplace, two bonus rooms, cedar closet, and extensive storage areas. Recent owner improvements include all new exterior paint, extensive landscaping and night lighting, engineered foundation piers, and French Oak flooring throughout living areas. Don't miss this wonderful opportunity to enjoy spacious, low-maintenance living in one of Wichita's desirable patio-home communities!
