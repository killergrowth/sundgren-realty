Property Description

Check out this hidden gem in Southeast Wichita! This charming 1.5-story home offers 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and multiple living areas designed to fit a variety of needs. Step through the charming arched foyer into a welcoming living room featuring wood flooring and large windows. The formal dining room sits just off the rejuvenated kitchen, complete with a breakfast nook that makes the perfect spot for your morning coffee or casual meals. The main floor offers two bedrooms and a full bathroom, while the finished basement brings even more room to spread out with an oversized rec/living area, an additional bedroom, a full bathroom, and an oversized laundry room. Upstairs, discover a private primary retreat featuring its own sitting room and en suite bathroom, creating a space to unwind at the end of the day. Outside, the oversized wooded lot features a convenient U-shaped driveway and a detached 3-car garage with plenty of room for parking, projects, and storage. With its thoughtful remodel, distinctive character, and generous living spaces across three levels, this home offers a little something for everyone. Come see all the possibilities waiting for you on E Navajo Lane!
