Property Description

A Grand Restoration of a Historic College Hill Treasure! A remarkable transformation where nearly a century of architectural charm meets the beauty of modern luxury! Rich in history, this distinctive College Hill residence has a fascinating story to tell. Historical research has uncovered details about its original owners and a connection to one of the most highly regarded architects of the era. The home's architectural heritage makes its extensive restoration even more special. Additional historical information will be available in the MLS documents and at the property. This beautifully restored home makes a lasting impression from the moment you enter through its grand front door. Graceful rounded archways, a sweeping staircase, and timeless architectural details celebrate the character of this distinctive residence, while extensive updates bring it beautifully into the present. Gorgeous windows throughout the home fill the rooms with an abundance of natural light, creating bright, welcoming spaces and highlighting its beautiful architectural features. Beautiful hardwood floors grace the main level and continue up the staircase and across the upstairs landing, adding warmth, elegance, and timeless appeal. The stunning kitchen is truly a centerpiece, featuring brand-new solid wood cabinetry with soft-close doors and drawers, gorgeous quartz countertops with a matching quartz backsplash, and brand-new appliances, including the stove, microwave, and dishwasher. An eat-in bar, pantry, and adjoining sitting area complement the open kitchen and dining space, creating an inviting setting for both everyday living and entertaining. Throughout the home, beautiful new light fixtures and recessed lighting enhance the thoughtfully restored spaces. Just off the kitchen, you'll find a convenient main-floor laundry area featuring a charming black-and-white patterned tile floor, a half bathroom, and access to the attached one-car garage, making everyday living as practical as it is beautiful. A grand hardwood staircase leads upstairs to three bedrooms, all featuring brand-new carpeting, and two full bathrooms. The lovely primary suite offers its own private bathroom with a walk-in shower and walk-in closet. One of the additional bedrooms provides wonderful flexibility as a dressing room, home office, or whatever best suits your needs, because of the stylish builtins. At the top of the staircase, a beautiful built-in window bench with hidden storage creates a charming architectural focal point on the hardwood landing—a delightful detail that captures the home's historic character. The basement offers additional flexibility with a bonus room that could serve as extra living space, a recreation room, or storage, along with a full bathroom. Outside, enjoy a courtyard-style yard accented by wrought iron, a concrete patio, and additional parking. Offering three bedrooms, three full bathrooms, and one half bath, this home is a beautiful blend of historic sophistication and modern convenience. Homes with this kind of architectural character, fascinating history, and such an extensive restoration are truly special. A rare opportunity to own a beautifully renewed piece of College Hill history!
