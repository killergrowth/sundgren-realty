Property Description

Welcome to your private retreat on 3.20 acres—where comfort, space, and functionality come together in a beautifully maintained home offering approximately 3,758 square feet of living area. Only a mile away from city limits. Enjoy the quiet peaceful evenings surrounded by open Kansas farmland. This property blends peaceful rural living with the convenience of a thoughtfully designed residence and a versatile shop, ideal for work, storage, or hobbies. Inside, the home features a bright, inviting layout with generous living spaces perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Large windows bring in natural light and frame views of the expansive acreage. The custom kitchen with large eating island offers tons of space for large gatherings during special occasions. The main living areas flow comfortably, creating a warm and practical environment. The bedrooms are well-sized, and the bathrooms are designed for convenience flow throughout the home. With 4 total bedrooms, and 4 full baths, plus a main floor office for working from home! This spacious home has everything you ever dreamed of in a home! The main floor also features a nice formal dining room off the front door, nice main floor laundry room with sink, a door to the outside back deck for easy accessibility to the home from outside. Tons of extra storage or pantry space, a full bath off the garage is great for busy workdays outside. The master suite has a jacuzzi tub with a large walk-in shower and a huge walk-in closet! The basement space adds even more comfort to this custom home, with 3 more bedrooms and 2 full baths, a nice open layout with game room/ family, a door that opens up to the amazing outdoors for easy convivence of outdoor and indoor use for guests or family to come and go as they please!! Outside, the land offers room to breathe—ideal for gardening, recreation, livestock, or simply enjoying the outdoors. With a Generac generator ready for back up electricity to the home if any incline weather occurs, this home is move in ready with so much to offer. A standout feature of this property is the large 30x50 concrete shop, offering excellent utility for vehicles, equipment, projects, or business use. With newly installed heat and spray foam insulation inside, this is great for any activity or project year-round! Whether you need a workshop, storage for recreational gear, or a dedicated space for trades, this shop adds tremendous value. Another new feature to the home is brand new custom cut and installed tech savvy app for the garage doors to be used from wherever you are with your phone! This property is a rare opportunity to own, a well-sized home with acreage and a functional shop—perfect for buyers seeking space, versatility, and the freedom of rural living! Make it yours today! All kitchen appliances stay with the home, it is on rural water, with a newly pumped septic system, new main water line from the pit to the home. Only minutes from town this country custom built home has only had 3 homeowners!! Very well kept and maintained throughout!!
