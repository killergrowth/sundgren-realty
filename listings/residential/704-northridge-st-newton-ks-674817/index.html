Property Description

Who do you know that is looking for a beautiful, modern home for sale in one of Newton's most desirable communities? Welcome to 704 Northridge Dr! This stunning ranch property offers the ultimate in low-maintenance luxury and effortless main-level living. It is a perfect opportunity for anyone looking to downsize or find a completely turn-key house in a great location. Inside, you will find a bright, open layout with fresh neutral paint, and newer flooring throughout. The spacious living room flows right into a modern kitchen that features stylish cabinets, lots of counter space, and a huge center island that is perfect for eating and hanging out. The bedroom floor plan gives everyone plenty of privacy. It features a large master suite with a private master bathroom. Every room gets a ton of awesome natural light. The basement features a large family room, a finished living space and a remodeled bathroom. The new buyer can choose to keep the main floor laundry or to have it in the basement. Outside, there is a cool concrete patio that is perfect for chilling out on warm Kansas evenings. The home also has a big two-car attached garage with plenty of extra room for storage. Located in the highly sought-after Northridge area of Harvey County, this home is super quiet but still just minutes away from local schools, parks, and shopping. Everything is clean, efficient, and ready for you to move right in. Schedule your private tour today!
