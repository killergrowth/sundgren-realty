Property Description

Located at 421 N. Spruce St. in Abilene, KS, "The Flower Box" offers a unique turn-key commercial opportunity situated in a prime downtown location. Constructed in 1983 by Mike Wilson Builders, the structure features approximately 2,000 square feet of primary space on a 6,100-square-foot lot, alongside a full basement that provides ample additional storage and utility options. The annual taxes for the property are estimated at $2,635.02. The interior of the building is fully fitted for retail and floral operations, featuring high vaulted ceilings, modern light fixtures, display areas, and floral coolers—including a dedicated walk-in cooler. The sale includes the real estate, all existing inventory at closing, fixtures, displays, operational equipment, a delivery van, as well as the established business name and goodwill. Operationally, the business holds a dominant position as the only flower shop serving Abilene and one of only two in Dickinson County, drawing a strong regional customer base from surrounding communities. The business is currently run with the owner, one full-time employee, and seasonal part-time staff during peak periods, making it an ideal turn-key venture for an owner-operator, family business, or strategic buyer.
