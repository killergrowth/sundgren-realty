Located at 1204 E Park Glen St. in Clearwater, Kansas, The Clubhouse is a unique property offering plenty of possibilities for its next owner. Built by the Park Glen Estate HOA and now fully separated from the association, this building heads to auction with no HOA attached. The building features a spacious main room with a kitchenette, bathroom, and a separate utility room. Outside, you’ll find a nice yard, patio, and fire pit, creating a great setup for gatherings and time spent with family, friends, or neighbors. Conveniently located near Clearwater High School and the local ball fields, The Clubhouse could make an excellent spot to gather between games, host birthday parties, hold small meetings, or create a neighborhood hangout. Whether you’re looking for a gathering space, small event venue, or a property with a little creativity and vision, this is an opportunity to make it your own. With its layout, outdoor space, and great location, The Clubhouse offers a different kind of property with plenty of potential. Take a closer look at this one-of-a-kind Clearwater property. *The mailboxes will be removed from the property prior to closing.
