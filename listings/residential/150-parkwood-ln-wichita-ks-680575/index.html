Property Description

Welcome home to the charming, painted brick, shuttered 150 N. Parkwood located in the established, beautiful Crown Heights neighborhood. A true surprise of updated and renovated space that will not disappoint. 3 bedrooms, 2 updated spacious bathrooms, beautifully refinished hardwood floors, painted wood work, a complete remodeled kitchen, dining combo where the long eating island is the anchor to the space, quartz counters, new appliances, open to dining area.Your guests will feel comfortable for visits in the living room featuring fireplace, natural light from the windows. The space flows seamlessly throughout this main level for entertaining. An alluring looking staircase to the upper level providing 2 large in scope bedrooms with great closet space. Convenient Stackable laundry in hallway for well thought out access to bedrooms. The partially finished basement provides versatile room for exercise, ideal for storage, hobbies, or other practical uses. Additional improvements include new roof in 2022, Convenient access to shopping, restaurants, entertainment, parks, and neighborhood amenities, this thoughtfully maintained home offers a wonderful combination of character, functionality, and meaningful updates.
