Property Description

Country living just west of Salina on 4.2 acres! This 4-bedroom, 2-bath home features an open-concept kitchen, formal dining area, and living room just off the patio doors leading to the pool—creating a great space for entertaining. The spacious primary suite offers a comfortable retreat. The finished basement includes two bedrooms, a rec room, a bathroom, and office space. Recent updates include a newer roof and gutters, plus a new pool pump. Equestrian features include a 3-stall horse barn with additional garage space, a run-in shelter, and horse and steel fencing. An oversized attached garage offers additional parking and storage.
