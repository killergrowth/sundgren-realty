About This Property

Introducing “The Coleman” by Perry Family Custom Homes — an elegant zero-step residence thoughtfully designed for comfortable, contemporary living. Ideally situated at the back of a cul-de-sac on a beautiful 1.27-acre lot, this 2,530 sq ft home offers four spacious bedrooms, two-and-a-half bathrooms, and an oversized three-car side-load garage. From the moment you enter, the open-concept layout impresses with 10-foot ceilings that rise to a vaulted great room, an electric fireplace, and expansive windows that fill the home with natural light. The kitchen showcases high-end finishes, a large island, and a generous walk-in pantry, blending beauty with everyday functionality. The private primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, roomy walk-in closet and an en-suite bath with a walk-in shower, freestanding soaking tub, and dual vanities. The split-bedroom design provides added privacy, with three additional bedrooms and a hall bath located separately from the primary suite. The built-in 4' x 9' safe room with an industry-approved steel door offers added peace of mind during severe weather. Currently under construction, “The Coleman” may allow buyers to select certain finishes, depending on the stage of construction at the time of contract. Located in desirable Dry Creek Estates, this home offers the perfect balance — well-regarded schools, convenient access to city amenities and major highways, and the peaceful country feel buyers love.
