Property Description

What a JOY it would be to LAND in this home! Located near the former Joyland Park, this thoughtfully renovated home offers the perfect combination of modern updates, comfortable living, and room to roam on .82 acres! Originally a 4-bedroom, 1-bath home, the layout has been reimagined into a spacious 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath retreat designed for today's lifestyle. The former galley kitchen has been opened up with properly installed structural support beams, creating an inviting, open-concept living space perfect for gathering and entertaining. Beautifully refinished walls throughout provide a smooth, quality finish and a fresh, polished feel.
