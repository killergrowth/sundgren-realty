Property Description

Welcome home! This 2-story stunner with a fully finished basement is ready to hold ALL your stuff (and then some). Upstairs you'll find 4 bedrooms, including TWO primary en-suites, plus 2 more bedrooms with their own guest bath. Oh, and there's a balcony made for sipping your morning coffee. The main floor was basically built for hosting. A living room with a fireplace splits the space into a cozy reading nook/playroom on one side and a proper couch-hangout zone on the other. Step right out the kitchen onto the huge back deck for grilling season, and can we PLEASE talk about this pantry? It's giving organized energy. The mudroom drop zone comes with a coat closet plus storage for all your overflow chaos. Not sure if you need a dining room or a home office? This flex room says "yes." And a powder bath on the main floor?! Absolute win. Down in the basement, things keep going: a bedroom, a full bath, and two separate den spaces with a wet bar, hello, game night headquarters. Now let's talk location and layout, because this is where it really shines: close to WSU and shopping, paid-off solar panels (yes, you read that right), a pull-off spot for your RV or extra vehicles, and an extra-long 2-car garage for all the gear. Need space AND move-in ready? This house said "why not both."
