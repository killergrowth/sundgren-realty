Property Description

This is the popular neighborhood in Augusta you have been waiting on. This all-brick home brings plenty of reasons to put down roots of your own! Offering 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and nearly 2,100 total square feet, this property delivers space, features, and value in one great package. Hardwood floors run throughout the main level, while the kitchen features solid wood cabinetry, solid surface countertops, a built-in oven, and stainless steel appliances. The full basement offers room for storage, hobbies, or future finishing, and the attached 2-car garage adds everyday convenience. New electrical box. Out back, a fully fenced yard, New deck for grilling, and private in-ground pool make Kansas summers even better. Just minutes from Castle Park Splash Pad and local Augusta amenities, this home offers plenty of space, great features, and the opportunity to make a home your own while keeping your budget in mind. Sometimes, the right home is the one that simply makes sense.
