Property Description

Nestled on historic 9th Avenue in Winfield, this lovely home features a wrap-around porch and abundant shade. Offering 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, the main level includes a family room that can serve as a master bedroom, complemented by well-maintained oak woodwork throughout the main floor. Additional interior highlights include built-in dining room hutches, floor-to-ceiling bookshelves in the main-floor library/den, and a large walk-in pantry in the kitchen. Exterior updates include a new roof installed in September 2026 and fresh paint on the detached garage. The home has gutter covers as well to protect your gutters from clogging.
