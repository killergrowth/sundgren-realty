Property Description

3213 S Bluelake Ct offers a rare combination of space, privacy, and lake community living in the Wichita area. This 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home features a 3-car garage, finished view-out basement, large privacy-fenced backyard, and no special taxes. The open floor plan connects the living room, dining area, and kitchen for comfortable everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen features custom cabinetry, a large island, pantry, and all appliances. A wood-burning fireplace anchors the living area, while main-floor laundry and a split-bedroom layout add convenience and privacy. The finished view-out basement expands the living space with two additional bedrooms and a third full bathroom. There is ample space for movie nights, game-day gatherings, guest accommodations, home office, or hobby area to suit your needs. Outside, enjoy a large privacy-fenced backyard backing to a wooded area with no neighbors to the back. A sprinkler system, covered deck, and patio provide space for outdoor dining, grilling, and relaxing. One of the standout features is the private lake community. HOA amenities include access to a stocked lake with a sandy beach, boat dock, boat ramp, and picnic area. Enjoy fishing, swimming, kayaking, paddle boarding, and boating (electric-powered motors only). Additional community amenities include pickleball and basketball court and a playground. Conveniently located near popular Wichita amenities, grocery stores, and Eisenhower National Airport, the home also offers quick access to Kellogg, I-235, and I-135 for getting around the area. If you are searching for a 5-bedroom home for sale in Wichita with a finished basement, 3-car garage, private fenced backyard, lake community amenities, and no special taxes in the Haysville School District, 3213 S Bluelake Ct is worth a closer look.
