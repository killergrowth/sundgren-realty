Property Description

Discover Your Clean Slate on the Quiet Side of Toronto Lake! Looking for a peaceful retreat, a hunter’s paradise, or a primary home with endless potential? Situated on 6 plus lush wooded acres on the desirable East side of Toronto Lake, this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom cabin (plus a non-conforming bunk room) offers the privacy you crave with the community growth you want. The Property: A Canvas for Your Vision. This cabin is a true "clean slate." Whether you're dreaming of a modern cottage near the lakeside or a rugged hunting basecamp, the entire property is ready for your personal touch. • Space to Grow: Features 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, plus a dedicated bunk room (no closet) perfect for extra guests or a home office. • The Interior: the home features areas with new drywall and subflooring, providing a head start for your custom finishes. • Functional Layout: Includes a spacious kitchen with wood cabinetry and a laundry area already equipped with a washer and dryer. Two living areas- provides versatility and endless options for the layout of your home. • Lakeside Living: Located on the quiet side of the lake with only a handful of neighbors, you’ll enjoy seclusion and a very private retreat. A Hunter & Nature Lover’s Dream • Abundant Wildlife: The 6-plus acre lot comes with an established tree stand. Deer season here is legendary, and with a few corn feeders, you’ll have a front-row seat to Kansas’ finest wildlife. • Prime Fishing: Toronto Lake is widely considered the best crappie lake in Kansas. You’re also just a short drive from the beautiful Woodson State Fishing Lake. • Trailblazer’s Location: You are literally at the doorstep of adventure. Blackjack Road serves as the trailhead for the Overlook Trail, a popular, kid-friendly hiking destination with stunning views. A Community on the Rise - Toronto is currently undergoing a massive modernization. This isn't just a sleepy town; it’s a community investing in its future: • Infrastructure: New water lines are being installed now, with sewer and electric upgrades to follow. A brand new bridge is nearing completion. • Local Gems: Home to the best library in Kansas and a brand new playground coming Summer 2026. • Dining & Convenience: Enjoy local favorites like Lizard Lips and Grumpy Bear for food and groceries, plus a local bar for evening relaxation. A new Post Office is also slated for Summer 2026. Location & Flexibility: Located at the corner of three counties, this property offers rare educational flexibility with bus service available to Eureka, Fredonia, and Yates Center schools. • Easy Access: A straight shot from Wichita via Hwy 254. • Nearby Amenities: Only 20 minutes from Eureka, where you’ll find hospitals, doctors, a fantastic local grocery store, and familiar fast-food staples. Don’t miss the chance to claim your piece of the Kansas landscape. Whether it's for the hunt, the harvest, or the hike—your journey starts at 169 Blackjack Rd.
