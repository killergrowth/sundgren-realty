Property Description

This beautifully designed duplex offers 3 spacious bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a 2-car attached garage on each unit, and all conveniently situated on a single level with zero basement steps to navigate. Step inside to find a light and bright interior featuring crisp white cabinetry and trim throughout, stunning granite countertops in the kitchens and baths, and durable, low-maintenance luxury vinyl plank flooring that combines style with everyday functionality. The exterior boasts great curb appeal with a durable composition roof, a full sprinkler system to keep the lawn green, and a fully fenced backyard perfect for pets, play, or privacy. Complete with central gas heat and central electric cooling for year-round comfort, plus an ideal location near Kellogg and 135th St W giving you quick access to local dining, shopping, and easy commuting!
