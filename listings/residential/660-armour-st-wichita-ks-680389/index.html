Property Description

This sprawling mid-century modern ranch offers timeless architectural character, inviting living spaces, and plenty of room to spread out on one of the best blocks in Woodlawn Village! Vaulted ceilings accented by expansive glass windows create an airy feel, while the brick fireplace with a built-in surround serves as a warm focal point in the living room. A separate "flex" room just off the living area provides a versatile space for hobbies, music room, a home office, or additional entertaining. The granite kitchen is fully equipped with appliances and features an abundance of cabinetry, a center island, and a generous layout that incorporates both an eating area PLUS and a sitting area that could be a cozy den/reading room. The main-level primary suite offers a walk-in closet, vaulted ceiling, private bathroom with 2 sinks, and convenient exterior access to a wood deck. Two additional bedrooms (one with a loft and vaulted ceiling) and a full bathroom complete the main floor. Downstairs, the finished basement expands the living space with a recreation room, a true bedroom with an escape window, a full bathroom, and ample storage. Outside, enjoy an impressive backyard with a sport court and fenced yard, offering plenty of room for recreation, entertaining, and outdoor enjoyment! A breezeway/carport connects the home to the oversized garage, adding both convenience and character to this distinctive property.
