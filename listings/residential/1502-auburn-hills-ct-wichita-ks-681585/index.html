Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, plus finished bonus room, 3-bath home situated on a spacious .46-acre cul-de-sac pond lot in the desirable Auburn Hills neighborhood! From the moment you arrive, you'll appreciate the pride of ownership, stunning custom landscaping, and attention to detail throughout this exceptional property. Step inside to an inviting open floor plan featuring gorgeous wood floors, spiral staircase, soaring vaulted ceilings with beautiful wood beams, and a striking stone gas fireplace that creates a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Newer paint throughout the home and newer carpet add to the fresh, well-maintained feel. The spacious kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, offering a large island with eating bar and convenient pull-out cabinetry. Enjoy multiple dining options with a formal dining room and a charming breakfast nook featuring beautiful designer ceiling details. Just off the kitchen, you'll find a spacious and thoughtfully designed pantry, drop zone, and laundry area offering a multitude of cabinets, generous storage, and plenty of room to keep everyday essentials organized. This versatile space combines functionality and convenience, making everyday living a breeze! Primary bedroom has walk in closet and primary bath has double sinks, granite counters, whirlpool tub, tile floors and separate shower. Two remaining bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level.The basement is a view out/ walk out at grade and offers an abundance of additional living and entertaining space, complete with a spacious family room, a second fireplace with stone surround, wet bar, and beautiful built-in shelving. Two generous bedrooms, both with walk-in closets, provide plenty of room for family or guests. You'll also love the additional finished bonus room featuring custom built-in cabinetry and a clever drop-down worktable that conveniently stows away when not in use! Whether you need a craft room, home office, homeschooling space, or hobby room, the possibilities are endless. Two additional storage areas provide plenty of room to keep everything organized. Step outside and discover your own private retreat! The expansive .46-acre lot features a fenced backyard and multiple outdoor living spaces. Relax on the covered composite deck or gather around the fire pit on the paver patio, the perfect spot for cozy evenings, entertaining friends, or simply enjoying the beautiful outdoor surrounding.The oversized, finished three-car garage is another outstanding feature, offering generous space for vehicles, storage, and more! Living in Auburn Hills offers even more to love! This wonderful neighborhood features several swimming pools, clubhouses, sidewalks, playgrounds and beautiful lakes.The convenient location offers easy access to highways, shopping, restaurants, and everyday necessities while still enjoying the charm of an established neighborhood.Additional highlights include a New roof in 2019 and New carpet and paint in 2025 and 2026. Meticulously cared for and filled with character and functional living spaces, this is a home you won't want to miss!
