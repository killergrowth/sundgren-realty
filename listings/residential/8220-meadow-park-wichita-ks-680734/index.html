Property Description

This Tuscan inspired Mediterranean home showcases beautiful stonework, dramatic beamed ceilings, and extraordinary spaces—where luxury, entertainment, and resort-style living come together—await at 8220 W Meadow Park, located from within a gated community. From the sweeping driveway and mature landscaping to the welcoming courtyard, this home makes a memorable first impression. The courtyard offers a charming setting for morning coffee and leads to an arched double-door entrance that introduces the home’s rich architectural character. Inside, soaring ceilings with exposed wood beams, wood flooring, and abundant natural light create an impressive yet inviting living space. The living room fireplace provides a warm focal point, while the open layout keeps conversation flowing between the living area and kitchen. Designed for gathering, the kitchen features an oversized island with generous seating, beautiful knotty alder cabinets, ample counter space, and a spacious cooking area accented by a statement range hood, decorative backsplash, and pot filler. An adjoining eating area provides a comfortable spot for everyday meals, while the separate formal dining room showcases a chandelier and detailed ceiling for an elegant entertaining experience. A dedicated office with rich wood built-ins offers a beautiful setting for working, reading, or displaying favorite collections. The spacious primary suite provides a peaceful retreat with a truly distinctive bathroom. A ceiling-mounted waterfall fills the illuminated bathtub, creating a fountain-like effect, while an overflow feature adds to the spa-inspired experience. A separate enclosed shower with a sauna feature offers another way to relax and unwind. Generous vanity space and extensive closet storage with built-in shelving and drawers complete the suite. Additional bedrooms and flexible rooms provide options for guests, hobbies, or a home exercise space. The lower level brings an entirely different dimension to entertaining. An expansive recreation area provides room for billiards, watching a game, and gathering with friends, while the large bar offers ample seating and serving space. A striking wine cellar features stone walls, a brick ceiling, and bottle displays that create a distinctive atmosphere. Your own private bowling alley makes game nights at home an experience to remember. Storage is another standout, with substantial unfinished space and a separate garage space equipped with a vehicle lift that lowers cars into the basement storage room. This unique feature provides convenient access to additional vehicle storage and valuable room for collections, equipment, or hobbies. Outdoor living is just as inviting. The in-ground pool with a slide is surrounded by generous patio space for lounging, dining, and summer gatherings. A covered porch offers a shaded place to relax overlooking the pool, while an enclosed gathering room with stone walls, a warm wood ceiling, and a fireplace creates another comfortable retreat. Mature trees and landscaping frame the backyard, adding to its established setting. With multiple garage bays, a separate stone storage building, and a remarkable variety of spaces to gather or retreat, this home combines everyday comfort with amenities that make staying home feel like a special occasion.
