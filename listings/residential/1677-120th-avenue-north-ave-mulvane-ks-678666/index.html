Property Description

Just for you! If you're tired of looking for that perfect country home, try this spacious, welcoming, and refreshingly peaceful home with 4 bedrooms, 3 1/2 baths, two recreation areas, walk out basement and multiple new decks for relaxing! Just two miles off paving, southeast of the small city of Mulvane! Treat the family and your friends to lush green grass, a winding creek, and a bountiful forest on this irregularly shaped and unrestricted 20+ acre tract! Picture a sharing-sized kitchen with double ovens, gas range, trash compactor, granite counter tops with an extended bar, a main floor laundry, and a huge formal dining with an adjacent entertainment/buffet area! Treat yourself to a separate master suite with walk-out, vaulted and skylighted office/study/dressing area! With the 30' x30" finished attached garage, zoned heating and cooling, Rural Water, detached machine shed an separate smaller storage building, you'll love this escape from the city!
