Property Description

Located in a convenient central Wichita location, this brand-new duplex offers an incredible opportunity for both investors and owner-occupants looking to offset their mortgage by living in one side while renting out the other. Both units are currently vacant and move-in ready, allowing the new owner the flexibility to choose their tenants, establish rental income, or occupy one side immediately. One of the biggest advantages of this property is having no HOA fees and no special taxes, a rare find with new construction that can save owners thousands of dollars over time and help maximize cash flow! Each unit features quality craftsmanship throughout, including granite countertops, an open-concept layout, and a spacious kitchen complete with a walk-in pantry. The seamless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas makes each home perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Each side also offers two patios, including a beautiful east-facing patio with no rear neighbors, providing additional privacy and outdoor living space. Whether you're looking to house hack, expand your rental portfolio, or enjoy the flexibility of owning both sides, this property offers exceptional value without the added expenses of HOA fees or special assessments.
