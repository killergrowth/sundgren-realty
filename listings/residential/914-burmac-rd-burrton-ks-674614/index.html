About This Property

This welcoming 76.45 acre country property is perfect for animal lovers, offering plenty of room for horses, pets, and livestock to roam. The 1,388 sq ft home sits on a paved road just 1.75 miles north of Burrton and was tastefully updated in 2025 with granite countertops and modern finishes. Inside, you’ll find 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, a cozy mud room with laundry hookups, and a large full basement with a potential office and spacious family room. Major updates—including HVAC, hot water tank, and electrical—were completed in 2025. The land features two large farm buildings, cattle pens, and ample space to create the horse or hobby animal setup you’ve always wanted. A 2 car garage, carport, and optional farm items add convenience. Priced at $625,000, this is a move in ready rural retreat designed for comfortable living with your animals close by.
