Property Description

Welcome to this charming, well-maintained home in West Wichita! From the lovely curb appeal to the mature trees that greet you upon arrival, this home offers warmth and character from the moment you step inside.The main level features upgraded luxury vinyl flooring, a beautiful bay window that fills the space with natural light and a welcoming living room with convenient access to the formal dining room. You’ll also find a conveniently located half bath and a spacious kitchen with an additional dining area, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, the generously sized primary suite offers a walk-in closet and private ensuite bathroom featuring an updated vanity and an oversized tub with tiled shower walls. Two additional bedrooms and a second full bathroom complete the upper level. The finished basement provides even more living space, featuring a spacious family room with a cozy gas fireplace, a fourth bedroom and an additional half bath.Step outside and enjoy the covered deck overlooking the privacy-fenced backyard. A bonus feature is the storage shed with electricity, offering added convenience. This West Wichita gem has so much to offer. Beautifully maintained, thoughtfully updated, and move-in ready, this is one you won't want to miss!
