Property Description

Located in the desirable Stone Lake addition, this beautiful three-year old home offers modern style, spacious living, and neighborhood lake access where you can enjoy fishing and kayaking. Featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and a fully finished basement, there is room for everyone to spread out and enjoy. The kitchen features granite countertops, with granite finishes in the bathrooms as well. Main floor laundry adds everyday convenience. Recent exterior improvements include a new roof featuring Class 3 impact resistant shingles, along with new gutters. The impact-resistant shingles may qualify you for an insurance premium discount! Outside, you'll find a fenced backyard with an established lawn and an extra parking pad for added convenience. A two-car attached garage completes this wonderful property. With its newer construction and desirable features, this home is ready for its next owners to enjoy!
