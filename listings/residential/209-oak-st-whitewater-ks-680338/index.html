Property Description

Historic Charm. Modern Updates. An Incredible Transformation! Welcome to 209 N Oak St, where timeless character meets an extensive renovation in the heart of Whitewater! From the inviting covered front porch and mature shade trees to the beautifully refreshed interior, this two-story home offers the perfect blend of classic charm and modern comfort. Step inside to discover bright, welcoming living spaces filled with natural light, beautiful new flooring, fresh neutral finishes, and updated lighting throughout. The stunning kitchen features rich wood cabinetry, gorgeous quartz countertops, updated fixtures, and a functional layout that combines warmth with modern style. Spacious rooms, charming architectural details, and a beautiful bay window add character you simply don't find in every home. But the transformation goes far beyond what meets the eye! Extensive improvements include a new roof, siding, house wrap, windows, plumbing and sewer lines, electrical panel and components, water heater, and HVAC updates. Significant foundation improvements have also been completed, including structural reinforcement, along with a sump pump and cellar improvements. Outside, enjoy relaxing mornings on the expansive covered front porch or entertaining on the new backyard deck overlooking a spacious, tree-shaded yard. A detached two-car garage provides additional parking and storage. With so many major improvements already completed, this home offers the character of an established property with the appeal of extensive modern updates.
