Property Description

Welcome to this beautifully designed new-construction twin home in the desirable South Pointe neighborhood. Situated on a corner lot with an east-facing backyard overlooking a scenic reserve pond, this property offers modern living, quality finishes, and convenient access to Wichita-area amenities and major thoroughfares. Each side of this well-appointed duplex features 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a 2-car attached garage. The spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet and private walk-in shower. An inviting exterior patio and fully fenced backyard provide comfortable outdoor living space. Inside, the home features 9-foot ceilings and durable LVP flooring throughout, creating a clean, modern feel. The kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining with an island sink, walk-in pantry, and quartz countertops. Quartz countertops are also featured in both bathrooms for a cohesive, upscale finish. The HOA provides lawn maintenance, mowing, sprinkler maintenance, and well services, allowing for low-maintenance living. Conveniently located along Maize Road between US-54/400 (Kellogg Avenue) and K-42, this home provides easy access to major employment centers, shopping, dining, and surrounding communities. Nearby recreational amenities include Pawnee Prairie Park, Prairie Sunset Trail, Tex Consolver Golf Course, and Eisenhower National Airport. Located within the Goddard Public School District, this new duplex combines modern construction, desirable amenities, and an excellent west Wichita location. A fantastic opportunity for comfortable, low-maintenance living in a growing neighborhood.
