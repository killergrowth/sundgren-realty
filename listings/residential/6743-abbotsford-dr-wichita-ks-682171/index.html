Property Description

Estate sale through 11/21/26. Buyers may tour during sale or schedule a showing 11/27/26 or later. 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch in a convenient northeast Wichita location! Pride of ownership shines throughout this meticulously maintained home, lovingly cared for and updated by the same owners for nearly 40 years. The updated eat-in kitchen features wood floors, granite countertops, and appliances that all stay. Two spacious living areas offer plenty of room to relax and entertain, including a cozy family room with a beautiful brick fireplace, built-ins, and double sliding doors overlooking the backyard, plus a large formal living room just off the family room. The main floor also features a primary suite with walk-in closet and private bath, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a spacious laundry room. Downstairs, you'll find a huge finished rec room, half bath, an additional room with potential for finishing, and plenty of hobby or storage space. Outside, enjoy the updated deck overlooking a spacious, mature, fenced backyard. Numerous updates and upgrades throughout make this home a must-see!
