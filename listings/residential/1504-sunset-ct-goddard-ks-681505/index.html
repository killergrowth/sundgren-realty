Property Description

Welcome home to this move-in ready 3 bed, 2 bath tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Goddard! Step inside to soaring vaulted ceilings and abundant natural light in the living room, which flows openly into the dining area and kitchen. The kitchen offers stainless steel appliances and a peninsula that adds extra counter space and storage. Durable vinyl plank flooring runs throughout the main level, including both bedrooms. The primary bedroom features a walk-in closet and en suite access to the main floor bathroom. A second bedroom and a convenient main floor laundry room complete this level. Downstairs you’ll find a spacious family room, a third bedroom, a full bathroom, and a utility area with plenty of storage. Out back, relax on the deck overlooking the fully fenced yard. With a 2-car garage and storage shed, there’s room for all your toys and tools. Schedule your showing today!
