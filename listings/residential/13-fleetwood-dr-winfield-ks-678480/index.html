Property Description

This is your opportunity to own a wonderful home in the desirable Fleetwood area! This all brick ranch has much to offer you. The sunlit living room has a beautiful new floor that compliments the fireplace. You can access the large deck and fully fenced back yard from here too. The formal dining room is big enough for you to host large family gatherings or to entertain friends. The chef's kitchen is roomy enough for multiple cooks. It has loads of counter space and many, many cabinets. And, all the kitchen appliances will remain! To complete the main floor, there are two guest bedrooms, an updated guest bath and a large master bedroom with it's private updated bathroom. In the basement, the large family room has a wood burning stove to keep everyone cozy and warm during the coming winter months. In addition, there is a light and airy bedroom with escape window, a dedicated office space, a large laundry room and a large, updated bathroom! The radon mitigation system is in the roomy storage area which has lots of shelving units to help you stay organized. Enjoy evenings on the deck watching the kids and pets play in the wonderful, fully fenced yard. There is a good sized shop area that can be accessed from the back yard (with a big overhead garage door) or from the garage. It is perfect for DIYers, hobbyists or crafters. It is a huge bonus. The Fleetwood area is highly desirable so come see this lovely home soon!
