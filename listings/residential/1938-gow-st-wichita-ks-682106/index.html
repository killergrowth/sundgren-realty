Property Description

Welcome to 1938 North Gow St! Set in the beautiful Sunset Heights addition filled with mature trees, this inviting ranch greets you with great curb appeal. As you enter into the home from the front stoop, you’ll step into the bright living area where original hardwood floors bring warmth and flow throughout. To the left, the kitchen and dining area offer generous cabinetry and granite countertops. A separate laundry area adds convenience and leads out to a spacious covered patio, ideal for morning coffee or unwinding at sunset. The shed out back is perfect for keeping extra yard supplies clear from any clutter! Down the hall from the living room, you’ll find a spacious primary bedroom with a closet system, two additional bedrooms and a shared hall bath. All three bedrooms feature original hardwood floors beneath the carpet. A new roof and windows, both installed within the past two years, offer added peace of mind with two major updates already taken care of for the future owners.
