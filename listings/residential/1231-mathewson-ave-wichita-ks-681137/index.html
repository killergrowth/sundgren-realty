Property Description

Fully updated 3 bedroom, 2 bath ranch-style home with a new roof, siding, windows, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC. A covered front porch opens to the living room with LVP flooring. The kitchen has new cabinets, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a back door out to the concrete patio. The split bedroom plan puts the primary bedroom on its own side of the home, with a private bath and a tiled walk-in shower. The hall bath has a full tile tub surround. Outside there’s a new concrete driveway with room for two cars and a one-car attached garage.
