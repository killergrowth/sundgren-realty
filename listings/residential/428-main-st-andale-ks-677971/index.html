Property Description

Built in 1910, this charming two story home has been part of Andale’s Main Street landscape for more than a century and is filled with the character and charm that comes with its history. Offering 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 1,936 sq. ft. of living space and a HUGE 28x28 detached garage on a .24 acre lot, this property offers the space, storage and small town location you’ve been looking for. Step onto the inviting wraparound front porch with porch swing and you’ll immediately notice the character that makes this home special, including a beautiful stained glass detail in the front window. Inside, spacious rooms and large bedrooms provide plenty of room to spread out. The main level features a living room plus an additional family room that could easily serve as a formal dining room, home office or flexible living space. The large kitchen offers plenty of cabinets and storage, along with a center island featuring the stovetop and additional seating, giving you plenty of room for cooking, gathering and everyday living. Outside, the back patio provides a great space for entertaining, relaxing or enjoying those small town evenings. And if garage, workshop or storage space is high on your wish list, this one delivers! The oversized 28x28 detached two car garage features two garage doors, built in shelving and plenty of additional storage space. The home also includes a cellar offering additional storage and a place to take shelter during Kansas storms. And the location is hard to beat! Right in the heart of Andale, this N. Main Street home is within walking distance of Andale schools, St. Joseph Catholic Church, local businesses and more. Historic character, spacious living, a large kitchen, an oversized garage and so much walkability, all in the heart of Andale!
