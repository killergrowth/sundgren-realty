Property Description

All-Brick Ranch Home, all electric, on 7.5 Acres with an Impressive 50’ x 30’ Shop! Discover the perfect combination of comfortable living, acreage, and exceptional workspace with this all-brick ranch home on 7.5 acres! Offering 1,921 square feet, 3 bedrooms, and 2 full bathrooms, this property features a spacious home and multiple outbuildings with room for hobbies, equipment, livestock, and more. The standout feature is the 50’ x 30’ metal shop, complete with a concrete floor, overhead door, 220-volt electrical service, overhead heater, and an air compressor with airlines throughout. Exterior shelving and a workbench provide added convenience, making this an appealing setup for projects, equipment maintenance, or workshop use. The property also includes a 30’ x 24’ shop with a concrete floor, overhead door, and electricity, along with a metal livestock shed with electric service and a horse barn. The horse barn’s electrical service is currently not working. Inside the home, enjoy 1,921 square feet of living space, 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, central heat and air, and a two-car attached garage. The durable metal roof adds to the home’s practical features. Mature trees, established landscaping, a gated driveway entrance, and guttering enhance the property’s exterior appeal. If you’ve been searching for a home with acreage and substantial shop space, this property deserves a closer look! With a comfortable ranch home, multiple outbuildings, and 7.5 acres, there’s plenty of room to make the property work for your lifestyle.
