Property Description

Country Living Just Minutes from Town! Welcome home to this immaculate homestead nestled on 2 beautiful acres, offering the perfect blend of peaceful country living and convenient access to town. This move-in-ready property has been thoughtfully maintained and offers plenty of space for both everyday living and entertaining. The home features beautiful woodwork throughout, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, while the spacious primary bedroom offers a large walk-in closet for excellent storage. A built-in safe provides added convenience and security. For those needing room to work, play, or store their toys, the property boasts an impressive 40’ x 60’ insulated shop—perfect for working on cars, entertaining, hobbies, or additional storage. There is also a 20’ x 20’ work shed with an overhead garage door, providing even more workspace and storage options. The property is serviced by city electric, rural water, and a septic system, providing the benefits of country living without sacrificing convenience. If you’ve been looking for an immaculate property with acreage, exceptional shop space, and a home that’s ready for you to move right in, this one is a must-see!
