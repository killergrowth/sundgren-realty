Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully renovated 5-bedroom, 3-bath ranch in Tallgrass East, an established master-planned neighborhood in northeast Wichita known for its mature trees and wonderful amenities. The sellers have called this house home and thoughtfully updated it throughout, including the kitchen, bathrooms, flooring, and fresh interior and exterior paint. The big-ticket items have been taken care of, too, with a brand-new roof and HVAC system! The main floor features a spacious living room, an updated kitchen with an eating bar and pantry, and a primary suite complete with a large walk-in closet, laundry chute, and private en-suite bathroom. Two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level. Downstairs, the finished basement offers a generous rec room, two additional bedrooms with spacious walk-in closets, a full bathroom, and plenty of storage space. Step outside to the deck and fenced backyard, where you can enjoy beautiful scenery and frequent visits from local wildlife. You'll also appreciate the oversized, extended 3-car garage with plenty of built-in storage. All kitchen appliances stay! Living in Tallgrass East means enjoying fantastic neighborhood amenities, including a community pool, playground, tennis and pickleball courts, sand volleyball, ball fields, a clubhouse, outdoor pavilion, and walking trails that wind throughout the neighborhood. Conveniently located just minutes from K-96, with easy access to Bradley Fair, The Waterfront, Greenwich Place, and Chisholm Creek Park. With so many updates already completed and a neighborhood full of amenities, this home is ready for its next chapter!
