Property Description

All brick ranch home with vinyl eaves and soffits, gutter covers, and a class IV shingle roof in the heart of Benton, Kansas. Sprawling ranch just under 2000 sq ft on the main level. Classic side-load garage and separate 24 x 24 detached garage for the hobby enthusiast, there is also a tidy shed with shelving and room for the lawn equipment. Enter the home through recently updated composite double doors with decorative glass! Formal living room with an abundance of natural light. Open kitchen layout with gorgeous granite counters, all stainless appliances, separate pantry, and just step away from a dining area with bay out windows. The oven/range has gas access if preferred by the new owner. Sunken living room with tiled flooring, woodburning fireplace insert, and access to an enclosed sunroom. Three full bedrooms on the main level, including the primary bedroom with attached full bath with a remodeled shower! Enjoy time in the lower-level family room with wired surround sound system and tv that remain with the home! Ample storage in the lower level for convenience! The backyard has is enclosed with long term composite fencing. The detached has a separate electric panel for projects, 220 outlet, and cooling!
