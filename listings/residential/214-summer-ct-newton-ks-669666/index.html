Property Description

Check out this edge of town, 5 bed 3 bath new home in Summer Crossing. You'll love the open floor plan for the living area that has great lighting from the windows, plus a mudroom with built in bench and hooks. In the kitchen you'll find soft close staggard kitchen cabinets with crown molding, solid surface countertops, and stainless-steel appliances. The split bedroom plan features a vaulted ceiling master bedroom with ensuite bathroom and walk in closet. The hall bath has an added transom window for extra lighting. Easily entertain guests out on the concrete patio easily accessible from your living room and kitchen. The daylight basement has 2 fully finished bedrooms, bath and family room with 2 additional daylight windows. Extra finishes on the exterior include guttering, a wide approach driveway, larger garage with additional door to the backyard and decorative timber in the gable. Summer Crossing is a great neighborhood that is low traffic and just a short walk from lakeside walking paths, splash pad and playground park. Taxes are estimated. Pictures are from similar home.
