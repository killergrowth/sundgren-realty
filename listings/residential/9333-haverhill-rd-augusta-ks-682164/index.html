Property Description

RARE BUSINESS AND INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY ON OVER 40 ACRES IN AUGUSTA, KS! Discover an exceptional property offering a unique combination of established commercial event space, expansive acreage, and investment potential. This one-of-a-kind property features two distinctive barn-style event venues, a farmhouse, and over 40 acres of scenic Kansas countryside. The two venue spaces provide beautiful settings for weddings, receptions, private celebrations, and special events, offering flexibility to accommodate a variety of gatherings. The farmhouse serves as an additional amenity, typically utilized as a getting-ready space for wedding parties and overnight accommodations associated with events. An exceptional advantage for prospective investors and business owners is the established event calendar, with 33 events already scheduled for 2027 and all existing bookings to transfer with the sale. This provides an incoming owner with a valuable foundation of contracted future business, offering immediate revenue potential and an opportunity to build upon an established operation. Beyond the event venues, the expansive acreage offers additional potential for agricultural, recreational, residential, or future commercial uses. Whether you're an entrepreneur looking to take over an established venue business, an investor seeking a unique commercial property, or a buyer envisioning a versatile live/work opportunity, this property offers tremendous flexibility. Enjoy the peaceful setting of the countryside while remaining conveniently located near Augusta and surrounding communities. A rare opportunity to acquire acreage, improvements, and an established event venue operation in one remarkable offering. Schedule your private showing and explore everything this property has to offer! All intended uses and development opportunities are subject to buyer verification and applicable zoning requirements.
