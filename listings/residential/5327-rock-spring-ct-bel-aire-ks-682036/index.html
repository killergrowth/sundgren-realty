Property Description

Welcome home to 5327 N Rock Spring Ct in Bel Aire! This inviting ranch-style home offers a wonderful combination of comfortable main-floor living, additional finished living space, and desirable features throughout. Built in 2017, this home features 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, with 2 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms on the main level and 2 additional bedrooms and a full bathroom in the finished basement. The main-floor living room features a gas fireplace, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere for relaxing or gathering with family and friends. The kitchen features granite countertops, adding a timeless touch to the heart of the home. Downstairs, the finished basement provides even more room to spread out, complete with a second gas fireplace in the family room. With two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom, the lower level offers excellent flexibility for guests, hobbies, entertaining, or additional living arrangements. Step outside to enjoy the deck overlooking the fenced backyard, offering a great space for outdoor gatherings, morning coffee, or simply unwinding at the end of the day. The yard has recently been aerated and seeded in preparation for spring, and the property also features an underground sprinkler system, scheduled for winterization this week. The attached three-car garage provides plenty of room for vehicles, storage, and additional flexibility. Location is another standout feature, with Northeast Magnet located directly across the street! Conveniently situated in Bel Aire, this home combines functional living space, desirable amenities, and a location that adds even more appeal. With four bedrooms, three full bathrooms, two gas fireplaces, a finished basement, granite countertops, a deck, fenced backyard, underground sprinkler system, and a three-car garage, this home offers plenty of room to live, relax, and entertain. Come see everything 5327 N Rock Spring Ct has to offer!
