Property Description

Welcome to AeroPlains, Salina’s newest neighborhood, where modern construction meets comfort, convenience, and a thoughtfully designed floor plan! This beautiful, move-in-ready townhome offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and exceptional flexibility with TWO spacious master suites. The main floor features an inviting living area that flows seamlessly into the dining space and kitchen, complete with a breakfast bar and pantry. Main-floor laundry adds everyday convenience, while the primary suite offers a full bath, walk-in shower, and sliding glass doors leading to the back patio. An additional main-floor bedroom and full bath provide the perfect setup for guests, a home office, or extra living space. Upstairs, discover a generous second master suite with a walk-in closet and full bath, plus an adjoining loft area ideal for a reading nook, hobby space, or additional living area. A two-car attached garage and storm shelter add even more practicality. Outside, enjoy the ease of a fully established yard with sod, sprinkler system, and landscaping already in place. With modern design, versatile living spaces, and low-maintenance convenience, this exceptional townhome is ready for you to call it home.
