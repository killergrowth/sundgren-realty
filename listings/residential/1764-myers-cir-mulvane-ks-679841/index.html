***Motivated Sellers*** Welcome home to this beautifully maintained, move-in ready ranch-style home situated in an established and highly desirable Mulvane neighborhood! Perfect for anyone searching for small-town charm with just a quick drive to Derby and Wichita amenities, this property features 3 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and a full finished basement, all with NO SPECIAL TAXES! From the moment you arrive, you will appreciate the major exterior and mechanical upgrades already underway. Brand-new siding is scheduled to be installed by 11/20, followed by all-new exterior paint by 12/4. Buyers will have the exciting opportunity to choose the exterior paint colors before that date! Combined with a brand-new roof and a new HVAC system, this home offers standout curb appeal, ultimate efficiency, and financial peace of mind for years to come. Step inside to find a warm, inviting living room with an open-concept feel that flows seamlessly into the heart of the home: an expansive dine-in kitchen boasting beautiful real hardwood floors and plenty of counter space for family meals and entertaining. Main-level convenience is maximized with a dedicated laundry room and mudroom combo located right off the garage. The main level also hosts a huge primary suite serving as a private retreat, complete with a large walk-in closet and a spacious en-suite bathroom featuring a relaxing corner tub and separate shower. Downstairs, the fully finished basement dramatically expands your living space.
