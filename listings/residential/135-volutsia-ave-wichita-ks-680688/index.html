Property Description

Delightful and full of character, this 3 bedroom, 1 bath bungalow has had only two owners since the late 1950s! With a newer roof (2021) and newer HVAC (approx 1 yr), shared single car driveway to a 2 car detached garage with a neighbor-friendly middle wall, the fun has just begun! A covered front porch and stunning windows beckon you and an original solid wood door opens to a beautiful wood-floored living area with a built-in bookcase and electric fireplace! Turn to the right and enter the old-style formal dining room complete with original oak trim! The refreshed kitchen gives plenty of workspace, a coffee corner, and access to the side house entry foyer! Three exceptionally-sized bedrooms and 1 bath are at your disposal here before you head to the upstairs bonus room for crafting, composing, or relaxing...window unit remains, but bring your own warmth! Your unfinished basement (14'11x16' laundry and 14'x15' furnace area) houses a washer, dryer, separate wash sink, and the handy laundry chute depository! Close to Wesley, doctors, shopping, and more! We think you'll love the style, the space, and the location! Just give a call or text for your appointment to view!
