Property Description

Welcome to 2530 Glacier Drive, a spacious ranch tucked into The Park, a distinctive neighborhood filled with unique homes and a wonderfully tucked-away feel. A charming front courtyard and beautiful mature oak tree create an inviting first impression, while the home’s neutral exterior and wrought-iron fencing add to its timeless character. Inside, you’ll find three bedrooms on the main level, a spacious living room with Engineered hardwood flooring and a wood-burning fireplace, and a kitchen designed for gathering. A large granite island with gas cooktop anchors the kitchen, complemented by stainless steel appliances that remain with the home. Just off the kitchen is a separate laundry room with additional storage. The finished basement offers an impressive amount of additional living space with two separate family/rec areas, including one with a cozy fireplace. A distinctive wet bar with rustic wood and corrugated metal details gives the second gathering space plenty of personality and makes it a fun spot for entertaining. You’ll also find a non-conforming room offering flexible space for guests, an office, hobby room or workout area, along with a full bathroom featuring a shower. Outside, enjoy the privacy and charm of the front courtyard or head to the backyard, where a low-profile composite deck provides plenty of room for outdoor dining, relaxing and entertaining. With three bathrooms, multiple living spaces, two fireplaces and abundant room to spread out, this home offers a combination of space, character and versatility that is hard to find. Come discover this hidden gem in The Park!
