Property Description

Tucked away at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac in Derby's highly sought-after Stone Creek neighborhood, this beautifully maintained ranch offers the perfect blend of timeless style, generous living spaces, and an unbeatable location. With five bedrooms, three bathrooms, a three-car garage, and over 3,100 square feet, this is a home designed for both everyday comfort and effortless entertaining. A charming covered front porch welcomes you and provides the perfect spot for morning coffee or quiet evenings. From the moment you step inside, you'll notice the calm, inviting atmosphere created by light oak-look luxury vinyl plank flooring, crisp board and batten detailing, updated lighting, and oversized picture windows that flood the home with natural light. The open main living spaces feel bright and connected, while a stunning double-sided fireplace adds warmth and character to both the living room and dining area. The kitchen is truly the heart of the home, featuring a large island with wrap-around seating, a hidden walk-in pantry, energy-efficient double oven, and plenty of room for gathering with family and friends. The connected dining area is open and spacious, with plenty of natural light and a cozy fireplace. Just outside, the covered composite deck becomes a natural extension of the living area, perfect for morning coffee, summer dinners, or simply relaxing while overlooking the expansive, fully fenced backyard. Sitting on nearly .37 acres, there's plenty of room to play, garden, entertain, or simply enjoy having space to spread out. The spacious primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with a tray ceiling, large picture window, and a thoughtfully designed ensuite complete with dual vanities, a jetted soaking tub, separate shower, private water closet, and walk-in closet. The split-bedroom floor plan provides privacy, with two additional bedrooms and a full bath on the main level. Downstairs, the daylight basement is made for hosting game days, movie nights, and holiday gatherings. The sprawling family room also features a stylish wet bar with striped tile accents, counter seating, and space for a full-size refrigerator. Two additional bedrooms, another full bath, and abundant storage make this lower level as functional as it is inviting. Life in Stone Creek means more than just a beautiful home. You're just moments from Stone Creek Elementary, directly across from Derby Golf & Country Club with its private golf course, pools, fitness facilities, and clubhouse (membership required), and minutes from Derby's favorite shopping, restaurants, and everyday conveniences. Thoughtful updates include tasteful front landscaping, a new front sprinkler system, and backyard drainage improvements. Additionally, all kitchen appliances plus the washer and dryer remain with the home, making this move-in-ready property one you'll love from day one.
