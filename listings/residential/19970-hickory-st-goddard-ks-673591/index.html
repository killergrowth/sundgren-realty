About This Property

This one-of-a-kind luxury estate seamlessly blends modern elegance, exceptional craftsmanship, and resort-style living on just over 2 private acres just minutes from west Wichita or Goddard. Designed with clean architectural lines and thoughtfully curated spaces, this extraordinary home offers over 3500 square feet of refined living paired with unparalleled garage and storage space. Car enthusiasts and hobbyists will appreciate the rare dual "his and hers" six-car garages, plus an additional oversized garage capable of accommodating 6 to 8 more vehicles, RV's, or recreational equipment for a total of 16+ parking spaces. Every garage space is fully finished with built-in cabinetry, versatile workspaces, epoxy floors and room to add vehicle lifts for even greater capacity. Only three years old, this builder-owned custom home showcases 22 foot vaulted ceilings spanning the living room and kitchen, anchored by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling European Ledge Stone fireplace. Built with efficiency and durability in mind, the home features 2x6 construction with additional insulation throughout. The gourmet kitchen is designed to impress, featuring premium Thermador appliances including a gas cook top, triple ovens, industrial-sized refrigerator and dishwasher. A remarkable 7'x16' walk-in pantry functions as a room of it's own, offering exceptional storage and organization. The luxurious primary suite delivers a true retreat experience with a spa-inspired bath, custom tile work and expansive custom closets complete with built-ins and a quartz-topped oversized island. Every inch of this home reflects intentional design, from the stunning stone and tile finishes to the oversized windows and doors that flood the home with natural light. Indoor and outdoor living merge effortlessly through he massive 16'x8' California sliding door leading to the resort in the outdoor entertaining space. Enjoy the fully equipped outdoor kitchen with gas grill, linear fireplace and 65" TV, all adjacent to the spectacular 20'x44' heated Gunite pool with sun deck and automatic safety cover. Elegant travertine tile surrounds the pool and patio areas, keeping your feet cooler on hot summer days. Additional amenities include: Private pool storage room (10'x10'); Convenient Full pool bath with dedicated towel drop zone; Custom privacy fencing; expansive motor court between the east and west wings, and a 13'x17' safe room on the west garage wing. No HOA dues and NO Specials! This exceptional property is close to restaurants and shopping, just 10 minutes to west Wichita and only 5 minutes to Goddard. Renwick schools.
