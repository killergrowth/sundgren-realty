Property Description

NEARLY HALF AN ACRE, A 12x24 WORKSHOP, AND AN OUTDOOR ENTERTAINING SPACE YOU'LL LOVE! Welcome to 103 Brookview Ct, where spacious living, unique features, and plenty of room to spread out come together on a quiet cul-de-sac! Situated across two generous lots totaling approximately .49 acres, this 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers a fully fenced backyard, a 2-car attached garage, two large storage sheds, and an incredible outdoor setup that's hard to find! Step inside to discover beautiful hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and a functional floor plan with plenty of space for everyday living and entertaining. The main living room flows into the kitchen and dining areas, creating a welcoming atmosphere for gatherings. The kitchen features rich wood cabinetry, and plenty of cabinet and counter space. The main-floor primary suite offers a private ensuite bathroom and a spacious walk-in closet. You'll also appreciate the convenience of a separate main-floor laundry room, a feature that sets this floor plan apart. Downstairs is where the entertaining continues! The finished basement features an expansive family room with multiple seating areas, a wet bar, an additional bedroom, and a full bathroom. Whether you're hosting game nights, watching movies, or simply looking for extra living space, there's room for it all! Two additional unfinished storage areas are cleverly tucked away behind custom-built bookshelf doors, adding a unique touch while keeping storage out of sight. And just wait until you see the backyard! Step outside to a spacious 32' x 15' covered patio, perfect for outdoor dining, relaxing, or entertaining. An additional 18' x 8' sitting area surrounds a large water feature, creating another inviting place to unwind. The fully fenced backyard stretches across the two lots, offering plenty of open space for outdoor activities, gardening, or future possibilities. Need a workshop or extra storage? You've got options! The property features two large sheds, including a 12' x 24' workshop complete with electricity, insulation, and a loft accessible by stairs. An underground sprinkler system supplied by its own well adds convenience when maintaining the spacious lawn. Come explore everything 103 Brookview Ct has to offer!
