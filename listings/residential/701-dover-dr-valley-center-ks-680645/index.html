Property Description

Extremely well cared for and move-in ready, this home is ready to be yours in Valley Center School District! Conveniently located just off Ford Street, you'll pull up to adorable curb appeal, mature trees, and a great neighborhood! Inside, you'll find a formal dining room, a kitchen with a built in space for a breakfast/coffee nook, and a spacious living room featuring a beautiful fireplace and direct access to the backyard. The upstairs features three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, main floor laundry, and great cabinet/storage space! Downstairs, you'll find living space and another 1,000 square feet of unfinished area. A buyer can finish the additional space to add equity or keep it all for storage. You'll love being with family and friends in the fenced in back yard! There is patio space and a designated area to hook in your hot tub. Recent updates include a new water heater and fresh exterior paint in September of this year. New lighting throughout and fresh interior paint were completed two years ago. Great pride in homeownership is hard to find now days, and this home has it! You can't beat the location, price, and school district! Welcome home!
