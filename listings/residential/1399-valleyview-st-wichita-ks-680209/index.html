Property Description

Fresh updates and flexible living space make this west Wichita home one to see! Much of the interior has been recently professionally painted, and new LVP flooring plus updated bathrooms give the home a refreshed feel. The main level offers a comfortable living room, kitchen with an eating area, and access to the deck.
