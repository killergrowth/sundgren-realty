Property Description

Welcome to 2609 S Elizabeth, where comfort, convenience, and charm come together to create the perfect place to call home. This inviting 3-bedroom ranch offers easy single-level living and sits on a spacious lot in an established South Wichita neighborhood. Step outside to enjoy the fully fenced backyard, complete with a private patio that's perfect for summer barbecues, morning coffee, or simply unwinding at the end of the day.
