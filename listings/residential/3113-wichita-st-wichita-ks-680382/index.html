Property Description

Welcome to 3113 S. Wichita St.! This charming 3-bedroom, 1-bath ranch-style home offers a combination of original character, recent improvements, and plenty of potential to make it your own! Inside, you’ll find beautiful hardwood flooring and a kitchen featuring original wood cabinetry, a built-in electric cooktop, oven, and a brand-new kitchen sink. The basement provides additional living space with newly installed luxury vinyl plank flooring, along with a generous unfinished area offering plenty of room for storage or hobbies. Recent improvements include a brand-new water heater, while a working attic fan adds another convenient feature. Outside, enjoy a spacious, fenced backyard with mature shade trees, a storage shed, and plenty of room for outdoor activities. An attached garage adds everyday convenience. Whether you're looking for a place to call home and add your personal touches, or adding a rental property to your portfolio, this property has plenty to offer! The home is being sold AS IS. The surrounding fence belongs to the property, the refrigerator does not remain with the home.
