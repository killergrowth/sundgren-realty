Property Description

Welcome to this completely renovated 4 bed, 2 bath property on a corner lot with an RV concrete pad & hookup. What else could you want? The kitchen has newer appliances, and a stackable washer and dryer on the main floor. There is a main floor bedroom off of the kitchen. Two bedrooms upstairs with hardwood floors and a renovated bathroom with onyx shower surround and corian vanity countertop. The lower level is two rooms converted into one large room with an egress window and can be used as a large rec room, a bedroom, or both. The lower level bathroom offers mid-century modern tile with a stand up shower. Outside features a covered patio, a concrete pad for a fire pit, hot tub, or small pool, turf for easy maintenance, and a storage shed. Also, freshly painted interior and exterior and a new roof.
