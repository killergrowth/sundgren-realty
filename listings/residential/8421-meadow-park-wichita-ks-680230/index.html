Property Description

Welcome to the home that has room for everyone and everything! Tucked away at the back of a quiet cul-de-sac in Fossil Rim within the Maize South school district, this impressive 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home offers more than 2,300 sq. ft. of above-ground living space, a full walkout basement and a backyard made for Kansas summers! From the moment you step inside, the grand entrance makes an impression with beautiful barrel ceilings leading into a spacious living room centered around the fireplace and built-ins. The split-bedroom floor plan provides wonderful privacy, with the main-floor bedrooms positioned on opposite sides of the home. The grand primary suite offers plenty of space to unwind with a coffered ceiling, private deck access and a spacious ensuite featuring a soaking tub, separate tiled walk-in shower and large walk-in closet that conveniently connects to the laundry room. A true retreat! If you love to entertain, you'll appreciate something you don't find every day in three incredible dining spaces! Enjoy casual meals just off the kitchen, host holidays in the formal dining room, or take dinner outside to the screened-in deck overlooking your backyard oasis. Head downstairs to a beautifully remodeled walkout basement designed for more entertaining! The gorgeous modern wet bar features a refrigerator and dishwasher and opens to a spacious rec room ready for game days, movie nights and gatherings. Two additional oversized bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bathroom, while a half bathroom provides added convenience for guests. Don’t miss the storage room as the amount of space it offers is hard to beat! Outside, get ready for the backyard of the summer! The gorgeous pool is ready for hot Kansas days, while the screened-in deck gives you the perfect place to relax and entertain. A brand-new roof adds peace of mind, and the 3-car garage plus oversized driveway means there's plenty of parking when everyone comes over to enjoy your new home. With incredible space inside and out, a private cul-de-sac setting in Fossil Rim and convenient access to everything West Wichita has to offer, this is a home built for living, entertaining and making memories!
