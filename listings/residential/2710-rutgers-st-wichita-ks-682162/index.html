Property Description

Beautiful 5BR, 3BA home in a fantastic Northwest Wichita location, just behind NewMarket Square and within the highly desirable Maize School District! This home features an open floor plan and an impressive list of updates, including a granite kitchen, brand-new carpet throughout, newer interior and exterior paint, updated plumbing fixtures, and a brand-new roof! The spacious main floor offers an inviting layout that's perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Downstairs, you'll find a large family/rec room with a masonry fireplace, 2 additional bedrooms, and a full bath. Step outside and enjoy the incredible newly poured concrete patio spanning the entire width of the home! The east-facing backyard provides plenty of afternoon and evening shade, making it ideal for outdoor entertaining. Additional exterior amenities include an automatic sprinkler system with irrigation well and a detached storage shed. And you can't beat the location! Within walking distance of NewMarket Square's restaurants, shopping, and entertainment, plus quick access to major highways. With extensive updates, great outdoor living space, Maize schools, and one of Northwest Wichita's most convenient locations, this home checks all the boxes!
