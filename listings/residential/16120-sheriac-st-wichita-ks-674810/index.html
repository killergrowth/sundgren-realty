Property Description

Welcome to a home where everyday living feels effortless. This beautifully maintained ranch combines thoughtful design, stylish updates, and the lifestyle amenities buyers are searching for! Step inside and you'll immediately notice the tall ceilings, abundant natural light, and open-concept layout that creates an inviting flow throughout the main living area. The kitchen was designed to be the heart of the home, featuring granite countertops, an oversized island, and a hidden walk-in pantry that keeps everything organized while maintaining a clean, polished look. The main floor offers three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a beautiful bathroom and generous walk-in closet. Downstairs, the finished walkout basement expands your living space with a large family room, wet bar, two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and room to gather, entertain, or simply unwind. Outside, enjoy evenings on the covered deck overlooking the backyard. Beyond the property itself, Talia offers an unmatched lifestyle with a clubhouse, pool, pickleball courts, playground, and more, all within the Goddard School District. This move-in ready home offers the benefits of newer construction without the wait. Welcome home!
