Property Description

Welcome to 317 N Doris St, a beautifully designed brand-new half duplex offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a 2-car garage, and 1,293 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space. Featuring modern finishes, distinctive design details, and a fresh contemporary feel, this home offers a level of style that stands out from typical new construction. Step inside to an inviting, open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area with modern gray LVP flooring flowing throughout. The kitchen is a true standout, featuring a large island, rich green cabinetry, a light tile backsplash, white quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and stylish brass-toned cabinet hardware that adds warmth and contrast. Modern lighting, recessed lighting, and ceiling fans complement the clean, contemporary design throughout the home. The attention to detail continues in both bathrooms, where quartz countertops and beautifully tiled surfaces create a polished, upscale look. The guest bathroom features distinctive black-and-white tile flooring and a full tile shower and tub surround, while the primary bathroom offers a stylish full-tile walk-in shower. Additional highlights include decorative interior doors with designer-inspired detailing, modern black closet rods, a spacious walk-in closet in the primary bedroom, and a separate laundry room. Outside, enjoy a generously sized backyard with plenty of room to spread out, relax, and make the most of your outdoor space. An 8' x 8' concrete patio off the primary bedroom will also be installed prior to closing, creating a great spot to enjoy the backyard. Please note that final grading has not yet been completed, particularly in the backyard. The property will be fully graded soon, and the front yard and a portion of the backyard will be seeded with grass. With its eye-catching green kitchen, elevated finishes, spacious backyard, and thoughtful details throughout, this brand-new home delivers modern style without sacrificing functionality.
