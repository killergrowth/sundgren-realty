Property Description

Meticulously maintained and tastefully updated, this stunning story-and-a-half home occupies a truly one-of-a-kind lot on a quiet street in Reflection Ridge. Backing to the 17th and 18th holes of the golf course—with the lush driving range bordering the north side—the home is surrounded by mature trees, manicured grounds, and remarkable privacy. The peaceful setting feels timeless, elegant, and worlds away from the everyday. A sweeping entry welcomes you through beautiful French doors into a light-filled interior with soaring vaulted ceilings. The spacious living room features a gas fireplace and expansive windows framing picturesque views of the lush landscaping. The well-equipped kitchen offers granite countertops, a double oven, range hood, and generous workspace. Just beyond, the inviting hearth room and breakfast nook feature wood floors, a second gas fireplace, and large picture windows—creating the perfect setting for entertaining, preparing dinner, or enjoying a quiet morning at home. Step outside onto the large covered deck, where mature trees and sweeping golf course views provide an exceptional backdrop. A separate formal dining room is ideal for family dinners, holiday gatherings, and special occasions. The luxurious main-floor primary suite includes a spacious bedroom and private bath with dual vanities, a soaking tub, and tiled shower. A laundry room with granite folding counter and a guest half bath complete the main level. Upstairs, you will find three additional spacious bedrooms and a large hall bath with dual vanities. The fully finished walkout basement continues to impress with an expansive family room, sitting area with gas fireplace, game-table space, wet bar, and access to a covered patio. A massive fifth bedroom and full bath complete the lower level. With extraordinary privacy, elegant updates, and unforgettable golf course views, this exceptional Reflection Ridge home is one you will not want to miss!
