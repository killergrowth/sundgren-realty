Property Description

Spacious 4-Bedroom Home with Two Primary Suites and Flexible Living Space. Welcome to this spacious 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home, thoughtfully designed for comfort, versatility, and convenience. Hardwood floors enhance the main living area, while durable tile flooring extends through the kitchen and bathrooms. Recently refreshed interior paint creates a bright, inviting atmosphere throughout. The home’s two generously sized primary suites are a standout feature, each offering a full bathroom and walk-in closet. The main-level suite includes separate exterior access, providing excellent flexibility for a private guest retreat, home studio, or multigenerational living arrangement. Recent updates add to the home’s appeal and peace of mind. The roof, gutters, and water heater were new when the seller purchased the property in 2023, and the HVAC system was replaced by the seller that same year. Outside, the fenced backyard offers a private setting for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying time outdoors. A detached two car garage provides room for vehicles and storage. Conveniently located within walking distance of local shopping and restaurants, this home combines spacious living, adaptable accommodations, and easy access to everyday amenities.
