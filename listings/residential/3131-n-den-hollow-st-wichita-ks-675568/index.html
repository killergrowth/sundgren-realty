Property Description

Located in desirable Fox Ridge Estates, this custom full-brick ranch offers nearly 2,500 square feet on the main floor plus a full finished walk-out basement, 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and an attached finished 3-car garage. A new CertainTeed Presidential Shake IR roof will be installed along with new wire mesh gutter covers, and the home is loaded with energy-efficient upgrades including a high-efficiency Lennox Elite HVAC system with one new AC unit, Milgard SunCoatMAX argon gas-filled low-E insulated windows, TechShield aluminum-laminated roof sheathing designed to block up to 97% of radiant heat from entering the attic, DuPont Tyvek ThermaWrap, a 10 mil polyolefin geomembrane vapor/radon barrier under the basement floor, sealed sump pump vents to the exterior, Pella insulated storm doors, custom window well covers, and a 220 outlet in the garage ready for EV charging. The main level features formal living and dining rooms, a spacious kitchen with large eating bar, walk-in pantry, custom maple cabinetry, double oven, built-in desk, hardwood floors, and an informal dining/hearth room with gas fireplace. The large primary suite includes two walk-in closets, dual sinks, and a separate tub and shower. The finished walk-out basement offers a large family room with gas fireplace, impressive wet bar with dishwasher, sink, and disposal, a separate game room area, two bedrooms, one full bathroom, and an unfinished room with potential for a 6th bedroom. Additional highlights include a large laundry room with sink, abundant storage, neutral finishes, and a clean, move-in ready condition. Enjoy one of the prettiest private backyards you will find, surrounded by mature oversized trees and enclosed with wrought iron fencing. Fox Ridge is conveniently located near Maize South Schools and New Market Square, Wichita’s largest open-air shopping center, with dining, retail, Sam’s Club, and everyday conveniences nearby. Community amenities include two swimming pools, including a main pool with diving board, kiddie pool, and lifeguards, extensive paved walking paths, playground, stocked fishing lakes, native grasses, privacy berms, scenic lake views, and over one-third of the development dedicated to nature reserves and lakes.
