Property Description

The only double lot in St. Ives means extra parking and plenty of beautiful green space without the hassle of mowing or watering! Easy living awaits on Killarney Court in this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath home featuring multiple living areas and a wonderful addition that blends seamlessly with the original home. The sunroom addition just off the kitchen feels like it was always meant to be there! Both decks have been replaced, creating even more room to enjoy outdoor living and the beautiful double lot. Other great features include a roof replaced in 2020 and an efficient tankless water heater for endless hot water. Add award-winning Andover schools, Sedgwick County taxes, and a location just a golf cart ride from Crestview Country Club, with easy access to east Wichita shopping and dining, and you've got a winning combination!
