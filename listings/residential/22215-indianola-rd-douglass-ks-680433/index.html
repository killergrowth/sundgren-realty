Property Description

Every house has a story, and this one is filled with memories of childhood, hard work, creativity, and love. It’s a place where laughter filled the rooms, imaginations ran wild, and life unfolded in all the little moments that make a house a home. Now, the next chapter is waiting to be written. Set on 4.8 acres, this remodeled manufactured home on a permanent foundation offers room to spread out, make memories, and make it your own. With two family rooms, a third non-conforming bedroom on the main level, and a partially finished basement - there’s space for everyone. The attached garage is big enough for a boat, two vehicles, a mower, and an ATV. While outside, you will find mature trees, a partially fenced yard, and a 32x30 concrete shop with electricity for even more room to create, grow, garden, and explore. This property has been more than just a house. It’s been a home filled with love, learning, and laughter, and it’s ready for someone new to bring their own dreams, traditions, and memories to life. The next chapter could be yours. Home to be sold as-is, in its current condition.
