Property Description

Enjoy comfortable one-level living in this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch home in Newton, conveniently located near Newton High School. With 2,726 total square feet, the home offers an accessible layout, a 2-car garage, and plenty of room to spread out. The dream kitchen features abundant counter space, cabinetry, and a pantry area. Main-level laundry adds convenience, with additional hookups in the basement. Downstairs, you’ll find generous storage and additional livable space. Relax or entertain in the fenced backyard, complete with a Trex deck. HVAC and a water heater less than two years old, and a partial roof replacement completed in September 2024 (remaining back portion is only 10 years old).
