Property Description

Honey, stop the car! This home is an absolute showstopper! Located just outside of Eureka, KS, off US-54 Highway, this property is one you won't want to miss! Upon arrival, you'll be greeted by a beautiful stone exterior and an impact-resistant roof that's only 4 years old. Step inside to discover an inviting open-concept main floor designed for comfortable living and entertaining. The spacious living room, kitchen, and dining area flow together beautifully, highlighted by a cozy wood-burning fireplace and stunning quartz kitchen countertops. If you love to cook and entertain, you'll appreciate all the space this kitchen has to offer! Just off the main living area, enjoy a peaceful sunroom overlooking a beautiful tillable field. The main floor also features two bedrooms, each with its own private bathroom, along with convenient main-floor laundry. The finished basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring two spacious bonus rooms with a shared Jack-and-Jill bathroom. The recreation area is perfect for entertaining, complete with a wet bar and a second fireplace. Additional features include a central vacuum system, newer Pella windows, a 3-year-old water heater, an oversized insulated attached 2-car garage, and a 20' x 18' detached garage. This home has been exceptionally well-maintained and is ready for its next family to make it their own! Schedule your private showing today!
