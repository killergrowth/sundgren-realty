Property Description

Step into a truly one-of-a-kind property where history, character, and wide-open Kansas landscapes come together. Nestled on 20 acres of picturesque pastureland, this beautifully restored 1918 Craftsman farmhouse offers an extraordinary opportunity to own a private rural retreat unlike any other. Boasting approximately 4,000 square feet of living space, the three-story home features SIX bedrooms and FOUR full bathrooms. Other details include- a beautiful wrap around porch to enjoy the Kansas sunsets, impressive 10-foot ceilings on both the main and second floors. Main floor master bedroom with attached en-suite with double sinks, a large walk-in shower, and two walk-in closets. All 6 spacious bedrooms have walk-in closets, a second floor sun room with a door leading out to the balcony, beautiful kitchen with quartz countertops, pantry, double ovens, farmhouse sink and beaming with natural light, large formal dining with built in book cases, main floor laundry and mud room, oversized 3-car attached garage. The finished attic provides additional living space full of character, and a kids dream play room! This home's original craftsmanship shines throughout with hardwood floors, rich woodwork, glass doors, and large windows that fill the home with natural light. Thoughtfully restored to blend historic charm with modern comfort, the home retains the original architectural details while incorporating modern updates that complement its timeless farmhouse appeal. Surrounded by complete privacy, the 20 acres of property has views in every direction. Expansive wheat fields stretch to the west, while a tree-lined creek, and wooded area perfect for hunting. Additional improvements include: Five livestock corrals with durable pipe-and-cable fencing 60' x 40' Quonset building with high-voltage power and drive-through access Historic grain silo and classic windmill Hay barn and lean-to Fully fenced pastureland ideal for livestock, recreation, or agricultural use. The farm currently supports cattle and horses and offers endless possibilities for ranching, hobby farming, hunting, outdoor recreation, event hosting, or simply enjoying a peaceful country lifestyle. Additional 50 acres available (See MLS 674759) includes two scenic ponds create a tranquil natural setting rich with wildlife and beauty. Do not miss your opportunity to own one of Kansas' premier homes!
