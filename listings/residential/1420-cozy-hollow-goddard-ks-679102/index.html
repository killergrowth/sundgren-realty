Property Description

Some homes simply check the boxes. This one creates an experience. Built by Chapman Custom Homes, this thoughtfully designed residence showcases elevated finishes, exceptional craftsmanship, and timeless design throughout. Offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and 1,600 square feet on the main level, every detail has been carefully selected to create a home that feels both luxurious and welcoming. From the moment you arrive, you’ll appreciate the striking curb appeal featuring black-accented front windows, cedar porch posts, brick detailing, professional landscaping, sprinkler system, irrigation well, a spacious 3-car garage, and an expansive covered composite deck designed for relaxing or entertaining outdoors. The full-width driveway, additional patio space, and covered outdoor living area provide the perfect extension of the home’s interior living spaces. Step inside to discover an open-concept floor plan filled with natural light and custom finishes throughout. The living room serves as the heart of the home, featuring a raised ceiling, stunning floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, custom wood hearth, floating shelving with accent lighting, and an impressive four-panel sliding glass door that seamlessly blends indoor and outdoor living. Luxury vinyl flooring flows throughout the main living areas, creating both beauty and durability for everyday life. The kitchen was designed to impress, offering quartz countertops, dramatic waterfall island edges, upgraded cabinetry, designer lighting, a custom wood vent hood, under-cabinet lighting, tile backsplash, and a beautiful wood ceiling detail above the island that adds warmth and character. Whether hosting guests or enjoying a quiet evening at home, this space effortlessly combines function and style. Just beyond the kitchen, the spacious walk-in pantry offers custom shelving, appliance stations, stained wood countertops, built-in spice storage, and abundant organization options. The dining area continues the home’s attention to detail with raised ceilings, transom windows, and custom stair features that add architectural interest and charm. The private owner’s suite offers a true retreat, featuring a custom accent wall, transom window, and a spa-inspired bathroom designed for relaxation. Enjoy dual vanities with quartz countertops, framed mirrors, designer fixtures, and an oversized custom tiled shower complete with bench seating, built-in shelving, and a frameless glass enclosure. The spacious walk-in closet offers custom shelving and exceptional storage. Two additional bedrooms provide generous closet space and convenient access to a beautifully finished hall bathroom featuring quartz countertops, framed mirrors, and quality finishes throughout. Additional highlights include a custom drop zone bench, pocket doors, upgraded fixtures and hardware, painted shelving, humidifier, and countless designer touches that reflect the quality and craftsmanship Chapman Custom Homes is known for. The unfinished view-out basement offers incredible future potential, providing space for additional bedrooms, a recreation room, wet bar, and bathroom, allowing the next owner the flexibility to expand and customize the home to fit their lifestyle. Every space has been thoughtfully designed to deliver comfort, functionality, and lasting style. Taxes and special assessments are estimated.
