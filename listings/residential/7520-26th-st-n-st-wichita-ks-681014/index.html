Property Description

Spacious Ranch with Multiple Living Areas and Wonderful Outdoor Space! This spacious ranch-style home offers nearly 2,500 square feet of comfortable living space, with a versatile layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. The main floor features a formal living and dining room combination, plus a welcoming family room with a cozy fireplace that opens to the kitchen and casual dining area. Enjoy views of the backyard and easy access to the screened-in patio, creating a great spot to relax or entertain. Three bedrooms, two full baths, and a convenient powder room are located on the main level. The primary suite features a private bath with a shower and a walk-in closet, while main-floor laundry adds everyday convenience. The finished basement expands the living space with a large family room and second fireplace, a full bath, and a finished bonus room ideal for a variety of uses. Two generous storage areas provide excellent flexibility, including one with built-in shelving and another well suited for a workshop or exercise room. Outside, the privacy-fenced backyard features mature trees that enhance the established setting. Conveniently located near grocery stores, dining, and shopping, with easy access to K-96 and Kellogg, this well-maintained home offers space, versatility, and convenience.
