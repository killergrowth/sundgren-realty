Property Description

Golf course living at its finest in the beautiful Willowbend neighborhood! Imagine your daily commute on a scenic drive through this established community, surrounding yourself with beautiful fountain-adorned lakes, well-maintained commons areas, and lush Willowbend golf course greens, arriving at this stately home backing to the 6th tee box. Sweeping views of the 5th and 6th fairways can be enjoyed from nearly every room, and the sunsets are stunning! As you step inside, the spacious living room features a grand double-sided fireplace that connects the living area with the kitchen and dining spaces. You'll love the trendy color palette, beautiful wood floors, and updated fixtures throughout. The kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, a convenient pull-out drawer pantry, and easy access to the main-floor laundry room. Two bedrooms are located on the main floor, including a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceiling and direct access to the expansive composite deck. Downstairs, the possibilities are endless! A second fireplace anchors the generous family / entertaining space, complete with wet bar and plenty of room to gather. Two sizeable bedrooms offer beautiful golf course views, while an additional non-conforming bedroom with closet provides flexible space for an exercise room, playroom, or whatever your family needs. A large storage room completes the floorplan. Recent improvements include a new HVAC system and composite deck within the past five years, a dishwasher less than one year old, and fresh exterior paint completed in August 2026. When you head out to your back deck at the end of the day, take in the incredible beauty of Willowbend Golf Course right in your backyard. Now open to the public, this fantastic 18-hole course makes it easy to book a tee time and enjoy the game just steps from home. This neighborhood and home have been well-maintained, with a wonderful sense of pride and community throughout. Come experience the views, space, and lifestyle that make Willowbend such a special place to call home! Seller is a licensed Realtor in the State of Kansas.
