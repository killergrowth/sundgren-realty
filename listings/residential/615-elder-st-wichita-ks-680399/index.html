Property Description

Add a rental property with income already in place to your investment portfolio! This tenant-occupied Wichita twin home offers 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and a 1-car garage, currently generating $1,100 per month in rental income. Renovated by the previous owner within the last three years, the home also features a new garage door and a newer roof that is less than a year old. The generously sized primary bedroom stands out with a walk-in closet and private half bath, while a separate laundry room adds everyday convenience. Seller-owned kitchen appliances will remain with the property, excluding the microwave. The tenant is responsible for all utilities and lawn care, helping simplify ownership. With a tenant already in place and several recent updates, this property is ready for its next investor to take advantage of the opportunity!
