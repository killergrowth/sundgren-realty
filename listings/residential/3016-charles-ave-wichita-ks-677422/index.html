Property Description

If you’ve been waiting for a home with space, a big backyard, and NO HOA, this might be the one that gets you moving! Sitting on just under a half-acre lot, this 3-bedroom home gives you room to spread out without giving up quick access to Wichita. Inside, you’ll find a bright living room, spacious bedrooms, an updated bathroom with tile flooring and a tiled tub/shower, and a kitchen with plenty of storage and stainless steel appliances. But the backyard may just steal the show! A brand-new sliding glass door opens to the new back deck, giving you the perfect setup for summer nights, weekend cookouts, or your morning coffee overlooking the oversized yard and mature trees. Add in an detached 2-car garage, no specials, no HOA, and easy access to K-96 and I-235, and this one checks some BIG boxes. Large lot properties with this kind of convenience don’t come along every day. Come see why this one deserves to be at the top of your list!
