Property Description

Set on a spacious 0.62-acre lot in Rose Hill, this 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers plenty of space both inside and out. The heart of the home features a gorgeous two-way fireplace shared between the living room and kitchen, creating a warm focal point and an inviting atmosphere. The spacious basement rec/family room provides even more room to relax, entertain, or gather, complete with another fireplace for added comfort and charm. Step outside to the covered back deck, the perfect spot to enjoy the surrounding space and spend time outdoors in any season. A 3-car garage provides ample room for vehicles, storage, hobbies, or additional workspace. With generous living areas, multiple fireplaces, a large lot, and plenty of room to spread out, this Rose Hill property is ready to welcome its next owner.
