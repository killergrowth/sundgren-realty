Property Description

Space, style, and room to make memories! This spacious 5-bedroom, 3-bath home offers a comfortable layout, modern updates, and plenty of room to gather, entertain, and spread out. Step inside to a welcoming living room that flows seamlessly into the dining area, complete with access to the back deck—making indoor and outdoor entertaining a breeze. The spacious, modern kitchen is ready to be the heart of your gatherings, featuring updated fixtures and plenty of room to bring everyone together. The main level offers a primary bedroom with a private en suite bathroom featuring a dual-sink vanity, along with two additional bedrooms that share convenient bathroom access. Head downstairs to discover even more living space, including a large rec/family room with a cozy fireplace, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a bonus room ready to fit your needs—whether that's a home office, hobby space, playroom, or workout area. With five bedrooms, three bathrooms and a 2-car garage this home offers the space and flexibility to make yourself right at home!
