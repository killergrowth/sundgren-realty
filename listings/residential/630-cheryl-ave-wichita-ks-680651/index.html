Property Description

Rare opportunity in an established neighborhood where homes seldom come on the market! This full-brick, 3-bedroom home sits on nearly half an acre and offers plenty of space inside and out. The main floor features 1 full bath, 1 half bath, a convenient laundry room, and a galley-style kitchen. New carpet and vinyl flooring have been installed throughout the home, making it move-in ready! The partially finished basement offers a spacious family room, an additional half bath, and a versatile craft or non-conforming room. Step outside to enjoy the large, fenced backyard with mature trees, a storage shed, and a spacious shaded patio perfect for relaxing or entertaining. While the interior features vintage finishes and wallpaper, this well-maintained home offers a wonderful opportunity to move right in and update to your personal style over time. With its generous lot, solid brick construction, and desirable location, this is a property you won't want to miss!
