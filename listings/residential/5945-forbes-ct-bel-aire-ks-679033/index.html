Property Description

This spacious patio home situated in the Iron Gate subdivision of Bel Aire spared no cost when it came to being built. 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and an oversized 3 car garage. No steps whatsoever throughout the property, as well as a roll-in shower and HEATED FLOORS in the master bath, that extend into the laundry room. Generous 3 tiered master closet with built in shelving. The kitchen is equipped with a gas range, large walk-in pantry, granite countertops, and a large island with seating space. Even without a basement storms pose no threat, as their is a 6x7 concrete storm room, doubling as storage/closet space. Every feature has a purpose and every room has a use. The view of the pond from within the screened in back-porch is perfect for early morning coffee or late night talks. Fully landscaped and irrigated with security system as well.
