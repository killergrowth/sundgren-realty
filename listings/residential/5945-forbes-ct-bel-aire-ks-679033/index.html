Property Description

Zero-Step Luxury Patio Home in Iron Gate, Bel Aire, KS: 3 Bed, 2 Bath, Oversized 3-Car Garage, Pond View. No expense was spared in building this spacious patio home in the Iron Gate subdivision of Bel Aire, Kansas. It offers 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and an oversized 3-car garage on a single level with no steps anywhere on the property, making it a true zero-entry, accessible home. The primary suite includes a roll-in shower and heated floors that continue into the laundry room. It also has a generous three-tier walk-in closet with built-in shelving. The kitchen features a gas range, granite countertops, a large walk-in pantry, and an oversized island with seating. The home has no basement, but a 6x7 concrete storm room provides peace of mind during Kansas storm season and doubles as storage or closet space. Every feature has a purpose, and every room has a use. Step out to the screened-in back porch overlooking the pond for morning coffee or late-night conversations. The property is fully landscaped with an irrigation system and includes a security system. Low-maintenance living: The all-encompassing HOA covers lawn care, snow removal, and all other exterior maintenance. Pre-inspected for buyer confidence: The homeowners had the home professionally inspected before listing, and buyers will receive the full inspection report.
