Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully updated 5 bedroom, 2 bathroom home in Derby! Thoughtfully renovated throughout, this home combines modern finishes with comfortable living spaces. The main level features freshly painted walls, hardwood flooring, new carpet and recessed lighting that creates a bright, inviting atmosphere. The updated kitchen showcases quartz countertops, a stylish tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances and a spacious island with additional seating. The open layout makes entertaining and everyday living a breeze. Two comfortable bedrooms, a full bathroom and a large laundry room complete the main floor. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious primary bedroom featuring two closets and convenient access to the updated bathroom, complete with tiled shower walls and a double vanity. An additional bedroom and a large hall closet provide even more living space and storage. The finished basement offers a comfortable family room, a 5th bedroom and unfinished storage space. Outside, enjoy the fenced backyard, recently added patio and sprinkler system designed for easy lawn maintenance and outdoor entertaining. With beautiful updates, versatile living spaces and plenty of storage, this Derby home is ready for your to enjoy!
