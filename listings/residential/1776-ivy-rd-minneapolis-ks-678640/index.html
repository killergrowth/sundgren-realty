Property Description

Welcome to 1776 Ivy Road, a beautiful country property offering the perfect blend of space, privacy, and small-town convenience in the sought-after Bennington School District. Situated on 7.6 acres, this 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom home offers approximately 1,700 square feet of comfortable living space with an open floor plan designed for everyday living and entertaining. Step inside to find a spacious, inviting layout with plenty of natural light and a convenient main-floor laundry. The covered front porch is the perfect place to start your morning with a cup of coffee while enjoying the sunrise and watching the abundant wildlife that surrounds the property. In the evenings, head out to the back deck and take in beautiful Kansas sunsets. Outside, the property truly shines. A 48 x 24 shop provides an excellent space for hobbies, equipment, projects, or storage and features a wood-burning stove for year-round comfort. The shop also includes a large pull-through overhang, offering additional covered space for vehicles, equipment, or outdoor entertaining. Multiple additional outbuildings provide even more storage and functionality, including a chicken coop for those looking to embrace a little country living. A newly added bar room that creates the perfect hangout space for relaxing, entertaining friends, watching the game, or simply enjoying a quiet evening at home.
