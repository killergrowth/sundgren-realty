Property Description

Renovate, build, or expand! 5 acres of opportunity between Derby and southeast Wichita! Situated just off blacktop Greenwich Road and a short drive from McConnell Air Force Base, this property combines a convenient location with space to renovate, build, or expand. Featuring an approximately 1800 square foot earth-sheltered home, this property will be offered at online-only auction. Location & Access: Enjoy acreage living with Derby and southeast Wichita close by! This location provides convenient access to shopping, dining, employment, and everyday services while offering room to spread out beyond a typical residential lot. Nearby Greenwich Road provides blacktop access to the surrounding area, and proximity to McConnell Air Force Base adds to the property's convenient position within the Wichita metro. Opportunity & Potential: Bring your ideas and explore the possibilities this 5-acre property offers. Fix up the existing home, explore building a new home, or consider adding a shop alongside the current residence. Whether your plans involve a renovation project or a different long-term vision for the acreage, the combination of location and usable space makes this a property worth a closer look. Earth-Sheltered Home & Acreage: Built in 1985, the existing home has custom architectural design and features 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, an attached garage, and approximately 1800 square feet of living space. Its earth-sheltered design helps moderate indoor temperatures, supporting comfortable conditions through hot Kansas summers and cold winters while potentially reducing heating and cooling demands. A water well and yard irrigation system provide practical benefits for maintaining the grounds. Auction & Buyer Information: This property will be offered at online-only auction on November 17th, 2026. An inspection report is available for potential buyers to review, providing an opportunity to learn more about the home's condition before bidding. The property is selling "as-is, where-is" with no contingencies.
