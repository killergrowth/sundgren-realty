Property Description

Enjoy the best of country living with the convenience of being just minutes from town, all within the desirable Buhler School District. Situated on nearly three acres, this property offers room to spread out, grow, and make your dreams a reality. The home is all electric and features over 1,600 square feet of main-floor living space, a welcoming front porch perfect for relaxing evenings, and four bedrooms with two-and-a-half bathrooms. The spacious, light-filled living room provides an inviting setting for everyday living and holiday gatherings alike. The open kitchen offers plenty of versatility, featuring a breakfast bar, generous dining area, and all kitchen appliances included. The primary bedroom suite is a wonderful retreat, complete with a private deck overlooking the backyard. The partially finished basement adds even more potential, with two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and unfinished space ready for your personal touch. Whether you need extra living space, a recreation area, or room to customize, there's opportunity here to build equity and make it your own. Outside, the possibilities continue! The property includes a large shop with attached former living quarters with half bath that could be restored, a storm shelter, two storage sheds, and a lean-to building. Whether you're looking for workspace, hobby space, storage, or simply room to enjoy the outdoors, this property delivers.
