Property Description

Fresh updates and room to grow make this 3-bedroom, 2-bath corner-lot home a standout! The heart of the home is the kitchen, finished with granite countertops, a striking matte black tile backsplash, and brand-new stainless steel appliances, ready for everything from weeknight dinners to weekend get-togethers. Both bathrooms have been redone with clean, modern finishes. A spacious bonus room gives you the flexibility to create a craft space, home gym, office, or game room, whatever your hobbies call for. Outside, the large yard offers plenty of space to play and garden, with a cozy front patio for morning coffee and a back patio for evening grilling. A two-car garage adds room for vehicles, tools, and storage. Come see this one in person and picture yourself at home!
