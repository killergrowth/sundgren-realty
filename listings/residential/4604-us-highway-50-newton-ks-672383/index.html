About This Property

Come fall in LOVE with Cattail Springs! A beautiful one-owner executive-style custom-build offering country living at its finest. This beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath ranch home sits on approximately 17.7 acres of secluded, tree-lined landscape just east of Newton, KS. The home features 1,649 square feet of thoughtfully designed main-level living space, plus a fully finished basement, totaling over 3,100 square feet. Inside, you’ll find an open-concept layout with warm oak flooring, a spacious living area ideal for entertaining, and a well-equipped kitchen with electric appliances. The main floor also includes a dedicated office, laundry room, and expansive gathering spaces filled with natural light. The large primary suite offers a private retreat with a generous bedroom, walk-in closet, en-suite bath, with access to the deck with an extraordinary waterfront view surrounded by creation's finest. Downstairs, the finished basement adds additional living flexibility with a full bath and multiple bonus rooms. Step outside to enjoy the peaceful surroundings from the walkout patio, or unwind on the impressive two-level deck overlooking one of the scenic Cattail Springs ponds. The property also includes access to a golf cart bridge connecting two ponds and acres of prairie views. Additional features include a 2-car attached garage with walk-through access, plus a 115’ x 40’ outbuilding complete with a fully finished approx 1,000 sq ft one-bedroom apartment, covered parking for three vehicles, and a 16’ x 24’ covered patio area, perfect for guests, hobbies, or income potential. Nature lovers will appreciate the private, golf cart-friendly trails winding through an “enchanted garden” of mature trees. Amenities include rural water, a private well, owned propane tank, a shooting range, and a 24-foot windmill. Located just minutes from Newton and within easy reach of the Wichita metro area, this unique property offers the tranquility of country living without sacrificing accessibility. Cattail Springs would make an amazing event venue, complete with fruit bearing arbor. The grounds are full of beautiful flowering plants, over 1500 trees with irrigation, and an abundance of wildlife. Come soak in all the serenity Cattail Springs has to offer and schedule your showing today.
