INVESTOR SPECIAL! HUGE PRICE DROP AFTER STORM DAMAGE! Welcome home to this beautiful 3 bedroom, 3 bathroom waterfront retreat in Wagon Wheel Ranch with direct access to Santa Fe Lake and located in the highly rated Andover school district. This home offers a year-round resort feel featuring vaulted wood-beamed ceilings, tongue-and-groove accents, an open living and dining area with a Hearthstone wood stove, and a spacious kitchen with breakfast nook, pantry, and pressed metal ceilings. This home has an abundance of windows filling the rooms with natural light. The spacious primary suite offers a pellet stove, skylights, and a full bath, includes two walk-in closets and en suite bath. Two additional bedrooms share a Jack & Jill bath with peaceful creek views. Recent updates include new carpet, LVP flooring, fresh paint, and a new HVAC system(2024). Outdoor amenities include a private dock, screened gazebo with electricity and fan, landscaped yard with sprinkler system, andan oversized garage. The community provides walking trails, sports courts, picnic areas, playground, and a boat ramp, with fishing, paddleboarding, and kayaking right outside your door. Some photos are virtually staged.
