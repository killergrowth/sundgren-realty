Property Description

Easy to live in, easy to get around from. This 2-bedroom, 1-bath home offers 1,088 square feet of practical living space with a new roof (April 2026), fresh paint inside, & LVP flooring in the living room and kitchen. A separate laundry room off the kitchen adds extra storage. The bonus room at the back has new carpet and plenty of flexibility: use it as an additional family room or possibly a third bedroom. Outside, the fenced backyard comes with a storage shed, and the attached one-car garage keeps daily life simple. Quick access to Kellogg puts the rest of Wichita within easy reach, whether you’re headed to work, shopping, or across town. It’s a smart fit for first-time buyers, anyone looking to downsize, or investors searching for a solid rental. Call to schedule your showing!
