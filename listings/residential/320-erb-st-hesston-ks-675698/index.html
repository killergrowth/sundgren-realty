About This Property

Located at 320 S. Erb Street in Hesston, Kansas, this well-maintained ranch-style home offers approximately 2,178 square feet of finished living space on a 0.3 acre corner lot in a quiet residential neighborhood. The main level features a functional floor plan with three bedrooms and a full bathroom, providing comfortable living space for a variety of household needs. An open living and dining area creates a welcoming setting for everyday living and entertaining. Large windows provide natural light throughout the home, while a sliding glass door opens directly onto the backyard patio, creating an easy transition between indoor and outdoor living. The kitchen offers generous cabinet and countertop space for meal preparation and storage. The stove, microwave, dishwasher, and refrigerator are included with the home, allowing the next owner to move in with many of the essential appliances already in place. The finished basement adds substantial flexibility to the home. It includes two non-conforming rooms along with an additional finished room with an egress that previously served as a small kitchen area. This room could continue to serve as a kitchenette, hobby room, storage area, or other flexible living space depending on a buyer's needs. A three-quarter bathroom with a shower is also located downstairs for added convenience. An additional highlight of the basement is a dedicated office area with sliding doors that provide privacy for working from home, studying, or managing household activities. Outside, the property continues to offer practical amenities. The fenced backyard provides space for outdoor activities, gardening, or pets, while the patio creates an inviting place for grilling, outdoor dining, or relaxing. The oversized one-car garage offers parking along with additional room for storage, lawn equipment, bicycles, or workshop space. The corner lot provides extra yard area while allowing convenient access from the quiet residential street. Several important mechanical and exterior improvements have already been completed. The furnace, central air conditioning system, and water heater were replaced in 2018, helping reduce the need for immediate major mechanical updates. More recently, the home received a new roof, flashing, gutters, and gutter covers in March 2026, providing additional value and peace of mind for future ownership. The property's location is another notable feature. Hesston offers the convenience of small-town living while remaining within commuting distance of larger communities throughout south central Kansas. The home sits just west of Hesston College and is only minutes from local schools, grocery stores, restaurants, and parks. Whether you are searching for a primary residence, a home with additional finished living space, or an investment property with flexible layout options, this property offers a combination of recent updates, functional living areas, outdoor amenities, and a convenient Hesston location.
