Property Description

This home provides affordability, storage, space and several updates. Located in the town of Caldwell KS, the town itself provides everything you need for small town living with a thriving community and the only hospital in Sumner County. The home offers 1204 sq ft with 3 bedrooms and 1 bath, a separate laundry room and one of the largest kitchens with an abundance of cabinets and workspace. Upon entry you will come into a foyer that could really be the welcoming entry you desire. The living room has built in shelves. The kitchen offers reverse osmosis, smooth top electric stove, microwave with vent, dishwasher, space for your fridge with water hookup and a huge pantry and 2 corner shelves. The kitchen provides plenty of space for your dining table. 3 bedrooms with good closet and storage space, hall storage and separate laundry room with more storage space. The hall has an attic fan and wainscotting. The plumbing and electrical has been updated, windows have been replaced throughout, and the exterior walls have been insulated, providing good improvements any buyer would appreciate. Did I mention there is also a pull down attic for more storage space? The home sits on a .29 ac lot with plenty of off-street parking and a detached 30' x 40’ garage with 2 roll up doors and rocked floor and alley access plus an additional building with partial cement flooring which can provide an incredible amount of storage with alley access. At an affordable $110,000, this property offers an opportunity to own a home with three bedrooms, a generous lot, extensive storage and an oversized detached garage without breaking the budget.
