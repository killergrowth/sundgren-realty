Property Description

Step into this inviting 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath ranch nestled in a quiet neighborhood. Inside, you’ll find two comfortable living spaces, giving you plenty of room to relax, entertain, or set up a home office or playroom. The easy, single-level layout makes everyday living convenient and functional. The kitchen and dining area flow nicely toward the covered patio, perfect for morning coffee, grilling, or enjoying the outdoors in any weather. The fenced backyard offers a safe space for kids, pets, or gardening. All three bedrooms are nicely sized, and the 1.5 baths provide extra convenience for busy mornings. An additional storage room in the garage means you’ll have a dedicated spot for tools, bikes, seasonal décor, or hobbies—keeping the main living areas clutter-free. As an additional benefit a new roof will be installed before closing as well as a privacy fence on the west side of the backyard. With its peaceful setting, practical floor plan, and great indoor-outdoor living, this home is a wonderful find for anyone looking for comfort and convenience in a quiet neighborhood.
