Property Description

Spectacular, fully updated ranch in the highly desirable Maize South School District with an incredible backyard built for entertaining! This move-in ready home offers an outstanding floor plan, beautifully updated kitchen and baths, luxury vinyl flooring throughout the house, a walkout basement at ground level, and an east-facing backyard designed for year-round enjoyment. A beautiful wood and glass front door with dual sidelights and an elliptical transom welcomes you inside. The spacious great room features a soaring 10-foot ceiling, gas fireplace with a floor-to-ceiling shiplap accent wall, natural wood mantel, large windows, a ceiling fan, plant shelves, and new LVT flooring. The stunning kitchen has been completely remodeled with stylish cabinetry, quartz countertops with an eating bar, stainless steel range hood, coffee bar, a large pantry, and new LVT flooring. The adjoining dining area features a unique boxed-out window seat with built-in storage, providing extra seating around the table, plus patio doors leading to the covered deck. Just off the kitchen is a true separate laundry room with a shiplap accent wall, cabinetry, and shelving. The spacious primary suite is a relaxing retreat with oversized triple windows, a coffered ceiling, and ceiling fan. The beautifully remodeled ensuite bath offers dual quartz vanities with a makeup station, an impressive tiled walk-in shower with built-in shelving, and a frameless glass barn-style door. Two additional main-floor bedrooms feature large windows, ceiling fans, and attractive wood-and-glass closet doors. The updated hall bath includes a new cabinet style vanity, decorative mirror, and tile flooring. The walkout basement is made for entertaining with a huge family room featuring a corner decorative fireplace with stone surround, plus a flue for a pellet stove, built-in glass shelving, surround sound, recessed lighting, ceiling fan, and direct access to the backyard patio. The exceptional wet bar includes a raised granite bar, stone accents, wine rack, stemware rack, microwave shelf, space for a full-size refrigerator, and window. Additionally, there are two spacious bedrooms, one with a viewout window and one with a daylight window, both offer large walk-in closets. The updated basement bath features dual quartz vanities, decorative mirrors, and a tub/shower combination. Step outside and enjoy your own private backyard oasis. The covered composite deck is sheltered on three sides by the home, creating a comfortable outdoor living space. Below, a stamped concrete patio spans the entire width of the house. One side features a spectacular stone-faced gas fire pit with matching stone walls around the seating area, while the other side includes a gas line already installed for a future outdoor kitchen. Mature trees provide excellent privacy, and the beautifully landscaped yard is kept lush with a sprinkler system supplied by an irrigation well. This exceptional home combines thoughtful updates, quality finishes, and an amazing outdoor living space that is perfect for relaxing or entertaining family and friends.
