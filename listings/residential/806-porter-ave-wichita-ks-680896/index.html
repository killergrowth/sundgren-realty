Property Description

806 N Porter Ave brings together the historic charm of Wichita's beloved Riverside neighborhood with the fresh updates that make a home easy to fall in love with. This beautifully renovated 3 bedroom, 2 full bathroom home offers original hardwood floors, a detached 2 car garage, and a location surrounded by some of Wichita's favorite parks and attractions. Step inside to a long, light-filled living room where the original wood floors, classic fireplace, and built-in cabinets give the space a character you simply can't recreate in a newer home. There's plenty of room to gather, with a dedicated dining area connecting the living room to the beautifully updated kitchen. Here, soft green cabinetry, quartz countertops, a tile backsplash, and durable luxury vinyl flooring bring a fresh look while keeping the home's original personality intact. A main-floor bedroom offers flexibility for guests or a home office, while a full bathroom and additional storage make everyday living convenient. Upstairs, a cozy second bedroom could easily become an art studio, reading room, or creative workspace. Across the hall, the primary bedroom features vaulted ceilings, natural light, and its own updated full bathroom, creating a comfortable place to unwind. Out back, you'll find a manageable yard with room for pets, gardening, or enjoying an evening outside, plus the convenience of a detached 2 car garage. And then there's Riverside itself. Enjoy being near Central Riverside Park, Botanica, the Wichita Art Museum, the Arkansas River, and the local coffee shops and restaurants that give this neighborhood so much personality. With historic character, modern updates, and a location that makes enjoying Wichita easy, 806 N Porter Ave offers a wonderful opportunity to make Riverside home.
