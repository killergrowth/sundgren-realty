Property Description

Renovation is finally complete on this beauty!! Welcome to 701 E 4th St, a charming Tudor-style home situated on a large corner lot and within walking distance of Chisholm Middle School, this impressive home offers 5 spacious bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and multiple levels of living space. Step inside to beautifully refinished wood floors that preserve the home's original charm while complementing its fresh updates. The generous-sized bedrooms provide plenty of space, including a spacious primary bedroom with its own walk-out deck the perfect spot for morning coffee or relaxing at the end of the day. With living areas throughout the main level, upstairs, and basement, there's room to spread out and make the space your own. Two fireplaces add even more warmth and character to this already inviting home. Major updates include a NEW ROOF and NEW HVAC, giving the next owner added peace of mind. From the refinished hardwood floors, quartz countertops and classic Tudor architecture to the oversized corner lot and flexible multi-level floor plan, this home blends timeless character with the updates today's buyers are looking for. Five bedrooms, 2.5 baths, two fireplaces, and plenty of room inside and out 701 E 4th St is ready to impress!
