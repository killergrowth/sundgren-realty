Property Description

This spacious home offers a unique blend of character, generous living spaces, and incredible indoor and outdoor entertaining opportunities. From the beautiful stone exterior and mature trees to the backyard swimming pool, this property has plenty to offer! Inside, you'll find multiple living and dining areas, beautiful wood flooring, and distinctive architectural details throughout. A stunning floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace anchors the kitchen/hearth living area, creating a warm and inviting gathering space. The kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, generous counter space, and plenty of storage, with a nearby formal dining room perfect for hosting. The expansive family room is truly a standout, featuring soaring vaulted ceilings, exposed wood beams, a wet bar, and large windows overlooking the backyard. And don't miss the unique loft. With multiple gathering spaces, there's room for everyone to spread out, relax, or entertain. The spacious primary bedroom features beautiful wood flooring and its own impressive stone fireplace, adding warmth and character to the space. Along with a patio door straight the back yard deck and pool. The finished lower level provides even more room to enjoy, complete with a large recreation area and another stone fireplace and even more space to finish or fill up a large storage room with all your treasures. Step outside to your own backyard retreat! The in-ground saltwater swimming pool is surrounded by an expansive deck and patio area, offering plenty of space for summer gatherings, outdoor dining, and relaxing poolside. A fenced backyard and mature landscaping add to the appeal and privacy. Don't miss the 500 sq feet finished room on the back side of the detached garage complete with a mini split unit. Use for a pool house or mother-in-law suite! So many options. With its spacious layout, multiple fireplaces, distinctive stonework, and incredible entertaining spaces, this home is full of character and potential. Don't miss your opportunity to make it your own!
