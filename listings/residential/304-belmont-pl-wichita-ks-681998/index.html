Property Description

Come live beneath the arches on one of the most sought-after streets in College Hill! Belmont is sure to impress with its incredible size, thoughtful layout, four levels of living space, corner lot, and rich Wichita history. From the moment you step inside, you'll be captivated by the home's character and charm. A stunning, wide staircase featuring custom stair carpet runner welcomes you in. To your right, you'll find an expansive living room complete with a beautiful fireplace and windows lining every wall, filling the space with natural light. To your left, a stately dining room offers a built-in bar and ample storage, making it the perfect setting for hosting unforgettable dinner parties. The dining room flows seamlessly into the kitchen, creating a wonderful space for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, generous countertop space, a pantry, dedicated space for an ice machine, and high-end appliances. The main floor and second floor were fully repainted and skim coated within the last two years. Every window in this house had custom blinds installed as well. Just off the kitchen, a door leads to the backyard, where the fun continues! Enjoy plenty of patio space, an in-ground pool, basketball court, and even a treehouse. This backyard truly has something for everyone! Head upstairs to the second floor, where you'll find three spacious bedrooms, each featuring newly refinished hardwood floors. Both bathrooms on this level have been beautifully renovated with hand-selected tile and custom vanities, adding thoughtful style and detail throughout. The laundry room is just off of the master closet which makes it convenient when all of the main bedrooms are on this floor. And don't forget about the third floor! This special space features a built-in bed with plenty of additional room to lounge, play, or create the perfect hangout. After a long day, retreat to the basement, where you'll discover two fully finished living areas with impressive 9-foot ceilings, an additional bedroom, a full bathroom, a second laundry area, and abundant storage. Finding this kind of finished basement space in a historic home of this age is truly rare and sets Belmont apart from the rest! Belmont is more than a home; it's a College Hill experience filled with character, history, space, and endless opportunities to make memories. You truly have to see this one to appreciate all it has to offer!
