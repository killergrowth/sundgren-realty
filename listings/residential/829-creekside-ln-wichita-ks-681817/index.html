Property Description

Welcome home to this spacious and beautifully updated 3 bed/4 bath/2 car two-story home in East Wichita's Springdale Lakes neighborhood! Offering over 2,500 square feet of finished living space, numerous recent improvements, NO SPECIAL TAXES, and a convenient location just minutes from shopping, dining, and major highways, this home has so much to offer! As you arrive, you'll appreciate the mature trees and welcoming front entry, along with the peace of mind that comes with updated siding (2026), and recently replaced concrete on the driveway, front walkway, side stairs, and patio. Step inside to discover a spacious living room with vaulted ceilings and an adjoining formal dining area, perfect for entertaining. The comfortable main-floor family room features a wood burning fireplace and flows nicely into the updated kitchen, complete with an eating bar, newer appliances, reach-in pantry, and attractive new LVP flooring. Just off the kitchen, the sunny breakfast nook features large windows overlooking the backyard, bringing in an abundance of natural light. A convenient half bath and main-floor laundry complete this level. Upstairs, you'll find the spacious primary suite with its own private bath and new shower, along with two additional bedrooms and a full hall bathroom. Downstairs, the finished basement offers even more room to spread out, with a large family/rec room, separate office or bonus room, half bath, and additional storage space. Newer LVP flooring and epoxy-coated floors in the workshop area add to the functionality of this level. Outside, enjoy the beautiful, tree-shaded backyard featuring a spacious deck, patio, and wood privacy fencing, with two sides replaced in 2026. Additional improvements include new dining room windows (2025), a new water heater (2026), newer electrical panel, updated HVAC components, and leaf guards on the gutters. Conveniently located just outside the hustle and bustle of Wichita, yet only minutes from everyday amenities, this wonderful home offers a great combination of space, updates, and location.
