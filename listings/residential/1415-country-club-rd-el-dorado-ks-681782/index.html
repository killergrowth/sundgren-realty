Property Description

Home Sweet Home! Welcome to 1415 Country Club Rd. in El Dorado, located in the desirable El Dorado Country Club neighborhood! With the golf course right across the street, nearby schools, and convenient access to walking and jogging paths, this home offers a great combination of location, comfort, and convenience. Featuring 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, all conveniently located on the main floor, this home offers a functional layout with plenty of room to spread out. Enjoy a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, main-floor laundry, and an updated kitchen and bathrooms. You'll love the fresh updates throughout, including all-new flooring, fresh interior and exterior paint, and new siding! The partially finished basement provides additional flexibility, along with plenty of storage space and a concrete safe room. Outside, the home sits on a large corner lot with great curb appeal and a back deck that's perfect for enjoying the outdoors. The oversized attached two-car garage offers plenty of room for vehicles, storage, and even your golf cart! With its desirable location, spacious layout, and numerous updates, this home is ready for its next owner. Schedule your showing today!
