Property Description

From the moment you pull up, you'll appreciate the inviting curb appeal and 2-car garage of this West Wichita home. Step inside to an open-concept living area featuring a fireplace, dining area and kitchen with an eating bar, creating a comfortable space for everyday living and entertaining. Sliding glass doors lead to the back deck, replaced approximately one year ago, overlooking the fully privacy-fenced backyard with a large storage shed. The main floor offers two bedrooms, including the primary suite, a second bathroom and the convenience of main-floor laundry. Head downstairs to discover an additional living area, third bedroom and bathroom, providing plenty of extra space to spread out. Several major improvements have already been made, including a new roof in 2019 and the replacement of both the HVAC system and water heater during the seller’s ownership. Bring your favorite colors, decorating ideas and personal style to make this home your own while building equity along the way. Property is being sold as-is, where-is.
