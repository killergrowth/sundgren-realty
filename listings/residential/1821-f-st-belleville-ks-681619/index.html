Property Description

Beautifully remodeled modern 3-bedroom, 1-bath home on three spacious city lots. The huge backyard and side lot would be a great space to add your desired outbuilding or detached garage! With tons of space to enjoy the new back deck off of the back of the home. Built in 1930 and upgraded throughout with high-end custom finishes, this home has been redone from the bottom to the top! Nice curb appeal with the wrap-around covered porch, lighting all the way around the outside great for late night chats outside! With a nice open floor plan, the spacious family room features tech-enabled led lighting in the shelving and nice space to decorate family pictures or decor. Custom high-end kitchen with soft close cabinets, large eating island with granite countertops throughout. The property includes high end new kitchen appliances, master bedroom, the gorgeous spacious bathroom with walk in customized shower, features lighting and audio already installed just have to download app, a main floor laundry area and extra closet space. With 2 additional bedrooms located upstairs, the modern staircase in the formal open dining room offers easy access for kids to get to their bedrooms or for guests to enjoy the privacy of the upstairs space. With major updates throughout and the abundant outdoor space, this home offers modern comfort in a peaceful small-town setting. This modern move in ready home is great for entertaining guests or hosting your family for the holidays!! Must see to appreciate all the high end finishes this home has to offer!!
