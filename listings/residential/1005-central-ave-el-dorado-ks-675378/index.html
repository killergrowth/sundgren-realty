About This Property

Step into timeless elegance with this enchanting 1921 carriage house in the heart of Eldorado, rich in history, romance and character. This stunning 5-bedroom, 3.5 bath home showcases beautiful hardwood floors throughout, exquisite walnut finishes, leaded glass details, and thoughtfully preserved built-ins that highlight it's original charm. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home features a picturesque courtyard framed by the dining and living rooms, creating a serene and inviting atmosphere. Outdoors, enjoy a private patio complete with a grill, fire pit, and built-in fireplace, all overlooking a breathtaking mature magnolia tree. The property also includes a charming fully furnished 2 bedroom- 1bath apartment above the garage, equipped with three mini-split systems and ideal for guests, rental income, or extended living. Modern updates blend seamlessly with the home's historic appeal, including a 50-year metal roof on the main house, updated windows on the main floor, a new HVAC system with two condensers, updated water heater, and beautifully refreshed landscaping, all completed within the last seven years. Owned solar panels above the apartment add efficiency and long term value. Unique features such as an original ice block refrigerator, boiler and a hidden safe room make this truly one of a kind. With so many distinctive details, this is a property that must be experienced in person to fully appreciate it's charm and story.
