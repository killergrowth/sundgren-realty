Property Description

A little New England charm right in the heart of College Hill! From the inviting front porch and arched doorways to the beautiful hardwood floors and detailed ceilings, this home is full of the character that makes College Hill so special. But there's more here than just charm. With 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, a primary suite with its own private balcony, and a finished basement rec room with an additional bathroom, this home offers the space and functionality that can be hard to find in the neighborhood. And the location? That's just as good. Spend your mornings walking to College Hill Park, grab dinner at Belmont or Larcher's, or explore the shops and restaurants at Clifton Square. This is the kind of location that makes you fall in love with College Hill living. Character, space, and one of Wichita's most loved neighborhoods. This one checks a lot of boxes.
