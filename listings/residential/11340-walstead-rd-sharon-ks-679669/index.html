Property Description

Country Living at Its Best — 2.8 Acres, Updated Home & Brand-New 40x60 Shop If you’ve been looking for a country property that offers space, quality improvements, and room to make it your own, this 2.8-acre property deserves a close look. This spacious 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath home combines a flexible floor plan with extensive recent updates and an impressive new shop that adds tremendous versatility. The main level features a comfortable living and dining area, two bedrooms, one full bathroom, abundant storage, and a large laundry room complete with a sink and built-in cabinet storage. The lower level adds even more living space with a spacious family room, office area, third bedroom, full bathroom, and mechanical room. The kitchen was completely refreshed in September 2026 with additional cabinetry, a large center island, granite countertops, tile backsplash, and a new sink. Both bathrooms were remodeled in 2021, and new flooring was installed in the upstairs bedrooms. Additional improvements include exterior paint in 2022, a new concrete patio in 2024, and a new roof installed in August 2026 featuring Tamko Titan XT Class 3 impact-resistant shingles in Weathered Wood. Outside, the home offers both a covered front porch and an uncovered rear deck, providing comfortable spaces to enjoy the surrounding acreage. A convenient two-car garage is connected to the house by a breezeway, with new garage doors and openers installed in July 2026. The real showstopper is the new 40’ x 60’ shop, constructed in June 2026. With 14’ sidewalls and two electric overhead doors, the shop provides a substantial 40’ x 40’ working area with plenty of room for equipment, vehicles, hobbies, or projects. The north 20’ x 40’ portion is walled off and ready to become an impressive game room, man cave, entertainment area, or additional living/recreation space. A bathroom is already framed and rough-plumbed, making the finishing process that much easier. A covered porch wraps around the northeast corner of the shop, creating an ideal gathering space and adding to the building’s character and functionality. With 2.8 acres to spread out, a spacious home, extensive recent improvements, and a brand-new shop with tremendous potential, this property offers the kind of flexibility that is hard to find. Whether you’re looking for a place to raise a family, need room for your business or hobbies, want a serious workspace, or simply want the freedom that comes with acreage, this property is ready to deliver.
