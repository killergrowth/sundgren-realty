Property Description

Not your average cookie-cutter home! Welcome to 338 N Joann in Wichita, where character, space, and possibilities come together! This home offers approximately 1,088 sq. ft., 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, and TWO separate living spaces. The standout feature? A bonus step-down family room, with vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, and a cozy elevated loft nook that gives this home a little extra personality! You’ll also love the outdoor space with a generous half-acre lot offering lost of privacy, a large oversized 2 car garage, an additional outbuilding that can be turned into anything you want! Anyone say "she shed?!" Whether you love unique architecture, need extra room for hobbies, or simply want a home with character, this one deserves a look.
