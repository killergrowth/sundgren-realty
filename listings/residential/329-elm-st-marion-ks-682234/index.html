Property Description

Room for everyone on a wooded half-acre lot! This home has 5 bedrooms, 4 full baths, 2 half baths and 3,751 square feet, with hardwood floors, lots of built-ins, and a metal roof. On the main floor, the updated kitchen has an eating bar and flows to a formal dining area and a huge living room with a fireplace. A separate tiled area off the living room can be an office, play area, or workout space. Another family room is finished in knotty pine and has its own fireplace, and a screened-in patio/sunroom adds another place to relax. The main-floor primary bedroom has its own bath, and another bedroom on that level has the potential for its own bathroom. Upstairs are three more bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. Walk-in closets, a cedar closet, a dedicated laundry room, and a storage room add function throughout. Outside are mature trees, a fenced yard, and an attached 2-car garage.
