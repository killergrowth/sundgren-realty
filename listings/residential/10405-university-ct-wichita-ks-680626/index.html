Property Description

Tucked away on a cul-de-sac in the Goddard School District, this beautifully updated 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers a spacious, open floor plan and plenty of room to spread out! The main living area features updated flooring, a beautifully refreshed fireplace, and an open layout that flows into the dining area and kitchen. The kitchen offers updated countertops, white cabinetry, a tile backsplash, and a large eating bar that’s perfect for everyday living or entertaining. The main floor features three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings and a private bathroom with double sinks and a relaxing soaking tub. Downstairs, the large finished basement provides a second living area, two additional bedrooms, and a third bathroom. Additional improvements include renovations to both main floor bathrooms and updated flooring and carpet. Complete with a 2-car garage, a desirable cul-de-sac location, and NO SPECIAL ASSESSMENTS, this home has so much to offer!
