Property Description

MID-CENTURY MODERN SHOWSTOPPER ON .75 ACRES WITH POOL, SOLAR & WALKOUT LOWER LEVEL! Welcome to this beautifully updated and lovingly restored mid-century modern gem in the sought-after Benjamin Hills Estates! Set on an expansive, tree-filled .75-acre lot, this exceptional home combines timeless architectural character with modern updates, abundant space, and an incredible indoor-outdoor lifestyle. With over 4,000 square feet, this impressive home offers generous bedrooms, oversized closets, a thoughtful split-bedroom layout, and multiple living and entertaining spaces across two levels. Nearly every detail has been refreshed, including new flooring throughout, while the original entry slate has been preserved to maintain its mid-century charm. The main level is designed for comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. The spacious living room features a new electric fireplace, while the beautifully appointed main kitchen provides plenty of room to gather. Multiple dining areas offer flexibility for everything from casual meals to larger gatherings. The primary suite is a private retreat with a luxurious separate tub and shower, while two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and guest bath complete the main level. A dramatic hallway overlook provides a wonderful connection to the enormous family room below. The walkout lower level expands the living and entertaining possibilities with a completely remodeled entertaining kitchen/bar, additional dining space, a huge family room, two more bedrooms, a bathroom, workout/bonus room, and laundry room. Whether hosting game nights, watching movies, entertaining a crowd, or accommodating guests, this level offers incredible versatility. Step outside and discover your own private backyard oasis. The in-ground swimming pool, expansive patio, and large wraparound deck create multiple spaces for summer gatherings, BBQs, relaxing, and enjoying the peaceful, maturely landscaped setting. With so much room to spread out, this property truly delivers the kind of outdoor lifestyle that is hard to find. Modern updates and energy-conscious features add even more appeal. The home features 90% new windows and sliding glass doors, zoned HVAC, plus an additional mini-split serving the primary bedroom for personalized comfort. Owned solar panels and a Tesla Powerwall battery help reduce energy costs while providing valuable backup power during outages. From its iconic mid-century design and beautifully preserved character to its expansive lot, pool, remodeled lower level, solar power, and abundance of living space, this is a truly special property. Come experience the space, style, and flexibility of this one-of-a-kind Benjamin Hills Estates home!
