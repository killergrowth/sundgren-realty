Property Description

Enjoy country living in the city with room to spread out on nearly an acre! This 2 bed, 2 full bath manufactured home sits on a 0.91-acre lot in Applewood Farm and offers 1,056 sq ft of living space with luxury vinyl flooring throughout. The open kitchen features a large island with eating bar, dark cabinetry, and included dishwasher, refrigerator, and range/oven, flowing into the dining and living areas. The primary suite includes a private bath with double vanity and shower, and the second bedroom is served by the second full bath. A sunroom adds bonus space, and a dedicated laundry area with built-in shelving keeps everyday chores organized. Outside, enjoy the covered deck and patio, a carport plus detached garage/shop, additional storage buildings, chain-link and wood fencing, and RV parking. Located in Haysville schools (USD 261).
