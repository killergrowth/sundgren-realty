Property Description

Character, charm, and thoughtful updates come together beautifully in this Marion home located in the highly sought after Tanglewood Addition! Offering 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and 2,132 square feet of living space, this beautifully remodeled property blends timeless appeal with modern comforts. At the heart of the home is a stunning gourmet kitchen, perfect for everyday living, entertaining, and gathering with family and friends. Thoughtful updates throughout create inviting spaces while preserving the character that makes this home so special. Beyond the beautiful interior, buyers will appreciate major improvements, including a new roof in July 2026, and a Carrier HVAC system installed in 2020. Located in the welcoming community of Marion, Kansas, just minutes from Marion County Lake, this property offers the charm of small-town living with outdoor recreation close by.
