Property Description

Beautifully remodeled and thoughtfully updated, this charming 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch in West Wichita's established Westlink neighborhood offers nearly 1,800 square feet of finished living space, a newer roof and an exceptional backyard oasis that makes this home truly stand out. From the moment you step inside, you'll appreciate the inviting atmosphere, tasteful finishes and open-concept floor plan that seamlessly connects the living, dining and kitchen areas. The remodeled kitchen features white shaker cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and a peninsula with an eating bar, creating a functional and stylish space for everyday living and entertaining. Luxury vinyl plank flooring, updated bathrooms and abundant natural light complement the home's original character while providing a fresh, contemporary feel. The finished daylight basement offers an expansive family and recreation room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, along with a third bedroom and full bathroom, providing additional space to relax, entertain or accommodate guests. One of the most impressive features of this property, however, is the beautifully enhanced outdoor living space. The expansive covered patio and fully fenced, landscaped backyard create a private retreat perfect for morning coffee, weekend cookouts, evenings with friends or simply unwinding at the end of the day. Whether you love entertaining outdoors or prefer a quiet place to relax, this backyard offers an inviting extension of the home's living space. Additional highlights include a newer roof, an attached two-car garage and a generous .23-acre lot with no HOA. Conveniently located near Central and Maize, this home offers easy access to West Wichita's shopping, restaurants, parks, schools and major thoroughfares while maintaining the charm of an established residential neighborhood. With its extensive updates, distinctive character, expanded outdoor living space and desirable Westlink location, 628 N Shefford St offers the perfect combination of comfort, convenience and move-in-ready appeal.
