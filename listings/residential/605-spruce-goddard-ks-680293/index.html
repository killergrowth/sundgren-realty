Property Description

Welcome to 605 S Spruce in the heart of Goddard. This 4 bedroom, 3 full bath ranch offers an easy, functional layout with three bedrooms on the main level, a 2 car attached garage and a finished basement that adds plenty of flexibility. The main floor features a spacious living room with vaulted ceilings and a fireplace, an eat-in kitchen and three conveniently located bedrooms. Several rooms also feature unique hand-painted murals that add a little personality and charm. The finished basement expands the living space with a large rec room, additional bonus space, a fourth bedroom and a third full bathroom. Outside, the fully fenced backyard is shaded by mature trees and offers a covered deck with electrical outlets already in place, plus a storage shed for all of the extras. The sellers have also kept up with regular maintenance, including twice-yearly HVAC service, annual servicing of the water softener and reverse-osmosis system, quarterly pest control and lawn care, making this home truly move-in ready. If you love the feel of a smaller community but still want everyday conveniences close by, Goddard offers the best of both worlds with groceries, restaurants, a library, gyms, gas and other essentials nearby, plus easy access to Wichita just minutes away. With Goddard schools, parks, the community pool and walking trails all close to home, 605 S Spruce puts you right in the middle of it all. Come see what makes this one feel like home!
