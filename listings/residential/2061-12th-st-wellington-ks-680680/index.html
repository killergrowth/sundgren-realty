Property Description

A smart investment with options! Built in 2016 with the efficiency and practicality of days gone by, this well-maintained duplex offers an excellent opportunity for both investors and owner-occupants. Conveniently located near the Kansas Turnpike/I-35 and Wellington High School, this property combines classic curb appeal with comfortable, functional living. Each side offers 1,161 square feet of living area, featuring 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, an open-concept floor plan, an electric fireplace, included appliances, and a 1-car attached garage. The attractive exterior showcases a stone facade, cedar shutters, and sliding glass doors leading to private patios, with privacy between the two units. Single-level living, all electric, with no stairs adds everyday convenience, while a storm shelter provides additional peace of mind. Whether you want to offset your mortgage by living in one unit and renting the other or add a full duplex to your investment portfolio, the possibilities are appealing. The currently rented unit generates $840 per month in rental income. With no special assessments and a price well below courthouse appraised value, this property presents an affordable opportunity with plenty of potential.
