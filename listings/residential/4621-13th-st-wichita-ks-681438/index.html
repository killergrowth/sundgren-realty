Property Description

Back on the market—your second chance is here! Discover unmatched value at $299,000 with this spacious, custom-built all-brick ranch situated on an expansive 0.42-acre double lot. Designed with efficiency and durability in mind, the main level features rich custom hardwood floors across the living spaces and hallway, paired with easy-care tile through the kitchen and dining area. Practical upgrades abound, including fresh carpet, a newer water heater, an oversized laundry room packed with storage, and a newly installed insulated garage door. The fully finished lower level adds serious square footage, boasting an expansive 35' x 13' family room, a flexible bonus room (currently staged as a bedroom), and a dedicated 32' x 11' indoor workshop. Outside, the property truly shines. Crafters, mechanics, and hobbyists will love the detached 23' x 21' workshop—fully climate-controlled (currently a quilting studio) with a built-in cellar perfect for canning, cold storage, or storm shelter use. Two additional outbuildings keep lawn equipment neatly tucked away. Best of all: the home, detached workshop, and both sheds all feature brand-new roofs installed this year. From versatile living quarters to exceptional hobby spaces on nearly half an acre, this property offers rare utility at an unbeatable price point.
