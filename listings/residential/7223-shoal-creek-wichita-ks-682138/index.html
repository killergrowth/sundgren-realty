Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully appointed Patio home, offering warmth, elegance, and exceptional living spaces throughout. Enjoy the Patio Home lifestyle with lawn care service, snow removal and security of living in a safe maintained community. Rich, inviting tones. gorgeous hickory wood flooring and the cozy statement stone fireplace create an inviting atmosphere across the main level. The "Heart of the Home"....The kitchen, showcases custom knotty alder cabinetry with a timeless style, making it the perfect gathering space for family and friends. The thoughtfully designed main floor features two bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite, complete with a spa-inspired master bath and an impressive walk-in closet. Located in a highly sought-after area, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and lifestyle. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy low-maintenance patio home living with beautiful surroundings and room for everyone! Make this house your HOME Today!!!
