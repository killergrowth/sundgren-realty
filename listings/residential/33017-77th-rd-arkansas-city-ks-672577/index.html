About This Property

Tucked away in the country on 4.80 beautiful acres, this inviting 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home offers the kind of space and freedom so many dream about. Whether you’re looking for room for animals, a big garden, space for hobbies, or simply a place where kids can run and play under wide open skies, this property gives you the opportunity to truly enjoy country living. And with all paved roads leading home, convenience comes right along with the peaceful setting. Step inside and you’ll immediately appreciate the warm, open feel of the home. The spacious living room creates the perfect place to gather with family and friends, while the large kitchen offers an abundance of space for cooking, entertaining, and making everyday memories. The primary suite is a relaxing retreat of its own, complete with a private bathroom featuring both a walk-in shower and a soaking tub perfect for unwinding at the end of the day. Outside, the property continues to impress with multiple outbuildings ready to fit whatever your lifestyle may need — from workshops and storage to livestock spaces, hobby buildings, or creative projects. Separate fencing is already in place for larger animals, and the setup allows plenty of flexibility to truly make the property your own. A circle drive offers an easy welcome for guests, while the garage just off the home provides additional storage and workspace alongside covered parking with the attached carport. And Apple, Pear & Cherry Trees Line the Landscape. If you’ve been searching for a place with room to breathe, space to grow, and the charm of country living, this property is ready to welcome you home.
