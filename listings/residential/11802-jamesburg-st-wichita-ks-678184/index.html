Property Description

NEW PRICE! The sellers have moved out, and this home is ready for its next chapter. This 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers more than 2,000 square feet in an established neighborhood with mature trees, a sought-after school district, and a convenient central location. With the rooms now empty, it’s easy to see the space you’re working with and picture how you’d make it your own. The living room, kitchen, and dining area flow together openly while still offering enough separation to give each space its own purpose. The spacious primary suite features an en suite bath with a tub and shower, plus a walk-in closet. Two more bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level, along with hookups for main-floor laundry tucked inside a bedroom closet. Downstairs, the huge basement is a favorite, with a neutral color scheme, a cozy wood-burning fireplace, a large fourth bedroom, and a full bath with shower. There’s also a ton of storage in addition to the utility room. Outside, the freshly painted exterior gives the home a clean, updated look, while the fenced backyard and pergola create a private setting with plenty of room to relax. Vacant and easy to show, it’s priced to move. Come see the potential at 11802 Jamesburg for yourself!
