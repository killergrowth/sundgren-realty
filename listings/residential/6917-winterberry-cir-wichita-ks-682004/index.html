Property Description

East Wichita 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offering 2,642 square feet, a 2-car garage, cul-de-sac location, and walking distance to Gammon Elementary. Brand-new LVP flooring, timeless granite countertops, and an inviting eating bar bring fresh appeal to the kitchen, while multiple living areas give you room to spread out. A versatile front flex space offers the perfect spot for a sitting room, home office, or playroom, while the formal dining room and separate living room with a real wood-burning fireplace add character you won't find in every floor plan. The main-floor primary suite features a double-sink granite vanity, walk-in tiled shower, and two closets. Downstairs, the finished basement is ready for game days and gatherings with a built-in entertainment center, ceiling speakers, and a dry bar featuring lower cabinetry, open shelving, and a mirrored backdrop perfect for displaying your favorite sports memorabilia. Two additional bedrooms with egress windows, a full bathroom, and a separate laundry room complete the basement. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced backyard with a pergola-covered wood deck and additional pergola coverage extending over the lawn, creating shaded spaces for outdoor entertaining. With extensive updates, versatile living spaces, and 2,642 square feet of move-in-ready appeal, this East Wichita home is one worth seeing!
