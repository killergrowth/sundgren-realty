Property Description

Welcome to a rare find on one of Newton’s most desirable cul-de-sacs. Built in 2006 and thoughtfully designed for both everyday comfort and effortless entertaining, this 3,031-square-foot ranch offers an expansive open floor plan, a finished basement, and a private, tree-lined backyard—all just minutes from local schools and conveniences. Step inside to a generously proportioned living room that flows naturally into the heart of the home. The kitchen is built for real life, featuring ample cabinetry, an eating bar for casual mornings, and a large walk-in pantry. Whether you are hosting holidays in the formal dining room or enjoying a quiet dinner at home, you are just steps from the back deck—an ideal spot for morning coffee or unwinding at Sunset. A smart split-bedroom layout gives everyone their own space. The master is a peaceful retreat complete with a walk-in closet and an en-suite bathroom featuring double sinks., tub and shower combo. Two well-sized bedrooms and a full bathroom tucked on the opposite wing of the main floor. Main-Floor Laundry is conveniently located right where you need it, keeping daily routines simple. The finished basement adds 1,350 square feet of versatile living space. Anchored by an oversized family room and a dedicated recreation area has egress windows and there is plenty of room for movie nights, a game table, or a home gym. A fourth bedroom and third full bathroom make it an ideal setup for guests, while a dedicated storage room keeps seasonal gear neatly out of sight. Set on a 7,405-square-foot cul-de-sac lot, the exterior is framed by mature trees and a fenced backyard with wood and wrought-iron detailing. A built-in sprinkler system keeps the lawn lush with minimal effort, and the attached two-car garage provides everyday convenience.
