Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully maintained former model home in Andover’s desirable Cornerstone neighborhood! From the moment you step inside, you’ll appreciate the care and attention that has gone into keeping this home updated and move-in ready. The main level features newer carpet, while the fully finished walk-out basement offers fresh carpet installed in 2026 and provides excellent additional living space. The kitchen includes several thoughtful updates, including a new dual-oven and stove, a newer dishwasher, and a garbage disposal. With numerous updates completed over the past several years, this former model home offers the comfort of a well-maintained property without the long list of projects waiting for its new owner.
