Property Description

Escape to the country with this one-of-a-kind rural property offering endless possibilities for outdoor enthusiasts, families, or anyone looking for space and privacy. Nestled on more than 11 acres overlooking the lake, this property combines the feel of a secluded hunting camp with the comfort and charm of a country home. Known for exceptional upland bird hunting, waterfowl opportunities, and trophy whitetail deer, the surrounding area is a sportsman’s paradise. After a day outdoors, return to a classic farmhouse filled with warmth and character, featuring spacious living areas, detailed wood craftsmanship, soaring ceilings, and vintage architectural touches that are difficult to replicate today. The acreage is set up for both recreation and relaxation with numerous outbuildings and amenities including a large barn, dog kennels, armory, tree house, fire pit gathering area, and zip lines that make the property enjoyable for all ages. Wildlife activity and peaceful evenings create a setting that truly captures the best of country living. Whether you're looking for a hunting headquarters, weekend retreat, or permanent residence with unmatched recreational appeal, this property is a rare opportunity to own a place where memories are waiting to be made.
