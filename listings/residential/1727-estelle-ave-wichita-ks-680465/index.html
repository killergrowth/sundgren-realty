Property Description

Welcome to your dream starter home! This light, bright, and cheerful 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom residence has been updated throughout and is ready for its new owners. The kitchen boasts sleek granite countertops, a pantry, and plenty of workspace, perfect for meal prep and entertaining overlooking the living room. The spacious master bedroom provides a comfortable retreat, and the larger-sized bathroom includes convenient dual sinks for easy morning routines. Plus, enjoy the added easy access of laundry located just off the hallway near all the bedrooms! Outside a solid concrete slab in the backyard provides an ideal patio setup or a ready foundation for a future shed or shop. Don't forget the converted garage, a versatile bonus space ready for your final custom touches as a home office, den, or playroom. Move-in ready and full of potential!
