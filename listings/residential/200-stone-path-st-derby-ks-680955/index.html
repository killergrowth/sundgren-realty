Property Description

There's something about a home with character that you just can't recreate. Located in Derby's Stone Creek neighborhood, 200 Stone Path offers 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and 2,450 square feet of living space with beautiful woodwork, thoughtful architectural details, and a floor plan that makes great use of the space. The main living room features vaulted ceilings, a beautiful fireplace with a wood mantel, and plenty of natural light. You'll notice the attention to detail throughout, from the rich wood trim and cabinetry to the softer architectural lines that give this home its own personality. The kitchen is tucked just off the dining area, offering a little separation from the main living space while still keeping everything connected. All kitchen appliances stay! The main floor also features a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet, double sinks, a separate shower, and a bathtub. And one of those everyday conveniences buyers love? Main-floor laundry! No carrying baskets up and down the stairs. Downstairs, the walkout basement adds even more living space and flexibility. With five bedrooms throughout the home, there's room for guests, a home office, hobbies, or whatever fits your needs. The outdoor space is another highlight. Step directly from the dining area onto the elevated deck, or enjoy the spacious covered patio below with access from the walkout basement. Two separate outdoor spaces make it easy to enjoy the backyard in just about any weather. If you've been searching for a home in Derby with room to spread out, a functional layout, and details that set it apart, 200 Stone Path is one you'll want to see in person.
