Property Description

Beautifully maintained home in the desirable Central Park Addition of Bel Aire, offering a fantastic NE Wichita location with neighborhood ponds, scenic walking paths, and convenient access to Bel Aire's city park and playground. Pride of ownership is evident throughout this clean, neutral, and move-in-ready home. The split-bedroom, open-concept floor plan features 10-foot ceilings throughout the main living area, beautiful oak woodwork, all-new lighting, and new luxury vinyl plank flooring. The spacious living room is highlighted by a gas fireplace and large picture windows overlooking the private, wooded backyard, creating a peaceful setting with wonderful natural light. The open kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring granite countertops, an oversized eating bar, island, walk-in pantry, and newer black stainless steel appliances. Just off the kitchen is an oversized main-floor laundry room with excellent storage and a built-in ironing board. The private main-floor primary suite offers his-and-hers closets, a newly updated tile shower, and separate water closet. Generously sized closets and abundant storage can be found throughout the home. The true walk-out basement expands the living space with a large family room, two additional bedrooms, a third full bathroom, utility room, and separate storage area. Outside, enjoy the private wooded setting from the new maintenance-free covered deck measuring more than 300 square feet and featuring a new ceiling fan. An inviting backyard pergola creates the perfect space for outdoor dining and entertaining. The yard also features wrought-iron fencing along with an invisible pet fence in both the front and backyard. Car enthusiasts and anyone needing extra storage will appreciate the impressive 35' x 22' tandem three-car garage with extensive built-in storage. Additional updates include a new HVAC system in 2023 and north- and south-facing windows that are approximately three years old. Specials are scheduled to pay out in 2026. With its spacious open floor plan, exceptional storage, wooded backyard, extensive updates, and desirable Central Park location, this home offers an outstanding combination of comfort, quality, and convenience.
