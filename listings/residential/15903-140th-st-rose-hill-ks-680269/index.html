Property Description

Welcome to 15903 SW 140th St, located just outside of Rose Hill! Situated on 9.10 acres, this 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers 2,042 sq. ft. of living space and underwent a major remodel in 1985. Inside, you’ll find spacious bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with a full bathroom. The large kitchen offers ample cabinet and counter space, with a formal dining area nearby for meals and gatherings. The living room features vaulted ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace, creating a comfortable space to relax. The unfinished basement provides additional flexibility, whether you choose to use it for storage or finish the space to add even more usable living area. Outside, enjoy the beautiful mature trees, expansive acreage, and an outbuilding that offers additional space for storage or other needs. With its generous lot, spacious interior, and peaceful country setting just outside of town, this property offers plenty of possibilities.
