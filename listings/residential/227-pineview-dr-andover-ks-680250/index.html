Property Description

Spacious 4-Bedroom Home in the Highly Desired Andover School District! Welcome to this one-of-a-kind 4-bedroom, 3-full-bathroom home, perfectly situated on a quiet cul-de-sac and within walking distance of Andover Middle and Andover High School. Offering generous living spaces, custom woodwork, and a variety of special features, this home is designed for comfortable living and entertaining. The main level showcases beautiful bamboo flooring in the entry, living room and dining room/office, complemented by custom woodwork throughout. New carpet has been installed in all of the main floor bedrooms, adding a fresh and comfortable touch. The spacious basement offers even more room to enjoy, featuring a 21' x 23' recreation room with durable stained concrete flooring. Adjacent to the recreation room is a dedicated theatre room with carpet flooring, a 10-foot diagonal projection TV, surround sound, and receiver—perfect for movie nights, watching the big game, or entertaining friends and family. The 14 x 24 storage/mechanical room also has w/d hookups for an additional laundry room downstairs. The oversized two-car garage includes an extended 20-foot bay with convenient access to the fully fenced backyard, providing excellent space for additional vehicles, hobbies, or storage. Outside, new concrete sidewalks add to the home's curb appeal and functionality. Located in the highly desired Andover School District and just a short walk from the middle and high schools, this exceptional home combines space, unique features, and an outstanding location.
