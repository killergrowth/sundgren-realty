Property Description

Enjoy maintenance-free living in this spacious Maize home offering 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and an oversized 2-car garage! The main floor features approximately 2,100 square feet, including a large primary suite with a comfortable sitting area and walk-in closet, a dedicated home office, and wood laminate flooring throughout the entry, living, dining and kitchen areas. With an additional 830-square-foot bonus suite on the second level, there’s nearly 3,000 total square feet of space to enjoy! The upstairs includes a bedroom, full bathroom, and separate living room, offering flexibility for guests, extended household needs, or a private retreat. Situated on a great lot with multiple seating areas, an outdoor kitchen and beautiful views, this home combines generous living space with versatile options to fit your needs. If you're looking for low-maintenance living without sacrificing space, this Maize home is worth a look!
