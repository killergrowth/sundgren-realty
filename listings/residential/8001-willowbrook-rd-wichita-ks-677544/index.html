Property Description

Welcome home to this beautifully updated home in the desirable Bonnie Brae Addition! Featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, plus an additional non-conforming room, this home offers tons of space with generously sized rooms throughout and a bright, open-concept layout. The beautifully updated kitchen features quartz countertops, skylights that bring in tons of natural light, and a large island with additional seating perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Enjoy brand-new flooring, stylish designer accent walls, and a fireplace in the dining room. The huge sunroom provides even more space to relax or entertain. Downstairs you’ll find a second fireplace along with tons of storage space. Outside, enjoy a large fenced backyard with mature trees and a basketball goal. Main-floor laundry, a corner lot, and a two-car garage add even more convenience. Ideally located near shopping, restaurants, and private schools, with everything you need just minutes away. Beautifully updated, move-in ready, and plenty of room to spread out.
