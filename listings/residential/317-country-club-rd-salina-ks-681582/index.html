Property Description

A rare opportunity across from the Salina Country Club! This spacious two-story home is impeccably maintained and is situated on almost a half acre lot. Natural light fills the generous rooms and versatile living spaces. Upstairs, three spacious bedrooms include a primary suite with two closets and an attached three-quarter bath. An additional four-piece bathroom serves the other two bedrooms. The expansive main floor offers plenty of room for everyday living and entertaining. A family room addition behind the garage features a fireplace, easy-care luxury vinyl plank flooring, and French doors opening to a brick patio. The flooring continues into the kitchen, where all appliances remain, and an adjoining breakfast nook provides a casual dining spot. For larger gatherings, the formal dining room is perfect for the upcoming holidays. The entry hall includes a convenient drop zone and could be ideal for an office or craft area. A half-bath is conveniently located on this level, while the expansive formal living room offers another fireplace and even more space to relax and gather. The partial basement provides additional living space with beautiful built-ins, ample storage, and two wine racks. Laundry room is also located in the basement along with a bathroom. Outside, the large, beautifully landscaped lot features raised flower beds that add privacy to the brick patio. A screened-in patio offers another welcoming spot to unwind and enjoy the outdoors. This well-maintained home checks all of the wish list boxes with abundant living space, a desirable location, and an attached two-car garage. Showings by appointment only.
