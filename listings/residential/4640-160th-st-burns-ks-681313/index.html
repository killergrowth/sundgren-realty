Property Description

Auction Date: BIDDING ENDS MONDAY NOVEMBER 30, 2026 4:00 PM * Auction Location: ONLINE ONLY AUCTION! * PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: We are excited to present a unique opportunity to experience a rural lifestyle with this well-maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bath home situated on 8.8 acres between Burns and El Dorado in Butler County, Kansas. Room to spread out, usable acreage, and excellent outbuildings for hobbies, livestock, equipment, or storage. The home features approximately 1,200 square feet of finished living space on the main level, with 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath. The full, mostly finished basement provides an additional 2 bedrooms, a full bath, and a basement safe room. All-new carpet throughout gives the home a fresh feel. Built in 1965, the home received a new foundation in 2012, and the roof was new in 2019. Outside, the 8.8 acres offer a wonderful mix of tillable farm ground, mature trees, and native Flint Hills pasture. Whether you’re looking for a little acreage to farm, garden, room for animals, space to enjoy the outdoors, or simply a quieter country lifestyle, this property has plenty to offer. Additional improvements include 54' x 72' building, a detached garage with an attached woodshop area, and a playhouse. There is plenty of room for vehicles, tractors, equipment, storage, projects, and hobbies. Additional property features include: Rural water service to the home, Private water well supplying 5 exterior hydrants, Propane heat, Lagoon wastewater system. If you’re looking for a clean, sound, well-cared-for home with acreage, excellent shop space, and a beautiful mix of farmland, trees, and native Kansas pasture, this property checks all the boxes!
