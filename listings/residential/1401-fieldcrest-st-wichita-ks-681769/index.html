Property Description

If you've been dreaming of a little more breathing room without giving up city convenience, 1401 S. Fieldcrest might just be your kind of place! Tucked along one of Wichita's charming tree-lined streets, this spacious split-level sits on a rare .58-acre lot with no HOA and a backyard that's practically its own destination. We're talking a 20' x 40' in-ground pool, a huge concrete patio, fire pit, mature maple and pine trees, beautiful landscaping, raised garden beds, and even a chicken coop. Summer pool parties? Absolutely. Backyard barbecues? You bet. Quiet mornings in the garden? There's room for that, too. Inside, you'll find plenty of space to make yourself at home. The main level features a generous living room, dining area, and kitchen with sliding doors that open right onto the backyard. Upstairs are three bedrooms, including one with direct access to the full bath and views overlooking the pool. Need even more room? The daylight basement delivers with a comfortable family room centered around a dramatic stone fireplace, a fourth bedroom, and a second full bath. There's also a bonus room ready to become your game room, home gym, media space, or whatever your lifestyle calls for, plus a large storage room. And the extras matter: new carpet, 2023 white siding with 30-year paint, updated HVAC, sump pump, covered front porch, two-car garage, and Speed Queen washer and dryer that stays with the home. A big lot, a backyard made for living, and room for just about everything. This isn't cookie-cutter living. It's your chance to spread out, settle in, and enjoy a little more of what makes a house feel like home. Come see what makes 1401 S. Fieldcrest special!
