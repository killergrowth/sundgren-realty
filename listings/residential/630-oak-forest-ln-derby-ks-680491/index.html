Property Description

Welcome home to 630 N Oak Forest Ln in Derby! Full of charm and inviting spaces, this 3-bedroom, 3-bath home offers 2,266 square feet of comfortable living space and sits on a spacious corner lot. Step inside to find fresh new paint and updated flooring that give the home a bright, refreshed feel. With plenty of room to spread out, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort and functionality. The full finished basement provides even more space for movie nights, entertaining guests, or creating your own cozy retreat.
