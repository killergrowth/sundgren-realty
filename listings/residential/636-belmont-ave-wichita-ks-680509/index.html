Property Description

The home is a 2-bedroom, 1-bath home with a small, covered porch and a nice-sized backyard with a pavilion. Inside there is a large living room, formal dining, kitchen, bedroom and a newly remodeled bathroom with a walk-in shower that is almost complete. The unfinished basement provides for a substantial amount of storage and/or workspace. Although this is certainly a fixer-upper, it offers a lot of potential for the right person.
