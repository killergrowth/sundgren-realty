Property Description

Beautiful lake lot with no walking path giving you privacy and impressive water views from almost every room! Located in the highly desirable NW Chadsworth neighborhood with Maize schools. This 4 bedroom, and 3 bath home is just waiting for that special family to call this home. The main floor features formal and kitchen dining, with a spacious living room and fireplace to snuggle up in front of on those cold winter nights. Adjacent to the spacious living room is a kitchen with granite countertops, stone back splash and island with an electric range. Off the kitchen is the main floor laundry room with access to the garage. You will also find on the main floor the primary with an en-suite and large walk-in closet, and guest bathroom with an additional bedroom. The finished view-out basement provides a large family room with a second fireplace, two good-sized bedrooms with walk-in closets and a full bath with a double vanity. The neighborhood has lots to offer beside just the location being close to New Market Square with shopping and dining, the neighborhood includes walking paths, playground, and several ponds. Call for your showing today and make this your home before the holidays.
