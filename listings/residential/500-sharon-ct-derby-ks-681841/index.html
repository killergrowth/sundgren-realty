Property Description

Exceptional Derby home tucked on a quiet tree-lined cul-de-sac in Oakwood Valley Estates! Features a dramatic 2-story vaulted living room with white beams, stone fireplace, and new luxury plank flooring. Fully updated kitchen includes granite, soft-close cabinetry, rollout pantry, and coffee bar. 100% carpet-free interior! Main floor hidden wet bar adds an extra touch for entertaining. Spacious upper-level primary suite boasts a private balcony, walk-in closet, and updated bath. Two large secondary bedrooms connected by a Jack-and-Jill bath. The finished basement offers a 2nd fireplace, large rec room, 4th bedroom with sitting area, full bath, and storage. Private backyard features a two-tier deck and wrought-iron fence. Incredible heated & insulated 4+ car garage with tandem workshop area, a rare find in Derby!
