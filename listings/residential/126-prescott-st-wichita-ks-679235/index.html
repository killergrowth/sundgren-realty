Property Description

Captivating Colonial style, an incredible front porch, and NO HOA make this West Wichita home an instant standout! Located in Oak Cliff Estates, this home features a stately two-story façade with classic columns and architectural character that is hard to find in the area. Adding even more value, impact-resistant shingles were installed in 2018 — a fantastic upgrade that offers added peace of mind. Step inside to just over 3,000 total square feet with 4 bedrooms and 3.5 baths. The kitchen has been beautifully updated with timeless white cabinetry, while the formal dining room provides the perfect setting for family gatherings, holidays, and entertaining. The spacious primary suite is truly impressive, offering plenty of room to unwind along with a private ensuite bath. Main-floor laundry adds everyday convenience. Outside, the fully fenced backyard is just as inviting, with mature trees providing wonderful shade and a beautiful established setting. A well and sprinkler system help keep the lawn looking its best, while gutter guards on the front gutters offer added convenience. Two sheds provide excellent additional storage, including one with power and A/C, creating great potential for a workshop, hobby space, or more. With nearby Buffalo Park offering additional opportunities for outdoor recreation, this West Wichita location adds even more appeal. Combining timeless Colonial charm, generous living space, thoughtful updates, and no HOA, this home truly offers a style all its own!
