Property Description

This is more than a home. It's an award-winner — earning Pick of the Parade at the Spring 2020 Parade of Homes. Built by Paul Gray Homes, this stunning home delivers wow at every turn. Wood floors run throughout the main living areas, setting a warm, elevated tone the moment you walk in. A bright, open great room flows into the family dining area and a true gourmet kitchen — framed by picture windows and built for both daily life and serious entertaining. The kitchen is outfitted with a Thermador range and double ovens, a large island with a 36-inch cooktop, an oversized walk-in pantry, and abundant storage. A whole-house water filtration system ensures pure water from every tap. A dramatic 15-foot multi-slide glass door opens the entire back of the home to the deck and private backyard, blurring the line between indoor and outdoor living. Throughout the home, Alexa-connected surround sound, a mesh WiFi system, and thoughtful smart-home technology add effortless modern convenience. The main-floor primary suite is a retreat unto itself, with private access to the covered deck — the perfect spot for morning coffee. Inside you will find heated floors, a freestanding soaking tub, an expansive tile shower, and a generous walk-in closet with direct laundry room access complete the spa-like experience. A smart split floor plan places two additional main-level bedrooms — each with its own bath and walk-in closet — for privacy and flexibility. A striking curved staircase leads to a lower level built for living large, finished with sleek polished concrete floors and custom tv framed with grass cloth and custom added oak cabinets: a spacious family room, a wet bar complete with a fridge, ice maker, sink, and dishwasher, a glass-walled wine room, and two additional bedrooms, each with its own full bath and walk-in closet. A downstairs bonus room showcases custom white oak built-ins, adding warmth and function to the space, while a basement safe room offers peace of mind. In total, the home offers 5 bedrooms, 5 full bathrooms, and 2 half baths — comfort and convenience for family and guests alike. Step out from the basement walkout to a backyard transformed into a private resort escape, anchored by a heated sport pool added in October 2024 with an electric safety pool cover, WiFi-connected pool heater and lights — poolside comfort at the touch of a button. A cozy seating area around a gas fire pit rounds out the outdoor living space, made for both quiet evenings for smores and unforgettable gatherings. For future expansion, the area just outside the basement walkout is already plumbed, offering the opportunity for an outdoor shower and bathroom. A 4-car garage with high ceilings completes this exceptional offering, providing ample space for vehicles, storage, and hobbies. The Reserve at Reed's Cove is one of the area's most sought-after addresses — a gated community in the acclaimed Andover school district. Residents enjoy scenic lake walking paths, a pool, a playground, and a clubhouse.
