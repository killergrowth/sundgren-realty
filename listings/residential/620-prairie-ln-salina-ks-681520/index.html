Property Description

Enjoy the convenience of zero-entry living in this 1,408-square-foot home! The open floor plan creates a welcoming space for everyday living and entertaining, while the popular split-bedroom layout offers added privacy. The primary suite features a walk-in closet and a private three-quarter bath with a walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms and a four-piece bathroom are located on the opposite side of the home. The kitchen offers ample cabinet space, a pantry, and a prep-and-eating bar, with laundry conveniently located nearby. Spend your evenings relaxing on the deck overlooking the privacy-fenced backyard. An attached two-car garage provides additional storage with pull-down attic access. Special taxes are scheduled to be paid off in 2028!
