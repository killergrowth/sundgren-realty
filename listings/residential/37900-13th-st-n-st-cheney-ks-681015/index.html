Property Description

Welcome to your 20-acre paradise! If you've been dreaming about room to roam, stunning country views, and top-tier construction, this is the one you've been waiting for! Now fully completed and finished out beautifully, this over 3,000 sq ft. red-steel constructed home features an expansive, open-concept living space perfect for entertaining. The heart of the home is the large kitchen complete with a massive gathering island and an incredible walk-in pantry that's the size of a small kitchen! Offering 5 spacious bedrooms, 2.5 baths, a dedicated storm safe room, and breathtaking country views out every window. The primary suite boasts a beautifully finished bathroom complete with a walk-in shower and a massive walk-in closet with a custom framed-out area ideal for a gun safe or extra storage. Step outside to enjoy wide-open skies from your covered patio! A truly perfect place to relax and unwind, all just 17 miles from 21st and Maize. Bonus: A huge 40x80 insulated red-steel shop featuring 6-inch concrete floors, 14-foot eaves, and a full bath rough-in, endless possibilities for tools, toys, or a workshop. Everything is done, just move in and start living the country dream!
