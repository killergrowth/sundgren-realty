Property Description

Welcome to 1231 S Sheridan Ave, where a peaceful riverfront setting, more than an acre of land, and the convenience of Valley Center come together to create a truly special place to call home. Situated on approximately 1.22 acres and backing to the Little Arkansas River, this 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers 1,732 square feet of living space—but the backyard is what truly sets this property apart. Step outside and discover your own private retreat. The expansive backyard stretches toward the Little Arkansas River, surrounded by mature trees and natural beauty that create a sense of privacy and serenity rarely found this close to town. Start your mornings with coffee while taking in the peaceful river views, spend quiet evenings unwinding beneath the trees, or simply enjoy watching the seasons and wildlife change from your own backyard. Whether you're looking for a place to escape the pace of everyday life or a setting where friends and family will love to gather, this property offers an outdoor experience that's difficult to duplicate. With more than an acre to enjoy, there is plenty of room to host summer cookouts, backyard games, family celebrations, evenings around a fire, or relaxed get-togethers overlooking the river. The patio provides a natural extension of the home's living space and an inviting spot for outdoor dining and entertaining. From large gatherings to quiet evenings by the water, this backyard was made for creating memories. Inside, the home offers warmth and character with beautiful hardwood flooring, a wood-burning fireplace, and an updated kitchen featuring granite countertops, an island, and abundant cabinetry. The comfortable living and dining spaces flow together nicely, making entertaining just as enjoyable indoors as it is outside. Additional finished space downstairs provides even more flexibility for a recreation room, game room, media area, home office, workout space, or second gathering area. A 2-car attached garage, metal roof, and no HOA add even more practicality and appeal. Located within the Valley Center School District with convenient access to Wichita, Park City, shopping, dining, and major roadways, this home offers the best of both worlds—the accessibility of being close to town with the peaceful feeling of your own private river retreat. Properties offering this combination of approximately 1.22 acres, mature trees, a spacious backyard, and a setting directly along the Little Arkansas River don't come along every day. If you've been searching for a Valley Center home where you can entertain, spread out, reconnect with nature, and come home each day to the peace and serenity of the river, 1231 S Sheridan Ave deserves to be at the top of your list. Schedule your private showing and experience this remarkable setting for yourself.
