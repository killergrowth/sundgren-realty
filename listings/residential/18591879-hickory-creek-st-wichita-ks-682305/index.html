FOR SALE! WEST WICHITA INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY – 4 DUPLEXES – 8 RENTAL UNITS – BUILT IN 2025 – $153,120 POTENTIAL ANNUAL GROSS RENTAL INCOME! Excellent opportunity to acquire a recently constructed multifamily investment package located along Hickory Creek Court in west Wichita, Kansas. The package consists of four duplexes totaling 8 rental units and includes 1859–1861, 1865–1867, 1871–1873 and 1877–1879 Hickory Creek Court. Built in 2025, each duplex offers approximately 2,488 SF of combined finished living area, with each unit featuring 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, an attached 2-car garage and single-level living on a slab foundation. Interior finishes showcased include open-concept floor plans, vaulted ceilings, modern cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, wood-look flooring, recessed lighting and primary suites with private bathrooms. Exterior features shown include covered patios and fenced backyards. Based on the seller-provided rent roll, each unit is scheduled at $1,595 per month, representing a potential gross rental income of $3,190 per duplex monthly and $12,760 monthly ($153,120 annually) for the entire package, assuming full occupancy. Conveniently located near W. Kellogg and S. 135th Street West, the properties offer easy access to shopping, dining, major roadways and Wichita Dwight D. Eisenhower National Airport. This package presents an attractive opportunity for investors seeking recently constructed residential rental properties within a concentrated geographic area.
