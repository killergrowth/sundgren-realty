Property Description

Situated on a corner lot in West Wichita, this beautifully updated home offers spacious, open-concept living, a stunning remodeled kitchen, and a finished basement complete with a second kitchen! The bright and inviting main level features gorgeous original hardwood floors, a beautifully remodeled kitchen with ceiling-height cabinetry, granite countertops, and a large island that opens to the dining and living areas. The spacious living room features exposed ceiling beams, a cozy fireplace, and direct access to the covered back deck, making this home perfect for entertaining. Downstairs, you'll find an additional living area, second kitchen, bathroom, and generous storage space with potential for a fourth bedroom. This flexible layout is ideal for multigenerational living, extended family, or hosting guests. Major improvements include a NEW HVAC system (2025) with warranty coverage through 2030, radon mitigation system (2024), new sump pump (2025), updated flooring, refreshed bathrooms, and more! Additional highlights include main-floor laundry, a well and irrigation system, and a covered back deck. Beautifully maintained, thoughtfully updated, and offering space for just about every stage of life, this is one you'll want to see in person!
