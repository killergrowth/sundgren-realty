Property Description

If you’ve been waiting for a home with actual character, craftsmanship, and a location on a tree-lined street just steps from College Hill, this is it! With 2150 square feet, 4 bedrooms, and 2 baths, this home offers charm and function. The primary bedroom has both access to the second-floor bathroom, as well as its own attached dressing room with additional closet space, vanity, and sink. The second floor also has 2 additional bedrooms. On the main floor, you will find the 4th bedroom, flanked by French doors for endless possibilities. The main floor also includes 2 large living spaces, multiple dining areas, and a kitchen with main-floor laundry. An unfinished basement offers space for storage, utilities, and protection from Kansas storms. The backyard features a large shed for storage of tools and toys. Enjoy time outside with your deck, patio space, and a fully fenced yard for privacy.
