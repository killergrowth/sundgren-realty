Property Description

Enjoy the space, privacy, and possibilities of country living with this 5-acre property conveniently located just off K-15, offering easy access to both Wichita and Derby! Once home to a Christmas tree farm, this beautiful acreage features gently rolling terrain, mature trees, and a peaceful setting with plenty of room to make it your own. The brick home offers 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a functional layout with room to add your personal touches. Inside, the spacious living room is filled with natural light and connects through French doors to the formal dining room. Just beyond, a large composite deck overlooks the scenic backyard, providing the perfect spot for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or simply enjoying the views. The kitchen includes all appliances, while the main-floor laundry room comes complete with a washer and dryer and convenient access to a half bath. Also located on the main level are two bedrooms, including the primary suite with its own private bathroom and walk-in closet. Downstairs, the finished walk-out basement offers even more room to spread out, featuring a generous family room with a wood-burning fireplace that is ducted to provide heat to the main floor. Plus, take advantage of the additional bedroom with an egress window, full bathroom, bonus/non-conforming room, and dedicated storage space with a large safe. When you step outside, the possibilities continue! The property is partially fenced and zoned for horses, making it a great option for those looking to enjoy a little more room for animals, hobbies, or outdoor activities. A spacious 3-car garage, additional carport, and barn with a lean-to provide plenty of room for vehicles, equipment, and projects. Whether you're looking for a peaceful place to call home, space for your hobbies, or the opportunity to enjoy acreage without sacrificing convenience, this property has so much to offer!
