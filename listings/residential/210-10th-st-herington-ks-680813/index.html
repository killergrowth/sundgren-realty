Property Description

Got a toolbox, a little ambition, and a vision? Here’s your next project! Located in Herington, Kansas, this 792 sq. ft. fixer-upper sits on approximately 0.48 acres and is ready for someone to roll up their sleeves and bring it back to life! The interior has already been stripped down to the studs and subfloor, so you can skip much of the demolition and get straight to rebuilding. Some improvements have already been started, including replacement windows, a new electrical breaker panel with power and meter connected, and a water manifold connected to city water. The home also features a corrugated-style roof, partial basement, covered porch, and an additional outbuilding. With nearly half an acre, there’s plenty of room to spread out, clean things up, and make the property your own. Is it move-in ready? Nope! Does it need work? Absolutely! But for the right buyer, this could be an interesting opportunity for an investor, handyman, or anyone looking for a renovation project. With the interior already opened up, there’s also an opportunity to customize the layout to suit your needs. Bring your tools, bring your imagination, and see what you can make of it! Selling AS IS, CASH ONLY.
