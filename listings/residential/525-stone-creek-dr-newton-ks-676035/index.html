About This Property

Some homes simply can’t be fully appreciated until you walk through them—and this is one of them. Set on a beautifully landscaped one-acre lot in Stone Creek Estates, this four-bedroom, three-and-a-half-bath home offers an incredible combination of space, comfort and indoor-outdoor living. Inside, spacious rooms and an open, easy floor plan make the home feel welcoming from the moment you walk in. The kitchen features granite countertops, stainless appliances, an island, pantry and plenty of cabinet and counter space, all open to the dining and living areas. The main floor is bright, comfortable and designed for both everyday living and having people over. But the way this home connects to the outdoors is what truly sets it apart. The covered deck has been significantly expanded, creating an incredible outdoor living space overlooking the in-ground pool and beautiful new landscaping. Whether it’s a quiet morning outside, dinner with friends or a houseful of people, there is room to enjoy it all. And then there’s the garage—because calling it simply a three-car garage doesn’t begin to tell the story. The added third bay is separated from the rest of the garage and has become an incredible extension of the home’s entertaining space. A glass garage door opens directly to the pool deck, and a convenient half bath means pool guests never need to traipse through the house. Open the door and the entire space connects to the pool and backyard; close it and you have a flexible bonus space that can be used in countless ways. And yes—it can still be a garage. The finished basement adds another full level of living, with a spacious rec room, game room, office area, bedroom and full bath. Add the garden shed, sprinkler system, electric key-controlled pool cover, neighborhood lake and walking path—and no specials—and you have a home with an unusual amount to offer. Beautifully updated, wonderfully spacious and designed to be enjoyed inside and out, this is one you really do need to see to fully understand.
