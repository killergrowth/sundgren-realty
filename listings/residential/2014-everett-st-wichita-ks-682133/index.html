Property Description

Just in time for the holidays, this charming 1,560 sq. ft., 4-bedroom, 2-bath home is ready to welcome you! From the curb, you’ll appreciate the fully fenced front and back yards, a brand-new roof, and updated windows. Step inside to a sunlit 17x11 living room featuring gorgeous original hardwood floors that flow seamlessly into an open-concept dining area and kitchen. The kitchen offers tile flooring, an eating bar, and a Bosch Tronic 3000T point-of-use water heater for instant hot water at the sink (refrigerator and oven/range are negotiable). Off the kitchen is a 12x9’8 office leads out to a brand-new 9x9 deck. The thoughtful split-bedroom floor plan offers ideal privacy for the primary suite, which boasts a sitting area, two closets, a brand-new skylight with an integrated blind, dressing area, and an en-suite bath with a tiled shower and convenient laundry hookups. Three additional spacious bedrooms and a full hall bath with a tub/shower combo complete the layout. Includes a 2016 HVAC and 2019 electric water heater. Note: This is an estate property and is being sold "as-is" in its current condition.
