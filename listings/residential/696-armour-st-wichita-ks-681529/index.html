Property Description

Welcome home to this charming, mostly brick home on an incredible .44-acre lot in the heart of East Wichita! This home has been lovingly cared for and is ready for its next chapter. One of the most remarkable features is the beautifully refinished hardwood floors. Once hidden beneath carpet, these floors have been brought back to life and are absolutely stunning! Combined with fresh paint, new carpet in the family room, and an updated laundry room, the home has undergone an incredible transformation. You’ll appreciate the generous storage throughout, a finished basement recreation room, mature trees, and a master bedroom with its own private bath—a rare and wonderful find in this area! Located directly across from award-winning Price-Harris Elementary, this home offers an exceptional location with easy access to Dillons, shopping, local businesses, and all the conveniences of East Wichita. A home with so much character, charm, and love poured into it—now ready for its next owner to make it their own!
