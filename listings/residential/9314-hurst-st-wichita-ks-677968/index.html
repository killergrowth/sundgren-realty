Welcome to the Franklin floor plan by Liberty Communities! This 1 story home features 4 carpeted bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a kitchen with granite counter tops, soft close cabinets and one large pantry, LVP flooring, window blinds, ceiling fan in primary bedroom and living room, a concrete patio, and a 2-car tandem garage with a FEMA approved storm shelter.
