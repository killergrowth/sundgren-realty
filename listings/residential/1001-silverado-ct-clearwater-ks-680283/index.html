Property Description

4 bedroom, 3 bath duplex features fresh interior and exterior paint and new flooring throughout. It offers 1,074 sq ft on the main floor plus 866 sq ft of finished basement. The main floor has 2 bedrooms, 2 baths and laundry. Downstairs you'll find 2 more bedrooms, a full bath, a huge rec room and a large amount of storage. Includes a 2-car garage and back patio. Enjoy small-town living just southwest of Wichita, with a hometown grocery store and local fishing ponds. Served by Clearwater USD 264. Available individually or with the adjoining side.
