Property Description

Two-bedroom, 1-bath cottage in El Dorado offering classic character, practical living space, and excellent investment potential. Conveniently located near an elementary school, this home features a fenced backyard with a storage shed, providing useful outdoor space for pets, gardening, or additional storage. Inside, you’ll find original hardwood floors in the living room and primary bedroom, adding warmth and timeless appeal. Both bedrooms feature cedar-lined closets for added storage and character. The kitchen includes an electric range and refrigerator, both of which remain with the home. The full bathroom is finished with luxury vinyl flooring, a pedestal sink, and a tub/shower combination. A convenient main-floor laundry/mud room adds to the home’s everyday functionality. Most recently used as a rental property, this cottage offers an appealing opportunity for investors looking to add to their portfolio, while also providing potential for an owner-occupant seeking an affordable home with character and a manageable footprint. With its fenced yard, storage shed, original hardwood floors, and convenient El Dorado location, this property is ready for its next chapter. Call for your private showing today!
