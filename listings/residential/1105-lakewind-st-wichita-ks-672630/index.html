Property Description

Nestled in the highly desirable Sandpiper Bay community of West Wichita, this beautifully updated waterfront condominium offers exceptional lakefront living with sweeping mid-lake views and direct access to a private beach and recreational amenities. Residents enjoy exclusive use of the lake for swimming, fishing, kayaking, canoeing, and paddle boating, along with community features including a dog park, covered pavilion, and maintained grounds covered by low-maintenance HOA dues. Ideally positioned in the center of the bay, this residence boasts expansive water views and stunning west-facing sunsets from the private balcony off the primary bedroom. The main level features an open-concept kitchen with granite countertops, newer appliances (all within five years), wood laminate flooring, a convenient half bath, and an attached one-car garage. The upper level offers two spacious bedrooms with walk-in closets and a Jack-and-Jill bathroom layout, including private vanities in each room and a shared tub/shower area. The finished view-out basement provides additional living space with a family room, remodeled full bathroom with shower, washer and dryer, and ample storage. Recent updates include new HVAC (2022), water heater, roof (approximately 1.5 years old), new carpet (2022), luxury vinyl plank flooring in the basement, new sliding glass door, front door, and maintenance-free front decking, as well as low-maintenance turf in the backyard. All appliances, including refrigerator, washer, and dryer, remain with the property, making this an exceptional turnkey opportunity for refined waterfront living.
