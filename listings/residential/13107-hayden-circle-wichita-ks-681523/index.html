Property Description

Welcome to this gorgeous 4-bedroom, 2-bath Auburn Hills home nestled on an expansive 1/3+ acre cul-de-sac lot within the desirable Goddard Eisenhower School District! Backing directly to the golf course, this home offers beautiful views, a peaceful setting, and incredible spaces for both everyday living and entertaining. Enjoy the outdoors from the low-maintenance composite deck or step outside from the convenient mid-level walkout.
