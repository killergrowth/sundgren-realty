Property Description

Located just north of Longton in Elk County, Ks this exceptional 210-acre tract offers a rare opportunity to own a true multi-use property with an outstanding balance of productive pastureland, reliable water sources, and premier wildlife habitat. Whether you're looking to expand a cattle operation, invest in recreational acreage, or create your own private hunting retreat, this property checks all the boxes. The land is comprised of quality native grass pasture with serviceable perimeter fencing already in place, including newer fencing along the south boundary. Designed with both livestock production and long-term usability in mind, the property features four ponds strategically scattered throughout the acreage, along with a developed spring and spring-fed tank near the center of the property, providing dependable water access year-round for both cattle and wildlife. Outdoor enthusiasts and hunters will immediately appreciate the natural habitat this tract provides. Two wooded draws run along the east and west sides of the property, creating ideal travel corridors and bedding cover for Kansas whitetail deer and other native wildlife. The combination of grass cover, timber, and water creates an excellent environment for recreation and hunting opportunities. Adding even more value, the property is currently open and unleased for both grazing and hunting, allowing immediate possession and complete flexibility for the next owner to manage and utilize the land however they choose. Hard-to-find properties with this level of versatility, natural beauty, and functionality rarely come available. If you've been searching for productive pasture with strong recreational appeal, this impressive 210-acre tract deserves a closer look.
