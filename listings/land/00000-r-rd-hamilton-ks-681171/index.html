Property Description

78± Acres | Flint Hills Hunting, Fishing & Cattle Grazing | Hamilton, Kansas Discover 78± acres of outstanding recreational and agricultural opportunity in the heart of Kansas' beautiful Flint Hills. Located near Hamilton and just 11 miles north of Eureka, this scenic property offers native tallgrass prairie, mature trees, abundant wildlife habitat, fishing, and cattle grazing opportunities. A hunter's paradise, the property features five deer stands, two corn feeders, multiple trail cameras, and a dedicated hunting shed. Deer stands, feeders, and trail cameras are negotiable with the sale. The combination of timber, open grassland, and reliable water sources creates excellent habitat for whitetail deer and other wildlife. Two ponds provide fishing opportunities and water for wildlife and livestock. An exceptional feature is a historic hand-dug water well, reportedly never known to run dry. The native Flint Hills tallgrass prairie offers cattle grazing potential, combining agricultural utility with exceptional recreational enjoyment. Location is another outstanding feature. Just 11 miles south is Eureka, Kansas, home of the historic Eureka Downs Horse Racing Track, which recently reopened after years of inactivity. The return of live horse racing adds another recreational attraction to this Flint Hills community. Own your private piece of the Flint Hills—hunt whitetail deer, fish your own ponds, graze cattle, and enjoy the natural beauty of Kansas tallgrass prairie, all just minutes from Eureka Downs.
