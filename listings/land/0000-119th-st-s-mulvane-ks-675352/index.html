About This Property

Looking for land that feels like your own private world? This 62-acre tract sits less than a half mile east of Mulvane on paved E 119th St, with quick access to K15 — close enough for an easy commute, private enough that you'll forget Kansas is right outside the gate. Multiple build sites throughout the property offer a homesite that's tucked away and hard to beat, with mature timber and a 3.5-acre pond at the heart of the property adding privacy and character to whichever site you choose. That same pond and timber also set up excellent whitetail deer and hunting potential, giving this tract great recreational value alongside its homesite potential. The property is zoned Rural Residential and located within the Mulvane School District, with access via a shared driveway. Whether you're building your dream home, looking for a recreational getaway, or making a solid land investment, this tract checks every box.
