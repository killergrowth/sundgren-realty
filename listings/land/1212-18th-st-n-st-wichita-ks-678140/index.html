Property Description

Discover the possibilities at 1212 W. 18th St N, a well -located duplex in Wichita's North Riverside area. Built in 1964, this approximately 2,558 sq ft property features two separate units, with each unit offering its own private entrance, 2 bedrooms, and 1 full bathroom. This flexible layout is ideal for an owner-occupant, house hacker, or investor seeking income-producing potential. Situated on a generous .25-acre lot, both units have their own separate fenced backyard spaces. The property features brick-veneer construction, central cooling, warm-air heating, and convenient covered carport parking with storage. Public records identify the property as a duplex and indicate TF-3 zoning, which allows for a two-family residential use. The North Riverside location offers easy access to neighborhood amenities, parks, schools, shopping, and major Wichita destinations. With its substantial lot, separate entrances, two - bedroom, one-bathroom units, and desirable central location, this property presents an excellent opportunity to add value and make it your own.
