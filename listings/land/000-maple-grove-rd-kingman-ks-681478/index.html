Property Description

Auction Date: BIDDING ENDS THURSDAY DECEMBER 3, 2026 4:00 PM * Auction Location: ONLINE ONLY AUCTION! * TRACT 2 OF 3 PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: 80+- acres of Reno County, Kansas land offering excellent agriculture production and hunting! Located west of Pretty Prairie and north of Kingman, this property offers mostly tillable farm ground with some bonus hunting. Game camera pictures below were taken at the north end of this tract. There is a working water well and electric on the property, offering utilities in place and the opportunity to build a house, cabin, barn home or structure of your choosing! In addition to quick and easy drives to both Kingman and Pretty Prairie, this property is also less than 1 mile from Hooray Ranch, a world-class, five-star luxury hunting compound and agritourism lodge Hooray Ranch is widely regarded as one of the premier waterfowl, whitetail deer, turkey, and wingshooting destinations in North America. This property offers excellent wildlife habitat and recreational opportunities. Enjoy great Kansas whitetail deer hunting, along with good pheasant, turkey and quail numbers. The combination of pasture, agricultural ground, water, and natural cover creates an attractive environment for wildlife. Located along the Central Flyway migration pattern, known as a top destination for duck and goose hunting, this property would be a prime candidate for waterfowl development. Take a look at this excellent opportunity to Invest In Land! MANNER OF AUCTION: These Tracts will sell individually and in numerical order. Bidding on Tract 2 will not begin concluding until 5 minutes after the conclusion of Tract 1, and Bidding on Tract 3 will not begin concluding until 5 minutes after the conclusion of Tract 2.
