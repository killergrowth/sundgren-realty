**LIVE AND ONLINE AUCTION** **PROPERTY:** 76.82 ± Acres in Harvey County, Kansas **AUCTION DATE:** October 13, 2026, at 6 PM Central **AUCTION LOCATION:** Bentley Eagle Senior Center 504 E Sterling St, Bentley, KS 67016 Irrigated Farm Ground Auction in Harvey County On behalf of BBB Farms, presenting 76.82 ± acres of high-quality irrigated farm ground in Harvey County, Kansas! Located just 3 miles north of Bentley and 5 miles south of Halstead along blacktop Halstead Road, this tract combines productive soils, established irrigation infrastructure, valuable water rights, and excellent year-round access in an area where quality farm ground rarely changes hands. With a center pivot, 101 acre-feet of permitted water, and an 800 GPM irrigation well, this property is set up for serious crop production and offers an incredible opportunity for investors, local producers, and neighboring landowners looking to expand their holdings. IRRIGATION & WATER RIGHTS Water is the cornerstone of this farm. A center pivot irrigation system is supported by an 800 GPM well and a permitted water right of 101 acre-feet. All associated water rights will transfer to the new owner. With an established well, pivot, and water rights already in place, the property provides the infrastructure needed to continue irrigated row-crop production without starting from scratch. TILLABLE GROUND & PRODUCTION Nearly the entire 76.82 ± acres are devoted to irrigated tillable production. Soils consist entirely of Class 2 Solvay loamy fine sand and Nalim-Shellabarger sandy loam, well suited to irrigated corn and soybean rotations. Historical corn yields have ranged from approximately 225–250 bushels per acre, with soybean yields of approximately 60–80 bushels per acre. These production figures, combined with irrigation and productive soils, make the farm appealing to operators and land investors alike. The property is currently planted to soybeans. Possession will occur after the current soybean crop has been harvested. LOCATION & INVESTMENT POTENTIAL Blacktop frontage along Halstead Road provides convenient year-round access for equipment and grain trucks. Farmers Cooperative Elevator Company operates locations in nearby Bentley and Halstead, placing grain handling and cooperative services just a few miles away. Newton offers additional equipment, parts, and agricultural supply options within convenient driving distance. For investors, this tract offers productive irrigated acreage with rental appeal to area operators. For producers, it provides an opportunity to add quality acres in a manageable tract size. Nearby grain markets, established irrigation, and hard-surface access position the property well for continued agricultural use.
