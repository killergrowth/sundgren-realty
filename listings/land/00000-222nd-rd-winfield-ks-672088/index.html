About This Property

This exceptional 161.5± acre hilltop farm in Cowley County, Kansas offers a rare combination of seclusion, changing topography, and proven wildlife habitat. With newly improved access and only one way in and out, the property provides the kind of privacy and security that serious hunters seek. Surrounded by large neighboring landowners and no bordering roads, this tract feels significantly larger than its footprint. The farm features over 100 feet of elevation change, creating a dynamic landscape with a well-defined draw running south to north. This draw is lined with springs and large hardwoods. This natural terrain funnels wildlife movement and enhances hunting opportunities as deer travel up the draw to bedding on the north side of property and food along the south side. A diverse mix of cedar thickets and native grasses create the ideal bedding cover, while the expansive 28± acre food plot—comprised of productive Class II soils—offers a reliable food source to hold, grow and attract wildlife year-round. Water availability on the property is strong, further supporting the farm’s ability to hold deer. With a documented history of producing mature Kansas whitetail bucks, this farm is well-positioned for both recreational hunting and long-term land investment. The farms size will qualify any landowner for 2 Kansas buck tags every year. Whether you’re looking proven premier hunting property or secure a secluded piece of Kansas ground, this farm checks all the boxes.
