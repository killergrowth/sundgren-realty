Property Description

Finally! Acreage for sale with no covenants and no HOA! Welcome to this 4-acre tract located at E 77th St N and N 111th St E, just north of Bel Aire. Enjoy the perfect balance of convenience and country living, with easy access to shopping, dining, and entertainment while still embracing the peace and privacy of a rural setting. Enjoy the freedom of no covenants or restrictions, allowing you to build your vision your way. With no build timeframe and an open builder, you have the flexibility to create exactly what you want—whether it’s your dream home, a shop, animals, or homestead. Located in the Circle School District, this property offers the feel of small-town living with nearby amenities in Kechi, Belle Aire, Wichita, and Valley Center miles away. There is another 4 acre tract right next to this one, if a buyer is interested in owning additional acreage or bringing a family or friend to be their neighbor! Rural Water District #1 is available along 77th St., with buyers responsible for verifying availability and tap fees. The access to the tract will be served by a access reserve easement from 77th St. You can build your Kansas dream here! Welcome home!
