Auction Date: FRIDAY NOVEMBER 20, 2026 11:00 AM * Auction Location: WOODSON COUNTY 4-H COMMUNITY BUILDING, 701 S. FRY ST., YATES CENTER, KS 66783 * PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: Here is an outstanding opportunity to own 1,120 acres of native bluestem cattle grazing pasture in Woodson County, Kansas, west of Yates Center. This scenic tract showcases the rolling terrain and natural beauty characteristic of the Kansas tallgrass prairie, offering an excellent setting for a working cattle operation. Native bluestem grasses provide a traditional Kansas grazing resource, while a variety of ponds, springs, small creeks, and wet-weather draws offer multiple sources of water throughout the property. The ranch is cross fenced into four separate grazing units, allowing for strategic grazing units or rotational grazing. The property features a nice set of guardrail livestock pens, providing a practical asset for cattle handling and ranch operations. Access is convenient via an excellent township-maintained gravel road, located just 2 1/2 miles from U.S. Highway 54. Whether you are looking to expand an existing cattle operation or invest in a substantial block of native Kansas grassland, this 1,120-acre property offers the combination of productive grazing pasture, water, attractive rolling terrain, and convenient access.
