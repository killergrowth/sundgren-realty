Property Description

Exceptional Country Homestead & premier Hunting Property with income, in Barber County KS. Discover an incredible opportunity to own a beautiful country homestead nestled in the heart of Barber County, Kansas, within the famed Unit 16. Offering approximately 179 acres of scenic countryside, this property combines comfortable country living, income-producing opportunities, and some of the finest whitetail hunting in the region. Conveniently situated on the west side of the property sits a spacious 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home featuring a full basement and a 10x20 safe room, providing added peace of mind. A cozy front porch welcomes you home, while the spacious covered deck overlooking the back offers the perfect setting to unwind, entertain, and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Whether youre looking for a permanent residence, a weekend retreat, or a hunting headquarters, this home offers the space and setting to make it your own. Further complimenting the homestead are multiple outbuildings, including an impressive 48x96 machine shed offering ample room for equipment, vehicles, storage, or recreational use. Two 50-amp RV hookups provide additional convenience for guests, hunting parties, or those looking to accomodate a recreational lifestyle. Beyond the homestead, this property offers an attractive income-producing component, with approximately 91 acres enrolled in the Conservation Reserve Program (CRP), generating $56.00 per acre annually. Oil and gas production is also present on the property, adding another layer of potential value. Mineral rights are available for negotiation. Situated in the heart of Barber Countys renowned whitetail country, this property offers exceptional recreational appeal. Unit 16 is known for producing quality whitetail deer, and the surrounding landscape provides an outstanding setting for hunting, wildlife enjoyment, and outdoor recreation. With its combination of acreage, income potential, improvements, and premier location, the possibilities are truly endless. Flexible purchase opportunities are available. The property is being offered as a whole, consisting of approximately 179 acres, or buyers may purchase the homestead, improvements, and approximately 16 acres, with the remaining 163 acres being offered separately. Whether youre searching for a peaceful country home, a productive investment, or a premier hunting property in one of kansas' most sought-after areas, this exceptional barber county offering deserves your attention.
