Auction Date: BIDDING ENDS THURSDAY DECEMBER 3, 2026 4:00 PM * Auction Location: ONLINE ONLY AUCTION! * TRACT 3 OF 3 PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: 80+- acres of Reno County, Kansas land offering excellent agriculture production and hunting! Located west of Pretty Prairie and north of Kingman, this property offers mostly tillable farm ground with a really nice thick shelter belt of trees all along the south end of the property. There is a water hydrant on the property hooked up to the water well across the road to the north on Tract 2. If there are two separate buyers, it will be up to the Tract 2 buyer if you are allowed to have continued use of that water. In addition to quick and easy drives to both Kingman and Pretty Prairie, this property is also less than 1 mile from Hooray Ranch, a world-class, five-star luxury hunting compound and agritourism lodge Hooray Ranch is widely regarded as one of the premier waterfowl, whitetail deer, turkey, and wingshooting destinations in North America. This property offers excellent wildlife habitat and recreational opportunities. Enjoy great Kansas whitetail deer hunting, along with good pheasant, turkey and quail numbers. Located along the Central Flyway migration pattern, known as a top destination for duck and goose hunting, this property would be a prime candidate for waterfowl development.
