Property Description

133.05 acres of all grassland in Marion Co, KS to be Sold at Public Auction on December 11, 2026 2:00 pm at the Hillsboro, KS Scout House at 200 Memorial Drive Hillsboro, KS 67063. This is a nice quarter of grass that has mixed native and tame grasses. The pasture has perimeter fence and cross fencing for rotational grazing. There are 3 ponds and RURAL water for watering livestock. A nice set of all steel gathering, sorting and loading pens are in the NE corner. This is a nice pasture with good fence, reliable water and good pens in a location where grass is hard to come by. Come take a look or contact the Listing agent/auctioneer for more information.
