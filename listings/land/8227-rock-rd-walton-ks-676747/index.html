Rock Rd., Walton, KS 67151 (On-Site) * LAND FEATURES: There is approximately 116 acres of productive tillable farm ground currently planted to corn. The balance of the property is pasture, trees, a wet weather draw and Beaver Creek. Known as being in big farm country, this property also has some added hunting and recreation value. * PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: 155+- acres north of Walton in Harvey County, Kansas. If you are looking for a rural residence with a combination of good quality agriculture and hunting/recreation habitat, do we have the place for you! The 4 bedroom 2 bath farm house was built in 1890, with 1878 square feet of finished living area above ground, 1152 square feet of which are on the main level, and vinyl siding. There is a small one room basement adequate as a storm shelter. The house has rural water, central heat and air, leased propane tank and a lagoon. A fairly extensive remodel was done in the 70’s. Other improvements include a large 40' x 120' equipment building with a 20' x 120' lean-to and multiple other buildings and utility buildings. In addition to the rural water meter, there is also a water well that services the automatic waterers and pens. * PERSONAL PROPERTY AUCTION: There will be a large personal property auction starting at 10:00 AM with vehicles, equipment, tools, furniture and other miscellaneous items. We will stop at 11:00 AM to auction the real estate.
