Property Description

Discover an exceptional opportunity to own 148± acres of diverse Kansas land located just 6 miles southeast of Salina, Kansas. Offering an ideal combination of annual income, grazing potential, and outstanding recreational opportunities, this property has something for everyone. The property features approximately 87 acres enrolled in the Conservation Reserve Program (CRP), generating $6,625 in annual income, providing a dependable return while maintaining valuable wildlife habitat. The remaining 61± acres consist of open pasture and the scenic timber-lined corridor of West Branch Gypsum Creek, creating an attractive blend of grazing land, natural cover, and recreational appeal. Wildlife enthusiasts will appreciate the well-established habitat, mature timber, and nice-sized pond that provide excellent opportunities for hunting, fishing, and enjoying the outdoors. The diverse landscape offers ideal cover and natural resources for deer, turkey, waterfowl, and other native wildlife. Conveniently located along K-4 Highway with blacktop frontage, this property offers excellent accessibility while maintaining the rural character and privacy that make Kansas land so desirable. Whether you're looking for an income-producing investment, a hunting and recreational property, pastureland, or a combination of all three, this 148± acre tract checks all the boxes. Don't miss this opportunity to own a versatile piece of Kansas real estate in a highly desirable location near Salina!
