Property Description

Located in the Southwest corner of Woodson County, just 3 miles east of Toronto, Kansas, along paved State Highway 105, this exceptional 234 +/- acre Flint Hills property offers an outstanding combination of productive native grass, year-round water, quality hunting, and beautiful potential building sites. With approximately 200 +/- acres of well-maintained native grass and 34 +/- acres of mature hardwood timber, this diverse tract is equally suited for cattle production, recreation, and a forever home. The native grass stand is in good condition, featuring Big and Little Bluestem, Switchgrass, Indian Grass, and Buffalo Grass. A solar-powered water well, numerous natural springs, that feed Negro Creek provide excellent water throughout the property, even during dry conditions livestock and wildlife will thrive on this property. Five-wire perimeter fencing is in above-average condition, with no current grazing lease in place. The hardwood creek bottoms are highlighted by massive Burr Oak, White Oak, Black Walnut, Maple, and Cottonwood trees, many producing abundant acorns and providing outstanding wildlife habitat. For the outdoorsman, this farm offers tremendous whitetail and turkey hunting potential. The property creates a natural sanctuary, with over 110 feet of elevation changes, 2 large creek draws, secluded pockets, established deer trails, rubs, scrapes, and excellent turkey habitat. Multiple pockets offer great opportunities for future food plots. Located in Kansas Deer Unit 12, the property also benefits from its proximity to the expansive Toronto Wildlife Area, encompassing more than 4,600 acres of public hunting and recreational ground featuring timber, native grasslands, agricultural fields, and marsh habitat. The adjoining public land provides additional opportunities for deer, turkey, waterfowl, and fishing year-round. Beyond the farm itself, the location is hard to beat. Toronto Lake and Cross Timbers State Park offer outstanding year-round recreation, including fishing, boating, hiking, biking, camping, and additional outdoor activities. Toronto lake itself is home to some of the best white bass, crappie and flat-head fishing in the region. Properties offering this level of diversity and proximity to Toronto Lake and the State Park with over 4,600 acres of public wildlife habitat, boating, hiking and camping are hard to find. Whether you're looking for a productive cattle operation, a private hunting retreat, or the perfect setting to build your forever home, this 234 +/- acre farm delivers an outstanding combination of grass land to expand your cattle operation, build your recreational dreams with breath-taking Flint Hill views. The property has highway frontage, power along the west boundary, a solar water well already in place, and several attractive build-site locations giving any buyer the opportunity to view the gently rolling Flint Hills landscape daily.
