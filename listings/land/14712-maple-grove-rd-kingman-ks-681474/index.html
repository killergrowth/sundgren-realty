Located north of Kingman and west of Pretty Prairie, this versatile property offers an excellent combination of pasture, tillable farm ground, wildlife habitat, and established improvements. Whether you’re looking for a premier hunting property, a working farm, a weekend getaway, or a place to enjoy the Kansas outdoors, this property has plenty to offer. The property features a single-wide mobile home with a stick-built addition, providing a place to stay and enjoy the land. The home has an existing water well, septic system, and electric meter. Additional improvements include: 100' x 60' farm utility and storage shed, 32' x 28' tool shed, Other misc. utility buildings, Livestock pens, Established pasture ground, Tillable farm ground, Pond, Natural wet-weather draw, Easy access from afar! Located just 4 miles from Kingman Municipal Airport. Outstanding Kansas Hunting! In addition to quick and easy drives to both Kingman and Pretty Prairie, this property is also less than 1 mile from Hooray Ranch, a world-class, five-star luxury hunting compound and agritourism lodge Hooray Ranch is widely regarded as one of the premier waterfowl, whitetail deer, turkey, and wingshooting destinations in North America. This property offers excellent wildlife habitat and recreational opportunities. Enjoy great Kansas whitetail deer hunting, along with good pheasant, turkey and quail numbers. The combination of pasture, agricultural ground, water, and natural cover creates an attractive environment for wildlife. Located along the Central Flyway migration pattern, known as a top destination for duck and goose hunting, this property would be a prime candidate for waterfowl development. With its mix of productive ground, livestock facilities, substantial storage buildings, water features, and hunting opportunities, this 240± acre property offers something for everyone. A great opportunity to own a versatile piece of Kansas land with room to farm, hunt, enjoy the outdoors, and make it your own! Invest In Land!
