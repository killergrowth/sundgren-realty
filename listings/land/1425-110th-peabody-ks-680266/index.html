This property has the setup you would hope to find in a rural Kansas farm setting; good house, modern machine shop, vintage barn, excellent setting, quality farm ground and some bonus character and recreation value. This property offers an excellent opportunity to Invest In Land! * TRACT 2 PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: Excellent opportunity to own a country residence on 22+- acres north of Peabody in Marion County, Kansas. This 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom ranch-style home, built in 1960, offers 2,120 square feet of finished living space on the main level, with a practical floor plan designed for comfortable everyday living. Inside, the home features multiple living and family room areas, a functional kitchen, and four main-floor bedrooms, providing plenty of space for a growing family, guests, or a home office. A partially finished basement adds even more room for recreation, storage, or future living space. The appeal continues outdoors. The 80' x 40' building provides excellent space for equipment, vehicles, livestock, a workshop, or other projects. Additional utility is provided by the detached garage. A vintage barn and silo add character while showcasing the property’s agricultural roots. The 22 acres offer a mix of pasture and trees. The pasture has historically been stockpiled and used for winter livestock grazing, providing a practical option for those looking to keep livestock or simply enjoy additional acreage. The tree-lined waterway creates natural cover and habitat for deer and turkey, making them frequent visitors visible right out the font of the house. The property is serviced by rural water, electric, septic, propane, and a private water well. Whether you’re looking to start or expand a farming and livestock operation, a hobby farm, room for horses, or simply the space and privacy that country living provides, this property offers a perfect combination of acreage, quality improvements, and rural lifestyle.
