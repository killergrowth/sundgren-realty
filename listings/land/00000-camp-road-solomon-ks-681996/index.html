Property Description

Horizon Farm & Ranch Realty, LLC is proud to present the Ingermanson Trust Land Auction, featuring 1,140 +/- acres of premier Kansas land distributed across Dickinson, Saline & Ottawa Counties. Offered in 8 individual tracts, this multi-parcel offering represents an extraordinary opportunity for local farm operators and land investors to acquire highly productive central Kansas real estate. The portfolio highlights a diverse land composition tailored to a wide range of agricultural and recreational goals. Bidders will find highly productive upland and wetland tillable cropland with strong soil ratings, well-maintained waterways and spaces with mature wildlife habitat. If you are looking to expand an active row-crop enterprise, these 8 tracts offer flexible sizing and multi-use utility.
