Property Description

Some properties offer acreage. Others offer opportunity. This one offers both! Discover 39.2 beautiful acres perfectly positioned in northern Harvey County, with easy access to K-15. The peaceful countryside views, established trees, a natural spring feeding into the watershed along the eastern portion of the property, and the wide-open beauty of Kansas farmland, makes this the kind of property that invites you to dream a little bigger! Currently planted to winter wheat, this land offers agricultural appeal today while presenting exciting possibilities for tomorrow. Imagine building your dream country home, enjoying the privacy of your own acreage, watching Kansas sunsets stretch across the horizon, and having room to roam, explore, and enjoy the outdoors. With its combination of open ground, mature trees, and a natural water source, the property also offers wonderful potential for wildlife habitat, hunting, and recreation. And the location? Exceptional! Situated in the fantastic Goessel School District, this beautiful acreage offers the rare combination of peaceful country living and everyday convenience. With county-confirmed residential building potential, easy access from a well-maintained county road, utilities serving adjacent properties, and convenient proximity Newton, and Hesston, the possibilities are exciting! Enjoy the freedom of country living while remaining within easy reach of shopping, healthcare, schools, and surrounding communities. Buy the land now. Envision what comes next. Whether your plans include building a custom country home, continuing agricultural use, or enjoying the recreational opportunities this property offers, there’s something special about owning a piece of Kansas countryside with so much potential. The current winter wheat crop is anticipated to be harvested this next season, with tenant rights applying through harvest. Following harvest, the property offers flexibility for continued agricultural use or other permitted uses. From golden wheat fields to the home you’ve always dreamed of, this is more than a piece of land. It’s the beginning of whatever comes next. Come experience the beauty, privacy, and possibilities of North Harvey County. Your country living dreams could start right here!
