Property Description

The perfect setting to build your dream home ~ This stunning 40-acre property offers a beautiful combination of rolling hills, mature trees, open pastures, and two picturesque ponds. Enjoy the peace and privacy of country living while being conveniently located just 5 minutes from Augusta and 15 minutes from Andover. With a wonderful mix of wooded areas and open land, there are plenty of possibilities for choosing the perfect homesite while enjoying the natural beauty of the property. A major bonus is the existing water service with a meter already in place, offering significant savings and convenience for future construction. The property also features a hay barn, adding functionality for agricultural or recreational use. Mineral rights convey to the buyer as well! Whether you're dreaming of a private country estate, hobby farm, or simply a peaceful retreat with room to roam, this exceptional 40-acre property offers endless potential!
