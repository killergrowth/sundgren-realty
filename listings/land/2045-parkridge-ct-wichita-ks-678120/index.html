Property Description

Move-in ready and beautifully updated, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch with a 3-car garage is located in the highly sought-after Maize School District! The remodeled kitchen is a true showstopper, featuring soft-close cabinetry with pull-out drawers, quartz countertops, shiplap backsplash, stainless steel gas range, custom light fixtures, and a versatile bar area that would also make the perfect coffee station. Waterproof wood laminate flooring flows seamlessly from the kitchen into the living room and down the hallway, creating a fresh, cohesive feel throughout the main level. The finished basement offers a spacious family room, third bedroom, and plenty of room to spread out. Step outside to the large, partially covered deck just off the kitchen; ideal for grilling, entertaining, or relaxing while overlooking the fully fenced backyard with mature trees and a storage shed. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, or anyone needing extra workspace will appreciate the insulated 3-car garage (doors are insulated too), including a divided third stall that makes an ideal workshop and features its own rear garage door for added convenience. Fantastic west-side location with quick access to everything! Just minutes down 21st Street from NewMarket Square for shopping, dining, and entertainment, plus an easy drive north on 119th to Maize schools. Updated, move-in ready, and packed with features - this one is ready to welcome you home!
