Property Description

Something new is coming to Valley Center! Welcome to ValePointe, a thoughtfully planned single-family community designed to bring together attainable new construction, neighborhood amenities and the convenience of everything Valley Center has to offer. With homes targeting a price point under $300,000, ValePointe is an exciting opportunity to build new without giving up the neighborhood feel you’ve been looking for. Phase 1 is anticipated to be complete by December 31, 2026, with home construction expected to begin in early 2027. The community is planned around plenty of green space, walking paths and a well-maintained pond, giving residents room to get outside, walk the neighborhood and enjoy their surroundings. Location is another reason to love ValePointe. The community is conveniently located near Wheatland Elementary, Valley Center Intermediate School, the new Valley Center High School and the new Valley Center Recreation Center, with easy access to parks, schools, recreation and everything this growing community has to offer. Homes will feature one- and two-story, slab-on-grade construction. **Work with one of our preferred builders or bring your own!** Buyers can create a custom-built home using one of our popular floor plan options while selecting finishes and details to make it their own. All builders and plans are subject to approval and applicable neighborhood covenants. A $500 lot reservation fee and executed Lot Reservation Agreement are required to reserve a lot. The reservation agreement will transition to a purchase contract once the lot is buildable. Once the lot is purchased and closed, construction must begin within 30 days. HOA to be established. Specials are estimated and subject to change. Several Phase 1 lots are already spoken for! Contact our sales team as soon as possible for the current list of available lots and options. Pick your lot, choose your plan and start imagining your new home at ValePointe!
