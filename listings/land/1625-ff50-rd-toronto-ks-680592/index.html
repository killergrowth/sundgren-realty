Property features include a 5-bedroom, 2½-bath farmhouse, barn, buildings & livestock pens. The home is serviced by rural water, electric, propane and offers a great opportunity for those looking for a rural residence with land and agricultural amenities. Access is excellent, with HWY 54 at the southern boundary to the property. The acreage includes good native pasture and tame grass cross fenced into multiple units, providing opportunities for livestock, horses, hay production, or other agricultural uses. In addition to a water well that supplies water to a couple of the grazing units, there are two ponds on the property. This scenic property includes nice elevation changes and a high hill west of the house that provides the setting you expect in this region of tallgrass prairie.
