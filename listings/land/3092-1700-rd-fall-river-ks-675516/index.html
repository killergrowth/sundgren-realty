This 28.4± acre parcel just east of Fall River checks all the boxes — pavement, location, water, timber, and utilities — all in a manageable size that's increasingly hard to find. Located on paved 1700 Rd less than a half mile north of Highway 400, this property offers exceptional access with an entrance off 1700 Rd plus a second entrance on the north end off Deer Trail Drive. The property is almost entirely wooded with an abundance of mature hardwoods, offering the seclusion, recreation, and hunting potential that buyers dream about. A wet weather creek winds through the property, and the rolling topography makes it perfect for hiking and exploring. Five ponds are scattered throughout, four of which are strategically placed to feed one another through a series of spillways and dams — a truly unique water feature you won't find on most parcels this size. Several clearings throughout the timber are ready-made locations for food plots. On the south end near the road, you'll find cleared and semi-improved areas with clearings and gravel where improvements once stood, plus a cased well and electric on site — giving you a head start whether you're planning a homesite, weekend getaway, or hunting camp. Whether you're looking for a place to build, a recreational retreat, or an easy land investment, this one delivers.
