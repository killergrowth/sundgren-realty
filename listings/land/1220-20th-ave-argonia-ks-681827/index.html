ABSOLUTE, SELLING REGARDLESS OF PRICE!!! If you’ve been looking for a place to put down roots and farm with a little bit of everything, Parcel 1 is it. This 154± acre property sits at the northeast corner of N Bluff Rd and W 20th Ave N, just 1 mile north of Hwy 160. It brings together a homestead, cropland, pasture, timber, and the outbuildings to run it all. The 2.5± acre homesite is anchored by a 2,371 sq ft ranch home with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and an attached 1-car garage. A large open entry with a bay window welcomes you in, with one bedroom just off it. Down the hall are two more bedrooms, a full bathroom, and an office. The large living room is centered on a wood-burning fireplace, and the open kitchen has an island and dining area that lead out to the patio. A mudroom and laundry, the second full bathroom, and the garage are all just off the kitchen. The home needs work, but the layout and space are there. It’s a real opportunity to build sweat equity and make it the home you want. The outbuildings are a big part of what sets this property apart. Two 40x60 metal buildings give you plenty of room to work and store equipment. The shop has a full concrete floor, electric, 14' tall by 20' wide sliding doors, and an 11' tall by 16' wide overhead door on the side. The second building has a partial concrete floor and electric, and its sliding doors make it a great equipment shed. There’s also an older barn that opens to the pasture, making it work for storage or livestock. Beyond the homestead is the farm ground. Just over 125 acres are tillable, mostly Class 2 Shellabarger sandy loam, Nalim loam, and Milan loam. You can run it yourself, lease it out, or do a bit of both while you get settled. In the southeast corner, about 20 acres of pasture with native grass and roughly 6 acres of heavy woods give the property privacy and cover for wildlife.
