For Sale! 400± ACRES – WALNUT CREEK – PREMIER KANSAS HUNTING LAND – MATURE TIMBER – CROPLAND – PASTURE – TROPHY WHITETAIL DEER & TURKEY HUNTING – RECREATIONAL USE. This is tract 4 of four tracts being offered. This tract consists of approximately 400 acres and is located east of NW Hoch Road and NW 94th Street in northwest Shawnee County near Silver Lake, Kansas. This is a premier recreational and hunting property located in an area of Kansas known for producing exceptional whitetail deer and outstanding turkey hunting. Walnut Creek winds through the property, creating a major wildlife corridor lined with mature timber and providing an excellent combination of water, cover and habitat. The property’s diverse landscape of quality cropland, pasture, heavy timber, creek bottom and secluded pockets creates the type of habitat serious hunters look for, with natural travel corridors, bedding areas, food sources and multiple potential stand locations. The combination of Walnut Creek, mature timber and surrounding agricultural ground gives this property exceptional recreational appeal while maintaining productive agricultural use and income potential. Whether looking for a premier Kansas hunting property, private recreational retreat, agricultural investment or a combination of uses, this is a rare opportunity to purchase a large, contiguous tract in a highly desirable area. Conveniently located northwest of Topeka and near Silver Lake. Seller’s mineral interest transfer with the property. Possession at closing, subject to tenant's rights.
