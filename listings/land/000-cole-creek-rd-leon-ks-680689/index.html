Property Description

Auction Date: WEDNESDAY NOVEMBER 18, 2026 4:00 PM * Auction Location: EL DORADO CIVIC CENTER, 201 E CENTRAL AVE, EL DORADO KS * PROPERTY DESCRIPTION: (2) 160+- acre tracts of Flint Hills pasture between El Dorado and Leon in Butler County, Kansas. Tract 1 has blacktop frontage all along the west boundary, township gravel road along the north boundary, a large pond, wet weather draw and a rural water meter! Tract 2 has township gravel road frontage along the east and south boundaries, 2 ponds and a wet weather draw. Both tracts offer an excellent opportunity to purchase quality livestock grazing pasture in an easily accessible location of Butler County, Kansas. There is an access point located between these two properties that allow the movement of livestock back and forth between the two properties that will be included with Tract 1. That unique feature allows for quick and easy movement back and forth to allow rotational grazing. Quality clean pasture tracts are increasingly difficult to find, so take advantage of this opportunity to Invest In Land! MANNER OF AUCTION: These tracts will be offered high bidders choice by the acre, with the option to take either or both tracts. If the initial high bidder only takes one tract, the remaining tract will then be offered in the same manner.
