Online Land Auction – 403.5± Acres, Wilson County, Kansas | Premier Fall River Hunting Ranch – Whitetail, Turkey & Waterfowl – Productive Cropland – Cabin & Shop – Mineral Income. Online Bidding Only. Ends Friday, August 21 at 10:30 AM. This is truly a one-of-a-kind premium Kansas hunting ranch. Nearly a mile of Fall River frontage cuts through 403.5± acres of mature timber, river bottom cover, and thick travel corridors — the exact terrain that holds mature whitetail, turkey, and stacks of waterfowl on the river itself. This area is known for holding some of Kansas's largest whitetail deer and healthy numbers of turkey. If you've hunted river-bottom country before, you know what this kind of habitat produces, and this tract has it in a scale that's rare to find intact and for sale. Beyond the hunting, productive Class II and III cropland and established pasture add real income to the ground, and mineral production adds another revenue stream on top of that. A cabin and shop are already in place with rural water, septic, and electric — sleep on the property the night before opening morning instead of driving in. Whether you're building a family hunting camp or looking for a turnkey recreational property with income to offset it, 403.5± acres like this doesn't come up often.
