Property Description

Some land gives you space. These 6.9 acres give you a vision: a home overlooking the pond, a porch facing the water, and trees creating a private retreat. Located off Butler County Road in Benton, Kansas, with blacktop access, this property is just minutes from Andover and Stearman Airfield, with convenient access to Wichita’s shopping, dining, and everyday amenities. A gorgeous large pond anchors the property, with open stretches of grass and a secluded tree line framing the landscape. The water becomes the focal point, the trees provide a natural sense of privacy, and the setting invites you to slow down and picture what life could look like here. Imagine a custom home or barndominium designed to capture the pond views, morning coffee on the porch, and evenings spent watching the light settle across the water. Beyond the building vision, the pond and wooded surroundings offer opportunities to explore fishing, hunting, and simply enjoying your own piece of Kansas countryside. They’re not making more land—and acreage that brings together water, privacy, paved access, and a convenient location deserves a closer look. Come walk these 6.9 acres and picture the possibilities.
