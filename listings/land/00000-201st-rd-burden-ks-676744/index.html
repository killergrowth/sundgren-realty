Property Description

A property rarely checks all the boxes when it comes to land uses, this place does just that with a mix of tillable, timber, and grass. It is 74.5 surveyed acres and sits just east of Winfield and south of Burden off of 160 Highway. The land will lay east and west with road frontage off 201st road on the west side. It has approximately 49 acres of farm ground that was last planted to wheat. Mainly consisting of class II soils this farm offers room for growth to your current operation or a source of hands free income to the investor. A trail running along the fields edge will lead you back to a hay meadow lying on the south east side of the property. It is very secluded and has scattered groves of trees creating great habitat for deer, turkey, and upland bird hunting. The property has several build site locations in a very rural area close to the highway. This meadow also has a pond providing a water source for livestock or wildlife. The whole property has a perimeter hedge row making good funnels and travel corridors for deer movement. Along the road runs electric and rural water allowing easy access to utilities. 7.5 miles to Burden KS 10 miles to Winfield KS 55 miles to Wichita KS 124 miles to Tulsa OK Land that will continue to give back to its owner for years to come with several great attributes. These properties are hard to come by in this area, don't overlook your next investment!
