Property Description

Terrific commercial development potential in this established, high traffic area of Central and Tyler!!!! This 3.67 +/- acre parcel offers 2 paved access points; one directly on Central and the other just north off of Socora St. The property is zoned both LC Limited Commercial and GO General Office and utilities are available/nearby. This intersection is a west-side business hub with an estimated daily traffic count, upwards of 33,000 vehicles. Nearby substantial commercial development includes the current new construction of Indian Hills Animal Clinic, Intrust Bank, Enterprise, Hatchett Hyundai, Starbucks, Taco Bell, Wendy's, Jimmy's Egg, Cici's Pizza and more! An approximately 0.831-acre lot split is nearing final approval with Sedgwick County. Upon completion, the property could consist of Lot 1 (approximately 2.84 acres) and Lot 2 (approximately 0.831 acres). Both lots are included in this offering for $630,000 however if you are interested in either of these different lot sizes (either 2.84+/- or 0.831+/_ acre) call for that individual pricing.
