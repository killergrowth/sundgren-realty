ONLINE ONLY!!! 149± Acre Premier Equine Facility in Southwest Geary County! It’s rare to find this much land and this much quality in one place. This 149± acre property sits at the corner of Hwy 77 and Hwy 157, just south of Geary County State Lake, and pairs classic Flint Hills ground with improvements that would be difficult to recreate. It was designed and built as a high-end horse facility and has been home to Rainbow Meadows Horse Rescue. Nearly everything on the property was built in 2019 or newer, and it’s ready to go on day one. It can carry on as a premier equine operation or become a farmstead, ranch, family homesite, or long-term land investment. The main entrance off Hwy 157 leads to the main building. Inside is an open entry with conference and office space, a break room, a full bathroom, and a large storage room. There’s also a well-equipped shop and work area with laundry hookups, an overhead door, a wash sink, and plenty of cabinets, plus an underground tornado shelter. A breezeway connects to the residence, which has an open kitchen and living room around a large island, two bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a laundry room. Out back, a covered patio overlooks the pipe-fenced horse area, the centerpiece of the property. A 75x80 covered open-air arena sits in the middle, surrounded by four 15x80 horse shelters set in an octagon to block the wind. Each shelter has 10 pipe-fence stalls, hinged windows for airflow, and electric, and walkways connect all the way around. At the front of the property, a 36x40 horse barn has 4 stalls, each with its own paddock, plus overhead doors, water, and electric. A 40x100 hay and storage barn, two loafing sheds, and another 15x80 shed complete the setup. Pipe fencing, alleyways, gates, and water are placed with care throughout, and a long alleyway leads out to the multiple large fenced pastures. The land itself is just as appealing, with rolling Flint Hills topography, wooded draws, and ponds. In the southwest corner off Hwy 157, a 50x30 shop sits on a separately surveyed 9.25± acre parcel. It has its own meter, concrete floors, electric, and two 9x8 overhead doors, and can serve as a shop or be converted into a second residence. With highway frontage on both sides, you’re in the country without giving up convenience. Junction City is about 15 minutes away, I-70 is close by, Manhattan is 30 minutes, and Salina is 53 miles.
