Property Description

Professional functionality and distinctive character come together at 104 E. Iron Avenue in downtown Salina. This well-maintained brick office building is in excellent condition inside and out, offering an impressive setting for your business. A spacious reception area with 13-foot ceilings creates a welcoming first impression. Six main-floor office spaces and a conference room provide a practical layout for daily operations, client meetings, and collaboration. At the top of the stairs, a presidential-style executive office with a private bathroom offers a distinguished workspace. A large basement provides abundant storage, while the expansive unfinished attic offers additional space and future possibilities. Adding to its character, the property was once home to Homestead Building & Loan Association, documented at this address in 1949. Two safes from its banking days remain, preserving a unique connection to downtown Salina’s financial history.
