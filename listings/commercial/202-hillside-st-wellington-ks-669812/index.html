Property Description

55,300 SF industrial warehouse facility on 5.5 acres offering exceptional space and flexibility for owner-users, investors, or distribution operations. Constructed in 1950 with prefab steel and wood construction, the building features 13'6" clear ceiling height, durable 4" concrete floors, Phase 3 electrical service, fire sprinkler system, heating, and security cameras. The facility includes four overhead doors along with one loading dock, providing efficient access for shipping, receiving, and equipment operations. Two vehicle lifts are included in the sale, and a portion of the building is currently being used by the owner for automotive repair. A small portion of the building is currently leased for storage generating approximately $3,200 in rental income, allowing for immediate income while leaving the majority of the building available for owner occupancy, expansion, or additional tenants. Situated on 5.5 acres, the property offers ample room for parking, outdoor storage, equipment staging, or future improvements. This versatile industrial facility is ideal for warehouse distribution, contractor operations, manufacturing, logistics, or storage.
