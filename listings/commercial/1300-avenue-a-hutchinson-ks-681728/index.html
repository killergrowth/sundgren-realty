Don’t miss this rare opportunity to own a versatile commercial property right off K-61 in Hutchinson, Kansas. Formerly used as a training and evaluation center and most recently the headquarters of Dig-Rite, this 33,000+ square foot building connects finished offices and conference space with expansive warehouse space on 2.41± acres, all zoned I-3 Heavy Industrial. A diverse building like this is hard to come by, offering the visibility, power, and room to serve your own operation or become a real income producer. Just over 8,000 square feet of finished area includes private offices, open work areas, conference rooms, a multipurpose room, a breakroom, storage, and handicap-accessible entrances and restrooms, with a partial unfinished basement for additional storage. Multiple areas with their own entrances and exits give you the option to divide the space for separate users or keep it all together for one operation. Connected to the finished area, 24,000± square feet of warehouse and shop space is divided into three bays. The south bay is ready for use with 17-foot sidewalls, a 22-foot peak, and a 2–3-ton bridge crane with travel the full length of the bay. A large opening connects it to the middle bay, which delivers 10,000± square feet of shop space and gives you 18,000± combined square feet to work across. The north bay is currently leased, giving a new owner income from day one, and offers 6,160± square feet with drive-through capability, an on-site office, and both dock-high and grade-level loading. Three-phase power and separate meters add to the flexibility for industrial users and multiple tenants alike. The site works as hard as the building does. Three road frontages make for easy access, and a large parking lot serves employees and customers, with plenty of space for heavy equipment or outdoor storage.
