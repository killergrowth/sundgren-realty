Property Description

Welcome to exceptional waterfront living in this stunning custom-built home, perfectly situated in a sought-after subdivision within the desirable Maize School District. Offering over 4,300 finished square feet, 5 bedrooms, and 3.5 bathrooms, and a new roof (8/2026), this beautifully designed residence combines timeless craftsmanship, spacious living areas, and an incredible outdoor lifestyle. From the moment you arrive, the impressive brick and stone exterior and beautifully landscaped grounds set the tone for what awaits inside. Custom woodwork, rich architectural details, soaring ceilings, and stunning finishes create an elegant yet inviting atmosphere throughout the home. The spacious living room showcases a dramatic stone fireplace surrounded by custom built-ins, creating a warm focal point for everyday living and entertaining. The beautifully appointed kitchen features an abundance of custom cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a large center island, fantastic walk-in pantry, and beautiful architectural details. The spacious primary suite is a private retreat with a luxurious primary bathroom including a 2-way fireplace, and huge walk-in closet, highlighted by gorgeous waterfront views and an abundance of natural light. The lower level is designed for entertaining, featuring a spacious family room with a true theater feel, fireplace, built-ins, and plenty of room for gatherings, movie nights, and recreation. Step outside and discover your own private retreat. The huge screened-in deck overlooks the water and provides the perfect setting for morning coffee, relaxed evenings, or entertaining guests. Beyond the deck, a sparkling in-ground pool and expansive, beautifully manicured yard create a resort-like setting you'll enjoy all summer long. With breathtaking waterfront views, exceptional craftsmanship, generous living spaces, an unbeatable location just minutes from shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences, this remarkable home offers a lifestyle that is truly hard to find.
