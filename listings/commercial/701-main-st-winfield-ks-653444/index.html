About This Property

This wonderful stone building has had many upgrades over the years. With a 50' frontage on Main Street (Highway 77) and 140' frontage on 7th Street, this building has plenty of room for multiple business opportunities. With 7000 SF on the main floor and 4000 SF on the second floor, there is plenty of room for multiple setups. There are two entrances on Main and two handicap accessible entrances on 7th. This building could easily be turned into three or four separate spaces on the first level. There is one handicap accessible bathroom on the main floor plus otw0 additional small baths. The same family has owned the building and operated businesses in it for over 70 years. Most recently it has been the home of Virginia Jarvis Antiques. Much of the wiring and plumbing has been updated. There are three electric panels that are well marked. There are 4 heating systems and 2 air conditioning systems as well as a wonderful attic fan, all on the main floor. Utilities are very reasonable for a building of this size. The north wall is insulated. There is an additional entrance and several windows that are covered in insulation and siding that could be reopened if needed. Upstairs there are two offices and multiple open spaces. Also upstairs, were two bathrooms and a kitchenette space which were used daily until the 1980's. Two staircases (one to Main St and one to 7th Street) are already in place. Plenty of street parking is available on 7th and Main. Upstairs would make a great apartment space or owner's apartment with your business on the main floor. The roof was inspected in March of 2025 and pronounced in 'very good condition'. Located in the downtown business district on State Highway 77 (Main Street), this highly visible location is a terrific opportunity.
