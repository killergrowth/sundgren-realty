Property Description

Built in 1900, this charming 1,726 square foot commercial building combines turn-of-the-century character with extensive infrastructure improvements. Most recently operated as a bakery and ice cream parlor, the property offers excellent potential for a cafe, boutique eatery, specialty retail business, or professional office. The electrical system has been extensively updated, including new wiring, electrical panel, exterior disconnect, and LED lighting. Three-phase electrical service is reportedly available. The heating and cooling system was replaced approximately three years ago. Interior improvements include new drywall and finishes, updated kitchen plumbing and sinks, and an improved ADA restroom. Previously configured for food service, the building offers an attractive opportunity for a new business owner or investor. Buyers should independently verify zoning, permitted uses, electrical capacity, and applicable health department requirements. Additional property information includes central heating and air conditioning, a 3,289 square foot lot, and commercial and industrial property classification.
