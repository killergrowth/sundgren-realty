Property Description

Opportunities like this do not pop up often! Great location in Downtown Wichita, centrally located just off the busier main street and close to the courthouse. This suite is located in a beautiful 1928 building, offering a large reception area, 3 large offices, 1 bathroom, kitchenette and storage space in the basement. This suite offers around 1500sqft of usable space not including the basement unfinished storage area. Lots of natural light in this space and is move in ready! This building will come with 2 assigned parking spaces. The new tenant can lease more spaces if needed. There is also street parking available. The previous tenant was a Law Firm. This is a Full Service lease at $2k a month. Tenant is only responsible for internet and phone if they would live those services.
