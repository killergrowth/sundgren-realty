Property Description

Madison Garden Villa COMING SOON! Showings start September 3rd! Feels like new without the wait! This beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bath home in the desirable Madison Garden Villas area offers upgraded finishes, thoughtful design, and the kind of flexible living space you don’t expect in a low-maintenance home. The open main level features a beautiful kitchen with large island, gas range, quartz countertops, and a walk-in pantry. The inviting living and dining spaces are perfect for everyday living or entertaining. The primary suite is truly a retreat, featuring a separate sitting room that can easily serve as a dressing area, exercise room, home office, hobby space—or whatever fits your lifestyle. The primary bathroom has double vanity sinks plus a gorgeous tiled walk-in shower. For ultimate convenience, the primary walk-in closet connects directly to the laundry room. Finishing off the main floor, you'll find the 2nd bedroom with walk-in closet, 2nd full bathroom, plus a coat closet and linen closet. The finished basement provides even more room to spread out with additional living space and game room area, 2 more bedrooms, a full bath, and storage room. The 2-car garage is insulated and finished out with epoxy floors. Enjoy the ease of the Madison Garden Villas lifestyle, where the HOA provides maintenance-free living and takes care of exterior maintenance and lawn care, plus offers a clubhouse with regular HOA social events to keep you active. This location is hard to beat! Step outside and you’re just minutes—or a short walk—from High Park with walking/biking trails, splash pad, playground, fishing and more! Plus you're right down the street from all the Derby conveniences with retail and restaurants. If you’re looking for the feel of a newer home with more space, upgraded finishes and a location that puts everything close by, this Madison Garden Villa checks all the boxes.
