Property Description

155.6 acres of scenic native Flint Hills pasture north of Eureka in Greenwood County, Kansas. This beautiful property has a huge upward elevation change from east to west, over 100 feet! The upper west portion of the property consists of a high plateau from which you can see for many miles in every direction! Water is supplied by a large pond. This excellent livestock grazing pasture presents a wonderful opportunity to Invest In Land!
