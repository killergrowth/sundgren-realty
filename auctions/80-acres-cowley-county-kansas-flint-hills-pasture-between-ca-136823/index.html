Property Description

80+- acres of native Flint Hills pasture located between Cambridge and Latham in Cowley County, Kansas. This bluestem grass pasture has a pond and a small tree lined creek running over 4/10 of a mile through the property. Excellent access is provided by blacktop 281st Rd all along the east boundary, and township gravel 66th Rd all along the north boundary. Nestled in a very scenic region of the Flint Hills, this property offers both agriculture and recreation value. Take a look at this excellent opportunity to Invest In Land!
