Property Description

20+- acres with house and improvements located just east of Flint Hills National Golf Course southeast of Andover Kansas. Home is 4 bed 2 bath offering over 2300 square feet on the main level. The unfinished basement offers additonal space. Detached 2 car garage has lean-to for additional storage. The home is serviced by rural water, a well provides water to outdoor hydrants. Numerous additional outbuilding will provide ample space for your equipment and projects. The west pasture has most recently been utilized as hay meadow. The east field was previously in alfalfa. This property is in a great location, just minutes from Andover, Rose Hill, and Augusta.
