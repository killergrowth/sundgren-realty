Property Description

Sitting on just over an acre in Mount Hope, Kansas, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath home delivers the space and character that are increasingly hard to find. Classic oak woodwork runs throughout, complemented by custom cabinets and quality craftsmanship that give the home a timeless feel. Outside, a large detached shop is ready for vehicles, equipment, and weekend projects, while a circle driveway, spacious backyard, and deck round out the exterior. Mature privacy trees along the front provide a natural buffer from the street without sacrificing the convenience of in-town living. Extra space, a great shop, and a large lot — all within city limits. Schedule your showing today!
