Property Description

54.5 acres bordering the El Dorado Lake property northeast of El Dorado, Kansas! This unique opportunity sits just 400′ from the lake shoreline, and less than 1/2 mile from a public boat ramp. Views of the El Dorado lake and surrounding area are beautiful! Currently utilized as native Flint Hills hay meadow, this property would make a great investment for continued agriculture use or a future home building site. There is an electric line along the south boundary road frontage. With the Corps of Engineers owning most all of the land around the lake, this truly is one of the few opportunity to have such a location! Invest In Land!!
