Property Description

155+- acres north of Walton in Harvey County, Kansas. If you are looking for a rural residence with a combination of good quality agriculture and hunting/recreation habitat, do we have the place for you! The 4 bedroom 1 1/2 bath farm house was built in 1890, with 1878 square feet of finished living area above ground, 1152 square feet of which are on the main level. There is a small one room basement adequate as a storm shelter. Other improvements include a large 40′ x 120′ equipment building with a 20′ x 120′ lean-to and mutiple other buildings and utility buildings. LAND FEATURES: There is approximately 116 acres of productive tillable farm ground currently planted to corn. The balance of the property is pasture, trees, a wet weather draw and Beaver Creek. Known as being in big farm country, this property also has some added hunting and recreation value.
