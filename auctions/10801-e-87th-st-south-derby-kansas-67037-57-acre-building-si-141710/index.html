Property Description

5.7 acres located near Derby, Kansas. This property has a wide variety of appealing characteristics; blacktop frontage, mature trees, and a vintage concrete grain silo just to name a few. There are multiple old buildings on the property, usable for storage or potentially useful for repurposing of materials as you proceed with building your dream home. There is an active rural water meter with Sedgwick RWD #3. There is not an electric meter currently, but the area provider is Butler Rural Electric. The property location offers a nice rural setting with blacktop frontage, but less than 2 miles from Walmart. Convenience, location, and a nice amount of acreage to spread out and make your own!
